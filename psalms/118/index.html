Psalm 118

His Love Endures Forever

“Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good; his love endures forever.” 1

Better to take Refuge

“It is better to take refuge in the Lord than to trust in humans.” 2
“It is better to take refuge in the Lord than to trust in princes.”
“The Lord is my strength and my song; he has become my salvation.”

The Rejected Cornerstone

“The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone.” 3
“The Lord has done this, and it is marvelous in our eyes.”

This is the Day

“This is the day the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it.” 4
“Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.” 5
“Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good; his love endures forever.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Give Thanks (Ho-du la-YHWH): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of the Final Hallel." The "Chesed" (*Love*) of God is the "Frame" (*Verses 1 and 29*) of the "Redemptive Journey." It is the "Root of Festive Gratitude."

2 Take Refuge (la-Cha-soth): The scrolls highlight the "Security Root." Trusting in "Humans" (*Adam*) or "Princes" (*Ne-di-bim*) is "Uprooted" by the "Reliability" of the "Refuge" (*Chasoth*). It signifies "Foundational Trust."

3 Rejected Cornerstone (E-ben Ma-a-su ha-Bo-nim): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Paradox Root." The "Stone" (*Eben*) that was "Uprooted/Rejected" (*Ma-a-su*) by the "Builders" is now the "Head of the Corner" (*Rosh Pin-nah*). It signifies "Vindication and Centrality."

4 This is the Day (ze-hay-Yom A-sah YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Appointed Root." The "Day" (*Yom*) of "Salvation" is a "Divine Manufacture" (*Asah*). It is the "Root of Present Rejoicing."

5 He who Comes (hab-Ba be-Shem YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Prophetic Entry Root." This was the "Anthem" of the "Passover Procession" (and later Palm Sunday). It signify "Covenantal Recognition."

6 His Love Endures Forever (le-Olam Chas-do): The scrolls conclude the Egyptian Hallel with this "Eternal Anchor." It marks the transition to the "Meditation on the Law" in *Psalm 119*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Unending Grace." He is good.
