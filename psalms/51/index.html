Psalm 51

For the director of music. A psalm of David. When the prophet Nathan came to him after David had committed adultery with Bathsheba.

The Cry for Mercy

“Have mercy on me, O God, according to your unfailing love; according to your great compassion blot out my transgressions.” 1
“Wash away all my iniquity and cleanse me from my sin.”

Whiter than Snow

“Cleanse me with hyssop, and I will be clean; wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.” 2

A Pure Heart

“Create in me a pure heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me.” 3
“Do not cast me from your presence or take your Holy Spirit from me.”

The Broken Spirit

“My sacrifice, O God, is a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart you, God, will not despise.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Have Mercy (Cho-ne-ni): In the scrolls, this is the "Ultimate Penitential Root." David is "Rooting" his "Hope" in God’s "Unfailing Love" (*Chesed*). He asks to have his transgressions "Blotted Out" (*Me-cheh*). It is the "Uprooting of a Tainted Past."

2 Cleanse me with Hyssop (The-chat-the-e-ni be-E-zob): Literally "Un-sin me with hyssop." Hyssop was used in ritual purification. The scrolls highlight the "Root of Transformation." To be "Whiter than Snow" (*me-She-legh Al-bin*) is the "Maximum Cleansing." It signifies that "Rootedness" requires "Absolute Purity."

3 Create in me a Pure Heart (Leb Ta-hor Be-ra-Li): This is a "Genesis-level Root." The word for "Create" (*Bera*) is used of God creating the world. David is asking for a "New Internal Root." He needs a "Steadfast Spirit" (*Ruach Na-khon* - literally 'fixed/established spirit'). It is the "Uprooting of the Fallen Nature."

4 Broken and Contrite Heart (Leb-Nish-bar we-Nid-keh): The scrolls highlight the "Upside-down Logic of Grace." God "Delights" in "Brokenness" (*Nish-bar*). It is the "Root of Humility." A "Sacrifice" (*Ze-bach*) of the "Ego" is the "Only Way Back." It marks the transition from "Judgment" (*Psalm 50*) to "Restoration." It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Repentant." God does not despise the broken.
