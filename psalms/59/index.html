Psalm 59

For the director of music. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” Of David. A miktam. When Saul had sent men to watch David’s house in order to kill him.

The High Tower

“Deliver me from my enemies, O God; be my fortress against those who are attacking me.” 1

The Prowling Dogs

“They return at evening, snarling like dogs, and prowl about the city.” 2
“See what they spew from their mouths—the words from their lips are sharp as swords.”

The Strength I Watch

“You are my strength, I watch for you; you, God, are my fortress, my God on whom I can rely.” 3

The Morning Song

“But I will sing of your strength, in the morning I will sing of your love; for you are my fortress.” 4
“You are my strength, I sing praise to you; you, God, are my fortress, my God on whom I can rely.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Deliver me (Hat-zi-la-ni): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Rescue from the Siege." David is "Rooted" in his "House" (*Beth*), but Saul’s men are "Watching" (*Shomerim*). God is the "Fortress" (*Mis-gab* - literally 'high place'). It is the "Uprooting of the Threat at the Door."

2 Prowl like Dogs (ye-He-mu ka-Ka-leb): The scrolls highlight the "Vicious Persistence" of the enemy. "Dogs" in the Ancient Near East were "Scavengers." They "Roam" (*ye-Sobe-bu*) the city looking for a "Kill." It is the "Root of Predator/Prey Dynamics."

3 I Watch for You (Esh-mo-rah): Literally "I will keep/guard for you." This is a "Stunning Reversal" in the scrolls. While the enemies "Watch" David to kill him (*Verse 1*), David "Watches" God to praise Him. It is the "Uprooting of Reactive Fear." God is the "God of my Mercy" (*Elo-he Chas-di*).

4 In the Morning (la-Bo-qer): The scrolls highlight the "Temporal Victory." The "Night" is the time of the "Dogs" (*Verse 6*), but the "Morning" is the time of the "Song" (*A-shir*). It signifies that "Rootedness" outlasts the "Darkness."

5 God on whom I can Rely (Elo-he Chas-di): The scrolls conclude with this "Covenantal Anchor." God is "Exalted" beyond the "Snarls." It marks the transition from "Night-Terror" to "Day-Trust." It marks the transition to the "National Defeat" in *Psalm 60*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Besieged." I watch for You.
