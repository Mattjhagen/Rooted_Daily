Psalm 44

For the director of music. Of the Sons of Korah. A maskil.

The Ancestral Root

“We have heard it with our ears, O God; our ancestors have told us what you did in their days.” 1
“It was not by their sword that they won the land... it was your right hand.”

The Present Rejection

“But now you have rejected and humbled us; you no longer go out with our armies.” 2
“All this came upon us, though we had not forgotten you; our hearts had not turned back.”

Sheep for Slaughter

“Yet for your sake we face death all day long; we are considered as sheep to be slaughtered.” 3

Awake, O Lord!

“Awake, Lord! Why do you sleep? Rouse yourself!” 4
“Rise up and help us; rescue us because of your unfailing love.” 5

Translation Notes

1 We have Heard with our Ears (be-Oz-ne-nu Sha-ma-nu): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Tradition." The people are "Rooted" in the "Narrative" of God’s "Past Sovereignty." It is the "Uprooting of Personal Credit."

2 Rejected and Humbled us (Za-nach-ta wa-Thakh-li-me-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Communal Crisis." God is "Rooted" in the "History," but seems "Absent" from the "Present." It is the "Uprooting of Expectation." The "Heart" (*Lib-be-nu*) has not "Turned Back" (*Verse 18*), making the suffering a "Mystery."

3 Sheep for Slaughter (ke-Tzon Tib-chah): This is the "Martyr’s Root." The suffering is "For Your Sake" (*Al-e-kha*). In the New Testament, Paul quotes this to describe the "Christian Experience" (Romans 8:36). It signifies that "True Rootedness" can coexist with "Extreme Vulnerability."

4 Awake, Lord! (U-rah la-mah Thi-shan): Literally "Wake up! Why do you sleep?" This is a "Daring Anthropomorphism" in the scrolls. The people are "Shaking the Root" of the "Divine Promise." They are calling for the "End of the Nightmare."

5 Rescue us because of your Unfailing Love (u-Phed-e-nu le-Ma-an Chas-dekha): The scrolls conclude with the "Final Appeal." "Rescue" (*Phed-e-nu*) is "Rooted" in God’s "Character" (*Chesed*), not the people’s "Merit." It marks the transition to the "Royal Marriage" of *Psalm 45*. It is the "Rooted Daily" cry of the "Confused Faithful."
