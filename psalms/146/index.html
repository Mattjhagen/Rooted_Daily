Psalm 146

Praise the Lord

“Praise the Lord. Praise the Lord, my soul.” 1
“I will praise the Lord all my life; I will sing praise to my God as long as I live.”

Do Not Trust in Princes

“Do not put your trust in princes, in human beings, who cannot save.” 2
“When their spirit departs, they return to the ground; on that very day their plans come to nothing.”

Blessed is the Help

“Blessed is he whose help is the God of Jacob, whose hope is in the Lord his God.” 3
“He is the Maker of heaven and earth, the sea, and everything in them—he remains faithful forever.”

He Sets Prisoners Free

“He upholds the cause of the oppressed and gives food to the hungry.”
“The Lord sets prisoners free.” 4
“The Lord gives sight to the blind, the Lord lifts up those who are bowed down.”
“The Lord reigns forever, your God, O Zion, for all generations. Praise the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Praise the Lord (Hal-le-lu-Yah): The first of the final five "Hallelujah" psalms. In the scrolls, this is the "Closing Doxology Root." Every breath is now "Uprooted" from complaint and "Rooted" in the "Name."

2 Do not trust in Princes (al-Tib-te-chu bin-Di-bim): The scrolls highlight the "Root of False Security." Human "Generosity" (*Nedib*) or "Power" is "Fragile." To trust in them is to be "Rooted" in "Dust." It signifies "Political Realism."

3 Help... Hope (Ez-ro... Sib-ro): Literally "Whose help is in El-Shaddai/Jacob." The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Root." "Help" (*Ezer*) and "Hope" (*Saber*) must be "Rooted" in the "Maker" (*Oseh*) to survive the "Spirit's Departure."

4 Sets Prisoners Free (Mat-tir Asu-rim): The scrolls highlight the "Liberation Root." God "Uproots" the "Bonds" of the "Oppressed" (*Ashuqim*). It signifies "Active Social Justice."

5 Reigns Forever (Yim-lokh le-Olam): The scrolls conclude with this "Monarchical Finality." The "Reign" (*Malak*) of God is the "Only Permanent Structure." It marks the transition to the "Healing of the Brokenhearted" in *Psalm 147*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Eternal Throne." Praise Him.
