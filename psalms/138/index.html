Psalm 138

Of David.

With All My Heart

“I will praise you, Lord, with all my heart; before the ‘gods’ I will sing your praise.” 1
“I will bow down toward your holy temple and will praise your name for your unfailing love and your faithfulness.”

The Exalted Word

“For you have so exalted your solemn decree that it surpasses your fame.” 2
“When I called, you answered me; you greatly emboldened me.” 3

Kind to the Lowly

“Though the Lord is exalted, he looks kindly on the lowly; though lofty, he sees them from afar.” 4
“The Lord will vindicate me; your love, Lord, endures forever.” 5
“Do not abandon the works of your hands.”

Translation Notes

1 Before the 'Gods' (ne-ged E-lo-him): In the scrolls, "Elohim" can refer to "Angels," "Divine beings," or "Kings." David’s praise is "Rooted" in a "Public Defiance" of all other claims to deity. It is the "Root of Exclusive Worship."

2 Exalted your Solemn Decree (Hig-dal-ta al-kol-Shim-kha Im-ra-the-kha): Literally "You have magnified Your Word above all Your Name." The scrolls highlight the "Authority Root." God’s "Spoken Promise" (*Imra*) is the "Highest Foundation." It signifies "Linguistic Sovereignty."

3 Emboldened me (Tar-hi-be-ni be-Naph-shi Oz): Literally "You made me bold in my soul with strength." The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Root of Courage." God "Roots" the "Oz" (*Strength*) in the "Naphesh" (*Soul*). It is the "Uprooting of Timidity."

4 Kindly on the Lowly (Sha-phal Yir-eh): The scrolls highlight the "Paradoxical Root." The "Lofty" (*Ga-bo-ah*) God "Recognizes/Sees" (*Yir-eh*) the "Lowly" (*Sha-phal*). It signify "Covenantal Attentiveness."

5 Vindicate me (YHWH Yig-mor ba-a-di): Literally "The Lord will complete for me." The scrolls conclude with this "Completion Root." God will "Finish" the "Work" He "Started." It marks the transition to the "All-Searching Eye" in *Psalm 139*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Divine Completion." He is not done with me.
