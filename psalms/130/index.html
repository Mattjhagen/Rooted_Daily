Psalm 130

A song of ascents.

Out of the Depths

“Out of the depths I cry to you, Lord; Lord, hear my voice.” 1

Who Could Stand?

“If you, Lord, kept a record of sins, Lord, who could stand?” 2
“But with you there is forgiveness, so that we can, with reverence, serve you.”

More than Watchmen

“I wait for the Lord, my whole being waits, and in his word I put my hope.” 3
“My soul waits for the Lord more than watchmen wait for the morning.” 4

Full Redemption

“For with the Lord is unfailing love and with him is full redemption.” 5
“He himself will redeem Israel from all their sins.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Out of the Depths (mim-Ma-a-maq-qim): In the scrolls, "Ma-a-maq-qim" refers to the "Deepest Waters" or the "Pit." It is the "Root of Profound Need." The pilgrim "Ascends" (*Ascents*) by first admitting he is in the "Depths." It is the "Uprooting of Denial."

2 Record of Sins (A-wo-noth Tish-mar): Literally "If you should watch/guard iniquities." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Inescapability." If God "Roots" our "Sins" (*Awonoth*), we are "Uprooted" from existence. Forgiveness (*Se-li-chah*) is the "Uprooting of the Record."

3 My Whole Being Waits (Qi-we-tha Naph-shi): Literally "My soul has hoped/waited." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Expectant Patience." Hope is "Rooted" in the "Word" (*Da-bar*). It signify "Spiritual Endurance."

4 More than Watchmen (mi-Sho-me-rim lab-Bo-qer): The scrolls highlight the "Vigilance Root." The "Soul" (*Naphesh*) is as "Alert" as a city "Guard" (*Shomer*) watching for the "First Light" (*Morning*). It signifies "Certainty of Dawn."

5 Full Redemption (Har-beh im-mo Phe-duth): Literally "With Him is Much/Multiplied Ransom." The scrolls highlight the "Abundance Root." God’s "Redemption" (*Peduth*) is "Infinite." It is the "Root of Universal Hope."

6 Redeem Israel (He-yiph-deh eth-Yisra-el): The scrolls conclude with this "National Promise." God Himself "Redeems" (*Yiph-deh*) from "All Iniquities." It marks the transition to the "Still Soul" in *Psalm 131*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Morning Hope." He redeems us.
