Psalm 89

A maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite.

The Eternal Covenant

“I will sing of the Lord’s great love forever; with my mouth I will make your faithfulness known.” 1
“I have made a covenant with my chosen one, I have sworn to David my servant.”

Foundations of the Throne

“The heavens are yours, and yours also the earth; you founded the world.” 2
“Righteousness and justice are the foundation of your throne; love and faithfulness go before you.” 3

The Cast Down Throne

“But you have rejected, you have spurned, you have been very angry with your anointed one.” 4
“You have brought his splendor to an end and cast his throne to the ground.”

Amen and Amen

“Lord, where is your former great love, which in your faithfulness you swore to David?” 5
“Praise be to the Lord forever! Amen and Amen.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Great Love Forever (Chas-de YHWH Olam A-shi-rah): In the scrolls, "Love" (*Chesed*) is the "Foundational Root." The "Faithfulness" (*Emunah*) of God is the "Anchor." It is the "Root of Book III’s Finale."

2 Founded the World (At-tah Ye-sad-tham): The scrolls highlight the "Creator Root." God "Established" (*Ye-sad*) the "Fullness of the Earth" (*Te-bel u-Melo-ah*). It signifies "Unshakable Power."

3 Foundation of your Throne (Me-khon Kis-akha): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of Governance." "Tzedeq" (*Righteousness*) and "Mishpat" (*Justice*) are the "Pillars." "Chesed" and "Emeth" are the "Heralds" who go before the Face (*Panim*). It is the "Divine Constitution."

4 Rejected your Anointed One (Za-nach-ta we-Thim-as... Me-shi-cha-kha): This is the "Agonizing Uprooting Root." The Davidic Covenant (*Berith*) seems "Renounced" (*Ne-ar-ta*). The "Splendor" (*Mi-tha-ro*) is gone. It marks the "National Identity Crisis."

5 Where is your Former Great Love (Ay-yeh Chas-de-kha ha-Ri-sho-nim): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Desperate Questioning." The "Faithfulness" (*Emunah*) sworn to David seems "Invisibile." It is the "Root of the Exile."

6 Amen and Amen (Amen we-Amen): The scrolls conclude Book III with this "Double Affirmation." Despite the "Lament," the "Doxology" remains "Rooted." It marks the transition to the "Moses-Prayer" in *Psalm 90*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Covenantal Hope." Praise be to God.
