Psalm 8

For the director of music. According to gittith. A psalm of David.

The Majestic Name

“Lord, our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth! You have set your glory in the heavens.” 1
“Through the praise of children and infants you have established a stronghold... to silence the foe and the avenger.” 2

The Work of the Fingers

“When I consider your heavens, the work of your fingers, the moon and the stars, which you have set in place...” 3
“What is mankind that you are mindful of them, human beings that you care for them?” 4

Crowned with Glory

“You have made them a little lower than the angels and crowned them with glory and honor.” 5
“You made them rulers over the works of your hands; you put everything under their feet.”
“Lord, our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth!”

Translation Notes

1 How Majestic (Mah-Addir): In the scrolls, "Majesty" (*Addir*) implies a "Weight of Glory." God is the "Root of all Significance." His "Name" is the "Uprooting of Chaos."

2 Stronghold (Oz): Literally "Strength." The scrolls highlight the "Paradox of Power." God uses the "Weakest" (*infants*) to "Uproot" the "Foes" (*Avenger*). It signifies that "Rootedness" is about "Vulnerability" to God rather than "Human Might."

3 Work of your Fingers (Ma’aseh Etz-be’o-thekha): This is a "Beautiful Image of Precision" in the scrolls. The "Vast Universe" is not "Hard Work" for God; it is "Finger-work." It is the "Root of Divine Ease."

4 What is Mankind (Mah-Enosh): The scrolls highlight the "Contrast of Scale." *Enosh* implies "Frail/Mortal man." David is "Uprooting" human "Arrogance" in the presence of the "Moon and Stars." And yet, God is "Mindful" (*Thiz-ker-en-nu*). We are "Rooted" in the "Memory of God."

5 Crowned them with Glory (we-Khabod we-Hadar Tha-atre-hu): The scrolls conclude with the "Restoration of the Image." Humans are "Rooted" in "Royal Dignity." We are "A little lower than the Elohim" (*Angels/God*). By "Putting everything under their feet," God "Roots" our "Identity" as "Stewards of the Earth." It marks the "Sacred Vocation" of humanity. We are not "Accidents"; we are "Crown-bearers."
