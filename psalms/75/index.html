Psalm 75

For the director of music. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A psalm of Asaph. A song.

The Name is Near

“We praise you, God, we praise you, for your Name is near; people tell of your wonderful deeds.” 1

The Appointed Time

“You say, ‘I choose the appointed time; it is I who judge with equity.’” 2
“When the earth and all its people quake, it is I who hold its pillars firm.” 3 Selah

Elevation from God

“No one from the east or the west or from the desert can exalt themselves.” 4
“It is God who judges: He brings one down, he exalts another.”

The Foaming Wine

“In the hand of the Lord is a cup full of foaming wine mixed with spices.” 5
“I will cut off the horns of all the wicked, but the horns of the righteous will be lifted up.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Your Name is Near (Qa-rob She-me-kha): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Divine Immanence." God is not "Distant" in the "Ruins" (*Psalm 74*). His "Name" (*Shem*) is "Close" (*Qarob*). It signifies "Active Presence."

2 Appointed Time (Mo-ed): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereignty of Timing." God is the "Root of History." He "Chooses" (*Eq-qakh*) the "Moment" (*Mo-ed*). It is the "Uprooting of Human Control."

3 Hold its Pillars Firm (A-no-khi Thi-kan-thi Am-mu-de-ha): Literally "I, I have adjusted/established its pillars." The scrolls highlight the "Structural Stability." While the "People Quake" (*Ne-mo-ghim*), God "Roots" the "Foundations" (*Ammudim*). It is the "Root of Creation."

4 Exalt (Ha-rim): Literally "To lift up." The scrolls highlight the "Uprooting of Self-Promotion." True "Elevation" (*Harim*) is not a "Geographical Accident" (*East/West/Desert*), but a "Divine Decision" (*Verse 7*).

5 Cup of Foaming Wine (Kos be-Yad-YHWH we-Ya-yin Cha-mar): This is the "Root of Judgment." The "Wine" (*Yayin*) is "Foaming" (*Chamar* - literally 'bubbling/turbid'). All the "Wicked" (*Re-sha-im*) must "Drink" (*Yi-sh-thu*). It is the "Uprooting of Evil."

6 Horns (Qar-ne): The scrolls conclude with the "Horns of Power." The "Horns" (*Qar-na-yim*) represent "Strength" and "Dignity." God "Cuts Off" (*A-ga-de-a*) the prideful horn and "Exalts" (*The-ro-ma-mah*) the righteous one. It marks the transition to the "Majesty of Salem" in *Psalm 76*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Vindication." The time is set. God is near.
