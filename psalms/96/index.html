Psalm 96

A New Song

“Sing to the Lord a new song; sing to the Lord, all the earth.” 1
“Declare his glory among the nations, his marvelous deeds among all peoples.”

Splendor and Majesty

“Splendor and majesty are before him; strength and glory are in his sanctuary.” 2
“Worship the Lord in the splendor of his holiness; tremble before him, all the earth.” 3

The Resounding Sea

“Let the heavens rejoice, let the earth be glad; let the sea resound, and all that is in it.” 4
“Let all the trees of the forest sing for joy.”

He Comes to Judge

“For he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sing to the Lord a New Song (Shi-ru la-YHWH Shir Cha-dash): In the scrolls, a "New Song" (*Shir Chadash*) is the "Root of Redemption." It is "Global" (*All the earth*). Proclaiming "Salvation" (*Ye-shu-ah*) "Day after Day" (*mi-Yom le-Yom*) signifies the "Perpetual Root of Grace."

2 Splendor and Majesty (Hod we-Ha-dar): The scrolls highlight the "Atmospheric Root of Presence." "Splendor" (*Hod*) and "Majesty" (*Hadar*) are "Heralds." Strength (*Oz*) and Glory (*Thi-phe-reth*) are "Rooted" in the "Sanctuary" (*Miq-dash*). It signifies "Supreme Aesthetics."

3 Splendor of Holiness (be-Had-rath Qo-desh): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Awe." To "Worship" (*Hish-tha-cha-wu*) in this "Splendor" (*Hadrath*) requires "Trembling" (*Chi-lu*). It is the "Uprooting of Casual Worship."

4 Sea Resound (Yir-am ha-Yam): The scrolls highlight the "Cosmic Root of Jubilee." The "Sea" (*Yam*), the "Field" (*Sa-day*), and the "Forest" (*Ya-ar*) are "Alive." It is the "Uprooting of Inert Nature." Nature is a choir.

5 He Comes to Judge (ki Ba Lish-pot): The scrolls conclude with this "Telos Root." "Judgment" (*Mishpat*) is not "Dread," but "Restoration." He judges in "Righteousness" (*Tzedeq*) and "Faithfulness" (*Emunah*). It marks the transition to the "Fire of Presence" in *Psalm 97*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Global Hope." He is coming.
