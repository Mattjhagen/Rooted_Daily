Psalm 12

For the director of music. According to sheminith. A psalm of David.

The Vanishing Loyal

“Help, Lord, for no one is faithful anymore; those who are loyal have vanished from the human race.” 1

The Double Heart

“Everyone lies to their neighbor; they flatter with their lips but harbor deception in their hearts.” 2

The Flawless Word

“And the words of the Lord are flawless, like silver purified in a crucible, like gold refined seven times.” 3
“‘I will now arise,’ says the Lord. ‘I will protect them from those who malign them.’” 4
“The wicked freely strut about when what is vile is honored by the human race.” 5

Translation Notes

1 No one is Faithful (Gam-ar Chasid): In the scrolls, this is the "Uprooting of Integrity." The *Chasid* (godly/loyal) person has become "Extinct." It is a "Root of Societal Collapse." David is "Fleeing" to "God’s Help" (*Hoshi-ah*) as the only "Stable Root."

2 Double Heart (be-Leb wa-Leb): Literally in the scrolls, "With a heart and a heart." This refers to "Duality" or "Hypocrisy." The person is "Rooted" in "Deception." Their "Lips" are "Smooth" (*Chelaqoth*), but their "Soul" is a "Lie."

3 Silver purified in a Crucible (Kes-seph Tzar-uph ba-Alil): The scrolls highlight the "Purity of Divine Speech." Unlike the "Double Words" of men, God’s "Words" (*Im-roth*) have no "Impurity." They are "Refined Seven Times" (*Muz-qaq Shib-athayim*). They are the "Un-uprootable Foundation."

4 I will now Arise (Attah Aqum): This is the "Response of the Truth-speaker." God "Arises" to "Protect" (*A-shith be-Yesha*) those who are "Uprooted" by the "Boastful Tongue" (*Verse 3*). It signifies the "Active Intervention" of the "Word."

5 What is Vile is Honored (ke-Rum Zul-luth): The scrolls conclude with this "Inversion of Values." When "Shallow/Vile" things are "Exalted" (*Rum*), the "Wicked" flourish. It marks the "Cultural Atmosphere" that "Rooted Daily" users must resist. We choose the "Refined Silver" over the "Vile Strut."
