Psalm 23

A psalm of David.

The Good Shepherd

“The Lord is my shepherd, I lack nothing.” 1

The Quiet Waters

“He makes me lie down in green pastures, he leads me beside quiet waters, he refreshes my soul.” 2
“He guides me along the right paths for his name’s sake.”

The Shadow Valley

“Even though I walk through the darkest valley, I will fear no evil, for you are with me.” 3
“Your rod and your staff, they comfort me.”

The Overflowing Cup

“You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies. You anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows.” 4

The Pursuit of Goodness

“Surely your goodness and love will follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the Lord forever.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Lord is my Shepherd (YHWH Ro-i): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Care." David is "Rooting" his "Survival" in the "Divine Shepherd." To "Lack Nothing" (*lo Ech-sar*) is the "Final Result" of "Trusting the Source." It is the "Rooted Daily" motto.

2 Quiet Waters (Me Me-nu-choth): Literally in the scrolls, "Waters of resting-places." This is the "Uprooting of Anxiety." The "Soul" (*Naphesh*) is "Refreshed/Returned" (*Ye-sho-be-eb*). It is the "Spiritual Irrigation" of the "Rooted Tree."

3 Darkest Valley (be-Ge Tzal-ma-weth): Literally "The valley of the shadow-of-death" or "Deep darkness." The scrolls highlight the "Presence in the Pit." Even when the "Environment" is "Uprooted," the "Shepherd" is "With Me" (*Imma-di*). The "Rod" and "Staff" are "Symbols of Authority and Protection."

4 Table... in the Presence of my Enemies (Shul-chan Ne-ged Tzor-e-ray): This is a "Stunning Root of Security." God doesn't "Remove" the enemies; He "Serves a Feast" in front of them. It is the "Uprooting of Fear through Fellowship." The "Overflowing Cup" (*Re-wa-yah*) signifies "Abundant Life."

5 Goodness and Love will Follow me (Tob wa-Chesed Yir-de-phu-ni): Literally in the scrolls, "Goodness and Love will pursue/hunt me." This is a "Divine Reversal." Usually, "Enemies" pursue (*Chapter 7*), but for the "Rooted," "Grace" is the "Hunter." It signifies that the "House of the Lord" (*Beth-YHWH*) is our "Final and Eternal Root." It marks the transition from "Wanderer" to "Dweller."
