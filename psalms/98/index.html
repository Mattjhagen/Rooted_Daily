Psalm 98

A psalm.

His Holy Arm

“Sing to the Lord a new song, for he has done marvelous things.” 1
“His right hand and his holy arm have worked salvation for him.”

Jubilant Song

“Shout for joy to the Lord, all the earth, burst into jubilant song with music.” 2
“With trumpets and the blast of the ram’s horn—shout for joy before the Lord, the King.”

Rivers Clapping Hands

“Let the rivers clap their hands, let the mountains sing together for joy.” 3
“Let the sea resound, and everything in it.”

He Comes to Judge

“For he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Marvelous Things (Nipha-la-oth): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Wonder." The "Right Hand" (*Ye-min*) and "Holy Arm" (*Ze-ro-a Qo-d-sho*) are the "Instruments of Uprooting" the enemy. It is the "Root of Victory."

2 Burst into Jubilant Song (Pitz-chu we-Ran-ne-nu): Literally "Break forth and sing aloud." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Spontaneous Joy." "Shouting" (*Ha-ri-u*) to the King (*Melekh*) is the "Result of Witnessing Salvation." It is the "Uprooting of Silence."

3 Rivers Clap their Hands (Ne-ha-roth Yim-cha-u-Kaph): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Cosmic Harmony." The "Rivers" (*Ne-ha-roth*) and "Mountains" (*Har-im*) are depicted with "Anthropomorphic Jubilee." It signifies the "Redemption of the Whole Creation."

4 Judge the World in Righteousness (Yish-pot Te-bel be-Tze-deq): The scrolls conclude with this "Telos Root." "Judgment" (*Mishpat*) is the "Standard of the Kingdom." It marks the transition to the "Holy Throne" in *Psalm 99*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Global Jubilee." All the ends of the earth have seen it.
