Psalm 103

Of David.

The Inmost Being

“Praise the Lord, my soul; all my inmost being, praise his holy name.” 1
“Praise the Lord, my soul, and forget not all his benefits.”

Heals All Diseases

“Who forgives all your sins and heals all your diseases.” 2
“Who redeems your life from the pit and crowns you with love and compassion.”

As Far as the West

“He does not treat us as our sins deserve or repay us according to our iniquities.”
“As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us.” 3

He Remembers We are Dust

“As a father has compassion on his children, so the Lord has compassion on those who fear him.” 4
“For he knows how we are formed, he remembers that we are dust.” 5
“But from everlasting to everlasting the Lord’s love is with those who fear him.” 6

Translation Notes

1 My Soul... My Inmost Being (Naph-shi... Kol-Qera-bay): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Interior Worship." The "Inmost Being" (*Qera-bay*) refers to the "Bowels/Organs." Every fiber of the human "Root" must "Praise" (*Ba-ra-khi* - literally 'bless'). It is the "Uprooting of Externalism."

2 Heals all your Diseases (har-Ro-phe le-Khol-Thacha-lu-a-yekhi): The scrolls highlight the "Restorative Root." God is the "Physician" (*Rophe*) who "Crowns" (*At-ter*) the soul with "Chesed" (*Love*). It signifies "Total Redemption."

3 As far as the East is from the West (ki-Re-choq Miz-rach mim-Ma-a-rab): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Infinite Separation." Sin is not just "Forgiven," it is "Relocated" to a place of "No Return." It is the "Uprooting of Guilt."

4 As a Father has Compassion (ke-Ra-chem Ab): The scrolls highlight the "Paternal Root." God’s "Compassion" (*Ra-chem*) is "Womb-like/Deep." It signify "Parental Care."

5 He remembers that we are Dust (Za-khur ki-A-phar A-nach-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Humble Root of Formation." God "Knows" (*Ya-da*) our "Form" (*Yetz-re-nu* - the same word as for a potter’s clay). It is the "Uprooting of Pride."

6 Everlasting to Everlasting (me-Olam we-ad-Olam): The scrolls conclude with this "Eternal Root of Love." While the "Grass" (*Verse 15*) "Wither," the "Covenant Love" (*Chesed*) remains "Stable." It marks the transition to the "Creation Narrative" of *Psalm 104*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Mercy." Praise Him, my soul.
