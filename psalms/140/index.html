Psalm 140

Of David.

Rescue and Protect

“Rescue me, Lord, from evildoers; protect me from the violent.” 1
“They make their tongues as sharp as a serpent’s; the poison of vipers is on their lips.” 2

Hidden Nets

“The proud have hidden a snare for me; they have spread out the cords of their net.” 3
“They have set traps for me along my path.”

Justice for the Poor

“I know that the Lord secures justice for the poor and upholds the cause of the needy.” 4
“Surely the righteous will praise your name, and the upright will live in your presence.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Rescue me (Chal-le-tze-ni): In the scrolls, this is the "Extrication Root." The "Violent Man" (*Ish Cha-ma-sim*) seeks to "Uproot" the "Life" of the pilgrim. It is the "Root of Providential Intervention."

2 Poison of Vipers (Cha-math Akh-shub): The scrolls highlight the "Lethal Root of Speech." The "Tongue" (*Lashon*) is a "Weapon." It signifies "Spiritual Toxicity."

3 Cords of their Net (Cha-ba-lim Re-sheth): The scrolls highlight the "Entrapment Root." The "Proud" (*Ge-im*) "Uproot" the "Unsuspecting" through "Hidden Snares" (*Pakh*). It signifies "Premeditated Evil."

4 Secures Justice (Ya-a-seh Din): Literally "The Lord will execute judgment." The scrolls highlight the "Vindicatory Root." The "Needy" (*Eb-yo-nim*) are "Rooted" in the "Court of Heaven." It signifies "Absolute Equity."

5 Presence (Pa-ne-kha): The scrolls conclude with this "Spatial Root of Safety." The "Upright" (*Ye-sha-rim*) find their "Home" (*Dwell/Ye-she-bu*) in the "Presence" (*Face/Panim*) of God. It marks the transition to the "Incense of Prayer" in *Psalm 141*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Vindication." You uphold our cause.
