Psalm 145

A psalm of praise. Of David.

Exalt the King

“I will exalt you, my God the King; I will praise your name for ever and ever.” 1
“Great is the Lord and most worthy of praise; his greatness no one can fathom.”

Rich in Love

“The Lord is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and rich in love.” 2
“The Lord is good to all; he has compassion on all he has made.”

Everlasting Kingdom

“Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and your dominion endures through all generations.” 3

Open Your Hand

“The eyes of all look to you, and you give them their food at the proper time.”
“You open your hand and satisfy the desires of every living thing.” 4
“The Lord is near to all who call on him, to all who call on him in truth.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Exalt you... King (A-ro-mim-kha... ham-Me-lekh): In the scrolls, this is the "Acrostic Alphabet of Praise." Each verse begins with a successive letter of the Hebrew alphabet (*Aleph to Tav*). It signifies "Exhaustive/Total Praise." It is the "Root of Universal Acclamation."

2 Gracious and Compassionate (Chan-nun we-Ra-chum): The scrolls highlight the "Character Root." This formula (*from Exodus 34:6*) is the "Spiritual Nervous System" of the Law. It signify "Internal Mercy."

3 Everlasting Kingdom (Mal-khu-the-kha Mal-khuth kol-O-la-mim): Literally "Kingdom of all worlds/ages." The scrolls highlight the "Temporal Root." God’s "Dominion" (*Mem-shal-te-kha*) "Uproots" all temporary empires. It signifies "Eternal Stability."

4 Open Your Hand (Po-the-ach eth-Ya-de-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Providential Root." Satisfaction (*Ra-tzon*) is "Rooted" in the "Opening" (*Potheach*) of the "Divine Hand" (*Yad*). It signifies "Universal Sustenance."

5 Call on him in Truth (yik-ra-u-hu be-E-meth): The scrolls conclude with this "Authenticity Root." God is "Near" (*Qa-rob*) to those "Rooted" in "Truth" (*Emeth*). It marks the transition to the "Hallelujah" quintet (**Psalms 146-150**). It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Kingdom of Truth." Praise His holy name.
