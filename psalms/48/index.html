Psalm 48

A song. A psalm of the Sons of Korah.

The Holy Mountain

“Great is the Lord, and most worthy of praise, in the city of our God, his holy mountain.” 1
“Beautiful in its loftiness, the joy of the whole earth, is Mount Zion.”

The Panic of Kings

“When the kings assembled, they advanced together. They saw her and were astounded; they fled in terror.” 2
“God is in her citadels; he has shown himself to be her fortress.”

Unfailing Love

“Within your temple, O God, we meditate on your unfailing love.” 3
“Your right hand is filled with righteousness.”

Tell the Generation

“Walk about Zion, go around her, count her towers.” 4
“For this God is our God for ever and ever; he will be our guide even to the end.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Great is the Lord (Ga-dol YHWH): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of City-Praise." Jerusalem is "Rooted" in the "Presence of God." The "Holy Mountain" (*Har-Qodsho*) is the "Static Center" of the "Earth’s Joy" (*Me-shosh Kol-ha-Aretz*). It is the "Uprooting of Secular Space."

2 Astounded; they Fled in Terror (Tha-ma-hu... Ne-che-pha-zu): The scrolls highlight the "Visual Power of the Presence." The "Kings" (*Mela-khim*) didn't even need to be "Fought"; they saw the "Citadels" (*Ar-me-no-the-ha*) and "Uprooted" themselves in "Panic." God is the "Fortress" (*Mis-gab*).

3 Meditate on your Unfailing Love (Dim-mi-nu Chas-dekha): Literally "We have compared/likened your love." The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Reflection" within the "Temple" (*He-khal*). To "Meditate" (*Dim-mi-nu*) is to "Root" the "Mind" in "Character."

4 Count her Towers (Siphe-ru Migh-da-le-ha): This is the "Root of Detailed Observation." The people are encouraged to "Inspect" the "Evidence of Grace." By "Counting Towers" (*Migh-da-le-ha*) and "Viewing Citadels" (*Ar-me-no-the-ha*), they "Root" the "Testimony" for the "Next Generation."

5 Our Guide even to the End (He-na-ha-ge-nu al-Muth): Literally "He will lead us over/unto death." The scrolls conclude with this "Lifelong Root." God doesn't just "Build the City"; He "Leads the Citizen." It marks the transition to the "Wisdom on Wealth" in *Psalm 49*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem. He is our God forever.
