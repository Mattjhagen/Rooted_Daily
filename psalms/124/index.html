Psalm 124

A song of ascents. Of David.

On Our Side

“If the Lord had not been on our side—let Israel say—” 1
“If the Lord had not been on our side when people attacked us.”

Engulfed by the Torrent

“They would have swallowed us alive in their raging anger.” 2
“The flood would have engulfed us, the torrent would have swept over us.”

The Snare is Broken

“We have escaped like a bird from the fowler’s snare; the snare has been broken, and we have escaped.” 3

Help in the Name

“Our help is in the name of the Lord, the Maker of heaven and earth.” 4

Translation Notes

1 If the Lord (Lu-ley YHWH): In the scrolls, this is the "Counterfactual Root." It forces the listener to imagine the "Alternative History" of "Total Destruction." It is the "Root of Providential Recognition."

2 Swallowed us Alive (Cha-yim Be-la-u-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Predatory Root of Evil." The enemies are depicted as "Monsters" or "Floods" (*Ma-yim*) that "Uproot" life. It signify "Existential Threat."

3 Snare has been Broken (hap-Pakh Nish-bar): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Intervention Root." The "Snare" (*Pakh*) is not just "Opened," it is "Shattered" (*Nishbar*). The "Bird" (*Tzip-por*) is "Uprooted" from the trap and "Rooted" in "Freedom" (*Es-ca-ped*). It signifies "Miraculous Rescue."

4 Maker of Heaven and Earth (O-seh Sha-ma-yim wa-Aretz): The scrolls conclude with this "Creation Root of Security." This formula repeats from *Psalm 121*. It marks the transition to the "Unshakable Zion" in *Psalm 125*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Breaking Free." Our help is in the Name.
