Psalm 16

A miktam of David.

The Safe Place

“Keep me safe, my God, for in you I take refuge.” 1
“I say to the Lord, ‘You are my Lord; apart from you I have no good thing.’”

The Delightful Lines

“The Lord has assigned me my portion and my cup; you have made my lot secure.”
“The boundary lines have fallen for me in pleasant places; surely I have a delightful inheritance.” 2

The Right Hand

“I keep my eyes always on the Lord. With him at my right hand, I will not be shaken.” 3
“You will not let your faithful one see decay.” 4

The Path of Life

“You make known to me the path of life; you will fill me with joy in your presence.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Apart from You I have no Good thing (Bal-Ale-kha): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Contentment." David is "Uprooting" the "Idol of Independence." He identifies the "Lord" (*Adonai*) as his "Only Source" (*Chassi-thi*). It is the "Rooted Daily" declaration of "Total Dependence."

2 Boundary Lines (Cha-ba-lim): Literally "Measuring ropes." The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Appointment." David is "Rooting" his "Satisfaction" in the "Territory" God has given him. He has "Uprooted" the "Greed" of *Psalm 10* and replaced it with "Gratitude."

3 With Him at my Right Hand (mi-Yemini): This is the "Root of Stability." The "Right Hand" is the "Place of Honor and Defense." Because God is "Always" (*Sha-wi-thi*) there, David is "Un-shakable" (*lo Em-mot*). It links back to the promise of *Psalm 15*.

4 Not let your Faithful One see Decay (lo-Thit-then Chasid-kha lir-oth Sha-chath): This is a "Prophetic Root." While it applied to David’s "Immediate Deliverance," the scrolls (and the New Testament authors) see this as the "Resurrection Root." Death is the "Ultimate Uprooter," but God’s "Faithful One" (*Chasid*) is "Restored."

5 The Path of Life (Orach Chay-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Vision of Eternity." God "Makes Known" (*To-di-eni*) a "Way" that "Uproots" the "Grave." It leads to "Fullness of Joy" (*Soba Se-ma-choth*) and "Eternal Pleasures" (*Ne-imoth Net-zach*). It marks the "Confidence" of the "Rooted Soul." We are not just "Planting for Earth," but for "Presence."
