Psalm 125

A song of ascents.

Like Mount Zion

“Those who trust in the Lord are like Mount Zion, which cannot be shaken but endures forever.” 1

Mountains Surrounding

“As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the Lord surrounds his people both now and forevermore.” 2

The Scepter of Wickedness

“The scepter of the wicked will not remain over the land allotted to the righteous.” 3
“Lord, do good to those who are good, to those who are upright in heart.”
“Peace be on Israel.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Like Mount Zion (ke-Har Tzi-yon): In the scrolls, this is the "Immovable Root." To "Trust" (*hab-Bo-te-chim*) is to be "Rooted" in the "Stability" of the "Holy Mountain." It is the "Uprooting of Fluctuation."

2 Lord Surrounds (YHWH Sa-bib le-Am-mo): The scrolls highlight the "Circular Protection Root." God is the "Topography of Safety." The "Mountains" (*Har-im*) are the "Visual Root" of this spiritual reality. It signifies "Total Envelopment."

3 Scepter of Wickedness (She-bet ha-Re-sha): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Dominion." Evil may "Visit," but it will not "Rest" (*Ya-nu-ach*) on the "Allotment" (*Go-ral*) of the "Righteous" (*Tzad-di-qim*). It is the "Uprooting of Systemic Injustice."

4 Peace be on Israel (Sha-lom al-Yis-ra-el): The scrolls conclude with this "Liturgical Seal." It marks the transition to the "Dreams of Restoration" in *Psalm 126*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Immovable Hope." You surround us.
