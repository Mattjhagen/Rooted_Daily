Psalm 15

A psalm of David.

The Guest of the Almighty

“Lord, who may dwell in your sacred tent? Who may live on your holy mountain?” 1

The Heart-Truth

“The one whose walk is blameless, who does what is righteous, who speaks the truth from their heart.” 2

The Oath of Sacrifice

“...who keeps an oath even when it hurts, and does not change their mind.” 3

The Unshakable Life

“Whoever does these things will never be shaken.” 4
“They will stand firm in the presence of the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Dwell in your Sacred Tent (Ya-gur be-Oha-lekha): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Hospitality." To "Dwell" (*Ya-gur*) as a "Guest" of God implies a "Covenant Relationship." The "Holy Mountain" (*Har-Qod-shikha*) is the "Final Destination" of the "Rooted."

2 Speaks the Truth from their Heart (Do-ber Emeth be-Lebabo): The scrolls highlight the "Sincerity Root." It isn't just "External Accuracy," but "Internal Integrity." The "Truth" (*Emeth*) is "Rooted" in the "Heart" (*Lebab*). It is the "Uprooting of Flattery" (*Chapter 12*).

3 Oath even when it Hurts (Nish-ba le-Ha-ra): Literally in the scrolls, "Swears to [his own] hurt." This is the "Test of the Root." True "Integrity" is "Fixed" even when it "Costs." It is the "Uprooting of Self-Interest."

4 Will never be Shaken (lo Yim-mot le-Olam): The scrolls conclude with this "Rooted Assurance." To "Be Shaken" (*Yim-mot*) is to be "Uprooted" by the "Storms of Life." The man of "Integrity" is "Firmly Planted" (*Chapter 1*). He is "Rooted" in "Moral Reality."

5 Stand Firm: This "Entrance Liturgy" defines the "Citizenship of Zion." It signifies that "Rootedness" is not just "Spiritual Meditation," but "Ethical Living." It marks the transition from the "Denial" of *Psalm 14* to the "Devotion" of *Psalm 16*. It is the "Rooted Daily" standard for "Dwelling."
