Psalm 147

Heals the Brokenhearted

“Praise the Lord. How good it is to sing praises to our God!”
“He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.” 1

Calls the Stars by Name

“He determines the number of the stars and calls them each by name.” 2
“Great is our Lord and mighty in power; his understanding has no limit.”

The Young Ravens

“He provides food for the cattle and for the young ravens when they call.” 3
“The Lord delights in those who fear him, who put their hope in his unfailing love.”

The Word that Melts

“He sends his command to the earth; his word runs swiftly.”
“He sends his word and melts them; he stirs up his breezes, and the waters flow.” 4
“Praise the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Heals the Brokenhearted (ha-Ro-phe lish-bu-rey Leb): In the scrolls, this is the "Somatic Restoration Root." The "Wounds" (*Atz-tzbot*) are "Bound Up" (*me-chab-besh*). It signifies that God "Roots" His care in the "Fractures" of the soul. It is the "Uprooting of Internal Trauma."

2 Calls the Stars (le-kul-lam She-moth yiq-ra): The scrolls highlight the "Identity Root of Creation." If God "Names" the "Stars" (*Ko-ka-bim*), He certainly "Names" the "Gathered" (*Verse 2*). It signify "Intimate Sovereignty."

3 Young Ravens (li-Bney o-reb): The scrolls highlight the "Orphaned Root of Provision." Ravens were considered "Unclean" yet God "Hears" their "Cry." It is the "Uprooting of Scarcity-Mindset."

4 Word that Melts (Yish-lach De-ba-ro we-yam-sem): The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Power of Speech Root." God’s "Word" (*Dabar*) "Runs Swiftly" (*Verse 15*) and "Melts" (*Yamsem*) the "Ice" (*Qe-rach*). It signifies "The Word as a Force of Nature."

5 Praise the Lord (Hal-le-lu-Yah): The scrolls conclude with this "Closing Hallel." It marks the transition to the "Celestial Chorus" in *Psalm 148*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Healer of Stars." Praise Him.
