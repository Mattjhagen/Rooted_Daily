Psalm 1

The Path of Life

“Blessed is the one who does not walk in step with the wicked or stand in the way that sinners take or sit in the company of mockers.” 1
“But whose delight is in the law of the Lord, and who meditates on his law day and night.” 2

The Tree by the Water

“That person is like a tree planted by streams of water, which yields its fruit in season and whose leaf does not wither—whatever they do prospers.” 3

The Chaff of the Godless

“Not so the wicked! They are like chaff that the wind blows away.” 4
“Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous.”
“For the Lord watches over the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked leads to destruction.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Blessed (Ashre): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Happiness." It implies a "State of Flourishing." The progression of "Walk, Stand, Sit" indicates the "Deepening Root" of influence. The "Blessed Man" is "Uprooted" from the negative and "Rooted" in the divine.

2 Meditates (Yeh-geh): Literally in the scrolls, "Murmurs" or "Utters." It is a "Vocational Root." The man "Chews" on the "Law" (*Torah*) like a "Savoring Breath." It is the "Spiritual Photosynthesis" that leads to the next verse.

3 Tree Planted by Streams (Etz Shathul al-Phalge Mayim): This is the "Central Motif of the Rooted Daily." The "Streams" are "Artificially Channeled" (*Phalge*), implying that the man was "Intentionally Placed" there by God. His "Root" has "Unlimited Access" to "Divine Resource." His "Fruit" (*Chapter 14*) is "Inevitable."

4 Like Chaff (ka-Motz): The scrolls highlight the "Rootlessness of Evil." "Chaff" is the "Useless Skin" of the grain. It has "No Root" and no "Weight." It is "Blown Away" by the "Wind" (*Ruach*). It signifies "Transience" vs "Permanence."

5 The Lord Watches (Yode-a YHWH): Literally "The Lord Knows." In the scrolls, "Knowing" is "Intimate Concern." God is "Rooted" in the "Way" of the righteous. The "Way of the Wicked" has "No Destination" but "Loss." It marks the "Opening Distinction" of the whole Psalter. You are either "Rooted" or "Scattered."
