Psalm 45

For the director of music. To the tune of “Lilies.” Of the Sons of Korah. A maskil. A wedding song.

The Skillful Pen

“My heart is stirred by a noble theme as I recite my verses for the king; my tongue is the pen of a skillful writer.” 1

The Throne of Ages

“You are the most excellent of men and your lips have been anointed with grace.”
“Your throne, O God, will last for ever and ever; a scepter of justice will be the scepter of your kingdom.” 2
“God, your God, has set you above your companions by anointing you with the oil of joy.” 3

Ivory Palaces

“All your robes are fragrant with myrrh and aloes and cassia; from palaces adorned with ivory the music of strings makes you glad.” 4

The Gold of Ophir

“The king is enthroned by your beauty; honor him, for he is your lord.”
“The princess is decked in her chamber with gold-woven robes.” 5

Translation Notes

1 My Heart is Stirred (Ra-chash Libbi): Literally in the scrolls, "My heart bubbles over/overflows." This is the "Inspiration Root." The "Noble Theme" (*Dabar Tob* - literally 'good word') is "Uprooting" the "Silence." The "Tongue" (*Le-sho-ni*) is "Rooted" in "Divine Eloquence."

2 Your Throne, O God (Kis-akha Elohim): The scrolls highlight the "Messianic Identification." The King is addressed as "God" (*Elohim*), a "Pivotal Messianic Root." In the New Testament, this is applied directly to Christ (Hebrews 1:8). It is the "Root of Eternal Sovereignty."

3 Oil of Joy (She-men Sa-son): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic of Anointing." The "Joy" (*Sason*) is a "Root of Gladness" that sets the King "Above" (*me-Chabe-rekha*) all others. It is the "Uprooting of Mourning."

4 Myrrh, Aloes, and Cassia (Mor wa-Aha-loth Qe-tzi-oth): These are the "Sensory Roots of Royalty." The "Ivory Palaces" (*He-khe-le Shen*) represent the "Highest Earthly Luxury." The King is "Rooted" in "Fragrance" and "Music."

5 Gold-woven Robes (Mish-be-tzoth Za-hab): The scrolls conclude with the "Beauty of the Bride." The "Princess" (*Bath-Melekh*) is "Rooted" in "Glory" (*Khabod*). It marks the transition to the "Refuge" of *Psalm 46*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Royal Marriage." The King has come for His Bride.
