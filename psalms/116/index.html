Psalm 116

He Heard My Voice

“I love the Lord, for he heard my voice; he heard my cry for mercy.” 1

The Cords of Death

“The cords of death entangled me, the anguish of the grave came over me.” 2
“Return to your rest, my soul, for the Lord has been good to you.” 3

The Cup of Salvation

“How can I repay the Lord for all his goodness to me?”
“I will lift up the cup of salvation and call on the name of the Lord.” 4

Precious in His Sight

“Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his faithful servants.” 5
“I will sacrifice a thank offering to you.”

Translation Notes

1 I Love the Lord (A-hab-ti ki-Yish-ma YHWH): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Emotional Response." "Love" (*Ahabti*) is the "Fruit" of being "Heard" (*Yishma*). It is the "Root of Reciprocal Intimacy."

2 Cords of Death (Cheb-le Ma-weth): The scrolls highlight the "Entanglement Root." "Anguish" (*Metz-a-re*) literally means "Narrow places." To be "Uprooted" by death is to be "Bound." It signify "Existential Suffocation."

3 Return to your Rest (Shu-bi Naph-shi li-Me-nu-cha-ye-khi): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Settledness." The "Soul" (*Naphesh*) finds its "Rest" (*Me-nu-chah*) only after the "Deliverance" (*He-chal-ta*). It is the "Uprooting of Restlessness."

4 Cup of Salvation (Kos Ye-shu-oth): Literally "Cup of Salvations" (plural). The scrolls highlight the "Root of Commemorative Joy." To "Lift" (*Es-sa*) the "Cup" is a "Ritual of Acknowledgment." It signifies "Abundant Rescue."

5 Precious (Ya-qar): The scrolls highlight the "Value Root." The "Death" (*Ma-weth*) of a "Saint" (*Cha-sid*) is "Costly/Weighted" (*Yaqar*) to God. He does not let them go "Lightly." It marks the transition to the "Universal Praise" in *Psalm 117*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Personal Rescue." You heard me.
