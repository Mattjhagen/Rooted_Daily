Psalm 66

For the director of music. A song. A psalm.

The Global Shout

“Shout for joy to God, all the earth!” 1
“Sing the glory of his name; make his praise glorious.”

Awesome Deeds

“Come and see what God has done, his awesome deeds for mankind.” 2
“He turned the sea into dry land, they passed through the waters on foot.”

Refined like Silver

“For you, God, tested us; you refined us like silver.” 3
“We went through fire and water, but you brought us to a place of abundance.” 4

Listen to my Story

“Come and listen, all you who fear God; let me tell you what he has done for me.”
“Praise be to God, who has not rejected my prayer or withheld his love from me!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Shout for Joy (Ha-ri-u le-Elohim): In the scrolls, this is the "Universal Root of Victory." The "Earth" (*Eretz*) is summoned to "Shout." It is the "Uprooting of Gloom." God’s "Name" (*Shem*) is the "Center of the Glory."

2 Awesome Deeds (No-ra A-li-lah): The scrolls highlight the "Visual Evidence of Power." The "Deeds" (*Alilah*) are "Awesome" (*Nora* - literally 'fearing/awe-inspiring'). The "Sea" (*Yam*) being turned to "Dry Land" (*Ya-ba-shah*) is the "Historical Root of Faith."

3 Refined us like Silver (Tze-raph-tha-nu ki-Tze-roph Ka-seph): The scrolls highlight the "Purification Root." Suffering is seen as "Smelting" (*Tze-roph*). It is the "Uprooting of Dross."

4 Place of Abundance (le-Ra-wa-yah): Literally "A place of saturation/overflow." The scrolls highlight the "Result of the Trial." Fire and Water lead to "Rooted Prosperity." It is the "Transition from Burden to Bounty."

5 Not Rejected my Prayer (lo-He-sir Te-phil-la-thi): The scrolls conclude with this "Individual Confirmation." God has not "Turned Away" (*He-sir*) the "Prayer" or the "Love" (*Chesed*). It marks the transition to the "Aaronic Blessing" of *Psalm 67*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Survivor." He heard me.
