Psalm 56

For the director of music. To the tune of “A Dove on Distant Oaks.” Of David. A miktam. When the Philistines had seized him in Gath.

The Hot Pursuit

“Be merciful to me, my God, for my enemies are in hot pursuit; all day long they press their attack.” 1

Trust when Afraid

“When I am afraid, I put my trust in you.” 2
“In God, whose word I praise—in God I trust and am not afraid. What can mere mortals do to me?” 3

The Bottle of Tears

“Record my misery; list my tears on your scroll—are they not in your record?” 4

The Light of Life

“For you have delivered me from death and my feet from stumbling, that I may walk before God in the light of life.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Hot Pursuit (Sha-a-phani): Literally "Has panted after me." The scrolls highlight the "Predatory Root." The enemy is like a beast "Snapping" at the heels. David is "Uprooted" from "Safety" in Gath. He appeals for "Mercy" (*Cho-ne-ni*).

2 When I am Afraid (Yom I-ra): Literally "The day I fear." The scrolls highlight the "Honesty of the Rooted." Faith is not the absence of fear, but the "Response" to it. To "Trust" (*Eb-tach*) is to "Lean hard" on the "Helper."

3 In God whose Word I Praise (be-Elohim A-hal-lel De-ba-ro): This is a "Unique Root." David "Roots" his "Confidence" specifically in God’s "Word" (*Dabar*). What "Mere Mortals" (*Basar* - literally 'flesh') can do is "Limited," but the "Word" is "Eternal."

4 Tears on your Scroll (Sim-ah Dim-a-thi be-No-dekha): Literally "Put my tears in your skin-bottle." The scrolls highlight the "Preciousness of Sorrow." God doesn't just "Ignore" our pain; He "Collects" it. It is the "Root of Divine Compassion." Your "Misery" (*No-di*) is "Recorded" (*Sa-phra*).

5 Light of Life (be-Or ha-Chay-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Vital Finality." Deliverance from "Death" (*Ma-weth*) and "Stumbling" (*De-chi*) is so that David can "Walk" (*Hith-hal-lekh*) in "God’s Light." It marks the transition to the "Refuge in the Cave" of *Psalm 57*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem. God is on my side.
