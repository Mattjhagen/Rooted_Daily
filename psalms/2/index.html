Psalm 2

The Vain Plotting

“Why do the nations conspire and the peoples plot in vain?” 1
“The kings of the earth rise up against the Lord and against his Anointed One.”

The Divine Laughter

“The One enthroned in heaven laughs; the Lord scoffs at them.” 2

The King on Zion

“I have installed my king on Zion, my holy mountain.”
“You are my son; today I have become your father.” 3

The Call to Submission

“Therefore, you kings, be wise; be warned, you rulers of the earth.”
“Kiss his son, or he will be angry and your way will lead to your destruction.” 4
“Blessed are all who take refuge in him.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Plot in Vain (Yeh-gu-Riq): In the scrolls, this is the "Ironic Root." The same word used for the "Righteous Man’s Meditation" in *Psalm 1:2* (*Yeh-geh*) is used here for the "Wicked Man’s Plotting." One "Roots" in the Word; the other "Roots" in "Vanity" (*Riq*). One "Leads to Fruit"; the other "Leads to Laughter."

2 Lord Scoffs (Adonai Yil-ag Lamo): The scrolls highlight the "Symmetry of Judgment." The "Mockers" of *Psalm 1* are now being "Mocked" by the "Enthroned One." It signifies the "Futility" of "Uprooting" God’s "Decree."

3 Today I have become your Father (Ani ha-Yom Yelid-tikha): This is the "Messianic Root." It refers to the "Coronation" or "Adoption" of the King. In the scrolls, this is the "Legal Authorization" for the Son to "Inherit the Nations." It marks the transition from "Zion" as a "Local Hill" to "Zion" as a "Global Throne."

4 Kiss the Son (Nash-qu Bar): Literally in the scrolls, "Kiss [with] Purity" or "Kiss the Son." It is a "Gesture of Homage." To "Refuse the Kiss" is to choose "Uprooting." It signifies the "Urgency of Reconciliation."

5 Take Refuge (Chose Bo): The scrolls conclude with this "Rooted Security." While the nations "Conspire" (*Verse 1*), the "Righteous" simply "Refuge" (*Chose*). It links back to the "Blessedness" of *Psalm 1*. The "Tree" doesn't need to "Fight" the kings; it just needs to "Remain" in the "Refuge" of the "Water."
