Psalm 113

The Name of the Lord

“Praise the Lord. Praise, O servants of the Lord, praise the name of the Lord.” 1
“From the rising of the sun to the place where it sets, the name of the Lord is to be praised.”

The God Who Stoops

“Who is like the Lord our God, the One who sits enthroned on high?” 2
“Who stoops down to look on the heavens and the earth?”

From the Ash Heap

“He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the ash heap.” 3
“He seats them with princes, with the princes of his people.”

A Happy Mother

“He settles the childless woman in her home as a happy mother of children.” 4
“Praise the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Servants of the Lord (Ab-de YHWH): In the scrolls, this is the "Commissioned Root." The "Servants" (*Abdim*) are called to "Praise" (*Hal-le-lu*) the "Name" (*Shem*). It is the "Root of the Hallel."

2 Enthroned on High (ham-Mag-bi-hi la-Sha-be-th): Literally "The one who makes high to sit." The scrolls highlight the "Paradoxical Root." God is "Exalted" (*High*) yet "Stoops" (*ham-Mash-pi-li*) to "See" (*Lir-oth*). It signifies "Infinite Condescension."

3 From the Ash Heap (me-Ash-poth Ya-rim Eb-yon): The scrolls highlight the "Vertical Redemption Root." God "Raises" (*Me-qi-mi*) the "Poor" (*Dal*) from the "Dust" (*Aphar*) and the "Ash heap" (*Ashpoth*). It signifies the "Uprooting of Lowliness."

4 Happy Mother (E-mem ha-Ba-nim Se-me-khah): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Domestic Transformation." The "Barren" (*A-qe-reth*) is "Rooted" in a "Family" (*Home*). It signify "Restored Identity."

5 Praise the Lord (Hal-le-lu-Yah): The scrolls conclude the Hallel’s opening with this "Doxological Seal." It marks the transition to the "Exodus Memories" in *Psalm 114*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Elevation." He lifts us up.
