Psalm 26

Of David.

The Blameless Walk

“Vindicate me, Lord, for I have led a blameless life; I have trusted in the Lord and have not faltered.” 1

The Heart Audit

“Test me, Lord, and try me, examine my heart and my mind.” 2
“For I have always been mindful of your unfailing love and have lived in reliance on your faithfulness.”

The Dwelling of Glory

“Lord, I love the house where you live, the place where your glory dwells.” 3
“I do not sit with the deceitful, nor do I associate with hypocrites.” 4
“My feet stand on level ground; in the great congregation I will praise the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Blameless Life (be-Thum-mi): Literally in the scrolls, "In my integrity/completeness." David is "Rooting" his "Identity" in "Consistent Character." He has "Not Faltered" (*lo Em-ad*) because his "Trust" (*Batach-ti*) is "Firmly Fixed."

2 Examine my Heart and my Mind (Tza-re-phah Khil-yo-thay we-Lib-bi): Literally in the scrolls, "Refine my kidneys and my heart." The "Kidneys" were seen as the "Root of Emotion." David asks for a "Divine Audit" to "Uproot" any "Hidden Impurity."

3 Place where your Glory Dwells (Me-qom Mish-kan Khe-bod-ekha): The scrolls highlight the "Spatial Root of Love." David is "Drawn" to the "Sanctuary." It is the "Root of his Fellowship." It marks the transition from "Private Integrity" to "Public Worship."

4 Hypocrites (Na-a-la-mim): Literally "The hidden ones" or "Those who conceal themselves." David is "Uprooting" himself from "Shallow Company." He refuses to "sit" (*Ya-shab-ti*) with those whose "Roots" are "False."

5 My Feet stand on Level Ground (Rag-li Am-dah be-Mi-shor): The scrolls conclude with this "Rooted Stability." "Level Ground" (*Mishor*) is a "Place of Safety" where one cannot "Slip." David has moved from the "Danger" of the "Deceitful" to the "Solid Floor" of "God’s House." It marks the "Confidence" of the "Integrity-walker." It is the "Rooted Daily" result of "Vindication."
