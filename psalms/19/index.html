Psalm 19

For the director of music. A psalm of David.

The Silent Declaration

“The heavens declare the glory of God; the skies proclaim the work of his hands.” 1
“They have no speech, they use no words; no sound is heard from them.”
“Yet their voice goes out into all the earth, their words to the ends of the world.” 2

The Flawless Word

“The law of the Lord is perfect, refreshing the soul. The statutes of the Lord are trustworthy, making wise the simple.” 3
“They are more precious than gold... they are sweeter than honey, than honey from the honeycomb.” 4

The Heart’s Meditation

“May these words of my mouth and this meditation of my heart be pleasing in your sight, Lord, my Rock and my Redeemer.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Declare the Glory (Me-sap-pherim Khabod-El): In the scrolls, "Declaring" (*Sap-pher*) is the "Continuous Root." The "Heavens" are an "Ongoing Narrative." God is "Rooted" in "Communication" through His "Creation." It is "General Revelation."

2 Their Voice (Qaw-wam): Literally in the scrolls, "Their measuring line." The "Geometry of the Universe" is its "Voice." It is the "Uprooting of Language Barriers." Nature "Speaks" to "Every Root" of the human race regardless of "Speech" (*Verse 3*).

3 Refreshing the Soul (Me-shi-bath Naphesh): The scrolls highlight the "Restorative Root" of the Word. To "Refresh" (*Me-shi-bath*) is to "Bring Back." The "Torah" is the "Water" that "Rooted Daily" users need to "Return" to "Life." It is "Special Revelation."

4 Sweeter than Honey (u-Methu-qim mi-Debash): This is the "Aesthetic Root" of the Word. It isn't just "Instruction," it is "Delight." It "Fills" the "Internal Taste" of the soul. It is "Uprooting" the "Boredom" of "Religion" and replacing it with "Relish."

5 My Rock and my Redeemer (Tzuri we-Go-ali): The scrolls conclude with this "Personal Root." David moves from "Cosmos" to "Commandment" to "Commitment." He asks that his "Meditation" (*Heg-yon*)—the same word as *Psalm 1:2*—be "Pleasing" (*Rat-zon*). He is "Rooting" his "Daily Speech" in the "Rock" (*Tzur*) who "Redeems" (*Go-al*) his "Failures" (*Verse 12*). It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem.
