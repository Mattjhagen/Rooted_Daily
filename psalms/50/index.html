Psalm 50

A psalm of Asaph.

The Mighty One Speaks

“The Mighty One, God, the Lord, speaks and summons the earth from the rising of the sun to where it sets.” 1
“Our God comes and will not be silent; a fire devours before him.”

Cattle on a Thousand Hills

“For every animal of the forest is mine, and the cattle on a thousand hills.” 2
“If I were hungry I would not tell you, for the world is mine, and all that is in it.” 3

The Thank Offering

“Sacrifice thank offerings to God, fulfill your vows to the Most High.” 4
“Call on me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you, and you will honor me.”
“But to the wicked God says: ‘What right have you to recite my laws or take my covenant on your lips?’” 5
“Those who sacrifice thank offerings honor me, and to the blameless I will show my salvation.”

Translation Notes

1 The Mighty One, God, the Lord (El Elohim YHWH): The scrolls use three titles for God in a row, the "Triple Root of Majesty." God is the "Speaker" (*Dib-ber*) who "Summons" (*Yiq-ra*) the entire "Creation" (*Eretz*). It is the "Uprooting of Human Silence." Zion is "Perfect in Beauty" (*Mik-lal Yo-phi*).

2 Cattle on a Thousand Hills (Be-he-moth be-Har-re A-leph): The scrolls highlight the "Universal Root of Ownership." God does not "Lack" anything. The animals of the "Forest" (*Ya-ar*) are His. It is the "Uprooting of the Transactional View of God."

3 If I were Hungry (Im-Er-ab): This is a "Divine Sarcasm" in the scrolls. God "Re-roots" the concept of sacrifice. It is not "Feeding God," but "Acknowledging God." The "World" (*Te-bel*) is His.

4 Sacrifice Thank Offerings (Ze-bach le-Elohim Tho-dah): Literally "Slaughter to God a thanksgiving." The scrolls highlight the "Internal Root of Worship." God wants the "Heart" (*Thodah*) rather than the "Blood" (*Verse 13*). It signifies the "Root of Covenant Loyalty."

5 What Right have You (Mah-lekha le-Sapher Chu-qay): The scrolls conclude with the "Rebuke of Hypocrisy." To "Recite Laws" while "Casting Words Behind" (*Verse 17*) is to be "Rootless" in truth. It marks the transition to the "Great Repentance" of *Psalm 51*. It is the "Rooted Daily" reminder that "Obedience" is the "Only Valid Offering."
