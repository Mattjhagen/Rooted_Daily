Psalm 94

The God who Avenges

“The Lord is a God who avenges; O God who avenges, shine forth.” 1
“Rise up, Judge of the earth; pay back to the proud what they deserve.”

He Who Formed the Eye

“They say, ‘The Lord does not see; the God of Jacob takes no notice.’”
“Does he who fashioned the ear not hear? Does he who formed the eye not see?” 2

Consolation in Anxiety

“When I said, ‘My foot is slipping,’ your unfailing love, Lord, supported me.” 3
“When anxiety was great within me, your consolation brought me joy.” 4
“But the Lord has become my fortress, and my God the rock in whom I take refuge.” 5

Translation Notes

1 God who Avenges (El Ne-qa-moth): In the scrolls, "Vengeance" (*Neqamah*) is the "Root of Righteous Restoration." It is not "Malice," but the "Uprooting of Injustice." God must "Shine Forth" (*Ho-phi-ah*).

2 He who Formed the Eye (Yo-tzer A-yin): The scrolls highlight the "Organic Root of Perception." If God "Planted" (*No-ta*) the "Ear" (*Ozen*), He must "Hear" (*Yish-ma*). It is the "Logical Argument for Omniscience."

3 Foot is Slipping (Ma-thah Rag-li): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Instability." When the "Step" is "Uprooted," God’s "Love" (*Chesed*) "Supports" (*Yis-a-de-ni*) the "Balance." It signifies "Spiritual Traction."

4 Anxiety was Great (be-Rob Sar-ap-pay): Literally "In the multitude of my disquieting thoughts." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Mental Turmoil." God’s "Consolations" (*Than-chu-me-kha*) "Delight" (*Ye-sha-a-shu*) the "Soul" (*Naphesh*). It is the "Uprooting of Panic."

5 Fortress and Rock (Mis-gab we-Tzur): The scrolls conclude with this "Stable Refuge Root." God is the "High Place" (*Misgab*). It marks the transition to the "Call to Joy" in *Psalm 95*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Justice and Peace." You see it all.
