Psalm 88

A song. A psalm of the Sons of Korah. For the director of music. According to mahalath leannoth. A maskil of Heman the Ezrahite.

Day and Night

“Lord, you are the God who saves me; day and night I cry out to you.” 1

The Lowest Pit

“You have put me in the lowest pit, in the darkest depths.” 2
“Your wrath lies heavily on me; you have overwhelmed me with all your waves.” Selah

Repulsive to Friends

“You have taken from me my closest friends and have made me repulsive to them.” 3

My Closest Friend

“From my youth I have suffered and been close to death; I have borne your terrors and am in despair.”
“You have taken from me friend and neighbor—darkness is my closest friend.” 4

Translation Notes

1 God who Saves Me (Elohe Ye-shu-a-thi): In the scrolls, this is the "Paradoxical Root." Even in the "Darkest Descent," Heman calls God "Savior." It is the "Root of Desperate Faith."

2 Lowest Pit (be-Bor Thakh-thi-yoth): The scrolls highlight the "Extreme of Uprooting." To be in the "Pit" (*Bor*) and the "Darkness" (*Ma-cha-shak-kim*) is to be "Uprooted from Light." It signifies the "Realm of the Dead" (*Re-pha-im* - *Verse 10*).

3 Repulsive (Tho-e-both): Literally "An abomination." The scrolls highlight the "Social Uprooting." The sufferer is "Shut in" (*Ka-lu*) and cannot escape (*Verse 8*). It is the "Root of Total Isolation."

4 Darkness is my Closest Friend (Ma-ch-shakh Me-yu-da-i): Literally "My acquaintance is darkness." The scrolls conclude with this "Chilling Root." There is no "Light" at the end of this tunnel. It is the "Honest End of a Broken Soul." It marks the transition to the "Great Love" of *Psalm 89*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Despair." Darkness is here.
