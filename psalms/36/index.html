Psalm 36

For the director of music. Of David the servant of the Lord.

The Oracle of Sin

“I have a message from God in my heart concerning the sinfulness of the wicked: There is no fear of God before their eyes.” 1
“Even on their beds they plot evil; they commit themselves to a sinful course.”

The Limitless Love

“Your love, Lord, reaches to the heavens, your faithfulness to the skies.” 2
“Your righteousness is like the highest mountains, your justice like the great deep.”

The Fountain of Life

“They feast on the abundance of your house; you give them drink from your river of delights.” 3
“For with you is the fountain of life; in your light we see light.” 4
“Continue your love to those who know you, your righteousness to the upright in heart.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Oracle of Sin (Ne-um Pe-sha): Literally in the scrolls, "The whisper of transgression." In the heart of the wicked, sin has its own "Revelation." It is the "Uprooting of Divine Authority." They are "Self-Flatterers" (*Verse 2*) who have "No Fear" (*En Pachad*).

2 Your Love reaches to the Heavens (be-Sha-ma-yim Chas-dekha): The scrolls highlight the "Scale of God’s Character." While the plots of the wicked are "Confined to Beds" (*Verse 4*), God’s "Love" (*Chesed*) is "Infinite." It is the "Ultimate Root of Reality."

3 River of Delights (Na-chal Ada-ne-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Satisfaction" of the believer. The word for "Delights" (*Adanim*) is related to the word "Eden." To drink from this river is to "Return to the Garden Root." It is the "Uprooting of Lack."

4 Fountain of Life (Me-qor Chay-yim): This is the "Vital Root." God is the "Spring" from which all "Existence" flows. In "His Light" (*be-Orekha*), we see "Light" (*Ne-re Or*). It means that "Truth" is only "Visible" when "Rooted" in "God."

5 Continue your Love (Me-shokh Chas-dekha): Literally "Draw out/Extend your love." The scrolls conclude with this "Petition for Continuity." David asks that the "Fountain" keep "Flowing" to the "Upright" (*Ye-sha-re-Leb*). It marks the transition to the "Wisdom Instruction" of *Psalm 37*. It is the "Rooted Daily" source of "Clarity."
