Psalm 18

For the director of music. Of David the servant of the Lord. He sang to the Lord the words of this song when the Lord delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. He said:

The Rock and the Fortress

“I love you, Lord, my strength.”
“The Lord is my rock, my fortress and my deliverer; my God is my rock, in whom I take refuge.” 1

The Ride of the Almighty

“He parted the heavens and came down; dark clouds were under his feet.” 2
“He rode on a cherub and flew; he soared on the wings of the wind.” 3
“The Lord thundered from heaven; the voice of the Most High resounded.”

Drawn from the Depths

“He reached down from on high and took hold of me; he drew me out of deep waters.” 4
“He brought me out into a spacious place; he rescued me because he delighted in me.”

The Training of the Anointed

“It is God who arms me with strength and makes my way perfect.”
“He trains my hands for battle; my arms can bend a bow of bronze.” 5

Translation Notes

1 The Lord is my Rock (YHWH Sal-i): In the scrolls, this is the "Uprooting of Instability." David uses eight different titles for God to "Root" his "Deliverance." The "Rock" (*Sela*) and "Fortress" (*Metzu-da-thi*) are "Static Roots of Protection." It is the "Final Result" of "Refuge-taking."

2 Parted the Heavens (Way-Yeth Sha-ma-yim): Literally "He bent/inclined the heavens." The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Intervention." God "Uproots" the "Distance" between heaven and earth to "Rescue" one man. It is the "Root of the Theophany."

3 Rode on a Cherub (Way-Yir-khab al-Kerub): This is a "Majestic Anthropomorphism" in the scrolls. A "Cherub" is a "Celestial Throne-Bearer." God is "Rooted" in "Sovereign Motion." The "Wings of the Wind" (*Kan-phe Ruach*) signify "Surpassing Speed."

4 Drew me out of Deep Waters (Yam-sheni mi-Ma-yim Rab-bim): The scrolls highlight the "Personal Rescue." *Ma-yim Rab-bim* ("Many Waters") refers to the "Abyss of Chaos." God "Uproots" David from "Endless Ruin" (*Verse 18*). It is the "Root of Resurrection."

5 Bow of Bronze (Qe-sheth Ne-chu-shah): The scrolls conclude with this "Image of Empowerment." God "Roots" His servant in "Supernatural Strength." To "Bend a Bronze Bow" was a "Humanly Impossible Task." It signifies that the "Anointed One" (*Ma-shi-ach*) is "Equipped" by "Divine Energy." It marks the transition from "Victim" to "Victor." David is "Rooted" in the "Unfailing Love" (*Chesed*) that continues to his "Descendants Forever" (*Verse 50*).
