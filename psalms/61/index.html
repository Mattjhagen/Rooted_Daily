Psalm 61

For the director of music. With stringed instruments. Of David.

The Faint Heart

“Hear my cry, O God; listen to my prayer.”
“From the ends of the earth I call to you, I call as my heart grows faint.” 1

The Higher Rock

“Lead me to the rock that is higher than I.” 2
“For you have been my refuge, a strong tower against the foe.”

Shelter of Wings

“I long to dwell in your tent forever and take refuge in the shelter of your wings.” 3 Selah

The Heritage

“For you, God, have heard my vows; you have given me the heritage of those who fear your name.” 4
“Then I will ever sing in praise of your name and fulfill my vows day after day.” 5

Translation Notes

1 From the Ends of the Earth (mi-Qe-tzah ha-Aretz): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Exile." David is "Uprooted" from the "Sanctuary." His "Heart" (*Lib-bi*) is "Faint" (*A-thoph* - literally 'shrouded/covered'). It is the "Cry of the Marginalized."

2 Lead me to the Rock (be-Tzur Ya-rum Mim-men-ni): Literally "In a rock that is high above me, lead me." The scrolls highlight the "Need for Elevation." David is "Rooting" his "Safety" in a "Divine Ascent." He cannot reach the "Rock" (*Tzur*) on his own. It is the "Uprooting of Self-Sufficiency."

3 Shelter of your Wings (be-Se-ther Ke-na-phe-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Secret Root." The "Shelter" (*Sether*) is the "Hidden Place." David longs for the "Tabernacle" (*Ohel*). It signifies "Permanent Presence."

4 Heritage (Me-u-ra-shah): The scrolls highlight the "Spiritual Legacy." David is "Rooted" in the "Inheritance" of the "Faithful" (*Yi-re She-me-kha*). It is the "Uprooting of the Fatherless Identity."

5 Fulfill my Vows day after day (le-Sha-le-mi Ne-da-ray Yom Yom): The scrolls conclude with this "Continuity Root." Praise is not a "Moment," but a "Daily Habit." It marks the transition to the "Resting Soul" of *Psalm 62*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem. Lead me higher.
