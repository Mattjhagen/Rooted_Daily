Psalm 143

Of David.

Not into Judgment

“Lord, hear my prayer, listen to my cry for mercy.”
“Do not bring your servant into judgment, for no one living is righteous before you.” 1

Like a Parched Land

“I spread out my hands to you; I thirst for you like a parched land.” 2

Morning Word

“Let the morning bring me word of your unfailing love, for I have put my trust in you.” 3
“Show me the way I should go, for to you I entrust my life.”

Level Ground

“Teach me to do your will, for you are my God.” 4
“May your good Spirit lead me on level ground.” 5

Translation Notes

1 No one Living is Righteous (lo-Yitz-daq le-Pha-ne-kha kol-Chay): In the scrolls, this is the "Universal Inadequacy Root." The pilgrim acknowledges that "Righteousness" (*Tzedaq*) cannot be "Rooted" in "Human Performance." It is the "Root of Grace."

2 Like a Parched Land (ke-Eretz ay-ye-phah): Literally "As a weary/fainting land." The scrolls highlight the "Hydration Root of the Soul." The "Thirst" (*Naph-shi*) for God is an "Existential Necessity." It signifies "Vital Dependence."

3 Morning... Unfailing Love (bab-Bo-qer Chas-de-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Temporal Hope." The "Morning" (*Boqer*) is the "Symbol of Deliverance." God’s "Chesed" is the "First Word" of the day. It is the "Uprooting of Night-Terror."

4 Teach me to do your Will (Lam-me-de-ni la-a-soth Re-tzo-ne-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Educational Root of Obedience." To "Do" (*Asoth*) the "Will" (*Rat-zon*) is the "True Fruit" of being "Rooted" in God. It signify "Active Faith."

5 Level Ground (be-Eretz Mi-shor): Literally "In a land of uprightness/plain." The scrolls conclude with this "Topographical Root of Stability." The "Good Spirit" (*Ru-cha-kha To-bah*) "Uproots" the soul from "Rocky Paths" to "Even/Fair Ground." It marks the transition to the "Rock of War" in *Psalm 144*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Morning Guidance." Lead me, Lord.
