Psalm 139

Of David.

You Have Searched Me

“Lord, you have searched me and you know me.” 1
“You know when I sit and when I rise; you perceive my thoughts from afar.”

Where Can I Go?

“Where can I go from your Spirit? Where can I flee from your presence?” 2
“If I go up to the heavens, you are there; if I make my bed in the depths, you are there.”

Fearfully and Wonderfully Made

“For you created my inmost being; you knit me together in my mother’s womb.” 3
“I am fearfully and wonderfully made; your works are wonderful.” 4
“All the days ordained for me were written in your book before one of them came to be.”

The Way Everlasting

“Search me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts.”
“See if there is any offensive way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Searched me (Cha-qar-ta-ni): In the scrolls, "Chaqar" refers to the "Deepest Investigation." To be "Searched" and "Known" (*Ya-da-ta*) is the "Root of Divine Omniscience." It is the "Uprooting of Secrecy."

2 Spirit... Presence (Ru-cha-kha... Pa-ne-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Spatial Root of God." "Spirit" (*Ruach*) and "Face" (*Panim*) fill the "Cosmos." From "Heavens" (*Shamayim*) to "Sheol" (*Depths*), there is no "Escape." It signifies "Absolute Proximity."

3 Knit me Together (te-Suk-ke-ni): Literally "You wove/covered me." The scrolls highlight the "Biological Root of Design." The "Womb" (*Beten*) is a "Secret Place" (*Se-ther*) where God’s "Hand" is the "Loom." It signify "Intimate Intentionality."

4 Fearfully and Wonderfully Made (No-ra-oth Niph-le-thi): Literally "I am awesome/terrifyingly distinguished." The scrolls highlight the "Dread-filled Beauty Root." Human life is a "Miracle" (*Niph-le-thi*) that should "Uproot" all low self-estimation. It is the "Root of Individual Worth."

5 Way Everlasting (be-De-rekh O-lam): The scrolls conclude with this "Foundational Root of Destiny." To be led in the "Everlasting Way" is to be "Rooted" in the "Final Truth." It marks the transition to the "Plea for Protection" in *Psalm 140*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The All-Known Soul." You know me completely.
