Psalm 135

Servants of the Lord

“Praise the Lord. Praise the name of the Lord; praise him, you servants of the Lord.” 1
“Praise the Lord, for the Lord is good; sing praise to his name, for that is pleasant.”

Wind from the Storehouses

“The Lord does whatever pleases him, in the heavens and on the earth.” 2
“He makes clouds rise from the ends of the earth; he sends lightning with the rain and brings out the wind from his storehouses.” 3

Mighty Kings

“He struck down many nations and killed mighty kings.” 4
“Sihon king of the Amorites, Og king of Bashan.”
“Praise the Lord from Zion, for he dwells in Jerusalem. Praise the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Praise the Lord (Hal-le-lu-Yah): In the scrolls, this is the "Imperative Root of Global Praise." It mirrors the opening of *Psalm 113* and *Psalm 134*. It is the "Root of Universal Service."

2 Does whatever Pleases Him (Kol a-sher Cha-phetz A-sah): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Root." God’s "Will" (*Chaphetz*) is the "Engine of Creation" (*Asah*). It signify "Absolute Freedom."

3 Wind from Storehouses (Ru-ach may-Ot-ze-ro-thaw): The scrolls highlight the "Meteorological Root." God "Roots" the "Weather" in His "Ot-zer" (*Treasury/Storehouse*). It signifies "Divine Control over the Invisible."

4 Mighty Kings (Me-la-khim At-zu-mim): The scrolls highlight the "Historical Root of Victory." Og and Sihon were "Giants" (*Anakim*). Their "Uprooting" (*Verse 12*) provided the "Heritage" (*Na-cha-lah*) for Israel. It signifies "The God who Acts."

5 Dwells in Jerusalem (Sho-khen Ye-ru-sha-la-im): The scrolls conclude with this "Inhabiting Root." The "Transcendent God" who controls lightning also "Resides/Tabernacles" (*Shokhen*) in the "City of Peace." It marks the transition to the "Refrain of Love" in *Psalm 136*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Presence." Praise the Lord.
