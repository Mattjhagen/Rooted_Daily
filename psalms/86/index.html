Psalm 86

A prayer of David.

Poor and Needy

“Hear me, Lord, and answer me, for I am poor and needy.” 1
“Guard my life, for I am faithful to you; save your servant who trusts in you.”

None Like You

“Among the gods there is none like you, Lord; no deeds can compare with yours.” 2

The Undivided Heart

“Teach me your way, Lord, that I may rely on your faithfulness; give me an undivided heart.” 3

Abounding in Love

“But you, Lord, are a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, abounding in love.” 4
“Give me a sign of your goodness, that my enemies may see it and be put to shame.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Poor and Needy (A-ni we-Eb-yon): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Spiritual Poverty." David, though a king, identifies as "Rooted" in "Need." It is the "Uprooting of Self-Sufficiency."

2 None like You (En-Ka-mo-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Absolute Root of Uniqueness." Among the "gods" (*Elim*), YHWH stands alone. It signifies "Monotheistic Integrity."

3 Undivided Heart (Ya-ched Le-ba-bi): Literally "Unite my heart." The scrolls highlight the "Integrative Root." A "Scattered Heart" is an "Uprooted Heart." To "Fear your Name" (*le-Yir-ah She-me-kha*) requires a "Single Focus." It is the "Root of Wholeness."

4 Compassionate and Gracious (El-Ra-chum we-Chan-nun): This is the "Exodus 34:6 Root." The scrolls highlight the "Revealed Character" of God. He is "Slow to Anger" (*E-rekh Ap-pay-im*). It signifies "Covenantal Patience."

5 Sign of Goodness (Oth le-To-bah): The scrolls conclude with this "Visible Root of Comfort." David asks for an "Oth" (*Sign*) so that "Comfort" (*Ni-cham-tha-ni*) is "Rooted" in reality. It marks the transition to the "Holy Mountain" of *Psalm 87*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Needy Soul." You are my God.
