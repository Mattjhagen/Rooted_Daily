Psalm 85

For the director of music. Of the Sons of Korah. A psalm.

Restored Fortunes

“You, Lord, showed favor to your land; you restored the fortunes of Jacob.” 1
“You forgave the iniquity of your people and covered all their sins.” Selah

Revive Us Again

“Restore us again, God our Savior, and put away your displeasure toward us.”
“Will you not revive us again, that your people may rejoice in you?” 2
“I will listen to what God the Lord says; he promises peace to his people.” 3

The Divine Meeting

“Love and faithfulness meet together; righteousness and peace kiss each other.” 4
“Faithfulness springs forth from the earth, and righteousness looks down from heaven.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Showed Favor (Ra-tzi-tha): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Acceptance." God’s "Favor" (*Ratzon*) leads to the "Return" (*She-buth*) of Jacob. It is the "Uprooting of Exile."

2 Revive us Again (Tha-shub The-cha-ye-nu): Literally "Will you not return and cause us to live?" The scrolls highlight the "Vitality Root." Revival (*Cha-yah*) leads to "Rejoicing" (*Sim-khah*). It is the "Uprooting of Spiritual Lethargy."

3 I will Listen (Esh-me-ah): The scrolls highlight the "Receptive Root." Peace (*Sha-lom*) is the "Promise" (*Da-bar* - word). It signifies "Alignment with the Divine Voice."

4 Meet... Kiss (Niph-ga-shu... Na-sha-qu): This is the "Relational Root of Theology." "Chesed" (*Love*) and "Emeth" (*Faithfulness*) "Collide" in grace. "Tzedeq" (*Righteousness*) and "Shalom" (*Peace*) "Kiss" (*Nashqu*). It signifies the "Symphony of Divine Attributes."

5 Springs Forth (Thetz-mach): The scrolls conclude with this "Organic Root of Truth." Truth (*Emeth*) grows from "Below" (*Earth*) while Righteousness (*Tzedeq*) looks from "Above" (*Heaven*). It marks the transition to the "Personal Cry" of *Psalm 86*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Revival." Love has met us.
