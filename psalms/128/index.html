Psalm 128

A song of ascents.

Walking in Obedience

“Blessed are all who fear the Lord, who walk in obedience to him.” 1
“You will eat the fruit of your labor; blessings and prosperity will be yours.”

Vine and Olive Shoots

“Your wife will be like a fruitful vine within your house.” 2
“Your children will be like olive shoots around your table.” 3

Blessing from Zion

“May the Lord bless you from Zion; may you see the prosperity of Jerusalem.” 4
“Peace be on Israel.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Fear the Lord (Ye-re YHWH): In the scrolls, this is the "Prosperity Root." The "Walk" (*ha-Ho-lekh*) in "Obedience" is the "Uprooting of Futility." The "Fruit of Labor" (*Ye-gi-a*) is "Blessed" (*Ash-re-kha*). It signifies "Organic Success."

2 Fruitful Vine (Ge-phen Po-ri-yah): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Domestic Vitality." The "Vine" (*Gephen*) signifies "Abundance" and "Joy" within the "Inner Chambers" (*be-Yar-ke-the*). It is the "Uprooting of Sterility."

3 Olive Shoots (She-thi-le Ze-thi-m): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Potential." "Olive Shoots" (*Shethilim*) signify "Longevity" and "Future Oil/Light." They surround the "Table" (*Shul-cha-ne-kha*). It signify "Generational Continuity."

4 Prosperity of Jerusalem (Tu-b Ye-ru-sha-la-im): The scrolls highlight the "Communal Root." The "Individual Blessing" is "Rooted" in the "Welfare" (*Tub/Good*) of the "City." It signifies "Integrated Flourishing."

5 Peace be on Israel (Sha-lom al-Yis-ra-el): The scrolls conclude with the same "Ascent Seal" as *Psalm 125*. It marks the transition to the "Ploughed Back" in *Psalm 129*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Family Peace." Blessings to you.
