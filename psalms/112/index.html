Psalm 112

The Delight in Commands

“Praise the Lord. Blessed are those who fear the Lord, who find great delight in his commands.” 1

Light Dawns in Darkness

“Even in darkness light dawns for the upright.” 2
“For those who are gracious and compassionate and righteous.”

No Fear of Bad News

“They will have no fear of bad news; their hearts are steadfast, trusting in the Lord.” 3
“Their hearts are secure, they will have no fear.” 4

Righteousness Endures

“They have scattered abroad their gifts to the poor, their righteousness endures forever.” 5
“The wicked will see and be vexed... their longings will come to nothing.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Blessed... Delight (Ash-re... Cha-phetz Me-od): In the scrolls, this is the "Acrostic Root of Character." This psalm mirrors *Psalm 111*. To "Fear" (*Yare*) and "Delight" (*Chaphetz*) in God’s "Commands" (*Mitz-vo-thaw*) is the "Stable Root of Happiness." It is the "Uprooting of Secular Anxiety."

2 Light Dawns (Za-rach be-Cho-shekh Or): The scrolls highlight the "Illuminated Root." Even in "Darkness" (*Cho-shekh*), the "Light" (*Or*) "Rises/Dawns" (*Za-rach*). It signifies "Supernatural Clarity."

3 No Fear of Bad News (mi-She-mu-ah Ra-ah lo Yi-ra): The scrolls highlight the "Unshakable Root." The heart is "Steadfast" (*Na-khon*) and "Trusting" (*Ba-tu-ach*) in YHWH. It is the "Uprooting of Reactive Panic."

4 Secure Heart (Sa-mukh Lib-bo): Literally "Supported heart." The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of Courage." The heart is "Braced" (*Samukh*). It signifies "Emotional Resilience."

5 Scattered Gifts (Piz-zar Na-than la-Eb-yo-nim): The scrolls highlight the "Generous Root." "Scattering" (*Pizzar*) is the "Seed" that "Roots" "Righteousness" (*Tze-da-qah*) "Forever" (*la-Ad*). It signifies "Impactful Altruism."

6 Wicked... come to Nothing (Ta-a-bath Re-sha-im To-bed): The scrolls conclude with the "Futile Root of Evil." While the righteous "Endure," the "Desire" (*Ta-a-bath*) of the "Wicked" (*Re-sha-im*) "Perishes" (*To-bed*). It marks the transition to the "High Praise" in *Psalm 113*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Invincibility." The light dawns.
