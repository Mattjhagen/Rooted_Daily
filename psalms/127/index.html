Psalm 127

A song of ascents. Of Solomon.

Unless the Lord Builds

“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labor in vain.” 1
“Unless the Lord watches over the city, the guards stand watch in vain.”

Sleep for the Beloved

“In vain you rise early and stay up late, toiling for food to eat.”
“For he grants sleep to those he loves.” 2

Arrows in the Hand

“Children are a heritage from the Lord, offspring a reward from him.” 3
“Like arrows in the hands of a warrior are children born in one’s youth.”
“Blessed is the man whose quiver is full of them.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Unless the Lord Builds (Im YHWH lo-Yib-neh): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Agency." Human "Labor" (*Amal*) is "Empty/Vain" (*Shav*) if it is "Uprooted" from the "Divine Blueprint." It signifies "Spiritual Architecture."

2 Sleep for the Beloved (She-na li-Di-do): The scrolls highlight the "Restorative Root." While the world "Toils" (*Etzeb*), the "Beloved" (*Yedid*) is "Rooted" in "Trusting Sleep." It is the "Uprooting of Anxiety."

3 Heritage... Reward (Na-cha-lath... Sa-khar): The scrolls highlight the "Generational Root." Children are not a "Burden," but a "Legacy" (*Heritage*) and a "Prize" (*Reward*). It signify "Organic Continuity."

4 Quiver is Full (Ash-pa-tho): The scrolls conclude with this "Strategic Root." Children are "Arrows" (*Chiz-zim*) that "Uproot" the "Opponents" (*O-ye-bim*) in the "Gate." It marks the transition to the "Blessed Family" in *Psalm 128*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Household Blessing." Build with Him.
