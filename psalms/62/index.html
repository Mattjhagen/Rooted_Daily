Psalm 62

For the director of music. For Jeduthun. A psalm of David.

Rest in God Alone

“Truly my soul finds rest in God; my salvation comes from him.” 1
“Truly he is my rock and my salvation; he is my fortress, I will never be shaken.”
“How long will you assault me? Would all of you throw me down—this leaning wall, this tottering fence?” 2

Pour Out Your Hearts

“Trust in him at all times, you people; pour out your hearts to him, for God is our refuge.” 3 Selah

Power and Love

“One thing God has spoken, two things I have heard: ‘Power belongs to you, God.’” 4
“And with you, Lord, is unfailing love.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Truly my Soul finds Rest (Ak el-Elohim Du-mi-yah Naphe-shi): Literally "Only unto God is my soul silence." The scrolls use the word "Ak" (*Truly/Only/Alone*) six times in this psalm. It is the "Root of Exclusive Trust." The "Rest" (*Dumiyah*) is the "Silence of Surrender." It is the "Uprooting of Internal Noise."

2 Leaning Wall (Qir Na-tuy): The scrolls highlight the "Vulnerability of the Human Root." Without God, David is a "Tottering Fence" (*Gader ha-De-chu-yah*). The enemies try to "Uproot" what is already "Leaning."

3 Pour out your Hearts (Shiph-khu le-Pha-naw Lebab-khem): Literally "Spill out your hearts before Him." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Emotional Honesty." "Rootedness" doesn't mean "Hiding pain," but "Pouring it into the Refuge" (*Mach-seh*).

4 Power belongs to You (Az le-Elohim): The scrolls highlight the "Divine Strength." God’s "Sovereignty" is the "Foundational Root." It is the "Uprooting of Human Self-Importance" (*Verse 9*).

5 With You, Lord, is Unfailing Love (u-lekha Adonai Cha-sed): This is the "Stunning Paradox of the Scrolls." God is "Power" (*Az*) AND "Love" (*Chesed*). This "Dual Root" is the "Final Anchor." It marks the transition to the "Thirst" of *Psalm 63*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Silence." He is enough.
