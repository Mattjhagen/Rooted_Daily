Psalm 136

“Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good.”
“His love endures forever.” 1
“Give thanks to the God of gods.”
“His love endures forever.”
“To him who alone does great wonders.”
“His love endures forever.” 2
“Who by his understanding made the heavens.”
“His love endures forever.”
“Who spread out the earth upon the waters.”
“His love endures forever.”
“To him who struck down the firstborn of Egypt.”
“His love endures forever.”
“And brought Israel out from among them.”
“His love endures forever.”
“With a mighty hand and outstretched arm.”
“His love endures forever.” 3
“To him who divided the Red Sea asunder.”
“His love endures forever.”
“He remembered us in our low estate.”
“His love endures forever.” 4
“He gives food to every creature.”
“His love endures forever.”
“Give thanks to the God of heaven.”
“His love endures forever.” 5

Translation Notes

1 His Love Endures Forever (ki le-Olam Chas-do): In the scrolls, this is the "Great Refrain Root." 26 times the people chant the "Endurance" of "Chesed." It is the "Root of Unbreakable Fidelity."

2 Alone does Great Wonders (O-seh Niph-la-oth Ge-do-loth le-Bad-do): The scrolls highlight the "Exclusive Agency Root." God’s "Wonders" (*Niph-la-oth*) are "Rooted" in His "Solitary Power" (*le-Bad-do*). It signifies "The Only Source."

3 Mighty Hand... Outstretched Arm (be-Yad Cha-za-qah u-bi-Zro-a ne-tu-yah): The scrolls highlight the "Anthropomorphic Root of Power." The "Exodus" was a "Physical Act" of "Strength" (*Cha-za-qah*). It is the "Uprooting of Slavery."

4 Our Low Estate (be-Shiph-le-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Humble Root." God "Remembers" (*Za-khar*) us when we are "Low/Crushed" (*Shiph-le-nu*). It signifies "Compassionate Memory."

5 God of Heaven (El ha-Sha-ma-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Transcendent Root." The One who provides "Food" (*Le-chem*) to "All Flesh" (*Kol Ba-sar*) is the "Celestial Master." It marks the transition to the "Lament of Exile" in *Psalm 137*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Enduring Love." His love remains.
