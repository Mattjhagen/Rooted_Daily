Psalm 17

A prayer of David.

The Just Plea

“Hear me, Lord, my plea is just; listen to my cry.” 1
“Though you probe my heart and examine me at night... you will find that I have planned no evil.”

The Apple of the Eye

“Keep me as the apple of your eye; hide me in the shadow of your wings.” 2
“From the wicked who are out to destroy me, from my mortal enemies who surround me.”

The Reward of this Life

“By your hand save me from such people, Lord, from those of this world whose reward is in this life.” 3

The Likeness of God

“As for me, I will be vindicated and will see your face; when I awake, I will be satisfied with seeing your likeness.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Plea is Just (Tzedeq): In the scrolls, "Justice" is the "Root of David’s Appeal." He is "Rooting" his "Survival" in "Divine Fairness." He invites God to "Probe" (*Ba-chan-ta*) his "Heart" (*Leb*) at "Night" (*Laylah*). It is the "Rooted Daily" standard of "Audit."

2 Apple of the Eye (k-Ishon Bath-Ayin): Literally in the scrolls, "As the little-man/pupil of the eye-daughter." It is the "Most Sensitive Root of Protection." David asks for the "Highest Degree" of "Care." To be "Hidden in the Shadow of Wings" (*be-Tzel Knaph-ekha*) is the "Final Uprooting of Fear."

3 Reward is in this Life (Chel-qam ba-Chay-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Materialism." The "Men of this World" (*me-Methim Me-cheled*) are "Satisfied" (*Yis-be-u*) with "Food" and "Inheritance" (*Verse 14*). David is "Uprooted" from this "Shallow Satisfaction."

4 Satisfied with seeing Your Likeness (Es-be-ah be-Heqitz Thmuna-thekha): The scrolls conclude with the "Ultimate Satisfaction." David’s "Root" is "Deepened" in the "Vision of God." While others are "Satisfied" with "Things," David is "Satisfied" (*Es-be-ah*) with "Presence." To "Awake" (*Heqitz*) refers to the "Morning of Resurrection" or the "Morning of Deliverance." It marks the transition from "Surrounding Enemies" to "Surrounding Glory."
