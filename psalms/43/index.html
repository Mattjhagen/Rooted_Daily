Psalm 43

The Plea for Justice

“Vindicate me, my God, and plead my cause against an unfaithful nation.” 1

Light and Faithful Care

“Send me your light and your faithful care, let them guide me; let them bring me to your holy mountain, to the place where you dwell.” 2

The Altar of Delight

“Then I will go to the altar of God, to God, my joy and my delight.” 3
“I will praise you with the lyre, O God, my God.”

The Downcast Soul

“Why, my soul, are you downcast? Why so disturbed within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise him, my Savior and my God.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Vindicate me (Shophthe-ni): In the scrolls, this is the "Legal Root of Book II." David asks for "Judgment" against the "Unfaithful" (*lo-Chasid*). It is the "Uprooting of False Accusations."

2 Light and Faithful Care (Orekha wa-Emitthekha): Literally "Your light and your truth." The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Guidance." God’s "Character" acts as a "Beacon" to "Re-root" the "Exiled Soul" in the "Holy Mountain" (*Har-Qodshekha*). It is the "Vertical Path."

3 God, my Joy and my Delight (El Sim-chath Gili): Literally "God, the joy of my gladness." The scrolls highlight the "Superlative Satisfaction." To go to the "Altar" (*Mizbe-ach*) is to "Return to the Sacrificial Root of Presence."

4 Refrain: This repeats from *Psalm 42:5* and *42:11*. It serves as the "Thematic Root" of the entire sequence. "Savior" (*Yeshu-oth*) and "God" (*Elo-hay*) are the "Final Anchor." It marks the transition to the "Historical Lament" of *Psalm 44*. It is the "Rooted Daily" reminder to "Dialogue with the Soul." Hope is coming. Praise is coming.
