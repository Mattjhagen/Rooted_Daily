Psalm 110

Of David. A psalm.

Sit at My Right Hand

“The Lord says to my lord: ‘Sit at my right hand until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet.’” 1
“The Lord will extend your mighty scepter from Zion.” 2

Priest Forever

“The Lord has sworn and will not change his mind: ‘You are a priest forever, in the order of Melchizedek.’” 3

Dew of Your Youth

“Your troops will be willing on your day of battle.” 4
“Arrayed in holy splendor, your young men will come to you like dew from the morning’s womb.”
“He will drink from a brook by the way, and so he will lift up his head.” 5

Translation Notes

1 The Lord says to my Lord (Ne-um YHWH la-Do-ni): In the scrolls, "Neum" is a "Prophetic Oracle." This is the "Messianic Root." The first "Lord" is YHWH; the second is *Adoni* (the King). "Right Hand" (*Yamin*) signifies "Ultimate Authority." It is the "Root of the Cosmic Throne."

2 Mighty Scepter (Mat-teh Uz-ze-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Extended Power Root." God’s "Strength" (*Uz*) goes out from "Zion." It signify "Victory in the Midst of Enemies" (*be-Qe-reb O-ye-be-kha*).

3 Order of Melchizedek (al-Dib-ra-thi Mal-ki-Tze-deq): The scrolls highlight the "Eternal Priesthood Root." Melchizedek ("King of Righteousness") was the Priest-King of Salem (Genesis 14). This "Oath" (*Nish-ba*) "Roots" the King in a "Timeless Order" (*Forever*). It is the "Uprooting of the Levitical Limitation."

4 Dew from the Womb (me-Re-chem Mish-char): The scrolls highlight the "Vitality Root." The "Troops" (*Am-me-kha*) are "Willing" (*Ne-da-both*). They are as "Fresh" as "Dew" (*Tal*) from the "Womb of Dawn" (*Mishchar*). It signifies "Supernatural Reinforcement."

5 Lift up His Head (Ya-rim Rosh): The scrolls conclude with this "Refreshment Root." The King "Drinks" (*Yish-the*) and is "Restored." It marks the transition to the "Acrostic Praise" in *Psalm 111*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Exalted Messiah." He reigns forever.
