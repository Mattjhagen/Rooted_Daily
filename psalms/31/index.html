Psalm 31

For the director of music. A psalm of David.

The Committal

“In you, Lord, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame.” 1
“Into your hands I commit my spirit; deliver me, Lord, my faithful God.” 2

Contempt and Dread

“Be merciful to me, Lord, for I am in distress; my eyes grow weak with sorrow.”
“Because of all my enemies, I am the utter contempt of my neighbors and an object of dread to my closest friends.” 3

My Times in Your Hands

“But I trust in you, Lord; I say, ‘You are my God.’”
“My times are in your hands.” 4

The Hidden Goodness

“How abundant are the good things that you have stored up for those who fear you!” 5
“In the shelter of your presence you hide them from all human intrigues.”
“Be strong and take heart, all you who hope in the Lord.”

Translation Notes

1 Taken Refuge (Chassi-thi): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Deliverance." David is "Rooting" his "Safety" in "Divine Righteousness" (*Tzedaqath-kha*). It is the "Uprooting of Self-Reliance."

2 Into your Hands I Commit my Spirit (be-Yad-kha Aph-qid Ru-chi): The scrolls highlight the "Final Act of Trust." To "Commit" (*Aph-qid*) is to "Deposit for Safe-keeping." The "Spirit" (*Ruach*) is the "Life-Force." David is "Rooting" his "Eternal Identity" in the "Faithful God" (*El Emeth*).

3 Object of Dread (Pa-chad): Literally "A fear." The scrolls highlight the "Social Uprooting." When a person is in crisis, friends often "Flee" (*Verse 11*). David is "Rootless" on "Earth," but "Rooted" in "God."

4 My Times are in Your Hands (be-Yad-kha Ith-thothay): This is the "Root of Sovereignty." "Times" (*Ith-thoth*) refers to the "Seasons of Life." David is "Resigning" his "Timeline" to "God’s Hand." It is the "Uprooting of Control."

5 Stored up (Tza-phan-tha): Literally in the scrolls, "Hidden/Treasured." God has a "Hidden Root of Goodness" (*Tob*) that is not visible to the "Enemies." It is "Abundant" (*Rab*). He "Hides" (*Tha-sith-rem*) his people in the "Shelter of His Presence" (*be-Sether Pha-ne-kha*). It marks the "Confidence" of the "Hope-bearer." It is the "Rooted Daily" secret of "Strength."
