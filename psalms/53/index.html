Psalm 53

For the director of music. According to mahalath. A maskil of David.

The Heart’s Denial

“The fool says in his heart, ‘There is no God.’” 1
“They are corrupt, and their ways are vile; there is no one who does good.”

The Search from Heaven

“God looks down from heaven on all mankind to see if there are any who understand, any who seek God.” 2
“Everyone has turned away, all have become corrupt; there is no one who does good, not even one.” 3

Overwhelmed with Dread

“But there they are, overwhelmed with dread, where there was nothing to dread.” 4

Salvation from Zion

“Oh, that salvation for Israel would come out of Zion! When God restores his people, let Jacob rejoice and Israel be glad!” 5

Translation Notes

1 The Fool (Na-bal): In the scrolls, "Folly" (*Na-bal*) is the "Moral Uprooting." It is not a lack of "IQ," but a lack of "Fear of God." The "Heart" (*Lib-bo*) is the "Center of the Denial." It is the "Uprooting of Divine Presence."

2 God looks down (Elohim me-Sha-ma-yim Hish-qiph): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Audit." God is "Rooted" in "Heaven" (*Shamayim*) and "Inspects" the "Understander" (*Mas-kil*). He finds "Universal Decay" (*Al-ne-la-khu*).

3 Not even One (Gam-En Echad): This is the "Root of Human Depravity." The "Corruption" is "Comprehensive." In the New Testament, Paul uses this to prove the "Need for Grace" (Romans 3:10-12). It is the "Uprooting of Human Self-Righteousness."

4 God Scattered the Bones (Phiz-zar Elohim Atz-moth): This is a "Vivid Image of Defeat" in the scrolls. God "Despises" (*Me-a-sam*) those who "Besiege" (*Cho-nakh*). He "Uproots" their "Army." It marks the transition from "Arrogance" to "Ashes."

5 Salvation... out of Zion (Ye-shu-oth mi-Tzi-yon): The scrolls conclude with the "Messianic Hope." True "Restoration" (*She-bub*) comes from the "Holy Mountain." It marks the transition to the "Refuge" of *Psalm 54*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Rejoicing" (*Sa-mach*). Come, Lord Zion.
