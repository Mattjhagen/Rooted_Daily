Psalm 34

Of David. When he pretended to be insane before Abimelek, who drove him away, and he left.

The Constant Praise

“I will extol the Lord at all times; his praise will always be on my lips.” 1
“Glorify the Lord with me; let us exalt his name together.”

The Radiant Face

“Those who look to him are radiant; their faces are never covered with shame.” 2

Taste and See

“Taste and see that the Lord is good; blessed is the one who takes refuge in him.” 3
“The angel of the Lord encamps around those who fear him, and he delivers them.”

The Close Presence

“The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.” 4
“He protects all his bones, not one of them will be broken.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Extol the Lord at all Times (A-bare-khah eth-YHWH be-Kol-Eth): In the scrolls, this is the "Acrostic Opening" (*Aleph*). To "Bless" (*Barakh*) "At All Times" is to "Root" the "Will" in "Gratitude" regardless of the "Season." It is the "Uprooting of Circumstantial Faith."

2 Radiant (Na-ha-ru): The scrolls highlight the "Reflective Root." Those who "Look" (*Hab-bitu*) to God become "Luminous." It is the "Uprooting of Gloom." It signifies the "Inner Glow" of the "Rooted."

3 Taste and See (Ta-a-mu u-Re-u): This is the "Experiential Root." "Taste" (*Ta-am*) implies "Sensory Knowledge." God is not just a "Theory," but a "Delight." It links back to the "Honey" of *Psalm 19*. It is the "Root of Personal Discovery."

4 Close to the Brokenhearted (Qa-rob le-Nish-be-re-Leb): Literally in the scrolls, "Near to those whose heart is shattered." The scrolls highlight the "Paradox of Proximity." God is "Rooted" in the "Pain" of His people. It is the "Uprooting of Isolation."

5 Protects all his Bones (Sho-mer Kol-Atz-mo-thaw): The scrolls conclude with this "Structural Root of Mercy." To "Protect the Bones" is to "Preserve the Core." In the New Testament, this is seen as the "Root of the Crucifixion" (John 19:36). It marks the transition from "Instruction" to "Redemption" (*Verse 22*). It is the "Rooted Daily" secret of "Preservation."
