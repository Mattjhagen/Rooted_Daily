Psalm 55

For the director of music. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David.

The Anguish within

“My heart is in anguish within me; the terrors of death have fallen on me.” 1
“Oh, that I had the wings of a dove! I would fly away and be at rest.” 2

The Treachery of a Friend

“If an enemy were insulting me, I could endure it; if a foe were rising against me, I could hide.”
“But it is you, a man like myself, my companion, my close friend.” 3
“...with whom I once enjoyed sweet fellowship at the house of God.”

Evening, Morning, and Noon

“Evening, morning and noon I cry out in distress, and he hears my voice.” 4

Cast Your Cares

“Cast your cares on the Lord and he will sustain you; he will never let the righteous be shaken.” 5
“But as for me, I trust in you.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Anguish within me (Ya-chil be-Qir-bi): Literally "My heart writhes/twists inside me." The scrolls highlight the "Visceral Root of Pain." David is "Rooted" in "Terror" (*E-mah*). It is the "Uprooting of Internal Peace."

2 Wings of a Dove (E-ber ka-Yo-nah): The scrolls highlight the "Request for Flight." The "Dove" (*Yonah*) is the "Symbol of Rest" (*Me-nu-chah*). David wants to "Uproot" from the "Storm" (*Su-ah*) and the "Tempest" (*Verse 8*).

3 My Companion, my Close Friend (Al-lu-phi u-Me-yu-da-i): Literally "My leader/guide and my known-one." The scrolls highlight the "Betrayal of the Inner Circle." This was not an "External Enemy," but an "Internal Root." The "Sweet Fellowship" (*Verse 14* - *Sod*) refers to "Confidential Counsel."

4 Evening, Morning, and Noon (E-reb wa-Bo-qer we-Tzo-ho-ra-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Pervasive Root of Prayer." David is "Calling" (*A-shi-chah*) all day long. It is the "Rooted Daily" discipline of "Distress."

5 Cast your Cares (Hash-lekh al-YHWH Ye-hab-kha): Literally "Throw upon YHWH your burden/gift." The scrolls highlight the "Act of Resignation." To "Cast" (*Hash-lekh*) is to "Uproot" the weight from yourself and "Root" it in the "Sustainer" (*Kl-kl*). He will "Never let the Righteous be Shaken" (*lo-Yit-then le-Olam Mot*).

6 I Trust in You (Ani Eb-tach Bakh): The scrolls conclude with this "Personal Anchor." Despite the "Bloodthirsty Men" (*Verse 23*), David’s "Final Root" is "Fixed." It marks the transition to the "Pursuit" of *Psalm 56*. It is the "Rooted Daily" triumph over "Treachery."
