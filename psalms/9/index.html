Psalm 9

For the director of music. To the tune of “The Death of the Son.” A psalm of David.

The Whole Heart

“I will give thanks to you, Lord, with all my heart; I will tell of all your wonderful deeds.” 1

The Righteous Throne

“For you have upheld my right and my cause, sitting enthroned as the righteous judge.” 2
“You have rebuked the nations and destroyed the wicked; you have blotted out their name forever.”

The High Tower

“The Lord is a stronghold for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble.” 3
“Those who know your name trust in you, for you, Lord, have never forsaken those who seek you.” 4
“But God will never forget the needy; the hope of the afflicted will never perish.” 5

Translation Notes

1 With all my Heart (be-Kol-Libbi): In the scrolls, "Giving thanks" (*Odeh*) is the "Root of Spiritual Health." David is "Rooting" his "Gratitude" in his "Entire Being." It is the "Uprooting of Resentment."

2 Upheld my Right (Asitha Mishpati): The scrolls highlight the "Legal Victory." God has "Vindicated" David’s "Root." He is the "Enthroned Judge" (*Shopheth Tzedeq*) who "Uproots" the "Wicked Nations."

3 Stronghold (Mis-gab): Literally in the scrolls, "A high place" or "Inaccessible retreat." It is the "Vertical Root of Safety." When "Trouble" (*Tzarah*) makes the world feel "Narrow," God provides "Height."

4 Know your Name (Yode-e Shemekha): The scrolls highlight the "Intimacy of Knowledge." To "Know the Name" is to "Root" one’s "Trust" in the "Identity of God." It is the "Assurance" that God never "Forsakes" (*Azab*) those who "Seek" (*Darash*) Him.

5 Hope... will never Perish (Tiq-wath Aniyim lo-Tho-bad): The scrolls conclude with this "Eternal Root of Hope." While the "Name of the Wicked" perishes (*Verse 5*), the "Hope of the Afflicted" is "Permanent." It signifies the "Sovereign Memory" of God. He is the "Root" who "Never Forgets" the "Needy." It marks the "Confidence" required to face the "Arrogance" of the next Psalm.
