Psalm 115

Not to Us, but to Your Name

“Not to us, Lord, not to us but to your name be the glory, because of your love and faithfulness.” 1
“Our God is in heaven; he does whatever pleases him.”

Mouths that Cannot Speak

“But their idols are silver and gold, made by human hands.”
“They have mouths, but cannot speak, eyes, but cannot see.” 2
“Those who make them will be like them.” 3

Help and Shield

“Trust in the Lord—he is your help and shield.” 4

Blessing of the Maker

“May the Lord cause you to flourish, both you and your children.”
“May you be blessed by the Lord, the Maker of heaven and earth.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Not to Us (Lo-la-nu): In the scrolls, this is the "Uprooting of Ego Root." Glory (*Khabod*) belongs "Exclusively" to the "Name" (*Shem*). It signifies "Covenantal Humility."

2 Mouths... Eyes (Pe-la-hem... Ey-na-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Spiritual Impotence." The "Idols" (*At-zab-be-hem*) are "Lifeless" (*Silver/Gold*). It is the "Contrast of Agency." God "Does" (*Asah*) what pleases Him; idols "Cannot" (*Lo*) do anything.

3 Like them (Ke-mo-hem): The scrolls highlight the "Assimilative Root." We "Become" what we "Root" ourselves in. To worship the "Lifeless" is to "Uproot" one’s own "Vitality." It signify "Ontological Devolution."

4 Help and Shield (Ez-ram u-Ma-gin-nam): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Protective Trust." Three groups (*Israel, Aaron, Fearers*) are called to "Trust" (*Be-ta-chu*). God is the "Active Support" (*Ezrah*) and the "Barrier" (*Magen*). It is the "Root of Communal Security."

5 Maker of Heaven and Earth (O-seh Sha-ma-yim wa-Aretz): The scrolls conclude with this "Creation Root of Blessing." The "Source" (*Maker*) of the "Cosmos" is the "Source" of the "Flourishing" (*Yo-seph*). It marks the transition to the "Personal Love" in *Psalm 116*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Living Hope." Glory to Your name.
