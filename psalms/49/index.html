Psalm 49

For the director of music. Of the Sons of Korah. A psalm.

The Global Riddle

“Hear this, all you peoples; listen, all who live in this world.” 1
“I will turn my attention to a proverb; with the harp I will expound my riddle.”

The Costly Ransom

“No one can redeem the life of another or give to God a ransom for them.” 2
“The ransom for a life is costly, no payment is ever enough.”

The Common End

“For all can see that the wise die, that the foolish and the senseless also perish.” 3
“They are like sheep and are destined for the grave; death will be their shepherd.”

The Redemption

“But God will redeem me from the realm of the dead; he will surely take me to himself.” 4 Selah
“People who have wealth but lack understanding are like the beasts that perish.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Hear this, all you Peoples (Shim-u-zoth Kol-ha-Am-mim): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Universal Wisdom." This message is not just for Israel, but for the "World" (*Cha-led*). It is the "Uprooting of Material Pride." The "Riddle" (*Chi-da*) is the "Mystery of Mortality."

2 Ransom (Ko-pher): The scrolls highlight the "Financial Futility of the Soul." A "Ransom" (*Kopher*) can buy a slave, but it cannot buy "Continuity" (*Verse 9*). The "Root of Life" is "Costly" (*Yaqar*). It is the "Uprooting of the Illusion of Purchase."

3 Death will be their Shepherd (Ma-weth Yi-re-em): This is a "Grim Pastoral Root" in the scrolls. While the righteous have the "Lord as Shepherd" (*Psalm 23*), the wealthy fool is led by "Death" (*Maweth*) into the "Grave" (*Sheol*). It is the "End of the Worldling’s Root."

4 God will Redeem me (Elohim Yiph-deh Naphe-shi): The scrolls highlight the "Redemptive Root." God is the "Only One" who can pay the "Ransom." He "Takes" (*Yiq-qa-che-ni*) the psalmist. It signifies the "Uprooting from Sheol."

5 Like the Beasts that Perish (ka-Be-he-moth Nid-mu): The scrolls conclude with this "Refrain of Understanding." Wealth without "Understanding" (*Bi-nah*) reduces a man to the "Animal Root." It marks the transition to the "Judgment" of *Psalm 50*. It is the "Rooted Daily" reminder that we are "More than what we Own."
