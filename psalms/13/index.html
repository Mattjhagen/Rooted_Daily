Psalm 13

For the director of music. A psalm of David.

The Fourfold Cry

“How long, Lord? Will you forget me forever? How long will you hide your face from me?” 1
“How long must I wrestle with my thoughts and day after day have sorrow in my heart?”

Light to the Eyes

“Look on me and answer, Lord my God. Give light to my eyes, or I will sleep in death.” 2

The Resting Heart

“But I trust in your unfailing love; my heart rejoices in your salvation.” 3
“I will sing the Lord’s praise, for he has been good to me.” 4

Translation Notes

1 How Long (Ad-An-nah): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Prolonged Agony." David feels "Forgotten" (*Thish-ka-cheni*) and "Hidden" from God’s "Face." It is the "Uprooting of Certainty." The "Wrestling" (*Eshith Etzoth*) describes the "Internal Friction" of the soul.

2 Light to my Eyes (He-irah E-nay): The scrolls highlight the "Request for Vitality." "Dark eyes" are a "Sign of Death" in Hebrew poetry. David is asking God to "Re-root" his "Sight" in "Hope." To "Sleep in Death" is the "Final Uprooting."

3 Unfailing Love (Chesed): This is the "Strongest Root" in the Psalter. *Chesed* is God’s "Covenant Loyalty." Even when God "Seems" to forget, his "Love" is "Un-uprootable." David chooses to "Trust" (*Batach-ti*) in the "Salvation" (*Yeshu-ath-kha*) he cannot yet see.

4 He has been Good to me (Gamal Alay): Literally in the scrolls, "He has dealt bountifully with me." The psalm concludes with a "Paradox." David is "Singing" while still in the "Trial." He has "Uprooted" his "Mood" from his "Circumstance" and "Rooted" it in the "Memory of God’s Goodness." It marks the "Victory of Faith." It is the "Rooted Daily" secret of "Perseverance."
