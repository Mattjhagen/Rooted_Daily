Psalm 81

For the director of music. On gittith. Of Asaph.

The Festival Shout

“Sing for joy to God our strength; shout aloud to the God of Jacob!” 1
“Sound the ram’s horn at the New Moon, and when the moon is full, on the day of our festival.”

Freedom from the Basket

“I removed the burden from their shoulders; their hands were set free from the basket.” 2
“Open wide your mouth and I will fill it.” 3

Stubborn Hearts

“But my people would not listen to me; Israel would not submit to me.” 4
“So I gave them over to their stubborn hearts to follow their own devices.”

Honey from the Rock

“If my people would only listen to me, if Israel would only follow my ways.”
“With honey from the rock I would satisfy you.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sing for Joy (Har-ni-nu le-Elohim): In the scrolls, this is the "Festival Root of Victory." The "Shout" (*Ha-ri-u*) and the "Horn" (*Sho-phar*) signal the "Re-Rooting of the People in Joy." It is the "Uprooting of the Toil-Mindset."

2 Free from the Basket (mi-Dud Tha-a-bor-nah): The scrolls highlight the "Labor Root." The "Basket" (*Dud*) was used for carrying clay for bricks in Egypt. God "Uprooted" them from "Slavery" (*Sik-low* - literally 'his burden'). It is the "Root of Exodus Freedom."

3 Open Wide your Mouth (Har-cheb Pi-kha wa-Ama-le-hu): Literally "Enlarge your mouth and I will fill it." The scrolls highlight the "Divine Generosity Root." God is the "Source" (*Elohe-kha*) who "Brought you up" (*ham-Ma-a-lekha*). It is the "Uprooting of Lack."

4 Stubborn Hearts (be-She-ri-ruth Lib-bam): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Inflexibility." Israel "Turned away" (*lo-A-bah*) and followed their "Own Devices" (*Mo-atzo-the-hem*). It signifies the "Uprooting of Divine Guidance."

5 Honey from the Rock (u-mit-Tzur De-bash As-bi-e-kha): The scrolls conclude with this "Miraculous Satiety Root." God provides sweetness (*Honey*) from the most "Improbable Place" (*The Rock*). It marks the transition to the "Judgment of the Gods" in *Psalm 82*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Provision." Open wide.
