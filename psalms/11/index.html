Psalm 11

For the director of music. Of David.

The Refuge Refusal

“In the Lord I take refuge. How then can you say to me: ‘Flee like a bird to your mountain’?” 1

The Shaking Foundations

“For look, the wicked bend their bows; they set their arrows against the strings.”
“When the foundations are being destroyed, what can the righteous do?” 2

The Heavenly Throne

“The Lord is in his holy temple; the Lord is on his heavenly throne.” 3
“He observes everyone on earth; his eyes examine them.” 4
“For the Lord is righteous, he loves justice; the upright will see his face.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Flee like a Bird (Nudu Har-khem Tzippor): In the scrolls, this is the "Advice of Panic." The advisors suggest "Uprooting" David from his position and "Fleeing" to the wild. David’s "Refuge" (*Chassi-thi*) is not "Local" but "Spiritual." He refuses to be "Uprooted" by "Fear."

2 Foundations are being Destroyed (ha-Sha-thoth Ye-ha-re-sun): The scrolls highlight the "Societal Root." If the "Social Foundations" (*Law, Truth, Order*) are "Uprooted," the righteous man cannot "Stand" on "Earthly Ground." He must "Root" himself in something "Higher."

3 On His Heavenly Throne (ba-Sha-ma-yim Kis-o): This is the "Answer to the Shaking Foundations." While the "Earthly Floor" is "Uprooted," the "Heavenly Seat" is "Permanent." God is the "Root of Universal Order."

4 His Eyes Examine (E-nay-im Ye-che-zu): Literally in the scrolls, "His eyes behold." It is the "Uprooting of Anonymity." Even when the wicked "Shoot from the Shadows" (*Verse 2*), God "Sees" them. He is "Rooted" in "Omniscience."

5 The Upright will see His Face (Ya-shar Ye-che-zu Pha-ne-mo): The scrolls conclude with the "Ultimate Reward." While the wicked face "Scorching Wind" (*Verse 6*), the "Righteous" (*Ya-shar*) receive the "Vision of God." It is the "Final Root of Joy." To "See the Face" is to be "Restored" and "Vindicated." It marks the transition from "Hidden Arrows" to "Open Glory."
