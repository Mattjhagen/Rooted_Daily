Psalm 92

A psalm. A song. For the Sabbath day.

Good to Praise

“It is good to praise the Lord and make music to your name, O Most High.” 1
“Proclaiming your love in the morning and your faithfulness at night.”

Profound Thoughts

“For you make me glad by your deeds, Lord; I sing for joy at what your hands have done.”
“How great are your works, Lord, how profound your thoughts!” 2

The Palm and the Cedar

“The righteous will flourish like a palm tree, they will grow like a cedar of Lebanon.” 3
“Planted in the house of the Lord, they will flourish in the courts of our God.” 4

Fruit in Old Age

“They will still bear fruit in old age, they will stay fresh and green.” 5
“Proclaiming, ‘The Lord is upright; he is my Rock.’” 6

Translation Notes

1 It is Good to Praise (Tob le-Ho-doth): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Spiritual Health." The "Sabbath" (*Shabbath*) is "Rooted" in "Gratitude" (*Ho-doth*). It is the "Uprooting of the Toil-Mindset."

2 Profound Thoughts (Am-qu Ma-cha-she-bo-the-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Depth Root of Divine Wisdom." God’s "Works" (*Ma-a-sekha*) are "Visible," but His "Thoughts" (*Mach-sha-ba*) are "Rooted" in the "Infinite Deep" (*Amqu*). It signifies "Unfathomable Design."

3 Palm tree... Cedar (ka-Thamar yiphrach... ke-Erez bal-Le-ba-non): The scrolls highlight the "Organic Root of Growth." The "Palm" (*Thamar*) signifies "Sweetness and Resilience," while the "Cedar" (*Erez*) signifies "Strength and Fragrance." It is the "Rooted Life."

4 Planted in the House (She-thu-lim be-Beth YHWH): Literally "Transplanted." The scrolls highlight the "Spatial Root." Flourishing (*Yiph-ri-chu*) is a "Product of Proximity" to the "Presence." It is the "Root of Sacred Space."

5 Fresh and Green (De-she-nim we-Ra-a-nan-nim): The scrolls highlight the "Perpetual Root of Vitality." Unlike the "Grass" (*Verse 7*) which withers, the "Righteous" (*Tzaddiq*) stay "Green" (*Ra-a-nan*) even in "Old Age" (*be-Sey-bah*). It signifies "Eternal Nutrient Flow."

6 My Rock (Tzu-ri): The scrolls conclude with this "Stable Finality." Proclaiming God’s "Uprightness" (*Ya-shar*) is the "Fruit" of the "Fresh" life. It marks the transition to the "Majesty of the Reign" in *Psalm 93*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Timeless Flourishing." Fresh and green.
