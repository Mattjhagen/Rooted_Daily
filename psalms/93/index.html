Psalm 93

The Majesty of the Reign

“The Lord reigns, he is robed in majesty; the Lord is robed in majesty and armed with strength.” 1
“The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved.”

The Eternal Throne

“Your throne was established long ago; you are from all eternity.” 2

Mightier than the Waves

“The seas have lifted up, Lord, the seas have lifted up their voice; the seas have lifted up their pounding waves.” 3
“Mightier than the thunder of the great waters... the Lord on high is majestic.” 4
“Your statutes, Lord, stand firm; holiness adorns your house for endless days.” 5

Translation Notes

1 The Lord Reigns (YHWH Ma-lakh): In the scrolls, this is the "Present-Perfect Root." God has reigned, is reigning, and will reign. He is "Robed" (*La-bash*) in "Majesty" (*Ge-uth* - literally 'rising/exaltation'). It is the "Root of Absolute Sovereignty."

2 Throne Established (Na-khon Kis-a-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Stability." The "Throne" (*Kisse*) is "Fixed" (*Nakhon*) before "Time" (*Az*). It signifies "Primordial Authority."

3 Seas have Lifted Up (Na-se-u Ne-ha-roth): Literally "The floods/rivers have lifted up." The scrolls highlight the "Chaos Root." The "Roar" (*Voice*) of the "Waters" (*Ma-yim*) represents the "Turbulence of the Nations." It is the "Uprooting of Peace."

4 Mightier than Thunder (mi-Qo-loth Ma-yim Rab-bim): The scrolls highlight the "Superior Root of Power." God’s "Majesty" (*Ad-dir*) is "Greater" than the "Breakers" (*Mish-be-re*) of the "Sea" (*Yam*). It is the "Root of Calm Authority."

5 Statutes stand Firm (Edo-the-kha Ne-em-nu Me-od): The scrolls conclude with the "Legal Root." God’s "Testimonies" (*Statutes*) are "Very Faithful" (*Ne-em-nu*). "Holiness" (*Qo-desh*) is the "Aesthetic" of His "House" (*Beth*). It marks the transition to the "God of Vengeance" in *Psalm 94*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Cosmic King." Majesty is Yours.
