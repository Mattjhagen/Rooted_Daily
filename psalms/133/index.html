Psalm 133

A song of ascents. Of David.

How Good and Pleasant

“How good and pleasant it is when God’s people live together in unity!” 1

Precious Oil

“It is like precious oil poured on the head, running down on the beard.” 2
“Running down on Aaron’s beard, down on the collar of his robe.”

Dew of Hermon

“It is as if the dew of Hermon were falling on Mount Zion.” 3

Life Forevermore

“For there the Lord bestows his blessing, even life forevermore.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Unity (Ya-chad): In the scrolls, this is the "Collective Root." To live "Together" (*Ya-chad*) is the "Organic Flourishing" of the people. It is the "Uprooting of Division." "Good" (*Tob*) and "Pleasant" (*Na-im*) are the "Nutrients" of the community.

2 Precious Oil (ha-She-men hat-Tob): The scrolls highlight the "Anointing Root." "Unity" is compared to the "Holy Oil" (*Shemen*) that "Flows Down" (*Yo-red*). It signifies "Supernatural Connection" from the "Head" (*Rosh*) to the "Edge" (*Pi*) of the garment. It is the "Uprooting of Friction."

3 Dew of Hermon (Tal-Cher-mon): The scrolls highlight the "Hydration Root." "Hermon" was the "High Source" of water; "Zion" was the "Spiritual Destination." Unity is the "Dew" (*Tal*) that "Roots" life in the "Mountains." It signifies "Mutual Refreshment."

4 Life Forevermore (Cha-yim ad-ha-Olam): The scrolls conclude with this "Vitality Root." The "Place of Unity" is the "Place of Blessing" (*Ba-rak*). It marks the transition to the "Night Praise" in *Psalm 134*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Social Harmony." Life flows here.
