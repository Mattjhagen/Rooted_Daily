Psalm 73

A psalm of Asaph.

The Slipping Feet

“Surely God is good to Israel, to those who are pure in heart.” 1
“But as for me, my feet had almost slipped; I had nearly lost my foothold.”

Envy of the Arrogant

“For I envied the arrogant when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.” 2
“Surely in vain I have kept my heart pure and have washed my hands in innocence.”

The Sanctuary Vision

“...till I entered the sanctuary of God; then I understood their final destiny.” 3
“Surely you place them on slippery ground; you cast them down to ruin.”

Near to God

“Whom have I in heaven but you? And earth has nothing I desire besides you.” 4
“But as for me, it is good to be near God. I have made the Sovereign Lord my refuge.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Pure in Heart (Ba-re Le-bab): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Integrity." Book III begins with a "Thesis Statement" of God’s "Goodness" (*Tob*). But Asaph is "Uprooted" by "External Observation."

2 Envied the Arrogant (Qin-ne-thi ba-Ho-le-lim): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Comparison." "Prosperity" (*Sha-lom*) of the "Wicked" (*Re-sha-im*) causes "Spiritual Vertigo." The "Steps" (*A-shu-ray*) nearly "Slipped" (*Shup-pe-khu* - literally 'poured out'). It is the "Uprooting of the Walk."

3 Entered the Sanctuary (A-bo el-Miq-de-she-El): This is the "Pivotal Root of Book III." Perspective is not "Intellectual," but "Spatial." By moving into "God’s Space" (*Sanctuary*), Asaph "Roots" his "Mind" in "Eternal Destiny" (*A-cha-ri-tham*). It is the "Uprooting of Temporal Optics."

4 Earth has nothing I Desire (we-Im-me-kha lo-Cha-phatz-thi ba-Aretz): Literally "With you I have no delight in the earth." The scrolls highlight the "Ultimate Root of Satisfaction." God is the "Strength of the Heart" (*Tzur Leba-bi*) and the "Portion" (*Chel-qi*) forever (*Verse 26*).

5 Good to be Near God (Qi-ra-bath Elohim Li-Tob): The scrolls conclude with this "Experiential Root." "Good" (*Tob*) is not "Wealth," but "Proximity." It marks the transition to the "Ruins of the Sanctuary" in *Psalm 74*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Stable Heart." Nearness is goodness.
