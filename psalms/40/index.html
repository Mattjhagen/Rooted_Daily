Psalm 40

For the director of music. Of David. A psalm.

The Slimy Pit

“I waited patiently for the Lord; he turned to me and heard my cry.” 1
“He lifted me out of the slimy pit, out of the mud and mire; he set my feet on a rock and gave me a firm place to stand.” 2

The Opened Ear

“Sacrifice and offering you did not desire—but my ears you have opened—burnt offerings and sin offerings you did not require.” 3
“I desire to do your will, my God; your law is within my heart.” 4

The Unsealed Lips

“I proclaim your saving acts in the great congregation; I do not seal my lips, Lord, as you know.”

The Thinking Lord

“But as for me, I am poor and needy; may the Lord think of me.” 5
“You are my help and my deliverer; you are my God, do not delay.”

Translation Notes

1 Waited Patiently (Qaw-woh Qi-wi-thi): Literally in the scrolls, "In waiting I waited." It is the "Root of Endurance." David is "Rooting" his "Hope" in "Divine Attention." It is the "Uprooting of Impatience."

2 Slimy Pit (Bor Sha-on): Literally "The pit of noise/tumult." The scrolls highlight the "Chaos of the Depth." Mud and Mire (*Yewen ha-Metzu-lah*) represent the "Instability of Sin." God "Roots" his servant on a "Rock" (*Sela*). It is the "Transformation from Mire to Mountain."

3 My Ears you have Opened (Ozna-yim Ka-ri-tha Li): Literally "Ears you have dug/hollowed for me." This is a "Stunning Hebrew Metaphor." God has "Pierced the Ear" of his servant to make him "Attentive." In the Septuagint and Hebrews 10, this is rendered "A body you have prepared for me." It is the "Root of Obedience."

4 Law is within my Heart (Tho-rath-kha be-thokh Me-ay): Literally "Your Torah is in the midst of my bowels/innards." The scrolls highlight the "Internalization of Revelation." The "Word" is not on "Stone," but "Rooted" in the "Organs of Life." It is the "New Covenant Root."

5 May the Lord think of me (Adonai Ya-cha-shob Li): The scrolls conclude with this "Intimate Root." David is "Poor" (*Ani*), but he is "Minded" (*Ya-cha-shob*) by God. It signifies that "Rootedness" is a "Mutual Memory." It marks the transition to the "Mercy" of *Psalm 41*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem. God thinks of me.
