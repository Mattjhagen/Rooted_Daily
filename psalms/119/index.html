Psalm 119

The Alphabetical Psalm of the Word.

א Aleph
“Blessed are those whose ways are blameless, who walk according to the law of the Lord.” 1
“Blessed are those who keep his statutes and seek him with all their heart.”
“Oh, that my ways were steadfast in obeying your decrees!”
ב Beth
“How can a young person stay on the path of purity? By living according to your word.” 2
“I have hidden your word in my heart that I might not sin against you.”
ג Gimel
“Open my eyes that I may see wonderful things in your law.” 3
“I am a stranger on earth; do not hide your commands from me.”
ד Daleth
“My soul is weary with sorrow; strengthen me according to your word.” 4
“My soul is weary with sorrow; strengthen me according to your word.”
ה He
“Turn my eyes away from worthless things; preserve my life according to your word.”
ל Lamedh
“Your word, Lord, is eternal; it stands firm in the heavens.” 5
מ Mem
“Oh, how I love your law! I meditate on it all day long.”
“How sweet are your words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth!”
נ Nun
“Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path.” 6
פ Pe
“The unfolding of your words gives light; it gives understanding to the simple.”
ש Shin
“Great peace have those who love your law, and nothing can make them stumble.” 7
ת Tav
“I have strayed like a lost sheep. Seek your servant, for I have not forgotten your commands.” 8

Translation Notes

1 Law (To-rah): In the scrolls, "Torah" is the "Foundational Root." This psalm uses 8 synonyms for the Word (*Torah, Statues, Precepts, Commands, Decrees, Word, Promise, Laws*). It is the "Root of Spiritual Nutrition."

2 Stay on the Path of Purity (be-Ma-gal To-har): The scrolls highlight the "Navigational Root." For the "Youth" (*Na-ar*), the path is "Rooted" in the "Word" (*Da-bar*). It signify "Moral Traction."

3 Open my Eyes (Gal Ey-nay): Literally "Uncover/Unveil my eyes." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Revelation." The "Law" (*Torah*) contains "Wonders" (*Niph-la-oth*) that are invisible until the "Veil" is "Uprooted." It signifies "Deep Insight."

4 Weary with Sorrow (Dal-phah Naph-shi mit-Thugah): Literally "My soul drips/leaks with grief." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Emotional Depletion." The "Word" is the "Seal" that "Strengthens" (*Qu-ye-me-ni*) the leak. It is the "Spiritual Transfusion."

5 Eternal... Firm in the Heavens (le-Olam... Nitz-zav ba-Sha-ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Cosmic Root of Truth." God’s "Word" is not "Uprooted" by time; it is "Fixed" (*Nitz-zav*) in the "Celestial Foundation." It signifies "Absolute Continuity."

6 Lamp for my Feet (Ner le-Rag-li): The scrolls highlight the "Practical Root of Guidance." The "Word" is a "Lamp" (*Ner*) for the "Immediate Step" and "Light" (*Or*) for the "Long Distance" (*Path/Na-thib*). It is the "Uprooting of Stumbling."

7 Great Peace (Sha-lom Rab): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Tranquility." Those "Rooted" in the "Law" have "Great Shalom" and no "Stumbling Block" (*Mik-shol*). It signifies "Unshakable Peace."

8 Lost Sheep (k-E-seh O-bed): The scrolls conclude the longest psalm with this "Humble Finality Root." Even the most "Law-loving" soul feels like a "Lost Sheep" (*O-bed* - literally 'perishing'). The final "Root" is "Seeking" (*Ba-qesh*) by the "Shepherd." It marks the transition to the "Songs of Ascents" in *Psalm 120*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Exhaustive Word." Seek me, Lord.
