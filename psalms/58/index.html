Psalm 58

For the director of music. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” Of David. A miktam.

The Unjust Rulers

“Do you rulers indeed speak justly? Do you judge people with equity? No, in your heart you devise injustice.” 1

The Deaf Cobra

“Their venom is like the venom of a snake, like that of a cobra that has stopped its ears.” 2
“That will not heed the tune of the charmer, however skillful the enchanter may be.”

The Broken Fangs

“Break the teeth in their mouths, O God; Lord, tear out the fangs of those lions!” 3

The Reward of the Righteous

“Then people will say, ‘Surely the righteous still are rewarded; surely there is a God who judges the earth.’” 4

Translation Notes

1 Do you Rulers speak Justly? (Ha-um-nam E-lem Tzedeq Te-dab-be-run): In the scrolls, "Rulers" (*Elem*) literally means "Silence" or "Mutes." It is a "Stunning Hebrew Irony." Those who are supposed to speak for justice are "Mute." It is the "Uprooting of the Judicial Function."

2 Venom... Cobra (Cha-math... Pe-then): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Incorrigibility." The "Wicked" (*Re-sha-im*) are "Wayward from the Womb" (*Verse 3*). Like a "Cobra" (*Pethen*) that "Shuts its Ear" (*Ya-a-them Oz-no*), they refuse the "Skillful Enchanter" (*Cho-ber Cha-ba-rim*). It is the "Uprooting of the Ability to Change."

3 Break the Teeth (Ha-ros Shin-ne-mo): This is the "Root of Divine Restraint." David asks God to "Uproot" the "Power" of the wicked to "Bite" (*Lion's fangs* - *Mal-the-oth Ke-phi-rim*). It is the "Judgment Root."

4 God who Judges the Earth (Elohim Sho-phe-thim ba-Aretz): The scrolls conclude with this "Vertical Affirmation." Despite the "Unjust Rulers" (*Verse 1*), the "Ultimate Judge" (*Sho-phe-thim*) is "Rooted" in "Truth." There is a "Reward" (*Peri* - literally 'fruit') for the "Righteous." It marks the transition to the "Enemies at the Door" in *Psalm 59*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Ultimate Justice."
