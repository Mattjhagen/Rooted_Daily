Psalm 64

For the director of music. A psalm of David.

The Secret Plot

“Hear me, my God, as I voice my complaint; protect my life from the threat of the enemy.” 1
“Hide me from the conspiracy of the wicked, from the plots of evildoers.”

Tongues like Swords

“They sharpen their tongues like swords and aim cruel words like deadly arrows.” 2
“They say, ‘Who will see it?’”

God’s Arrow

“But God will shoot them with his arrows; they will suddenly be struck down.” 3

Proclaim the Works

“All people will fear; they will proclaim the works of God and ponder what he has done.” 4
“The righteous will rejoice in the Lord and take refuge in him.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Voice my Complaint (Shi-chi): In the scrolls, "Complaint" (*Sich*) can also mean "Meditation" or "Musings." David is "Rooting" his "Anxiety" in "Divine Attention." He is "Uprooting" the "Terror" (*Pa-chad*) of the enemy.

2 Sharpen their Tongues (Sha-ne-nu kheb-Khe-reb): The scrolls highlight the "Linguistic Warfare." The "Tongue" (*Le-shon*) is treated as a "Weapon" (*Sword/Arrow* - *Khe-reb/Chitz*). The "Innocent" (*Tham*) is targeted from "Ambush" (*be-Mis-ta-rim*). It is the "Root of Slander."

3 God will Shoot them (wa-Yo-rem Elohim): This is the "Divine Irony Root." The "Arrows" (*Chitz*) of the wicked are met by the "Arrows" of God. Their own "Tongue" (*Le-sho-nam*) will "Trip them Up" (*Verse 8*). It is the "Uprooting of the Conspiracy."

4 Ponder what He has Done (u-Ma-a-se-hu His-ki-lu): The scrolls highlight the "Educational Root of Judgment." When God acts, people "Intelligently Observe" (*Mas-kil*). It is the "Root of Global Awe."

5 The Righteous will Rejoice (Yis-mach Tzad-diq): The scrolls conclude with this "Stable Joy." The "Upright in Heart" (*Yish-re Leb*) find "Glory" (*Yith-ha-le-lu*). It marks the transition to the "Bounty of Zion" in *Psalm 65*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Refuge." The arrows of truth are coming. Praise is coming.
