Psalm 101

Of David. A psalm.

The Song of Love and Justice

“I will sing of your love and justice; to you, Lord, I will sing praise.” 1

The Blameless Heart

“I will walk within my house with a blameless heart.” 2
“I will not look with approval on anything that is vile.” 3

Eyes on the Faithful

“My eyes will be on the faithful in the land, that they may dwell with me.” 4
“No one who practices deceit will dwell in my house.”

The Morning Silence

“Every morning I will put to silence all the wicked in the land.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Love and Justice (Che-sed u-Mish-pat): In the scrolls, this is the "Dual Root of Governance." David "Roots" his "Song" (*A-shi-rah*) in the tension between "Mercy" and "Rule." It is the "Root of the Royal Code."

2 Blameless Heart (be-Thom Le-ba-bi): Literally "In the integrity of my heart." The scrolls highlight the "Internal Root of Integrity." Even "Within my House" (*be-Qe-reb Be-thi*), where no one sees, David is "Rooted." It is the "Uprooting of Hypocrisy."

3 Anything that is Vile (De-bar Be-li-ya-al): Literally "A thing of Belial/Worthlessness." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Visual Purity." To "Set" (*A-shith*) one's "Eyes" (*Ey-nay*) on evil is to "Uproot" the "Soul." It signifies "Intentional Guarding."

4 Eyes on the Faithful (Ey-nay be-Ne-em-ne-Eretz): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Community." The King "Roots" himself among the "Faithful" (*Ne-em-ne*). Deceit (*Re-mi-yah*) and Lies (*She-qa-rim*) are "Uprooted" from the "Dwelling" (*Verse 7*).

5 Morning I will put to Silence (la-Be-qa-rim Atz-mith): The scrolls conclude with this "Administrative Discipline Root." "Every Morning" signifies "Diligence." To "Put to Silence" (*Atzmith*) is to "Exterminate" the "Influence of Evil" from the "City of the Lord" (*I-ir YHWH*). It marks the transition to the "Afflicted Prayer" in *Psalm 102*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Integrity." I will walk purely.
