Psalm 111

Extol with All My Heart

“Praise the Lord. I will extol the Lord with all my heart in the council of the upright and in the assembly.” 1

Great are the Works

“Great are the works of the Lord; they are pondered by all who delight in them.” 2
“Glorious and majestic are his deeds, and his righteousness endures forever.”
“He has caused his wonders to be remembered.”

Trustworthy Precepts

“The works of his hands are faithful and just; all his precepts are trustworthy.” 3
“They are established for ever and ever, enacted in faithfulness and uprightness.”

Beginning of Wisdom

“The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom; all who follow his precepts have good understanding.” 4
“To him belongs eternal praise.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Extol... Heart (O-deh... be-Khol Le-bab): In the scrolls, this is the "Acrostic Root of Gratitude." Each line follows the Hebrew alphabet. Praise is "Rooted" in the "Full Heart" (*Lebab*) and "Public Council" (*Sod*). It is the "Root of the Alphabetical Anthem."

2 Great are the Works (Ge-do-lim Ma-a-se YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Divine Activity." God’s "Works" are "Sought/Pondered" (*De-ru-shim*) by those who "Delight" (*Che-phetz*) in them. It signifies "Intellectual Worship."

3 Precepts are Trustworthy (Ne-em-nim Kol Piq-qu-daw): The scrolls highlight the "Legal Root of Stability." God’s "Statutes" (*Precepts*) are "Rooted" in "Truth" (*Emeth*) and "Justice" (*Mishpat*). It signify "Unshakable Moral Law."

4 Beginning of Wisdom (Re-shith Cho-khmah): The scrolls highlight the "Foundational Root." "Wisdom" (*Cho-khmah*) starts with "Awe" (*Fear/Yir-ah*). To "Follow" (*As-seh*) is to have "Good Understanding" (*Se-khel Tob*). It is the "Uprooting of Foolishness."

5 Eternal Praise (The-hil-la-tho o-me-deth la-Ad): The scrolls conclude with this "Permanent Root." God’s "Praise" (*Te-hil-lah*) "Stands" (*O-me-deth*) "Forever" (*Ad*). It marks the transition to the "Blessed Life" in *Psalm 112*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Great Wonders." Wisdom begins here.
