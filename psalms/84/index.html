Psalm 84

For the director of music. On gittith. Of the Sons of Korah. A psalm.

How Lovely

“How lovely is your dwelling place, Lord Almighty!” 1
“My soul yearns, even faints, for the courts of the Lord.”

The Valley of Baka

“Blessed are those whose strength is in you, whose hearts are set on pilgrimage.” 2
“As they pass through the Valley of Baka, they make it a place of springs.” 3

One Day in Your Courts

“Better is one day in your courts than a thousand elsewhere.” 4
“I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God than dwell in the tents of the wicked.”

Sun and Shield

“For the Lord God is a sun and shield; the Lord bestows favor and honor.” 5
“Lord Almighty, blessed is the one who trusts in you.” 6

Translation Notes

1 How Lovely (Mah-Ye-di-doth): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Affection." The "Dwelling Place" (*Mish-ke-no-the-kha*) is "Beloved." Even the "Sparrow" (*Tzip-por*) and "Swallow" (*Deror*) find a "Rooted Home" near the "Altars" (*Verse 3*). It signifies "Universal Belonging."

2 Hearts set on Pilgrimage (Me-sil-loth bi-Leba-bam): Literally "Highways are in their heart." The scrolls highlight the "Internal Root of Journey." Strength (*Oz*) is "Rooted" in the "Direction" of the soul. It is the "Uprooting of Stagnation."

3 Valley of Baka (Emeq ha-Ba-kha): Literally "Valley of Weeping/Balsam trees." The scrolls highlight the "Transformative Root." The "Dry/Tearful Place" is turned into a "Spring" (*Ma-yan*). Pilgrims go from "Strength to Strength" (*Chayil el-Chayil*). It signifies "Spiritual Alchemy."

4 Better is One Day (Tob-Yom be-Cha-tze-re-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Value." A "Doorkeeper" (*His-to-phaph* - literally 'to stand at the threshold') in God’s "House" (*Beth*) is "Superior" to "Residency" in "Wickedness" (*Re-sha*). It is the "Uprooting of False Ambition."

5 Sun and Shield (She-mesh u-Ma-gen): The scrolls highlight the "Dual Root of Providence." God is "Light/Growth" (*Sun*) and "Protection/Safety" (*Shield*). He bestows "Favor" (*Chen*) and "Honor" (*Kha-bod*). It signifies "Total Sufficiency."

6 Blessed is the One who Trusts (Ash-re Adam Bo-te-ach Bakh): The scrolls conclude with this "Stable Beatitude." "Rootedness" is "Trust" (*Bi-ta-chon*). It marks the transition to the "Restoration of the Land" in *Psalm 85*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Sanctuary-Longer." How lovely You are.
