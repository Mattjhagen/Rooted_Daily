Psalm 78

A maskil of Asaph.

Teaching the Next Generation

“My people, hear my teaching; listen to the words of my mouth.” 1
“We will not hide them from their descendants; we will tell the next generation the praiseworthy deeds of the Lord.”

Wonders in Egypt

“He did miracles in the sight of their ancestors in the land of Egypt.”
“He rained down manna for the people to eat; he gave them the grain of heaven.” 2

The Stubborn Heart

“Their hearts were not loyal to him; they were not faithful to his covenant.” 3
“Yet he was merciful; he forgave their iniquities.”

The Chosen Shepherd

“He chose David his servant and took him from the sheep pens.” 4
“And David shepherded them with integrity of heart; with skillful hands he led them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Hear my Teaching (Ha-a-zi-nah Am-mi Tho-ra-thi): In the scrolls, "Teaching" (*Torah*) is a "Instructional Root." Asaph uses a "Parable" (*Ma-shal*) to "Root" the "Next Generation" (*Dor A-cha-ron*) in "Truth." It is the "Uprooting of Ignorance."

2 Grain of Heaven (De-ghan Sha-ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Supernatural Provision Root." God "Opened the Doors of the Heavens" (*Verse 23*) to "Root" His "People" in "Survival." It is the "Uprooting of Scarcity."

3 Hearts were not Loyal (we-Lib-bam lo-Na-khon Im-mo): Literally "Their heart was not prepared/fixed with Him." The scrolls highlight the "Unstable Root of Rebellion." Despite "Miracles" (*Nipha-la-oth*), they "Forgot" (*Verse 11*). It is the "Pattern of Human Fickleness."

4 Took him from the Sheep Pens (mi-Mikh-le-oth Tzon): The scrolls highlight the "Humility Root of Leadership." God "Uproots" David from the "Folds" to "Root" him on "Mount Zion" (*Verse 68*). It signifies "Divine Selection."

5 Integrity of Heart (be-Thom Leba-bo): The scrolls conclude with this "Stable Ideal." David’s "Heart" (*Leb*) is "Perfect/Whole" (*Thom*). His "Hands" (*Kap-paw*) are "Skillful" (*The-bu-noth* - literally 'wise/understanding'). It marks the transition to the "Invasion of the Inheritance" in *Psalm 79*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "History." We must not forget.
