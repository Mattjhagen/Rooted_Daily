Psalm 82

A psalm of Asaph.

The Great Assembly

“God presides in the great assembly; he renders judgment among the gods.” 1
“How long will you defend the unjust and show partiality to the wicked?” Selah

Defend the Weak

“Defend the weak and the fatherless; uphold the cause of the poor and the oppressed.” 2
“Rescue the weak and the needy; deliver them from the hand of the wicked.”

You will Die like Mortals

“I said, ‘You are gods; you are all sons of the Most High.’” 3
“But you will die like mere mortals; you will fall like every other ruler.”

Judge the Earth

“Rise up, O God, judge the earth, for all the nations are your inheritance.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Great Assembly (be-A-dath El): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Divine Jurisprudence." God (*Elohim*) stands (*Nitz-tzab*) in the "Congregation" of the "mighty" (*Elim/gods*). It is the "Uprooting of Autonomous Human Authority."

2 Defend the Weak (Shiph-thu Dal): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Judicial Duty." The "Dal" (*Weak*) and "Yathom" (*Fatherless*) are the "Litmus Test" of "Rooted Justice." It signifies "Protecting the Uprooted."

3 You are Gods (Elohim At-tem): This is a "Sarcastic Root" in the scrolls. While they hold "Divine Office" (*Sons of the Most High* - *Bene El-yon*), they lack "Divine Character." Because they "Understand Nothing" (*Verse 5*), the "Foundations of the Earth" (*Mo-se-de Eretz*) are "Shaken" (*Yim-mo-thu*). It is the "Uprooting of the Arrogant."

4 Judge the Earth (Shoph-tah ha-Aretz): The scrolls conclude with this "Global Imperative." God must "Rise" (*Qu-mah*) because the "Nations" (*Go-yim*) are His "Inheritance" (*Tin-chal*). It marks the transition to the "Conspiracy of Nations" in *Psalm 83*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Justice." All belong to You.
