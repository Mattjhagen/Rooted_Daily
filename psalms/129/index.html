Psalm 129

A song of ascents.

Oppressed from My Youth

“‘They have greatly oppressed me from my youth,’ let Israel say.” 1
“But they have not gained the victory over me.”

Furrows on the Back

“Plowmen have plowed my back and made their furrows long.” 2
“But the Lord is righteous; he has cut me free from the cords of the wicked.” 3

Grass on the Roof

“May they be like grass on the roof, which withers before it can grow.” 4
“A reaper cannot fill his hands with it, nor one who gathers fill his arms.”

Translation Notes

1 Oppressed from My Youth (Tze-ra-ru-ni min-Ne-u-ray): In the scrolls, this is the "Resilience Root." The "Youth" (*Ne-u-ray*) of Israel was a time of "Narrowness" (*Tzarar*). It is the "Root of National Survival."

2 Plowmen have Plowed (al-Gab-bi Cha-re-shu Cho-re-shim): The scrolls highlight the "Metaphorical Root of Pain." The "Back" (*Gab*) is treated like "Soil" for "Long Furrows" (*Ma-a-ni-tham*). It signify "Systemic Suffering."

3 Cut free from Cords (Qit-tztz A-bo-th Re-sha-im): The scrolls highlight the "Liberation Root." God "Cuts" (*Qit-tztz*) the "Thick Ropes" (*Aboth*) of the "Wicked" (*Resha-im*). It is the "Uprooting of Captivity."

4 Grass on the Roof (ke-Cha-tzir Gag-go-th): The scrolls highlight the "Futile Root of Hostility." Zion’s "Enemies" have no "Soil" (*Root*). They "Wither" (*Ya-besh*) before being "Pulled" (*Sha-laph*). It marks the transition to the "Depths" in *Psalm 130*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Unbreakable Spirit." They did not win.
