Psalm 20

For the director of music. A psalm of David.

The Sanctuary Help

“May the Lord answer you when you are in distress; may the name of the God of Jacob protect you.” 1
“May he send you help from the sanctuary and grant you support from Zion.”

The Banners of the Name

“May we shout for joy over your victory and lift up our banners in the name of our God.” 2

The Name Over the Chariots

“Some trust in chariots and some in horses, but we trust in the name of the Lord our God.” 3
“They are brought to their knees and fall, but we rise up and stand firm.” 4
“Lord, give victory to the king! Answer us when we call!” 5

Translation Notes

1 God of Jacob Protect you (Ye-sag-geb-kha): Literally "Set you on high." In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Safety." God’s "Name" (*Shem*) is a "High Place" (*Mis-gab*) where the enemy cannot reach. It is the "Uprooting of Vulnerability."

2 Lift up our Banners (Nid-gol): The scrolls highlight the "Identity of the Army." They are "Rooted" in the "Name" (*Shem*) of their God. To "Lift a Banner" (*Nid-gol*) is to declare a "New Allegiance."

3 Trust in Chariots... in Horses (E-leh be-Rekheb... be-Su-sim): This is the "Central Contrast of the Rooted." "Chariots" and "Horses" are "Material Roots of Power." They are "Temporary" and "Shakable." The "Name of the Lord" is the "Infinite Root." To "Trust" (*Naz-kir*) is to "Invoke/Remember."

4 Rise up and Stand Firm (Anachnu Qam-nu wa-Nith-odad): The scrolls highlight the "Result of the Root." While those on "Horses" are "Uprooted" (*Kar-e-u* - literally 'bowed/collapsed'), the "Trusting" ones "Arise" and are "Sustained" (*Nith-odad*). It marks the "Victory of Character" over "Technology."

5 Lord, give Victory (YHWH Hoshi-ah): The scrolls conclude with this "Urgent Cry." *Hoshi-ah* is the "Root of Salvation." It marks the transition from "Liturgy" to "Action." The "King" (*Melekh*) is the "Mediator," but the "Lord" is the "Source." It is the "Rooted Daily" prayer for "Divine Help."
