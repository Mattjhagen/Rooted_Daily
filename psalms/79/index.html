Psalm 79

A psalm of Asaph.

The Invaded Inheritance

“O God, the nations have invaded your inheritance; they have defiled your holy temple.” 1
“They have reduced Jerusalem to rubble.”

Food for the Birds

“They have left the dead bodies of your servants as food for the birds of the sky.” 2
“We are objects of contempt to our neighbors, of scorn and derision to those around us.”

Quickly Meet Us

“May your mercy come quickly to meet us, for we are in desperate need.” 3

Groans of the Bound

“May the groans of the prisoners come before you; by your strong arm preserve those condemned to die.” 4
“Then we your people, the sheep of your pasture, will praise you forever.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Invaded your Inheritance (Ba-u Go-yim be-Na-cha-la-the-kha): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Sacrilegious Entry." The "Inheritance" (*Na-cha-lah*) is "Defiled" (*Tim-u*). Jerusalem is "Rubble" (*I-yim*). It is the "Uprooting of the Holy City."

2 Food for the Birds (Ma-a-khal le-Oph ha-Sha-ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Ultimate Indignity Root." To be "Unburied" was the "Curse of Uprooting." The "Blood" (*Da-mam*) is "Poured Out" (*Sha-phe-khu*) like "Water" (*Verse 3*).

3 Mercy come Quickly (ye-Qa-de-mu-nu Ra-cha-me-kha): Literally "May your tender-mercies anticipate us." The scrolls highlight the "Urgency of Grace." Israel is "Desperate" (*Dal-lo-nu* - literally 'thinned/weakened'). It is the "Uprooting of Past Sins."

4 Groans of the Prisoners (En-qath A-sir): The scrolls highlight the "Audible Pain Root." God is asked to "Preserve" (*Ho-ther*) the "Children of Death" (*Be-ne Tha-mu-thah*). It signifies "Redemptive Rescue."

5 Sheep of your Pasture (Tzon Mar-i-the-kha): The scrolls conclude with this "Identity Root." Despite the "Invaded Rubble," the "Relationship" remains. It marks the transition to the "Vine of Israel" in *Psalm 80*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Intergenerational Praise." We are still Your sheep.
