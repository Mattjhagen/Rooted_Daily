Psalm 25

Of David.

The Lifted Soul

“In you, Lord my God, I put my trust; to you I lift up my soul.” 1

The Ways and Paths

“Show me your ways, Lord, teach me your paths.” 2
“Guide me in your truth and teach me, for you are God my Savior, and my hope is in you all day long.”

The Memory of Mercy

“Remember, Lord, your great mercy and love, for they are from of old.”
“Do not remember the sins of my youth and my rebellious ways; according to your love remember me.” 3

The Eyes on the Lord

“My eyes are ever on the Lord, for only he will release my feet from the snare.” 4
“Redeem Israel, O God, from all their troubles!” 5

Translation Notes

1 I lift up my Soul (E-ssa Naphe-shi): In the scrolls, this is the "Acrostic Opening" (*Aleph*). To "Lift" the soul is to "Uproot" it from "Earthly Anxiety." David is "Rooting" his "Identity" in "God’s Character." It is the "Rooted Daily" morning posture.

2 Ways and Paths (Derakh-ekha... Orecho-thekha): The scrolls highlight the "Request for Orientation." David asks to be "Rooted" in "Truth" (*Emeth*). He is "Uprooting" his "Own Intuition" and seeking "Divine Instruction."

3 Sins of my Youth (Chat-toth Ne-u-ray): Literally in the scrolls, "Sins of my early-growth." David asks God to "Uproot" the "Memory" of his "Failures." He wants his "Relationship" to be "Rooted" in God’s "Unfailing Love" (*Chesed*) rather than his "Record."

4 Release my Feet from the Snare (Yo-tzi me-Resheth Rag-lay): This is a "Vivid Root of Liberation." A "Snare" (*Resheth*) "Uproots" a man by "Trapping" his "Feet." Only God can "Exert" the "Pull" that "Releases" the "Rooted." It marks the "Confidence" of the "Petitioner."

5 Redeem Israel (Phed-eh Elohim eth-Yisrael): The scrolls conclude with this "Corporate Root." David moves from "Personal Crisis" to "National Hope." It marks the transition from "One Man’s Prayer" to the "People’s Salvation." It is the "Rooted Daily" intercession for the "Community of Faith."
