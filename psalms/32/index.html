Psalm 32

Of David. A maskil.

The Blessed Forgiven

“Blessed is the one whose transgressions are forgiven, whose sins are covered.” 1
“Blessed is the one whose sin the Lord does not count against them and in whose spirit is no deceit.”

The Heavy Hand

“When I kept silent, my bones wasted away through my groaning all day long.” 2
“For day and night your hand was heavy on me; my strength was sapped as in the heat of summer.” Selah

The Acknowledgement

“Then I acknowledged my sin to you... and you forgave the guilt of my sin.” 3 Selah

The Song Surround

“You are my hiding place; you will protect me from trouble and surround me with songs of deliverance.” 4
“I will instruct you and teach you in the way you should go; I will counsel you with my loving eye on you.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Blessed (Ash-re): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Happiness." It implies a "State of Flourishing." Forgiveness (*Nes-uy*) is the "Uprooting of Guilt." It signifies that "Rootedness" begins with "Reconciliation."

2 Bones wasted away (Ba-lu Atz-ma-thay): The scrolls highlight the "Physical Cost of Secrecy." Unconfessed sin is an "Internal Rot." The "Hand of God" (*Yad-kha*) feels "Heavy" (*Thikh-bad*) because it is "Pressing" the "Root" to "Confess." It is the "Uprooting of the Heat" (*Verse 4*).

3 Acknowledged my Sin (O-di-a-kha): This is the "Avent of Healing." Confession is the "Uprooting of the Lie." The "Guilt" (*A-won*) is "Removed." It is the "Root of Transparency."

4 Surround me with Songs of Deliverance (Ran-ne Phalet Tha-sobe-beni): Literally in the scrolls, "Shouts of escape will encompass me." This is a "Stunning Image of Protection." God doesn't just "Give" deliverance; He "Surrounds" (*Tha-sobe-beni*) His servant with the "Sound" of it. It is the "Root of Security."

5 Counsel you with my Loving Eye (I-atzah Ale-kha E-ni): The scrolls conclude with the "Gentle Root of Guidance." God moves from the "Heavy Hand" to the "Loving Eye." It marks the "Intimacy of the Forgiven." We are not "Horses or Mules" (*Verse 9*) who need "Bit and Bridle." We are "Instructed" (*As-kile-kha*) by "Presence." It marks the transition to the "Joyful Shout" of *Psalm 33*. It is the "Rooted Daily" result of "Mercy."
