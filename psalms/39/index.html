Psalm 39

For the director of music. For Jeduthun. A psalm of David.

The Muzzled Mouth

“I said, ‘I will watch my ways and keep my tongue from sin; I will put a muzzle on my mouth as long as the wicked are in my presence.’” 1
“My heart grew hot within me; while I meditated, the fire burned; then I spoke with my tongue.” 2

The Handbreadth of Days

“Show me, Lord, my life’s end and the number of my days; let me know how fleeting my life is.” 3
“You have made my days a mere handbreadth; the span of my years is as nothing before you. Everyone is but a breath.” 4 Selah
“Surely everyone goes around like a phantom; in vain they rush about, heaping up wealth without knowing who will get it.”

The Eternal Hope

“But now, Lord, what do I look for? My hope is in you.” 5
“For I dwell with you as a foreigner, a stranger, as all my ancestors were.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Muzzle on my Mouth (Mach-som le-Phi): In the scrolls, "Muzzle" (*Mach-som*) is the "Root of Self-Restraint." David is "Rooting" his "Speech" in "Caution." He is "Uprooting" the "Impulse" to complain before the "Wicked."

2 The Fire Burned (Ba-arah Esh): The scrolls highlight the "Internal Pressure of Truth." "Silence" (*Du-mi-yah*) leads to "Distress" (*Verse 2*). The "Meditation" (*Hag-yig*) creates a "Spiritual Combustion." David must "Speak" to "Release" the "Rooted Fire."

3 How Fleeting my Life is (Mah-Cha-del Ani): Literally "How frail/ceasing I am." The scrolls highlight the "Uprooting of Human Illusion." We think we are "Solid," but we are "Ceasing." It is the "Root of Humility."

4 Everyone is but a Breath (Kol-Hebel Kol-Adam): Literally "All-breath all-man." *Hebel* is the same word used in *Ecclesiastes*. It is a "Vapor." It is the "Root of Transience." Even at his "Best" (*Nit-tzab*), man is a "Vapor."

5 My Hope is in You (To-chal-thi Le-kha Hi): This is the "Shift in Root." David "Uproots" his "Expectation" from "Wealth" (*Verse 6*) and "Striving" and "Roots" it in "YHWH." It is the "Only Permanent Root."

6 Foreigner, a Stranger (Ger we-Tho-shab): The scrolls conclude with this "Identity Root." We do not "Belong" to the "Earth." We are "Guests" of God. It marks the transition to the "Patience" of *Psalm 40*. It is the "Rooted Daily" reminder that we are "Passing Through."
