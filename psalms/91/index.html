Psalm 91

The Shadow of the Almighty

“Whoever dwells in the shelter of the Most High will rest in the shadow of the Almighty.” 1
“I will say of the Lord, ‘He is my refuge and my fortress, my God, in whom I trust.’”

Feathers and Wings

“He will cover you with his feathers, and under his wings you will find refuge.” 2
“A thousand may fall at your side, but it will not come near you.” 3

Command of Angels

“For he will command his angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways.” 4
“You will tread on the lion and the cobra; you will trample the serpent.”

The Divine Response

“‘Because he loves me,’ says the Lord, ‘I will rescue him; I will protect him.’” 5
“With long life I will satisfy him and show him my salvation.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Shelter of the Most High (Se-ther El-yon): In the scrolls, "Shelter" (*Sether*) is the "Secret Root." To "Dwell" (*Yo-sheb*) in God’s "Secret Place" is to be "Rooted" in "Inaccessible Safety." The "Shadow" (*Tzel*) of the "Almighty" (*Shaddai*) is the "Ultimate Umbrella." It is the "Uprooting of Exposure."

2 Cover you with His Feathers (be-Eb-ra-tho Ya-sek-lakh): The scrolls highlight the "Nurturing Root of Protection." God is depicted as a "Parent Bird" (*He-wings* - *Ke-na-pha-yim*). It signify "Covenantal Intimacy."

3 It will not Come Near You (E-le-kha lo Yig-gash): This is the "Root of Divine Immunity." While "Chaos" (*Night-terror/Arrow/Pestilence*) "Uproots" thousands, the one in the "Shelter" remains "Rooted." It is the "Exemption Root."

4 Command His Angels (Mal-a-khaw Ye-tzaw-weh-lakh): The scrolls highlight the "Celestial Administration Root." God "Dispatches" (*Command*) His "Messengers" (*Mal-a-khim*) to "Guard" (*Lish-mar-kha*) the "Steps." In the New Testament, Satan quoted this to Jesus (Matthew 4:6). It is the "Root of Active Agency."

5 Because He Loves Me (ki-Bi Cha-shaq): Literally "Because he has set his desire on me." The scrolls highlight the "Reciprocal Root of Affection." Rescue (*A-phal-le-te-hu*) is the "Response" to "Attachment." It is the "Uprooting of Distance."

6 Show him my Salvation (we-Ar-e-hu bi-Ye-shu-a-thi): The scrolls conclude with this "Visual Revelation Root." To "See" (*A-re-hu*) the "Salvation" (*Ye-shu-ah*) is the "Ultimate Satisfaction" (*As-bi-e-hu*). It marks the transition to the "Song of the Sabbath" in *Psalm 92*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Invulnerability." I am safe in You.
