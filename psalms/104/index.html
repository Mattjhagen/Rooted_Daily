Psalm 104

Clothed with Splendor

“Praise the Lord, my soul. Lord my God, you are very great; you are clothed with splendor and majesty.” 1
“The Lord wraps himself in light as with a garment.”

The Chariot of Clouds

“He lays the beams of his upper chambers on their waters. He makes the clouds his chariot.” 2
“He makes winds his messengers, flames of fire his servants.”

The Flourishing Earth

“He makes grass grow for the cattle, and plants for people to cultivate.” 3
“Wine that gladdens human hearts, oil to make their faces shine.”

Leviathan at Play

“There is the sea, vast and spacious, teeming with creatures beyond number.”
“There the ships go to and fro, and Leviathan, which you formed to frolic there.” 4

The Breath of Life

“When you send your Spirit, they are created, and you renew the face of the ground.” 5
“I will sing to the Lord all my life; I will sing praise to my God as long as I live.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Clothed with Splendor (Hod we-Ha-dar La-bash-ta): In the scrolls, this is the "Imperial Root of Creation." God "Wears" (*Labashta*) His own attributes as "Regalia." "Light" (*Or*) is the "Garment" (*Sal-mah*). It is the "Root of Divine Visibility."

2 Upper Chambers (A-li-yo-thaw): The scrolls highlight the "Architectural Root." God "Lays the Beams" (*ham-Ma-qe-reh*) of His palace in the "Waters" (*Ma-yim*) above. It signifies "Heavenly Sovereignty."

3 Plants for People (A-bo-dath ha-Adam): Literally "The service/work of man." The scrolls highlight the "Nutritional Root." God provides for "Cattle" (*Be-he-mah*) and "Man" (*Adam*) through the "Earth’s Yield" (*Bread/Wine/Oil*). It signifies "Universal Provision."

4 Leviathan to Frolic (Li-wya-than... le-Sa-chaq): The scrolls highlight the "Playful Root of Power." The "Chaos Monster" (*Leviathan*), feared by men, is a "Pet" to God, created to "Play" (*Sachaq*). It is the "Uprooting of Terror."

5 Send your Spirit (The-shal-lach Ru-cha-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Biological Root of Renewal." "Spirit" (*Ruach*) is the "Agent of Creation" (*Yib-ba-re-un*) and "Renewal" (*Chad-desh*). It signifies "Divine Vitality."

6 Sing as long as I live (A-zam-me-rah le-Elohay be-O-di): The scrolls conclude with the "Personal Doxology Root." The psalmist joins the "Cosmic Song." It marks the transition to the "History of the Covenant" in *Psalm 105*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Life." Praise the Lord, my soul.
