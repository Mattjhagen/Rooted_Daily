Psalm 114

Judah his Sanctuary

“When Israel came out of Egypt, Jacob from a people of foreign tongue.” 1
“Judah became God’s sanctuary, Israel his dominion.”

The Sea Fled

“The sea looked and fled, the Jordan turned back.” 2
“The mountains leaped like rams, the hills like lambs.” 3

Tremble, Earth

“Tremble, earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob.” 4

Springs of Water

“Who turned the rock into a pool, the hard rock into springs of water.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Foreign Tongue (Am Lo-ez): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Cultural Separation." "Egypt" (*Mitz-ra-yim*) was a place of "Confusion." The "Exodus" (*be-Tzeth*) was an "Uprooting" from the "Alien Language." It is the "Root of Identity."

2 Sea... Fled (ha-Yam Ra-ah way-Ya-nos): The scrolls highlight the "Reactive Root of Nature." The "Sea" (*Yam*) is depicted with "Conscious Vision" (*Ra-ah*). Its "Flight" (*Ya-nos*) signifies "Absolute Submission" to the "Creator’s Will." It is the "Uprooting of Physical Obstacles."

3 Leaped like Rams (Ra-qe-du khe-E-lim): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Terrestrial Jubilee." The "Mountains" (*Har-im*) "Dance" (*Skip/Leap*). It signifies "Cosmic Participation" in the "Deliverance."

4 Presence of the Lord (mil-li-phe-ne A-don): Literally "From before the face of the Master." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Divine Manifestation." Nature "Trembles" (*Chu-li*) because the "Face" (*Panim*) is "Revealed." It signify "Theophanic Power."

5 Hard Rock into Springs (Chal-la-mish le-Ma-ye-no Ma-yim): The scrolls conclude with the "Fluidity Root." God "Inverts" the "Nature of Matter." The "Hardness" (*Chal-la-mish*) becomes "Springs" (*Ma-ye-no*). It marks the transition to the "Glory of the Name" in *Psalm 115*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Sovereignty." The earth trembles.
