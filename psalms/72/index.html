Psalm 72

Of Solomon.

Endow the King

“Endow the king with your justice, O God, the royal son with your righteousness.” 1
“May he judge your people in righteousness, your afflicted ones with justice.”

Rain on a Mown Field

“May he be like rain falling on a mown field, like showers watering the earth.” 2
“May he rule from sea to sea and from the River to the ends of the earth.”

The Needy who Cry Out

“For he will deliver the needy who cry out, the afflicted who have no one to help.” 3
“All nations will be blessed through him, and they will call him blessed.”

His Glory Fills

“Praise be to the Lord God, the God of Israel, who alone does marvelous deeds.” 4
“May his glorious name be praised forever; may the whole earth be filled with his glory.”
“This concludes the prayers of David son of Jesse.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Endow the King with your Justice (Elohim Mish-pa-te-kha le-Melekh Ten): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Delegated Sovereignty." The King is only a "Vessel" for "Divine Justice" (*Mishpat*). It is the "Uprooting of Autocracy."

2 Rain on a Mown Field (ke-Ma-tar al-Gez): This is a "Stunning Pastoral Root." A "Mown Field" (*Gez*) is "Vulnerable" and "Thirsty." The King’s "Influence" is like "Life-giving Water." The "Dominion" (*Verse 8*) is "Universal." It is the "Root of Flourishing."

3 He will Deliver the Needy (Ya-tzil Eb-yon): The scrolls highlight the "Merciful Root of Power." True "Majesty" is defined by "Compassion" for the "Afflicted" (*Ani*). He "Redeems" (*Yiph-deh*) their "Soul" (*Naphesh*) from "Oppression" (*Verse 14*). It signifies "Redemptive Governance."

4 Who alone does Marvelous Deeds (O-seh Nipha-la-oth le-Bad-do): The scrolls conclude Book II with this "Superlative Doxology." God is the "Only Root" of "Wonder" (*Nipha-la-oth*). It is the "Amen and Amen" (*Verse 19*).

5 Concludes the Prayers of David (Kal-lu The-phil-loth Da-wid): This is a "Structural Note" in the scrolls. While David appears in later books, this marks the "End of the Primary Collection." It signifies the "Completion of the Second Major Root." It marks the transition to the "Book of Asaph" in *Psalm 73*. It is the "Rooted Daily" finale of the "Davidic Cycle." The King is here.
