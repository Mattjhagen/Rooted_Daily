Psalm 144

Of David.

My Rock and Fortress

“Praise be to the Lord my Rock, who trains my hands for war, my fingers for battle.” 1

A Fleeting Shadow

“Lord, what are human beings that you care for them?”
“Mortals are like a breath; their days are like a fleeting shadow.” 2

Part Your Heavens

“Part your heavens, Lord, and come down; touch the mountains, so that they smoke.” 3
“Reach down your hand from on high; deliver me and rescue me from the mighty waters.”

Pillars of a Palace

“Then our sons in their youth will be like well-nurtured plants.”
“Our daughters like pillars carved to adorn a palace.” 4
“Blessed is the people whose God is the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 My Rock (Tzu-ri): In the scrolls, "Tzur" is the "Unshakable Root of Stability." God "Trains" (*ha-me-lam-med*) the "Hands" (*Ya-day*) for "War" (*Mil-cha-mah*). It signifies "Divine Empowerment for Struggle."

2 Fleeting Shadow (ke-Tzel o-ber): The scrolls highlight the "Transience Root." Mortal life (*Adam*) is compared to "He-bel" (*Breath*) and a "Passing Shadow." It is the "Uprooting of Human Self-Importance."

3 Part your Heavens (Hat Sha-ma-ye-kha): Literally "Bend Your Heavens." The scrolls highlight the "Intervention Root." The "Mountains" (*Har-im*) "Smoke" (*Ye-e-sha-nu*) at His "Touch." It signify "Cosmic Authority."

4 Pillars carved (Za-wi-yoth me-chut-ta-both): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Beauty Root." The "Daughters" (*Be-no-the-nu*) are the "Elegant Cornerstones" (*Za-wi-yoth*) of the home. It signifies "Social and Familial Flourishing."

5 God is the Lord (YHWH E-lo-haw): The scrolls conclude with this "Covenantal Beatitude." "Blessed" (*Ash-re*) is the "People" (*Ha-Am*) "Rooted" in the "True Elohim." It marks the transition to the "Alphabet of Exaltation" in *Psalm 145*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Blessed Kingdom." You are our God.
