Psalm 77

For the director of music. For Jeduthun. A psalm of Asaph.

The Untiring Hands

“I cried out to God for help; I cried out to God to hear me.”
“At night I stretched out untiring hands, and I would not be comforted.” 1

Has Love Vanished?

“Will the Lord reject forever? Will he never show his favor again?” 2
“Has his unfailing love vanished forever? Has his promise failed for all time?”

The Deeds of Old

“I will remember the deeds of the Lord; yes, I will remember your miracles of long ago.” 3
“Your ways, God, are holy. What god is as great as our God?”

Unseen Footprints

“The waters saw you, God, the waters saw you and writhed; the very depths were shaken.” 4
“Your path led through the sea, your way through the mighty waters, though your footprints were not seen.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Untiring Hands (Ya-di Lay-lah Nig-ge-rah): Literally "My hand by night was poured out." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Incessant Prayer." Asaph is "Uprooted" from "Rest." His "Soul" (*Naphesh*) "Refused" (*Me-a-nah*) comfort (*Hin-na-chem*). It is the "Night of the Soul."

2 Reject Forever (ha-le-Olamim Yiz-nach Adonai): The scrolls highlight the "Theological Crisis Root." The "Questions" (*Verses 7-9*) target the "Covenant Root." Has God "Forgotten" (*Sha-khakh*) to be "Gracious" (*Chan-noth*)? It signifies the "Uprooting of Certainty."

3 I will Remember (Ez-ko-rah): This is the "Root of Recovery." The word "Remember" (*Za-khar*) is repeated. Faith is "Re-Rooted" in "History" (*Mi-qe-dem*). It is the "Uprooting of Amnesia."

4 Waters Saw You (Ra-u-kha Ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Cosmic Root of Awe." The "Depths" (*Te-ho-moth*) "Writhed" (*Ya-chi-lu*) before the Creator. It is the "Exodus Root."

5 Footprints were not Seen (Iq-be-the-kha lo No-da-u): The scrolls conclude with this "Paradoxical Root." God’s "Path" (*Dar-ke-kha*) is "Real," but His "Footprints" (*Iq-bah*) are "Invisible." He leads His "People" (*Am-me-kha*) like a "Flock" (*Tzon*). It marks the transition to the "Historical Narrative" of *Psalm 78*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Unseen Path." You are leading us.
