Psalm 28

Of David.

The Rock’s Silence

“To you, Lord, I call; you are my Rock, do not turn a deaf ear to me.” 1
“For if you remain silent, I will be like those who go down to the pit.”

Toward the Holy Place

“Hear my cry for mercy as I call to you for help, as I lift up my hands toward your Most Holy Place.” 2

Strength and Shield

“The Lord is my strength and my shield; my heart trusts in him, and he helps me.” 3

The Carrying Shepherd

“Save your people and bless your inheritance; be their shepherd and carry them forever.” 4
“Support them as a father supports his child.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Do not turn a deaf ear (Al-the-cheresh mi-menni): Literally in the scrolls, "Do not be deaf from me." David is "Rooting" his "Hope" in "Divine Attention." If the "Rock" (*Tzur*) is "Silent," the "Environment" becomes a "Pit" (*Bor*). It is the "Uprooting of Connection."

2 Hands toward your Most Holy Place (Yaday el-De-bir Qod-shekha): The scrolls highlight the "Physical Posture of Prayer." The "Hands" (*Yaday*) are "Lifted" toward the "Inmost Sanctuary" (*Debir*). It is the "Root of Direction." It signifies that "Rootedness" is "Oriented" toward "Presence."

3 My Heart Trusts in Him (bo Ba-tach Libbi): The scrolls highlight the "Shift in State." David moves from "Calling" (*Verse 1*) to "Praising" (*Verse 6*). His "Heart" (*Leb*) is "Rooted" in "Trust." He describes God as a "Shield" (*Maginni*). It is the "Internal Root of Help."

4 Be their Shepherd and Carry them (u-Re-em we-Nas-se-em): Literally in the scrolls, "Shepherd them and lift/carry them." This is a "Beautiful Pastoral Root." God doesn't just "Watch" the people; He "Carries" (*Nas-se-em*) them. It signifies that "Rootedness" involves being "Lifted" by the "Stronger Root."

5 Forever (Ad-ha-Olam): The scrolls conclude with this "Eternal Continuity." The "Shepherding" of God is not "Temporary." It is "Permanent." It marks the transition from "Private Crisis" to "Public Blessing." It is the "Rooted Daily" assurance. He carries us today, and He carries us forever.
