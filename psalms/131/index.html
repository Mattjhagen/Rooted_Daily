Psalm 131

A song of ascents. Of David.

Not Proud

“My heart is not proud, Lord, my eyes are not haughty.” 1
“I do not concern myself with great matters or things too wonderful for me.” 2

Like a Weaned Child

“I have calmed and quieted myself, I am like a weaned child with its mother.” 3
“Israel, put your hope in the Lord both now and forevermore.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Not Proud (lo-Ga-bah Lib-bi): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Lowliness." The "Heart" (*Libbi*) and "Eyes" (*Eynay*) are not "Lofty" (*Ga-bah*). It is the "Uprooting of Ambition."

2 Matters too Wonderful (ni-phla-oth mim-men-ni): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Intellectual Restraint." The pilgrim "Uproots" himself from "Complexity" (*Niph-la-oth* - 'wonders/difficult things') to find "Simplicity." It signifies "Spiritual Focus."

3 Weaned Child (k-Ga-mul a-ley Im-mo): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Contentment." A "Weaned" (*Gamul*) child does not "Cry" for milk; it is "Quiet" (*Shaw-wi-thi*) simply to be in the "Mother’s Presence." It is the "Uprooting of Restless Desire."

4 Hope in the Lord (Ya-chel Yisra-el el-YHWH): The scrolls conclude with this "Communal Hope." Humility is not "Apathy," but "Rooted Trust" (*Ya-chel*). It marks the transition to the "Royal Covenant" in *Psalm 132*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Stillness." My soul is content.
