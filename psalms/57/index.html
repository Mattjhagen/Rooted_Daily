Psalm 57

For the director of music. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” Of David. A miktam. When he had fled from Saul into the cave.

The Shadow of Wings

“Have mercy on me, my God, have mercy on me, for in you I take refuge.” 1
“I will take refuge in the shadow of your wings until the disaster has passed.”

Ravenous Beasts

“I am in the midst of lions; I am forced to dwell among ravenous beasts.” 2

Exalted Above the Heavens

“Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; let your glory be over all the earth.” 3

The Awakening Dawn

“My heart, O God, is steadfast, my heart is steadfast; I will sing and make music.” 4
“Awake, my soul! Awake, harp and lyre! I will awaken the dawn.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Refuge in the Shadow of your Wings (be-Tzel Ke-na-phe-kha): In the scrolls, this is the "Ultimate Interior Root." David is "Rooted" in a "Cave" (*Me-a-rah*) physically, but "Rooted" in "Divine Protection" spiritually. It is the "Uprooting of External Danger."

2 Ravenous Beasts (Lo-ha-thim): Literally "Those who lick up/devour." The scrolls highlight the "Vulnerability of the Fugitive." David is "Uprooted" from the "Throne" and surrounded by "Lions" (*Leba-im*). It is the "Root of Crisis."

3 Be Exalted, O God (Ru-mah al-ha-Sha-ma-yim): This is the "Acrostic-like Refrain" of *Psalm 57*. David moves from "Private Shelter" to "Global Glory" (*Khabod*). It is the "Root of Perspective." God’s "Greatness" (*Verse 10*) "Uproots" the "Smallness" of the cave.

4 My Heart is Steadfast (Na-khon Libbi): Literally "My heart is prepared/fixed." The scrolls highlight the "Un-shakable Interior Root." Even while "Hiding," David is "Rooted" in "Worship."

5 I will Awaken the Dawn (A-irah Sha-char): The scrolls conclude with this "Creative Root of Hope." David doesn't wait for the sun to "Wake" him; he "Wakes" the "Sun" with his "Praise." It marks the transition from "Night-Grief" to "Day-Victory." It marks the transition to the "Judgment on Rulers" in *Psalm 58*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Cavedwelling King."
