Psalm 97

Let the Earth be Glad

“The Lord reigns, let the earth be glad; let the distant shores rejoice.” 1

Lightning and Fire

“Clouds and thick darkness surround him; righteousness and justice are the foundation of his throne.” 2
“His lightning lights up the world; the earth sees and trembles.” 3
“The mountains melt like wax before the Lord.”
“All who worship images are put to shame... worship him, all you gods!” 4

Light for the Righteous

“Let those who love the Lord hate evil, for he guards the lives of his faithful ones.”
“Light is shed on the righteous and joy on the upright in heart.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Distant Shores Rejoice (Yis-me-chu I-yim Rab-bim): Literally "Let the many islands rejoice." In the scrolls, the "Reign" (*Ma-lakh*) is "Universal." Even the "Farthest Regions" (*Islands/Shores*) are "Rooted" in the "Joy" (*Sim-khah*) of His sovereignty.

2 Clouds and Thick Darkness (A-nan we-Ara-phel): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Divine Mystery." God’s "Presence" is "Obscured" (*Clouds/Darkness*) but His "Throne" (*Kisse*) is "Rooted" in "Tzedeq" (*Righteousness*) and "Mishpat" (*Justice*). It signifies "Hidden but Stable."

3 Lightning lights up (He-i-ru Be-ra-qaw): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Instant Revelation." God’s "Judgment" is like "Lightning" (*Be-ra-qaw*) that "Illuminates" (*He-i-ru*) the "Inhabited World" (*Te-bel*). Mountains (*Har-im*) "Melt" (*Na-mas-su*) like "Wax" (*Donag*). It signifies the "Uprooting of Earthly Obstacles."

4 Worship him, all you gods (Hish-tha-cha-wu-lo Kol-Elohim): The scrolls highlight the "Supreme Root." Every "Lesser Authority" (*Elohim/gods/idols*) must "Bow" (*Rooted Prostration*). It signifies the "End of Idolatry."

5 Light is Shed (Or Za-ru-a): Literally "Light is sown." The scrolls highlight the "Agricultural Root of Joy." "Light" (*Or*) is a "Seed" (*Za-ru-a*) that "Flourishes" for the "Righteous" (*Tzaddiq*). It marks the transition to the "New Song of Victory" in *Psalm 98*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Illuminated Joy." Hate evil; love the light.
