Psalm 71

The Lifelong Refuge

“In you, Lord, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame.”
“For you have been my hope, Sovereign Lord, my confidence since my youth.” 1

When Strength is Gone

“Do not cast me away when I am old; do not forsake me when my strength is gone.” 2
“Even when I am old and gray, do not forsake me, my God, till I declare your power to the next generation.” 3

More and More

“As for me, I will always have hope; I will praise you more and more.” 4
“My mouth will tell of your righteous deeds... though I know not its full measure.”

Restore My Life

“Though you have made me see troubles, many and bitter, you will restore my life again.” 5
“My tongue will tell of your righteous acts all day long.”

Translation Notes

1 Confidence since my Youth (Mib-ta-chi min-Ne-u-ray): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Consistency." The psalmist has been "Rooted" in God from "Conception" (*Verse 6*). It is the "Uprooting of Temporary Faith."

2 Strength is Gone (ki-kheloth Ko-chi): Literally "At the failing of my power." The scrolls highlight the "Vulnerability of the Elder." While the "Physical Root" weakens, the "Spiritual Root" must "Deepen." It is the "Prayer against Abandonment."

3 Old and Gray (ad-Ziq-nah we-Se-bah): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Legacy." "Gray hair" (*Sebah*) is seen as a "Crown" of the "Declaration" (*Neg-gid*). The purpose of "Survival" is to "Declare your Power" (*Zero-akha* - literally 'your arm') to the "Next Generation."

4 Praise you More and More (we-Ho-saph-thi al-Kol-The-hil-la-the-kha): Literally "I will add upon all your praise." The scrolls highlight the "Cumulative Root of Worship." As life "Diminishes," praise "Increases." It is the "Uprooting of Cynicism."

5 Restore my Life again (Tha-shub The-cha-ye-nu): Literally "You will return and cause us to live." The scrolls conclude with this "Resurrection Root." Out of "Bitter Troubles" (*Tza-roth Ra-oth*), God "Roots" the "Soul" in "Greatness" (*Verse 21*). It marks the transition to the "Royal Prayer" of *Psalm 72*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Faithful Veteran." I will always have hope.
