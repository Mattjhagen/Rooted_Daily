Psalm 105

Tell of His Wonderful Acts

“Give praise to the Lord, proclaim his name; make known among the nations what he has done.” 1
“Glory in his holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the Lord rejoice.”

Covenant Remembered

“He remembers his covenant forever, the promise he made, for a thousand generations.” 2

The Testing of Joseph

“He sent a man before them—Joseph, sold as a slave.”
“Until what he foretold came to pass, until the word of the Lord proved him true.” 3

The Gushing Rock

“He spread out a cloud as a covering, and a fire to give light at night.”
“He gave them the bread of heaven.”
“He opened the rock, and water gushed out; it flowed like a river in the desert.” 4

Shouts of Joy

“He brought out his people with rejoicing, his chosen ones with shouts of joy.” 5
“Praise the Lord.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Make known among the Nations (Ho-di-u ba-Am-mim): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Proclamation." Israel’s "History" is a "Testimony" for the "World" (*Ammim*). It is the "Uprooting of Secretive Faith."

2 He Remembers His Covenant (Za-khar le-Olam be-Ri-tho): The scrolls highlight the "Unbroken Memory Root." God’s "Promise" (*Da-bar*) is "Rooted" in "Eternity" (*le-Olam*). It signifies "Covenantal Persistence."

3 Word of the Lord Proved Him (Im-rath YHWH Tze-ra-pha-thu): Literally "The word of YHWH refined/melted him." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Spiritual Refinement." Joseph’s "Prison" was his "Forge." It signifies "Preparatory Suffering."

4 Water Gushed Out (Ya-zu-bu Ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Miraculous Fluidity Root." In the "Arid Desert" (*Tzi-yah*), God "Roots" a "River" (*Na-har*). It is the "Uprooting of Dehydration."

5 Shouts of Joy (be-Rin-nah): The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Root of Celebration." The "Exodus" (*He-tzi*) is "Noisy." It signifies the "Redemptive Jubilee."

6 Praise the Lord (Hal-le-lu-Yah): The scrolls conclude with this "Imperative Hallelujah." It marks the transition to the "Confession of Sins" in *Psalm 106*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Covenantal History." He remembers forever.
