Psalm 102

A prayer of an afflicted person who has grown weak and pours out a lament before the Lord.

The Cry for Help

“Hear my prayer, Lord; let my cry for help come to you.” 1
“Do not hide your face from me when I am in distress.”

Blighted Like Grass

“For my days vanish like smoke; my bones burn like glowing embers.”
“My heart is blighted and withered like grass; I forget to eat my food.” 2
“I have become like a bird alone on a roof.”

Enthroned Forever

“But you, Lord, sit enthroned forever; your renown endures through all generations.” 3
“Let this be written for a future generation, that a people not yet created may praise the Lord.” 4

You Remain

“In the beginning you laid the foundations of the earth, and the heavens are the work of your hands.” 5
“But you remain the same, and your years will never end.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Hear my Prayer (Shi-me-ah The-phil-la-thi): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Afflicted Appeal." The "Cry" (*Shav-a-thi*) is the "Audible Uprooting" of the soul’s pain. It is the "Penitential Root."

2 Blighted and Withered (Hu-kak ka-Eseb wa-Yibash): Literally "Struck like grass and dried up." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Frailty." To "Forget to eat" (*Sha-khakh-thi mi-Akhol*) is the "Ultimate Symptom of Uprooting." It signify "Total Physical Collapse."

3 Renown endures (Zikh-re-kha le-Dor wa-Dor): Literally "Your memorial/remembrance from generation to generation." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Divine Continuity." While the person "Washes away" (*Verse 11*), God is "Stable" (*Yo-sheb*). It is the "Root of Eternal Perspective."

4 Future Generation (Dor A-cha-ron): The scrolls highlight the "Intergenerational Root of Hope." The suffering of today is "Recorded" (*Tik-ka-theb*) to "Root" the "Faith" of those "Not yet Created" (*Am Nib-ra*). It signifies "Redemptive Legacy."

5 Foundations of the Earth (ha-Aretz Ya-sad-tha): The scrolls highlight the "Creator Root." God "Established" (*Ya-sad*) the "Beginning" (*Le-pha-nim*). It signifies "Unshakable Origins."

6 You remain the same (we-At-tah-Hu): Literally "But You are He." The scrolls conclude with this "Identity Root." While the "Heavens" (*Sha-ma-yim*) "Wear out like a garment" (*Verse 26*), God is "Constant." It marks the transition to the "Praise of the Soul" in *Psalm 103*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Immutability." You are always the same. We will live in Your presence.
