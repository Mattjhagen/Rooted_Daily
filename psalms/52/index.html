Psalm 52

For the director of music. A maskil of David. When Doeg the Edomite had gone to Saul and told him: “David has gone to the house of Ahimelek.”

The Boasting Hero

“Why do you boast of evil, you mighty hero? Why do you boast all day long, you who are a disgrace in the eyes of God?” 1
“You plot destruction; your tongue is like a sharpened razor.”

The Snatching Away

“Surely God will bring you down to everlasting ruin; he will snatch you up and pluck you from your tent; he will uproot you from the land of the living.” 2 Selah

The Olive Tree

“But I am like an olive tree flourishing in the house of God; I trust in God’s unfailing love for ever and ever.” 3
“For what you have done I will always praise you in the presence of your faithful people. And I will hope in your name, for your name is good.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Why do you Boast of Evil (Mah-Thith-hal-lel be-Ra-ah): In the scrolls, "Boasting" (*Thith-hal-lel*) is the "Pride Root." The "Mighty Hero" (*Gibbor*) is "Uprooting" his "Humanity" by "Loving Evil" (*Verse 3*). His "Tongue" (*Le-sho-ne-kha*) is a "Sharpened Razor" (*The-ar Me-lu-tash*). It signifies the "Destructive Power of Speech."

2 Uproot you from the Land of the Living (we-She-re-she-kha me-Eretz Chay-yim): This is one of the most "Explicit Uprooting Roots" in the Bible. To be "Snatched" (*Yikh-tha-kha*) and "Plucked" (*Yis-sa-kha-kha*) is to be "Violently Removed." It is the "Death of the False Root."

3 Like an Olive Tree Flourishing (ke-Za-yith Ra-a-nan): The scrolls highlight the "Vitality of the Righteous." The "Olive Tree" (*Zayith*) is "Rooted" in the "House of God" (*be-Beth Elohim*). Unlike the "Tent" of the wicked (*Verse 5*), the "House" is "Permanent." It is the "Rooted Daily" image of "Trust."

4 Hope in Your Name (wa-A-qaw-weh Shim-kha): The scrolls conclude with this "Vertical Anchor." David moves from the "Disgrace" (*Verse 1*) of Doeg to the "Goodness" (*Tob*) of the "Divine Name." It marks the transition to the "Folly of Atheism" in *Psalm 53*. It is the "Rooted Daily" result of "Vindication." Flourish today. Hope forever.
