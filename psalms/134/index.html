Psalm 134

A song of ascents.

Minister by Night

“Praise the Lord, all you servants of the Lord who minister by night in the house of the Lord.” 1

Lift Up Your Hands

“Lift up your hands in the sanctuary and praise the Lord.” 2

Blessing from Zion

“May the Lord bless you from Zion, he who is the Maker of heaven and earth.” 3

Translation Notes

1 Minister by Night (ha-Om-dim bal-Ley-loth): Literally "Those who stand in the nights." In the scrolls, this is the "Vigilant Root." Even when the world sleeps, the "Servants" (*Abdi*) are "Rooted" in the "House" (*Beyth*). It signifies "Unceasing Worship."

2 Lift up your Hands (Se-u Ye-de-khem Qo-desh): The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Root of Praise." The "Sanctuary" (*Qodesh*) is where the "Hands" (*Yed*) are "Uprooted" from labor to "Bless" (*Baraku*) the Lord. It signifies "Total Surrender."

3 Bless you from Zion (Ye-ba-re-khe-kha mi-Tzi-yon): The scrolls conclude the "Songs of Ascents" with this "Reciprocal Root." The pilgrim "Blesses" God, and God "Blesses" (*Barak*) the pilgrim. The "Maker" (*O-seh*) of the "Cosmos" is the source of the "Personal Blessing." It marks the end of the "Journey Up" and the transition to the "Great Hallelujah" in *Psalm 135*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Night Light." Bless the Lord.
