Psalm 27

Of David.

The Stronghold of Life

“The Lord is my light and my salvation—whom shall I fear? The Lord is the stronghold of my life—of whom shall I be afraid?” 1

The One Thing

“One thing I ask from the Lord, this only do I seek: that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life, to gaze on the beauty of the Lord.” 2

Seeking the Presence

“My heart says of you, ‘Seek his face!’ Your face, Lord, I will seek.” 3
“Though my father and mother forsake me, the Lord will receive me.”

The Land of the Living

“I remain confident of this: I will see the goodness of the Lord in the land of the living.” 4
“Wait for the Lord; be strong and take heart and wait for the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 The Lord is my Light (YHWH Ori): In the scrolls, "Light" (*Or*) is the "Uprooting of Fear." Darkness is the "Atmosphere of the Enemy," but God is the "Atmosphere of Safety." The "Stronghold" (*Ma-oz*) is the "Final Root" of security. It marks the transition from "External Army" (*Verse 3*) to "Internal Peace."

2 Gaze on the Beauty of the Lord (la-cha-zoth be-No-am YHWH): Literally in the scrolls, "To behold the pleasantness/delight of YHWH." This is the "Aesthetic Root of Worship." It is the "One Thing" (*A-chath*) that "Roots" the entire life. All other "Desires" are "Uprooted" by this "Supreme Focus."

3 Seek His Face (Baq-shu Pha-nay): The scrolls highlight the "Personal Invitation." The "Heart" (*Leb*) responds to the "Divine Command." It is the "Root of Relationship." Even if the "Primary Human Roots" (*Father and Mother*) are "Uprooted" (*Verse 10*), the "Lord" is the "Eternal Root" who "Receives" (*Ya-as-phe-ni*).

4 Land of the Living (be-Eretz Chay-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Temporal Assurance." David isn't just looking for "Heaven"; he expects to see "Goodness" (*Tob*) in "This Life." It is the "Rooted Daily" expectation. It signifies that "Grace" is "Visible."

5 Wait for the Lord (Qaw-weh el-YHWH): The scrolls conclude with the "Patience Root." To "Wait" (*Qaw-weh*) is to "Expect with Tension." It is the "Action" of the "Rooted" while the "Storm" passes. It marks the "Final Victory" of "Taking Heart" (*ya-am-etz Lib-bekha*). It is the "Rooted Daily" finale. Wait. Wait. Wait.
