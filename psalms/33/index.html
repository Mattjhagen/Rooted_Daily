Psalm 33

The New Song

“Sing joyfully to the Lord, you righteous; it is fitting for the upright to praise him.” 1
“Sing to him a new song; play skillfully, and shout for joy.”

The Breath of Creation

“By the word of the Lord the heavens were made, their starry host by the breath of his mouth.” 2

The Plans of the Lord

“The Lord foils the plans of the nations; he thwarts the purposes of the peoples.” 3
“But the plans of the Lord stand firm forever, the purposes of his heart through all generations.”

The Eye of Favor

“No king is saved by the size of his army; no warrior escapes by his great strength.” 4
“But the eyes of the Lord are on those who fear him, on those whose hope is in his unfailing love.” 5
“We wait in hope for the Lord; he is our help and our shield.”

Translation Notes

1 Praise is Fitting (Na-wah The-hil-lah): In the scrolls, "Praising" (*The-hil-lah*) is the "Natural Ornament" of the "Upright" (*Ye-sha-rim*). It is the "Root of Aesthetic Order." To sing a "New Song" (*Shir Chadash*) is to "Root" the "Ancient Faith" in "Present Experience."

2 Breath of His Mouth (u-be-Ruach Piw): The scrolls highlight the "Effortless Power" of God. The entire "Starry Host" (*Tze-ba-am*) is "Rooted" in a "Whisper." It is the "Infinite Root of the Cosmos." It signifies that the "Word" (*Dabar*) is the "Final Reality."

3 Thwarts the Purposes (He-ni Ma-cha-she-both): Literally in the scrolls, "He makes the thoughts [of nations] ineffective." God "Uproots" the "Human Narrative" to "Root" His "Eternal Plan" (*Etzath YHWH*). It is the "Sovereignty Root."

4 No King is Saved by the Size of His Army (En ha-Melekh No-sha be-Rob Cha-yil): This is the "Uprooting of Military Pride." Power is a "Vain Hope" (*Verse 17*). It links back to *Psalm 20:7*. True "Security" is not "Rooted" in "Numbers."

5 Hope in His Unfailing Love (ha-Me-ya-cha-lim le-Chas-do): The scrolls conclude with the "Watchful Root." God’s "Eye" (*Ayin*) is the "Source of Rescue." We "Wait" (*Nik-ka-tha*) in "Hope." It marks the transition to the "Extolling" of *Psalm 34*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Trust." We are not "Accidents"; we are "Watched."
