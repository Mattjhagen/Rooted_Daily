Psalm 69

For the director of music. To the tune of “Lilies.” Of David.

The Waters of Agony

“Save me, O God, for the waters have come up to my neck.” 1
“I sink in the miry depths, where there is no foothold.”

Consumed by Zeal

“For zeal for your house consumes me, and the insults of those who insult you fall on me.” 2

Gall and Vinegar

“They put gall in my food and gave me vinegar for my thirst.” 3

Thanksgiving

“I will praise God’s name in song and glorify him with thanksgiving.” 4
“For God will save Zion and rebuild the cities of Judah.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Waters come up to my Neck (Ba-u Ma-yim ad-Na-phesh): Literally "Waters have come unto the soul." The scrolls highlight the "Extreme Risk of Drowning." David is "Uprooted" from "Breath" and "Stability." There is no "Foothold" (*Ma-a-mad*). It is the "Depth of Chaos."

2 Zeal for your House (Qin-ath Beth-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Messianic Root of Passion." To be "Consumed" (*Akha-lath-ni*) by God’s "House" (*Beth*) is to identify "Self" with "Sanctuary." In the New Testament, this is applied to Jesus (John 2:17). It is the "Uprooting of Apathy."

3 Gall and Vinegar (Rosh wa-Cho-metz): The scrolls highlight the "Bitterness of Rejection." "Gall" (*Rosh*) is a "Poisonous Root." "Vinegar" (*Chometz*) mockingly offers "Thirst-relief." In the crucifixion of Christ, this "Root of Suffering" reached its "Apogee."

4 Better than an Ox (mi-Shor Pa-rah): The scrolls highlight the "Superiority of Internal Worship." A "Song" (*Shir*) and "Thanksgiving" (*Thodah*) are more "Rooted" in "Pleasure" for God than the "Sacrifice" of a "Horned Bull." It is the "Uprooting of Ritualism."

5 God will save Zion (Elohim Yo-shi-ah Tzi-yon): The scrolls conclude with this "Corporate Hope." Individual "Agony" leads to "National Restoration" (*Bi-nah*). It marks the transition to the "Urgency" of *Psalm 70*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Sufferer." Zion is coming.
