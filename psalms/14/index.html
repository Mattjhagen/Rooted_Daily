Psalm 14

For the director of music. Of David.

The Heart’s Denial

“The fool says in his heart, ‘There is no God.’” 1
“They are corrupt, their deeds are vile; there is no one who does good.”

The Gaze from Heaven

“The Lord looks down from heaven on all mankind to see if there are any who understand, any who seek God.” 2
“All have turned away, all have become corrupt; there is no one who does good, not even one.”

The Presence in the Company

“But there they are, overwhelmed with dread, for God is present in the company of the righteous.” 3

The Hope of Zion

“Oh, that salvation for Israel would come out of Zion! When the Lord restores his people, let Jacob rejoice and Israel be glad!” 4

Translation Notes

1 The Fool (Na-bal): In the scrolls, *Nabal* refers not to a lack of "IQ," but to "Moral Insensibility." It is a "Deadening of the Soul’s Root." The denial "There is no God" (*En Elohim*) is a "Vow of Autonomy" that leads to "Vile Deeds" (*Verse 1*).

2 Looks down from Heaven (Hash-qi-pha mi-Sha-ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Audit." God is "Rooted" in "Observation." He searches for "Understanding" (*Mas-kil*)—those whose "Mental Roots" are "Wired" to the "Divine."

3 Overwhelmed with Dread (Pacha-du Pha-chad): Literally "They feared a fear." The scrolls highlight the "Inherent Insecurity" of the "Practical Atheist." Because they have "Uprooted God," they are "Vulnerable" to "Existential Terror." God is "Present" (*be-Dor*) with the righteous.

4 Salvation out of Zion (mi-Tzi-yon Yesh-u-ath): The scrolls conclude with this "Messianic Longing." Zion is the "Root of Restoration." The "Rejoicing" (*Yagel*) of Jacob is the "Fruit" of God’s "Salvation." It marks the "Confidence" that despite the "Folly of the World," the "Lord" will "Uproot" the "Exile" and "Root" His people in "Gladness." It is the "Rooted Daily" prayer for a "Better World."
