Psalm 80

For the director of music. To the tune of “The Lilies of the Covenant.” Of Asaph. A psalm.

The Shepherd of Israel

“Hear us, Shepherd of Israel, you who lead Joseph like a flock.” 1
“You who sit enthroned between the cherubim, shine forth.”

The Refrain of Restoration

“Restore us, O God; make your face shine on us, that we may be saved.” 2

The Transplanted Vine

“You transplanted a vine from Egypt; you drove out the nations and planted it.” 3
“You cleared the ground for it, and it took root and filled the land.”

The Son of Man

“Let your hand rest on the man at your right hand, the son of man you have raised up for yourself.” 4
“Restore us, Lord God Almighty; make your face shine on us, that we may be saved.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Shepherd of Israel (Ro-eh Yis-ra-el): In the scrolls, this is the "Guiding Root." God leads "Joseph" (*Yoseph*) like a "Flock" (*Tzon*). To "Shine Forth" (*Ho-phi-ah*) is to break the "Darkness of Defeat."

2 Restore us (Ha-shi-be-nu): Literally "Cause us to return." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Reversal." To have the "Face" (*Panim*) "Shine" (*Or*) is the "Aaronic Blessing" applied to a "National Crisis."

3 Transplanted a Vine (Ge-phen mi-Mitz-ra-yim Thas-si-a): The scrolls highlight the "Agricultural Root of Identity." Israel is a "Vine" (*Gephen*) that God "Plucked Up" (*Uprooted from Egypt*) to "Root" in the "Land." It signifies "Intentional Cultivation."

4 Son of Man (Ben-Adam): The scrolls highlight the "Messianic Root of Representative Leadership." The "Man at your Right Hand" (*Ish Ye-mi-ne-kha*) is the "Key to Restoration." It signifies "Divine Support for the Chosen One."

5 Restore us, Lord God Almighty (YHWH Elohim Tze-ba-oth Ha-shi-be-nu): The scrolls conclude by adding the "Full Divine Title." The "Refrain" builds in intensity. It marks the transition to the "Call to Joy" in *Psalm 81*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Revival." Restore us, King.
