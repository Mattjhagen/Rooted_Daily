Psalm 54

For the director of music. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David. When the Ziphites had gone to Saul and said: “Is not David hiding among us?”

The Power of the Name

“Save me, O God, by your name; vindicate me by your might.” 1
“Hear my prayer, O God; listen to the words of my mouth.”

Ruthless People

“Arrogant foes are attacking me; ruthless people are trying to kill me—people without regard for God.” 2 Selah

The Sustainer

“Surely God is my helper; the Lord is the one who sustains me.” 3

The Freewill Offering

“I will sacrifice a freewill offering to you; I will praise your name, Lord, for it is good.” 4
“You have delivered me from all my troubles.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Save me by your Name (be-Shim-kha Ho-shi-e-ni): In the scrolls, "The Name" (*ha-Shem*) is the "Root of Identity and Power." David is "Rooting" his "Safety" in "God’s Character." It is the "Uprooting of Human Betrayal."

2 Without Regard for God (lo-Sa-mu Elohim le-Neg-dam): Literally "They do not set God before them." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Ruthlessness." When God is "Uprooted" from the mind, "Arrogance" (*Zarim*) and "Violence" (*A-ri-tzim*) take hold.

3 Sustains me (be-Som-khe Naphe-shi): Literally "With those who support/prop up my soul." The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Support Root." God is the "Upholder." He "Uproots" the "Threat" by "Establishing" the "Soul."

4 Freewill Offering (be-Ni-da-bah): The scrolls highlight the "Voluntary Root of Worship." David doesn't just sacrifice because he "Must," but because he "Desires" (*Nidabah*). It signifies the "Joy" of the "Delivered."

5 Delivered me from all my Troubles (mi-Kol-Tza-rah Hit-zi-la-ni): The scrolls conclude with this "Root of Accomplished Rescue." David is "Uprooted" from the "Danger" and "Planted" in "Victory." It marks the transition to the "Treachery" of *Psalm 55*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Exposed Fugitive." Help is at hand.
