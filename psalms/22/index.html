Psalm 22

For the director of music. To the tune of “The Hind of the Dawn.” A psalm of David.

The Abandoned Cry

“My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?” 1
“Why are you so far from saving me, so far from my cries of anguish?”

The Scorn of Men

“But I am a worm and not a man, scorned by everyone, despised by the people.” 2
“All who see me mock me; they hurl insults, shaking their heads.”

The Poured Out Soul

“I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint.”
“Dogs surround me, a pack of villains encircles me; they pierce my hands and my feet.” 3
“They divide my clothes among them and cast lots for my garment.”

The Ends of the Earth

“You have answered me!” 4
“All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the Lord, and all the families of the nations will bow down before him.” 5
“They will proclaim his righteousness to a people yet unborn—for he has done it!”

Translation Notes

1 Why have you Forsaken me (Eli Eli Lamah Azab-thani): In the scrolls, this is the "Ultimate Uprooting." It is the cry of a soul that has lost its "Connection" to the "Source." It is "Spiritual Exile." It signifies that "Rootedness" sometimes feels like "Total Abandonment."

2 I am a Worm (Ani Tho-la-ath): The scrolls highlight the "Deepest Humiliation." A "Worm" is "Rootless" and "Vulnerable" to being "Trampled." David is describing the "Stripping of Human Dignity."

3 They Pierce my Hands and Feet (Ka-aru Ya-day we-Rag-lay): This is a "Highly Significant Prophetic Root." While the Masoretic text is debated (*Ka-ari* - 'like a lion'), the Dead Sea Scrolls (*5/6HevPs*) clearly use the word for "Pierced/Dug." It is the "Root of the Passion." It describes the "Violence" of the "Villains."

4 You have Answered me (An-i-tha-ni): This single word in the scrolls marks the "Turning Point." The "Silence" of *Verse 1* is broken. The "Lament" is "Uprooted" and "Re-rooted" in "Deliverance." It is the "Dawn" after the "Night of the Hind."

5 All the Ends of the Earth (Kol-Aphse-Eretz): The scrolls conclude with the "Universal Fruit" of this suffering. The "One Man’s Agony" leads to the "Nations’ Worship." It is the "Root of Global Salvation." The "Posterity" (*Zera*) that serves Him is the "Future Fruit." It marks the "Confidence" that "God Reigns" (*Verse 28*) over the "Chaos." It is the "Rooted Daily" victory of the "Forsaken One."
