Psalm 90

A prayer of Moses the man of God.

From Everlasting to Everlasting

“Lord, you have been our dwelling place throughout all generations.” 1
“Before the mountains were born or you brought forth the whole world, from everlasting to everlasting you are God.”

Numbering Our Days

“Our days may come to seventy years, or eighty, if our strength endures.” 2
“Teach us to number our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom.” 3

The Morning Love

“Satisfy us in the morning with your unfailing love, that we may sing for joy all our days.” 4

Establish our Hands

“May the favor of the Lord our God rest on us; establish the work of our hands for us.” 5
“Yes, establish the work of our hands.”

Translation Notes

1 Dwelling Place (Ma-on): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Ultimate Belonging." Moses, a "Wanderer" in the desert, finds his "Permanent Home" (*Ma-on*) in God. It is the "Uprooting of Nomadic Anxiety."

2 Days... Seventy Years (Ye-me She-na-the-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Mortality." Life "Quickly Passes" (*Ga-z Chish*) and we "Fly Away" (*Na-u-phah*). It signifies the "Fragility of the Human Root."

3 Number our Days (Lim-noth Ya-me-nu): Literally "To count our days." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Value." "Wisdom" (*Cho-khmah*) is "Rooted" in the "Awareness of Time." It is the "Uprooting of Procrastination."

4 Satisfying Morning Love (Sab-be-e-nu ba-Bo-qer Chas-de-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Renewal Root." God’s "Love" (*Chesed*) is the "Morning Nutrient." It signifies "Spiritual Satiety."

5 Establish the Work of our Hands (u-Ma-a-seh Ya-de-nu Ko-ne-na-hu): The scrolls conclude Book IV’s opening with this "Durability Root." Our "Works" (*Ma-a-seh*) are "Fleeting" unless God "Roots" (*Ko-ne-na-hu*) them. It marks the transition to the "Shadow of the Almighty" in *Psalm 91*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Eternity." Establish us, Lord.
