Psalm 76

For the director of music. With stringed instruments. A psalm of Asaph. A song.

The Tent in Salem

“In Judah God is known; his name is great in Israel.” 1
“His tent is in Salem, his dwelling place in Zion.”

Shattered Weapons

“There he broke the flashing arrows, the shields and the swords, the weapons of war.” 2 Selah

Radiant with Light

“You are radiant with light, more majestic than mountains rich with game.” 3
“At your rebuke, God of Jacob, both horse and chariot lie still.”

Restrained Wrath

“Surely your wrath against mankind brings you praise.” 4
“He breaks the spirit of rulers; he is feared by the kings of the earth.” 5

Translation Notes

1 In Judah God is Known (No-da bi-Yhu-dah Elohim): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Localized Revelation." God is "Rooted" in a "Place" (*Judah/Israel*). "Salem" (*Sha-lem*) is the "Ancient Name" for Jerusalem. It signifies "Total Peace."

2 Broken the Flashing Arrows (Shib-bar Rish-phe-Qa-sheth): Literally "He smashed the fiery-bolts of the bow." The scrolls highlight the "Neutralization of Aggression." God "Uproots" the "War Machine." It is the "Root of Disarmament."

3 Radiant with Light (Na-or At-tah): Literally "Illuminated are You." The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of Majesty." God’s "Presence" is more "Real" than the "Mountains of Prey" (*Har-re Tha-re-ph*). It is the "Uprooting of Earthly Power."

4 Restrained (Thach-gor): Literally "Girded/Bound." The scrolls highlight the "Divine Boundary." God "Uses" human "Wrath" (*Cha-math*) for His "Praise" (*Yo-du-kha*) and "Restrains" (*Girds*) the remainder. It signifies "Sovereign Limitation of Evil."

5 Breaks the Spirit of Rulers (Yib-tzor Ru-ach Ne-gi-dim): The scrolls conclude with this "Vertical Authority." The word "Breaks" (*Yib-tzor*) also means "To Grape-gather/Prune." God "Prunes" the "Spirit" of the proud. It marks the transition to the "Night of Questioning" in *Psalm 77*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Victorious Sanctuary." Salem is secure.
