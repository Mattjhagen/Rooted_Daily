Psalm 70

For the director of music. Of David. A petition.

The Urgent Cry

“Hasten, O God, to save me; come quickly, Lord, to help me.” 1

Shame and Confusion

“May those who seek my life be put to shame and confusion; may all who desire my ruin be turned back in disgrace.” 2

The Lord is Great

“But may all who seek you rejoice and be glad in you.”
“May those who long for your saving help always say, ‘The Lord is great!’” 3

Do Not Delay

“But as for me, I am poor and needy; come quickly to me, O God.” 4
“You are my help and my deliverer; Lord, do not delay.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Hasten (le-hat-zi-la-ni): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Rescue-Urgency." David is "Uprooted" from "Patience" by the "Immediacy of Danger." It is a "Mismore-petition" (*le-Haz-kir* - literally 'to cause to remember').

2 Disgrace (Be-shu): The scrolls highlight the "Reputational Uprooting" of the wicked. Those who say "Aha! Aha!" (*He-akh He-akh*) are "Turned Back" (*Ya-shu-bu*). It is the "Root of Vindication."

3 The Lord is Great (Yig-dal Elohim): Literally "May God be magnified." The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Focus." Instead of "Magnifying the Problem," the seekers "Magnify the Solution." It is the "Root of Worship."

4 Poor and Needy (A-ni we-Eb-yon): The scrolls highlight the "Dependent Root." David identifies with the "Anawim" (the bowed ones). His "Wealth" is not his "Root," but his "Need" for God is. It signifies "Total Reliance."

5 Do Not Delay (Al-the-a-khar): The scrolls conclude with this "Imperative of Love." God is the "Help" (*Ez-ri*) and "Deliverer" (*Me-phal-thi*). It marks the transition to the "Refuge of Old Age" in *Psalm 71*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Emergency." Come now.
