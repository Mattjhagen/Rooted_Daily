Psalm 99

Enthroned Between Cherubim

“The Lord reigns, let the nations tremble; he sits enthroned between the cherubim, let the earth shake.” 1
“Great is the Lord in Zion; he is exalted over all the nations.”

The Justice of the King

“The King is mighty, he loves justice—you have established equity.” 2
“Exalt the Lord our God and worship at his footstool; he is holy.” 3

The Pillar of Cloud

“Moses and Aaron were among his priests, Samuel was among those who called on his name.” 4
“He spoke to them from the pillar of cloud.”

Forgiving and Holy

“Lord our God, you answered them; you were to Israel a forgiving God.” 5
“Exalt the Lord our God... for the Lord our God is holy.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Enthroned between Cherubim (Yo-sheb Ke-ru-bim): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Arcane Majesty." The "Cherubim" (*Kerubim*) represent the "Guardians of the Presence." When the King "Sits" (*Yo-sheb*), the "Earth" (*Aretz*) must "Shake" (*Ta-nud*). It is the "Root of Theocratic Awe."

2 Established Equity (At-tah Ko-nan-ta Me-sha-rim): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of Law." God "Roots" (*Konanta*) "Uprightness" (*Mesharim*). It is the "Uprooting of Arbitrary Power."

3 He is Holy (Qa-dosh Hu): This is the "Tripartite Refrain Root." God is "Exalted" (*Ro-me-mu*) because His nature is "Set Apart" (*Holy*). "Worship at His Footstool" (*Hadom Raglaw*) signifies "Total Submission."

4 Called on His Name (Qo-re-im bi-She-mo): The scrolls highlight the "Intercessory Root." Moses, Aaron, and Samuel are "Rooted" in "Petition." God "Answers" (*Ya-a-nem*) from the "Pillar of Cloud" (*be-Am-mud A-nan*). It signifies "Divine-Human Communication."

5 Forgiving God (El No-se Hayi-tha La-hem): Literally "A God who lifts/carries for them." The scrolls highlight the "Substitutionary Root of Grace." He "Lifts" (*Forgives*) the "Iniquity" but "Vindicates" (*Punishes*) the "Deeds" (*A-li-lo-tham*). It signifies "Merciful Justice."

6 Exalt... Holy Mountain (Ro-me-mu... Har-Qod-sho): The scrolls conclude with the "Spatial Finale." Worship is "Rooted" in the "Holy Mountain" (*Har-Qodesh*). It marks the transition to the "Shout of Thanksgiving" in *Psalm 100*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Holy King." Holy is He.
