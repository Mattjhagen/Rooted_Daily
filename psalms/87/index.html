Psalm 87

Of the Sons of Korah. A psalm. A song.

The Holy Mountain

“He has founded his city on the holy mountain.” 1
“The Lord loves the gates of Zion more than all the other dwellings of Jacob.”
“Glorious things are said of you, city of God.” Selah

Born in Zion

“I will record Rahab and Babylon among those who acknowledge me.” 2
“Indeed, of Zion it will be said, ‘This one and that one were born in her.’” 3

The Register of Peoples

“The Lord will write in the register of the peoples: ‘This one was born in Zion.’” 4 Selah

All My Fountains

“As they make music they will sing, ‘All my fountains are in you.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 He has Founded (Ye-su-da-tho): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Divine Establishment." Zion is "Rooted" in "Holiness" (*be-Har-re Qo-desh*). It is the "Uprooting of Secular Cities."

2 Record Rahab and Babylon (Az-kir Ra-hab u-Ba-bel): Rahab is the poetic name for Egypt. The scrolls highlight the "Universal Root of Inclusion." God "Records" (*A-zki-r*) even the "Enemies" as being "Born" (*Yul-lad*) in Zion. It is the "Uprooting of Nationalistic Exclusion."

3 Most High... Establish Her (El-yon ye-Kho-ne-ne-ha): The scrolls highlight the "Vertical Security." God Himself "Roots" the city. It signifies "Permanent Belonging."

4 Register of the Peoples (be-Khthob Am-mim): Literally "In the writing of the peoples." The scrolls highlight the "Divine Census Root." "Rootedness" is "Recorded" by the Lord. It signifies "Official Adoption."

5 All my Fountains (Kol-Ma-ya-nay Bakh): The scrolls conclude with this "Hydraulic Root of Joy." "Fountains" (*Ma-ya-nay*) represent the "Sources of Life." Music-makers (*Cho-le-lim*) find their "Source" in Zion. It marks the transition to the "Darkest Prayer" in *Psalm 88*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Global Citizenship." Zion is my home.
