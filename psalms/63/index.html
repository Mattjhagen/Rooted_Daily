Psalm 63

A psalm of David. When he was in the Desert of Judah.

The Parched Land

“You, God, are my God, earnestly I seek you; I thirst for you, my whole being longs for you, in a dry and parched land where there is no water.” 1

Better than Life

“Because your love is better than life, my lips will glorify you.” 2
“I will praise you as long as I live, and in your name I will lift up my hands.”

Richest of Foods

“I will be fully satisfied as with the richest of foods; with singing lips my mouth will praise you.” 3

Clinging to You

“I cling to you; your right hand upholds me.” 4
“On my bed I remember you; I think of you through the watches of the night.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Thirst for You (Tza-me-ah le-kha Naphe-shi): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Desperate Pursuit." The "Desert" (*Mid-bar*) of Judah is the "Setting." The "Soul" (*Naphesh*) and "Flesh" (*Basar*) are "Rooted" in "Spiritual Thirst." It is the "Uprooting of Physical Comfort."

2 Better than Life (Tob mi-Chay-yim): This is one of the most "Stunning Hebrew Superlatives." God’s "Love" (*Chesed*) is more "Essential" than "Existence" (*Chayyim*) itself. It is the "Transcendental Root."

3 Satisfied as with Richest of Foods (ke-Che-leb wa-De-shen): Literally "As with marrow and fatness." The scrolls highlight the "Satiety of the Spirit." Praise is a "Feast" (*Verse 5*). It is the "Uprooting of Spiritual Anorexia."

4 I Cling to You (Da-be-qah Naphe-shi A-cha-re-kha): Literally "My soul sticks/glues after you." The scrolls highlight the "Ultimate Attachment Root." David is "Fixed" to God. God’s "Right Hand" (*Ye-mi-ne-kha*) is the "Support" (*Tha-me-khah*).

5 Watches of the Night (be-Ash-mu-roth): The scrolls conclude with the "Incessant Memory." Even in "Sleep" (*Verse 6*), David is "Rooted" in "Meditation" (*Eg-heh*). It marks the transition to the "Enemies" in *Psalm 64*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Passion." You are my God.
