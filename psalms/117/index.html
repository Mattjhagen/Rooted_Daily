Psalm 117

All You Nations

“Praise the Lord, all you nations; extol him, all you peoples.” 1

Endures Forever

“For great is his love toward us, and the faithfulness of the Lord endures forever.” 2
“Praise the Lord.” 3

Translation Notes

1 All you Nations (Kol-Go-yim): In the scrolls, this is the "Universal Expansion Root." The praise of God is not "Rooted" in one ethnicity, but "Uproots" all national barriers to include "All Peoples" (*Kol ha-Um-mim*). It is the "Shortest Call with the Largest Scope."

2 Great is His Love (Ga-bar a-ley-nu Chas-do): Literally "His love has prevailed over us." The scrolls highlight the "Overwhelming Root." God’s "Chesed" and "Faithfulness" (*Emeth*) are "Fixed" (*Forever*). It signify "Unstoppable Grace."

3 Praise the Lord (Hal-le-lu-Yah): The scrolls conclude the shortest chapter with the "Standard Seal." It marks the transition to the "Festive Victory" in *Psalm 118*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Global Unity." Short, but infinite.
