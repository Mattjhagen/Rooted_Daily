Psalm 150

In His Sanctuary

“Praise God in his sanctuary; praise him in his mighty heavens.” 1

Surpassing Greatness

“Praise him for his acts of power; praise him for his surpassing greatness.” 2

The Full Symphony

“Praise him with the sounding of the trumpet, praise him with the harp and lyre.” 3
“Praise him with timbrel and dancing, praise him with the strings and pipe.”
“Praise him with the clash of cymbals, praise him with resounding cymbals.” 4

The Breath of Life

“Let everything that has breath praise the Lord.” 5
“Praise the Lord.”

Translation Notes

1 In His Sanctuary (be-Qod-sho): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Sacred Space." Praise begins in the "Holy Place" (*Qodesh*) and expands to the "Mighty Heavens" (*Re-qi-a Uz-zo*). It signifies the "Universal Reach of Worship."

2 Surpassing Greatness (ke-Rob Gud-lo): Literally "According to the abundance of His greatness." The scrolls highlight the "Infinite Root." Praise must "Match" the "Abundance" (*Rob*) of the "Maker." It signifies "Limitless Adoration."

3 Trumpet... Harp... Lyre (Sho-phar... Ne-bel... Kin-nor): The scrolls highlight the "Technological Root of Praise." Every "Instrument" (*Kli*) developed by man is "Rooted" in the purpose of "Glorifying" the King. It signifies "Integrated Creativity."

4 Clash of Cymbals (Tzil-tze-ley Sha-ma): Literally "Cymbals of Hearing/Resounding." The scrolls highlight the "Auditory Root of Impact." Praise should be "Resonant" and "Unavoidable." It signifies "Public Boldness."

5 Everything that has Breath (Kol han-Ne-sha-mah): The scrolls conclude the entire Psalter with this "Vital Root." "Neshamah" is the "Breath of Life" given in *Genesis 2:7*. To "Breathe" is to have a "Rooted Responsibility" to "Praise" (*Te-hal-lel*). It marks the transition from the "Songs of the Heart" to the "Wisdom of the Mind" in *Proverbs*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Final Hallelujah." Every breath, praise Him.
