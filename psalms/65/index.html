Psalm 65

For the director of music. A psalm of David. A song.

Praise in Zion

“Praise awaits you, our God, in Zion; to you our vows will be fulfilled.” 1
“When we were overwhelmed by sins, you forgave our transgressions.” 2

The Hope of All Earth

“You answer us with awesome and righteous deeds, God our Savior, the hope of all the ends of the earth.” 3
“...who stilled the roaring of the seas and the turmoil of the nations.”

Streams of God

“You care for the land and water it; you enrich it abundantly. The streams of God are filled with water.” 4
“You crown the year with your bounty, and your carts overflow with abundance.”

Shouting for Joy

“The meadows are covered with flocks and the valleys are mantled with grain; they shout for joy and sing.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Praise awaits You (le-kha Du-mi-yah The-hil-lah): Literally "To you silence is praise." The scrolls highlight the "Rest of Adoration." To be "Rooted" in Zion is to find "Quietude" before God. It is the "Uprooting of Religious Clatter."

2 Forgave our Transgressions (pe-sha-e-nu At-tah The-khap-pe-rem): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Atonement." While sins (*A-wo-noth*) "Overwhelm" (*Ga-be-ru*) us, God "Covers" (*Ka-phar*) them. It signifies "Spiritual Cleansing."

3 Hope of all the Ends of the Earth (Mib-tach Kol-Qat-ze-Eretz): The scrolls highlight the "Universal Root of Security." God is not just a "Local Deity," but the "Trust" (*Mibtach*) of the "Farthest Seas" (*Yam Re-cho-qim*). He "Roots" the "Mountains" (*Verse 6*) by His "Strength."

4 Streams of God (Pe-legh Elohim): Literally "The river of God." The scrolls highlight the "Unfailing Source." Unlike human canals, God’s "Stream" is "Full" (*Ma-le*). It is the "Root of Sustenance." He "Enriches" (*Thash-re-nah*) the earth. It is the "Uprooting of Scarcity."

5 Valleys are Mantled with Grain (A-ma-qim Ya-at-phu Bar): The scrolls conclude with this "Aesthetic of Abundance." The "Valleys" (*Amaqim*) are "Wrapped" in "Wheat" (*Bar*). The "Hills" (*Geba-oth*) are "Girded with Joy" (*Verse 12*). It marks the transition to the "Universal Shout" of *Psalm 66*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Harvest." The earth is singing.
