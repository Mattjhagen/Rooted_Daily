Psalm 100

A psalm. For giving grateful praise.

Shout for Joy

“Shout for joy to the Lord, all the earth.” 1
“Worship the Lord with gladness; come before him with joyful songs.”

We are His

“Know that the Lord is God. It is he who made us, and we are his.” 2
“We are his people, the sheep of his pasture.”

Gates and Courts

“Enter his gates with thanksgiving and his courts with praise.” 3
“Give thanks to him and praise his name.”

Goodness and Love

“For the Lord is good and his love endures forever.” 4
“His faithfulness continues through all generations.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Shout for Joy (Ha-ri-u la-YHWH): In the scrolls, this is the "Universal Root of Jubilee." The "Shout" (*Teruah*) is the "Announcement of the King’s Presence." It is the "Root of Unfiltered Worship."

2 It is He who Made Us (Hu-A-sa-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Ownership." We are not "Self-Rooted." We belong to the "Maker" (*Asanu*). "We are His" (*we-Lo An-ach-nu*) signifies "Total Belonging." It is the "Uprooting of Autonomous Identity."

3 Enter His Gates (Bo-u She-a-raw): The scrolls highlight the "Spatial Root of Gratitude." "Thanksgiving" (*Tho-dah*) is the "Key" to the "Gates" (*She-a-rim*). It signifies "Protocol of Presence."

4 The Lord is Good (ki-Tob YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Character Root." "Goodness" (*Tob*) and "Love" (*Chesed*) are "Eternal" (*le-Olam*). It is the "Anchor Root of Faith."

5 Through all Generations (ad-Dor we-Dor): The scrolls conclude with the "Intergenerational Root." God’s "Faithfulness" (*Emunah*) is "Unbroken." It marks the transition to the "Integrity of the King" in *Psalm 101*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Belonging." Enter in.
