Psalm 108

A song. A psalm of David.

My Heart is Steadfast

“My heart, O God, is steadfast; I will sing and make music with all my soul.” 1
“Awake, harp and lyre! I will awaken the dawn.” 2

Higher than the Heavens

“For great is your love, higher than the heavens; your faithfulness reaches to the skies.” 3
“Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; let your glory be over all the earth.”

Trample Down the Enemy

“Save us and help us with your right hand, that those you love may be delivered.” 4
“With God we will gain the victory, and he will trample down our enemies.” 5

Translation Notes

1 My Heart is Steadfast (Na-khon Lib-bi): In the scrolls, this is the "Stabilized Root." To be "Steadfast" (*Nakhon*) is to be "Rooted" in "Unwavering Purpose." The "Soul" (*Khabod* - literally 'glory/liver') is the "Seat of Passion." It is the "Uprooting of Double-Mindedness."

2 Awaken the Dawn (A-i-rah Sha-char): The scrolls highlight the "Proactive Root of Praise." David does not wait for the "Dawn" (*Shachar*); he "Awakens" (*A-i-rah*) it with his "Music." It signifies "Spiritual Initiative."

3 Higher than the Heavens (me-al Sha-ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Transcendent Root of Love." God’s "Chesed" is "Boundless." "Faithfulness" (*Emunah*) reaches the "Clouds" (*She-cha-qim*). It signifies "Infinite Nutrient Flow."

4 Right Hand (Ye-mi-ne-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Divine Action." The "Right Hand" is the "Instrument of Rescue" (*Ho-shi-ah*). It is the "Uprooting of Human Limitations."

5 Trample Down (Ya-bus Tza-re-nu): The scrolls conclude with this "Conquering Root." God "Tramples" (*Ya-bus*) the "Adversaries" (*Tzari*). It marks the transition to the "Silence of God" in *Psalm 109*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Victory." With God we win.
