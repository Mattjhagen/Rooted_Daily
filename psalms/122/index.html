Psalm 122

A song of ascents. Of David.

Let Us Go

“I rejoiced with those who said to me, ‘Let us go to the house of the Lord.’” 1
“Our feet are standing in your gates, Jerusalem.” 2

Closely Compacted

“Jerusalem is built like a city that is closely compacted together.” 3
“There stand the thrones for judgment, the thrones of the house of David.”

Pray for Peace

“Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: ‘May those who love you be secure.’” 4
“May there be peace within your walls and security within your citadels.”
“For the sake of my family and friends, I will say, ‘Peace be within you.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 I Rejoiced (Sa-mach-thi): In the scrolls, this is the "Arrival Root." The "Rejoicing" (*Simkhah*) is triggered by the "Invitation" (*Bo-u*) to the "Presence." It is the "Root of Spiritual Anticipation."

2 In your Gates (bi-She-a-ra-ye-ikh): The scrolls highlight the "Spatial Completion Root." The "Feet" (*Rag-le-nu*) that "Wandered" (*Psalm 120*) are now "Fixed" (*Standing/Om-doth*). It signifies "Foundational Stability."

3 Closely Compacted (she-Chub-be-rah-lah Yach-daw): Literally "Joined together to her as one." The scrolls highlight the "Unity Root." "Jerusalem" (*Yerushalayim*) is the "Symbol of Oneness." It is the "Uprooting of Fragmentation."

4 Peace of Jerusalem (Sha-lom Ye-ru-sha-la-im): The scrolls highlight the "Linguistic Root." "Jerusalem" means "City of Peace." To "Pray" (*Sha-a-lu*) for her "Shalom" is to pray for the "Sovereignty of God." It signify "Communal Flourishing."

5 Peace be within You (Sha-lom bakh): The scrolls conclude with this "Blessing Root." The "Pilgrim" carries the "Shalom" of the "House of the Lord" (*Verse 9*) into his "Relationships." It marks the transition to the "Eyes on the Master" in *Psalm 123*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Holy Arrival." Peace be with you.
