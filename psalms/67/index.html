Psalm 67

For the director of music. With stringed instruments. A psalm. A song.

The Shining Face

“May God be gracious to us and bless us and make his face shine on us.” 1 Selah

Salvation for Nations

“...that your ways may be known on earth, your salvation among all nations.” 2

Let the Peoples Praise

“May the peoples praise you, God; may all the peoples praise you.” 3
“May the nations be glad and sing for joy, for you rule the peoples with equity.”

The Land Yields

“The land yields its harvest; God, our God, blesses us.” 4
“May God bless us still, so that all the ends of the earth will fear him.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Make His Face Shine (Ya-er Pa-naw): In the scrolls, this is the "Aaronic Root of Favor." To have the "Face" (*Panim*) of God "Shine" (*Or*) is to be "Rooted" in "Divine Approval." It is the "Uprooting of Rejection."

2 Your Ways may be Known (la-Da-ath ba-Aretz Dar-ke-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Missional Root." The blessing of Israel is for the "Discovery" of "God’s Ways" (*Derekh*) by the "Whole Earth." It signifies "Global Integration."

3 May all the Peoples Praise You (Yo-du-kha Am-mim Kul-lam): This is the "Refrain of Unity." The "Nations" (*Le-um-mim*) are glad because God "Rules" (*Shoph-et*) with "Equity" (*Mi-shor*). It is the "Uprooting of Tyranny."

4 The Land yields its Harvest (Eretz Na-the-nah Ye-bu-lah): The scrolls highlight the "Material Result of Spiritual Alignment." The "Yield" (*Ye-bul*) is a "Root of Sustenance." It signifies that "Rootedness" produces "Fruit."

5 All the ends of the Earth will Fear Him (we-Yi-re-u Otho Kol-Aph-se-Eretz): The scrolls conclude with this "Global Awe." The "Blessing" (*Yeba-re-khe-nu*) leads to "Awe" (*Yire-u*). It marks the transition to the "Triumphant Ascent" of *Psalm 68*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Intercession." Let the whole world know.
