Psalm 29

A psalm of David.

The Heavenly Court

“Ascribe to the Lord, you heavenly beings, ascribe to the Lord glory and strength.” 1
“Worship the Lord in the splendor of his holiness.”

The Voice of the Lord

“The voice of the Lord is over the waters; the God of glory thunders.” 2
“The voice of the Lord breaks the cedars; the Lord breaks in pieces the cedars of Lebanon.” 3
“The voice of the Lord twists the oaks and strips the forests bare.”
“And in his temple all cry, ‘Glory!’”

King Over the Flood

“The Lord sits enthroned over the flood; the Lord is enthroned as King forever.” 4

The Gift of Peace

“The Lord gives strength to his people; the Lord blesses his people with peace.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Ascribe to the Lord (Ha-bu la-YHWH): In the scrolls, "Ascribing" (*Ha-bu* - literally 'give') is the "Root of Acknowledgment." Even the "Heavenly Beings" (*Bene Elim*) are "Rooted" in "Divine Sovereignty." It is the "Uprooting of Autonomous Pride."

2 Voice of the Lord (Qol YHWH): This phrase appears seven times in the scrolls. It is the "Sevenfold Root of Power." The "Voice" (*Qol*) is not just "Sound," but "Action." It "Uproots" the "Waters" (*Verse 3*) and "Shakes" the "Desert" (*Verse 8*). It is the "Creator’s Roar."

3 Breaks the Cedars (Sho-ber Ara-zim): The scrolls highlight the "Shattering of Strength." The "Cedars of Lebanon" were the "Symbol of Un-shakable Earthly Power." God’s "Voice" "Uproots" even the "Mighty." It signifies that "True Rootedness" is found in the "Speaker," not the "Tree."

4 Sits Enthroned over the Flood (le-Ma-bul Ya-shab): This is a "Pivotal Theological Root." The "Flood" (*Mabul*) refers to the "Chaos of Genesis." Even in the "Greatest Uprooting" of history, God is "Rooted" as "King" (*Melekh*). He is the "Static Center" of the "Storm."

5 Blesses His People with Peace (be-Sha-lom): The scrolls conclude with the "Result of the Storm." After the "Thunder" and the "Stripping of Forests," there is "Peace" (*Shalom*). God "Roots" His people in "Strength" (*Oz*) and "Quietness." It marks the transition from "Chaos" to "Covenant." It is the "Rooted Daily" end to every storm.
