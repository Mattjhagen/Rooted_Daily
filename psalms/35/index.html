Psalm 35

Of David.

The Divine Contention

“Contend, Lord, with those who contend with me; fight against those who fight against me.” 1
“Brandish spear and javelin against those who pursue me. Say to me, ‘I am your salvation.’”

Evil for Good

“Ruthless witnesses come forward; they question me on things I know nothing about.”
“They repay me evil for good and leave me like one bereaved.” 2

The Sackcloth Memory

“Yet when they were ill, I put on sackcloth and humbled myself with fasting.” 3
“But when I stumbled, they gathered in glee... they slandered me without ceasing.”

The Shout of Vindication

“May those who delight in my vindication shout for joy and gladness!” 4
“My tongue will proclaim your righteousness and your praises all day long.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Contend (Ri-bah): In the scrolls, this is a "Legal Root." David is asking God to be his "Advocate" in the "Court of Conflict." The "Spear" (*Cha-nith*) and "Javelin" (*Se-gor*) are "Dynamic Roots of Protection." It is the "Uprooting of Personal Vengeance."

2 Evil for Good (Ra-ah tha-chath To-bah): The scrolls highlight the "Ultimate Betrayal." David’s "Kindness" was "Uprooted" by the "Malice" of those he helped. It is the "Root of Sorrow" (*She-khol* - literally 'bereavement/childlessness').

3 Sackcloth and Fasting (Saq... Tsom): The scrolls highlight David’s "Intercessory Root." He was "Genuinely Planted" in the "Well-being" of his enemies. Their "Glee" (*Sa-me-chu*) at his "Stumbling" (*Verse 15*) is the "Anti-Root" of community.

4 Delight in my Vindication (Cha-phe-tzey Tzid-qi): The scrolls conclude with the "Restoration of Joy." God "Delights" (*Cha-phetz*) in the "Well-being" (*Shalom*) of His servant. It is the "Root of Divine Pleasure."

5 My Tongue will Proclaim (Le-sho-ni the-he-geh): Literally in the scrolls, "My tongue will meditate/mutter." David is "Rooting" his "Speech" in "Vindication." He moves from the "Ravages of Lions" (*Verse 17*) to the "Congregation of Praise" (*Verse 18*). It marks the transition to the "Message from God" in *Psalm 36*. It is the "Rooted Daily" triumph over "Betrayal."
