Psalm 149

A New Song

“Praise the Lord. Sing to the Lord a new song, his praise in the assembly of his faithful people.” 1
“Let Israel rejoice in their Maker; let the people of Zion be glad in their King.”

Dancing and Music

“Let them praise his name with dancing and make music to him with timbrel and harp.” 2
“For the Lord takes delight in his people; he crowns the humble with victory.” 3

Double-Edged Sword

“May the praise of God be in their mouths and a double-edged sword in their hands.” 4
“This is the glory of all his faithful people. Praise the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sing... a New Song (Shi-ru... Shir Cha-dash): In the scrolls, this is the "Restoration Root." A "New Song" (*Shir Chadash*) is required when the "Assembly" (*Qe-hal*) has been "Uprooted" from exile and "Rooted" in "Home." It signifies "Spiritual Renewal."

2 Praise... with Dancing (Ye-hal-le-lu She-mo be-Ma-chol): The scrolls highlight the "Kinetic Root of Praise." The "Name" (*Shem*) is celebrated with the "Body" (*Machol*). It signifies "Holistic Worship."

3 Crowns the Humble (Ye-pha-er A-na-wim): Literally "He will beautify the humble with salvation." The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Victory." God "Roots" His "Beauty" (*Pe-er*) in the "Lowly" (*Anawim*). It is the "Uprooting of Oppression."

4 Double-Edged Sword (Che-reb Pi-phi-yoth): The scrolls highlight the "Vindicatory Root." The "High Praises" (*Ro-me-moth*) in the "Mouth" are linked to the "Execution of Justice" (*Mish-pat*) in the "Hand." It signifies "Active Holiness."

5 Praise the Lord (Hal-le-lu-Yah): The scrolls conclude with this "Victorious Seal." It marks the transition to the "Universal Crescendo" in *Psalm 150*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Triumph of the Humble." Praise the King.
