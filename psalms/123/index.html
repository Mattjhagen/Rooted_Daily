Psalm 123

A song of ascents.

Enthroned in Heaven

“I lift up my eyes to you, to you who sit enthroned in heaven.” 1

Eyes toward the Hand

“As the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master.” 2
“So our eyes look to the Lord our God, till he shows us his mercy.”

End of Contempt

“Have mercy on us, Lord, have mercy on us, for we have endured no end of contempt.” 3
“We have endured no end of ridicule from the arrogant, of contempt from the proud.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Enthroned in Heaven (hay-Yo-she-bi ba-Sha-ma-yim): In the scrolls, this is the "Vertical Root of Focus." The "Eyes" (*Eyna*) are "Uprooted" from the "Earthly Scene" to the "Heavenly Throne." It is the "Root of Transcendent Waiting."

2 Hand of their Master (Yad A-do-ne-hem): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Submission." The "Hand" (*Yad*) is the "Instrument of Guidance/Provision." Just as a "Maid" (*Shiph-chah*) watches her "Mistress" (*Ge-bir-tah*), the soul is "Rooted" in "Expectant Obedience."

3 No End of Contempt (Rab-bath Sa-ba-nu Buz): Literally "We are abundantly sated with contempt." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Emotional Saturation." The "Soul" is "Full" (*Sabanu*) of the "Mockery" (*Buz*) of the world. It is the "Uprooting of Dignity."

4 Contempt from the Proud (hab-Buz lig-e-yo-nim): The scrolls highlight the "Hostile Root." The "Proud" (*Ge-yo-nim*) "Uproot" the "Humble" through "Ridicule." It marks the transition to the "Lord on our Side" in *Psalm 124*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Waiting for Mercy." Our eyes are on You.
