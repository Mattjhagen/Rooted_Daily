Psalm 38

A psalm of David. A petition.

The Piercing Arrows

“Lord, do not rebuke me in your anger or discipline me in your wrath.” 1
“Your arrows have pierced me, and your hand has come down on me.”

The Heavy Burden

“My guilt has overwhelmed me like a burden too heavy to bear.” 2
“My wounds fester and are loathsome because of my sinful folly.”

The Light Gone Out

“My heart pants, my strength fails me; even the light has gone from my eyes.” 3
“My friends and companions avoid me because of my wounds; my neighbors stay far off.”

The Confession

“I wait for you, Lord; you will answer, Lord my God.”
“I confess my iniquity; I am troubled by my sin.” 4
“Come quickly to help me, my Lord and my Savior.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Arrows have Pierced me (Ni-cha-thu Bi): In the scrolls, "Pierced" (*Ni-cha-thu*) literally means "settled/descended into." David is "Rooted" in "Divine Discipline." His "Body" is the "Target" of God’s "Wrath" (*Verse 3*). It is the "Uprooting of False Health."

2 Burden too Heavy (Ma-ssa Khabed): The scrolls highlight the "Psychological Weight of Sin." Guilt (*A-won*) is not just an "Idea," it is a "Physical Pressure." It is "Uprooting" David’s "Stability." His "Wounds" (*Chab-bu-ro-thay*) reflect the "Corruption of the Root."

3 Light has gone from my Eyes (Or-Enay En-Itham): Literally in the scrolls, "The light of my eyes—even they—is not with me." This refers to the "Uprooting of Vitality." "Eyes" are the "Windows of the Soul’s Root." To lose "Light" is to be near "Death."

4 Troubled by my Sin (Ed-ag me-Chat-ta-thi): Literally "I am anxious/sorrowful." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Penitence." David doesn't "Hide" (*Psalm 32*); he "Declares" (*Ag-gid*) his "Iniquity." It is the "Re-rooting" of the soul in "Honesty."

5 My Lord and my Savior (Adonai Yeshu-athi): The scrolls conclude with this "Urgent Root of Hope." David is "Stripped" of "Friends" (*Verse 11*) and "Health" (*Verse 7*). Only the "Lord" (*Adonai*) remains. It marks the transition to the "Silence" of *Psalm 39*. It is the "Rooted Daily" cry for "Rescue."
