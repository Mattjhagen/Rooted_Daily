Psalm 4

For the director of music. With stringed instruments. A psalm of David.

The Relief of the Distressed

“Answer me when I call to you, my righteous God. Give me relief from my distress.” 1

Silence on the Bed

“Tremble and do not sin; when you are on your beds, search your hearts and be silent.” 2 Selah

The Greater Joy

“Let the light of your face shine on us.” 3
“You have filled my heart with greater joy than when their grain and new wine abound.” 4
“In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, Lord, make me dwell in safety.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Give me Relief (Har-chab-ta Li): Literally in the scrolls, "You have made room for me." It is the "Uprooting of Confinement." David feels that "God’s Answer" provides "Space" in the middle of "Distress" (*Tzar* - literally 'narrowness'). It is the "Root of Freedom."

2 Search your Hearts and be Silent (Imru bi-Lebabkhem... we-Dommu): The scrolls highlight the "Internal Root." David advises his enemies to "Uproot" their "Delusions" (*Verse 2*) through "Night-time Introspection." To "Be Silent" (*Dommu*) is to let the "Noise of the World" fade so the "Truth" can "Rise."

3 Light of your Face (Or Phanekha): This is the "Benedictive Root." It refers to "Divine Favor." In the scrolls, "Light" is the "Uprooting of Confusion." When God "Shines" His "Face," the "Darkness of Distress" vanishes.

4 Greater Joy than Grain and Wine (mi-Eth Deganam we-Thiro-sham): The scrolls highlight the "Superior Root of Happiness." "Material Abundance" (*Grain and Wine*) is a "Seasonal Joy." David’s "Heart-Joy" is "Spiritual and Eternal." It is "Rooted" in "Presence" rather than "Possession."

5 In Peace I will Lie Down (be-Shalom Yachad Eshkebah): The scrolls conclude with the "Physical Manifestation of Faith." David "Sleeps" (*Eshkebah*) because he is "Rooted" in the "Safety" (*Betach*) of the "Lord Alone." It links back to *Psalm 3:5*. "Rooted Daily" means "Peaceful Nightly." It marks the "Confidence" of the "Righteous Petitioner."
