Psalm 95

Rock of Our Salvation

“Come, let us sing for joy to the Lord; let us shout aloud to the Rock of our salvation.” 1
“Let us come before him with thanksgiving and extol him with music and song.”

Kneel Before the Maker

“Come, let us bow down in worship, let us kneel before the Lord our Maker.” 2
“For he is our God and we are the people of his pasture.”

Do Not Harden Your Hearts

“Today, if only you would hear his voice: ‘Do not harden your hearts as you did at Meribah.’” 3
“They are a people whose hearts go astray, and they have not known my ways.” 4
“They shall never enter my rest.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Rock of our Salvation (Tzur Yish-e-nu): In the scrolls, this is the "Immovable Root of Rescue." To "Shout" (*Na-ri-ah*) to the Rock is to acknowledge "Absolute Stability." It is the "Root of Book IV’s Worship."

2 Lord our Maker (YHWH O-se-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Origin Root." We do not "Root" ourselves; we are "Fashioned" (*Osenu*) by Him. "Bowing" (*Nish-tha-cha-weh*) and "Kneeling" (*Nib-re-khah*) are the "Postures of the Rooted."

3 Do not Harden your Hearts (al-Thaq-shu Lebab-khem): Literally "Do not stiffen/make difficult your heart." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Inflexibility." "Meribah" (*Quarreling*) and "Massah" (*Testing*) are the "Uprooting of Trust."

4 Hearts go Astray (Am Tho-e Lebab Hem): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Disorientation." When the "Heart" (*Leb*) "Strays" (*Tho-e*), the "Knowledge" (*Da-ath*) of God’s "Ways" (*De-ra-khay*) is "Uprooted."

5 Enter my Rest (be-Me-nu-cha-thi): The scrolls conclude with this "Stable Finality Root." "Rest" (*Me-nu-chah*) is the "Settled State" of the Rooted. To be "Forbidden Entry" is the "Ultimate Uprooting." It marks the transition to the "New Song" in *Psalm 96*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Obedience." Hear His voice today.
