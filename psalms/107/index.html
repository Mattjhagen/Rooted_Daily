Psalm 107

Let the Redeemed Tell their Story

“Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good; his love endures forever.” 1
“Let the redeemed of the Lord tell their story.”

Desert Wastelands

“Some wandered in desert wastelands, finding no way to a city where they could settle.” 2
“Then they cried out to the Lord in their trouble, and he delivered them.”

Stilled to a Whisper

“Some went out on the sea in ships; they saw the works of the Lord.”
“He stilled the storm to a whisper; the waves of the sea were hushed.” 3

Unfailing Love

“Let them give thanks to the Lord for his unfailing love and his wonderful deeds.” 4
“Let the one who is wise ponder the loving deeds of the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Redeemed tell their Story (Ge-u-le YHWH Yo-me-ru): In the scrolls, this is the "Narrative Root of Redemption." To be "Redeemed" (*Ge-u-le*) is to be "Bought back" and "Uprooted from Exile." It is the "Root of Book V’s Opening."

2 Desert Wastelands (be-De-rekh Ye-shi-mon): The scrolls highlight the "Disorientation Root." To "Wander" (*Ta-u*) in the "Solitary Way" is the "Uprooting of Belonging." God "Gathers" (*Qib-be-tzam*) them from the "Four Corners" (*Verse 3*).

3 Stilled to a Whisper (Ya-qem Se-a-rah li-De-ma-mah): Literally "He caused the storm to stand for a silence." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Absolute Control." The "Waves" (*Gal-le-hem*) are "Rooted" in "Quietness" (*De-ma-mah*). It signify "Spiritual Peace."

4 Unfailing Love (Chas-do): The scrolls highlight the "Refrain Root." Four times in this psalm (*Verses 8, 15, 21, 31*), the people are called to "Give Thanks" (*Yo-du*) for "Chesed." It is the "Heartbeat of the Redeemed."

5 Wise ponder (Ya-bi-nu Chas-de YHWH): The scrolls conclude with the "Intellectual Root of Worship." "Wisdom" (*Cho-kham*) is found in "Pondering" (*Ya-bi-nu*) the "Mercies" (*Chas-de*). It marks the transition to the "Steadfast Heart" in *Psalm 108*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Deliverance." He stilled the storm.
