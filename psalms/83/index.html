Psalm 83

A song. A psalm of Asaph.

The Silence of God

“O God, do not remain silent; do not turn a deaf ear, do not stand aloof, O God.” 1

Wipe Out the Name

“With cunning they conspire against your people; they plot against those you cherish.” 2
“‘Come,’ they say, ‘let us destroy them as a nation, so that Israel’s name is remembered no more.’”

The Plot of Nations

“With one mind they plot together; they form an alliance against you.”
“The tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, of Moab and the Hagrites...” 3

Tumbleweed in the Storm

“Make them like tumbleweed, my God, like chaff before the wind.” 4
“Let them know that you, whose name is the Lord—that you alone are the Most High over all the earth.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Do not remain Silent (al-De-mi Lakha): In the scrolls, "Silence" (*Demi*) is the "Enemy of the Afflicted." David appeals to God’s "Audible Sovereignty." It is the "Root of Crisis Intercession."

2 Those you Cherish (Tze-phu-ne-kha): Literally "Your hidden/treasured ones." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Divine Value." While the nations plot "Cunningly" (*Ya-a-ri-mu*), God’s "Cherished" are "Rooted" in His "Protection." It is the "Uprooting of the Secret Plot."

3 Tents of Edom (Oho-le Edom): The scrolls list the "Geopolitical Root of the Confederacy." Ten nations/groups form an "Alliance" (*Be-rith* - literally 'covenant') against God. It signifies the "Uprooting of the Covenant People."

4 Make them like Tumbleweed (Shi-the-mo ghal-Ghal): Literally "Make them like a whirling thing/wheel." The scrolls highlight the "Root of Instability." Like "Chaff" (*Qash*), the enemies have no "Foothold." It is the "Judgment of the Storm" (*Se-a-ra-the-kha*).

5 The Lord... Most High (YHWH... El-yon): The scrolls conclude with this "Vertical Finality." The purpose of the "Conflict" is "Global Recognition" of the "Name." It marks the transition to the "Lovely Dwelling" of *Psalm 84*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Victory through the Storm." You alone are God.
