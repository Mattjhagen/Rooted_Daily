Psalm 30

A psalm. A song. For the dedication of the temple. Of David.

Lifted from the Depths

“I will exalt you, Lord, for you lifted me out of the depths and did not let my enemies gloat over me.” 1
“Lord my God, I called to you for help, and you healed me.”

The Morning Joy

“For his anger lasts only a moment, but his favor lasts a lifetime; weeping may stay for the night, but rejoicing comes in the morning.” 2

Wailing into Dancing

“You turned my wailing into dancing; you removed my sackcloth and clothed me with joy.” 3
“...that my heart may sing your praises and not be silent. Lord my God, I will praise you forever.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Lifted me out of the Depths (Dil-lithani): Literally in the scrolls, "You drew me up like water from a well." David is "Rooting" his "Survival" in "Divine Rescue." He was "Uprooted" by illness/death, but God "Re-rooted" him in the "Land of the Living."

2 Rejoicing comes in the Morning (la-Boqer Rin-nah): The scrolls highlight the "Temporal Contrast." "Weeping" (*Beki*) is a "Lodger" (*Ya-lin*) that stays only for the night. "Rejoicing" (*Rinnah* - literally 'shout/song') is the "Permanent Root." It marks the transition from "Night-Grief" to "Day-Grace." It is the "Rooted Daily" promise.

3 Turned my Wailing into Dancing (Hapha-khta Mis-pe-di le-Ma-chol): This is a "Complete Internal Re-rooting." God "Changes the Substance" of the experience. "Sackcloth" (*Saq*) is the "Symbol of Uprooting," while "Dancing" (*Ma-chol*) is the "Symbol of Life."

4 My Heart may sing... and not be Silent (Ye-zam-mer-kha Kha-bod we-lo Yi-dom): The scrolls conclude with the "Purpose of Restoration." God "Heals" so that His servant can "Praise" (*O-de-kha*). It marks the "Active Voice" of the "Rooted." Silence is for the "Pit" (*Verse 9*); Song is for the "Saints." It marks the transition to the "Refuge" of *Psalm 31*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Life."
