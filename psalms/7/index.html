Psalm 7

A shiggaion of David, which he sang to the Lord concerning Cush, a Benjamite.

The Lion’s Pursuit

“Lord my God, I take refuge in you; save and deliver me from all who pursue me.” 1
“...or they will tear me like a lion and rip me to pieces with no one to rescue me.”

The Oath of Innocence

“Lord my God, if I have done this and there is guilt on my hands...”
“Then let my enemy pursue and overtake me; let him trample my life to the ground.” 2 Selah

The Shield of the Upright

“My shield is God Most High, who saves the upright in heart.” 3

The Self-Constructed Trap

“Whoever is pregnant with evil conceives trouble and gives birth to disillusionment.” 4
“They dig a hole and scoop it out; they fall into the pit they have made.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Tear me like a Lion (Yith-roph ke-Aryeh): In the scrolls, this refers to the "Lethal Slander" of Cush. Slander is "Uprooting" David’s reputation. He seeks "Refuge" (*Chassi-thi*) in the "Divine Character" as his only "Un-uprootable" shelter.

2 Trample my Life to the Ground (Yerammos la-Eretz Chay-yay): The scrolls highlight David’s "Legal Confidence." He is so "Rooted" in his "Innocence" that he invites "Judgment" if he is guilty. He is "Binding" his "Survival" to his "Integrity."

3 My Shield is God Most High (Maginni al-Elohim): Literally in the scrolls, "My shield is upon/with God." God is the "Active Defender." He is the "Root" of "Vindication." He "Saves" the "Upright" (*Yish-re-Lebab*).

4 Conceives Trouble (Harah Amal): This is a "Biological Metaphor" for "Sin" in the scrolls. Evil has a "Gestation Period." It is a "Root" that eventually "Fruits" in "Disillusionment" (*Sheqer* - literally 'lie/deception').

5 Fall into the Pit (Way-Yippol be-Shachath): The scrolls conclude with the "Law of Reversal." The "Effort" the wicked put into "Uprooting Others" becomes the "Cause" of their own "Uprooting." Their "Trouble" (*Amal*) "Recoils" on their own "Heads" (*Verse 16*). It marks the "Ironic Justice" of God. David ends in "Praise" (*Verse 17*) because he is "Rooted" in the "Righteousness" of the "Most High."
