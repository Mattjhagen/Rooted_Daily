Psalm 132

A song of ascents.

David’s Vow

“Lord, remember David and all his self-denial.” 1
“Until I find a place for the Lord, a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob.”

Fields of Jaar

“We heard it in Ephrathah, we came upon it in the fields of Jaar.” 2
“Arise, Lord, and come to your resting place, you and the ark of your might.” 3

A Sure Oath

“The Lord swore an oath to David, a sure oath he will not revoke.” 4
“For the Lord has chosen Zion, he has desired it for his dwelling.”

Shimmering Crown

“Here I will make a horn grow for David and set up a lamp for my anointed one.”
“But on his head will be a shimmering crown.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Self-Denial (An-no-tho): In the scrolls, this is the "Sacrificial Root." David "Denied" (*Afflicted/Anoth*) himself of sleep and comfort until the "Presence" was "Rooted" in a "Place." It signifies "Spiritual Priority."

2 Fields of Jaar (Sde-Ya-ar): Literally "Fields of the forest." The scrolls highlight the "Hidden Root of the Ark." The "Dwelling" (*Mish-ka-no-thaw*) was found in a "Wild place." It is the "Uprooting of the Ark’s Exile."

3 Ark of your Might (A-ron Uz-ze-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Root of Power." The "Ark" (*Aron*) is the "Footstool" (*Verse 7*) of the "King." It signify "Divine Enthronement."

4 Chosen Zion (Ba-char be-Tzi-yon): The scrolls highlight the "Election Root." Zion is the "Resting Place" (*Me-nu-cha-thi*) "For ever and ever" (*Ad-e-ad*). It is the "Root of Eternal Dwelling."

5 Shimmering Crown (Ya-tzitz Ni-zo): Literally "His crown will sparkle/blossom." The scrolls conclude with this "Radiant Root." The "Crown" (*Nez-er*) "Blossoms" (*Ya-tzitz*) like a flower of light. It marks the transition to the "Unity of Brethren" in *Psalm 133*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Eternal Throne." Zion is His home.
