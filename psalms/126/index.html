Psalm 126

A song of ascents.

Like Those Who Dreamed

“When the Lord restored the fortunes of Zion, we were like those who dreamed.” 1
“Our mouths were filled with laughter, our tongues with songs of joy.”

Great Things for Us

“The Lord has done great things for us, and we are filled with joy.” 2
“Restore our fortunes, Lord, like streams in the Negev.” 3

Reaping in Joy

“Those who sow with tears will reap with songs of joy.” 4
“Those who go out weeping, carrying seed to sow, will return with songs of joy, carrying sheaves with them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Like those who Dreamed (ha-yi-nu ke-Cho-le-mim): In the scrolls, this is the "Surreal Root of Restoration." The "Return" (*Shu-bah*) from exile is so "Incredible" it feels like a "Dream" (*Cholem*). It is the "Uprooting of Despair."

2 Great things for Us (Hig-dil YHWH la-a-soth im-ma-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Magnified Root." Even the "Nations" (*Goyim*) "Witness" the "Scale" of God’s action. It signifies "Public Vindication."

3 Streams in the Negev (ka-Aphi-qim ban-Ne-geb): The scrolls highlight the "Irrigation Root." The "Negev" is the "Arid South." God’s restoration is like "Flash Floods" (*Aphiqim*) that "Root" life in the "Desert." It is the "Uprooting of Drought."

4 Sow with Tears (ha-Zo-re-im be-Dim-ah): The scrolls highlight the "Agricultural Root of Suffering." "Tears" (*Dim-ah*) are the "Water" for the "Seed." To "Sow" (*Zo-re-im*) in pain is the "Requirement" for the "Harvest" (*Qa-tzir*) of "Joy" (*Rin-nah*). It signifies "Productive Sorrow."

5 Carrying Sheaves (No-se A-lum-mo-thaw): The scrolls conclude with the "Weighty Root of Reward." The "Weeping" (*Ba-kho*) is replaced by the "Singing" (*Rin-nah*) and the "Burden" of the "Sheaves" (*Alummoth*). It marks the transition to the "Labor of the House" in *Psalm 127*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Vindication." We reap in joy.
