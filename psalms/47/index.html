Psalm 47

For the director of music. Of the Sons of Korah. A psalm.

The Hand-Clap

“Clap your hands, all you nations; shout to God with cries of joy.” 1
“For the Lord Most High is awesome, the great King over all the earth.”

The Royal Ascent

“God has ascended amid shouts of joy, the Lord amid the sounding of trumpets.” 2
“Sing praises to God, sing praises; sing praises to our King, sing praises.”

King of Nations

“God reigns over the nations; God is seated on his holy throne.” 3
“The nobles of the nations assemble as the people of the God of Abraham.” 4
“For the kings of the earth belong to God; he is greatly exalted.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Clap your Hands (Thiq-u-Khaph): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Global Celebration." The "Hand" (*Khaph*) is used in a "Victory Clap." It is the "Uprooting of Mourning." God is the "Awesome" (*No-ra*) King. It signifies that "Sovereignty" is a "Source of Joy."

2 God has Ascended (Al-ah Elohim): The scrolls highlight the "Moment of Coronation." Whether referring to the "Ark" being carried or a "Vision of Heaven," God "Rises" (*Al-ah*) to His "Seat of Power." The "Shouts" (*The-ru-ah*) and "Trumpets" (*Sho-phar*) are the "Root of Public Acknowledgment."

3 Reigns over the Nations (Ma-lakh al-Go-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Universal Root." God is not just the "King of Israel," but the "King of the Gentiles." His "Holy Throne" (*Kis-se Qod-sho*) is the "Static Center" of the "Global Gathering."

4 People of the God of Abraham (Am Elo-he Ab-ra-ham): This is a "Stunning Covenantal Root." The "Nobles" (*Ne-di-be*) of the "Gentiles" are now "Integrated" into the "Family of Faith." It is the "Uprooting of Exclusion."

5 He is Greatly Exalted (Me-od Na-a-leh): The scrolls conclude with this "Vertical Finality." The "Kings" (*Ma-gin-ne* - literally 'shields/protectors') "Belong" (*la-Elohim*) to God. It marks the transition to the "City of God" in *Psalm 48*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Victory." The earth is His.
