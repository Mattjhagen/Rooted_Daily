Psalm 74

A maskil of Asaph.

The Perpetual Ruins

“O God, why have you rejected us forever? Why does your anger smolder against the sheep of your pasture?” 1
“Turn your steps toward these perpetual ruins.”

Axes in the Sanctuary

“They burned your sanctuary to the ground; they defiled the dwelling place of your Name.” 2
“We are given no signs from God; no prophets are left.” 3

Crushing Leviathan

“But God is my King from long ago; he brings salvation on the earth.”
“It was you who crushed the heads of Leviathan and gave him as food to the creatures of the desert.” 4

Remember the Covenant

“Have regard for your covenant, because haunts of violence fill the dark places of the land.” 5
“Rise up, O God, and defend your cause.” 6

Translation Notes

1 Rejected us Forever (Za-nach-ta la-Ne-tzach): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Despair." The "Sheep" (*Tzon*) of the "Pasture" (*Mar-i-the-kha*) are "Uprooted" from their "Shepherd." It is the "Lament of the Exile."

2 Defiled the Dwelling Place (Chil-le-lu la-Eretz Mish-kan-She-me-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Spatial Sacrilege." The "Sanctuary" (*Miq-dash*) is "Burned" (*Shil-khu ba-Esh*). It signifies the "Uprooting of the Place of Presence."

3 No Prophets are Left (we-En Od Na-bi): This is the "Epistemological Root of the Crisis." In the "Ruins," there is "Silence." No one "Knows How Long" (*Mah-ad-Ma-thay*). It is the "Uprooting of Divine Communication."

4 Heads of Leviathan (Ra-she Li-wya-than): The scrolls highlight the "Chaos-Combat Root." God is the "Warrior King" who "Smashed" (*Rib-ba-tz-ta*) the "Chaos Monster" (*Leviathan*). It is the "Appeal to Primordial Sovereignty." God "Roots" the "Day and Night" (*Verse 16*).

5 Dark Places of the Land (Ma-cha-shak-ke-Eretz): Literally "The dark places of the earth." The scrolls highlight the "Prevalence of Evil." When the "Sanctuary" is "Uprooted," the "Land" is filled with "Violence" (*Cha-mas*). David appeals to the "Covenant" (*Be-rith*).

6 Defend your Cause (Ri-bah Ri-be-kha): The scrolls conclude with this "Legal Imperative." God must "Rise" (*Qu-mah*) because His own "Honor" is at stake. It marks the transition to the "Coming Judgment" of *Psalm 75*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of the "Exiled." Rise up, King.
