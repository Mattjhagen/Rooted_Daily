Psalm 46

For the director of music. Of the Sons of Korah. According to alamoth. A song.

The Ever-Present Help

“God is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in trouble.” 1
“Therefore we will not fear, though the earth give way and the mountains fall into the heart of the sea.”

The River of Gladness

“There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God, the holy place where the Most High dwells.” 2
“God is within her, she will not fall; God will help her at break of day.”
“The Lord Almighty is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress.” Selah

Be Still

“He says, ‘Be still, and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the earth.’” 3
“The Lord Almighty is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress.” Selah

Translation Notes

1 Refuge and Strength (Ma-cha-seh wa-Oz): In the scrolls, "Refuge" (*Ma-cha-seh*) is a "Place of Hiding," and "Strength" (*Oz*) is "Internal Power." God is the "Uprooting of Terror" in the face of "Cosmic Collapse" (*Verse 2*). He is "Ever-Present" (*Nim-tza Me-od* - literally 'found greatly').

2 There is a River (Na-har Pe-la-gaw): The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Source of Peace." While the "Nations Uproar" (*Verse 6*), the "City of God" is "Watered" by a "Silent River." It is the "Root of Spiritual Gladness." God being "Within Her" (*be-Qir-bah*) is the "Anchor of Stability."

3 Be Still (Har-phu): Literally in the scrolls, "Relax/Let go/Slacken your hands." It is the "Command to Cease Striving." It is the "Uprooting of Human Effort." To "Know" (*De-u*) is the "Result of Stillness." God’s "Exaltation" (*A-rum*) is the "Goal of History." It marks the transition to the "Shout" of *Psalm 47*. It is the "Rooted Daily" secret of "Peace." Stop. Let go. He is God.
