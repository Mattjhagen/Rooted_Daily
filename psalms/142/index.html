Psalm 142

A maskil of David. When he was in the cave. A prayer.

The Cave Prayer

“I cry aloud to the Lord; I lift up my voice to the Lord for mercy.”
“I pour out before him my complaint; before him I tell my trouble.” 1

Faint Spirit

“When my spirit grows faint within me, it is you who watch over my way.” 2
“Look and see, there is no one at my right hand; no one is concerned for me.” 3

My Portion

“I cry to you, Lord; I say, ‘You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living.’” 4

Set Me Free

“Set me free from my prison, that I may praise your name.” 5
“Then the righteous will gather about me because of your goodness to me.”

Translation Notes

1 Pour out my Complaint (Esh-pokh le-pha-naw Si-chi): In the scrolls, this is the "Hydraulic Root of Grief." The "Complaint" (*Sich*) is "Poured" (*Shapakh*) like water. It is the "Root of Emotional Release."

2 Spirit grows Faint (be-Hi-ta-teph a-lay Ru-chi): Literally "When my spirit is overwhelmed/shrouded over me." The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Root of Exhaustion." God is the one who "Knows/Watches" (*Ya-da-ta*) the "Path" (*Na-thi-ba-thi*) when the pilgrim is lost. It signifies "Foundational Guidance."

3 No one at my Right Hand (eyn-ma-kir): Literally "There is no one who recognizes/knows me." The scrolls highlight the "Social Isolation Root." The cave is a place where "Belonging" is "Uprooted." It signifies "Existential Loneliness."

4 My Portion (Chel-qi): The scrolls highlight the "Essential Root." When all other "Inheritance" is lost, God is the "Portion" (*Cheleq*) in the "Land of the Living" (*Aretz ha-Chayyim*). It signifies "Ultimate Security."

5 Prison (mi-Mas-ger): Literally "From the enclosure." The "Cave" has become a "Prison" (*Masger*) of the soul. The "Uprooting" from the enclosure leads to "Praise" (*Ho-doth*). It marks the transition to the "Relief in Righteousness" in *Psalm 143*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Secret Refuge." Set me free.
