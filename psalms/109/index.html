Psalm 109

For the director of music. Of David. A psalm.

Do Not Remain Silent

“My God, whom I praise, do not remain silent.” 1
“In return for my friendship they accuse me, but I am a man of prayer.” 2

The Curse Returns

“Appoint an evil man to oppose him; let an accuser stand at his right hand.” 3
“He loved to pronounce a curse—may it come back on him.”

Wounded Heart

“For I am poor and needy, and my heart is wounded within me.” 4
“I fade away like an evening shadow; I am shaken off like a locust.”

At the Right Hand of the Needy

“With my mouth I will greatly extol the Lord; in the great throng I will praise him.”
“For he stands at the right hand of the needy, to save their lives.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Do Not Remain Silent (Al-Te-che-rash): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Divine Intervention." The "Silence" (*Cheresh*) of God is the "Greatest Trial" of the "Praising Soul" (*Te-hil-la-thi*). It is the "Root of Desperate Appeal."

2 I am a Man of Prayer (wa-An-i Phe-phil-lah): Literally "But I am prayer." The scrolls highlight the "Identity Root of the Sufferer." David doesn't just "Pray," his "Existence" is "Rooted" in "Petition." It signify "Total Dependence."

3 An Accuser (Sa-tan): The word here is "Satan," meaning an "Adversary" or "Legal Accuser." The scrolls highlight the "Judgment Root." The "Imprecations" (*Curses*) are a call for the "Enemy’s Slander" to "Uproot" his own life. It is the "Law of Reciprocity."

4 Heart is Wounded (Lib-bi Cha-lal be-Qir-bi): Literally "My heart is pierced within me." The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Root of Betrayal." The "Shadow" (*Tzel*) and "Locust" (*Ar-beh*) imagery signify "Instability and Fading." It is the "Uprooting of Internal Strength."

5 Right Hand of the Needy (Ya-min Eb-yon): The scrolls conclude with this "Inverting Root." While the "Accuser" stands at the "Right Hand" of the wicked (*Verse 6*), God stands at the "Right Hand" (*Yamin*) of the "Needy" (*Ebyon*). It marks the transition to the "Royal Priest" in *Psalm 110*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Justice for the Broken." Save my life.
