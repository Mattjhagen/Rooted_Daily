1 Kings 17

1Now Elijah the Tishbite... said to Ahab, “As the LORD, the God of Israel, lives, whom I serve, there will be neither dew nor rain in the next few years except at my word.” 1

The Kerith Ravine

“You will drink from the brook, and I have directed the ravens to supply you with food there.” 2
“The ravens brought him bread and meat in the morning and bread and meat in the evening, and he drank from the brook.”

The Widow’s Jar

“As surely as the LORD your God lives, I don’t have any bread—only a handful of flour in a jar and a little olive oil in a jug.” 3
“For this is what the LORD says: ‘The jar of flour will not be used up and the jug of oil will not run dry until the day the LORD sends rain on the land.’”

Life Restored

21Then he stretched himself out on the boy three times and cried out to the LORD...
“The LORD heard Elijah’s cry, and the boy’s life returned to him, and he lived.” 4
24Then the woman said to Elijah, “Now I know that you are a man of God and that the word of the LORD from your mouth is the truth.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Neither Dew nor Rain: This was a "Theological Declaration of War" in the scrolls. Baal was worshipped as the "Storm God" and the "Giver of Rain." By stopping the rain, Elijah was essentially saying that Baal was "Powerless" in his own domain. Only the "God of Israel" has control over the heavens.

2 The Ravens: In the scrolls, this is a "Spiritual Irony." Ravens were considered "Unclean Birds" (Leviticus 11), yet God used them to sustain His prophet. It shows that God can use the "Unlikely" and the "Excluded" to accomplish His purpose while the "Wealthy Palace" of Ahab was starving.

3 Zarephath: This location in the scrolls is "Enemy Territory." Zarephath was in Sidon—the home of Jezebel. God was sending Elijah to the "Heart of Baal-Worship" to show that even there, Yahweh is the "Provider." The widow’s willingness to feed the prophet *first* showed a "Faith" that was absent in the royal house of Israel.

4 Life Returned (Va-Tashav Nefesh): Literally in the scrolls, "The Soul returned." This is the first "Resurrection" recorded in the Bible. It showed that the "Word of the LORD" is not just for the "Global Environment" (stopping rain) but for the "Individual Soul." God has power over the "Keys of Death."

5 Now I Know: This confession in the scrolls is the "Goal of the Miracle." The woman had experienced the "Provision" of the jar, but it was the "Resurrection" that confirmed the "Authority" of the Word. It represents the "Shift" from seeing God as a "Utility" (source of food) to seeing Him as the "Truth" (source of life).
