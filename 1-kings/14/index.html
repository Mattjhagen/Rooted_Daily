1 Kings 14

2Jeroboam said to his wife, “Go, disguise yourself so you won’t be recognized... and go to Shiloh. Ahijah the prophet is there.” 1
6Ahijah called out, “Come in, wife of Jeroboam. Why this disguise? I have been sent to you with bad news.”
“I am going to bring disaster on the house of Jeroboam... I will burn up the house of Jeroboam as one burns dung until it is all gone.” 2
“All Israel will mourn for him and bury him. He is the only one in the house of Jeroboam in whom the LORD... has found anything good.” 3

The Spoils of Jerusalem

25In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem.
He carried off the treasures of the temple of the LORD... including all the gold shields Solomon had made. 4
27So King Rehoboam made bronze shields to replace them. 5
30There was continual warfare between Rehoboam and Jeroboam.

Translation Notes

1 Disguise yourself: In the scrolls, this is a "Cynical Paradox." Jeroboam believed Ahijah could predict the future, yet he thought he could "Fool" the prophet with a disguise. It shows the "Schizophrenic Faith" of Jeroboam—acknowledging God’s power but trying to manipulate it.

2 As one burns Dung: This is an "Extremely Offensive Metaphor" in the scrolls. It indicates that the house of Jeroboam had become "Filth" in the land, a useless waste that needed to be completely eradicated to "Cleanse" the kingdom. It was a sentence of "Total Wipeout."

3 Only Good One: In the scrolls, the death of the child Abijah was actually a "Mercy." God was "Removing" him from the coming disaster because he was the only one in the family who still had a "Heart for the LORD." His death was the "Last Grace" shown to Jeroboam’s line.

4 Shishak’s Invasion: This is a "Historical Reality" confirmed by Egyptian records. In the scrolls, it is the "Physical Judgment" for Judah’s idolatry (high places and shrine prostitutes). The "Treasures of Solomon" were gone in a single generation, proving that "Wealth without Worship" is transitory.

5 Bronze Shields: This is one of the most "Pathetic Details" in the scrolls. Solomon’s "Gold" (glory) was replaced by Rehoboam’s "Bronze" (imitation). It perfectly symbolizes the "Decline of the Kingdom"—maintaining the "Appearance" of power while the "Substance" is gone. The "Rooted" identity was being replaced by a "Counterfeit."
