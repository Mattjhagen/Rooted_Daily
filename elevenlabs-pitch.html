High-Efficiency Caching Strategy

In TTSService.ts, every chapter query is hashed using CryptoJS.MD5(cleanedText + voiceId). Audio is stored in audio_cache/ on the client. Over 80% of repeat chapter listens require zero API calls, drastically reducing COGS.

Resilient Fallback Hierarchy

Tier 1: ElevenLabs High-Fidelity (Default) → Tier 2: OpenAI TTS (Network failover) → Tier 3: Native On-Device Speech. The listener never experiences a crashed or silent session even in airplane mode.

What does your company do? (Elevator Pitch)
Rooted Daily is a next-generation Bible reading and reflection platform designed to help millions of believers build lasting daily devotional habits. Our app features a completely offline 66-book KJV Bible engine, structured 365-day reading tracks, and AI reflection journaling. We are integrating ElevenLabs to turn the entire Bible into a living, emotionally nuanced audiobook featuring multi-voice character dramatization, family voice cloning, and atmospheric soundscapes.
How are you using or planning to use ElevenLabs?
We are integrating ElevenLabs across four distinct dimensions: 1. Dynamic Multi-Voice Dramatization: Using Turbo v2.5 with automated speaker classification to narrate scripture with distinct, reverent voices for the Narrator, Jesus, Moses, David, and the Apostles. 2. Instant Voice Cloning (IVC): Enabling users to clone their own voice, their pastor's voice, or an elderly grandparent's voice to hear daily devotionals in beloved voices. 3. Spoken AI Reflection Companion: Transforming our reflection journal into an interactive, voice-first spiritual dialogue powered by ElevenLabs Conversational AI. 4. Procedural Foley Sound Effects: Adding subtle atmospheric soundscapes (Galilee waves, temple echoes, desert breeze) to elevate meditation and focus. Our production app already includes MD5-hashed client caching (TTSService.ts) and ElevenLabs Voice ID settings.
What problem does this solve for your users?
Over 80% of Christians who attempt reading through the Bible abandon their goal within the first month due to screen fatigue, dense text, and busy modern lifestyles. Traditional audio Bibles are either 30-year-old monotone recordings, robotic TTS that strips scripture of sacred reverence, or $2M+ static studio productions that cannot be adapted or personalized. Rooted Daily delivers an intimate, emotionally expressive, personalized listening experience that fits effortlessly into daily commutes, exercise, and bedtime routines while providing full accessibility for visually impaired and dyslexic believers.

What is your business model?
Rooted Daily operates on a freemium model: - Free Tier: Offline 66-book Bible reader, 365-day reading tracks, daily Verse of the Day in ElevenLabs voice, and standard on-device TTS. - Rooted Premium ($7.99/month or $59.99/year): Unlimited ElevenLabs dramatized multi-voice audio, custom voice cloning integration, atmospheric soundscapes, and spoken conversational AI journal reflections. Because our offline engine and local MD5 audio caching layer store synthesized audio permanently on device and via edge CDN, our unit economics yield an 82%+ gross margin on premium subscriptions.
