Ezra 1

The Proclamation of Cyrus

In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia... the LORD moved the heart of Cyrus to make a proclamation throughout his realm. 1
“The LORD, the God of heaven... has appointed me to build a temple for him at Jerusalem.”

The Stirring of the Spirit

Then the family heads of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests and Levites—everyone whose heart God had moved—prepared to go up and build the house of the LORD. 2

The Return of the Vessels

King Cyrus brought out the articles belonging to the temple of the LORD, which Nebuchadnezzar had carried away from Jerusalem. 3
“In all, there were 5,400 articles of gold and of silver.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Moved the Heart (He’ir): Literally in the scrolls, "Stirred up" or "Awakened." This is the "Root of Restoration." The Exile didn't end because of a "Political Deal," but because God "Awakened" a superpower king. It proves that the "Presence" of God can operate even through those who don't know Him (Isaiah 45:4).

2 Everyone whose Heart God had Moved: The scrolls highlight that the return was "Selective." Not everyone left Babylon. Only those who felt the "Stirring" in their "Roots" were willing to leave their comfort and rebuild the ruins. It marks the birth of the "Remnant."

3 Articles (Kelei): In the scrolls, these were the "Vessels of Service." They had been "Desecrated" by Belshazzar (Daniel 5), but they were still "Holy to the LORD." Cyrus was returning the "Tools of Worship." It signifies that the "Continuity" of the Temple was being preserved.

4 5,400 Articles: The scrolls record this "Precise Inventory." Every dish and bowl was "Accounted For." It proves that God "Tracked" the details of His house even during 70 years of "Desolation." Nothing was "Lost" in the Exile that God intended to "Restore." It is the "Rooted" promise of "Total Recovery."
