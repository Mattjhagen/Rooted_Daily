Ezra 2

These are the people of the province who came up from the captivity of the exiles... they returned to Jerusalem and Judah, each to their own town. 1

The Holy Seed

“They came with Zerubbabel, Joshua, Nehemiah...”
“The priests: the descendants of Jedaiah...”
“The temple servants: the descendants of Ziha...” 2

The Total Count

“The whole company numbered 42,360...”
“Besides their 7,337 male and female servants; and they also had 200 male and female singers.” 3

The Quest for Identity

“These searched for their family records, but they could not find them and so were excluded from the priesthood as unclean.” 4
“Until a priest should minister with the Urim and Thummim.”

The Foundations of Giving

“When they arrived at the house of the LORD in Jerusalem, some of the family heads gave free-will offerings...” 5

Translation Notes

1 Each to their own Town: In the scrolls, this is the "Re-rooting of the Map." They weren't just "Refugees"; they were "Heirs" returning to their "Ancestral Soil." The list of towns (*Bethlehem, Anathoth, etc.*) shows that the "Geography of the Promise" was being re-occupied.

2 Temple Servants (Nethinim): Literally in the scrolls, "The Given Ones." These were originally foreigners (like the Gibeonites) given to the Levites for Temple work. The fact that they returned from Babylon to "Serve" in a ruined Temple shows a "Deep Rooted Loyalty" to the God of Israel that surpassed many Israelites.

3 42,360: The scrolls record this as the "Seed of the New Nation." Compared to the millions who left Egypt, this was a small number. But it was a "Purified Remnant." It signifies that God can rebuild an entire "Civilization" from a "Handful of Faithful Hearts."

4 Could not find their Records: In the scrolls, "Identity" was the "Curse of the Exile." Seventy years in Babylon had "Blurred the Lines." Those who couldn't "Prove their Roots" were kept from the Priesthood. It shows that "Holy Service" requires "Holy Certainty."

5 Free-will Offerings (Nedavah): The scrolls conclude the census with "Generosity." Before they had "Houses," they gave for the "House of God." They were "Investing in the Foundation." The 61,000 darics of gold were a "Statement of Faith" that the "Future" was in Jerusalem, not Babylon.
