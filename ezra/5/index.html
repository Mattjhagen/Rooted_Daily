Ezra 5

The Prophetic Ignition

Now Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the prophet... prophesied to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem in the name of the God of Israel. 1
“Then Zerubbabel and Joshua set to work to rebuild the house of God... the prophets of God were with them, supporting them.” 2

The Eye of God

“But the eye of their God was on the elders of the Jews, and they were not stopped until a report could go to Darius.” 3

The Answer of the Remnant

“We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are rebuilding the temple that was built many years ago.” 4
“Now if it pleases the king, let a search be made in the royal archives...” 5

Translation Notes

1 Prophesied to the Jews (Nitnabbi): In the scrolls, this is the "Breakdown of the Standstill." The work didn't resume because the "Law" changed, but because the "Heart" changed through the Word. Haggai and Zechariah were the "Bellows" that reignited the "Root" of the people’s zeal.

2 Supporting Them (Mesay-adin): Literally in the scrolls, "Helping them." The prophets didn't just "Talk"; they "Worked." They were the "Spiritual Architects" who stood on the ruins alongside the builders. It marks the "Re-rooting" of the project in "Prophetic Encouragement."

3 Eye of their God (En Elah-hom): The scrolls highlight this "Divine Surveillance." While the "Eyes of the Governor" were on the stone and timber, the "Eye of God" was on the elders. It was a "Shield of Favor" that prevented a second "Standstill." God was "Watching the Watchers."

4 Servants of the God of Heaven (Abdu-hi di-Elah Shamayya): In the scrolls, this is a "Bold and Aramaic Identity." They didn't identify themselves as "Citizens of Judah" but as "Servants of God." They were claiming an "Authority" that bypassed the "Trans-Euphrates Bureaucracy."

5 Search the Royal Archives (Ginzayya): The scrolls record this "Appeal to History." They knew that the "Truth" was on their side. They were "Rooting" their legal defense in the "Documented Promise" of the past. It shows that "Spiritual Zeal" must be accompanied by "Practical Integrity."
