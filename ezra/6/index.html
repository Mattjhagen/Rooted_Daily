Ezra 6

The Decree of Darius

“Do not interfere with the work on this temple of God. Let the governor and the Jewish elders rebuild this house of God on its site.” 1
“The expenses are to be fully paid out of the royal treasury.”

The Temple Completed

“So the elders of the Jews continued to build and prosper under the preaching of Haggai and Zechariah... They finished the building.” 2
“Then the people of Israel celebrated the dedication with joy.” 3

The Joy of the Return

“So the Israelites who had returned from the exile ate it, together with all who had separated themselves from the unclean practices of their Gentile neighbors.” 4
“The LORD had filled them with joy by changing the attitude of the king of Assyria.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Do not Interfere (Rachoqin): Literally in the Aramaic scrolls, "Keep far away." Darius was "Re-rooting" the project in the legal precedent of Cyrus. He turned the "Obstacle of the Governor" into the "Support of the Treasury." It proves that the "Enemy’s Investigation" can become "God’s Provision."

2 Continued to Build and Prosper (Matz-lichin): The scrolls highlight the "Synergy" of the Prophetic Word and the Practical Work. The "Prospering" wasn't just "Economic," but "Structural." They "Finished" the building after decades of delay. The "Root" had finally broken through the "Soil of Opposition."

3 Dedication with Joy (Chanukkatah be-Chedvah): In the scrolls, this is the "Small and Holy Beginning." It was not as grand as Solomon’s dedication, but it was "Precious" because of the "Persistence" it represented. It signifies that "Restoration Joy" is deeper than "Initial Glory."

4 Separated themselves (ha-Nibdal): This is a "Key Rooted Term" in the scrolls. The returnees didn't just celebrate "Paschal Rituals"; they practiced "Ethical Separation." They refused the "Unclean Practices" of their neighbors. "Presence" always requires "Distinctiveness."

5 Changing the Attitude (He-Seb): Literally in the scrolls, "He turned the heart." God had "Turned" the heart of the world’s most powerful ruler (Darius) to "Favor" the most vulnerable people (the Jews). It is the "Chronicler’s Masterpiece" of Sovereignty: when you are "Rooted" in God, He "Rotates" the world to serve His purpose.
