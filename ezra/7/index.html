Ezra 7

The Scribe of the Heart

Ezra came up from Babylon. He was a teacher well versed in the Law of Moses... for the hand of the LORD his God was on him. 1

The Secret of the Return

“For Ezra had devoted himself to the study and observance of the Law of the LORD, and to teaching its decrees and laws in Israel.” 2

The Royal Authorization

“King Artaxerxes: ‘Whatever Ezra the priest... may ask of you, let it be done with all diligence.’” 3
“Appoint magistrates and judges to administer justice... and teach any who do not know them.” 4

Ezra’s Response

“Praise be to the LORD... who has put it into the king’s heart to bring honor to the house of the LORD in Jerusalem.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Hand of the LORD was on Him (Yad YHWH): In the scrolls, this is the "Empowerment of the Remnant." Ezra’s "Expertise" wasn't just academic; it was "Divinely Enabled." He was a "Rooted Intellectual" whose "Mind" was a "Weapon" for the restoration of Zion.

2 Devoted Himself (Hekhin Levavo): Literally in the scrolls, "He established his heart." This is a "Three-Step Process": 1. *Lidrosh* (Seek/Study), 2. *La'asot* (Observe/Do), 3. *Le-lammed* (Teach). Ezra didn't teach what he didn't practice. He was "Rooting" the nation back in the "Word" by living it first. It is the "Chronicler’s Blueprint" for spiritual revival.

3 With all Diligence (Osnarna): This Aramaic term in the scrolls suggests "Exacting Speed." Artaxerxes was essentially giving Ezra a "Blank Check" from the Persian treasury. It proves that when a "Man is Prepared" (Ezra), "God prepares the Resources" (Artaxerxes).

4 Teach any who do not Know: The scrolls record this as the "Mandate for Education." Ezra wasn't just rebuilding a "Building"; he was rebuilding a "Mindset." He was authorized to create a "Covenant Judiciary." It was the "Institutional Re-rooting" of the nation.

5 I took Courage (Hith-Chazzaq-ti): The scrolls conclude with Ezra’s "Internal Shift." Seeing the "King’s Heart" moved gave Ezra the "Boldness" to lead the journey. He realized that the "Invisible Hand" of God was "Visible" in the Persian decree. His "Courage" was the "Fruit" of his "Confidence" in God’s Sovereignty.
