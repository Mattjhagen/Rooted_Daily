Esther 6

The Sovereign Insomnia

“That night the king could not sleep; so he ordered the book of the chronicles, the record of his reign, to be brought in and read to him.” 1

The Forgotten Honor

“The king asked, ‘What honor and recognition has Mordecai received for this?’ ‘Nothing has been done for him,’ his attendants answered.” 2

The Master Reversal

“The King to Haman: ‘Go at once! Get the robe and the horse and do just as you have suggested for Mordecai the Jew!’” 3
“Haman led him through the city streets, proclaiming before him: ‘This is what is done for the man the king delights to honor!’” 4
“Since Mordecai... is of Jewish origin, you cannot stand against him—you will surely come to ruin!” 5

Translation Notes

1 The King could not Sleep (Nadadah Shenath): Literally in the scrolls, "The King’s sleep fled." This is the "Invisible Hand" of God at work. The entire fate of the Jewish people was "Rooted" in a single night of royal insomnia. God didn't use a "Miracle of Fire," but a "Miracle of Timing."

2 Nothing has been Done: The scrolls highlight the "Delayed Reward." Mordecai had been "Forgotten" for years (*Chapter 2*). This delay was "Providential." If he had been rewarded then, Haman would not have been the one to honor him now. God was "Storing the Honor" for the "Perfect Moment."

3 Do as you have Suggested (Maher): Xerxes’ command to "Hurry" is the "Supreme Irony" of the scrolls. Haman had come to the palace to "Uproot" Mordecai (on the gallows), but he was ordered to "Exalt" him. The "Plan of the Enemy" became the "Script of the Deliverance."

4 Proclaiming before Him: In the scrolls, this is the "Humiliation of the Amalekite." Haman, the descendant of Agag, was forced to act as the "Herald" for a descendant of Kish. It is the "Rooted" reversal of the ancient conflict. The "Mocker" was made the "Minion."

5 You will Surely Come to Ruin (Naphol Tippol): Literally in the scrolls, "Falling, you will fall." Haman’s own household recognized the "Unstoppable Force" of the "Jewish Root." They realized that when the "Exaltation of Mordecai" began, the "Destruction of Haman" was already "Sealed." It marks the "Beginning of the End" for the Agagite.
