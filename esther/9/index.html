Esther 9

The Day of the Reverse

“On the very day when the enemies of the Jews had hoped to overpower them, the reverse occurred, and the Jews got the upper hand.” 1
“They killed the ten sons of Haman... but they did not lay their hands on the plunder.” 2

The Institution of Purim

“Therefore these days were called Purim, from the word Pur. Because of everything written in this letter... the Jews took it on themselves to celebrate these two days every year.” 3
“Days of feasting and joy and giving presents of food to one another and gifts to the poor.” 4
“These days should be remembered and observed by every generation... and their memory should never die out among their descendants.” 5

Translation Notes

1 The Reverse Occurred (Na-hapokh Hu): Literally in the scrolls, "It was overturned." This is the "Root of the Reversal." What Haman had intended for the destruction of the Jews became the platform for their "Exaltation." It proves that the "Providence of God" can flip any "Script of the Enemy."

2 Did not lay their hands on the Plunder (u-ba-Bizzah lo Shalkhu): This is a "Key Integrity Point" in the scrolls. Unlike their ancestors who failed by taking the plunder of Amalek (1 Samuel 15), the "Remnant" refused to profit from the battle. They were "Rooting" their victory in "Justice" rather than "Greed." They were "Finishing the Job" correctly.

3 Purim (Lots): The scrolls highlight the "Irony of the Name." The festival is named after the "Tool of the Enemy" (*the Lot*). It signifies that what the world uses for "Chance" and "Doom," God uses for "Covenant" and "Deliverance." The "Pur" was "Re-rooted" in Jewish history.

4 Gifts to the Poor (Matanoth la-Ebyonim): In the scrolls, "Celebration" must include "Compassion." A "Rooted Joy" is one that "Spills Over" to those who have nothing. They were "Institutionalizing Generosity" as part of their "Identity."

5 Memory should never Die Out: The scrolls and the chapter emphasize "Generational Continuity." They weren't just "Celebrating a Win"; they were "Planting a Legacy." They wanted the "Root of the Deliverance" to be watered by every "Future Generation" of the Jews. It is the "Rooted" guarantee of "Survival."
