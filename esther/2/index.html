Esther 2

The Secret of Hadassah

“Mordecai had a cousin named Hadassah, whom he had brought up because she had neither father nor mother. This young woman, also known as Esther, had a lovely figure and was beautiful.” 1
“Esther had not revealed her nationality and family background, because Mordecai had forbidden her to do so.” 2

The Twelve-Month Consecration

“Before a young woman’s turn came to go in to King Xerxes, she had to complete twelve months of beauty treatments... six months with oil of myrrh and six with perfumes and cosmetics.” 3

The Crown of Persia

“The king was attracted to Esther more than to any of the other women... so he set a royal crown on her head and made her queen.” 4

The Vigilance of Mordecai

“During the time Mordecai was sitting at the king’s gate, Bigthana and Teresh... conspired to assassinate King Xerxes.”
“The two officials were impaled on poles. All this was recorded in the book of the annals.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Hadassah (Myrtle): In the scrolls, Esther’s original Hebrew name is "Myrtle"—a plant that is resilient and fragrant even when crushed. Her Persian name "Esther" (Ishtar/Star) was her "Outer Mask." She was a "Rooted Jewish Soul" hidden in an "Imperial Role."

2 Forbidden Her: The scrolls highlight the "Strategic Secrecy." Mordecai knew that the "Anti-Jewish" sentiment in Susa was a "Dormant Volcano." By keeping her identity hidden, he was "Rooting" Esther as a "Secret Weapon" for a future crisis. It signifies that "Timing" is as important as "Truth."

3 Oil of Myrrh (Mor): This is a "Sobering Detail" in the scrolls. Myrrh was often used for "Burials." The 12 months of preparation was a "Death to the Old Life" and a "Birth to the Royal Life." Esther was being "Purified" of her "Past" to inhabit her "Purpose."

4 The King was Attracted: Literally in the scrolls, "She won favor in his eyes." This was more than "Physical Beauty." Esther possessed a "Spirit of Grace" that the other women lacked. It was the "Presence of God" making her "Root" beautiful to a pagan king.

5 Recorded in the Book of Annals (Sepher Dibre ha-Yamim): The scrolls conclude with this "Administrative Detail." Mordecai saved the King, but he was "Not Rewarded" immediately. He was "Forgotten" by men but "Recorded" in the history. It signifies that "God’s Ledger" is never closed, and the "Forgotten Deed" will become the "Future Key."
