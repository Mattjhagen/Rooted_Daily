2 Chronicles 30

Hezekiah sent word to all Israel and Judah... inviting them to come to the temple of the LORD in Jerusalem to celebrate the Passover. 1
“Return to the LORD... that he may return to the remnant of you who have escaped.”

The Mockery

“The couriers went from town to town... but the people laughed and mocked them.” 2

The Unity

“Also in Judah the hand of God was on the people to give them unity of mind to carry out what the king had ordered.” 3

Hezekiah’s Prayer of Grace

“May the LORD, who is good, pardon everyone who sets their heart on seeking God... even if they are not clean according to the rules.” 4
“The LORD heard Hezekiah and healed the people.”

The Double Festival

The whole assembly decided to celebrate the festival seven more days; so they celebrated joyfully for another seven days. 5
“There was great joy in Jerusalem, for since the days of Solomon... there had been nothing like this.”

Translation Notes

1 Sent Word to All Israel: In the scrolls, this is a "Pan-Israelite Invitation." Hezekiah was attempting to "Heal the Breach" between the two kingdoms. He realized that the "Root" of the nation was one, even if the "Geography" was divided. It was an act of "Radical Spiritual Diplomacy."

2 Laughed and Mocked (Sachaq we-La’ag): The scrolls record this "Tragic Resistance" in the North. After decades of idolatry, the invitation to return to Jerusalem seemed "Absurd" to them. However, those who "Humbled themselves" (*Verse 11*) found the "Remedy" for their national trauma.

3 Unity of Mind (Lev Echad): Literally in the scrolls, "One Heart." This is the "Hand of God" in action. True unity is not "Enforced" by a king but "Inspired" by God. It made the massive logistics of the Passover possible.

4 Healed the People (Way-Yirpa): In the scrolls, this "Healing" was in response to Hezekiah’s "Intercession." It establishes the "Rooted" principle that "Heart-Seeking" is more vital than "Ritual-Purity." God was willing to bypass the "Technicality" to reward the "Sincerity."

5 Seven more Days: The scrolls highlight the "Spontaneity of the Spirit." They didn't want to go home. The joy was so "Abundant" that the legal requirement (7 days) was doubled. It was the first time since Solomon that the "National Atmosphere" had been so "Saturated" with the presence of God.
