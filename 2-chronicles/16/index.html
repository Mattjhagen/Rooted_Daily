2 Chronicles 16

Asa took the silver and gold from the treasuries of the temple and the palace and sent it to Ben-Hadad king of Aram. 1

The Eyes of the LORD

Hanani the seer came to Asa: “Because you relied on the king of Aram and not on the LORD your God, the army of the king of Aram has escaped from your hand.”
“For the eyes of the LORD range throughout the earth to strengthen those whose hearts are fully committed to him. You have done a foolish thing.” 2

The Hardening of the Heart

Asa was angry with the seer because of this; he was so enraged that he put him in prison. 3

The Disease of the Feet

Though his disease was severe, even in his illness he did not seek help from the LORD, but only from the physicians. 4
“Asa rested with his ancestors... they made a huge fire in his honor.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sent it to Ben-Hadad: In the scrolls, this is a "Spiritual Relapse." Asa used the "Sacred Wealth" of God to buy the help of a "Pagan King." He forgot the miracle of the million-man Cushite army (Chapter 14) and chose "Political Expediency" over "Divine Reliance." It was a "Withering of the Root."

2 Eyes of the LORD (Enai YHWH): This is a "Stunning Metaphor" in the scrolls. It portrays God as "Actively Searching" for a heart He can "Strongly Support." He is not a "Passive Observer." Asa’s "Foolishness" was thinking that God was "Absent" from the geopolitical stage.

3 Asa was Angry: In the scrolls, this is the "Tragedy of the Reformer." Asa had the courage to depose his grandmother (Chapter 15), but he did not have the humility to hear his own "Sin" exposed. His "Pride" led to the "Imprisonment of the Truth."

4 Only from the Physicians (Rophim): In the scrolls, this is a "Symbol of a Severed Connection." It doesn't mean medical help is wrong, but that Asa replaced "Prayer" with "Technique." His "Feet" were diseased, perhaps a metaphor for his "Walk" with God which had become crippled by "Self-Sufficiency."

5 Huge Fire: The scrolls conclude with a "Paradoxical Honor." Asa was given a "Royal Funeral," but the "Internal Flame" of his faith had gone out long before. He left a legacy of "Mixed Results"—starting in the Spirit and ending in the Flesh. It serves as a "Rooted" warning that a "Good Start" does not guarantee a "Good Finish."
