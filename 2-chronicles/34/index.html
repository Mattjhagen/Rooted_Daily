2 Chronicles 34

In the eighth year of his reign, while he was still young, he began to seek the God of his father David. 1

The Finding of the Law

“Hilkiah the priest found the Book of the Law of the LORD that had been given through Moses.” 2
“When the king heard the words of the Law, he tore his robes.” 3

The Message of Huldah

“Because your heart was responsive and you humbled yourself... I have heard you, declares the LORD.” 4

The Renewal

The king stood by his pillar and renewed the covenant in the presence of the LORD—to follow the LORD and keep his commands... with all his heart and all his soul. 5

Translation Notes

1 While he was still Young (we-hu od Na’ar): In the scrolls, Josiah was 16 when he began his "Personal Seeking." He didn't wait for "Political Maturity" to pursue "Spiritual Reality." He was "Rooted" in the God of David before he was faced with the "Idolatry" of his own kingdom. It is a "Rooted" testimony to the power of a "Young Heart."

2 Found the Book (Sepher): The scrolls record this "Sobering Discovery." The Law of God had been "Lost" in the very Temple built to house it. It shows how "Religion" can continue as a "Shell" long after the "Word" has been discarded. Finding the Book was like "Rediscovering the Foundation" of the house while it was being repaired.

3 He Tore his Robes (Way-Yiqra): In the scrolls, this is the "Reaction of Conviction." Josiah realized the "Gap" between the "Standard of God" and the "State of the Nation." His grief was "Immediate and Visceral." He didn't "Debate the Text"; he "Obeyed the Weight" of it. It was the "Root" of his greatness.

4 Heart was Responsive (Rak-Libbekha): Literally in the scrolls, "Your heart was tender/soft." This is the "Key to Mercy." Because Josiah didn't "Harden his Root" against the Word, God delayed the "Judgment." One "Tender Heart" in the palace could hold back the "Storm" for an entire generation.

5 With all his Heart and Soul: The scrolls conclude with the "National Pledge." Josiah didn't just "Read" the Law; he "Enforced" the Covenant. He realized that a "Rooted" king must lead a "Rooted" people. This was the "Last Great Light" before the darkness of the Exile.
