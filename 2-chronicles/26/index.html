2 Chronicles 26

“As long as he sought the LORD, God gave him success.” 1

The Lover of the Soil

“He had many livestock... he had people working his fields and vineyards, for he loved the soil.” 2

The Tech King

“In Jerusalem he made devices... to shoot arrows and hurl large stones.” 3

The Boundary of Pride

“But after Uzziah became powerful, his pride led to his downfall. He was unfaithful to the LORD...” 4
“While he was raging at the priests, leprosy broke out on his forehead.” 5
“King Uzziah had leprosy until the day he died. He lived in a separate house... excluded from the temple of the LORD.”

Translation Notes

1 As long as he Sought: In the scrolls, this is the "Secret of the 52-Year Reign." Uzziah’s success was not "Mechanical" but "Relational." His "Roots" were in the counsel of the prophet Zechariah. When he was "Rooted," he was "Unstoppable."

2 Loved the Soil (Ohev Adamah): Literally in the scrolls, "A Lover of the Earth." This is a "Rare Commendation" for a king. Uzziah wasn't just interested in "Power"; he was interested in "Provision." He saw the "Kingdom" as a "Garden" to be tended. It is a "Rooted" leadership quality.

3 Devices (Chishshabonot): In the scrolls, this refers to "Ingenious Inventions." Uzziah turned Jerusalem into a "Fortress of Technology." He used "Engineering" to protect the "Presence." His "Fame" spread because he was doing what no other king had done.

4 Pride led to Downfall (Gabhah Libbo): Literally in the scrolls, "His heart was lifted up." Unlike Jehoshaphat (whose heart was lifted in the *ways* of the LORD), Uzziah’s heart was lifted *above* the LORD. He forgot that he was a "Vassal King" and tried to act as a "Priest King." He tried to "Graft" himself into a role God had not assigned.

5 Leprosy on his Forehead (Tzara’at): In the scrolls, the location of the disease was "Ironic." The forehead was where the High Priest wore the "Holy to the LORD" plate. Uzziah tried to seize the "Holiness of the Priesthood," so God marked his "Forehead" with the "Uncleanliness of Leprosy." He began his reign "Seeking God" and ended it "Excluded from the House."
