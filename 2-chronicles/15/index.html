2 Chronicles 15

The Word of Azariah

“The LORD is with you when you are with him. If you seek him, he will be found by you, but if you forsake him, he will forsake you.” 1
“But as for you, be strong and do not give up, for your work will be rewarded.”

The National Oath

They entered into a covenant to seek the LORD, the God of their ancestors, with all their heart and soul. 2
“All Judah rejoiced about the oath, because they had sworn it wholeheartedly.” 3

The Removal of Maakah

King Asa also deposed his grandmother Maakah from her position as queen mother, because she had made a repulsive Asherah pole. 4
“Asa cut it down, broke it up and burned it in the Kidron Valley.”
“There was no more war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa’s reign.” 5

Translation Notes

1 If You Seek Him (Be-Doreshkhem): In the scrolls, this is the "Golden Rule of Presence." Seeking is not a "Passive Wish" but an "Active Pursuit." Azariah was grounding the national security of Judah in their "Spiritual Proximity" to God. If they "Rooted" themselves in Him, He would "Inhabit" them.

2 Seek with all their Heart and Soul: This was a "Holistic Covenant" in the scrolls. It wasn't just a "Legal Agreement" but an "Emotional Surrender." By including the "Defectors" from the North (Ephraim and Manasseh), Asa was creating a "New Israel" based on "Devotion" rather than "Geography."

3 Rejoiced about the Oath: In the scrolls, this is the "Joy of Alignment." Usually, an oath of death is terrifying, but for a people "Rooted" in God, it was "Freeing." It removed the "Ambiguity" of their lives and gave them a "Single Focus."

4 Repulsive Asherah (Miphletzet): Literally in the scrolls, "A Horror" or "A Thing of Terror." It likely refers to a "Phallic or Grotesque" idol. Asa’s willingness to "Depose his Grandmother" shows that his "Covenant Loyalty" was deeper than his "Blood Loyalty." He was willing to prune the "Royal Branch" to save the "National Root."

5 No More War: The scrolls conclude this phase of Asa’s reign with the "Dividend of Peace." Twenty years of "Stability" followed the reform. It proves that "Spiritual Order" produces "Geopolitical Rest." This was the "Reward" for their "Wholehearted Seeking."
