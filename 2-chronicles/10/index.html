2 Chronicles 10

“Your father put a heavy yoke on us, but now lighten the harsh labor and the heavy yoke he put on us, and we will serve you.” 1

The Choice of Two Voices

The elders said, “If you will be kind to these people and please them and give them a favorable answer, they will always be your servants.” 2
“But Rehoboam rejected the advice the elders gave him and consulted the young men who had grown up with him.”

The Harsh Answer

“My father scourged you with whips; I will scourge you with scorpions.” 3
“So the king did not listen to the people, for this turn of events was from God.” 4
“What share do we have in David? ... To your tents, Israel! Look after your own house, David!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Heavy Yoke (Ol Qasheh): In the scrolls, this is the "Shadow of the Golden Age." While Solomon brought "Wealth" to the nation, it came at the cost of "Forced Labor" and high taxation. The people were "Rooted" in a memory of burden that now threatened to "Shatter" the unity of the tribes.

2 Be Kind and Please them (Towb): The elders’ advice in the scrolls was to be a "Servant King." They understood that "Loyalty" is a "Reciprocal Root." If the King "Serves" the people, the people will "Serve" the King. Rehoboam’s "Rejection" of this wisdom was a "Severing of the Root."

3 Scorpions (Aqrabim): In the scrolls, this is a "Metaphor for Terror." Scorpions were whips with "Barbed Hooks" or "Metal Pieces." Rehoboam was trying to rule through "Fear" instead of "Covenant." He mistook "Arrogance" for "Strength."

4 Turn of Events from God (Sibbah): Literally in the scrolls, "The Turning." This is the "Chronicler’s Sovereignty Note." Even Rehoboam’s "Folly" was being used to "Fulfill" the divine judgment spoken against Solomon’s later years. It shows that "Human Choices" and "Divine Destiny" are "Intertwined."

5 Look after your own House, David: The scrolls conclude with the "Great Fracture." The "House of David" was reduced to the "Tribe of Judah." The "United Monarchy" was dead. The "Northern Tribes" (Israel) chose a "New Path" that would eventually lead them away from the "Temple" and into "Idolatry."
