2 Chronicles 23

Jehoiada said to them, “The king’s son shall reign, as the LORD promised concerning the descendants of David.” 1

The Coronation

Jehoiada and his sons brought out the king’s son and put the crown on him; they presented him with a copy of the covenant and proclaimed him king. 2
“Long live the king!”

The Cry of Athaliah

“She saw the king standing by his pillar at the entrance... Athaliah tore her robes and shouted, ‘Treason! Treason!’” 3
“They seized her and she was put to death at the entrance of the Horse Gate.” 4

The Covenant Renewed

“Jehoiada made a covenant between himself and all the people and the king that they would be the LORD’s people.” 5
“All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet.”

Translation Notes

1 The King’s Son Shall Reign: In the scrolls, this is the "Legal and Prophetic Imperative." Jehoiada wasn't just doing a "Coup"; he was "Restoring the Constitutional Order." The "Promise to David" was the "Root" that Athaliah could not destroy. Jehoiada was the "Gardener" who brought the "Hidden Sprout" into the light.

2 Presented him with the Covenant (Edut): Literally in the scrolls, "The Testimony." This was the "Charter of the Kingdom." A Davidic king was not an "Absolute Dictator"; he was a "Vassal of the Covenant." The crown and the book belonged together. It signifies a "Rooted Authority" based on "Divine Law."

3 Treason! (Qesher): There is a "Bitter Irony" in the scrolls here. Athaliah, the "Greatest Traitor" in the history of Judah (who had murdered the entire royal family), was accusing the "Restorers" of treason. It shows how "Tyranny" often "Projects" its own crimes onto its victims.

4 Horse Gate (Sha’ar ha-Sussim): The scrolls record that she was killed "Outside the Sanctuary." Jehoiada was careful not to "Defile the Temple" with the blood of the "Usurper." Her death at the Horse Gate signifies the "Ending of her Royal Ride." The "House of Ahab" was finally purged from Judah.

5 They would be the LORD’s People: This was a "Tri-partite Covenant" in the scrolls (Priest, People, King). It wasn't just about "Political Stability," but about "Spiritual Identity." The "Quietness" of the city (*Verse 21*) was the "Fruit" of this "Right Alignment." The "Roots" were back in the "Holy Soil."
