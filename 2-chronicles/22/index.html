2 Chronicles 22

Ahaziah too followed the ways of the house of Ahab, for his mother encouraged him to act wickedly. 1
“Through Ahaziah’s visit to Joram, God brought about his downfall.” 2

The Murderous Queen

When Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she proceeded to destroy the whole royal family of the house of Judah. 3

The Hidden Seed

“Jehosheba... took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away... she put him and his nurse in a bedroom.” 4
“He remained hidden with them at the temple of God for six years while Athaliah ruled the land.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Mother Encouraged Him (Yo'atzto): Literally in the scrolls, "She was his counselor." This is the "Inversion of Order." A mother, who should be a "Root of Nurture," became a "Root of Wickedness." Athaliah, the daughter of Jezebel, was transplanting the "Baal-System" into the throne of Judah.

2 Downfall was from God (Tebusat): Literally in the scrolls, "The Trampling." Ahaziah’s death at the hands of Jehu was not a "Political Accident" but a "Judicial Sentence." He had "Rooted" himself in the house of Ahab, and he shared in its "Uprooting."

3 Destroy the whole Royal Family: In the scrolls, this is the "Ultimate Assault on the Promise." By killing the descendants of David, Athaliah was attempting to "Cut the Root" from which the Messiah would come. It was a "Satanic Strategic Strike" against the "House of God."

4 Jehosheba (Yehosheba): Her name in the scrolls means "Yahweh is an Oath." She was the "Instrument of Preservation." As the wife of the High Priest, she had access to the Temple. She "Rooted" the future of the nation in the "Security of the Sanctuary."

5 Hidden in the Temple: The scrolls record this "Divine Paradox." While the "Wicked Queen" ruled the land from the "Palace," the "True King" was being "Nurtured" in the "Temple." It represents the "Quiet Growth" of the Kingdom of God in the midst of "Public Chaos." For six years, the "Root" was underground, preparing for the "Sprout."
