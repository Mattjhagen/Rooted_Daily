2 Chronicles 36

Zedekiah did evil in the eyes of the LORD his God and did not humble himself before Jeremiah the prophet, who spoke the word of the LORD. 1

The Persistence of God

“The LORD... sent word to them through his messengers again and again, because he had pity on his people and on his dwelling place.”
“But they mocked God’s messengers... until the wrath of the LORD was aroused against his people and there was no remedy.” 2

The Fall

“They set fire to God’s temple and broke down the wall of Jerusalem; they burned all the palaces and destroyed everything of value.” 3
“The land enjoyed its sabbath rests; all the time of its desolation it rested, until the seventy years were completed.” 4

The Decree of Cyrus

“The LORD... has appointed me to build a temple for him at Jerusalem in Judah. Any of his people among you... let them go up.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Did not Humble Himself (Belo Nikna): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of the Final Fall." Zedekiah saw the "Example" of Josiah’s humility and Manasseh’s repentance, yet he chose "Rigid Rebellion." He refused to "Bow" to the Word spoken by Jeremiah. His "Pride" was the "Weight" that finally broke the nation.

2 No Remedy (Ad-le-En Marpe): Literally in the scrolls, "Until there was no healing." This is a "Terrifying Phrase." It signifies that the "Cancer of Sin" had become "Terminal." God’s "Pity" (*Verse 15*) had been exhausted by "Mockery." The nation had "Uprooted" itself so completely that it could no longer "Absorb" the grace of the prophets.

3 Set fire to God’s Temple: The scrolls record the "End of the Glory." The house built by Solomon, where the Fire from Heaven had fallen (Chapter 7), was now "Burned with Fire" from Babylon. The "Symbol of the Presence" was destroyed because the "Reality of the Presence" had already departed.

4 Sabbath Rests (Shabbatot): In the scrolls, this is a "Sovereign Audit." The land had been "Denied its rest" for centuries because of the people’s "Greed and Idolatry." God was "Forcing a Sabbath." The 70 years of Exile were the "Land’s Recovery Period." It proves that "God’s Creation" will have its "Order" even if He has to "Empty the Room" to get it.

5 Let them Go Up (we-Ya’al): These are the "Final Words" of the Hebrew Bible (in its original order). The book ends with a "Command of Resurrection." The "Root" was not destroyed; it was "Transplanted" back into the "Holy Soil" by a "Pagan King" moved by God. It is the "Ultimate Rooted Principle": God always has the "Final Word," and that word is "Return."
