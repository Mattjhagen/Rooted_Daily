2 Chronicles 27

He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD... but unlike his father he did not enter the temple of the LORD. 1

The Builder King

“He rebuilt the Upper Gate of the temple of the LORD and did extensive work on the wall at the hill of Ophel.” 2
“He built towns in the hill country of Judah and forts and towers in the forests.”

The Secret of Strength

“Jotham grew powerful because he walked steadfastly before the LORD his God.” 3
“He went to war against the king of the Ammonites and conquered them.” 4
“Jotham rested with his ancestors... and Ahaz his son succeeded him as king.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Did not Enter the Temple: In the scrolls, this is a "Mark of Wisdom." Jotham learned from his father’s "Transgression" (Chapter 26). He respected the "Divinely Ordained Boundaries." He was a "Rooted" king who knew how to "Honor the Priesthood" while "Ruling the Palace."

2 Wall at the Hill of Ophel: The scrolls record Jotham’s "Infrastructure Zeal." Ophel was the area between the City of David and the Temple. By strengthening this area, he was "Binding" the city together. It represents a "Kingdom" that is "Integrated" and "Secure."

3 Walked Steadfastly (Hekhin Darkav): Literally in the scrolls, "He established his ways." Jotham didn't have a "Rollercoaster Faith." He was "Consistent." This "Establishment" of his ways before God was the "Direct Cause" of his "Power" (*Gabhah*). It is the "Rooted" principle that "Success is the shadow of Obedience."

4 Conquered the Ammonites: The scrolls record that Jotham’s victory resulted in three years of "Heavy Tribute." His "Strength" translated into "National Wealth." Because he was "Aligned with God," the resources of the nations flowed into Judah.

5 Ahaz succeeded him: The scrolls conclude Jotham’s reign with a "Warning of Transition." Jotham was the "Last Great King" of this sequence. His son Ahaz would be a "Radical Departure" from the "Rooted" ways of his father. It shows that "Faithfulness" must be "Renewed" in every generation.
