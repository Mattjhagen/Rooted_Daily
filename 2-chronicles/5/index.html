2 Chronicles 5

King Solomon and the entire assembly... sacrificed so many sheep and cattle that they could not be recorded or counted. 1

The Ark’s Rest

The priests brought the ark... to its appointed place in the inner sanctuary... under the wings of the cherubim. 2
“There was nothing in the ark except the two tablets that Moses had placed in it at Horeb.”

The Unified Sound

The trumpeters and musicians joined in unison to give praise and thanks to the LORD. Accompanied by trumpets, cymbals and other instruments, they raised their voices in praise... 3
“He is good; his love endures forever.” 4

The Manifest Glory

“Then the temple of the LORD was filled with the cloud... the glory of the LORD filled the temple of God.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Could not be Recorded: In the scrolls, this is the "Extravagance of Worship." The numbers were so vast that they transcended "Accounting." It was a "Sacrificial Flood" that prepared the atmosphere for the "Glory." It signifies that "Human Resources" were being poured out without limit for the sake of the "Presence."

2 Under the Wings: The scrolls emphasize that the Ark was now "At Home." The journey that began in the wilderness and continued through the era of the Judges and the cave-dwelling of David was finally at its "Resting Place." The cherubim’s wings acted as a "Divine Canopy."

3 Joined in Unison (Ke-Echad): Literally in the scrolls, "As One." This is the "Key to the Manifestation." When the 120 trumpeters and the thousands of singers sounded a "Single Note" of praise, the "Presence" responded. It is the "Rooted" principle of "Unity preceding Glory."

4 His Love Endures Forever (Ki le-olam Chasdo): This was the "Agreed Anthem" of the nation in the scrolls. It was the "Frequency" that God responded to. By acknowledging His *Chesed* (steadfast love), they were "Rooting" themselves in the "Goodness" of His character.

5 Filled with the Cloud (Anan): The scrolls record this as the "Kabod" (Weight/Glory). It was not just a "Symbol," but a "Physical Weight" so heavy that the priests could not stand to perform their duties. God was "Evicting" the human servants to "Inhabit" His own house. It was the "Confirmation of Acceptance."
