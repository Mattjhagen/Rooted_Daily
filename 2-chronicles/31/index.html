2 Chronicles 31

When all this had ended... they smashed the sacred stones and cut down the Asherah poles... until they had destroyed them all. 1

The Heaps of Abundance

As soon as the order went out, the Israelites generously gave the firstfruits... they brought in an abundance, a tithe of everything. 2
“Since the people began to bring their contributions... we have had enough to eat and plenty to spare, for the LORD has blessed his people.” 3

The Heart of Hezekiah

“This is what Hezekiah did throughout Judah, doing what was good and right and faithful before the LORD his God.” 4
“In everything... he sought his God and worked wholeheartedly. And so he prospered.” 5

Translation Notes

1 When all this had Ended: In the scrolls, this is the "Momentum of Worship." The Passover didn't just "End"; it "Spilled over" into the culture. The people were so "Rooted" in the truth they had experienced in Jerusalem that they couldn't tolerate the "Idols" in their own towns. True worship always leads to "Cultural Cleaning."

2 Generously Gave (Paratz): Literally in the scrolls, "They broke forth." The generosity was like a "Bursting Dam." Because the "Spiritual Portals" were open, the "Economic Resources" flowed. It signifies a "Kingdom Economy" where "Giving" is the "Natural Response" to "Presence."

3 Plenty to Spare (Hoteir): The scrolls record this "Super-Abundance." The heaps were so large they had to build "Storerooms" (*Verse 11*). It proves that when the "Priests and Levites" are supported, the "National Blessing" is unlocked. The "Root" of the nation’s wealth was its "Stewardship."

4 Good and Right and Faithful (ha-Towb we-ha-Yashar we-ha-Emet): In the scrolls, this is the "Triune Character" of a godly leader. He was "Good" in intention, "Right" in action, and "Faithful" (Truthful) in his commitment. He wasn't a "Man of Performance" but a "Man of Character."

5 Worked Wholeheartedly (Be-Khol Libbo): Literally in the scrolls, "With all his Heart." This is the "Engine of Prosperity." Success was not a "Lucky Break"; it was the "Mathematical Result" of "Wholehearted Seeking." The "Roots" were deep, so the "Fruits" were many. It is the "Chronicler’s Final Word" on why Hezekiah was great.
