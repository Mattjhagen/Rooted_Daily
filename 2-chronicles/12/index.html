2 Chronicles 12

After Rehoboam’s position as king was established and he had become strong, he and all Israel with him abandoned the law of the LORD. 1

The Egyptian Invasion

In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem because they had been unfaithful to the LORD.
“Shemaiah the prophet said: ‘You have abandoned me; therefore, I now abandon you to Shishak.’” 2

The Lesson of Service

“The leaders of Israel and the king humbled themselves and said, ‘The LORD is just.’”
“They will become subject to him, so that they may learn the difference between serving me and serving the kings of other lands.” 3
“Shishak... carried off the treasures of the temple... and the gold shields Solomon had made.”
“So King Rehoboam made bronze shields to replace them.” 4
“Rehoboam rested with his ancestors... and Abijah his son succeeded him as king.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Abandoned the Law (Azav): In the scrolls, this is the "Cycle of Pride." Prosperity led to "Independence," and independence led to "Abandonment" of the Source. Rehoboam’s "Strength" became his "Weakness" because it was no longer "Rooted" in the Torah.

2 I Now Abandon You: This is the "Principle of Reciprocity" in the scrolls. If the people "Abandon" God’s protection, God "Withdraws" His hand. Shishak (Sheshonq I) was the "Instrument of Discipline" used to bring the nation back to its "Senses."

3 Difference of Service (Abodah): This is a "Brutal Education" in the scrolls. Serving God is a "Light Yoke," while serving the "Kings of the Earth" is "Oppressive." Shishak’s tribute was a "Heavy Burden" compared to God’s tithe. It is a "Rooted" lesson for all generations: there is no "Neutrality" in service.

4 Bronze Shields (Meginnei Nechoshet): In the scrolls, this is a "Symbol of Decline." The gold of Solomon was replaced by the bronze of Rehoboam. It looked similar from a distance, but the "Value" was gone. It represents a "Counterfeit Glory" that tries to hide the "Loss of Presence."

5 Abijah Succeeded Him: The scrolls conclude the reign of Rehoboam with the "Survival of the Line." Despite the invasion and the division, the "Covenant with David" remained. Abijah would inherit a "Diminished Kingdom," but one that had learned (temporarily) the "Necessity of Humility."
