2 Chronicles 11

“This is what the LORD says: Do not go up to fight against your fellow Israelites. Go home, every one of you, for this is my doing.” 1

The Defense of Judah

Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem and built up towns for defense in Judah. 2

The Holy Migration

The priests and Levites from all their districts throughout Israel sided with him. 3

The Apostasy of the North

Jeroboam appointed his own priests for the high places and for the goat and calf idols he had made. 4
“Those who set their hearts on seeking the LORD followed the Levites to Jerusalem.”
“Rehoboam loved Maakah... more than any of his other wives... he appointed Abijah to be the chief prince.” 5

Translation Notes

1 This is My Doing: In the scrolls, this is the "Sanctioned Split." God was preventing a "Fratricidal War." He was acknowledging that the "Division" was a necessary judgment. Rehoboam’s "Obedience" in this moment was his "First Wise Act" as king.

2 Built up Towns for Defense: The scrolls record the "Strategic Consolidation." Rehoboam was "Shrinking the Borders" to "Strengthen the Core." He created a "Ring of Fortresses" around Jerusalem. It signifies a "Rooted" defensive posture—protecting what remains.

3 Sided with Him (Hith-Yatzbu): Literally in the scrolls, "They presented themselves." The Levites realized that without the Temple, their "Identity" was lost. They chose "Ministry" over "Inheritance." Their migration brought the "Spiritual Heritage" of the entire nation into the small kingdom of Judah.

4 Goat and Calf Idols (Se’irim we-Agalim): In the scrolls, these were "Hairy Demons" and "Young Bulls." Jeroboam was creating a "Political Religion" to keep his people from going to Jerusalem. It was a "Rootless" innovation that would eventually lead the Northern Kingdom to destruction.

5 Chief Prince (Nagid): The scrolls highlight Rehoboam’s "Family Management." By appointing Abijah early, he was trying to prevent the "Succession Struggles" that had plagued David. He was learning to "Order his House," even if he couldn't order the entire nation.
