2 Chronicles 7

The Fire from Heaven

When Solomon finished praying, fire came down from heaven and consumed the burnt offering... 1
“He is good; his love endures forever.”

The Covenant Response

“If my people, who are called by my name, will humble themselves and pray and seek my face and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven, and I will forgive their sin and will heal their land.” 2
“I have chosen and consecrated this temple so that my Name may be there forever. My eyes and my heart will always be there.” 3

The Warning of the Uprooting

“Then I will uproot Israel from my land... and will reject this temple which I have consecrated for my Name.” 4
“Because they forsook the LORD... therefore he brought all this calamity on them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Fire came down (Esh Yaradah): In the scrolls, this is the "Ultimate Validation." It was the same sign given to Moses and Elijah. It signifies that God was not just "Listening" to the prayer, but "Answering" with his "Presence." The "Fire" accepted the sacrifice, clearing the way for the "Glory" to fill the house.

2 Heal their Land (Erpah): This is the "Redemptive Promise" of the scrolls. The "Health of the Land" was tied to the "Spiritual Condition" of the people. *Erpah* (Heal) is the same word used for physical medicine. God is the "Physician of the Nation." The "Root" of the healing is the "Turning" (*Shuv*) of the heart.

3 My Eyes and My Heart: The scrolls highlight this "Relational Proximity." The Temple was not just an "Architectural Achievement"; it was a "Emotional Investment" from God. He wasn't just "Watching" (*Eyes*); he was "Feeling" (*Heart*). It is the "Rooted" assurance of divine attention.

4 Uproot Israel (Netashtim): In the scrolls, this is the "Counter-Promise." The same God who "Planted" them could "Uproot" them. The "Permanence" of the land was conditional upon "Loyalty." For the post-exilic readers, this was a "Sobering Explanation" of why the Exile had happened.

5 Forsook the LORD (Azavu): The scrolls conclude with the "Anatomy of Failure." Calamity was not "Random Luck," but the "Fruit" of a "Severed Connection." When the nation chose other "Roots" (idols), they lost the "Life" of the Covenant. It is a "Permanent Warning" to stay "Rooted" in the True Source.
