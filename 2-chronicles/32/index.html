2 Chronicles 32

After all that Hezekiah had so faithfully done, Sennacherib king of Assyria came and invaded Judah. 1

The Arm of Flesh

“Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or discouraged... for there is a greater power with us than with him.”
“With him is only the arm of flesh, but with us is the LORD our God to help us and to fight our battles.” 2

The Angelic Intervention

“King Hezekiah and the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz cried out in prayer to heaven.”
“And the LORD sent an angel, who annihilated all the fighting men and the commanders and officers in the camp of the Assyrian king.” 3

The Testing of the Heart

“But Hezekiah’s heart was proud and he did not respond to the kindness shown him; therefore the LORD’s wrath was on him...” 4
“It was Hezekiah who blocked the upper outlet of the Gihon spring and channeled the water down to the west side of the City of David.” 5

Translation Notes

1 After all that... Sennacherib came: In the scrolls, this is the "Paradox of Faithfulness." Hezekiah’s obedience didn't prevent the crisis; it "Prepared" him for it. Sennacherib (the "Invincible" ruler) was the "Final Exam" for Hezekiah’s reforms. It proves that "Rootedness" is tested by the "Storm."

2 Arm of Flesh (Zero’a Basar): This is a "Staggering Contrast" in the scrolls. Assyria had the "Numerical Might" (Flesh), but Judah had the "Covenant Might" (Spirit). Hezekiah was stripping away the "Illusion of Power." The people "Leaned" (*Verse 8*) on his words because they were "Rooted" in the Character of God.

3 Annihilated all the Fighting Men: The scrolls record a "Surgical Strike." The LORD didn't need an "Army" to defeat an "Army." He used a "Single Messenger" (Angel). Sennacherib, who had mocked the God of Israel as "Just another idol," returned home in "Shame" only to be killed in his own temple. It is the "Ultimate Reversal."

4 Heart was Proud (Gabhah Libbo): In the scrolls, this is the "Tragedy of the Miracle." After seeing a million-man army destroyed by an angel, Hezekiah began to think he was the "Reason" for the success. God "Left him" (*Verse 31*) to test what was in his heart. Even the "Greatest Reformer" is susceptible to the "Cancer of Pride."

5 Gihon Spring / Hezekiah’s Tunnel: The scrolls record this "Engineering Marvel." Hezekiah’s Tunnel (still visible today) was a "Physical Manifestation" of his "Practical Wisdom." He combined "Absolute Faith" with "Absolute Preparation." He prayed to heaven, but he also dug the tunnel. It is "Rooted" leadership in action.
