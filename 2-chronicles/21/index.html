2 Chronicles 21

When Jehoram established himself... he put all his brothers to the sword along with some of the officials of Israel. 1
“He followed the ways of the kings of Israel... for he married a daughter of Ahab. He did evil in the eyes of the LORD.” 2

The Letter from Elijah

“Jehoram received a letter from Elijah the prophet: ‘Because you have not followed the ways of your father... but have followed the ways of the kings of Israel...’” 3
“You yourself will be very ill with a lingering disease of the bowels.”
“His bowels came out because of the disease, and he died in great pain.”
“He passed away, to no one’s regret.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Put all his Brothers to the Sword: In the scrolls, this is the "Anti-Davidic Act." David had multiple sons but never sought to "Eradicate" his house. Jehoram was trying to secure his throne by "Severing the Branches" of his own family. It shows a "Paranoid Rule" that is not "Rooted" in the promise of God.

2 Married a Daughter of Ahab (Athaliah): The scrolls identify this "Marriage Alliance" as the "Infection" that poisoned Judah. Athaliah brought the "Baal-Cult" and the "Tyrannical Spirit" of her parents (Ahab and Jezebel) into the "House of David." It is a "Rooted" warning about the "Company we Keep."

3 Letter from Elijah (Miktab): This is a "Prophetic intervention" in the scrolls. Elijah, who usually operated in the North, reached into Judah with a "Written Judgment." It signifies that God’s "Standard" is the same for both kingdoms. Jehoram had "Murdered his way to the throne," and God would "Remove him by disease."

4 To No One’s Regret (Belo Chemdah): Literally in the scrolls, "Without being desired." This is the "Most Chilling Epitaph" in the Bible. A king who reigned for eight years, yet when he died, the nation felt "Relief" rather than "Grief." He was "Uprooted" from the hearts of the people before he was uprooted from the earth. It is the ultimate "Failure of Legacy."
