2 Chronicles 20

The Prayer of the Powerless

“We have no power to face this vast army that is attacking us. We do not know what to do, but our eyes are on you.” 1

The Word of Jahaziel

“Do not be afraid or discouraged because of this vast army. For the battle is not yours, but God’s.” 2
“You will not have to fight this battle. Take up your positions; stand firm and see the deliverance the LORD will give you.”

The Strategy of Praise

Jehoshaphat appointed men to sing to the LORD and praise him for the splendor of his holiness as they went out at the head of the army. 3
“As they began to sing and praise, the LORD set ambushes against the men... and they were defeated.” 4
“On the fourth day they assembled in the Valley of Berakah, where they praised the LORD. That is why it is called the Valley of Berakah to this day.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Our Eyes are on You (Elekha Enenu): In the scrolls, this is the "Rooted posture of Crisis." Jehoshaphat wasn't "Glancing" at God; he was "Fixed" on Him. He was admitting that "Human Strategy" had hit a wall. His "Powerlessness" was not a "Defeat" but a "Prerequisite" for divine intervention.

2 The Battle is God’s (Ha-Milchamah le-Elohim): The scrolls highlight this "Sovereign Transfer." The burden was removed from the "Army" and placed on the "Creator." Jahaziel, a Levite (musician), was the one who brought this word—showing that "Worshipers" are often the best "Strategists."

3 At the Head of the Army (Lifnei ha-Chalutz): Literally in the scrolls, "Before the vanguard." This is an "Absurd Military Maneuver." Sending the "Choir" instead of the "Cavalry" proved that Judah believed the Word of God. They were "Weaponizing Praise."

4 As they Began to Sing: The scrolls record this as the "Trigger for the Trap." The "Sound of Praise" in Jerusalem echoed as "Panic" in the enemy camp. God didn't need "Swords"; He needed "Submission." The enemies "Ambushed each other," proving that "Evil" is self-destructive when it faces the "Presence."

5 Valley of Berakah (Emeq Berakah): In the scrolls, *Berakah* means "Blessing." The place of the "Threat" became the place of the "Thrive." It took three days just to collect the plunder. It teaches that "Victory" in the Kingdom of God is often "Exceedingly Abundantly" beyond the initial "Survival" we ask for.
