Job 29

The Days of the Lamp

“How I long for the months gone by, for the days when God watched over me...” 1
“When his lamp shone on my head and by his light I walked through darkness!”

The Honor of the Gate

“When I went to the gate of the city and took my seat in the public square...”
“The young men saw me and stepped aside and the old men rose to their feet.” 2

The Champion of the Needy

“I was eyes to the blind and feet to the lame.” 3
“I was a father to the needy; I took up the case of the stranger.”
“I broke the fangs of the wicked and snatched the victims from their teeth.” 4
“My roots will reach to the water, and the dew will lie overnight on my branches.” 5

Translation Notes

1 God Watched over me (Eloah Yish-mereni): In the scrolls, Job is "Uprooting" the current "Silence of God" by remembering the "Protection of God." He sees his "Past Life" as a time of "Divine Intimacy." The "Lamp" (*Ner*) was the "Root" of his "Confidence."

2 Old men Rose to their Feet (Yashishim Qamu): The scrolls highlight the "Unprecedented Respect." Job was not just "Wealthy," he was "Ethically Weighty." He "Rooted" his status in "Character," and the "Community" acknowledged it. It makes his current "Humiliation" (*Chapter 30*) even more "Bitter."

3 Eyes to the Blind (Einayim la-Iwwer): This is a "Beautiful Metaphor" in the scrolls for "Functional Mercy." Job didn't just "Give Alms," he "Shared his Capacity." He "Rooted" his "Power" in the "Service of the Vulnerable." It directly refutes Eliphaz’s invented charges in *Chapter 22*.

4 Broke the Fangs of the Wicked (Matalla-oth): Literally in the scrolls, "The jaw-teeth." Job was an "Active Force" for "Justice." He didn't just "Avoid Evil," he "Attacked Injustice." He was the "Rooted Protector" of the "Remnant."

5 Roots will Reach to the Water (Shorshi Phatuach el-Mayim): The scrolls conclude with this "Vision of Stability." Job expected his life to be like a "Well-Watered Tree" (*Chapter 14*). He "Rooted" his "Future" in his "Present Righteousness." The "Dew" (*Tal*) was his "Legacy." It signifies the "Logical Expectation" of a "Righteous Life" which has now been "Uprooted" by the "Mystery of Suffering."
