Job 41

The Untamable Hook

“Can you pull in Leviathan with a fishhook or tie down its tongue with a rope?” 1
“If you lay a hand on it, you will remember the struggle and never do it again!”

The Sealed Shields

“Its back has rows of shields tightly sealed together.” 2
“Each is so close to the next that no air can pass between them.”

The Breath of Flame

“Its snorting throws out flashes of light; its eyes are like the rays of dawn.” 3
“Its breath sets coals ablaze, and flames dart from its mouth.”

King of the Proud

“Its chest is as hard as rock, hard as a lower millstone.” 4
“It looks down on all that are haughty; it is king over all that are proud.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Leviathan (Li-wyathan): Literally in the scrolls, "The Twisted One" or "Coiled One." In ancient near-eastern mythology, this was the "Dragon of Chaos." God is "Uprooting" Job’s sense of "Control." If Job cannot even handle a "Creature" of God, how can he handle the "Creator"?

2 Sealed Together (Dabeq): The scrolls highlight the "Impenetrability." Leviathan has no "Exposed Root." It is a "Perfect System" of defense. It signifies the "Structural Power" of the "Wild."

3 Snorting throws out Flashes of Light (Athish-othav Ta-hel Or): This is a "Terrifyingly Beautiful" description in the scrolls. It suggests a "Supernatural" or "Luminous" quality to the beast. God is showing Job that "Beauty" and "Terror" are "Rooted" in the same "Divine Imagination."

4 Hard as a Millstone (ke-Phelach Thach-thith): The scrolls highlight the "Untouchable Heart." Leviathan has no "Empathy" or "Fear." It is "Rooted" in "Solid Substance." It is the "Opposite" of Job’s "Ebbing Life" (*Chapter 30*).

5 King over all that are Proud (Melekh al-Kol-Bne-Shachatz): The scrolls conclude with this "Ironic Label." Leviathan is the "King of Pride." By showing Job this creature, God is "Uprooting" Job’s own "Pride" (*his 'haughtiness' mentioned in Chapter 31*). God is saying: "If you can't stand before my servant, how can you stand before Me?" It is the final "Blow" to Job’s "Human-Centric" universe. It prepares him for "Final Repentance."
