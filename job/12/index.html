Job 12

The Sarcasm of the Suffering

“Doubtless you are the only people who matter, and wisdom will die with you!” 1
“But I have a mind as well as you; I am not inferior to you.”

The Wisdom of the Creatures

“Ask the animals, and they will teach you, or the birds in the sky, and they will tell you.” 2
“In his hand is the life of every creature and the breath of all mankind.” 3

The Destroyer of Orders

“To God belong wisdom and power; counsel and understanding are his.”
“He makes fools of counselors and robs judges of their capacity.” 4
“He pours contempt on nobles and disarms the mighty.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Wisdom will die with you (Thammuth Chokhmah): In the scrolls, this is a "Biting Critique" of the friends’ "Intellectual Arrogance." Job is "Uprooting" their claim to have a "Monopoly on Truth." He argues that their "Wisdom" is just "Tradition" without "Context."

2 Ask the Animals (She’al-na Behemoth): The scrolls highlight the "Common Revelation." Job suggests that even the "Beasts" understand that "Life and Death" are in God’s hands, and that "Innocents" suffer. He is "Rooting" his argument in the "Raw Reality" of nature, which doesn't follow the friends’ "Retribution Principle."

3 Breath of all Mankind (Ruach Kol-Basar): Literally in the scrolls, "The Spirit of all Flesh." Job admits God’s "Absolute Ownership." He is "Rooting" human existence in "Divine Volition." This makes the "Pain" even worse—it is "Allowed" by the one who "Holds the Breath."

4 Makes Fools of Counselors (Molikh Yo’atzim Sholal): Literally in the scrolls, "He leads counselors away stripped/bare." Job is describing a God who "Destroys Human Institutions." He "Uproots" the "Wise" and "Dethrones" the "Stable." It is a "Chaotic Sovereignty" that terrifies the friends.

5 Disarms the Mighty (Meziach Aphikim Rippah): The scrolls conclude with this "Social Reversal." God is the "Great Leveler." He isn't just a "Judge" of individuals; He is the "Disrupter" of empires. Job is "Rooting" his suffering in a "Universal Pattern" of "Divine Unpredictability." It marks his "Refusal" to be "Explained Away" by "Human Logic."
