Job 34

The Scoffer’s Cup

“Who is like Job, who drinks up scoffing like water?” 1
“He says, ‘It profits a man nothing when he tries to please God.’”

The Impossibility of Wrong

“So listen to me, you men of understanding. Far be it from God to do evil, from the Almighty to do wrong.” 2
“It is unthinkable that God would do wrong, that the Almighty would pervert justice.”

The Sustainment of Life

“If it were his intention and he withdrew his spirit and breath...”
“All humanity would perish together and mankind would return to the dust.” 3

The Eye of the Creator

“God shows no partiality to princes and does not favor the rich over the poor, for they are all the work of his hands.” 4
“His eyes are on the ways of mortals; he sees their every step.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Drinks up Scoffing (Yishteh-La'ag): In the scrolls, Elihu is "Uprooting" Job’s "Attitude." He sees Job as someone who has become "Addicted to Cynicism." He argues that Job’s "Pain" has turned into "Rebellion."

2 Far be it from God (Chalilah la-El): Literally in the scrolls, "Profane/Sacrilege be it to God." Elihu is "Rooting" his entire argument in the "Integrity of the Creator." He believes that "Justice" is the "Essential Nature" of God. Therefore, if there is a "Discrepancy," it must be on the "Human Side."

3 Humanity would Perish (Yigwa Kol-Basar): The scrolls highlight the "Vital Dependence." Elihu argues that God is the "Source of Breath" (*Ruach*). If God were "Unjust," he wouldn't bother to "Sustain" the world. The fact that Job is "Still Breathing" is proof of "Divine Grace."

4 No Partiality to Princes (lo-Nissa Phne Sarim): This is a "Universalist Root" in the scrolls. Elihu "Uproots" the idea that God can be "Bought" or "Influenced" by "Status." Everyone is equally "Vulnerable" and equally "Significant" before the "Maker."

5 He sees their every Step (Kol-Tzo’adav Yir-eh): The scrolls conclude with this "Vision of Surveillance." Elihu is "Re-rooting" Job’s sense of "Isolation" (*Chapter 23*) into "Divine Observation." He argues that God doesn't need a "Trial" to know the "Truth"—He is already "Witnessing" every "Root" and every "Branch" of human life. It marks the "Preparation" for the Whirlwind.
