Job 32

The Wrath of the Buzite

“Elihu son of Barakel became very angry with Job for justifying himself rather than God.” 1
“He was also angry with the three friends because they had found no way to refute Job.”

The Breath of Understanding

“But it is the spirit in a person, the breath of the Almighty, that gives them understanding.” 2
“It is not only the old who are wise, nor only the aged who understand what is right.”

The Bottled-Up Wine

“For I am full of words, and the spirit within me compels me.”
“Inside I am like bottled-up wine, like new wineskins ready to burst.” 3
“I must speak so that I may find relief; I must open my lips and reply.” 4
“I will show partiality to no one, nor will I flatter any human being.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Justifying Himself rather than God (Tzidqo Napsh-o me-Elohim): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Elihu’s Anger." He feels that Job has placed his "Personal Integrity" above the "Honor of God." He is a "Theological Centrist"—he doesn't agree with the three friends, but he cannot tolerate Job’s "Protest."

2 Breath of the Almighty (Nishmath Shaddai): The scrolls highlight the "Source of Wisdom." Elihu "Uproots" the authority of "Age" (*Verse 9*) and replaces it with the authority of the "Spirit" (*Ruach*). He argues that "Insight" is a "Divine Gift," not a "Biological Accumulation."

3 New Wineskins ready to Burst (ke-Oboth Chadashim Yib-ba-qe-a): This is a "Vivid Metaphor" in the scrolls for "Intellectual Pressure." Elihu has been "Rooting" his silence in "Respect" (*Verse 4*), but the "Fermentation of his Thoughts" has become "Unbearable." He "Must Speak" or "Break."

4 Find Relief (we-Yirwach-li): Literally in the scrolls, "That there may be air/space for me." Elihu’s speech is a "Physical Necessity." He is "Breathing Out" what he has "Bottled Up." It marks the beginning of a "Four-Chapter Monologue."

5 I will show Partiality to No One (Al-na Essah Phne-Ish): The scrolls conclude with this "Oath of Impartiality." Elihu claims to be an "Objective Witness." He is "Rooting" his defense in "Truth" rather than "Etiquette." He refuses to "Flatter" (*Verse 22*) the "Old Men." It signifies the arrival of a "Brash, New Perspective" that will "Uproot" the previous "Deadlock."
