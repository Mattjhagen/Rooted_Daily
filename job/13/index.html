Job 13

The Worthless Physicians

“You, however, smear me with lies; you are worthless physicians, all of you!” 1
“If only you would be altogether silent! For you, that would be wisdom.”

The Defiant Faith

“Though he slay me, yet will I hope in him; I will surely defend my ways to his face.” 2

Warning the Defense

“Will you speak wickedly on God’s behalf? Will you speak deceitfully for him?” 3

The Fragility of the Target

“Why do you hide your face and consider me your enemy?”
“Will you torment a wind-driven leaf? Will you chase after dry chaff?” 4
“You fasten my feet in shackles; you keep close watch on all my paths.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Worthless Physicians (Rophe'e Elil): Literally in the scrolls, "Healers of nothing" or "Idol-physicians." Job is "Uprooting" the friends’ professional facade. They are "Prescribing" a "Cure" (Repentance) for a "Disease" (Sin) that Job knows he hasn't contracted. Their "Medicine" is "Toxicity."

2 Though he Slay me (Hen Yiq-tleni): This is one of the most "Controversial and Rooted" verses in the scrolls. The *Kethiv* (written) text says "I have no hope," but the *Qere* (read) text says "I will hope in him." In the "Rooted Translation," we embrace the "Paradox." Job is saying: "Even if God’s action results in my death, my "Root" of integrity is so deep that I will still "Argue for Truth." It is "Faith as Courage," not "Faith as Comfort."

3 Speak wickedly on God’s Behalf: The scrolls highlight Job’s "Spiritual Intelligence." He warns his friends that God doesn't need "Lies" to defend His justice. By "Ignoring the Facts" of Job’s innocence to protect their "Theology," they were actually "Insulting God." It proves that "Dishonest Piety" is a "Sin."

4 Wind-driven Leaf (Ale Nih-daph): This is a "Poignant Botanical Image" in the scrolls. Job is asking why the "Almighty" is using his "Infinite Power" to "Bully" a "Fragment of Nature." He is "Rooting" his plea in his own "Insignificance." If he is just "Chaff," why does God care enough to "Shackle" him?

5 Fasten my feet in Shackles (Sath): Literally in the scrolls, "You put my feet in a block." Job feels like a "Criminal" under "Divine Surveillance." He is "Uprooted" by the "Relentless Monitoring" of the Holy. It marks the "Intensity" of his feeling of "Persecution." He wants God to "Show the Charges" (*Verse 23*) or "Release the Watch."
