Job 27

The Oath of the Bitter Soul

“As surely as God lives, who has denied me justice, the Almighty, who has made my life bitter...” 1
“As long as I have life within me... my lips will not speak wickedness, and my tongue will not utter deceit.”

The Unyielding Root

“I will never admit you are in the right; till I die, I will not deny my integrity.” 2
“I will maintain my righteousness and never let go of it; my conscience will not reproach me as long as I live.” 3

The Fragile Security

“He builds his house like a moth’s cocoon, like a temporary shelter made by a watchman.” 4
“Terrors overtake him like a flood; a tempest snatches him up in the night.” 5

Translation Notes

1 God lives, who has Denied me Justice (Chay-El Hesir Mishpati): This is a "Stunning Paradox" in the scrolls. Job is "Swearing by the God" he is "Accusing." He "Roots" his "Truth" in the very "Being" of the one who is "Smiting" him. It proves that his "Atheism" is impossible; he is "Locked in a Covenant" with his "Adversary."

2 I will not Deny my Integrity (Lo-Asir Thummathi Mimmenni): Literally in the scrolls, "I will not cause my completeness to depart from me." Job sees his "Integrity" (*Thummah*) as his "Only Possession." He is "Uprooting" the friends' pressure to "Confess Sins" he didn't commit. He chooses "Pain with Truth" over "Peace with a Lie."

3 Never let go of it (Lo Arp-eha): The scrolls highlight the "Tenacity of the Soul." Job is "Grasping" his "Righteousness" like a "Weapon." He refuses to let his "Conscience" be "Uprooted" by his "Circumstance."

4 Moth’s Cocoon (Ke-Ash): Literally in the scrolls, "Like the moth." Just as a moth’s cocoon is destroyed by the slightest touch, the "House of the Wicked" has "No Structural Root." It is a "Temporary Shelter" (*Succah*) that cannot withstand the "Storm."

5 Terrors overtake him like a Flood (Thessig-hu ka-Mayim Ballahoth): The scrolls conclude with this "Hydraulic Disaster." For the wicked, "Judgment" is a "Flood" that "Uproots" everything in its path. Job is acknowledging the "Theology of the Friends" in theory, but "Refusing" to let them "Apply" it to his "Gold-Tested" life. It marks the "Final Separation" between Job and his "Comforters."
