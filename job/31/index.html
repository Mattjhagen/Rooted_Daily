Job 31

The Covenant of the Heart

“I made a covenant with my eyes not to look lustfully at a young woman.” 1
“Let God weigh me on honest scales and he will know that I am blameless.”

The Stewardship of Power

“If I have denied justice to any of my servants... what will I do when God confronts me?” 2
“Did not he who made me in the womb make them? Did not the same one form us both?”

The Prince’s Challenge

“Oh, that I had someone to hear me! I sign now my defense—let the Almighty answer me.” 3
“I would give him an account of my every step; I would present it to him like a prince.” 4
“The words of Job are ended.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Covenant with my Eyes (Berith Karatti le-Einay): In the scrolls, this is the "Internal Root" of morality. Job wasn't just "Externally Pure," he was "Internally Disciplined." He "Rooted" his behavior in a "Contract" with his own "Senses."

2 Justice to my Servants (Mishpath Abdi): The scrolls highlight Job’s "Egalitarian Ethics." He recognized the "Common Root" of humanity (*Verse 15*). He believed that "Servants" had a "Legal Claim" (*Mishpath*) even against their "Master." It marks him as a "Pioneer of Human Rights."

3 I sign now my Defense (Hen Tavi): Literally in the scrolls, "Behold my Taw" (*the last letter of the alphabet, used as a signature mark*). Job has "Completed the Book" of his defense. He is "Rooting" his case in a "Written Testimony." He is "Authorized" by his own "Innocence."

4 Like a Prince (Kemo Nagid): The scrolls highlight the "Confidence of the Clear Conscience." Job doesn't approach God as a "Worm" (*Chapter 25*) or a "Criminal," but as a "Nobleman" who has "Kept the Code." He wants a "Trial of Equals."

5 Words of Job are Ended (Thamm-u Dibre Iyyob): This is the "Structural Boundary" of the scrolls. Job has said "Everything." He has "Exhausted the Human Argument." He now falls into "Silence" to wait for the "Divine Response." But before God speaks, a "New Voice" (*Elihu*) will emerge to "Refill the Void."
