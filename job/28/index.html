Job 28

The Secrets of the Ore

“There is a mine for silver and a place where gold is refined.” 1
“People lay their hands on the flinty rock and uproot the mountains by their foundations.” 2

The Great Search

“But where can wisdom be found? Where does understanding dwell?” 3
“The deep says, ‘It is not in me’; the sea says, ‘It is not with me.’”
“It cannot be bought with the finest gold, nor can its price be weighed out in silver.”
“The price of wisdom is beyond rubies.”

The Final Definition

“God understands the way to it and he alone knows where it dwells.”
“The fear of the Lord—that is wisdom, and to shun evil is understanding.” 4

Translation Notes

1 A Mine for Silver (Motza la-Keseph): In the scrolls, this chapter serves as a "Bridge" between the "Dialogues" and the "Final Soliloquies." It highlights man’s "Technical Sovereignty." We can "Uproot" the "Gems" of the earth, but we cannot "Mining Wisdom" from our own resources.

2 Uproot the Mountains (Haphakh): Literally in the scrolls, "He overturns/uproots the mountains from the root." It is a "Stunning Image" of human "Disturbance of the Earth." We can penetrate the "Hidden Paths" (*Verse 7*) that even the "Falcon" (*Ayah*) has not seen. But "Wisdom" is "Deeper" than the "Ore."

3 Where can Wisdom be Found (we-ha-Chokhmah Me-Ayin Thimtza): The scrolls highlight the "Ontological Search." Wisdom is not a "Resource" to be "Extracted," but a "Relationship" to be "Received." It is "Hidden" from the "Deep" (*Tehom*) and the "Sea." It is a "Root" that exists only in the "Mind of God."

4 Fear of the Lord (Yir-ath Adonai): The scrolls conclude with this "Rooted Axiom." "Wisdom" is not "Theoretical Knowledge," but "Right Relatedness" to the Creator. To "Shun Evil" is the "Practical Root" of understanding. Job is "Restoring the Balance"—after all his "Legal Arguments," he returns to the "Fundamental Root" of his life. It marks the transition to his "Final Defenses."
