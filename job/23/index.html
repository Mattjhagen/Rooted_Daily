Job 23

The Hidden Courtroom

“If only I knew where to find him; if only I could go to his dwelling!” 1
“I would state my case before him and fill my mouth with arguments.”

The Four Directions

“But if I go to the east, he is not there; if I go to the west, I do not find him.”
“When he is at work in the north, I do not see him; when he turns to the south, I catch no glimpse of him.” 2

The Refining Fire

“But he knows the way that I take; when he has tested me, I will come forth as gold.” 3
“I have not departed from the commands of his lips; I have treasured the words of his mouth more than my daily bread.” 4
“That is why I am terrified before him; when I think of all this, I fear him.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Go to his Dwelling (Tkhunath-o): Literally in the scrolls, "His seat" or "His fixed place." Job is not just looking for "Comfort," he is looking for "Justice." He wants a "Face-to-Face" encounter to present his "Arguments" (*Verse 4*). It is the "Root" of his "Legal Faith."

2 East... West... North... South: The scrolls highlight the "Geographic Silence." Job is searching the "Entire Cosmos" for a "Responsive Creator." The "Hiddenness of God" is his "Greatest Torture." He is "Uprooted" by the "Divine Absence."

3 Come forth as Gold (ka-Zahab Etze): This is one of the most "Famous Roots" in the book. Job "Reframes" his suffering from "Punishment" to "Refinement." He asserts that his "Internal Integrity" is a "Precious Metal" that cannot be "Uprooted" by the "Fire." It marks his "Unshakable Self-Worth."

4 Daily Bread (mi-Chonqi): Literally in the scrolls, "From my allotted portion" or "My statute." Job is "Rooting" his "Survival" in the "Word of God." He claims that "Truth" is more "Nutritious" than "Food." It proves that he was "Nourished" by the "Commands" he is now accused of "Breaking."

5 Terrified before Him (Ebbachel): Literally in the scrolls, "I am filled with dread/trepidation." Even with his "Gold" integrity, Job is "Uprooted" by God’s "Unpredictable Power" (*Verse 13*). He realizes that "God stands alone" and no one can "Change His Mind." It marks the "Paradox" of a man who "Trusts the Character" of a God whose "Actions" he "Fears."
