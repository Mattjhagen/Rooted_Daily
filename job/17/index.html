Job 17

“My spirit is broken, my days are cut short, the grave awaits me.” 1

The Divine Security

“Give me, O God, the pledge you demand. Who else will put up security for me?” 2
“My eyes have grown dim with grief; my whole frame is but a shadow.” 3
“Nevertheless, the righteous will hold to their ways, and those with clean hands will grow stronger.” 4
“If I say to corruption, ‘You are my father,’ and to the worm, ‘My mother’... where then is my hope?” 5

Translation Notes

1 My Spirit is Broken (Ruchi Chubbalah): Literally in the scrolls, "My wind/breath is destroyed." Job feels that the "Root" of his vitality has been "Uprooted." He is "Breathing the Grave." It signifies the "Biological and Spiritual Exhaustion" of the long-term sufferer.

2 Pledge (Erab-ni): In the scrolls, this is "Legal Language." Job is asking God to be his "Bail" or his "Guarantor." He knows that he cannot "Vindicate" himself, so he asks the "Judge" to provide the "Security" for the "Defendant." It is a "Rooted" cry for "Justification" from the very source of his "Pain."

3 Frame is but a Shadow (Yetzuray ka-Tzel): Literally in the scrolls, "My members/muscles are as a shadow." Job has "Wasted Away." He is "Physically Uprooted." He is no longer a "Man of Substance," but a "Ghost" of his former self.

4 Righteous will hold to their Ways (Ye'echiz Tzaddiq Darko): This is a "Defiant Root" in the scrolls. Despite the "Appalling" nature of his state (*Verse 8*), Job asserts that the "Truly Righteous" will not "Waver." He is "Committing" to his "Integrity" even when it yields "No Profit." It proves that his "Root" is "Moral," not "Material."

5 Corruption... my Father (Shachath Abi Attah): The scrolls conclude with this "Inversion of Family." Job is "Adopting" the grave as his "New Kinship." He is "Rooting" his future in "Decay." By calling the worm his "Mother," he is acknowledging the "Total End" of his "Earthly Hopes." It marks the "Darkest Point" before his next "Breakthrough of Faith."
