Job 39

The Hidden Birth

“Do you know when the mountain goats give birth? Do you watch when the doe bears her fawn?” 1

The Free Spirit

“Who let the wild donkey go free? Who untied its ropes?” 2
“It laughs at the commotion in the town; it does not hear a driver’s shout.”

The Quivering Strength

“Do you give the horse its strength or clothe its neck with a flowing mane?”
“It laughs at fear, afraid of nothing; it does not shy away from the sword.” 3

The Command of the Heights

“Does the eagle soar at your command and build its nest on high?” 4
“It dwells on a cliff and stays there at night; a rocky crag is its fortress.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Do you Know (Hayada’tha): In the scrolls, God is "Uprooting" the "Illusion of Human Management." He describes events in the "Deep Wild" that happen without any "Human Witness" or "Interference." He is "Rooting" the "Universe’s Vitality" in His "Private Care."

2 Wild Donkey... Free (Pere... Chophshi): The scrolls highlight the "Untamable." The wild donkey "Laughs" (*Yischak*) at the "Systems of Men." God is showing Job that there are "Roots" in the world that refuse to be "Bound." It is a "Refutation" of the friends’ "Closed Legal System."

3 It laughs at Fear (Yischak le-Phachad): This is a "Stunning Portrait" of "Biological Courage" in the scrolls. The "War Horse" (*Sus*) is "Rooted" in a "Fearlessness" that God Himself "Clothed" him with. It is a "Reminder" to Job that "Strength" is a "Gift," not a "Human Achievement."

4 Eagle soar at your Command (al-Phikha): Literally "At your mouth." God is "Uprooting" Job’s "Pretension" to "Rule." The "Eagle" (*Nesher*) obeys a "Higher Frequency" than "Job’s Voice."

5 Rocky Crag is its Fortress (Shen-Sela): Literally "The tooth of the rock." The scrolls conclude with this "Image of Isolation." The eagle "Roots" its life in the "Inaccessible." God is saying that He "Cares" for the "High Crags" just as much as the "City Gates." It marks the end of the "Zoological Tour." God has shown Job a world that is "Magnificent," "Wild," and "Completely Independent" of "Human Moral Logic." It is a "Lesson in Humility."
