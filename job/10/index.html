Job 10

“I loathe my very life; therefore I will give free rein to my complaint and speak out in the bitterness of my soul.” 2

The Hands of the Potter

“Your hands shaped me and made me. Will you now turn and destroy me?” 2
“Did you not pour me out like milk and curdle me like cheese, clothe me with skin and flesh...?” 3

The Awesome Stalker

“If I hold my head high, you stalk me like a lion and again display your awesome power against me.” 4
“Are not my few days almost over? Turn away from me that I may enjoy a little moment of cheer.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Give free Rein (E'ezbah): Literally in the scrolls, "I will abandon my complaint to myself." Job is letting go of "Restraint." He is "Uprooting" the "Politeness of Piety" to speak the "Truth of his Agony." It is the "Rooted" honesty of a soul that has nothing left to lose.

2 Hands Shaped me (Yadekha Ittzebuni): The scrolls highlight the "Personal Investment" of God. Job is using "Pottery Language." He argues that God "Worked" on him, "Knotted" his bones together, and gave him "Life and Favor" (*Verse 12*). He is asking why a "Builder" would "Demolish" his own "Masterpiece."

3 Curdle me like Cheese (ha-Ke-Ghabinah Taqpi-eni): This is an "Intimate Botanical/Organic Metaphor" in the scrolls. It describes the "Biological Complexity" of conception and growth. Job is "Rooting" his existence in "God’s Deliberate Design."

4 Stalk me like a Lion (Ke-Shachal Thitzudeni): In the scrolls, Job feels he is being "Hunted" for "Sport" or "Spectacle." He sees God’s "Awesome Power" not as a "Comfort," but as a "Threat." He is "Uprooted" by the "Relentless Pursuit" of the Almighty. It signifies the "Emotional Terror" of feeling that God is "Against" you.

5 A little Moment of Cheer (we-Ab-ligah Me’at): Literally in the scrolls, "That I may brighten up a bit." Job is asking for a "Cessation of Hostilities." He wants a "Moment of Peace" before he vanishes into the "Land of Utter Darkness" (*Verse 22*). It marks the "Exhaustion" of the sufferer. He is no longer asking for "Vindication," just for "Silence."
