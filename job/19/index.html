Job 19

The Social Death

“He has alienated my family from me; my acquaintances are completely estranged.” 1
“My breath is offensive to my wife; I am loathsome to my own family.”
“Have pity on me, my friends, have pity, for the hand of God has struck me.” 2

The Iron Tool

“Oh, that my words were recorded, that they were written on a scroll!”
“That they were inscribed with an iron tool on lead, or engraved in rock forever!” 3

The Great Confession

“I know that my Redeemer lives, and that in the end he will stand on the earth.” 4
“And after my skin has been destroyed, yet in my flesh I will see God.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Estranged (Zaru): In the scrolls, this is the "Total Uprooting" of the social self. Job is not just "Sick," he is "Alien." His "Breath" (*Verse 17*)—the very sign of life—is "Offensive." He is a "Stranger" in his own "Home." It signifies the "Socio-Spiritual vacuum" that suffering creates.

2 Have Pity (Chonnu-ni): This is Job’s "Last Appeal" to human sympathy in the scrolls. He asks for "Grace" (*Chen*) from those who are "Crushing" him with "Logic." When "Explanation" fails, only "Pity" remains. The fact that they refuse it (*Chapter 20*) is the final blow to his earthly hope.

3 Inscribed with an Iron Tool (be-Et Barzel): The scrolls highlight Job’s "Longing for Permanence." Since he believes he is "Dying" in "Infamy," he wants his "Protest" to be "Carved in Rock." He wants a "Rooted Record" of his "Innocence" to survive the "Collapse of his Body."

4 My Redeemer Lives (Go’ali Chay): Literally in the scrolls, "My Kinsman-Redeemer." This is the "Pinnacle of Job’s Faith." The *Go'el* was the one who redeemed family members from debt or avenged their blood. Job is "Rooting" his vindication in a "Living Person" who will "Stand" (*Yaqum*) on the "Dust" (*Aphar*) at the end. It is a "Prophetic Breakthrough" beyond the grave.

5 In my Flesh I will see God (mi-Besari Echezeh Eloah): This is the "Root of Resurrection" in the scrolls. Job believes that even though his "Skin" is "Destroyed" (*Verse 26*), he will have a "Physical Encounter" with God. He "Self-Vindicates" through the "Divine Vision." It proves that "Integrity" is "Rooted" in an "Eternal Relationship" that even "Death" cannot "Uproot."
