Job 18

The Tearing of the Self

“You who tear yourself to pieces in your anger, is the earth to be abandoned for your sake?” 1

The Snare of the Godless

“His own feet thrust him into a net; he wanders into its mesh.”
“A noose is scattered in his path; a trap lies in his way.” 2

The King of Terrors

“Calamity is hungry for him; disaster is ready for him when he falls.” 3
“He is marched off to the King of Terrors.” 4

The Dried-Up Destiny

“His roots dry up below and his branches wither above.” 5
“The memory of him perishes from the earth; he has no name in the land.”

Translation Notes

1 Tear yourself to Pieces (Thoreph Napsh-o): In the scrolls, Bildad is "Uprooting" Job’s emotional validity. He sees Job’s "Pain" as "Arrogance." He asks if the "Laws of the Universe" (*the earth being abandoned*) should be changed just to accommodate Job’s "Innocence." He is "Rooting" his world-view in "Impersonal Stability."

2 A Noose is Scattered (Chebel): The scrolls highlight the "Inevitable Trap." Bildad is using "Hunting Imagery." He suggests that the "Wicked" create their own "Destruction" through their "Path." It is a "Rooted" warning that "Moral Drift" leads to "Fatal Entanglement."

3 Calamity is Hungry (Ye-hi Re-eb Ono): Literally in the scrolls, "His strength/wealth becomes hungry." It describes a "Consumption of the Self." The "Calamity" is not just an "Event," but a "Predator" that is "Waiting for the Fall."

4 The King of Terrors (Melekh Ballahoth): This is a "Terrifying Personification" in the scrolls. It likely refers to "Death" or the "God of the Underworld" (Mot). Bildad is saying that the "Final Destination" of the "Unrighteous" is a "Kingdom of Fear." It is the "Rooted" opposite of the "Kingdom of God."

5 His Roots dry up (Shorash-av Yibashu): The scrolls conclude with this "Botanical Curse." Bildad is "Uprooting" Job’s "Ancestry" and his "Legacy." By saying the "Roots dry up," he is pronouncing "Total Extinction." Since Job’s children are dead and his body is failing, Bildad is "Applying" this "Definition" directly to Job. It marks the "Maximum Severity" of the friends' assault.
