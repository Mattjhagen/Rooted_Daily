Job 37

The Pounding Heart

“At this my heart pounds and leaps from its place.” 1
“Listen! Listen to the roar of his voice, to the rumbling that comes from his mouth.”

The Command of the Elements

“He says to the snow, ‘Fall on the earth,’ and to the rain shower, ‘Be a mighty downpour.’” 2
“The breath of God produces ice, and the broad waters become frozen.”

The Balancing of the Clouds

“Do you know how the clouds hang poised, those wonders of him who has perfect knowledge?” 3

The Golden Majesty

“Out of the north he comes in golden splendor; God comes in awesome majesty.” 4
“The Almighty is beyond our reach and exalted in power.” 5

Translation Notes

1 My Heart Pounds (Ye-cheradh Libbi): In the scrolls, Elihu is "Reacting Physically" to the "Presence of the Storm." He is "Uprooting" the "Intellectual Detachment" of the previous dialogue. The "Voice" (*Qol*) of God is no longer a "Theological Concept"; it is a "Physical Vibration."

2 Mighty Downpour (Geshem Ma-tza-roth): The scrolls highlight the "Authority of God" over nature. He "Commands" the "Snow" (*Sheleg*) and the "Rain." It signifies that "Suffering" and "Comfort" are both "Dispatched" by the same "Sovereign Voice."

3 Clouds hang Poised (Miphle-she Ab): Literally in the scrolls, "The balancings of the clouds." Elihu is describing the "Weight and Tension" of the atmosphere. He is "Rooting" God’s "Wisdom" in the "Structural Integrity" of the universe. He asks Job if he can "Understand the Physics" of God’s "Mercy."

4 Golden Splendor (Zahab): Literally in the scrolls, "Gold." This likely refers to the "Brilliant Light" or "Sunlight" breaking through the "Storm Clouds" in the north. It is the "Root of the Theophany." God is "Approaching" not just in "Darkness," but in "Terrifying Radiance."

5 Beyond our Reach (lo-Metza-nu-hu): Literally "We cannot find Him." The scrolls conclude Elihu’s speech with a "Warning." Even as God "Approaches," He remains "Incomprehensible." He is "Exalted in Power" (*Sagi-Khoach*). It marks the end of "Human Speculation." Elihu stops speaking because the "Storm" has arrived. The "Silence" is now broken by the "Almighty" Himself. This is the "Rooted Threshold" of the book.
