Job 21

The Prosperous Godless

“Why do the wicked live on, growing old and increasing in power?” 1
“Their homes are safe and free from fear; no rod of God is on them.”

The Rejection of the Almighty

“They say to God, ‘Leave us alone! We have no desire to know your ways.’” 2
“Who is the Almighty that we should serve him? What would we gain by praying to him?”

The Equality of the Dust

“One person dies in full vigor, completely secure and at ease.”
“Another dies in bitterness of soul, never having enjoyed anything good.” 3
“Side by side they lie in the dust, and worms cover them both.” 4
“How can you console me with your empty notions? There is nothing left of your answers but falsehood!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Why do the Wicked live on? (Maddu’a Resha’im Yich-yu): In the scrolls, this is the "Ultimate Counter-Question." Job is "Uprooting" Zophar’s "Brief Joy" theory (*Chapter 20*). He points to the "Power" (*Gaberu Chayil*) and "Age" of the wicked as a "Theological Scandal" that the friends cannot explain.

2 Leave us Alone (Sur Mimennu): The scrolls highlight the "Active Rejection." The wicked aren't just "Forgetful"; they are "Intentionally Uprooted" from God’s ways. And yet, they prosper. It signifies that "Material Success" is not a reliable "Indicator of Spiritual Root."

3 Bitterness of Soul (be-Mar Naphesh): The scrolls record Job’s "Observation of Inequity." Some die in "Vigor" (*Verse 23*) and some die in "Bitterness." The "Justice" that the friends claim is "Visible" is actually "Hidden" or "Absent" in the material world.

4 Side by Side (Yachad): Literally in the scrolls, "As One." Death is the "Great Leveler." The "Dust" (*Aphar*) and the "Worms" (*Rimmah*) make no "Moral Distinctions." Job is "Uprooting" the idea that you can tell a man’s "Character" by his "End."

5 Falsehood (Ma-al): Literally in the scrolls, "Treachery" or "Infidelity." Job is calling his friends "Traitors to Truth." By "Lying" about the "State of the World" to protect their "Doctrine," they were being "Infidel" to reality. It marks the "Intellectual Collapse" of the friends' position. Job has "Uprooted" their logic using "Empirical Evidence."
