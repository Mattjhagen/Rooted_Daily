Job 25

The Order of the Heights

“Dominion and awe belong to God; he establishes order in the heights of heaven.” 1
“Can his armies be numbered? On whom does his light not rise?”

The Impurity of Stars

“If even the moon is not bright and the stars are not pure in his eyes...”

The Human Maggot

“How much less a mortal, who is but a maggot—a human being, who is only a worm!” 2

Translation Notes

1 Order in the Heights (Oseh Shalom bi-Meromav): Literally in the scrolls, "He makes peace in His high places." Bildad is "Uprooting" any "Challenge" to God’s administration. He argues that if the "Cosmic Forces" are "Submissive," then Job’s "Protest" is a "Stain" on the "Celestial Peace."

2 Only a Worm (Tole’ah): The scrolls highlight the "Ultimate Diminishment." After three rounds of dialogue, the friends have no "Answers" left, only "Insults." By calling man a "Maggot" (*Rimmah*) and a "Worm," Bildad is trying to "Uproot" human dignity entirely to protect "Divine Sovereignty." It signifies the "Exhaustion of the Legalists." They have "Nothing Left to Say." This is the "Last Word" from the three friends (Zophar does not even speak a third time). The stage is now set for Job’s final summary and the arrival of Elihu.
