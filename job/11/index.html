Job 11

The Silence of the Babbler

“Should all this babble go unanswered? Is this talker to be vindicated?” 1
“Know this: God has even forgotten some of your sin.” 2

The Fathomless Heights

“Can you fathom the mysteries of God? Can you probe the limits of the Almighty?” 3
“They are higher than the heavens above—what can you do?”

The Brightness of Repentance

“Then, free from fault, you will lift up your face; you will stand firm and without fear.” 4
“Life will be brighter than noonday, and darkness will become like morning.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Should all this Babble (Rob Deberim): Literally in the scrolls, "A multitude of words." Zophar is "Uprooting" Job’s emotional honesty as "Empty Verbiage." He is the most "Judgmental" of the friends, seeing Job’s pain as a "Prop for a Lie."

2 God has forgotten (Ya-ashekha): This is the "Cruelest Blow" in the scrolls. Zophar suggests that God is actually "Going Easy" on Job. He claims that Job’s "Sins" are so great that the current "Agony" is a "Discounted Penalty." It signifies a "Theology of Pure Wrath."

3 Can you Fathom (ha-Cheqer Eloah Thimtza): The scrolls highlight the "Limits of Human Reason." Zophar uses "Divine Mystery" as a "Club" to silence Job. He argues that Job’s "Lack of Understanding" is proof of his "Sin." It is the "Root" of the friends' circular reasoning: "God is great, you are small, therefore you must be guilty."

4 Stand Firm (Mutzaq): Literally in the scrolls, "Poured out" like metal into a mold—meaning "Solid" or "Firm." Zophar promises that "Repentance" will "Re-root" Job’s stability. He is offering a "Transaction"—if Job gives God a "Confession," God will give Job "Security."

5 Brighter than Noonday (mi-Tzohorayim Yaqum Chaled): The scrolls conclude with this "Vision of Restoration." Zophar paints a picture of a "Brilliant Future." He promises that the "Darkness" will be "Uprooted" by a "Perpetual Morning." But it is a "Hollow Hope" because it is "Rooted" in a "False Accusation." It marks the "Maximum Pressure" of the first round of speeches.
