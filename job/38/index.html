Job 38

The Whirlwind Speaks

“Then the Lord spoke to Job out of the storm.” 1
“Who is this that obscures my plans with words without knowledge?”
“Brace yourself like a man; I will question you, and you shall answer me.” 2

The Foundations

“Where were you when I laid the earth’s foundation? Tell me, if you understand.” 3
“While the morning stars sang together and all the angels shouted for joy?”

The Proud Waves

“Who shut up the sea behind doors when it burst forth from the womb?”
“When I said, ‘This far you may come and no farther; here is where your proud waves halt’?” 4

The Chains of the Heavens

“Can you bind the chains of the Pleiades? Can you loosen Orion’s belt?” 5
“Can you bring forth the constellations in their seasons or lead out the Bear with its cubs?”

Translation Notes

1 Out of the Storm (min ha-S arah): In the scrolls, this is the "Answer of Presence." God doesn't "Explain" Job’s suffering; He "Appears" within it. He "Uproots" the "Legal Debate" and replaces it with a "Cosmic Encounter." The "Whirlwind" is the "Manifestation of Divine Energy."

2 Brace yourself like a Man (Ezor-na khe-Gheber Chalatzekha): Literally in the scrolls, "Gird up your loins like a mighty-man." God is calling Job to the "Warrior Posture" (*Gheber*). He is "Rooting" Job’s "Humanity" in "Dignity" even while challenging his "Knowledge."

3 Laid the Earth’s Foundation (be-Yosdi Eretz): The scrolls highlight the "Ancient Origins." God asks Job to "Recollect" the "Root of the World." He is "Uprooting" Job’s "Temporal Perspective." If Job wasn't there at the "Beginning," how can he judge the "Process"?

4 Proud Waves Halt (Ge’on Gall-ekha): Literally in the scrolls, "The majesty of your waves." God is the "Master of Boundaries." He "Rooted" the sea in "Limits." He argues that "Chaos" (*the sea*) is not "Out of Control," but is "Held in Place" by His "Decree." It is a "Message" to Job that his "Chaos" is also "Contained."

5 Chains of the Pleiades (Ma’adannoth Kimah): The scrolls highlight the "Celestial Mechanics." God is the "Root of the Stars." He "Binds" and "Loosens" the "Constellations" (*Mazzaroth*). He is showing Job that the "Same Wisdom" that manages the "Vast Galaxy" is managing the "Small Life" of a man. It marks the shift from "Human Logic" to "Divine Perspective."
