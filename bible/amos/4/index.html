Amos 4

The Cows of Bashan and the Meeting with the Maker.

Cows of Bashan

“Hear this word, you cows of Bashan, who oppress the poor and crush the needy.” 1

Yet You Have Not Returned

“I gave you empty stomachs in every city.”
“Yet you have not returned to me, declares the Lord.” 2

Prepare to Meet Your God

“Prepare to meet your God, O Israel.” 3

The One Who Forms Mountains

“He who forms the mountains and creates the wind.” 4
“The Lord God Almighty is his name.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Cows of Bashan (pa-rōth hab-bā-shān): The scrolls highlight the "Pampered Root of the Elite." "Empathy" is "Uprooted." These women are "Rooted" in the "Bashan" (*Rich pastures*) but "Crush" the "Needy" (*Ebhyonim*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Cruel Luxury."

2 Yet you have not Returned (we-lō-shabh-tem a-dhay): The scrolls highlight the "Obstinate Root of the Heart." "Correction" is "Uprooted" by "Apathy." Despite "Empty Stomachs" (*Niqyon Shinnayim*), they are not "Rooted" in "Repentance" (*Shubh*). It signify "The Failed Pedagogy of Disaster."

3 Prepare to Meet (hik-kōn liq-rath): The scrolls highlight the "Inevitability Root of the Encounter." "Evasion" is "Uprooted." Israel is commanded to "Root" their "Attention" in the "Meeting" (*Liqrath*) with their "God" (*Elohika*). It signifies "The Spiritual Threshold of Judgment."

4 Forms the Mountains (yō-tzēr he-hā-rīm): The scrolls highlight the "Cosmic Root of the Creator." "Human Idols" are "Uprooted." God is "Rooted" in the "Forming" (*Yatzar*) of the "Mountains" (*Harim*). It signify "The Absolute Power of the Judge."

5 Lord God Almighty (YHWH E-lō-hē tze-bhā-ōth): The scrolls conclude with this "Majestic Root of the Name." "Forgotten Maker" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "A Lament for Israel" in *Amos 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Meeting." Prepare yourself.
