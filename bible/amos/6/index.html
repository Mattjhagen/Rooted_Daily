Amos 6

The Ivory Beds and the Feasting of the Complacent.

Woe to the Secure

“Woe to you who are complacent in Zion, and to you who feel secure on Mount Samaria.” 1

Beds of Ivory

“You lie on beds inlaid with ivory and lounge on your couches.” 2
“You drink wine by the bowlful and use the finest lotions.”

The Ruin of Joseph

“But you do not grieve over the ruin of Joseph.” 3
“You will be among the first to go into exile.” 4

The Pride of Jacob

“I abhor the pride of Jacob and detest his fortresses.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Complacent (hash-sha-a-nan-nīm): The scrolls highlight the "Apathetic Root of the Elite." "Vigilance" is "Uprooted." They are "Rooted" in "Quiet/Ease" (*Shaanan*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Spiritual Lethargy."

2 Beds of Ivory (mit-tōth shēn): The scrolls highlight the "Extravagant Root of Indulgence." "Simplicity" is "Uprooted." Their "Rest" is "Rooted" in "Ivory" (*Shen*). It signify "The Connection between Luxury and Hard-Heartedness."

3 Do not Grieve (we-lō-ne-che-lū): Literally "They are not sickened." The scrolls highlight the "Callous Root of the Rich." "Empathy" is "Uprooted." They are not "Rooted" in the "Sorrow" of the "Ruined" (*Sheber*). It signifies "The Moral Death of the Comfortable."

4 First to go into Exile (be-rōsh gō-līm): The scrolls highlight the "Priority Root of the Judgment." "Security" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Departure" (*Golah*) as the "Head" (*Rosh*) of the line. It signify "The Sudden End of the Feast."

5 Abhor the Pride (me-ā-ēbh ā-nō-khī eth-ghe-ōn Ya-a-qōbh): The scrolls conclude with this "Rejecting Root of the Ego." "National Pride" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Loathing" in their "Arrogance" (*Gheon*). It marks the transition to "The Visions of Amos" in *Amos 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Complacent." The city is delivered up.
