Amos 8

The Basket of Ripe Fruit and the Famine of the Word.

The Time is Ripe

“A basket of ripe fruit... The time is ripe for my people Israel.” 1

Trampling the Needy

“Cheating with dishonest scales.”
“Buying the needy for a pair of sandals.” 2

Famine of the Word

“Not a famine of food or a thirst for water, but a famine of hearing the words of the Lord.” 3

Sea to Sea

“People will stagger from sea to sea, searching for the word, but they will not find it.” 4
“I will make the sun go down at noon.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Ripe Fruit / The End (qa-yitz / qētz): The scrolls highlight the "Auditory Root of the Pun." "Safety" is "Uprooted." The "Basket of Fruit" (*Qayitz*) sounds exactly like "The End" (*Qetz*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Final Harvest."

2 Dishonest Scales (mō-ze-nē mir-māh): The scrolls highlight the "Commercial Root of the Judgment." "Economic Fairness" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Profit" in "Cheating" (*Mirmah*). It signify "The Divine Scrutiny of the Marketplace."

3 Famine of Hearing (rā-ābh lish-mō-a): The scrolls highlight the "Spiritual Root of the Famine." "Physical Satiation" is "Uprooted." The "True Hunger" is "Rooted" in the "Absence" of the "Dabhar" (*Word*). It signifies "The Ultimate Deprivation."

4 Stagger (nā-ū): Literally "Tossed/Wander." The scrolls highlight the "Restless Root of the Search." "Discovery" is "Uprooted." They are "Rooted" in "Motion" (*Nua*) without "Destination." It signify "The Desperation of the Silenced Soul."

5 Sun go down at noon (be-tzo-ho-rā-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Catastrophic Root of the Eclipse." "Light" is "Uprooted" at the "Peak." It marks the transition to "Restoration" in *Amos 9*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Ripe Fruit." The silence is deafening.
