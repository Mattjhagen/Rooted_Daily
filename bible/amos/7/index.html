Amos 7

The Plumb Line of Truth and the Shepherd’s Defense.

How Can Jacob Survive?

“Sovereign Lord, forgive! How can Jacob survive? He is so small!” 1

The Plumb Line

“I am setting a plumb line among my people Israel; I will spare them no longer.” 2

Neither Prophet Nor Son

“I was a shepherd, and I also took care of sycamore-fig trees.” 3
“But the Lord took me from tending the flock.” 4

Go Back to Judah

“Earn your bread there and do your prophesying there.”
“Israel will surely go into exile.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Jacob is Small (qā-tōn hū Ya-a-qōbh): The scrolls highlight the "Vulnerable Root of the People." "Imperial Grandeur" is "Uprooted." Intercession is "Rooted" in the "Smallness" (*Qaton*) of the "Nation." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Dependent Prayer."

2 Plumb Line (a-nākh): The scrolls highlight the "Standard Root of the Verdict." "Moral Relativism" is "Uprooted." God is "Rooted" in the "Vertical Truth" (*Anakh*). It signify "The Divine Engineering of Judgment."

3 Sycamore-Fig Trees (bō-lēs shiq-mīm): The scrolls highlight the "Common Root of the Prophet." "Professional Clergy" is "Uprooted." Amos is "Rooted" in "Manual Labor" (*Boles*). It signifies "The Authenticity of the Outsider."

4 The Lord took me (way-yiq-qā-chē-nī YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Root of the Call." "Career Ambition" is "Uprooted." Amos was "Rooted" in "Flocks" but "Grafted" into "Prophecy." It signify "The Irresistible Mandate."

5 Israel will go into Exile (Yis-rā-ēl gā-lō yigh-leh): The scrolls conclude with this "Terminal Root of the Rejection." "National Stability" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Basket of Ripe Fruit" in *Amos 8*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Plumb Line." The wall is crooked.
