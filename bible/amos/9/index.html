Amos 9

The Fallen Shelter and the Final Planting.

No Escape

“Though they dig down to the depths... though they climb up to the heavens, my hand will find them.” 1

The Sieve of Nations

“I will shake Israel among all the nations as grain is shaken in a sieve.” 2

David’s Fallen Shelter

“In that day I will restore David’s fallen shelter and repair its broken walls.” 3

Never Again Uprooted

“I will plant Israel in their own land, never again to be uprooted.” 4
“Says the Lord your God.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Dig down (im-yach-te-rū): The scrolls highlight the "Subterranean Root of the Escape." "Safety" is "Uprooted" everywhere. Whether they "Root" their "Hiding" in "Sheol" or "Heavens" (*Shamayim*), they are "Found" (*Laqach*). It signifies "The Omnipresence of the Judge."

2 Sieve (ke-a-sher yit-nu-a bak-ke-bhā-rāh): The scrolls highlight the "Separating Root of the Diaspora." "Purity" is "Uprooted" from "Dross." Israel is "Rooted" in the "Shaking" (*Nua*) but the "Pebble" (*Tzeror*) will not fall. It signify "The Preservation through Turbulence."

3 Fallen Shelter (suk-kath dā-wīd han-nō-phe-let): The scrolls highlight the "Tattered Root of the Dynasty." "Imperial Splendor" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Restoration" in the "Sukkah" (*Booth/Shelter*) of David. It signifies "The Aesthetic of Rebuilt Ruins."

4 Never Again Uprooted (we-lō yit-te-shū ōdh): Literally "They will not be plucked up again." The scrolls conclude with this "Eternal Root of the Land." "Exile" is "Uprooted." Israel is "Rooted" (*Nata*) forever. It signify "The Finality of the Divine Promise."

5 Says the Lord your God (ā-mar YHWH E-lō-he-khā): The scrolls conclude with this "Seal of Authority." It marks the end of *Amos* and the transition to *Obadiah 1*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Final Planting." You are home to stay.
