Amos 2

The Price of Sandals and the Crushing of the Cart.

A Pair of Sandals

“They sell the innocent for silver and the needy for a pair of sandals.” 1

Trampling the Poor

“They trample on the heads of the poor as on the dust of the ground.” 2

Wine and Silence

“You made the Nazirites drink wine and commanded the prophets not to prophesy.” 3

The Crushing Cart

“I will crush you as a cart with its load of grain crushes the ground.” 4
“The bravest warriors will flee naked on that day.” 5

Translation Notes

1 A Pair of Sandals (ba-a-bhur na-a-la-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Trivial Root of the Crime." "Justice" is "Uprooted." The "Needy" (*Ebhyon*) are "Rooted" in a "Price" as low as "Sandals" (*Naalayim*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Utter Disregard."

2 Trample on the Heads (hash-shō-a-phīm al-a-phar e-retz): Literally "Those who pant/gape after the dust." The scrolls highlight the "Obsessive Root of Oppression." "Equity" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Desire" even in the "Dust" (*Aphar*) on the "Poor" (*Dallim*). It signify "The Greed that seeks the last scrap."

3 Drink Wine (way-yish-qū): The scrolls highlight the "Subversive Root of the Corruption." "Dedication" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Control" in the "Violation" of the "Nazirite" vow. It signifies "The Suppression of the Sacred."

4 I will Crush (mē-īq): Literally "I will make it totter/press." The scrolls highlight the "Weighty Root of the Judgment." "Movement" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Action" in the "Crushing" (*Uq*) of the "Cart." It signify "The Irresistible Pressure of Guilt."

5 Flee Naked (ā-rōm yā-nūs): The scrolls conclude with this "Exposing Root of the Defeat." "Protection" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Witness against Israel" in *Amos 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Crushing Cart." There is no escape.
