Amos 5

The Lament of the Virgin and the River of Justice.

Seek Me and Live

“Seek me and live; do not seek Bethel.” 1

Justice into Wormwood

“There are those who turn justice into wormwood.” 2

Darkness, Not Light

“The day of the Lord will be darkness, not light.” 3

Roll On Like a River

“Let justice roll on like a river, righteousness like a never-failing stream!” 4
“I hate, I despise your religious festivals.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Seek me and Live (dir-shū-nī we-che-yū): The scrolls highlight the "Vital Root of the Search." "Stagnation" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Presence" in the "Seeking" (*Darash*) that leads to "Life" (*Chayah*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Persistent Faith."

2 Justice into Wormwood (mish-pāt le-la-a-nāh): The scrolls highlight the "Bitter Root of Corruption." "Sweetness" is "Uprooted." The "Justice" (*Mishpat*) is "Rooted" in "Wormwood" (*Laanah*). It signify "The Spiritual Toxicity of Inequity."

3 Darkness, not Light (chō-shekh we-lō ōr): The scrolls highlight the "Subverted Root of Expectation." "False Hope" is "Uprooted." The "Day" (*Yom*) is "Rooted" in "Obscurity" (*Choshekh*). It signifies "The Shock of the Unprepared Soul."

4 Never-Failing Stream (na-chal ē-thān): Literally "An ever-flowing/permanent wadi." The scrolls highlight the "Persistent Root of Righteousness." "Intermittent Mercy" is "Uprooted." "Tzedaqah" is "Rooted" in "Permanence" (*Ethan*). It signify "The Inexhaustible Nature of God’s Character."

5 I hate your Festivals (śā-nē-thī mā-as-tī): The scrolls conclude with this "Rejecting Root of the Ritual." "Hypocrisy" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Woe to the Complacent" in *Amos 6*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The River of Justice." Seek the Good.
