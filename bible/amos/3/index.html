Amos 3

The Chosen Family and the Compulsion of the Roar.

You Only Have I Chosen

“You only have I chosen of all the families of the earth; therefore I will punish you.” 1

Revealing the Plan

“Surely the Sovereign Lord does nothing without revealing his plan to his servants the prophets.” 2

Who Can But Prophesy?

“The lion has roared—who will not fear? The Sovereign Lord has spoken—who can but prophesy?” 3

Houses of Ivory

“The houses adorned with ivory will be destroyed.” 4
“The horns of the altar will be cut off and fall.” 5

Translation Notes

1 You only have I Chosen (raq eth-khem yā-dha-at-tī): Literally "Only you have I known." The scrolls highlight the "Intimate Root of Responsibility." "Anonymity" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Knowledge" (*Yada*) in Israel, which necessitates "Punishment" (*Paqad*) for sin. It signifies "The Weight of the Covenant."

2 Revealing his Plan (gā-lāh sō-dhō): Literally "He uncovers his secret/counsel." The scrolls highlight the "Transparent Root of the Word." "Obscurity" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Action" in the "Sod" (*Secret/Circle of Counsel*) shared with the prophets. It signify "The Divine Logic of History."

3 Who can but Prophesy? (mī lō yin-nā-bhē): The scrolls highlight the "Involuntary Root of the Prophet." "Silence" is "Uprooted." The "Word" is "Rooted" in the "Roar" (*Shaagh*). It signifies "The Irresistible Compulsion of Truth."

4 Houses of Ivory (bat-tē hash-shēn): The scrolls highlight the "Extravagant Root of the Decay." "Utility" is "Uprooted." Their "Identity" is "Rooted" in "Ivory" (*Shen*). It signify "The Aesthetic of Fragile Luxury."

5 Horns of the Altar (qar-nōth ham-miz-bē-ach): The scrolls conclude with this "Powerless Root of the Sanctuary." "Protection" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Prepare to Meet Your God" in *Amos 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Roar." The horns will fall.
