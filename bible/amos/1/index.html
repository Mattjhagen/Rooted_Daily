Amos 1

The Lion Roars from Zion and the Judgment of the Sledges.

The Lord Roars

“The Lord roars from Zion and thunders from Jerusalem.” 1

For Three Sins, Even Four

“For three sins of Damascus, even for four, I will not relent.” 2

Sledges of Iron

“Because she threshed Gilead with sledges having iron teeth.” 3

Casting Off Pity

“Because he pursued his brother with a sword and cast off all pity.” 4
“Because he ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead.” 5

Translation Notes

1 The Lord Roars (YHWH mit-tzi-yōn yish-āgh): The scrolls highlight the "Vocal Root of Sovereignty." "Silence" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Message" in the "Roar" (*Sha-agh*) of the "Lion." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Impending Justice."

2 Three Sins, Even Four (al-she-lō-shāh pish-ē... we-al-ar-bā-āh): The scrolls highlight the "Numerical Root of the Limit." "Endurance" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Judgment" in the "Exceeding" of the "Measure" (*Pesha*). It signify "The Finality of the Edict."

3 Sledges of Iron (ba-cha-rū-tzōth hab-bar-zel): The scrolls highlight the "Industrial Root of Cruelty." "Humanity" is "Uprooted." The "Oppression" is "Rooted" in "Iron" (*Barzel*). It signifies "The Mechanical Nature of Tyranny."

4 Cast off all Pity (shi-chēth ra-cha-māw): Literally "He corrupted/destroyed his compassions." The scrolls highlight the "Relational Root of the Crime." "Brotherhood" is "Uprooted." He "Rooted" his "Action" in "Sword" (*Cherebh*) rather than "Mercy" (*Rachamim*). It signify "The Spiritual Suicide of the Cruel."

5 Ripped Open (biq-ā-mām): The scrolls conclude with this "Brutal Root of Expansion." "Sanctity of Life" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Judgment on Moab and Israel" in *Amos 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Lion’s Roar." He does not relent.
