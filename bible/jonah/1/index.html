Jonah 1

The Flight to Tarshish and the Descent into the Deep.

Running from the Face

“But Jonah ran away from the Lord and headed for Tarshish.” 1

A Violent Storm

“The Lord sent a great wind on the sea, and such a violent storm arose.” 2

The Lot Fell on Jonah

“They cast lots and the lot fell on Jonah.” 3

The Great Fish

“Now the Lord provided a huge fish to swallow Jonah.” 4
“Three days and three nights in the belly of the fish.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Ran away (way-yā-qom yō-nāh lib-rō-ach): Literally "Jonah rose to flee." The scrolls highlight the "Reactive Root of Disobedience." "Submission" is "Uprooted." He "Roots" his "Direction" away from "The Face of YHWH" (*Milliphne*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Fugitive."

2 Violent Storm (sa-ar gā-dhōl): The scrolls highlight the "Atmospheric Root of the Pursuit." "Calm" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Message" in the "Great Wind" (*Ruach Gedolah*). It signify "The Divine Agency of the Storm."

3 Lot fell on Jonah (way-yip-pōl hag-gō-rāl al-yō-nāh): The scrolls highlight the "Chance Root of Discovery." "Anonymity" is "Uprooted." The "Lot" (*Goral*) is "Rooted" in "Sovereignty" to find the hidden man. It signifies "The Exposure of the Secret Heart."

4 Provided (way-man YHWH): Literally "The Lord appointed/assigned." The scrolls highlight the "Prepared Root of the Mercy." "Finality" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" the "Saving" in the "Great Fish" (*Dagh Gadol*). It signify "The Strange Grace of the Abyss."

5 Belly of the Fish (bim-ē had-dāgh): The scrolls conclude with this "Confining Root of the Incubation." "Freedom" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Jonah’s Prayer" in *Jonah 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Flight." You cannot hide from God.
