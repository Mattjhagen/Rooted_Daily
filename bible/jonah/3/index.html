Jonah 3

The Forty Days and the Sackcloth of the City.

A Second Time

“Then the word of the Lord came to Jonah a second time.” 1

Forty Days

“Forty more days and Nineveh will be overthrown.” 2

From Throne to Dust

“He rose from his throne, took off his royal robes, and sat down in the dust.” 3
“Let everyone call urgently on God.”

God Relented

“He relented and did not bring on them the destruction.” 4
“When God saw how they turned from their evil ways.” 5

Translation Notes

1 A Second Time (shē-nīth): The scrolls highlight the "Iterative Root of Grace." "Finality" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Message" in a "Second" (*Shenith*) opportunity. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Do-Over."

2 Forty Days (ar-bā-īm yōm): The scrolls highlight the "Temporal Root of the Probation." "Eternity" is "Uprooted." Nineveh is "Rooted" in a "Window" of "Forty" (*Arba-im*) days. It signify "The Divine Deadline."

3 From Throne to Dust (way-ye-ghe-as min-kis-ō): The scrolls highlight the "Hierarchical Root of Repentance." "Royal Pride" is "Uprooted." The "King" is "Rooted" in "Sackcloth" (*Saq*) and "Ashes" (*Epher*). It signifies "The Spiritual Equalizer."

4 God Relented (way-yin-nā-chem hā-E-lō-hīm): The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Root of the Divine Heart." "Fatalism" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Action" in "Relenting" (*Nacham*). It signify "The Responsiveness of the Sovereign."

5 Turned from Evil (shā-bhū mid-dar-kām): The scrolls conclude with this "Kinetic Root of Change." "Inertia" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Jonah’s Anger" in *Jonah 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Second Call." The city is spared.
