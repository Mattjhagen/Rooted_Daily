Jonah 4

The Leafy Plant and the Compassion of the Creator.

I Knew You Were Gracious

“I knew that you are a gracious and compassionate God, slow to anger and abounding in love.” 1

The Leafy Plant

“The Lord God provided a leafy plant and made it grow up over Jonah.” 2

Worm and Wind

“God provided a worm, which chewed the plant.”
“God provided a scorching east wind.” 3

Should I Not Have Concern?

“Should I not have concern for the great city of Nineveh?” 4
“And also many animals?” 5

Translation Notes

1 Gracious and Compassionate (chan-nūn we-ra-chūm): The scrolls highlight the "Merciful Root of the Divine Essence." "Judgment" is "Uprooted" by "Grace." God is "Rooted" in "Abounding Love" (*Rab-Chesed*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Persistent Mercy."

2 Leafy Plant (qī-qā-yōn): The scrolls highlight the "Ephemeral Root of the Comfort." "Endurance" is "Uprooted." The "Qiqayon" is "Rooted" in "Shade" (*Tzel*) but survives only a day. It signify "The Fragility of Human Happiness."

3 Scorching East Wind (rū-ach qā-dhīm chă-rī-shīth): The scrolls highlight the "Abrasive Root of the Lesson." "Comfort" is "Uprooted" by the "Wind" (*Ruach*). It signifies "The Divine Heat that reveals the heart."

4 Concern for Nineveh (e-chūs al-Nī-ne-wēh): Literally "Should I not pity/spare?" The scrolls highlight the "Universal Root of the Pity." "Nationalism" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Concern" (*Chus*) in the "Great City." It signify "The Scale of Divine Compassion."

5 Many Animals (be-hē-māh rab-bāh): The scrolls conclude with this "Inclusive Root of the Creation." "Anthropocentrism" is "Uprooted." It marks the end of *Jonah* and the transition to *Micah 1*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Concern." God loves the cattle too.
