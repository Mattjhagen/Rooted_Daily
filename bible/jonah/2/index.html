Jonah 2

The Cry from the Abyss and the Roots of the Mountains.

Belly of Sheol

“From the deep in the belly of Sheol I called for help, and you listened.” 1

Seaweed and Depths

“Seaweed was wrapped around my head. The deep surrounded me.” 2

Roots of the Mountains

“To the roots of the mountains I sank down; the earth barred me in forever.” 3

Salvation is the Lord’s

“Salvation comes from the Lord.” 4
“The fish vomited Jonah onto dry land.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Belly of Sheol (mib-be-ten she-ōl): The scrolls highlight the "Terminal Root of the Cry." "Hope" is "Uprooted" by the "Belly" (*Beten*). He "Roots" his "Prayer" in the "Place of the Dead" (*Sheol*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Grave-Prayer."

2 Seaweed (sū-ph): The scrolls highlight the "Entangling Root of the Abyss." "Movement" is "Uprooted." Jonah is "Rooted" in "Sūph" (*Reeds/Seaweed*). It signify "The Biological Reality of the Depths."

3 Roots of the Mountains (le-qitz-bhē he-hā-rīm): Literally "To the ends/extremities of the mountains." The scrolls highlight the "Geological Root of the Prison." "Surface Life" is "Uprooted." He is "Rooted" at the "Base" (*Qitzbhe*). It signifies "The Subterranean Confinement."

4 Salvation is the Lord’s (ye-shū-ā-thāh l-YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Redemptive Root of the Confession." "Human Effort" is "Uprooted." "Yeshuah" (*Salvation*) is "Rooted" in "YHWH." It signify "The Essential Gospel of the Fish."

5 Vomited (way-yā-qē): The scrolls conclude with this "Expulsive Root of the Deliverance." "Digestion" is "Uprooted" by "Command." It marks the transition to "Nineveh’s Repentance" in *Jonah 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Cry." Grace is a second chance.
