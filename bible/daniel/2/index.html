Daniel 2

The Dream of the Statue and the Stone of the Mountain.

A Troubled Mind

“Nebuchadnezzar had dreams; his mind was troubled and he could not sleep.” 1

The Secret Revealed

“During the night the mystery was revealed to Daniel in a vision.” 2

The Great Statue

“Head of pure gold... chest and arms of silver...”
“Legs of iron... feet of iron and clay.”
“A rock was cut out, but not by human hands.” 3

The Unshakable Kingdom

“The God of heaven will set up a kingdom that will never be destroyed.” 4
“Surely your God is the God of gods.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Mind was Troubled (wat-tiph-pā-em rū-chō): Literally "His spirit was pounded/beaten." The scrolls highlight the "Visceral Root of the Dream." "Peace" is "Uprooted" by "Mystery." It signifies "The Impact of Divine Revelation on the Unbeliever."

2 The Mystery was Revealed (rā-zā gal-lī): The scrolls highlight the "Apocalyptic Root of Discovery." "Hiddenness" is "Uprooted." The "Mystery" (*Raz*) is "Rooted" in the "Night Vision." It signify "The Divine Monopoly on Secrets."

3 Not by Human Hands (lā bī-dha-yin): In Aramaic. The scrolls highlight the "Supernatural Root of the Stone." "Human Agency" is "Uprooted." The "Kingdom" is "Rooted" in "Divine Origin." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Non-Mechanical Intervention."

4 Never be Destroyed (lā thith-cha-bhal): The scrolls highlight the "Permanent Root of the Kingdom." "Transience" is "Uprooted." It signify "The Finality of God’s Reign."

5 God of Gods (E-lāh e-lā-hīn): The scrolls conclude with this "Superlative Root of Acknowledgment." Nebuchadnezzar "Roots" Daniel’s "God" at the "Peak" of the "Hierarchy." It marks the transition to "The Fiery Furnace" in *Daniel 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Great Statue." The stone is coming.
