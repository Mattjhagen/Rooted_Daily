Daniel 1

The Choice Youths and the Trial of the Table.

Chosen for Babylon

“Youths without any physical defect, handsome, and showing aptitude for every kind of learning.” 1

Daniel’s Resolution

“Daniel resolved not to defile himself with the royal food and wine.” 2

The Ten Day Test

“Give us nothing but vegetables to eat and water to drink.” 3
“They looked healthier and better nourished than any of the young men.”

Ten Times Better

“He found them ten times better than all the magicians and enchanters.” 4
“And Daniel remained there until the first year of King Cyrus.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Physical Defect (mūm): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Selection." Nebuchadnezzar seeks "Perfection" (*Mumless*). It signify "The High Cost of Imperial Service."

2 Resolved not to Defile (śām Dā-ni-yēl al-lib-bō): Literally "Daniel set upon his heart." The scrolls highlight the "Intentional Root of Sanctity." "Babylonian Culture" is "Uprooted" from his "Diet." He "Roots" his "Identity" in the "Law." It signifies "The Courage of the Minority."

3 Vegetables (haz-zē-rō-īm): Literally "Sown seeds." The scrolls highlight the "Organic Root of Purity." Daniel "Roots" his "Health" in "Seeds" (*Zeroim*) rather than "Royal Meat" (*Pathbag*). It signify "The Power of Natural Law under Divine Favor."

4 Ten Times Better (e-śer yā-dhōth): Literally "Ten hands." The scrolls highlight the "Exponential Root of Wisdom." "Imperial Learning" is "Uprooted" by "Divine Understanding." It signifies "The Superhuman Advantage of Faithfulness."

5 Until King Cyrus (adh-she-nath a-chath le-Khō-resh): The scrolls conclude with this "Chronological Root of Endurance." Daniel "Roots" himself in "Babylon" through multiple empires. It marks the transition to "The King’s Dream" in *Daniel 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Vegetable Test." Better in every way.
