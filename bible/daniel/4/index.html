Daniel 4

The Great Tree and the Seven Times of Madness.

The Tree that Touched the Sky

“The tree grew large and strong and its top touched the sky; it was visible to the ends of the earth.” 1

Cut Down the Tree

“Cut down the tree and trim off its branches... but let the stump and its roots remain.” 2

Mind of an Animal

“Let his mind be changed from that of a man... till seven times pass by for him.” 3

The Most High is Sovereign

“I, Nebuchadnezzar, raised my eyes toward heaven, and my sanity was restored.” 4
“Those who walk in pride he is able to humble.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Touched the Sky (ye-mat-tē lish-ma-yā): The scrolls highlight the "Vertical Root of Hubris." "Moderation" is "Uprooted." The "Tree" (*Ilan*) is "Rooted" in the "Sky" (*Shumaya*). It signifies "The Spiritual Danger of Unbounded Ambition."

2 Stump and its Roots (iq-qar shar-shō-hī): The scrolls highlight the "Residual Root of Mercy." "Total Destruction" is "Uprooted." The "Stump" (*Iqqar*) is "Rooted" in the "Earth" (*Ar'a*) with a "Band of Iron." It signify "The Potential for Rebirth through Humiliation."

3 Mind of an Animal (lib-bhēh min-e-nā-shā): Literally "His heart from man." The scrolls highlight the "Degenerative Root of the King." "Human Dignity" is "Uprooted." He is "Rooted" in the "Beast" (*Cheyvah*). It signifies "The Divine Leveling of the Proud."

4 Sanity was Restored (man-de-ī a-lay yethūbh): Literally "My knowledge returned to me." The scrolls highlight the "Epistemic Root of Restoration." "Madness" is "Uprooted" through "Vertical Focus." It signify "The Re-Rooting of the Soul in the Most High."

5 Walk in Pride (ha-le-khīn be-ghē-wah): The scrolls conclude with this "Pedestrian Root of Arrogance." "Pride" is "Uprooted" by "Justice." It marks the transition to "Belshazzar’s Feast" in *Daniel 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Humbled Tree." He is sovereign over all.
