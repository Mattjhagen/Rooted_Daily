Daniel 5

The Banquet of Belshazzar and the Fingers on the Wall.

The Profaned Vessels

“Belshazzar gave orders to bring in the gold and silver vessels from the Temple.” 1

The Hand on the Plaster

“Suddenly the fingers of a human hand appeared and wrote on the plaster of the wall.” 2
“His knees were knocking.”

MENE, MENE, TEKEL, PARSIN

“This is the inscription that was written.”
“MENE: God has numbered your kingdom.” 3
“TEKEL: You have been weighed and found wanting.” 4

The Fall of Babylon

“That very night Belshazzar was slain.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Temple Vessels (mā-nē-khā dī hē-khelā): The scrolls highlight the "Sacrilegious Root of the Banquet." "Sacredness" is "Uprooted" by "Arrogance." The "Vessels" (*Manin*) of the "Palace" (*Hekhal*) are "Rooted" in a "Drunken Party." It signifies "The Spiritual Blindness of the Successor."

2 Fingers of a Hand (etz-be-ān dī yadh-e-nāsh): The scrolls highlight the "Manifest Root of the Oracle." "Imperial Security" is "Uprooted." The "Hand" is "Rooted" in the "Plaster" (*Gira*). It signify "The Unstoppable Announcement of Doom."

3 MENE (me-nē): Literally "Numbered." The scrolls highlight the "Terminal Root of the Reign." "Indefinite Power" is "Uprooted." God has "Rooted" the "End" in a "Number" (*Mene*). It signifies "The Divine Limitation of Human History."

4 TEKEL (te-qēl): Literally "Weighed." The scrolls highlight the "Evaluative Root of Character." "Pride" is "Uprooted." The "King" is "Rooted" in "Deficiency" (*Wanting*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Moral Balance."

5 Belshazzar was Slain (Be-lēsh-atztzar... qe-thīl): The scrolls conclude with this "Abrupt Root of the End." "Babylon" is "Uprooted" in a "Single Night." It marks the transition to "Daniel in the Lions' Den" in *Daniel 6*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Writing on the Wall." The kingdom is divided.
