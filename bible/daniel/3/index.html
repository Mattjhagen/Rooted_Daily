Daniel 3

The Golden Image and the Fourth Man in the Flame.

The Image of Gold

“King Nebuchadnezzar made an image of gold, sixty cubits high and six cubits wide.” 1

If It Be So

“If we are thrown into the blazing furnace, the God we serve is able to deliver us.” 2
“But even if he does not... we will not worship the image of gold.” 3

The Fourth Man

“Look! I see four men walking around in the fire, and the fourth looks like a son of the gods.” 4

No Smell of Fire

“There was no smell of fire on them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sixty Cubits (am-mīn shit-tīn): The scrolls highlight the "Gigantic Root of Pride." "Human Scale" is "Uprooted." The "Image" (*Tselem*) is "Rooted" in "Gold" (*Dehabh*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Totalitarian Worship."

2 Is Able to Deliver (i-thai E-lā-ha-nā... ye-shē-zi-bhin-nā): The scrolls highlight the "Competent Root of Deity." "Danger" is "Uprooted." God is "Rooted" in the "Rescue" (*Shezeb*). It signify "The Sovereignty of God over Natural Elements."

3 Even if He does Not (we-hēn lā): The scrolls highlight the "Unconditional Root of Faith." "Safety" is "Uprooted." "Integrity" is "Rooted" in "Devotion" regardless of the outcome. It signifies "The Ultimate Standard of Religious Freedom."

4 Son of the Gods (bar e-lā-hīn): Literally "A son of Elohin." The scrolls highlight the "Theophanic Root of Protection." "Isolation" is "Uprooted." The "Fourth" is "Rooted" in the "Flame." It signify "The Presence of the Divine in the Crisis."

5 No Smell of Fire (wē-rē-ach nūr lā a-dhāth): The scrolls conclude with this "Total Root of Deliverance." "Damage" is "Uprooted." Not even the "Memory" of the "Fire" (*Smell*) is "Rooted" in them. It marks the transition to "The King’s Madness" in *Daniel 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Fourth Man." You are free.
