Malachi 1

The Burden of Love and the Defiled Table.

I Have Loved You

“I have loved you,” says the Lord. 1
“But you ask, ‘How have you loved us?’”

A Great Name

“My name will be great among the nations, from the rising of the sun to its setting.” 2

The Blind and the Lame

“When you offer blind animals for sacrifice, is that not wrong?” 3
“And you say, ‘What a burden!’ and you sniff at it contemptuously.” 4

Translation Notes

1 I have loved you (ā-habh-tī eth-khem): The scrolls highlight the "Primordial Root of the Affection." "Ambivalence" is "Uprooted." The "Love" (*Ahabti*) is "Rooted" in the "Initial Choice." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Sovereign Heart."

2 Great among the Nations (gā-dōl bag-gō-yīm): The scrolls highlight the "Expansive Root of the Glory." "Parochialism" is "Uprooted." The "Name" is "Rooted" in "Universality." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Global Honor."

3 Blind (iw-wēr): The scrolls highlight the "Deficient Root of the Ritual." "Excellence" is "Uprooted." The "Sacrifice" is "Rooted" in "Neglect." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Casual Offering."

4 Sniff (he-phach-tem): Literally "You puffed at it." The scrolls highlight the "Disdainful Root of the Boredom." "Awe" is "Uprooted." The "Service" is "Rooted" in "Fatigue" (*Matlaah*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Contemptuous Breath."

5 Great King (me-lekh gā-dōl): The scrolls conclude with this "Majestic Root of the Authority." "Casualness" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Messenger of the Covenant" in *Malachi 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Burden of Love." He is the Great King. Don't sniff at the altar.
