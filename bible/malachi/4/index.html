Malachi 4

The Rising Sun and the Final Prophet.

The Day is Coming

“Surely the day is coming; it will burn like a furnace.” 1

Sun of Righteousness

“The sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its rays.” 2
“And you will go out and leap like calves released from the stall.” 3

The Return of Elijah

“See, I will send the prophet Elijah to you before that great and dreadful day of the Lord comes.” 4
“He will turn the hearts of the parents to their children, and the hearts of the children to their parents.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Burn like a furnace (bō-ēr kat-tan-nūr): The scrolls highlight the "Incendiary Root of the Judgment." "Arrogance" is "Uprooted." The "Day" is "Rooted" in "The Fire of Truth." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Absolute Consumption."

2 Healing in its rays (mar-pē bik-na-phey-hā): Literally "Healing in its wings." The scrolls highlight the "Solar Root of the Restoration." "Disease" is "Uprooted." The "Sun" (*Shemesh*) is "Rooted" in "Righteousness" (*Tsedaqah*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Radiant Wholeness."

3 Leap (pū-she-tem): The scrolls highlight the "Exuberant Root of the Freedom." "Confinement" is "Uprooted." The "People" are "Rooted" in "Joy." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Unbounded Vitality."

4 Elijah (ē-li-yāhū): The scrolls highlight the "Prophetic Root of the Forerunner." "Silence" is "Uprooted." The "Herald" is "Rooted" in "The Ancient Spirit." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Warning."

5 Turn the Hearts (he-shībh lēbh): The scrolls conclude the Old Testament with this "Reconciliatory Root of the Generation." "Estrangement" is "Uprooted." The "Family" is "Rooted" in "Love." It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Turning of Hearts." The Sun is Rising.
