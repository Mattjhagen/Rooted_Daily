The Book of Malachi

Chapter 2

"The Covenant Corrupted"
1

And now, O ye priests, this commandment is for you.

2

If ye will not hear, and if ye will not lay it to heart, to give glory unto my name, saith the LORD of hosts, I will even send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings: yea, I have cursed them already, because ye do not lay it to heart.

3

And ye shall know that I have sent this commandment unto you, that my covenant might be with Levi, saith the LORD of hosts.

4

My covenant was with him of life and peace; and I gave them to him for the fear wherewith he feared me, and was afraid before my name.

5

For the priest's lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth: for he is the messenger of the LORD of hosts.

6

Have we not all one father? hath not one God created us? why do we deal treacherously every man against his brother, by profaning the covenant of our fathers?

✨ Rooted Insight

The Lord admonishes the priests for violating the covenant of Levi and the people for treachery against their wives.

Translation Notes

The Bagad Root (Deal Treacherously): The tearing apart of covenants—both with God and within marriages—uproots the community from life and peace.
