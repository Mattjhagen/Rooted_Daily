Malachi 2

The Covenant of Life and the Treachery of the Heart.

Covenant of Life

“My covenant was with him, a covenant of life and peace.” 1

The Wife of your Youth

“Do not be unfaithful to the wife of your youth.” 2
“I hate divorce,” says the Lord. 3

Wearying the Lord

“You have wearied the Lord with your words.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Covenant of Life (be-rī-thī hay-thāh it-tō ha-chay-yīm): The scrolls highlight the "Vital Root of the Priesthood." "Death" is "Uprooted." The "Covenant" (*Berith*) is "Rooted" in "Life" (*Chayyim*) and "Peace" (*Shalom*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Living Service."

2 Wife of your Youth (ē-sheth ne-ū-re-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Primordial Root of the Fidelity." "Novelty" is "Uprooted." The "Relationship" is "Rooted" in "Youth" (*Neurim*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Original Bond."

3 I Hate Divorce (śā-nē shal-lach): Literally "He hates sending away." The scrolls highlight the "Severing Root of the Pain." "Unity" is "Uprooted." The "God of Israel" is "Rooted" in "Permanent Love." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Indissoluble Covenant."

4 Wearied (hō-ga-tem): The scrolls highlight the "Exhausting Root of the Cynicism." "Patience" is "Uprooted." The "Lord" is "Rooted" in "Truth," while the people say "All who do evil are good." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Moral Fatigue."

5 Messenger of the Covenant (mal-akh hab-be-rīth): The scrolls conclude with this "Heraldic Root of the Refining." "Complacency" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Refiner's Fire" in *Malachi 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Covenant of Life." Keep your heart guarded.
