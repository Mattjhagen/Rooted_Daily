The Book of Habakkuk

Chapter 3

"The Prayer of Faith"
1

A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet upon Shigionoth.

2

O LORD, I have heard thy speech, and was afraid: O LORD, revive thy work in the midst of the years, in the midst of the years make known; in wrath remember mercy.

3

God came from Teman, and the Holy One from mount Paran. Selah. His glory covered the heavens, and the earth was full of his praise.

4

And his brightness was as the light; he had horns coming out of his hand: and there was the hiding of his power.

5

Although the fig tree shall not blossom, neither shall fruit be in the vines; the labour of the olive shall fail, and the fields shall yield no meat; the flock shall be cut off from the fold, and there shall be no herd in the stalls:

6

Yet I will rejoice in the LORD, I will joy in the God of my salvation.

✨ Rooted Insight

Habakkuk offers a prayer of praise, remembering God's past deliverance and choosing to rejoice even in barrenness.

Translation Notes

The Gilah Root (Rejoice): A resilient joy that blossoms even when the visible world is stripped bare, rooted deeply in the God of salvation.
