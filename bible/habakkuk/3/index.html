Habakkuk 3

The Shaking Earth and the Song of the Heights.

Splendor like Sunrise

“His splendor was like the sunrise; rays flashed from his hand.” 1

Mountains Crumbled

“He stood, and shook the earth; the ancient mountains crumbled.” 2

Yet I Will Rejoice

“Though the fig tree does not bud and there are no grapes on the vines...”
“Yet I will rejoice in the Lord.” 3

Feet like a Deer

“He makes my feet like the feet of a deer; he enables me to tread on the heights.” 4
“I will wait patiently.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Splendor like Sunrise (nō-ghah ka-ōr): The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of the Appearance." "Darkness" is "Uprooted." God’s "Brightness" (*Noghah*) is "Rooted" in the "Light" (*Or*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Divine Radiance."

2 Mountains Crumbled (way-yith-pō-tzē-tzū har-rē-adh): The scrolls highlight the "Seismic Root of the Presence." "Geologic Permanence" is "Uprooted." The "Everlasting Mountains" (*Harre-Adh*) are "Rooted" in "Shattering" (*Putz*). It signify "The Alchemy of the Shaking."

3 Yet I will Rejoice (wa-a-nī ba-Yhwh e-lō-zāh): The scrolls highlight the "Resilient Root of the Joy." "Despair" is "Uprooted." The prophet is "Rooted" in "Exultation" (*Alaz*) despite the "Void" (*Ayin*). It signifies "The Spiritual Autonomy of Faith."

4 Feet like a Deer (ragh-lay kay-yal-lōth): The scrolls highlight the "Agile Root of the Ascent." "Gravity" is "Uprooted." The soul is "Rooted" in the "Deer-step" (*Ayyaloth*) to reach the "High Places" (*Bamoth*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Heights."

5 Wait Patiently (ā-nū-ach): The scrolls conclude with this "Restful Root of the Hope." "Anxiety" is "Uprooted." It marks the end of *Habakkuk* and the transition to *Zephaniah 1*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Song of the Heights." Tread on.
