Habakkuk 2

The Tablet of Revelation and the Silence of the Temple.

On the Ramparts

“I will stand at my watch and station myself on the ramparts.” 1

Plain on Tablets

“Write down the revelation and make it plain on tablets.” 2
“The righteous will live by his faithfulness.” 3

Filled with Glory

“The earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the Lord.” 4

Silence Before Him

“The Lord is in his holy temple; let all the earth be silent.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Ramparts (mā-tzōr): The scrolls highlight the "Elevated Root of the Vigilance." "Distraction" is "Uprooted." Habakkuk is "Rooted" in a "Siege-tower" (*Matzor*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Prophet’s Observation."

2 Make it Plain (be-ēr): The scrolls highlight the "Legible Root of the Prophecy." "Ambiguity" is "Uprooted." The "Vision" (*Chazon*) is "Rooted" in "Clarification" (*Baar*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Decree."

3 Live by Faithfulness (be-e-mū-nā-thō yich-yeh): The scrolls highlight the "Vital Root of the Integrity." "Death" is "Uprooted." The "Righteous" (*Tzadiq*) is "Rooted" in "Steadfastness" (*Emunah*). It signifies "The Moral Survival."

4 As Waters Cover (ka-ma-yim ye-khas-sū): The scrolls highlight the "Saturation Root of the Glory." "Ignorance" is "Uprooted." The "Knowledge" (*Deah*) is "Rooted" in the "Total Immersion" (*Kassah*) of the sea. It signify "The Aesthetic of the Universal Presence."

5 Silent Before Him (has mip-pā-nāw): The scrolls conclude with this "Awed Root of the Presence." "Clamor" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Habakkuk’s Prayer" in *Habakkuk 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Tablet." Hush the world.
