Matthew 1

The Roots of the Messiah and the Gift of Immanuel.

The Royal Roots

“The record of the genealogy of Jesus the Messiah, the son of David, the son of Abraham.” 1
Abraham was the father of Isaac...
Judah the father of Perez by Tamar...
Boaz the father of Obed by Rahab...
Jesse the father of King David...
After the exile to Babylon: Shealtiel...
And Jacob the father of Joseph, the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called the Messiah.
“Fourteen generations... fourteen generations... fourteen generations.” 2

God With Us

“She will give birth to a son, and you are to give him the name Jesus, because he will save his people from their sins.” 3
“They will call him Immanuel—which means, ‘God with us.’” 4

Translation Notes

1 Genealogy (ge-ne-sis): In Greek, this is *Biblos Geneseos*. The scrolls highlight the "Genesis Root of the Kingdom." "Chaos" is "Uprooted." The "King" is "Rooted" in the "Old Covenant." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Sovereign Continuity."

2 Fourteen Generations: The scrolls highlight the "Mathematical Root of the Fulfillment." "Randomness" is "Uprooted." The "Timing" is "Rooted" in the "Perfect Gematria (David = 14)." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Divine Precision."

3 Jesus (Iēsous): From the Hebrew *Yeshua* (Yahweh is Salvation). The scrolls highlight the "Soteriological Root of the Name." "Guilt" is "Uprooted." The "Son" is "Rooted" in "Salvation." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Redeeming Presence."

4 Immanuel (Em-ma-nou-ēl): The scrolls highlight the "Incarnational Root of the Mystery." "Distance" is "Uprooted." The "Godhead" is "Rooted" in the "Flesh." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Ultimate Proximity."
