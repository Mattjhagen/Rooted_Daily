Matthew 2

The Star, the Tyrant, and the Branch.

✨
After Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Judea, during the time of King Herod, Magi from the east came to Jerusalem
“Where is the one who has been born king of the Jews? We saw his star when it rose and have come to worship him.” 1
“‘But you, Bethlehem, in the land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers of Judah; for out of you will come a ruler who will shepherd my people Israel.’” 2

The Flight and the Return

“Get up,” he said, “take the child and his mother and escape to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, for Herod is going to search for the child to kill him.”
And so was fulfilled what the Lord had said through the prophet: “Out of Egypt I called my son.” 3
“He will be called a Nazarene.” 4

Translation Notes

1 The Star (as-tēra): The scrolls highlight the "Celestial Root of Authority." "Earthly Tyrants" are "Uprooted." The "King" is "Rooted" in the "Cosmos." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Universal Light."

2 Bethlehem (House of Bread): The scrolls highlight the "Nourishing Root of the Ruler." "Scarcity" is "Uprooted." The "Shepherd" is "Rooted" in the "Little Village." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Humble Origins."

3 Out of Egypt: A reference to Hosea 11:1. The scrolls highlight the "Exodus Root of the Messiah." "Slavery" is "Uprooted." The "Son" is "Rooted" in the "New Liberation." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Sovereign Recall."

4 Nazarene (Na-zō-rai-os): This likely connects to the Hebrew *Netzer* (Branch). The scrolls highlight the "Floral Root of the Messiah." "Desolation" is "Uprooted." The "Branch" is "Rooted" in the "Silent Growth." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Hidden Glory."
