Matthew 4

Tested and Triumphant. The Light in the Darkness.

The Wilderness Test

Then Jesus was led by the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil.
After fasting forty days and forty nights, he was hungry.
“Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every word that comes from the mouth of God.” 1
“The people living in darkness have seen a great light; on those living in the land of the shadow of death a light has dawned.” 2

The Calling of the Fishers

As Jesus was walking beside the Sea of Galilee, he saw two brothers, Simon called Peter and his brother Andrew. They were casting a net into the lake, for they were fishermen.
“Come, follow me, and I will send you out to fish for people.” 3
At once they left their nets and followed him.

Translation Notes

1 Bread vs Word (ar-to... rhe-ma-ti): The scrolls highlight the "Sustaining Root of the Mouth." "Physical Hunger" is "Uprooted." The "Spirit" is "Rooted" in the "Utterance of the Divine." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Spiritual Feast."

2 Light has Dawned (phos a-ne-tei-len): The scrolls highlight the "Illuminating Root of the Dawn." "Shadow" is "Uprooted." The "Soul" is "Rooted" in the "Radiance of the Messiah." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Eternal Sunrise."

3 Follow Me (Deu-te o-pi-so mou): The scrolls highlight the "Responsive Root of the Walk." "Fixed Nets" are "Uprooted." The "Disciple" is "Rooted" in the "Motion of the Teacher." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Relational Journey."
