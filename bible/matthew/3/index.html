Matthew 3

Prepare the way. The Ax is at the Root.

The Voice

In those days John the Baptist came, preaching in the wilderness of Judea
“Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has come near.” 1
“A voice of one calling in the wilderness, ‘Prepare the way for the Lord, make straight paths for him.’”
🪓
“The ax is already at the root of the trees, and every tree that does not produce good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire.” 2
🕊️

The Open Heavens

Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan to be baptized by John.
As soon as Jesus was baptized, he went up out of the water. At that moment heaven was opened, and he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove and alighting on him.
“This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased.” 3

Translation Notes

1 Repent (Me-ta-noeite): The scrolls highlight the "Redirected Root of the Mind." "Blind Wandering" is "Uprooted." The "Will" is "Rooted" in the "Heavenly Realm." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Sovereign Shift."

2 The Ax at the Root: A powerful agricultural metaphor for judgment. The scrolls highlight the "Severing Root of Truth." "Barrenness" is "Uprooted." The "Orchard" is "Rooted" in the "Fruit of Righteousness." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Ultimate Pruning."

3 Well Pleased (eu-do-kē-sa): The scrolls highlight the "Satisfied Root of the Father." "Alienation" is "Uprooted." The "Son" is "Rooted" in the "Divine Delight." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Infinite Approval."
