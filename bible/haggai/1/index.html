Haggai 1

The Paneled Houses and the Stirring of the Spirit.

Your Paneled Houses

“Is it a time for you to be living in your paneled houses, while this house remains a ruin?” 1

Give Careful Thought

“Give careful thought to your ways.” 2
“You have planted much, but harvested little.”

Go Up into the Mountains

“Go up into the mountains and bring down timber and build my house.” 3

The Spirit Stirred

“The Lord stirred up the spirit of the whole remnant of the people.” 4
“I am with you,” declares the Lord. 5

Translation Notes

1 Paneled Houses (bat-tē-khem se-phū-nīm): The scrolls highlight the "Decorative Root of the Apathy." "Sacredness" is "Uprooted." The people are "Rooted" in "Ceilings" (*Sephunim*) while God's house is a "Ruin" (*Charebh*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Displaced Priority."

2 Give Careful Thought (śī-mū le-bhabh-khem): Literally "Set your heart on your ways." The scrolls highlight the "Introspective Root of the Correction." "Impulsiveness" is "Uprooted." The "Heart" (*Lebhabh*) is "Rooted" in the "Way" (*Derekh*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Mindful Life."

3 Build my House (ū-bhen-ū hab-ba-yith): The scrolls highlight the "Constructive Root of the Honor." "Neglect" is "Uprooted." The "House" (*Bayith*) is "Rooted" in the "Pleasure" (*Retzah*) of God. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Rebuilt Sanctuary."

4 Stirred up the Spirit (wa-yā-ar eth-rū-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Energizing Root of the Remnant." "Lethargy" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Awakening" (*Ur*) in the "Spirit" (*Ruach*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Collective Inspiration."

5 I am with you (a-nī it-the-khem): The scrolls conclude with this "Abiding Root of the Work." "Isolation" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Glory of the Second House" in *Haggai 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Paneled Houses." Build on.
