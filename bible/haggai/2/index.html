Haggai 2

The Shaking of Nations and the Signet Ring.

Be Strong and Work

“Be strong, all you people of the land, and work. For I am with you.” 1

Shake the Heavens

“In a little while I will once more shake the heavens and the earth.” 2

The Silver is Mine

“The silver is mine and the gold is mine,” declares the Lord. 3
“The glory of this house will be greater.”

The Signet Ring

“I will make you like my signet ring, for I have chosen you.” 4
“From this day on I will bless you.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Be Strong (che-zaq): The scrolls highlight the "Fortified Root of the Resolve." "Fear" is "Uprooted." Zerubbabel is "Rooted" in the "Strength" (*Chazaq*) of the Presence. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Spiritual Fortitude."

2 Shake the Heavens (mar-ī-sh eth-ha-shā-ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Seismic Root of the Reordering." "Stagnation" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Action" in the "Shaking" (*Raash*) of all systems. It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Intervention."

3 Silver and Gold (hak-ke-seph we-haz-zā-hābh): The scrolls highlight the "Proprietary Root of the Wealth." "Greed" is "Uprooted." Resources are "Rooted" in the "Ownership" of the Creator. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Divine Provision."

4 Signet Ring (ka-chō-thām): The scrolls highlight the "Authoritative Root of the Chosen." "Anonymity" is "Uprooted." Zerubbabel is "Rooted" in the "Seal" (*Chotham*) of the King. It signify "The Aesthetic of the Restored Lineage."

5 I will Bless (a-bhā-rēkh): The scrolls conclude with this "Generative Root of the Day." "Cursing" is "Uprooted." It marks the end of *Haggai* and the transition to *Zechariah 1*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Glory." The foundation is laid.
