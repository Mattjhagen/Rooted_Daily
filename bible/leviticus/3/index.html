Leviticus 3

1“‘If someone’s offering is a fellowship offering, and they offer an animal from the herd, whether male or female, they are to present before the LORD an animal without defect.” 1
2“‘You are to lay your hand on the head of your offering and slaughter it at the entrance to the tent of meeting. Then Aaron’s sons the priests shall splash the blood against the sides of the altar.”
3“‘From the fellowship offering you are to bring a food offering presented to the LORD: the internal organs and all the fat that is connected to them,”
4“‘both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the long lobe of the liver, which you will remove with the kidneys.”
5“‘Then Aaron’s sons shall burn it on the altar on top of the burnt offering that is lying on the burning wood; it is a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.”
6“‘If you offer an animal from the flock as a fellowship offering to the LORD, you are to offer a male or female without defect.”
7“‘If you offer a lamb, you are to present it before the LORD.”
8“‘You are to lay your hand on the head of your offering and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting. Then Aaron’s sons shall splash its blood against the sides of the altar.”
9“‘From the fellowship offering you are to bring a food offering presented to the LORD: its fat, the entire fat tail cut off close to the backbone, the internal organs and all the fat that is connected to them,”
10“‘both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the long lobe of the liver, which you will remove with the kidneys.”
11“‘The priest shall burn them on the altar as a food offering presented to the LORD.”
12“‘If your offering is a goat, you are to present it before the LORD.”
13“‘You are to lay your hand on its head and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting. Then Aaron’s sons shall splash its blood against the sides of the altar.”
14“‘From what you offer you are to present this food offering to the LORD: the internal organs and all the fat that is connected to them,”
15“‘both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the long lobe of the liver, which you will remove with the kidneys.”
16“‘The priest shall burn them on the altar as a food offering, a pleasing aroma. All the fat is the LORD’s.” 2
17“‘This is a lasting ordinance for the generations to come, wherever you live: You must not eat any fat or any blood.’” 3

Translation Notes

1 Fellowship Offering (Shelamim): In the scrolls, this is the *Shelamim*, related to the word *Shalom* (peace). It was a shared meal; after the fat was burned for God and the priest received his portion, the worshiper and their family would eat the rest. It represents communion and celebrated peace between God and man.

2 All the fat is the LORD’s: In the ancient world, the fat (*Helev*) was considered the richest and best part of the animal. By giving the fat to God, the worshiper was acknowledging that the "best" of everything belongs to the Creator. It prevented the offering from becoming a mere barbecue.

3 No Blood: The prohibition of blood in the scrolls is grounded in the belief that "the life is in the blood" (Lev 17:11). Since life belongs only to God, humans are forbidden from consuming it. It was a constant reminder of the sanctity of life and the necessity of atonement.
