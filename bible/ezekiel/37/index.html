Ezekiel 37

The Valley of Dry Bones and the One King.

Can These Bones Live?

“Son of man, can these bones live?” 1

The Rattling Noise

“There was a noise, a rattling sound, and the bones came together.” 2

Come, Breath!

“Come, breath, from the four winds and breathe into these slain.” 3
“Breath entered them; they came to life and stood up—a vast army.” 4

The Joining of the Sticks

“Join them together into one stick so that they will become one in your hand.” 5
“My servant David will be king over them.”

Translation Notes

1 Can these Bones Live (ha-thich-ye-nāh): The scrolls highlight the "Biological Root of Hope." "Death" is "Uprooted" by the "Sovereign Question." It signifies "The Intersection of Human Impossibility and Divine Potential."

2 Rattling Sound (ra-ash): Literally "A quaking" or "Noise." The scrolls highlight the "Mechanical Root of Re-Assembly." "Chaos" is "Uprooted" by "Structure." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Resurrection."

3 Come, Breath (bō-ī ha-rū-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Pneumatic Root of Life." "Stillness" is "Uprooted" by the "Wind/Spirit" (*Ruach*). It signifies "The Dependence of Form upon Breath."

4 A Vast Army (cha-yil gā-dhōl me-ōdh): The scrolls highlight the "Military Root of the Remnant." "Vulnerability" is "Uprooted" by "Strength" (*Chayil*). It signify "The Purpose of Restoration: Service to the King."

5 Join them Together (we-qar-rēbh ō-thām): The scrolls conclude with this "Social Root of Unity." "Division" is "Uprooted." The "Two Sticks" (*Etzim*) become "One" (*Echad*). It marks the transition to "Gog and Magog" in *Ezekiel 38*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Living Army." Live again.
