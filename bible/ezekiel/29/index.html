Ezekiel 29

The Monster of the Nile and the Splintered Reed.

The Great Monster

“I am against you, Pharaoh, you great monster lying among your streams.” 1

The Staff of Reed

“You have been a staff of reed for the people of Israel; when they grasped you, you splintered.” 2

Forty Years of Silence

“No human foot will pass through it... for forty years it will be desolate.” 3

The Lowliest of Kingdoms

“It will be the lowliest of kingdoms and will never again exalt itself.” 4
“I will give Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar as a reward for his efforts.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Great Monster (hat-tan-nīn hag-gā-dhōl): Literally "The great dragon/crocodile." The scrolls highlight the "Mythological Root of Sovereignty." Pharaoh "Roots" his "Identity" in the "Nile" (*Ye'or*). God "Uproots" his "Claim" to the "River." It signifies "The Hubris of the Naturalist."

2 Staff of Reed (mish-e-neth qā-neh): The scrolls highlight the "Fragile Root of Alliance." Israel’s "Trust" is "Uprooted" by the "Reed" (*Qaneh*) that "Splinters." It signify "The Aesthetic of Unreliability."

3 Forty Years (ar-bā-īm shā-nāh): The scrolls highlight the "Punitive Root of Time." "Activity" is "Uprooted" for a "Generation." It signifies "The Sabbath of Desolation."

4 Lowliest of Kingdoms (shiph-lath mam-lā-khōth): The scrolls highlight the "Diminished Root of Status." "Imperial Pride" is "Uprooted." Egypt will be "Rooted" in "Humility" (*Shephelah*). It signify "The Divine Leveling of History."

5 Nebuchadnezzar (Ne-bhū-khadh-retz-tzar): The scrolls conclude with this "Compensatory Root of Conquest." Babylon is "Rooted" in "Egypt" as "Wages" (*Sakar*) for "Tyre." It marks the transition to "The Lament for Egypt" in *Ezekiel 30*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Splintered Reed." Pharaoh is caught.
