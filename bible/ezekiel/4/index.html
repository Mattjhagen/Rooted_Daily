Ezekiel 4

The Clay Tablet and the Defiled Bread.

The Siege Model

“Take a block of clay and draw Jerusalem on it.”
“Place an iron griddle as an iron wall between you and the city.” 1

Lying on the Side

“I have assigned you 390 days... to bear the sin of Israel.” 2
“Then 40 days for the sin of Judah.”

Siege Bread

“Take wheat, barley, beans and lentils...”
“In this way Israel will eat defiled food among the nations.” 3

The Manure Fuel

“Use human excrement for fuel.” 4
“I will let you use cow manure instead.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Iron Griddle (ma-cha-bhath bar-zel): In the scrolls, this is the "Impenetrable Root of Separation." The "Connection" between "God" and the "City" is "Uprooted" by an "Iron Wall." It signifies "The Absolute Blockage of Prayer."

2 390 Days (she-lōsh mē-ōth we-thi-shīm yōm): The scrolls highlight the "Chronological Root of Iniquity." Each "Day" (*Yom*) represents a "Year" of "Sin." Ezekiel is "Rooted" in "Immobility" to "Root" the "Judgment." It signify "The Physical Burden of the Prophet."

3 Defiled Food (ma-a-khā-lām ta-mē): The scrolls highlight the "Ritual Root of the Exile." "Purity" is "Uprooted" as the "Remnant" is forced to "Root" in "Foreign Lands" where the "Food Laws" are impossible. It signifies "The Spiritual Humiliation of the Captive."

4 Human Excrement (be-glē tze-ath ā-dhām): The scrolls highlight the "Polluting Root of the Siege." "Dignity" is "Uprooted." It signify "The Extremity of the Scarcity."

5 Cow Manure (tzi-phi-ē hab-bā-qār): The scrolls conclude with this "Merciful Root of Adjustment." God "Uproots" the "Harshness" in response to Ezekiel’s "Plea." It marks the transition to "The Divided Hair" in *Ezekiel 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Siege acted out." The iron wall stands.
