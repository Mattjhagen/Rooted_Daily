Ezekiel 39

The Cleansing of the Land and the Valley of the Multitude.

The Fallen Bow

“I will strike the bow from your left hand and make the arrows drop from your right hand.” 1

Seven Years of Fuel

“For seven years they will use the weapons for fuel.” 2

Valley of Hamon Gog

“It will be called the Valley of Hamon Gog... and so they will cleanse the land.” 3

Spirit Poured Out

“I will pour out my Spirit on the people of Israel.” 4
“I will no longer hide my face from them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Strike the Bow (we-hik-kē-thī qa-shat-te-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Disarming Root of the Invader." "Aggression" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" the "Defeat" in the "Mountains" (*Harim*). It signifies "The Impotence of Human Weapons before the Divine."

2 Seven Years (she-bha shā-nīm): The scrolls highlight the "Complete Root of Transformation." "Implements of War" are "Uprooted" and replaced by "Fuel" (*Esh*). It signify "The Total Conversion of Violence into Utility."

3 Valley of Hamon Gog (gē ha-mōn Gōgh): Literally "The Valley of Gog’s Multitude." The scrolls highlight the "Cemetery Root of Ambition." "Pride" is "Uprooted" and "Buried" (*Qabhar*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of a Cleansed Land."

4 Pour out my Spirit (shā-phakh-tī eth-rū-chī): The scrolls highlight the "Effusive Root of Renewal." "Distance" is "Uprooted." God’s "Spirit" (*Ruach*) is "Rooted" in the "House of Israel." It signify "The Climax of Restoration."

5 No longer Hide (we-lō as-tīr ōdh): The scrolls conclude with this "Revelatory Root of Presence." "Obscurity" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Vision of the New Temple" in *Ezekiel 40*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Cleansed Nation." My face is revealed.
