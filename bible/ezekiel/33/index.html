Ezekiel 33

The Watchman’s Trumpet and the Fallen City.

Blow the Trumpet

“If the watchman sees the sword coming and blows the trumpet to warn the people...” 1

Turn and Live

“Turn! Turn from your evil ways! Why will you die, people of Israel?” 2

The City has Fallen!

“A man who had escaped from Jerusalem came to me and said, ‘The city has fallen!’” 3
“My mouth was opened and I was no longer silent.” 4

The Beautiful Voice

“To them you are nothing more than one who sings love songs with a beautiful voice.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Blow the Trumpet (tā-qa wash-shō-phār): In the scrolls, this is the "Acoustic Root of Warning." "Safety" is "Uprooted" by the "Blast" (*Shophar*). It signifies "The Prophet as the Alarm of the Nation."

2 Turn! Turn! (shū-bhū shū-bhū): The scrolls highlight the "Recursive Root of Repentance." "Obstinacy" is "Uprooted" through "Return" (*Shubh*). It signify "The Urgency of the Divine Plea."

3 The City has Fallen (hūk-kā-thāh hā-īr): Literally "The city is struck." The scrolls highlight the "Terminal Root of the Siege." "Continuity" is "Uprooted." It signify "The Realization of the Prophetic Word."

4 No longer Silent (we-lō ne-e-lam-tī ōdh): The scrolls highlight the "Vocal Root of Release." "Speechlessness" is "Uprooted" by the "Fulfillment" of the vision. It signifies "The Re-Authorization of the Prophet’s Voice."

5 Beautiful Voice (ye-phēh qōl): The scrolls conclude with this "Aesthetic Root of Entertainment." The "Message" is "Uprooted" and treated as "Performance" (*Love Song*). It marks the transition to "The Shepherds of Israel" in *Ezekiel 34*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Watchman." Listen and obey.
