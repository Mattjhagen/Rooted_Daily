Ezekiel 24

The Day of the Siege and the Delight of the Eyes.

The Date of Doom

“Write down today’s date, for on this very day the king of Babylon has laid siege to Jerusalem.” 1

The Pot of Scum

“Woe to the city of bloodshed, to the pot whose scum is inside it!” 2
“Set the empty pot on the coals so its impurities may be melted.”

Delight of the Eyes

“I am about to take away from you the delight of your eyes.” 3
“In the evening my wife died.” 4

Ezekiel as a Sign

“Ezekiel will be a sign to you; you will do just as he has done.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Write down today’s Date (kethōbh-le-khā eth-shēm hay-yōm): The scrolls highlight the "Chronological Root of the Siege." "Time" is "Uprooted" from the ordinary. The "Prophet" must "Record" (*Kethobh*) the exact "Name of the Day." It signifies "The Intersection of Prophecy and History."

2 Scum (chel-ā-thāh): The scrolls highlight the "Impurified Root of the City." "Sanctity" is "Uprooted." The "Scum" (*Chelah*) is "Rooted" in the "Metal." It signify "The Total Corruption of Jerusalem."

3 Delight of your Eyes (mach-madh ē-ney-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Affectionate Root of Loss." "Personal Joy" is "Uprooted" (*Take away*). It signify "The Cost of the Prophetic Sign."

4 My Wife Died (wat-tā-math ish-tī): The scrolls highlight the "Domestic Root of Grief." Ezekiel’s "Household" is "Uprooted." His "Silence" (*Do not weep*) is "Rooted" in the "Sign" for the nation. It signifies "The Ultimate Sacrifice for the Message."

5 Ezekiel will be a Sign (ha-yā Ye-chez-qēl lā-khem le-mō-phēth): The scrolls conclude with this "Representative Root of the Prophet." Ezekiel’s "Life" is "Uprooted" to become a "Sign" (*Mopheth*). It marks the transition to "Oracles against the Nations" in *Ezekiel 25*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Silent Grief." Do just as he has done.
