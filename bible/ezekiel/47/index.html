Ezekiel 47

The River from the Threshold and the Healing of the Sea.

Water from the Temple

“I saw water coming out from under the threshold of the temple toward the east.” 1

Increasing Depth

“The water was ankle-deep... knee-deep... waist-deep.”
“It was a river that I could not cross.” 2

Healing the Waters

“When it empties into the sea, the salty water there becomes fresh.” 3

Leaves for Healing

“Their fruit will serve for food and their leaves for healing.” 4
“Because the water for them flows from the sanctuary.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Under the Threshold (mi-ta-chath miphtan hab-bā-yith): The scrolls highlight the "Subterranean Root of Life." "Aridity" is "Uprooted." The "Water" (*Mayim*) is "Rooted" in the "Foundation" of the "House." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Internal Overflow."

2 Could not Cross (lō ū-khal la-a-bhōr): The scrolls highlight the "Overwhelming Root of Grace." "Human Control" is "Uprooted" by the "Depth." It signify "The Progression from Human Effort to Divine Immersion."

3 Salty water becomes Fresh (we-nir-pū ham-mā-yim): Literally "The waters were healed." The scrolls highlight the "Therapeutic Root of the Arabah." "Bitterness" is "Uprooted." The "Dead Sea" is "Re-Rooted" in "Life." It signifies "The Transformation of the Impossible."

4 Leaves for Healing (we-a-lē-hū lith-rū-phāh): The scrolls highlight the "Botanical Root of Medicine." "Disease" is "Uprooted." The "Trees" (*Etzim*) are "Rooted" in the "Sanctuary Water." It signify "The Total Provision of the Kingdom."

5 Flows from the Sanctuary (min-ham-miq-dāsh): The scrolls conclude with this "Source-Root of Vitality." "Vitality" is "Uprooted" from the "World" and "Rooted" in the "Sanctuary" (*Miqdash*). It marks the transition to "The Division of the Land" in *Ezekiel 48*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The River of Life." Drink and be healed.
