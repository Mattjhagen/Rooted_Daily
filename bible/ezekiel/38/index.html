Ezekiel 38

The Chief Prince and the Unwalled Villages.

Gog of Magog

“I am against you, Gog, chief prince of Meshek and Tubal.” 1

Unwalled Villages

“I will invade a land of unwalled villages; I will attack a peaceful people.” 2

Hooks in the Jaws

“I will turn you around and put hooks in your jaws.” 3

The Great Earthquake

“On that day a great earthquake will rock the land.”
“I will pour down hailstones and burning sulfur.” 4
“They will know that I am the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Chief Prince (ne-śī rōsh): The scrolls highlight the "Terminal Root of Autocracy." "Gog" is "Uprooted" from his "Northern Seat." It signifies "The Final Challenge to Divine Sovereignty."

2 Unwalled Villages (e-retz pe-rā-zōth): The scrolls highlight the "Vulnerable Root of the Restored." "Fortifications" are "Uprooted." The "People" are "Rooted" in "Trust" (*Betach*). It signify "The Aesthetic of Defenseless Faith."

3 Hooks in your Jaws (wa-nā-tha-tī cha-chīm bi-le-chā-ye-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Coercive Root of Judgment." Gog’s "Free Will" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Movement" in a "Divine Hook" (*Chachim*). It signifies "The Control of History’s Villains."

4 Burning Sulfur (we-ghoph-rīth): The scrolls highlight the "Sodom-like Root of Destruction." "Earthly Power" is "Uprooted" by "Celestial Fire." It signify "The Severity of the Final Confrontation."

5 Know that I am the Lord (we-yā-dhe-ū kī a-nī Yah-weh): The scrolls conclude with this "Epistemic Root of Revelation." "Human Pride" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Burial of Gog" in *Ezekiel 39*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unwalled Land." Every knee shall bow.
