Ezekiel 10

The Burning Coals and the Whirling Wheels.

Burning Coals

“Fill your hands with burning coals from between the cherubim and scatter them over the city.” 1

Full of Eyes

“Their entire bodies, their hands and their wings, were completely full of eyes.” 2
“I heard the wheels being called ‘the whirling wheels.’” 3

The Glory Departs

“The glory of the Lord departed from over the threshold and stopped at the east gate.” 4

The Cloud of Brightness

“The temple was filled with the cloud, and the court was full of the radiance of the glory.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Burning Coals (ga-cha-lē ēsh): In the scrolls, this is the "Incendiary Root of Sanctification/Judgment." The "Fire" (*Esh*) from the "Presence" is "Uprooted" to "Root" the "Destruction" of the "City." It signifies "The Kinetic Nature of Holiness."

2 Full of Eyes (me-le-īm ē-na-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Omniscient Root of the Celestial Host." "Privacy" is "Uprooted" before the "Living Creatures." It signify "The Aesthetic of Total Awareness."

3 Whirling Wheels (hag-gal-gal): Literally "The Rolling" or "The Whirler." The scrolls highlight the "Rotational Root of Providence." "Stagnation" is "Uprooted" by the "Dynamic Spirit." It signifies "The Persistent Movement of the Chariot."

4 East Gate (pe-thach sha-ar... hak-qadh-mō-nī): The scrolls highlight the "Exit Root of the Presence." The "Glory" (*Kabod*) is "Uprooting" from the "Sanctum" to "Root" at the "Edge" (*East*). It signify "The Progressive Abandonment of the Defiled House."

5 The Radiance (eth-nō-ghah): The scrolls conclude with this "Photonic Root of Majesty." Despite the "Departure," the "Brightness" (*Nogah*) remains "Rooted" in the "Court" for a moment. It marks the transition to "The Judgment of the Leaders" in *Ezekiel 11*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Whirling Wheels." The Glory is leaving.
