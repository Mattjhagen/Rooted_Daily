Ezekiel 34

The False Shepherds and the One True David.

Shepherds of Self

“Woe to the shepherds of Israel who only take care of yourselves!” 1

I Myself will Search

“I myself will search for my sheep and look after them.” 2

One Shepherd David

“I will place over them one shepherd, my servant David.” 3

Showers of Blessing

“I will send down showers in season.”
“There will be showers of blessing.” 4
“They will live in safety.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Shepherds (rō-īm): In the scrolls, this is the "Pastoral Root of Leadership." "Service" is "Uprooted" by "Self-Interest." The "Shepherds" (*Ro'im*) feed "Themselves" (*Otam*) instead of the "Flock." It signifies "The Moral Failure of Governance."

2 I Myself will Search (hi-ne-nī ā-nī... we-dha-rash-tī): The scrolls highlight the "Diligent Root of Restoration." "Neglect" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" His "Identity" (*Anī*) in the "Search" (*Darash*) for the "Lost." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Shepherd."

3 One Shepherd David (rō-eh e-chādh... Dā-wīdh): The scrolls highlight the "Unifying Root of Royalty." "Fragmentation" is "Uprooted." The "True King" is "Rooted" in the "Line of David." It signifies "The Messianic Fulfillment of the Office."

4 Showers of Blessing (gish-mē bherā-khāh): The scrolls highlight the "Hydraulic Root of Grace." "Drought" is "Uprooted" by "Rain" (*Geshem*) of "Blessing." It signify "The Abundance of the New Covenant."

5 Safety (la-bhe-tach): The scrolls conclude with this "Secure Root of the Dwelling." "Fear" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Oracle against Edom" in *Ezekiel 35*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Good Shepherd." You are my flock.
