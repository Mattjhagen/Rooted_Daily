Ezekiel 36

The Restoration of the Land and the New Heart.

Mountains of Israel

“You, mountains of Israel, will produce branches and fruit for my people.” 1

For the Sake of My Name

“It is not for your sake that I am going to do these things, but for my holy name.” 2

Heart of Flesh

“I will sprinkle clean water on you.”
“I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit in you.” 3
“I will put my Spirit in you.” 4

Like the Garden of Eden

“This land that was laid waste has become like the garden of Eden.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Mountains of Israel (ha-rē Yis-rā-ēl): The scrolls highlight the "Geographic Root of Restoration." "Desolation" is "Uprooted." The "Land" is addressed as a "Living Participant" in the "Covenant." It signifies "The Re-Rooting of the Nation."

2 Sake of My Name (le-ma-an shēm qodh-shī): The scrolls highlight the "Theocentric Root of Grace." "Human Merit" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" His "Renewal" in His own "Reputation" (*Shem*). It signify "The Aesthetic of Divine Sovereignty."

3 New Heart (lēbh chā-dhāsh): The scrolls highlight the "Organic Root of Change." The "Heart of Stone" (*Petrified Root*) is "Uprooted" and replaced by a "Heart of Flesh" (*Responsive Root*). It signifies "The Miracle of Regeneration."

4 Put My Spirit (we-eth-rū-chī et-tēn): The scrolls highlight the "Pneumatic Root of Obedience." God’s own "Breath" (*Ruach*) is "Rooted" within the "Human Frame." It signify "The Source of True Compliance."

5 Garden of Eden (ke-ghan-ē-dhen): The scrolls conclude with this "Primeval Root of Restoration." "Ruin" is "Uprooted." The "Waste Land" is "Re-Rooted" in the "Original Beauty" (*Eden*). It marks the transition to "The Valley of Dry Bones" in *Ezekiel 37*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The New Spirit." Live again.
