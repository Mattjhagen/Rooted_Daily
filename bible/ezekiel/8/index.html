Ezekiel 8

The Secret Chambers and the Weeping for Tammuz.

Transported by the Spirit

“The Spirit lifted me up between earth and heaven and took me to Jerusalem.” 1

Dig Through the Wall

“I saw every sort of crawling thing and detestable animal painted on the wall.”
“What the elders of Israel are doing in the darkness, each at the shrine of his own idol.” 2

Weeping for Tammuz

“I saw women sitting there, mourning the god Tammuz.” 3

Worshiping the Sun

“With their backs toward the temple... they were bowing down to the sun.” 4
“They are putting the branch to their nose.” 5

Translation Notes

1 The Spirit lifted me up (wa-tis-śā ō-thī rū-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Pneumatic Root of Transit." Ezekiel is "Uprooted" from "Babylon" and "Rooted" temporarily in the "Temple" (*Beyth*). It signifies "The Prophet as a Witness to Secret Sin."

2 Shrine of his own Idol (be-cha-dher-rē maś-ki-thō): Literally "In the chambers of his imagery." The scrolls highlight the "Internalized Root of Idolatry." "Public Worship" is "Uprooted" by "Secret Devotion" (*Maskith*) to false gods. It signify "The Corruption of the Imagination."

3 Mourning the god Tammuz (me-bhak-kōth eth-hat-Tam-mūz): The scrolls highlight the "Seasonal Root of Paganism." Tammuz was the god of vegetation whose "Death" was "Rooted" in the summer. It signifies "The Infiltration of Foreign Myths."

4 Backs toward the Temple (a-chō-rē-hem el-hē-khal): The scrolls highlight the "Apostate Root of Posture." "Reverence" is "Uprooted." Turning the back is the ultimate sign of "Rejection." It signify "The Preference for Creation over the Creator."

5 Branch to their Nose (haz-ze-mō-rāh el-ap-pām): The scrolls conclude with this "Enigmatic Root of Insult." It likely refers to a ritual that is "Uprooted" from its "Meaning" and becomes a "Stench" to God. It marks the transition to "The Marking of the Faithful" in *Ezekiel 9*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Secret Chambers." Look at them.
