Ezekiel 44

The Shut Gate and the Faithful Zadokites.

The Shut Gate

“This gate is to remain shut. It must not be opened, for the Lord has entered through it.” 1

Uncircumcised in Heart

“No foreigner uncircumcised in heart and flesh is to enter my sanctuary.” 2

The Faithful Zadokites

“The Zadokites who faithfully carried out the duties... are to come near to minister.” 3

The Linen Garments

“They are to wear linen turbans and linen undergarments; they must not wear anything that makes them sweat.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Remain Shut (sā-ghūr yih-yeh): The scrolls highlight the "Exclusivity Root of the Presence." The "Public Path" is "Uprooted." The "Gate" (*Sha'ar*) is "Rooted" in the "Permanent Exit of the Glory." It signifies "The Sacred One-Way Threshold."

2 Uncircumcised in Heart (ar-lē lēbh): The scrolls highlight the "Internal Root of Identity." "Physical Ritual" is "Uprooted" without "Spiritual Intent." It signify "The Aesthetic of Sincerity."

3 Faithfully carried out (shā-me-rū eth-mish-me-reth): The scrolls highlight the "Consistent Root of Loyalty." The "Infidelity" of others is "Uprooted" by the "Zadokite Watch" (*Mishmereth*). It signifies "The Reward of Endurance."

4 Not make them Sweat (lō ya-ghe-rū bay-yā-ze-ā): Literally "Not gird themselves with sweat." The scrolls highlight the "Coolness Root of Service." "Human Effort/Struggle" (*Sweat*) is "Uprooted" in the "Presence." They wear "Linen" (*Pishtim*) to remain "Rooted" in "Divine Ease." It marks the transition to "The Sacred District" in *Ezekiel 45*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Faithful Servant." Minister without sweat.
