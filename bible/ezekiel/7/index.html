Ezekiel 7

The End has Come and the Day of Doom.

The End!

“The end! The end has come upon the four corners of the land!” 1

Doom has Burst Forth

“The day is here! It has come! Doom has burst forth.” 2

Worthless Wealth

“They will throw their silver into the streets... it will not be able to save them.” 3

Prepare Chains

“Prepare chains! For the land is full of bloodshed.” 4
“Disaster upon disaster... the teaching of the law will be lost.” 5

Translation Notes

1 The End (qētz): In the scrolls, this is the "Terminal Root of History." "Continuity" is "Uprooted." The "End" (*Qetz*) is "Rooted" in the "Four Corners" (*Arba Kanphot*). It signifies "The Total Exhaustion of Patience."

2 Doom has Burst Forth (yā-tzāh hatz-tze-phī-rāh): The scrolls highlight the "Explosive Root of Judgment." "Peace" is "Uprooted." The "Morning/Doom" (*Tzephirah*) "Roots" itself in the "Day" (*Yom*). It signify "The Immediacy of the Crisis."

3 Throw their Silver (kas-pām ba-chū-tzōth): The scrolls highlight the "Worthless Root of Mammon." "Financial Security" is "Uprooted" as "Money" becomes a "Pollution" (*Niddah*). It signifies "The Collapse of Materialism."

4 Prepare Chains (a-śēh har-rat-tōq): The scrolls highlight the "Binding Root of Captivity." "Freedom" is "Uprooted" by "Chains" (*Rattuq*). It signify "The Inevitable Exile."

5 Teaching will be Lost (tō-rāh tō-bhadh): The scrolls conclude with this "Spiritual Root of Silence." "Instruction" (*Torah*) is "Uprooted" from the "Priest" (*Kohen*). It marks the transition to "The Idolatry in the Temple" in *Ezekiel 8*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Day of Doom." The end is here.
