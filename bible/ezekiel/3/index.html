Ezekiel 3

The Honey of the Word and the Flint of the Forehead.

Sweet as Honey

“So I ate it, and it tasted as sweet as honey in my mouth.” 1

Harder than Flint

“I will make your forehead like the hardest stone, harder than flint.” 2

The Watchman

“Son of man, I have made you a watchman for the people of Israel.” 3
“Hear the word I speak and give them warning from me.”

Accountable for Blood

“I will hold you accountable for their blood.” 4
“But if you do warn them... you will have saved yourself.”

Translation Notes

1 Sweet as Honey (ki-dhe-bhāsh le-mā-thōq): The scrolls highlight the "Palatable Root of the Mandate." The "Words of Woe" are "Rooted" in "Sweetness" when "Accepted." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Obedience."

2 Harder than Flint (ke-shā-mīr): Literally "Like diamond" or "emery." The scrolls highlight the "Mineral Root of Resilience." Ezekiel’s "Vulnerability" is "Uprooted" and his "Forehead" (*Metzach*) is "Rooted" in "Incorruptible Hardness." It signifies "The Divine Defense against Stubbornness."

3 Watchman (tzō-pheh): The scrolls highlight the "Vigilant Root of the Prophet." Ezekiel is "Rooted" as the "Lookout" (*Tzopheh*). It signifies "The Communal Responsibility of the Individual."

4 Accountable for Blood (dā-mō mī-yā-dhē-khā): Literally "His blood from your hand." The scrolls highlight the "Legalistic Root of Responsibility." "Indifference" is "Uprooted." It signify "The Weight of the Message."

5 Forehead harder than Flint (me-tzach): The scrolls conclude with this "Anatomical Root of Resolve." It marks the transition to "The Acts of the Siege" in *Ezekiel 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Watchman’s Warning." Hear the Word.
