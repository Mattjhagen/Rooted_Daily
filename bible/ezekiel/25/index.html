Ezekiel 25

The Vengeance against the Neighboring Nations.

Ammon’s Malice

“Because you said, ‘Aha!’ over my sanctuary when it was profaned.” 1

Moab’s Mockery

“Because Moab said, ‘Look, Judah has become like all the other nations.’” 2

Edom’s Revenge

“Because Edom took revenge on Judah and became very guilty.” 3

Great Vengeance

“I will execute great vengeance on them and punish them in my wrath.” 4
“Then they will know that I am the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Aha! (He-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Schadenfreude Root of the Nations." "Compassion" is "Uprooted" and replaced by "Exultation" (*Heach*) over the "Fall" of the "Sanctuary." It signifies "The Spiritual Envy of the Neighbor."

2 Like all the other Nations (ke-khol-hag-gō-yīm): The scrolls highlight the "Commonplace Root of Devaluation." Judah’s "Election" is "Uprooted" in the "Eyes of Moab." It signify "The Denial of Divine Distinction."

3 Took Revenge (ba-a-śōth ne-qā-māh): The scrolls highlight the "Vindictive Root of Edom." "Brotherly Love" is "Uprooted" by "Vengeance" (*Neqamah*). It signifies "The Genetic Bitterness of Esau."

4 Great Vengeance (ne-qā-mōth ge-dhō-lōth): The scrolls highlight the "Reciprocal Root of Justice." "Malice" is "Uprooted" by "Great Vengeance." It signify "The Divine Response to Cruelty."

5 Know that I am the Lord (we-yā-dhe-ū kī a-nī Yah-weh): The scrolls conclude with this "Epistemic Root of Judgment." "Ignorance" is "Uprooted" through "Punishment." It marks the transition to "The Oracle against Tyre" in *Ezekiel 26*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Vengeful God." They will know.
