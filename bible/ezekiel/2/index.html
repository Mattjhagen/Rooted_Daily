Ezekiel 2

The Rebellious Nation and the Scroll of Lament.

Stand Up

“Son of man, stand up on your feet.”
“The Spirit came into me and raised me to my feet.” 1

Obstinate and Stubborn

“I am sending you to a rebellious nation.” 2
“Do not be afraid, though briers and thorns are all around you.” 3

The Scroll of Woe

“I saw a hand stretched out to me. In it was a scroll.”
“Written on both sides were words of lament and mourning and woe.” 4

Eat the Word

“Open your mouth and eat what I give you.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Spirit came into me (wa-thā-bhō bī rū-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Pneumatic Root of Posture." Ezekiel was "Uprooted" by "Awe" (*fell facedown*), but the "Spirit" (*Ruach*) "Roots" him on his "Feet" (*Raglayim*). It signifies "The Divine Empowerment for Duty."

2 Rebellious Nation (gō-yīm ham-mō-re-dhīm): The scrolls highlight the "Mutinous Root of the People." "Submission" is "Uprooted" and "Rebellion" (*Mored*) is "Rooted" in the "National Character." It signify "The Difficulty of the Task."

3 Briers and Thorns (śā-rā-bhīm we-sal-lō-nīm): The scrolls highlight the "Prickly Root of Opposition." Ezekiel is "Rooted" among "Scorpions." It signifies "The Hostile Environment of the Prophet."

4 Lament and Mourning (qī-nāh wā-he-ghāh): The scrolls highlight the "Literary Root of Judgment." The "Scroll" (*Meghillah*) is "Rooted" in "Sorrow." It signify "The Heavy Content of the Message."

5 Eat what I give you (e-khōl eth a-sher a-nī nō-thēn): The scrolls conclude with this "Digestive Root of Revelation." The "Word" must be "Uprooted" from the "Scroll" and "Rooted" in the "Internal Man." It marks the transition to "Eating the Scroll" in *Ezekiel 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Internalized Word." Stand and eat.
