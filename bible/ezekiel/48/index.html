Ezekiel 48

The Gates of the City and the Name of the Lord.

The Tribal Inheritance

“These are the tribes... Dan... Asher... Naphtali...”
“This is the land you are to allot as an inheritance.” 1

The Twelve Gates

“The gates of the city will be named after the tribes of Israel.” 2

The Lord is There

“And the name of the city from that time on will be: THE LORD IS THERE.” 3
“JEHOVAH SHAMMAH.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Allot as an Inheritance (ta-phī-lū be-na-cha-lāh): The scrolls highlight the "Distributive Root of the Future." "Exile" is "Uprooted." The "Land" is "Rooted" in the "Names" of the "Tribes." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Restored Sovereignty."

2 Named after the Tribes (al-she-mōth shibh-tē Yis-rā-ēl): The scrolls highlight the "Civic Root of Belonging." "Anonymity" is "Uprooted." Each "Gate" (*Sha'ar*) is "Rooted" in a "Brother" (*Tribe*). It signify "The City of Unified Families."

3 The Lord is There (Yah-weh shām-māh): This is the climax of Ezekiel. The scrolls highlight the "Final Root of Presence." "Separation" is "Uprooted." The "City" is "Rooted" in "Sham" (*There*). It signifies "The Ultimate Fulfillment of the Promise: I will be with you."

4 Jehovah Shammah: The scrolls conclude with this "Eternal Root of the Name." It marks the end of *Ezekiel* and the transition to *Daniel*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Eternal City." The Lord has found His home. He is there.
