Ezekiel 6

The Prophecy to the Mountains and the Smashed Altars.

Mountains of Israel

“Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Sovereign Lord.” 1

Smashed Altars

“I will destroy your high places. Your altars will be demolished.” 2
“I will lay the dead bodies of the Israelites in front of their idols.”

Remembering in Exile

“There among the nations... they will remember me.” 3
“They will loathe themselves for the evil they have done.” 4

Clap and Stamp

“Clap your hands and stamp your feet and cry out ‘Alas!’” 5

Translation Notes

1 Mountains of Israel (ha-rē Yis-rā-ēl): The scrolls highlight the "Geographic Root of Prophecy." The "Land" itself is addressed. "Security" is "Uprooted" from the "High Peaks." It signifies "The Spiritual Desolation of the Topography."

2 High Places (bā-mō-thē-khem): The scrolls highlight the "Elevated Root of Idolatry." False "Worship" is "Uprooted" by "Demolition" (*Harash*). It signify "The Cleansing of the Land."

3 They will Remember Me (we-zā-khe-rū ō-thī): The scrolls highlight the "Mnemic Root of Restoration." "Amnesia" is "Uprooted" in "Exile." They "Root" their "Identity" in "God’s Memory." It signifies "The Spiritual Awakening of the Captive."

4 Loathe Themselves (we-nā-qō-tū): Literally "They will feel a loathing/nausea." The scrolls highlight the "Visceral Root of Repentance." Their "Pride" is "Uprooted" by "Self-Abhorrence." It signify "The Psychological Cost of Sin."

5 Clap and Stamp (hak-kēh bhe-khap-pe-khā): The scrolls conclude with this "Physical Root of Emotion." The prophet "Roots" his "Message" in "Bodily Gestures." It marks the transition to "The End of the Nation" in *Ezekiel 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Mountains." Hear the word.
