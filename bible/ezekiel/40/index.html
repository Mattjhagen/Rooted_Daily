Ezekiel 40

The Vision of the High Mountain and the Measuring Rod.

The High Mountain

“He took me to the land of Israel and set me on a very high mountain.” 1

The Man of Bronze

“I saw a man whose appearance was like bronze, with a measuring rod in his hand.” 2

The Sacred Dimensions

“The rod was six long cubits... he measured the wall.”
“He went to the east gate... he climbed its steps.”
“The alcoves... the jambs... the portico.”
“He measured the portico of the temple...”

Tell the House of Israel

“Tell the house of Israel everything you see.” 3

Translation Notes

1 High Mountain (har ga-bhō-ah me-ōdh): The scrolls highlight the "Elevated Root of Perspective." Ezekiel is "Uprooted" from the "Plain" of Exile and "Rooted" in the "Presence" of the "Mountain." It signifies "The Spiritual Altitude required for Architectural Prophecy."

2 Appearance like Bronze (mar-ē-hū ke-mar-ēh ne-chō-sheth): The scrolls highlight the "Eternal Root of the Architect." "Fragility" is "Uprooted." The "Man" is "Rooted" in "Refined Metal" (*Bronze*). It signify "The Strength and Brilliance of the Divine Surveyor."

3 Tell the House of Israel (hag-gēdh le-bhēth Yis-rā-ēl): The scrolls highlight the "Communicative Root of the Vision." "Secrecy" is "Uprooted." The "Measurements" must be "Rooted" in the "Memory" of the "Nation." It marks the transition to "The Temple Interior" in *Ezekiel 41*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Measuring Rod." Record every cubit.
