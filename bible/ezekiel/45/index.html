Ezekiel 45

The Sacred Allotment and the Honest Scales.

The Sacred District

“You are to present to the Lord a portion of the land as a sacred district.” 1

Accurate ephah and Bath

“Put away violence and oppression. You are to use accurate scales.” 2
“An accurate ephah and an accurate bath.”

Special Gifts

“All the people will give this special offering to the prince.” 3

Passover Observed

“On the fourteenth day of the first month you are to observe the Passover.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Sacred District (qō-dhesh min-hā-ā-retz): The scrolls highlight the "Zonal Root of Dedication." "Secular Use" is "Uprooted." A portion of the "Land" (*Eretz*) is "Rooted" in "Holiness" (*Qodesh*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Sacred Reserve."

2 Accurate Scales (mōz-ney tze-dheq): Literally "Scales of Righteousness." The scrolls highlight the "Commercial Root of Justice." "Fraud" is "Uprooted." The "Weights" are "Rooted" in "Tzedeq" (*Righteousness*). It signify "The Integrity of the Marketplace."

3 Special Offering (hat-te-rū-māh): The scrolls highlight the "Elevated Root of Contribution." "Selfishness" is "Uprooted." The "Gift" (*Terumah*) is "Heaved/Rooted" toward the "Prince" (*Nasi*) for the people. It signifies "The Responsibility of the Leader."

4 Observe the Passover (hay-yā lā-khem hap-pā-śach): The scrolls conclude with this "Commemorative Root of Deliverance." "Forgetfulness" is "Uprooted." The "Nation" is "Rooted" in the "Passing Over" (*Pasach*). It marks the transition to "The Prince’s Worship" in *Ezekiel 46*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Honest ephah." Justice and worship are one.
