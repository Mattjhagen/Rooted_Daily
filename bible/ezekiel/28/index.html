Ezekiel 28

The Guardian Cherub and the Pride of the Prince.

I am a God

“You say, ‘I am a god; I sit on the throne of a god.’ But you are a mere mortal.” 1

In Eden, the Garden of God

“You were in Eden, the garden of God; every precious stone adorned you.” 2
“You were anointed as a guardian cherub.” 3

The Fall from the Mountain

“You were blameless in your ways... till wickedness was found in you.”
“Your heart became proud on account of your beauty.” 4

Israel in Safety

“They will live in their own land in safety.” 5

Translation Notes

1 I am a God (ēl ā-nī): In the scrolls, this is the "Blasphemous Root of Deification." "Humanity" is "Uprooted" by "Arrogance" (*Pride*). He "Roots" his "Identity" in "El" (*God*). It signifies "The Ultimate Delusion of Power."

2 Eden, the Garden of God (be-ē-dhen gan-e-lō-hīm): The scrolls highlight the "Primeval Root of Perfection." The "King" is "Rooted" in the "Original Sanctuary." It signify "The Aesthetic of Celestial Splendor."

3 Guardian Cherub (ke-rūbh mim-shach has-sō-khēkh): Literally "The Anointed Covering Cherub." The scrolls highlight the "Protective Root of the Threshold." This is one of the most enigmatic passages in the Bible. "Obedience" is "Uprooted" by "Wickedness" (*Avlah*). It signifies "The Cosmic Fall."

4 Proud on account of Beauty (gā-bha-h lib-be-khā be-yoph-ye-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Narcissistic Root of the Heart." "Wisdom" is "Uprooted" by "Splendor." It signify "The Corrupting Power of Self-Reflection."

5 Live in Safety (we-yā-she-bhū a-le-hā la-bhe-tach): The scrolls conclude with this "Secure Root of the Remnant." "Exile" is "Uprooted" for Israel. It marks the transition to "The Oracles against Egypt" in *Ezekiel 29*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Eternal Land." Safety at last.
