Ezekiel 32

The Monster in the Net and the Grave of the Nations.

The Monster in the Net

“I will spread my net over you with a great host of people, and they will haul you up in my mesh.” 1

Darkness over the Land

“I will cover the heavens and darken their stars; I will bring darkness over your land.” 2

The Earth Below

“Bring them down with the daughters of the nations to the earth below.”
“Assyria is there... Elam is there... Edom is there...”

Swords Under their Heads

“They went down to the realm of the dead with their weapons of war—their swords placed under their heads.” 3
“Pharaoh will see them and be consoled.” 4

Translation Notes

1 My Net (eth-rishtī): In the scrolls, this is the "Entangling Root of Judgment." The "Sea Monster" is "Uprooted" from the "Waters" (*Deep*) by the "Net" (*Resheth*). It signifies "The Divine Capture of the Unrestrained."

2 Darkness (chō-shekh): The scrolls highlight the "Obscuring Root of Desolation." "Light" (*Or*) is "Uprooted" from the "Land." It signify "The Aesthetic of Total Eclipse."

3 Swords under their Heads (char-bhō-thām ta-chath rō-shē-hem): The scrolls highlight the "Martial Root of Finality." Even in "Death," they are "Rooted" in their "Violence" (*Swords*). It signify "The Permanent Identity of the Fallen Warrior."

4 Consoled (yin-nā-chēm): The scrolls conclude with this "Ironic Root of Comfort." Pharaoh is "Rooted" in the "Company of the Dead." It marks the transition to "The Watchman's Renewal" in *Ezekiel 33*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Grave of the Nations." All go down together.
