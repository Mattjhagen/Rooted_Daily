Ezekiel 15

The Useless Wood and the Fire of Jerusalem.

The Vine vs. the Tree

“How is the wood of a vine better than any tree in the forest?” 1

No Useful Peg

“Is wood ever taken from it to make anything useful? Do they make pegs from it?” 2

Charred and Useless

“When both ends are burned and the middle is charred...”
“Is it then useful for anything?” 3

Jerusalem as the Vine

“I will treat the people of Jerusalem like that.” 4
“They have come out of one fire, but another fire will consume them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Wood of a Vine (ētz hag-ge-phen): In the scrolls, this is the "Functional Root of the Vineyard." A "Vine" (*Gephen*) is "Rooted" for "Fruit," not "Timber." Without "Fruit," its "Value" is "Uprooted." It signifies "The Purpose of the Nation."

2 Make Pegs (yū-qach mi-men-nū yā-thēdh): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of Utility." The "Vine Wood" cannot even be "Rooted" in a "Wall" as a "Peg" (*Yathed*). It signify "The Total Worthlessness of Apostasy."

3 Charred and Useless (ne-cha-rāh): The scrolls highlight the "Combustive Root of Judgment." "Vibrancy" is "Uprooted" by the "Fire." It signifies "The Irreversibility of the Destruction."

4 Treat the People (nā-tha-tī eth-yō-she-bhē): Literally "I will give/appoint the inhabitants." The scrolls highlight the "Judicial Root of Assignment." Jerusalem is "Rooted" in "Fuel." It signify "The Transformation from Garden to Furnace."

5 Fire will Consume them (hā-ēsh tō-khe-lēm): The scrolls conclude with this "Digestive Root of Fire." "Safety" is "Uprooted" permanently. It marks the transition to "The Unfaithful Wife" in *Ezekiel 16*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Charred Vine." Fruit or fire.
