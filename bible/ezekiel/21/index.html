Ezekiel 21

The Flashing Sword and the Fork in the Road.

A Sword! A Sword!

“A sword, a sword, sharpened and polished—sharpened for the slaughter!” 1
“Polished to flash like lightning!” 2

The King’s Divination

“The king of Babylon will stop at the fork in the road.”
“He will cast lots with arrows, consult idols, and examine the liver.” 3

The Rightful King

“Take off the helmet, remove the crown.”
“Until he comes to whom it rightfully belongs.” 4

Flash like Lightning

“It is grasped for the slaughter, it is polished to flash like lightning.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sharpened and Polished (chu-dāh... me-rū-tāh): The scrolls highlight the "Kinetic Root of Execution." "Peace" is "Uprooted" by the "Maintenance of the Blade." It signifies "The Intentionality of the Judgment."

2 Flash like Lightning (le-ma-an hey-yēh lāh bhā-rāq): Literally "In order that it might have lightning." The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of the Sword." It signifies "The Terrifying Aesthetic of Divine Justice."

3 Examine the Liver (rā-āh bhak-kā-bhēdh): The scrolls highlight the "Hepatoscopic Root of Decision." Pagan "Consultation" is "Rooted" in "Viscera" (*Liver*). Even "Occult Practices" are "Rooted" in "God’s Sovereignty" to direct the "King" to "Jerusalem." It signify "The Divine Steering of History."

4 Until he Comes (adh bō a-sher lō ham-mish-pāt): Literally "Until he comes whose is the judgment." The scrolls highlight the "Messianic Root of the Crown." The "Usurper" is "Uprooted" (*Remove the Crown*) and the "True King" (*Shiloh/Christ*) is "Rooted." It signifies "The Preservation of the Throne for the One."

5 Grasped (ma-a-tu-phāh): The scrolls conclude with this "Manual Root of Readiness." The "Sword" is "Rooted" in the "Hand" (*Yad*). It marks the transition to "The City of Bloodshed" in *Ezekiel 22*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Flashing Blade." The crown is removed.
