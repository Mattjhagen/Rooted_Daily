Ezekiel 43

The Radiance of the East and the Place of the Throne.

Radiant Glory

“His voice was like the roar of rushing waters, and the land was radiant with his glory.” 1

Place of My Throne

“This is the place of my throne and the place for the soles of my feet.” 2

Ashamed of Sins

“Describe the temple to the people of Israel, that they may be ashamed of their sins.” 3

The Law of the Temple

“All the surrounding area on top of the mountain will be most holy.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Roar of Rushing Waters (qōl may-īm rab-bīm): The scrolls highlight the "Acoustic Root of Majesty." "Silence" is "Uprooted." The "Voice" (*Qol*) of God is "Rooted" in the "Many Waters" (*Mayim Rabbim*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Overwhelming Power."

2 Place of my Throne (me-qōm kis-ī): The scrolls highlight the "Regal Root of Presence." "Absence" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" His "Throne" (*Kisse*) in the "New Temple." It signify "The Permanent Habitation of the Divine."

3 Ashamed of Sins (wey-yik-kā-le-mū mē-a-wō-nō-thē-hem): The scrolls highlight the "Ethical Root of the Architecture." "Apathy" is "Uprooted." The "Beauty" of the "Temple" (*Perfection*) is "Rooted" in the "Conscience." It signifies "The Convicting Power of Sacred Design."

4 Law of the Temple (tō-rath hab-bā-yith): The scrolls conclude with this "Legislative Root of Holiness." "Secularity" is "Uprooted." The "Mountain Top" is "Rooted" in "Torah." It marks the transition to "The Gate of the Prince" in *Ezekiel 44*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Radiant Glory." God has returned.
