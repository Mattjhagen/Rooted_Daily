Ezekiel 5

The Barber’s Sword and the Scales of the Wind.

The Sharp Sword

“Take a sharp sword and use it as a barber’s razor to shave your head.” 1

The Three Parts

“Burn a third, strike a third, scatter a third.”
“But take a few hairs and tuck them into the folds of your garment.” 2

Center of the Nations

“This is Jerusalem, which I have set in the center of the nations.” 3

The Pursuing Sword

“I will unsheathe my sword and pursue them.” 4
“Parents will eat their children, and children will eat their parents.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Barber’s Razor (ta-ar hag-gal-lā-bhīm): In the scrolls, this is the "Violent Root of Shaving." "Dignity" is "Uprooted" as the "Prophet’s Hair" (*Se’ar*) is removed with a "Sword" (*Cherebh*). It signifies "The Shame of the Exile."

2 Folds of your Garment (bi-khe-nā-phey-khā): Literally "In your wings/edges." The scrolls highlight the "Remnant Root of Safety." A "Few" (*Me’at*) are "Rooted" in the "Prophet’s Protection." It signify "The Survival of the Spark."

3 Center of the Nations (be-thōkh hag-gō-yīm): The scrolls highlight the "Central Root of Witness." Jerusalem was "Rooted" as the "Light" for the world. "Uprooting" the center causes "Chaos" in the "Periphery." It signifies "The Moral Weight of Geography."

4 Unsheathe my Sword (we-che-rebh ā-rīq): The scrolls highlight the "Relentless Root of Pursuit." "Safety" is "Uprooted" even for those who "Escape" the "City." It signify "The Reach of Divine Justice."

5 Parents will eat their Children (ā-bhōth yō-khe-lū): The scrolls conclude with this "Taboo Root of Famine." The "Biological Root" of "Affection" is "Uprooted" by "Survival." It marks the transition to "The Mountains of Israel" in *Ezekiel 6*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Barber’s Sword." The hair is scattered.
