Ezekiel 26

The Bare Rock and the Spreading of Nets.

The Gate to the Nations

“Aha! The gate to the nations is broken... I will prosper now that she lies in ruins.” 1

Scraped to the Bone

“I will scrape away her rubble and make her a bare rock.” 2

Place for Nets

“She will become a place to spread fishing nets in the midst of the sea.” 3

The King of Babylon

“I am bringing against Tyre Nebuchadnezzar... with a great army.” 4
“You will never be rebuilt.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Gate to the Nations (dal-thōth hā-am-mīm): In the scrolls, this is the "Commercial Root of Opportunity." Tyre’s "Prosperity" is "Rooted" in the "Destruction" of Jerusalem (*The Gate*). It signifies "The Mercenary Heart of the Merchant."

2 Scrape away her Rubble (we-sich-pī-thī a-phā-rāh): The scrolls highlight the "Terminal Root of Cleansing." "Architecture" is "Uprooted." The "City" is "Scraped" (*Sachaph*) down to the "Bare Rock" (*Tze-chi-ach*). It signify "The Aesthetic of Total Erasure."

3 Place to spread Nets (mish-tach che-rā-mīm): The scrolls highlight the "Primitive Root of Reversion." "Global Trade" is "Uprooted" and replaced by "Basic Fishing." It signifies "The Return to the Untamed Sea."

4 Great Army (qā-hāl ra-v): The scrolls highlight the "Military Root of Execution." "Security" is "Uprooted" by the "Gathering" (*Qahal*) of the nations under the "King of Kings" (*Nebuchadnezzar*). It signify "The Instruments of Divine Wrath."

5 Never be Rebuilt (lō thib-bā-neh ōdh): The scrolls conclude with this "Permanent Root of Desolation." "Continuity" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Ship of Tyre" in *Ezekiel 27*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Bare Rock." No more songs.
