Ezekiel 42

The Holy Rooms and the Separation of the Common.

The Holy Chambers

“There the priests who approach the Lord will eat the most holy offerings.” 1

The Holy Garments

“They must leave the garments in which they minister, for they are holy.” 2

The Outer Dimensions

“He measured the east side with the measuring rod; it was five hundred cubits.” 3

The Wall of Separation

“It had a wall around it to separate the holy from the common.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Most Holy Offerings (qō-dhesh qā-dhā-shīm): The scrolls highlight the "Sustaining Root of the Priesthood." The "Common Meal" is "Uprooted." The "Priests" are "Rooted" in the "Holy Food" (*Qodesh*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Sacred Nourishment."

2 Leave the Garments (yan-nī-chū shām bi-ghā-dhē-hem): The scrolls highlight the "Vestiary Root of Identity." "Ordinary Appearance" is "Uprooted." The "Ministering Garments" (*Bigdim*) are "Rooted" in the "Holy Space." It signify "The Sanctity of the Prophetic Role."

3 Five Hundred Cubits (cha-mēsh mē-ōth qā-neh): The scrolls highlight the "Mathematical Root of Separation." The "Infinite" is "Uprooted" by the "Measurement" (*Middah*). It signifies "The Boundary of the Divine Enclosure."

4 Separate the Holy from the Common (le-habh-dīl bēn haq-qō-dhesh le-chōl): The scrolls conclude with this "Binary Root of Holiness." "Confusion" is "Uprooted" by "Separation" (*Habdil*). It marks the transition to "The Return of the Glory" in *Ezekiel 43*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sacred Wall." The boundary is set.
