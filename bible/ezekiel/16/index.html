Ezekiel 16

The Abandoned Infant and the Everlasting Covenant.

Kicking in Blood

“I saw you kicking in your blood and I said to you, ‘Live!’” 1

Adorned in Splendor

“I put a ring on your nose, earrings on your ears, and a beautiful crown on your head.” 2

The Sin of Sodom

“She was arrogant, overfed and unconcerned; she did not help the poor and needy.” 3

Everlasting Covenant

“I will establish an everlasting covenant with you.” 4
“When I make atonement for you.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Kicking in your Blood (mit-bō-śe-śeth be-dhā-mā-yikh): The scrolls highlight the "Visceral Root of Rescue." Jerusalem was "Uprooted" at birth, left to "Wallow" (*Hitboses*) in her "Blood" (*Dam*). God "Roots" her "Life" by a "Spoken Command." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Pure Grace."

2 Beautiful Crown (a-te-reth tiph-e-reth): The scrolls highlight the "Regal Root of Election." "Nakedness" is "Uprooted" and replaced by "Splendor" (*Tiphereth*). It signify "The Royal Dignity of the Covenant."

3 Sin of Sodom (a-wōn Se-dhōm): The scrolls highlight the "Economic Root of Detestation." Sodom’s "Uprooting" was caused by "Selfish Abundance" (*Shalvat Hashqet*). It signifies "The Moral Weight of Prosperity."

4 Everlasting Covenant (be-rīth ō-lām): The scrolls highlight the "Perpetual Root of Restoration." The "Broken Covenant" is "Uprooted" to make way for one that is "Rooted" in "Eternity" (*Olam*). It signify "The Endurance of Divine Love."

5 Atonement (be-khaph-pe-rī): The scrolls conclude with this "Expiatory Root of Silence." "Guilt" is "Uprooted" by "Covering" (*Kaphar*). It marks the transition to "The Two Eagles" in *Ezekiel 17*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Rescued Queen." Live!
