Ezekiel 19

The Lioness of Judah and the Desert Vine.

The Mother Lioness

“What a lioness was your mother among the lions!” 1

Caught in the Snare

“He was caught in their pit... they led him with hooks to Egypt.” 2

The Vine in the Vineyard

“Your mother was like a vine in your vineyard... fit for a ruler’s scepter.” 3

Uprooted in Fury

“It was uprooted in fury and thrown to the ground.” 4
“Now it is planted in the desert, in a dry and thirsty land.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Lioness (le-bhī-yā): In the scrolls, this is the "Predatory Root of Royalty." Judah is "Rooted" as the "Lioness." It signifies "The Strength and Nobility of the Davidic Line."

2 Hooks (ba-cha-chīm): The scrolls highlight the "Captive Root of the King." "Freedom" is "Uprooted" by "Force." It signify "The Humiliation of the Captured Sovereign."

3 Ruler’s Scepter (ma-tōth mō-she-līm): The scrolls highlight the "Authoritative Root of Power." The "Vine" (*Gephen*) was "Rooted" with "Scepters" (*Matot*). It signifies "The Former Glory of the Kingdom."

4 Uprooted in Fury (wat-tit-ta-sh be-chē-māh): The scrolls highlight the "Violent Root of Dislocation." "Stability" is "Uprooted" (*Nathash*) by "Divine Wrath" (*Chemah*). It signify "The Aesthetic of Sudden Collapse."

5 Planted in the Desert (wat-tī-tā-ē-hū bam-midh-bār): The scrolls conclude with this "Arid Root of Displacement." The "Vine" is "Re-Rooted" in a "Thirsty Land" (*Babylon*). It marks the transition to "The History of Rebellion" in *Ezekiel 20*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Uprooted Lion." Lament for the princes.
