Ezekiel 41

The Most Holy Place and the Carved Cherubim.

The Most Holy Place

“He said to me, ‘This is the Most Holy Place.’” 1

Cherubim and Palm Trees

“Cherubim and palm trees were carved on the walls; every cherub had two faces.” 2

The Table before the Lord

“He said to me, ‘This is the table that is before the Lord.’” 3

Successive Levels

“The side rooms surrounding the temple became wider at each successive level.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Most Holy Place (qō-dhesh haq-qā-dhā-shīm): The scrolls highlight the "Ultimate Root of Sanctity." All "Profanity" is "Uprooted." The "Space" is "Rooted" in the "Essence of the Divine" (*Qodesh*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Absolute Center."

2 Two Faces (she-tē phā-nīm): The scrolls highlight the "Dual Root of the Cherubim." One "Face" (*Man*) and one "Face" (*Lion*) are "Rooted" in the "Carving." It signify "The Intersection of the Human and the Wild under the Palm Tree of Peace."

3 Table before the Lord (hash-shul-chān a-sher liph-nē Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Communal Root of Presence." The "Altar" is "Uprooted" as a place of death and "Rooted" as a "Table" (*Shulchan*) of "Fellowship." It signifies "The Transition from Sacrifice to Communion."

4 Successive Levels (ne-sis-sāh): The scrolls highlight the "Ascending Root of the Structure." "Stagnation" is "Uprooted." The "Temple" becomes "Wider" (*Expansion*) as it "Rises." It marks the transition to "The Priests' Rooms" in *Ezekiel 42*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Most Holy." God is in the architecture.
