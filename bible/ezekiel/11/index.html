Ezekiel 11

The Sanctuary in Exile and the Heart of Flesh.

The Cooking Pot

“This city is a pot, and we are the meat.”
“Your victims whom you have laid in it are the meat.” 1

Sanctuary in Exile

“I have been a sanctuary for them in the countries where they have gone.” 2

Heart of Flesh

“I will remove their heart of stone and give them a heart of flesh.” 3
“I will put a new spirit in them.” 4

The Mountain East

“The glory of the Lord went up from within the city and stopped above the mountain east of it.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Pot and Meat (sīr... bā-śār): The scrolls highlight the "Culinary Root of Cynicism." The leaders "Root" their "Security" in a metaphor of a "Pot" (*Sir*). God "Uproots" their "Comfort" by showing that the "Victims" are the only "Meat." It signifies "The Reversal of False Confidence."

2 Sanctuary in Exile (le-miq-dāsh me-at): Literally "A small sanctuary" or "Sanctuary for a little while." The scrolls highlight the "Portable Root of the Presence." "Geographic Worship" is "Uprooted" and God Himself becomes the "Root" of "Sanctuary" in "Babylon." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Displaced Presence."

3 Heart of Flesh (lēbh bā-śār): The scrolls highlight the "Organic Root of Responsiveness." The "Petrified Root" (*Heart of Stone*) is "Uprooted" and replaced by a "Living Root" (*Heart of Flesh*). It signifies "The Biological Miracle of Grace."

4 New Spirit (rū-ach cha-dhā-shāh): The scrolls highlight the "Pneumatic Root of the New Covenant." "Old Motivations" are "Uprooted." It signify "The Internal Engine of Obedience."

5 Mountain East of it (al-he-hār a-sher mip-qe-dhem): The scrolls conclude with this "Terminal Root of Departure." The "Presence" is "Uprooted" from the "Sanctuary" and "City," stopping at the "Mount of Olives." It marks the end of the first Temple vision. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The New Heart." The Glory has left the city.
