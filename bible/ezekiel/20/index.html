Ezekiel 20

The Sake of the Name and the Bond of the Covenant.

For the Sake of My Name

“For the sake of my name I acted, so that it would not be profaned among the nations.” 1

Statutes Not Good

“I gave them statutes that were not good and laws through which they could not live.” 2

Under the Rod

“I will make you pass under my rod and bring you into the bond of the covenant.” 3

The Holy Mountain

“For on my holy mountain... there the entire house of Israel will serve me.” 4
“I will accept you as a fragrant incense.” 5

Translation Notes

1 For the Sake of My Name (le-ma-an she-mī): The scrolls highlight the "Reputational Root of God." "Judgment" is "Uprooted" by "Identity." God "Roots" His "Actions" in His own "Character" (*Shem*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Divine Self-Consistency."

2 Statutes Not Good (chuq-qīm lō tō-bhīm): The scrolls highlight the "Punitive Root of Law." "Grace" is "Uprooted" and replaced by "Statutes" (*Chuqqim*) that serve as "Judgments." It signify "The Severity of Abandonment to Sin."

3 Under my Rod (ta-chath hash-shā-bhet): The scrolls highlight the "Disciplinary Root of Election." "Independence" is "Uprooted" as the "Sheep" are "Rooted" in the "Bond" (*Masoreth*) of the "Covenant." It signifies "The Re-Establishment of Authority."

4 Holy Mountain (be-har qodh-shī): The scrolls highlight the "Topographical Root of Worship." "Exile" is "Uprooted" and the people are "Re-Rooted" in the "High Place" (*Har*). It signify "The Final Destination of the Remnant."

5 Fragrant Incense (be-rē-ach nī-chō-ach): The scrolls conclude with this "Olfactory Root of Acceptance." "Stench" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Sharpened Sword" in *Ezekiel 21*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Holy Mountain." Serve the Lord.
