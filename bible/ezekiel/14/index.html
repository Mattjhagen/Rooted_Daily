Ezekiel 14

The Idols of the Heart and the Silence of Intercession.

Idols in the Heart

“These men have set up idols in their hearts.” 1

Wicked Stumbling Block

“They have put wicked stumbling blocks before their faces.” 2

Noah, Daniel, and Job

“Even if Noah, Daniel and Job were in it, they could save only themselves.” 3

The Consoled Exile

“When you see their conduct and their actions, you will be consoled.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Idols in their Hearts (gil-lū-lē-hem al-lib-bām): In the scrolls, this is the "Internalized Root of Apostasy." "Visual Worship" is "Uprooted" as the "Idols" (*Gillulim*) are "Rooted" in the "Internal Man" (*Heart/Leb*). It signifies "The Psychological Corruption of the Soul."

2 Wicked Stumbling Block (mik-shōl a-wō-nām): The scrolls highlight the "Obstructive Root of Iniquity." "Pathways" are "Uprooted" by "Stumbling Blocks" (*Mikshol*). It signify "The Self-Inflicted Sabotage of the Spiritual Life."

3 Noah, Daniel and Job (Nō-ach Dā-ni-yēl we-Ī-yōbh): The scrolls highlight the "Exemplary Root of Righteousness." The "Intercessory Power" of the "Individual" is "Uprooted" regarding the "Nation." They are "Rooted" only in their "Personal Salvation." It signifies "The Limit of Vicarious Merit."

4 Be Consoled (ne-cha-me-tem): The scrolls conclude with this "Explanatory Root of Comfort." "Grief" is "Uprooted" when the "Exiles" see that the "Judgment" was "Rooted" in "Justice." It marks the transition to "The Allegory of the Vine" in *Ezekiel 15*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "Internal Integrity." Renounce your idols.
