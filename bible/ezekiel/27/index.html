Ezekiel 27

The Perfect Ship and the Heart of the Sea.

Perfect in Beauty

“You said, Tyre, ‘I am perfect in beauty.’ Your domain was on the high seas.” 1

The Global Inventory

“Juniper from Senir... Cedar from Lebanon... Oaks from Bashan.”
“Fine linen from Egypt... Blue and purple from Elishah.”
“Tarshish... Javan... Tubal... Meshek...”

Broken in the Heart of the Sea

“The east wind has broken you in the heart of the sea.” 2
“Everything will fall into the heart of the sea on the day of your shipwreck.” 3

Who was ever like Tyre?

“Who was ever like Tyre, silenced in the midst of the sea?” 4
“You have come to a horrible end.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Perfect in Beauty (ke-lī-lath yō-phī): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Narcissism." Tyre "Roots" her "Identity" in "Perfection" (*Kelilah*) of "Form" (*Yophi*). It signifies "The Spiritual Danger of Physical Excellence."

2 East Wind (rū-ach haq-qā-dhīm): The scrolls highlight the "Meteorological Root of Judgment." The "Spirit/Wind" (*Ruach*) of the "East" (*Qadim*) is the "Instrument" that "Uproots" the "Naval Pride." It signify "The Unstoppable Force of Nature as Divine Messenger."

3 Heart of the Sea (be-lēbh yam-mīm): Literally "In the heart of the seas." The scrolls highlight the "Visceral Root of the Abyss." The "Heart" of the "Ship" meets the "Heart" of the "Sea." It signifies "The Total Submersion of Materialism."

4 Silenced (ne-dhū-māh): Literally "Brought to silence" or "Destroyed." The scrolls highlight the "Vocal Root of Extinction." The "Noisy Trade" is "Uprooted" by "Eternal Silence." It signify "The Peace of the Deep."

5 Horrible End (bal-lā-hōth): The scrolls conclude with this "Terrifying Root of Destruction." "Prosperity" is "Uprooted" by "Terrors" (*Ballahot*). It marks the transition to "The Fall of the King of Tyre" in *Ezekiel 28*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sunken Ship." The sea has taken it.
