Ezekiel 12

The Baggage of the Exile and the Blind Prince.

The Baggage of Exile

“Pack your baggage for exile and move to another place.” 1

Dig Through the Wall

“In the evening, dig through the wall and carry your things out.” 2
“Cover your face so that you cannot see the land.” 3

The Prince will not See

“I will bring him to Babylonia... but he will not see it, and there he will die.” 4

No More Delay

“There will be no more delay; whatever I say will be fulfilled.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Baggage for Exile (ke-lē gō-lāh): In the scrolls, this is the "Essential Root of the Refugee." "Domesticity" is "Uprooted." The "Vessels" (*Kele*) of the home are reduced to a "Pack" (*Baggage*). It signifies "The Reduction of Human Life to Survival."

2 Dig through the Wall (ba-qir cha-thōr): The scrolls highlight the "Subterranean Root of Escape." "Civic Order" is "Uprooted." The "Prophet" must "Dig" (*Chathor*) to show the "Desperation" of the "Exodus." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Fugitive."

3 Cover your Face (pā-ney-khā the-khak-seh): The scrolls highlight the "Visual Root of Shame." The "Sovereign" is "Uprooted" from "Vision." It signify "The Darkness of the Blind King."

4 He will not See it (we-ō-thāh lō yir-eh): A literal prophecy regarding Zedekiah whose eyes were put out before being taken to Babylon. The scrolls highlight the "Ironic Root of Captivity." "Sight" is "Uprooted" so that he "Roots" in "Exile" without seeing the "Land." It signifies "The Cruelty of the End."

5 No more Delay (lō thē-ā-shēkh ōdh): The scrolls conclude with this "Terminal Root of Fulfilment." "Procrastination" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The False Prophets" in *Ezekiel 13*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Fulfilled Word." The baggage is packed.
