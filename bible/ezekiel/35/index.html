Ezekiel 35

The Oracle against Mount Seir and the Pursuit of Blood.

Against Mount Seir

“I am against you, Mount Seir; I will make you a desolate waste.” 1

Ancient Enmity

“Because you harbored an ancient enmity at the time of their calamity.” 2

Blood for Blood

“I will give you over to bloodshed, and bloodshed will pursue you.” 3

The Lord was There

“You said, ‘These two nations will be ours,’ even though the Lord was there.” 4
“Mount Seir will be desolate.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Desolate Waste (shi-me-māh ū-me-sham-māh): In the scrolls, this is the "Arid Root of Oblivion." Edom’s "Stability" is "Uprooted." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Eternal Barrenness."

2 Ancient Enmity (ē-bhath ō-lām): The scrolls highlight the "Perpetual Root of Hatred." "Family Bonds" are "Uprooted" by "Borders" (*Enmity*). It signify "The Genetic Bitterness of the Rival."

3 Bloodshed will Pursue you (dām yir-de-phey-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Karmic Root of Violence." "Life" is "Uprooted" by the "Pursuit" (*Radaph*) of "Blood" (*Dam*). It signifies "The Reaping of the Harvest of Malice."

4 The Lord was There (Yah-weh shām hā-yā): The scrolls highlight the "Omnipresent Root of the Inheritance." Edom’s "Ambition" is "Uprooted" by the "Presence" (*Sham*) of "Jehovah." It signify "The Fatal Error of Ignoring the Divine Owner."

5 Mount Seir (Har Śē-īr): The scrolls conclude with this "Topographical Root of the Judgment." The "Mountain" is "Uprooted" from its "Inhabitants." It marks the transition to "The Restoration of the Mountains of Israel" in *Ezekiel 36*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Empty Peak." Edom falls.
