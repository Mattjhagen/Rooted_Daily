Ezekiel 30

The Day of Clouds and the Broken Arms of the Nile.

Alas for that Day!

“The day of the Lord is near—a day of clouds, a time of doom for the nations.” 1

Foundations Torn Up

“Her wealth will be carried away and her foundations torn up.” 2

The Arm of Pharaoh

“I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt.” 3
“I will break both his arms, the good one as well as the broken one.” 4

The Sword of Babylon

“I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon and put my sword in his hand.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Day of Clouds (yōm ā-nān): In the scrolls, this is the "Obscuring Root of Judgment." "Vision" is "Uprooted" by the "Clouds" (*Anan*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Coming Storm."

2 Foundations torn up (ne-her-sū ye-sō-dhō-the-hā): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of Collapse." "Permanence" is "Uprooted" (*Nā-haras*). The "Foundations" (*Yesod*) are removed. It signify "The Total Instability of Human Empire."

3 Broken the Arm (e-thze-rō-a... shā-bhār-tī): The scrolls highlight the "Manual Root of Powerlessness." Pharaoh’s "Ability" to "Act" is "Uprooted." It signifies "The Paralysis of the Mighty."

4 Good and Broken (eth-ha-cha-zā-qāh we-eth-han-nish-bā-reth): The scrolls highlight the "Progressive Root of Ruin." Even the "Strong" (*Chazaq*) arm will be "Uprooted." It signify "The Systematic Dismantling of Resistance."

5 Strengthen the Arms (we-chi-zak-tī eth-ze-rō-ōth): The scrolls conclude with this "Delegated Root of Execution." God "Roots" His "Sword" (*Chereb*) in the "Hand" of "Babylon." It marks the transition to "The Cedar of Lebanon" in *Ezekiel 31*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Day of Clouds." Pharaoh is broken.
