Ezekiel 1

The Heavens Opened and the Chariot of the Spirit.

Heavens Opened

“While I was among the exiles by the Kebar River, the heavens were opened and I saw visions of God.” 1

Four Living Creatures

“In the center of the fire looked like four living creatures.”
“The face of a human, a lion, an ox, and an eagle.” 2

The Wheels Full of Eyes

“Their rims were high and awesome, and all four rims were full of eyes all around.” 3
“For the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.” 4

The Lapis Lazuli Throne

“Above the vault was what looked like a throne of lapis lazuli.” 5
“This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the Lord.”

Translation Notes

1 Visions of God (mar-ōth e-lō-hīm): The scrolls highlight the "Visual Root of Revelation." "Ordinary Sight" is "Uprooted" and "Transcendental Sight" (*Maroth*) is "Rooted." It signifies "The Piercing of the Veil."

2 The Faces (pe-nē): The human, lion (king of wild), ox (king of domestic), and eagle (king of birds) represent the "Rooted Sovereignty" of God over all "Creation." It signifies "The Cosmic Presence."

3 Full of Eyes (me-lē-ōth ē-nay-im): The scrolls highlight the "Omniscient Root of the Chariot." "Blind Chance" is "Uprooted." The "Wheels" (*Ophanim*) are "Rooted" in "Intelligence" (*Eyes*). It signifies "The Purposeful Movement of History."

4 Spirit... was in the Wheels (rū-ach... bā-ō-phan-nīm): The scrolls highlight the "Vital Root of Mechanism." "Matter" is "Uprooted" from "Autonomy" and "Rooted" in the "Spirit" (*Ruach*). It signify "The Divine Engine of the Universe."

5 Lapis Lazuli (e-bhen sap-pīr): Literally "Stone of Sapphire." The scrolls conclude with this "Chromatic Root of the Throne." The "Exilic Soil" is "Uprooted" for the "Celestial Blue" (*Sappir*). It marks the transition to "The Call of Ezekiel" in *Ezekiel 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Glory of the Lord." Fell facedown.
