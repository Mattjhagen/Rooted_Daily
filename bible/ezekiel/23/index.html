Ezekiel 23

Oholah and Oholibah and the Cup of Ruin.

The Two Sisters

“Their names were Oholah and Oholibah.” 1

Portrayed on the Wall

“She saw men portrayed on a wall... the Chaldeans.”
“She lusted after them and sent messengers to them.” 2

The Cup of Desolation

“You will drink your sister’s cup, a cup large and deep... the cup of ruin.” 3

Stone and Sword

“The crowd will stone them and cut them down with their swords.” 4
“You will bear the penalty for your lewdness.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Oholah and Oholibah (O-ho-lāh we-O-ho-lī-bhāh): The names are meaningful. Oholah means "Her Tent" (Samaria had its own shrines). Oholibah means "My Tent is in Her" (Jerusalem had God’s Temple). The scrolls highlight the "Ecclesiastical Root of Possession." "Identity" is "Rooted" in the "Presence" or "Lack of Presence" of God’s "Tent" (*Ohel*). It signifies "The Moral Failure of the Religious Institution."

2 Portrayed on the Wall (cha-qu-qīm al-had-qīr): The scrolls highlight the "Visual Root of Temptation." "Imagination" is "Uprooted" by "Images" (*Chaquqim*) of foreign power. It signify "The Psychological Corruption of the Gaze."

3 Cup of Desolation (kōs sham-māh): The scrolls highlight the "Potable Root of Judgment." "Drunkenness" is "Uprooted" and replaced by the "Cup" (*Kos*) of "Horror" (*Shammah*). It signifies "The Inevitable Consequence of Sin."

4 Stone them (re-gham a-lē-hen qā-hāl): The scrolls highlight the "Communal Root of Execution." "Status" is "Uprooted" by the "Stone" (*Ragham*). It signify "The Aesthetic of Public Shame."

5 Bear the Penalty (a-wō-ney-khem): The scrolls conclude with this "Culpable Root of Accounting." "Iniquity" (*Avon*) is "Uprooted" through "Judgment." It marks the transition to "The Cooking Pot" in *Ezekiel 24*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Two Sisters." You will know I am Lord.
