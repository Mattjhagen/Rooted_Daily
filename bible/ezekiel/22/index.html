Ezekiel 22

The City of Bloodshed and the Furnace of Dross.

City of Bloodshed

“Will you judge this city of bloodshed? Confront her with all her detestable practices.” 1

The Melting Furnace

“As silver and copper are gathered into a furnace...”
“So I will gather you in my anger and my wrath and melt you.” 2

Stand in the Gap

“I looked for someone who would build up the wall and stand before me in the gap.” 3
“But I found no one.” 4

Detestable Practices

“In you they have treated father and mother with contempt.”
“You have forgotten me, declares the Sovereign Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 City of Bloodshed (īr had-dā-mīm): In the scrolls, this is the "Violent Root of the Metropolis." "Life" is "Uprooted" as the "City" (*Ir*) is "Rooted" in "Blood" (*Damim*). It signifies "The Moral Pollution of the Urban Space."

2 Melt you (we-hit-takh-tī): The scrolls highlight the "Metallurgical Root of Judgment." "Dross" (*Sī-ghīm*) is "Uprooted" from the "Pure Metal" through "Fire" (*Esh*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Refining Furnace."

3 Stand in the Gap (ō-mēdh bap-pe-retz): The scrolls highlight the "Architectural Root of Intercession." "Integrity" is "Uprooted" and there is a "Breach" (*Peretz*) in the "Wall" (*Gader*). It signifies "The Vacancy of the Spiritual Guard."

4 I Found No One (we-lō mā-tzā-thī): The scrolls highlight the "Absence of the Righteous Root." "Hope" is "Uprooted." It signify "The Total Corruption of the Social Fabric."

5 You have Forgotten Me (we-ō-thī shā-kha-chat): The scrolls conclude with this "Mnemic Root of Apostasy." "God" is "Uprooted" from the "Memory" (*Shakhach*). It marks the transition to "Oholah and Oholibah" in *Ezekiel 23*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Empty Gap." No one was found.
