Ezekiel 9

The Man in Linen and the Mark of Grief.

The Man with the Ink

“Among them was a man clothed in linen with a writing kit at his side.” 1

The Mark on the Forehead

“Put a mark on the foreheads of those who grieve and lament over all the detestable things.” 2

Begin at the Sanctuary

“Slaughter the old men, the young men and women... Begin at my sanctuary.” 3

The Glory Moves

“The glory of God rose from above the cherubim and moved to the threshold of the temple.” 4
“Alas, Sovereign Lord! Are you going to destroy the entire remnant of Israel?” 5

Translation Notes

1 Writing Kit (qe-seth has-sō-phēr): The scrolls highlight the "Scribal Root of Accountability." The "Executioners" are "Rooted" in "Violence," but the "Scribe" (*Sopher*) is "Rooted" in "Selection." It signifies "The Precision of Divine Mercy."

2 Put a Mark (we-thi-wī-thā thāw): Literally "Mark a Tav" (the last letter of the alphabet). The scrolls highlight the "Alphabetic Root of Protection." "Death" is "Uprooted" for those who "Root" their "Hearts" in "Grief" (*Anach*) for sin. It signify "The Aesthetic of the Remnant."

3 Begin at my Sanctuary (ū-mim-miq-dā-shī tē-khēl-lū): The scrolls highlight the "Liturgical Root of Judgment." The "House of Worship" is "Uprooted" because it has been "Defiled." It signifies "The Higher Standard for the Holy."

4 Threshold of the Temple (el-mip-tan hab-bā-yith): The scrolls highlight the "Transitional Root of Departure." The "Glory" (*Kabod*) is "Uprooting" itself from the "Inner Sanctum." It signify "The Beginning of the End of the Presence."

5 Destroy the Remnant (ma-sh-chīth eth kol-she-ē-rīth): The scrolls conclude with this "Anxious Root of Intercession." Ezekiel "Roots" his "Prayer" in the "Survival" of the people. It marks the transition to "The Coals of Fire" in *Ezekiel 10*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Marked Man." Grieve for the city.
