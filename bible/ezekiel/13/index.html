Ezekiel 13

The Jackals in the Ruins and the Whitewashed Wall.

Jackals among Ruins

“Your prophets, Israel, are like jackals among ruins.” 1

The Flimsy Wall

“When a flimsy wall is built, they cover it with whitewash.” 2
“A violent wind will burst forth, and the wall will fall.” 3

Hunting Souls

“Woe to the women who sew magic charms on all their wrists.” 4
“Will you ensnare the souls of my people but preserve your own?”

Own Imagination

“Woe to the foolish prophets who follow their own spirit and have seen nothing!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Jackals among Ruins (ke-shū-ā-līm ba-cha-rā-bhōth): In the scrolls, this is the "Opportunistic Root of the False Prophet." They "Root" their "Survival" in the "Decay" of the nation rather than "Uprooting" the "Causes of the Ruins." It signifies "The Predatory Nature of False Teaching."

2 Whitewash (tā-phēl): Literally "Untempered Mortar" or "Vain things." The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Deception." They "Uproot" the "Structural Reality" with a "Surface Appearance" (*Whitewash*). It signify "The Fragility of False Security."

3 Violent Wind (rū-ach se-ā-rōth): The scrolls highlight the "Meteorological Root of Judgment." The "Flimsy Root" of the wall is "Uprooted" by the "Spirit/Wind" (*Ruach*). It signifies "The Inevitability of the Collapse."

4 Magic Charms (ke-śā-thōth): The scrolls highlight the "Occultic Root of Manipulation." They try to "Root" the "Souls" (*Nephesh*) of the people in "Magic" (*Kestot*). It signify "The Ensnarement of the Vulnerable."

5 Follow their own Spirit (ha-hō-le-khīm a-cha-rē rū-chām): The scrolls conclude with this "Subjective Root of Vision." "Truth" is "Uprooted" by "Personal Imagination." It marks the transition to "The Elders with Idols" in *Ezekiel 14*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The True Vision." The wall falls.
