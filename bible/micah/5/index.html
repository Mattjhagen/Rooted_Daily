Micah 5

The Star of Bethlehem and the Shepherd of Strength.

Bethlehem Ephrathah

“But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, out of you will come for me one who will be ruler.” 1
“Whose origins are from of old, from ancient times.”

Standing in Strength

“He will stand and shepherd his flock in the strength of the Lord.” 2

Dew from the Lord

“The remnant of Jacob will be like dew from the Lord.” 3

Stripping the Chariots

“I will destroy your horses and demolish your chariots.” 4
“And he will be our peace.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Bethlehem Ephrathah (Bēth-le-chem Eph-rā-thāh): The scrolls highlight the "Geographic Root of the Messianic Hope." "Obscurity" is "Uprooted." The "Ruler" is "Rooted" in the "House of Bread" (*Bethlehem*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Humble Beginning."

2 Shepherd in Strength (we-rā-āh be-ōz): The scrolls highlight the "Pastoral Root of the Power." "Tyranny" is "Uprooted." He is "Rooted" in "Shepherding" (*Raah*) by the "Strength" (*Oz*) of YHWH. It signify "The Gentle Might of the King."

3 Like Dew (ke-tal): The scrolls highlight the "Refreshing Root of the Remnant." "Drought" is "Uprooted." Jacob is "Rooted" in the "Dew" (*Tal*) that comes from heaven. It signifies "The Involuntary Blessing of God’s People."

4 Demolish your Chariots (we-ha-a-bhādh-tī mar-ke-bhō-the-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Rejecting Root of False Confidence." "Militarism" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" out the "Chariots" (*Merkabhoth*) to restore trust in Himself. It signify "The Divine Disarmament."

5 He will be our Peace (we-hā-yāh zeh shā-lōm): The scrolls conclude with this "Irenic Root of the King." "Conflict" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Lord’s Case against Israel" in *Micah 6*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Star of Bethlehem." He is our Shalom.
