Micah 2

The Wicked on their Beds and the Breaker of the Way.

Evil on the Bed

“Woe to those who plot evil and plan wickedness on their beds!” 1
“At morning’s light they carry it out.”

They Covet and Seize

“They covet fields and seize them, and houses, and take them.” 2

Remnant of Israel

“I will bring them together like sheep in a pen.” 3

The One Who Breaks Open

“The One who breaks open the way will go up before them.” 4
“Their King will pass through before them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Plot Evil (chō-she-bhē ā-wen): The scrolls highlight the "Nocturnal Root of the Crime." "Rest" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Wickedness" (*Awen*) in the "Thinking" (*Chashabh*) done in secret. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Premeditated."

2 Seize them (we-ghā-zā-lū): The scrolls highlight the "Violent Root of the Greed." "Propriety" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Ownership" in "Seizing" (*Gazal*). It signify "The Connection between Power and Theft."

3 Sheep in a Pen (ke-tzōn bo-tzrāh): The scrolls highlight the "Collective Root of the Survival." "Isolation" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" the "Remnant" (*Shearith*) in a "Safe Enclosure" (*Botzrah*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Gathering."

4 The One who Breaks Open (hap-pō-rētz): Literally "The Breaker." The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Root of the Deliverance." "Confinement" is "Uprooted." The "Leader" is "Rooted" in "Breaking Through" (*Paratz*). It signify "The Divine Agency of Liberation."

5 Their King (mal-kām): The scrolls conclude with this "Monarchical Root of the Presence." "Anarchy" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Judgment on the Leaders" in *Micah 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Breaker." Follow the King.
