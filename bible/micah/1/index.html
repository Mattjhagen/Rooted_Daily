Micah 1

The Melting Mountains and the Howl of the Jackal.

The Lord is Coming

“Look! The Lord is coming from his dwelling place; the mountains melt beneath him.” 1

Heap of Rubble

“I will make Samaria a heap of rubble, a place for planting vineyards.” 2

The Gate of My People

“It has reached the very gate of my people, even to Jerusalem itself.” 3

The Howl

“I will howl like a jackal and moan like an owl.” 4
“Make yourself as bald as the vulture.” 5

Translation Notes

1 The Lord is Coming (hin-nē YHWH yō-tzē): The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Root of the Descent." "Static Transcendence" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Action" in "Stepping Forth" (*Yatza*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Shaking Earth."

2 Heap of Rubble (le-ī has-śā-dheh): Literally "A ruin of the field." The scrolls highlight the "Destructive Root of the City." "Civilization" is "Uprooted." Samaria is "Rooted" in "Rubble" (*Iy*). It signify "The Total Erasure of Samaria."

3 Gate of my People (sha-ar am-mī): The scrolls highlight the "Vulnerable Root of the Capitol." "Security" is "Uprooted." The "Plague" (*Makkah*) is "Rooted" in the "Gate" (*Sha-ar*) of Jerusalem. It signifies "The Imminent Danger to the Core."

4 Howl like a Jackal (ē-śeh mis-pēdh kat-tan-nīm): The scrolls highlight the "Primal Root of the Lament." "Composure" is "Uprooted." Micah "Roots" his "Sorrow" in the "Cry" (*Mispedh*) of "Jackals" (*Tannim*). It signify "The Rawness of the Grief."

5 Bald as the Vulture (har-chi-bhī qorchā-thēkh): The scrolls conclude with this "Exposing Root of the Mourning." "Dignity" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Woe to those who plot evil" in *Micah 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Melting Mountains." Mourn for your children.
