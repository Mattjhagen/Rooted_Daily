Micah 3

The Skinning of the People and the Plowing of Zion.

Meat from their Bones

“You tear the skin from my people and the meat from their bones.” 1
“They chop them up like meat for the pot.”

Night without Visions

“Night will come over you, without visions; the sun will set for the prophets.” 2

Filled with Power

“I am filled with power, with the Spirit of the Lord, and with justice.” 3

Zion Will Be Plowed

“Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become a heap of rubble.” 4
“Her leaders judge for a bribe.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Tear the Skin (gō-ze-lē ō-rām): The scrolls highlight the "Predatory Root of the Leadership." "Protection" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Rulership" in "Stripping" (*Gazal*) the "Skin" (*Or*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Cannibalistic Governance."

2 Sun will set (ū-bhā-āh hash-she-mesh): The scrolls highlight the "Obscuring Root of the Silence." "Light" is "Uprooted." The "Prophets" (*Nebhiim*) are "Rooted" in "Darkness" (*Chashakh*). It signify "The Cessation of the Divine Word."

3 Filled with Power (mā-lē-thī khō-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Root of the Authentic Prophet." "Fear" is "Uprooted." Micah is "Rooted" in "Spirit" (*Ruach*) and "Might" (*Ghebhurah*). It signifies "The Moral Authority of the Truth-Teller."

4 Zion Plowed (tzi-yōn śā-dheh tē-chā-rēsh): The scrolls highlight the "Agricultural Root of the Destruction." "Urban Glory" is "Uprooted." Zion will be "Rooted" in "Plowing" (*Charash*). It signify "The Return of the Holy to the Wild."

5 Judge for a Bribe (be-shō-chadh yish-pō-tū): The scrolls conclude with this "Mercenary Root of the Decay." "Justice" is "Uprooted" by "Money." It marks the transition to "The Future Glory of Zion" in *Micah 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Skinning." The hill will fall.
