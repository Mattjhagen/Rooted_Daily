Micah 4

The Highest Mountain and the Vine of Peace.

The Highest Mountain

“The mountain of the Lord’s temple will be established as the highest of the mountains.” 1

Swords into Plowshares

“They will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks.” 2

Under the Vine

“Everyone will sit under their own vine and under their own fig tree.” 3

To Babylon

“You will go to Babylon; there you will be redeemed.” 4
“I will give you horns of iron.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Highest of the Mountains (be-rōsh he-hā-rīm): Literally "At the head of the mountains." The scrolls highlight the "Elevated Root of the Temple." "Lowliness" is "Uprooted." Zion is "Rooted" as the "Chief" (*Rosh*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Spiritual Preeminence."

2 Swords into Plowshares (le-it-tīm): The scrolls highlight the "Transformative Root of Peace." "Violence" is "Uprooted." The "Cherebh" (*Sword*) is "Rooted" in the "Soil" as a "Plowshare" (*Et*). It signify "The Alchemy of the Kingdom."

3 Under the Vine (ta-chath gaph-nō): The scrolls highlight the "Peaceful Root of the Estate." "Fear" is "Uprooted." Each soul is "Rooted" under their own "Vine" (*Gephen*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Personal Security and Abundance."

4 To Babylon (adh-Bā-bhel): The scrolls highlight the "Exilic Root of the Redemption." "Home" is "Uprooted" for a time. But "Redemption" (*Gaal*) is "Rooted" in the "Far Land." It signify "The Saving that comes through the Furnace."

5 Horns of Iron (qe-ren bar-zel): The scrolls conclude with this "Potent Root of the Victory." "Weakness" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Ruler from Bethlehem" in *Micah 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Highest Mountain." No one will make them afraid.
