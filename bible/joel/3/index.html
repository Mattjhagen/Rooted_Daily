Joel 3

The Valley of Decision and the Mountains of New Wine.

Valley of Jehoshaphat

“I will gather all nations and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.” 1

Plowshares into Swords

“Beat your plowshares into swords and your pruning hooks into spears.” 2

The Valley of Decision

“Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision!” 3

Dripping New Wine

“In that day the mountains will drip new wine.” 4
“For the Lord dwells in Zion.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Valley of Jehoshaphat (ē-meq Ye-hō-shā-phāt): Literally "The Valley of YHWH Judges." The scrolls highlight the "Legal Root of the Judgment." "Chaos" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" the "Outcome" of the "Nations" (*Goyim*) in this specific "Place" (*Emeq*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Final Court."

2 Plowshares into Swords (it-tē-khem le-cha-rā-bhōth): The scrolls highlight the "Martial Root of the Preparation." "Agriculture" is "Uprooted." The "Tools of Life" are "Rooted" in "War" (*Cherebh*). It signify "The Intensity of the Day of the Lord."

3 Valley of Decision (ē-meq he-chā-rūtz): Literally "Valley of the Sharp Cut/Decision." The scrolls highlight the "Incised Root of the Verdict." "Indecision" is "Uprooted." The "Multitudes" (*Hamonim*) are "Rooted" in the "Final Cut" (*Charutz*). It signifies "The Spiritual Threshold."

4 Drip New Wine (yit-te-phū he-hā-rīm ā-sīs): The scrolls highlight the "Effusive Root of the Restoration." "Scarcity" is "Uprooted." The "Mountains" (*Harim*) are "Rooted" in "Abundance" (*Asis*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Kingdom Age."

5 Dwells in Zion (shō-khēn be-tzi-yōn): The scrolls conclude with this "Residential Root of the Presence." "Alienation" is "Uprooted." God is "Rooted" in "Zion" forever. It marks the end of *Joel* and the transition to *Amos 1*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The New Wine." The Lord has returned.
