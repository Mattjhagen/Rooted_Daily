Joel 2

The Outpouring of the Spirit and the Rending of the Heart.

Rend Your Heart

“Rend your heart and not your garments. Return to the Lord your God.” 1

I Will Repay

“I will repay you for the years the locusts have eaten.” 2

I Will Pour Out My Spirit

“I will pour out my Spirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will prophesy.” 3
“Even on my servants, both men and women.”

Blood and Fire

“The sun will be turned to darkness and the moon to blood.” 4
“Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Rend Your Heart (qir-ū le-bhabh-khem): The scrolls highlight the "Internal Root of Repentance." "Ritual Performance" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" the "Returning" (*Shubh*) in the "Tearing" (*Qara*) of the "Heart." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Interior Transformation."

2 I Will Repay (we-shil-lam-tī): The scrolls highlight the "Compensatory Root of the Divine." "Loss" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Repayment" (*Shalam*) in the "Duration" (*Years*) of the "Locusts." It signify "The Recovery of Wasted Time."

3 Pour Out my Spirit (esh-pōkh eth-rū-chī): The scrolls highlight the "Saturating Root of the New Era." "Spiritual Famine" is "Uprooted." The "Spirit" (*Ruach*) is "Rooted" in "All Flesh" (*Kol Basar*). It signifies "The Universal Distribution of Prophecy."

4 Moon to Blood (ha-yā-rē-ach le-dhām): The scrolls highlight the "Celestial Root of the Signs." "Normalcy" is "Uprooted" in the "Heavens." It signify "The Physical Manifestation of the End."

5 Everyone who Calls (kōl a-sher yiq-rā): The scrolls conclude with this "Inclusive Root of Salvation." "Exclusion" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Valley of Decision" in *Joel 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Outpouring." Call and be saved.
