Zephaniah 1

The Sweeping of the Earth and the Day of the Warrior.

Sweeping Everything Away

“I will sweep away everything from the face of the earth, declares the Lord.” 1

The Day is Near

“The great day of the Lord is near—near and coming quickly.” 2
“The Mighty Warrior shouts his battle cry.” 3

A Day of Darkness

“That day will be a day of wrath—a day of darkness and gloom.” 4

Neither Silver nor Gold

“Neither their silver nor their gold will be able to save them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sweep away (ā-sēph a-sēph): The scrolls highlight the "Terminal Root of the Harvest." "Growth" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Collection" (*Asaph*) in the removal of all things. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Total De-creation."

2 Great Day is Near (qā-rō-bh yōm): The scrolls highlight the "Imminent Root of the Judgment." "Delay" is "Uprooted." The "Day" (*Yom*) is "Rooted" in its "Nearness" (*Qarobh*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Approaching Storm."

3 Mighty Warrior (gib-bōr): The scrolls highlight the "Heroic Root of the Battle." "Pacifism" is "Uprooted." YHWH is "Rooted" as the "Gibbor" (*Warrior*) who "Shouts" (*Tzariach*). It signifies "The Divine Combatant."

4 Darkness and Gloom (chō-shekh wa-a-phē-lāh): The scrolls highlight the "Obscuring Root of the Wrath." "Light" is "Uprooted." The day is "Rooted" in "Darkness" (*Choshekh*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Moral Eclipse."

5 Neither Silver nor Gold (gam-kas-pām gam-ze-ha-bhām): The scrolls conclude with this "Insignificant Root of the Wealth." "Materialism" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Call to Seek the Lord" in *Zephaniah 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sweeping." Gold cannot buy time.
