Zechariah 7

Hollow Fasting and the Weight of Mercy.

The Question of Ritual

“Should I mourn and fast as I have done for so many years?” 1
“Was it really for me that you fasted?”

Administer True Justice

“Administer true justice; show mercy and compassion to one another.” 2
“Do not oppress the widow or the fatherless.” 3

Hearts of Flint

“They refused to pay attention... they made their hearts as hard as flint.” 4

Scattered by the Wind

“I scattered them with a whirlwind among all the nations.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Mourn and Fast (ib-bak-keh we-hin-nā-zēr): The scrolls highlight the "Performative Root of the Piety." "Sincerity" is "Uprooted." The "Fast" (*Nazar*) is "Rooted" in "Custom" rather than "Connection." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Empty Ritual."

2 True Justice (mish-pat e-meth): Literally "Judgment of Truth." The scrolls highlight the "Authentic Root of the Morality." "Hypocrisy" is "Uprooted." Justice is "Rooted" in "Emeth" (*Truth*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Ethical Bedrock."

3 Do not Oppress (we-al ta-a-shō-qū): The scrolls highlight the "Caring Root of the Community." "Exploitation" is "Uprooted." The "Widow" (*Almanah*) and "Poor" (*Ani*) are "Rooted" in the "Protection" of the Law. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Sacred Social Fabric."

4 Hard as Flint (shā-mīr): The scrolls highlight the "Impenetrable Root of the Rebellion." "Receptivity" is "Uprooted." The "Heart" (*Lebh*) is "Rooted" in "Diamond-hardness" (*Shamir*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Callous Soul."

5 Scattered (e-sa-a-rēm): The scrolls conclude with this "Atmospheric Root of the Judgment." "Stability" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Promise of Restoration" in *Zechariah 8*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Fast." Mercy is the only way back.
