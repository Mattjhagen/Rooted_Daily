Zechariah 6

The Bronze Mountains and the Crowning of the Branch.

Mountains of Bronze

“Four chariots coming out from between two mountains—mountains of bronze!” 1

The Spirits of Heaven

“These are the four spirits of heaven, going out from the presence of the Lord.” 2

The Gold and Silver Crown

“Make a crown, and set it on the head of Joshua.” 3
“Here is the man whose name is the Branch.”

The Priest-King

“He will be a priest on his throne. And there will be harmony between the two.” 4
“Those who are far away will come and build.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Mountains of Bronze (ha-rē ne-chō-sheth): The scrolls highlight the "Impenetrable Root of the Sovereignty." "Fragility" is "Uprooted." The "Mountains" (*Harim*) are "Rooted" in "Bronze" (*Nechosheth*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Eternal Foundation."

2 Spirits of Heaven (ar-ba rū-chōth ha-shā-ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Elemental Root of the Agency." "Isolation" is "Uprooted." They are "Rooted" in the "Presence" (*Al-yatzibh*) of the Lord. It signify "The Aesthetic of the Cosmic Oversight."

3 Make a Crown (a-śī-thā a-tā-rōth): The scrolls highlight the "Regal Root of the Priesthood." "Fragmentation" is "Uprooted." The "Crown" (*Atarah*) is "Rooted" in "Silver" (*Keseph*) and "Gold" (*Zahabh*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the United Office."

4 Harmony between the Two (a-tzath shā-lōm): Literally "The Counsel of Peace." The scrolls highlight the "Integrated Root of the Governance." "Conflict" is "Uprooted." The "Priest" (*Kohen*) and the "King" (*Moshel*) are "Rooted" in "Shalom." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Synthesis."

5 Far Away (re-chō-qīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Inclusive Root of the Expansion." "Exclusion" is "Uprooted." It marks the end of the night visions and the transition to "Justice and Mercy" in *Zechariah 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Chariots." The world is coming.
