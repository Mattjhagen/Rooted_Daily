Zechariah 4

The Golden Lampstand and the Spirit over Might.

The Solid Gold Lampstand

“I see a solid gold lampstand with a bowl at the top and seven lamps on it.” 1

Not by Might

“Not by might nor by power, but by my Spirit,” says the Lord. 2

The Day of Small Things

“Who dares despise the day of small things?” 3

The Two Anointed Ones

“These are the two who are anointed to serve the Lord of all the earth.” 4
“God bless it! God bless it!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Lampstand (me-nō-rath): The scrolls highlight the "Illuminative Root of the Testimony." "Darkness" is "Uprooted." The "Menorah" is "Rooted" in the "Bowl" (*Gullah*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Golden Witness."

2 By my Spirit (be-rū-chī): The scrolls highlight the "Invisible Root of the Efficacy." "Human Effort" is "Uprooted." Victory is "Rooted" in the "Ruach" (*Spirit*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Energy."

3 Small Things (qe-tan-nōth): The scrolls highlight the "Humble Root of the Magnitude." "Elitism" is "Uprooted." Greatness is "Rooted" in the "Small" (*Qatan*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Minute Beginning."

4 Anointed Ones (be-nē hay-yi-tz-hār): Literally "Sons of Fresh Oil." The scrolls highlight the "Liquid Root of the Election." "Stagnation" is "Uprooted." They are "Rooted" in the "Oil" (*Yitzhar*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Flowing Service."

5 God bless it (chēn chēn): Literally "Grace, Grace." The scrolls conclude with this "Gratuitous Root of the Completion." "Merit" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Flying Scroll" in *Zechariah 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Gold Lampstand." It is all grace.
