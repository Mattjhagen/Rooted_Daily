Zechariah 11

The Broken Staffs and the Price of the Shepherd.

Open Your Doors, Lebanon

“Open your doors, Lebanon, so that fire may devour your cedars!” 1

Favor and Union

“I took two staffs and called one Favor and the other Union.” 2
“Then I took my staff called Favor and broke it.”

Thirty Pieces of Silver

“So they paid me thirty pieces of silver.” 3
“Throw it to the potter—the handsome price at which they valued me!” 4

The Worthless Shepherd

“Woe to the worthless shepherd, who deserts the flock!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Fire devour (we-tō-khal ēsh): The scrolls highlight the "Destructive Root of the Arrogance." "Impenetrability" is "Uprooted." The "Cedars" (*Erez*) are "Rooted" in "Judgment" (*Esh*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Falling Mighty."

2 Favor (nō-am): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of the Grace." "Discord" is "Uprooted." The "Staff" is "Rooted" in "Pleasantness" (*Noam*). Its breaking signify "The Aesthetic of the Withdrawn Blessing."

3 Thirty Pieces of Silver (she-lō-shīm kā-seph): The scrolls highlight the "Calculated Root of the Rejection." "Value" is "Uprooted." The "Price" (*Sekhar*) is "Rooted" in the "Slave's Worth." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Divine Insult."

4 Throw it to the Potter (hash-lī-khē-hū el-hay-yō-tzēr): The scrolls highlight the "Industrial Root of the Disposal." "Sacredness" is "Uprooted." The "Potter" (*Yotzer*) is "Rooted" in "Common Clay." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Profane Transaction."

5 Worthless Shepherd (rō-ē ha-e-līl): The scrolls conclude with this "Negligent Root of the Failure." "Care" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Cup of Staggering" in *Zechariah 12*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Broken Staffs." The price is paid.
