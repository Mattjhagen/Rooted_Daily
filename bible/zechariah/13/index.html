Zechariah 13

The Fountain of Cleansing and the Refiner’s Fire.

The Fountain Opened

“On that day a fountain will be opened... to cleanse them from sin.” 1

Strike the Shepherd

“Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.” 2
“The wounds I was given at the house of my friends.” 3

Refined Like Silver

“I will refine them like silver and test them like gold.” 4
“I will say, ‘They are my people,’ and they will say, ‘The Lord is our God.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 Fountain (mā-qōr): The scrolls highlight the "Effluent Root of the Purity." "Filth" is "Uprooted." The "Fountain" (*Maqor*) is "Rooted" in the "Source of Life." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Endless Cleansing."

2 Strike the Shepherd (hak eth-har-rō-eh): The scrolls highlight the "Sacrificial Root of the Scattering." "Protection" is "Uprooted." The "Shepherd" is "Rooted" in "The Man close to Me" (*Geber amiti*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Blow."

3 House of my Friends (bēth me-a-ha-bhāy): The scrolls highlight the "Betrayal Root of the Intimacy." "Loyalty" is "Uprooted." The "Wounds" (*Makkoth*) are "Rooted" in the "House of Lovers." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Close Wound."

4 Refine (ū-tzeraph-tīm): The scrolls highlight the "Metallurgical Root of the Sanctification." "Dross" is "Uprooted." The "Remnant" is "Rooted" in the "Furnace." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Precious Quality."

5 They are My People (am-mī hū): The scrolls conclude with this "Covenantal Root of the Belonging." "Alienation" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Day of the Lord" in *Zechariah 14*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Fountain Opened." You are His.
