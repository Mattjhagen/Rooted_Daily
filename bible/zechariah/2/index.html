Zechariah 2

The Infinite City and the Apple of the Eye.

A City without Walls

“Jerusalem will be a city without walls because of the great number of people.” 1

A Wall of Fire

“I myself will be a wall of fire around it.” 2
“And I will be its glory within.”

The Apple of His Eye

“Whoever touches you touches the apple of his eye.” 3

Shout and Be Glad

“Shout and be glad, Daughter Zion. For I am coming.” 4
“Be silent before the Lord, all humanity.” 5

Translation Notes

1 City without Walls (pe-rā-zōth): The scrolls highlight the "Unbounded Root of the Growth." "Limitation" is "Uprooted." Jerusalem is "Rooted" in "Openness" (*Perazoth*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Infinite City."

2 Wall of Fire (chō-math ēsh): The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of the Defense." "Bricks and Mortar" are "Uprooted." God's "Wall" (*Chomah*) is "Rooted" in "Fire" (*Esh*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Invincible Barrier."

3 Apple of his Eye (be-bhāth ē-nō): Literally "The Pupil of the Eye." The scrolls highlight the "Sensitive Root of the Affection." "Indifference" is "Uprooted." The people are "Rooted" in the "Pupil" (*Babah*) of the Divine Gaze. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Sacred Vulnerability."

4 I am Coming (hin-nī-bhā): The scrolls highlight the "Imminent Root of the Presence." "Absence" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Arrival" (*Bo*) in the "Amidst" (*Tawekh*) of Zion. It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Inhabitation."

5 Roused Himself (nē-ōr): The scrolls conclude with this "Awakening Root of the Majesty." "Slumber" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Cleaning of the High Priest" in *Zechariah 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Measuring Line." He is here.
