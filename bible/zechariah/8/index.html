Zechariah 8

The City of Truth and the Streets of Joy.

The City of Truth

“Jerusalem will be called the City of Truth.” 1

Streets Filled with Play

“The city streets will be filled with boys and girls playing there.” 2

Festivals of Gladness

“The fasts... will become joyful and glad occasions.” 3
“Therefore love truth and peace.”

Ten Men and the Robe

“Ten people from all languages will take firm hold of the hem of a robe.” 4
“Let us go with you, because God is with you.” 5

Translation Notes

1 City of Truth (īr ha-e-meth): The scrolls highlight the "Veracity Root of the Inhabitation." "Deception" is "Uprooted." The "City" (*Ir*) is "Rooted" in "Truth" (*Emeth*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Moral Capital."

2 Playing there (me-śā-cha-qīm): The scrolls highlight the "Vibrant Root of the Security." "Fear" is "Uprooted." The "Children" (*Yeladim*) are "Rooted" in "Laughter" (*Sachaq*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Redeemed Public Space."

3 Joyful Occasions (le-śā-śōn ū-le-śim-chāh): The scrolls highlight the "Transformative Root of the Sorrow." "Grief" is "Uprooted." The "Fast" is "Rooted" in "Exultation" (*Sason*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Perpetual Celebration."

4 Ten People (a-śā-rā a-nā-shīm): The scrolls highlight the "Magnetic Root of the Zion." "Isolation" is "Uprooted." The "Nations" (*Goyim*) are "Rooted" in the "Hem" (*Kanaph*) of the garment. It signify "The Aesthetic of the Global Attraction."

5 God is with you (im-mā-khem e-lō-hīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Immanuel Root of the Testimony." "Absence" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Coming King" in *Zechariah 9*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The City of Truth." They see it in you.
