Zechariah 10

The Spring Rain and the Whistle of Redemption.

The Rain in Springtime

“Ask the Lord for rain in the springtime; it is the Lord who sends the thunderstorms.” 1

Cornerstone and Tent Peg

“From Judah will come the cornerstone, from him the tent peg.” 2

The Whistle of the Shepherd

“I will whistle for them and gather them in. I will surely redeem them.” 3

Walking in His Name

“In his name they will live, declares the Lord.” 4
“I will strengthen them in the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Rain in Springtime (ma-tār be-ēth mal-qōsh): The scrolls highlight the "Seasonal Root of the Provision." "Scarcity" is "Uprooted." The "Rain" (*Matar*) is "Rooted" in the "Latter Rain" (*Malqosh*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Timely Blessing."

2 Cornerstone (pin-nāh): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of the Kingdom." "Instability" is "Uprooted." The "Cornerstone" is "Rooted" in "Judah" (*Yehudah*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Unshakable Foundation."

3 I will Whistle (esh-re-qāh lā-hem): The scrolls highlight the "Acoustic Root of the Gathering." "Dispersion" is "Uprooted." The "Whistle" (*Sharaq*) is "Rooted" in the "Shepherd's Call." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Recall."

4 In His Name (ū-bhish-mō yit-hal-lā-khū): Literally "And in His Name they shall walk." The scrolls highlight the "Kinetic Root of the Faith." "Stagnation" is "Uprooted." They are "Rooted" in the "Walk" (*Halak*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Active Devotion."

5 I will Strengthen (we-gib-bar-tīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Potent Root of the Empowerment." "Weakness" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Two Staffs" in *Zechariah 11*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Spring Rain." Strength is your inheritance.
