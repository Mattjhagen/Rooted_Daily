Zechariah 1

The Horses in the Ravine and the Craftsmen of Doom.

Return to Me

“Return to me, declares the Lord, and I will return to you.” 1

Red Horse among Myrtles

“There before me was a man mounted on a red horse! He was standing among the myrtle trees.” 2

Jealous for Zion

“I am very jealous for Jerusalem and Zion.” 3

The Four Craftsmen

“Then the Lord showed me four craftsmen.” 4
“To throw down the horns of the nations.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Return (shū-bhū): The scrolls highlight the "Reciprocal Root of the Relationship." "Distance" is "Uprooted." To "Return" (*Shub*) is the "Root" of the encounter. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Spiritual U-Turn."

2 Among the Myrtles (bēn ha-had-das-sīm): The scrolls highlight the "Fragrant Root of the Vigilance." "Neglect" is "Uprooted." The "Myrtles" (*Hadassim*) in the "Ravine" (*Metzulah*) signify "The Aesthetic of the Secret Observation."

3 Jealous (qin-nē-thī): The scrolls highlight the "Intense Root of the Devotion." "Indifference" is "Uprooted." God is "Rooted" in a "Great Jealousy" (*Qinah Ghedolah*). It signify "The Divine Fire for the Beloved."

4 Four Craftsmen (ar-bā-ā cha-rā-shīm): The scrolls highlight the "Constructive Root of the Deconstruction." "Tyranny" is "Uprooted." The "Horns" (*Qeranoth*) are "Rooted" in "Shattering" by the "Workers" (*Charashim*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Divine Counter-force."

5 Throw down (le-had-dōth): The scrolls conclude with this "Violent Root of the Justice." "Injustice" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Measuring Line" in *Zechariah 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Horses." Watch the ravine.
