Zechariah 9

The Lowly King and the Prisoners of Hope.

Rejoice Greatly

“See, your king comes to you... lowly and riding on a donkey.” 1

Peace to the Nations

“He will proclaim peace to the nations. His rule will extend from sea to sea.” 2

Prisoners of Hope

“Return to your fortress, you prisoners of hope.” 3
“I will restore twice as much to you.”

Jewels in a Crown

“They will sparkle in his land like jewels in a crown.” 4
“How attractive and beautiful they will be!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Lowly (ā-nī): The scrolls highlight the "Submissive Root of the Majesty." "Hubris" is "Uprooted." The "King" (*Melekh*) is "Rooted" in "Affliction" (*Ani*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Humble Triumph."

2 Proclaim Peace (we-dib-ber shā-lōm): The scrolls highlight the "Verbal Root of the Diplomacy." "Violence" is "Uprooted." His "Rule" (*Moshlo*) is "Rooted" in "Shalom." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Universal Harmony."

3 Prisoners of Hope (a-śī-rē hat-tiq-wāh): The scrolls highlight the "Expectant Root of the Liberation." "Despair" is "Uprooted." They are "Rooted" in the "Cord" (*Tiqwah*) of hope. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Certain Future."

4 Jewels in a Crown (ab-nē nē-zer): Literally "Stones of a Diadem." The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of the Election." "Commonness" is "Uprooted." The "People" are "Rooted" in "Sparkling" (*Mit-nō-śe-śōth*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Adornment."

5 How Attractive (mah tō-bhō): The scrolls conclude with this "Exclamatory Root of the Beauty." "Ugliness" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Lord's Care" in *Zechariah 10*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Lowly King." Beauty is restored.
