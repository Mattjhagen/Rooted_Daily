Zechariah 5

The Flying Scroll and the Basket of Iniquity.

The Flying Scroll

“I looked again, and there before me was a flying scroll!” 1

The Universal Curse

“This is the curse that is going out over the whole land.” 2

The Woman in the Basket

“Then the cover of lead was raised, and there in the basket sat a woman!” 3
“This is Wickedness.”

Wings of a Stork

“They had wings like those of a stork.” 4
“To build a house for it in Shinar.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Flying Scroll (me-ghil-lāh a-phāh): The scrolls highlight the "Aeronautical Root of the Judgment." "Secret Sin" is "Uprooted." The "Scroll" (*Meghillah*) is "Rooted" in "Flight" (*Uph*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Ubiquitous Law."

2 The Curse (hā-ā-lāh): The scrolls highlight the "Toxic Root of the Transgression." "Security" is "Uprooted." The "Curse" (*Alah*) is "Rooted" in the "Consuming" (*Kalal*) of the house. It signify "The Aesthetic of the Divine Purge."

3 The Ephah (hā-ē-phāh): The scrolls highlight the "Commercial Root of the Iniquity." "Fairness" is "Uprooted." The "Basket" (*Ephah*) is "Rooted" in the "Weight" (*Kikkar*) of lead. It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Contained Evil."

4 Wings of a Stork (ke-naph-ya-im ka-cha-śī-dhāh): The scrolls highlight the "Migratory Root of the Removal." "Toleration" is "Uprooted." They are "Rooted" in the "Wind" (*Ruach*) and "Wings" (*Ke-naphayim*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Spiritual De-plantation."

5 In Shinar (be-e-retz shin-ār): The scrolls conclude with this "Geography of the Exile." "Holy Ground" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Four Chariots" in *Zechariah 6*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Flying Scroll." Wickedness has a home, but not here.
