Zechariah 14

The Day of the Lord and the Reign of the King.

The Feet on the Mount

“On that day his feet will stand on the Mount of Olives.” 1
“And the Mount of Olives will be split in two.”

King Over All the Earth

“The Lord will be king over the whole earth.” 2
“On that day there will be one Lord, and his name the only name.” 3

Living Waters

“On that day living water will flow out from Jerusalem.” 4

Holy to the Lord

“HOLY TO THE LORD” 5
Inscribed even upon the bells of the horses.

Translation Notes

1 Mount of Olives (har ha-zē-thīm): The scrolls highlight the "Geological Root of the Return." "Distance" is "Uprooted." The "Feet" (*Raglav*) are "Rooted" in the "Mountain." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Physical Presence."

2 King (me-lekh): The scrolls highlight the "Political Root of the Divine." "Chaos" is "Uprooted." The "Kingship" (*Malkuth*) is "Rooted" in the "Whole Earth." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Universal Order."

3 His Name the only Name (ū-she-mō e-chād): Literally "And His Name One." The scrolls highlight the "Nominal Root of the Unity." "Plurality" is "Uprooted." The "Name" (*Shem*) is "Rooted" in "Oneness" (*Echad*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Absolute Monotheism."

4 Living Water (ma-yim chay-yīm): The scrolls highlight the "Hydrological Root of the Life." "Stagnation" is "Uprooted." The "Water" (*Mayim*) is "Rooted" in "Vitality" (*Chay*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the Eternal Flow."

5 Holy to the Lord (qo-desh lay-hō-wāh): The scrolls conclude the book with this "Inscriptional Root of the Totality." "Profanity" is "Uprooted." Every "Common Pot" is "Rooted" in "Holiness." It marks the transition to the final oracle of the Minor Prophets in *Malachi*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Final Day." Everything is Sacred.
