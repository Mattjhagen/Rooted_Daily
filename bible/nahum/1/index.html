Nahum 1

The Whirlwind of Justice and the Stronghold of Peace.

Dust of His Feet

“His way is in the whirlwind and the storm, and clouds are the dust of his feet.” 1

The Lord is a Stronghold

“The Lord is good, a refuge in times of trouble.” 2
“He cares for those who trust in him.” 3

Overwhelming Flood

“With an overwhelming flood he will make an end of Nineveh.” 4

Feet on the Mountains

“Look, there on the mountains, the feet of one who brings good news.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Dust of his Feet (a-bhaq ragh-lāw): The scrolls highlight the "Meteorological Root of the Presence." "Inertia" is "Uprooted." God’s "Path" (*Derekh*) is "Rooted" in the "Whirlwind" (*Suphah*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Divine Motion."

2 Stronghold (mā-ōz): The scrolls highlight the "Defensive Root of the Mercy." "Vulnerability" is "Uprooted." God is "Rooted" as a "Fortress" (*Maoz*) in the "Day of Trouble" (*Yom Tzarah*). It signifies "The Sacred Asylum."

3 He cares (yo-dhē-a): Literally "He knows." The scrolls highlight the "Relational Root of the Trust." "Anonymity" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Knowledge" in those who "Seek Refuge" (*Chose*). It signify "The Intimacy of the Stronghold."

4 Overwhelming Flood (she-teph o-bhēr): The scrolls highlight the "Liquid Root of the Judgment." "Solid Walls" are "Uprooted." Nineveh’s "End" (*Kalah*) is "Rooted" in the "Flood" (*Sheteph*). It signifies "The Unstoppable Force of History."

5 Feet on the Mountains (ragh-lē me-bhas-śēr): The scrolls conclude with this "Heraldic Root of the Peace." "Terror" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Assault on Nineveh" in *Nahum 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Stronghold." Proclaim peace.
