Jeremiah 2

The Broken Cisterns and the Cravings of the Desert.

The Devotion of Youth

“I remember the devotion of your youth, how as a bride you loved me.” 1

Two Sins

“They have forsaken me, the spring of living water.” 2
“They have dug their own cisterns, broken cisterns that cannot hold water.” 3

Sniffing the Wind

“A wild donkey accustomed to the desert, sniffing the wind in her craving.” 4

Worthless Idols

“They followed worthless idols and became worthless themselves.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Devotion of your Youth (che-sedh ne-ū-rā-yi-kh): In the scrolls, this is the "Marital Root of the Covenant." Israel was "Rooted" in "Covenant Love" (*Chesed*) during the wilderness. It signifies "The Golden Age of Faith."

2 Spring of Living Water (me-qōr ma-yim chay-yīm): The scrolls highlight the "Hydrological Root of Life." God is the "Origin" (*Meqor*) of "Running Water." It signify "The Continuous Supply of the Spirit."

3 Broken Cisterns (be-ō-rōth nish-bā-rīm): The scrolls highlight the "Architectural Root of Futility." Human "Security" is "Rooted" in "Fractured Stone" (*Broken*) that cannot "Hold" (*Kūn*). It signifies "The Failure of Self-Reliance."

4 Sniffing the Wind (shā-a-phah rū-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Instinctual Root of Sin." Unfaithfulness is "Rooted" in "Uncontrollable Appetite" (*Sniffing*). It signify "The Loss of Volition."

5 Became Worthless (way-yeh-bā-lū): The scrolls conclude with this "Identity Root." Following "Vanity" (*He-bhel*) "Roots" "Vanity" in the "Self." It marks the transition to "The Call to Return" in *Jeremiah 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Thirsty Soul." Come back to the spring.
