Jeremiah 13

The Ruined Belt and the Unchangeable Spots.

The Linen Belt

“Buy a linen belt and put it around your waist.”
“I dug up the belt... it was ruined and completely useless.” 1

Darkening Mountains

“Before your feet stumble on the darkening mountains.” 2

The Leopard’s Spots

“Can an Ethiopian change his skin or a leopard its spots?” 3
“Neither can you do good who are accustomed to doing evil.”

Scattered like Chaff

“I will scatter you like chaff driven by the desert wind.” 4
“This is your lot, the portion I have decreed for you.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Ruined and Useless (nish-chath... lō yitz-lach lak-kōl): In the scrolls, this is the "Structural Root of Decay." The "Judah" represented by the "Belt" (*Ezōr*) is "Uprooted" from its "Beauty" and "Rooted" in "Corruption" (*Shachath*). It signifies "The Deterioration of the Chosen."

2 Darkening Mountains (ha-rē nā-sheph): Literally "Mountains of the Twilight." The scrolls highlight the "Spatial Root of Judgment." "Guidance" is "Uprooted" and "Stumbling" (*Nagaph*) is "Rooted" in the "Shadows." It signify "The Imminent Loss of Clarity."

3 Leopard its Spots (na-mēr cha-bar-bu-rō-thāw): The scrolls highlight the "Inherent Root of Nature." "Change" is "Uprooted" by "Long-term Habit" (*Accustomed*). It signifies "The Spiritual Entropy of Persistent Sin."

4 Scattered like Chaff (e-phī-tzēm ke-qash): The scrolls highlight the "Aerodynamic Root of Exile." "Stability" is "Uprooted" and the "People" are "Rooted" in the "Inconsistency" of the "Wind." It signify "The Loss of the Homeland."

5 Portion I have Decreed (me-nath mid-day-yi-kh): The scrolls conclude with this "Measuring Root." "Fate" is "Rooted" in the "Measured Portion" (*Menath*) of God’s justice. It marks the transition to "The Drought" in *Jeremiah 14*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Ruined Pride." Change while you can.
