Jeremiah 23

The Righteous Branch and the Hammer of the Word.

The Righteous Branch

“I will raise up for David a righteous Branch, a King who will reign wisely.” 1
“His name: The Lord Our Righteous Savior.” 2

Fire and Hammer

“Is not my word like fire, and like a hammer that breaks a rock in pieces?” 3

Stealing My Words

“I am against the prophets who steal from one another words supposedly from me.” 4

The Burden of the Lord

“If they ask, ‘What is the burden of the Lord?’”
“Say to them: ‘You are the burden, and I will cast you off.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 Righteous Branch (tze-mah tzad-dīq): In the scrolls, this is the "Regal Root of Restoration." The "Corrupt Lineage" is "Uprooted" and a "New Growth" (*Tzemah*) is "Rooted" in "Justice" (*Tzedeq*). It signifies "The Messianic Succession."

2 The Lord Our Righteous Savior (Yah-weh Tzid-qē-nū): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Root of Imputed Holiness." "Human Failure" is "Uprooted" by "Divine Righteousness." It signify "The Character of the New Kingdom."

3 Hammer that breaks the Rock (we-khe-phat-tīsh ye-phō-tētz sā-la): The scrolls highlight the "Impactful Root of Truth." "Hardened Hearts" are "Uprooted" or "Shattered" by the "Word" (*Debhar*). It signifies "The Kinetic Energy of Revelation."

4 Steal my Words (me-ghan-ne-bhē de-bhā-ray): The scrolls highlight the "Plagiaristic Root of Falsehood." "Originality" is "Uprooted" as prophets "Copy" each other’s "Lies." It signify "The Echo Chamber of Deception."

5 You are the Burden (eth-mah mas-śā): This is a powerful wordplay. The scrolls highlight the "Semantic Root of Rejection." "Mas-śā" means both "Prophecy" and "Weight." The people who ask for a "Burden" are told *they* are the "Weight" that God will "Uproot" (*Cast off*). It marks the transition to "The Two Baskets of Figs" in *Jeremiah 24*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The True Branch." Justice shall reign.
