Jeremiah 3

The Faithless Sisters and the Heart of the Shepherd.

Should He Return?

“If a man divorces his wife and she leaves him...”
“Should he return to her again? Would not that land be completely defiled?” 1

Return, Faithless Israel

“‘Return, faithless Israel,’ declares the Lord, ‘I will frown on you no longer.’” 2

Shepherds After My Heart

“I will give you shepherds after my own heart, who will lead you with knowledge.” 3

Throne of the Lord

“They will call Jerusalem The Throne of the Lord.” 4
“All nations will gather in her name.”

Translation Notes

1 Return to her (ha-yā-shūbh ē-ley-hā): In the scrolls, this is the "Legal Root of Irreversibility." Under Mosaic law, a divorced wife could not "Root" back in her first husband. God "Uproots" this "Legal Barrier" to invite her back anyway. It signifies "Grace exceeding Law."

2 Frown on you no longer (lō aph-pīl pā-nay): Literally "I will not cause my face to fall." The scrolls highlight the "Facial Root of Favor." "Judgment" is "Uprooted" and "Countenance" is "Rooted" in "Kindness." It signify "The Divine Smile."

3 Shepherds after My Heart (rō-īm ke-lib-bī): The scrolls highlight the "Pastoral Root of Leadership." "Hirelings" are "Uprooted" and "True Guides" are "Rooted" in "God’s Internal Logic" (*Heart/Leb*). It signifies "The Messianic Governance."

4 Throne of the Lord (kis-sē Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Root of Location." "Jerusalem" is "Re-Rooted" as the "Seat of Power" (*Kisse*) for the whole earth. It signify "The Center of the Kingdom."

5 Cure you of Backsliding (er-pāh me-shū-bhō-thē-khem): The scrolls conclude with this "Medical Root." "Wandering" is "Uprooted" by "Divine Healing" (*Rapha*). It marks the transition to "The Alarm from the North" in *Jeremiah 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Returning Heart." He will heal you.
