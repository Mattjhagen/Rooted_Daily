Jeremiah 52

The End of the City and the Seat of the King.

Fire in the Temple

“He set fire to the temple of the Lord, the royal palace and all the houses of Jerusalem.” 1

The Bronze Sea

“The Babylonians broke up the bronze pillars and the bronze sea.”
“They carried all the bronze to Babylon.” 2

A Seat of Honor

“Evil-Merodach released Jehoiachin from prison.”
“He gave him a seat of honor higher than those of the other kings.” 3

Daily Allowance

“Day by day the king of Babylon gave Jehoiachin a regular allowance.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Set Fire to the Temple (way-yish-rōph eth-bēth Yah-weh): In the scrolls, this is the "Combustive Root of Uprooting." The "Dwelling Place" (*Beth*) of God is "Uprooted" from the "Soil." It signifies "The End of the First Temple Era."

2 The Bronze Sea (eth-yam han-ne-chō-sheth): The scrolls highlight the "Industrial Root of Plunder." The "Sea" (*Yam*) represented the "Taming of Chaos." "Order" is "Uprooted" and "Transported" to "Babylon." It signify "The Stripping of the Nation’s Glory."

3 Seat of Honor (kis-ō mi-le-mal-lā): Literally "His throne above the thrones." The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Root of Survival." Despite the "Uprooting" of the "City," the "Davidic Line" is "Rooted" in "Babylon" through "Jehoiachin." It signifies "The Survival of the Spark."

4 Daily Allowance (a-ru-chath tā-mīd): The scrolls conclude with this "Nutritional Root of Grace." "Starvation" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to the dirges of "Lamentations." It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The King’s Table." Hope is not dead.
