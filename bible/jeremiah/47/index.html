Jeremiah 47

The Torrent of the North and the Sword of the Sea.

Rising Waters

“See how the waters are rising in the north; they will become an overflowing torrent.” 1

Limp Hands

“Parents will not turn to help their children; their hands will hang limp.” 2

Sword of the Lord

“Alas, sword of the Lord, how long till you rest? Return to your sheath.” 3
“How can it rest when the Lord has given it a command?” 4

Translation Notes

1 Rising Waters (ma-yim o-līm): In the scrolls, this is the "Hydrological Root of Invasion." "Stability" is "Uprooted" by the "Flood" (*Torrent/Nachal*) of the "Babylonian Army." It signifies "The Inevitable Overflow of Judgment."

2 Limp Hands (ri-phe-yōn yā-dhā-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Paralytic Root of Panic." "Parental Instinct" is "Uprooted" by "Total Terror." It signify "The Biological Collapse of the Society."

3 Sheath (tha-re-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Restless Root of the Sword." "Peace" is "Uprooted." The "Sword" (*Cherebh*) refuses to be "Rooted" in its "Case" (*Sheath*) because the "Commission" is not finished. It signifies "The Kinetic Nature of Justice."

4 Given it a Command (tzū-wāh lāh): The scrolls conclude with this "Imperative Root." The "Sword" is "Rooted" in "Obedience" to Yahweh. It marks the transition to "Moab" in *Jeremiah 48*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unresting Sword." The waters rise.
