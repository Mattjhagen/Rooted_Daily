Jeremiah 31

The Law of the Heart and the Everlasting Love.

Everlasting Love

“I have loved you with an everlasting love; I have drawn you with unfailing kindness.” 1

Rachel Weeping

“A voice is heard in Ramah, mourning and great weeping.”
“Rachel weeping for her children.” 2

Watch to Build

“As I watched to uproot... so I will watch over them to build and to plant.” 3

The New Covenant

“I will make a new covenant with the people of Israel.” 4
“I will put my law in their minds and write it on their hearts.” 5
“They will all know me, from the least to the greatest.”

Translation Notes

1 Everlasting Love (a-ha-bhath ō-lām): In the scrolls, this is the "Perpetual Root of Devotion." "Conditional Love" is "Uprooted" and "Eternal Love" (*Olam*) is "Rooted" in the "Divine Nature." It signifies "The Irreversible Grace."

2 Rachel Weeping (Rā-chēl me-bha-kāh): The scrolls highlight the "Matriarchal Root of Sorrow." "Exile" is "Uprooted" from "Silence" through the "Voice of the Mother." It signify "The Grief of the Nation’s Birth."

3 Watch to Build and Plant (esh-qōdh... libh-nōth we-lin-tō-a): This is the fulfillment of the *Jeremiah 1:10* mandate. The "Destructive Gaze" is "Uprooted" and "Restorative Vigilance" (*Shā-qādh*) is "Rooted." It signifies "The Divine Focus on Reconstruction."

4 New Covenant (be-rīth cha-dhā-shāh): The scrolls highlight the "Novelty Root of the Covenant." The "External/Broken" is "Uprooted" and the "Internal/New" (*Chadashah*) is "Rooted." It signify "A Change in System."

5 Write it on their Hearts (we-al lib-bām ek-te-bhen-nāh): The scrolls conclude with this "Inscriptional Root of Identity." The "Stone Tablet" is "Uprooted" as the "Medium" for the "Law" (*Torah*), and the "Human Heart" (*Leb*) is "Rooted" as the "New Scroll." It marks the transition to "The Field of Anathoth" in *Jeremiah 32*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Internal Torah." All shall know Him.
