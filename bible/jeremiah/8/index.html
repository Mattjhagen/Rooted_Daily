Jeremiah 8

The Lying Pen and the Summer that Ended.

The Lying Pen

“Actually the lying pen of the scribes has handled it falsely.” 1

Peace, Peace

“They dress the wound of my people as though it were not serious.”
“‘Peace, peace,’ they say, when there is no peace.” 2

The Summer has Ended

“The harvest is past, the summer has ended, and we are not saved.” 3

Balm in Gilead

“Since my people are crushed, I am crushed.”
“Is there no balm in Gilead? Is there no physician there?” 4
“Why then is there no healing?” 5

Translation Notes

1 Lying Pen of the Scribes (e-t she-qer sō-phe-rīm): In the scrolls, this is the "Literary Root of Deception." The "Law" is "Uprooted" by "False Interpretation" (*Sheqer*). It signifies "The Academic Betrayal of Truth."

2 Peace, Peace (Sha-lōm Sha-lōm): The scrolls highlight the "Medical Root of Negligence." The "Disease" of "Sin" is "Uprooted" from "Diagnosis" by "Superficial Comfort." It signify "The Danger of Optimistic Denial."

3 Harvest is Past (ā-bhar qā-tzīr): The scrolls highlight the "Seasonal Root of Opportunity." The "Time for Salvation" is "Uprooted" by "Delay." It signifies "The Tragedy of the Missed Moment."

4 Balm in Gilead (tzor-ī bhigh-le-ādh): The scrolls highlight the "Botanical Root of Healing." "Gilead" was "Rooted" in the "Production of Resin" (*Balm*) for medicine. It signify "The Availability of Grace vs. the Refusal to Apply it."

5 Is there no Physician (ēn rō-phē shām): The scrolls conclude with this "Somatic Root of Despair." "Healing" (*Arū-khāh*) is "Uprooted" from the "People." It marks the transition to "The Fountain of Tears" in *Jeremiah 9*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unhealed Wound." Search for the Balm.
