Jeremiah 5

The Search for One and the Yoke of Rebellion.

Find But One

“Go up and down the streets of Jerusalem.”
“See if you can find but one person who deals honestly and seeks the truth.” 1

Broken Yokes

“They made their faces harder than stone.”
“But with one accord they too had broken off the yoke.” 2

Eyes But No Sight

“Hear this, you foolish and senseless people, who have eyes but do not see.” 3

The People Love It

“The prophets prophesy lies, and my people love it this way.” 4
“But what will you do in the end?” 5

Translation Notes

1 Find But One (im-yēsh īsh): In the scrolls, this is the "Existential Root of Integrity." The "City" is "Rooted" in "Truth" (*Emunah*) only if its "Citizens" are. It signifies "The Moral Fragility of a Nation."

2 Broken off the Yoke (shā-bhe-rū ōl): The scrolls highlight the "Agricultural Root of Rebellion." The "Disciplined Path" (*Yoke*) is "Uprooted" by "Arrogance." It signify "The Rejection of Divine Guidance."

3 Eyes but do not See (ē-na-yim la-hem we-lō yir-ū): The scrolls highlight the "Sensory Root of Apostasy." "Perception" is "Uprooted" by "Willful Ignorance." It signifies "The Spiritual Coma of Judah."

4 My People Love It (am-mī ā-ha-bhū khēn): The scrolls highlight the "Appetitive Root of Deception." The "Community" is "Rooted" in the "Affirmation" of "Lies" (*Sheqer*). It signify "The Symbiosis of Corrupt Leaders and Complicit People."

5 What will you do in the End (ū-mah ta-a-śū le-a-cha-rī-thāh): The scrolls conclude with this "Teleological Root." "Consequence" is "Uprooted" from the "Present Mind" but "Rooted" in the "Finality" (*Acharith*). It marks the transition to "The Siege" in *Jeremiah 6*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Truth Search." Look to the end.
