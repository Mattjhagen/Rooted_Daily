Jeremiah 6

The Ancient Paths and the Refusal of Rest.

Disaster Looms

“Flee for safety, people of Benjamin!”
“For disaster looms out of the north, even terrible destruction.” 1

The Ancient Paths

“Stand at the crossroads and look; ask for the ancient paths.” 2
“Walk in it, and you will find rest for your souls.” 3

We Will Not Walk

“But you said, ‘We will not walk in it.’” 4

Rejected Silver

“The lead is consumed by the fire, but the dross is not removed.”
“They are called rejected silver, because the Lord has rejected them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Disaster Looms (rā-āh nish-qe-phāh): In the scrolls, this is the "Panoramic Root of Judgment." "Security" is "Uprooted" as the "North" (*Tzaphon*) "Leans" (*Nishqephah*) over the city. It signifies "The Impending Shadow."

2 Ancient Paths (ne-thi-bhōth ō-lām): The scrolls highlight the "Historical Root of Truth." The "Novelty" of idolatry is "Uprooted" to "Root" the "Soul" in the "Eternal" (*Olam*) "Paths." It signify "The Reliability of Tradition."

3 Rest for your Souls (mar-ghō-a le-naph-she-khem): The scrolls highlight the "Psychological Root of Obedience." "Anxiety" is "Uprooted" by "Finding" (*Matza*) the "Good Way" (*Derekh hat-tobh*). It signifies "The Restorative Nature of the Law."

4 We will Not Walk (lō nē-lēkh): The scrolls highlight the "Volitional Root of Rebellion." "Grace" is "Uprooted" by a "Collective Act of Will." It signifies "The Hardened Heart of Judah."

5 Rejected Silver (ke-seph mā-as): The scrolls conclude with this "Metallurgical Root." "Purity" is "Uprooted" by "Corrupted Ore" (*Dross*). It marks the transition to "The Temple Sermon" in *Jeremiah 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Ancient Path." Walk in it.
