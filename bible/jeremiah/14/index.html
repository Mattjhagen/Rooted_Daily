Jeremiah 14

The Parched Ground and the False Visions.

The Cracked Earth

“The ground is cracked because there is no rain in the land.” 1
“Wild donkeys stand on the barren heights and pant.”

Do Not Pray

“‘Do not pray for the well-being of this people.’” 2

Delusions of the Mind

“The prophets are prophesying lies... the delusions of their own minds.” 3

The True Rainmaker

“Do any of the worthless idols of the nations bring rain?”
“No, it is you, Lord our God. Therefore our hope is in you.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Ground is Cracked (ha-a-dhā-māh chat-thāh): In the scrolls, this is the "Biological Root of Despair." The "Earth" (*Adamah*) is "Terrified" (*Chatthah*) or "Shattered" by the lack of moisture. It signifies "The Environmental Cost of Spiritual Rebellion."

2 Do Not Pray (al-tith-pal-lēl): The scrolls highlight the "Intercessory Root of Limitation." "Access" to the "Divine Ear" is "Uprooted" for the "Unrepentant." It signify "The Severity of Finalized Judgment."

3 Delusions of their own Minds (tha-re-mīth lib-bām): The scrolls highlight the "Cognitive Root of Falsehood." "Reality" is "Uprooted" by "Internal Deception" (*Tharemith*). It signifies "The Narcissism of False Prophecy."

4 It is You (at-tāh hū): The scrolls highlight the "Theological Root of Provision." "Idols" are "Uprooted" as "Sources" of "Rain" (*Geshem*). It signify "The Exclusive Creative Authority of Yahweh."

5 Have you Rejected Judah? (ha-mā-ōs mā-as-tā): The scrolls conclude with this "Abject Root of Inquiry." "Confidence" is "Uprooted" by "Silence." It marks the transition to "Moses and Samuel" in *Jeremiah 15*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Thirsty Earth." Hope in Him alone.
