Jeremiah 19

The Smashed Jar and the Point of No Return.

The Fired Clay

“Go and buy a clay jar from a potter.”
“Then smash the jar in the sight of those who accompany you.” 1

Valley of Slaughter

“They built high places to burn their children.”
“It will no longer be called Topheth, but the Valley of Slaughter.” 2

Cannot Be Repaired

“I will smash this nation just as this potter’s jar is smashed and cannot be repaired.” 3

Like Topheth

“I will make this city like Topheth.” 4
“The houses will be defiled.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Smash the Jar (we-shā-bhar-tā hab-baq-buq): In the scrolls, this is the "Brittle Root of Finality." Unlike the "marred clay" in *Jeremiah 18*, the "Fired Jar" (*Baqbuq*) is "Uprooted" from "Pliability." Once broken, it is finished. It signifies "The End of Grace."

2 Valley of Slaughter (ghē hay-ha-rē-ghāh): The scrolls highlight the "Topographic Root of Consequence." The place of "Sacrifice" is "Rooted" as the place of "Massacre." It signify "The Harvest of Blood."

3 Cannot be Repaired (lō yū-khal le-hērā-phē ōdh): Literally "Cannot be healed again." The scrolls highlight the "Terminal Root of Ruin." "Restoration" is "Uprooted." It signifies "The Destruction of the Institutional Kingdom."

4 Like Topheth (ke-Thō-pheth): The scrolls highlight the "Infernal Root of the City." "Jerusalem" is "Uprooted" from "Holiness" and "Re-Rooted" as an "Ash-Heap." It signify "The Desecration of the Sacred."

5 Defiled (ha-te-mē-īm): The scrolls conclude with this "Contamination Root." "Purity" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Jeremiah and Pashhur" in *Jeremiah 20*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Smashed Nation." The jar is broken.
