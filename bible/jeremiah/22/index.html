Jeremiah 22

The Unjust Palace and the Burial of a Donkey.

Woe to the Builder

“Woe to him who builds his palace by unrighteousness, making his own people work for nothing.” 1

Burial of a Donkey

“He will have the burial of a donkey—dragged away and thrown outside the gates.” 2

The Signet Ring

“As surely as I live...”
“Even if you, Coniah, were a signet ring on my right hand, I would still pull you off.” 3

Record Him Childless

“Record this man as if childless, for none of his descendants will sit on the throne of David.” 4
“O land, land, land, hear the word of the Lord!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Palace by Unrighteousness (bē-thō be-lō tze-dheq): In the scrolls, this is the "Architectural Root of Oppression." "Majesty" is "Uprooted" when "Luxury" is "Rooted" in "Wage Theft." It signifies "The Moral Foundation of the Throne."

2 Burial of a Donkey (qebh-ū-rath cha-mōr): The scrolls highlight the "Ignominious Root of Failure." "Royal Honor" is "Uprooted" and "Animalism" is "Rooted" in the king’s "End." It signify "The Degradation of the Unjust Leader."

3 Signet Ring (chō-thām): The scrolls highlight the "Instrumental Root of Authority." The king is "Uprooted" from the "Hand of God" (*Signet*) and "Tossed Aside." It signifies "The Withdrawal of Divine Validation."

4 Record Him Childless (kith-bhū... a-rī-rī): The scrolls highlight the "Genealogical Root of Deposition." The "Lineage" is "Uprooted" from "Succession" (*Throne of David*). It signify "The End of the Current Dynasty."

5 O Land, Land, Land (e-retz e-retz e-retz): The scrolls conclude with this "Terrestrial Root of Witness." The "Triple Repetition" is an "Uprooting" of "Human Attention" to "Root" the "Divine Decree" in the "Soil." It marks the transition to "The Righteous Branch" in *Jeremiah 23*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Empty Throne." Listen to the Land.
