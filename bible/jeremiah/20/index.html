Jeremiah 20

The Fire in the Bones and the Terror on Every Side.

Magor-Missabib

“The Lord’s name for you is not Pashhur, but Terror on Every Side.” 1

Fire Shut Up in My Bones

“His word is in my heart like a fire, a fire shut up in my bones.” 2
“I am weary of holding it in; indeed, I cannot.”

The Mighty Warrior

“But the Lord is with me like a mighty warrior.” 3

Cursed Be the Day

“Cursed be the day I was born!” 4
“Why did I ever come out of the womb to see trouble and sorrow?” 5

Translation Notes

1 Terror on Every Side (Mā-ghōr mis-sā-bhībh): In the scrolls, this is the "Phobic Root of Judgment." "Peace" (*Pashhur*) is "Uprooted" and "Panic" is "Rooted" in the very "Circumference" (*Missabhibh*) of the life. It signifies "The Psychological Siege."

2 Fire shut up in my Bones (kē-ēsh bō-ā-reth ā-tzūr be-atz-mō-thāy): The scrolls highlight the "Thermodynamic Root of the Word." "Silence" is "Uprooted" by "Internal Combustion." The "Word" is "Rooted" in the "Skeletal Structure" (*Bones*). It signify "The Compulsion of Truth."

3 Mighty Warrior (gib-bōr ā-rītz): The scrolls highlight the "Protective Root of the Divine." The "Enemy" is "Uprooted" by the "Awe-inspiring" (*Aritz*) "Warrior" (*Gibbor*). It signifies "The Violent Protection of the Prophet."

4 Cursed be the Day (ā-rūr hay-yōm): The scrolls highlight the "Abyssal Root of Lament." "Identity" is "Uprooted" by "Regret." It signify "The Depth of the Prophet’s Suffering."

5 To see Trouble and Sorrow (lir-ōth ā-māl we-yā-ghōn): The scrolls conclude with this "Visual Root of Grief." "Vision" is "Rooted" in "Pain" (*Amal*). It marks the transition to "The Rejection of Zedekiah" in *Jeremiah 21*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Burning Word." It cannot be held in.
