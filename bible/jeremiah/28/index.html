Jeremiah 28

The Wooden Yoke and the Sentence of Iron.

Hananiah’s Prophecy

“I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.” 1

The Wooden Yoke Broken

“Then the prophet Hananiah took the yoke off the neck of Jeremiah and broke it.” 2

The Yoke of Iron

“You have broken a wooden yoke, but in its place you will get a yoke of iron.” 3

Sentence of Death

“This very year you are going to die, because you have preached rebellion.” 4
“In the seventh month... Hananiah the prophet died.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Break the Yoke (shā-bha-r-tī eth-ōl): In the scrolls, this is the "Premature Root of Deliverance." Hananiah "Roots" his hope in "Immediacy" (*Two years*). It signifies "The Counterfeit of Prophetic Timing."

2 Took the Yoke and Broke it (way-yiq-qach... way-yish-be-rē-hū): The scrolls highlight the "Violent Root of Symbolism." Hananiah "Uproots" Jeremiah’s "Sign" (*Wooden Yoke*) by force. It signify "The Confrontation of Symbols."

3 Yoke of Iron (mō-thōth bar-zel): The scrolls highlight the "Industrial Root of Inevitability." "Wood" (*Ets*) is "Uprooted" and "Iron" (*Barzel*) is "Rooted." It signifies "The Hardening of the National Fate."

4 Preached Rebellion (sā-rāh dhib-bar-tā): Literally "Spoken a Turning-Away." The scrolls highlight the "Apostate Root of Speech." "Truth" is "Uprooted" by "Rebellion" (*Sarah*). It signify "The Capital Crime of Misleading the People."

5 Hananiah... Died (way-yā-moth): The scrolls conclude with this "Chronometric Root of Verification." The prophecy of Jeremiah is "Rooted" in "Fact" (*Death*). It marks the transition to "The Letter to the Exiles" in *Jeremiah 29*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The True Prophet." Test the word.
