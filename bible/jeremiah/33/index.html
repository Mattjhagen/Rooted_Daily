Jeremiah 33

The Call, the Answer, and the Sprout of David.

Call to Me

“Call to me and I will answer you and tell you great and unsearchable things.” 1

Health and Healing

“I will bring health and healing to it; I will heal my people.” 2

The Righteous Branch

“I will make a righteous Branch sprout from David’s line.” 3
“The Lord Our Righteous Savior.”

Day and Night

“If you can break my covenant with the day and my covenant with the night...” 4
“Then my covenant with David can be broken.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Unsearchable Things (ū-ne-tzu-rōth): Literally "Fortified" or "Fenced-in" things. In the scrolls, this is the "Enclosed Root of Mystery." "Ignorance" is "Uprooted" as God "Unlocks" the "Fortress of Hidden Knowledge." It signifies "The Deep Intel of Heaven."

2 Health and Healing (a-rū-khāh ū-mar-pē): The scrolls highlight the "Restorative Root of the City." "Disease" is "Uprooted." "Arukah" is the "New Flesh" that grows over a wound. It signify "The Aesthetic Restoration of Jerusalem."

3 Branch Sprout (tze-mah tzad-dīq): The scrolls highlight the "Organic Root of the Messiah." The "Dead Stump" is "Uprooted" by the "New Sprout" (*Tzemah*). It signifies "The Vitality of the Davidic Promise."

4 Covenant with the Day (be-rīth hay-yōm): The scrolls highlight the "Cosmological Root of Faithfulness." "Instability" is "Uprooted" by the "Reliability of the Solar Cycle." It signify "The Biological Clock of God’s Word."

5 David... can be Broken (be-rī-thī eth-Dā-wid): The scrolls conclude with this "Dynastic Root." "Human Doubt" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Warning to Zedekiah" in *Jeremiah 34*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Great Answer." Call to Him.
