Jeremiah 1

The Womb of Identity and the Boiling Pot of the North.

Before the Womb

“Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, before you were born I set you apart.” 1
“I appointed you as a prophet to the nations.”

Do Not Be Afraid

“Do not say, ‘I am too young.’”
“Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you and will rescue you.” 2

Uproot and Plant

“See, today I appoint you over nations and kingdoms to uproot and tear down, to build and to plant.” 3

The Boiling Pot

“I see the branch of an almond tree.”
“I see a pot that is boiling; it is tilting from the north.” 4

A Fortified City

“Today I have made you a fortified city, an iron pillar and a bronze wall.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Before I Formed You (be-te-rem etz-tzā-r-khā): In the scrolls, this is the "Prenatal Root of Calling." The "Prophet" is "Rooted" in "Divine Foreknowledge" (*Yeda-tikha*) and "Consecration" (*Hiph-dash-tikha*). It signifies "The Pre-existence of Purpose."

2 Do Not be Afraid (al-tī-rā): The scrolls highlight the "Resilient Root of Courage." "Fear" is "Uprooted" by the "Presence" (*Im-ma-kha*) of the Lord. It signify "The Psychological Shield of the Prophet."

3 Uproot and Tear Down (lin-thōsh we-lin-thōtz): The scrolls highlight the "Architectural and Botanical Root of the Message." Jeremiah’s ministry is "Rooted" in "Destruction" of the false to "Root" the "True" (*Plant/Neta*). It signifies "The Necessity of Demolition before Reconstruction."

4 Boiling Pot (sīr nā-phū-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Caloric Root of Disaster." The "North" (*Tzaphon*) is the "Origin" of the "Overflowing Judgment." It signifies "The Impending Babylonian Invasion."

5 Fortified City (lī-īr mibh-tzār): The scrolls conclude with this "Infrastructural Root of Defense." The "Fragile Person" is "Rooted" as an "Impenetrable Wall" (*Chomah*). It marks the beginning of *Jeremiah*’s long struggle. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Pre-destined Voice." Stand firm.
