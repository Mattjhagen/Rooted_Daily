Jeremiah 11

The Iron Furnace and the Plot of the Lamb.

Listen to the Terms

“Cursed is the person who does not obey the terms of this covenant.” 1

The Iron Furnace

“When I brought them out of Egypt, out of the iron-smelting furnace.” 2

As Many Gods as Towns

“You, Judah, have as many gods as you have towns.” 3

Lamb to the Slaughter

“I had been like a gentle lamb led to the slaughter.” 4
“Let us destroy the tree and its fruit.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Cursed (ā-rūr): In the scrolls, this is the "Judicial Root of Separation." "Blessing" is "Uprooted" from the "Disobedient" (*Arur*). It signifies "The Spiritual Gravity of the Covenant."

2 Iron-smelting Furnace (kūr hab-bar-zel): The scrolls highlight the "Industrial Root of Suffering." "Egypt" was "Rooted" as a "Refining Fire" (*Kur*) for "Israel" (*Iron*). It signify "The Intensity of the Formative Years."

3 As Many Gods (mis-par ā-re-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Numerical Root of Prostitution." "Monotheism" is "Uprooted" by "Localized Idolatry" (*Towns/Are-kha*). It signifies "The Total Fragmentation of Faith."

4 Lamb led to Slaughter (ke-khe-seb al-lūph yū-bhāl lit-bhō-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Innocent Root of the Prophet." Jeremiah is "Rooted" in "Unawareness" (*Alluph*) of the "Plot" (*Machashaboth*). It signify "The Vulnerability of the Divine Messenger."

5 Destroy the Tree and its Fruit (nash-chī-thāh ētz be-lach-mō): Literally "Let us destroy the tree with its bread." The scrolls conclude with this "Arboreal Root of Assassination." The "Prophet" is the "Tree" and his "Message" is the "Bread" (*Lachmo*). It marks the transition to "Jeremiah’s Complaint" in *Jeremiah 12*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Gentle Lamb." Trust no man.
