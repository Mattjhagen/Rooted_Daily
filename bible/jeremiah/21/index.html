Jeremiah 21

The Outstretched Hand and the Choice of Life.

Zedekiah’s Inquiry

“Inquire now of the Lord for us.”
“Perhaps the Lord will perform a miracle as in times past.” 1

God Fighting Against You

“I myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and a mighty arm.” 2

Life and Death

“See, I am setting before you the way of life and the way of death.” 3

Justice in the Morning

“Administer justice every morning; rescue the one who has been robbed.” 4
“I will punish you as your deeds deserve.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Perform a Miracle (ya-a-śeh... niph-le-ō-thāw): Literally "Do according to his wonders." The scrolls highlight the "Nostalgic Root of False Hope." Zedekiah wants the "Past" to "Root" the "Present" in "Safety" without repentance. It signifies "The Abuse of Sacred History."

2 Outstretched Hand (be-yād ne-tū-yāh): This is the language of the Exodus (*Exodus 6:6*). The scrolls highlight the "Inverted Root of Deliverance." The "Hand" that once "Uprooted" Egypt is now "Rooted" against "Jerusalem." It signify "The Reversal of the Covenant Protection."

3 Way of Life and Death (de-rekh hay-chay-yīm we-e-rekh ham-mā-weth): The scrolls highlight the "Binary Root of Existence." "Neutrality" is "Uprooted." One must "Root" in "Surrender" (*Life*) or "Resistance" (*Death*). It signifies "The Paradox of Survival through Submission."

4 Justice in the Morning (di-nū lab-bō-qer mish-pāt): The scrolls highlight the "Diurnal Root of Integrity." "Corruption" must be "Uprooted" at the "Beginning" of every day. It signify "The Daily Requirement of Moral Order."

5 As your Deeds Deserve (ke-phe-rī ma-al-lē-khem): Literally "According to the fruit of your doings." The scrolls conclude with this "Botanical Root of Karma." "Consequence" is "Rooted" in "Action." It marks the transition to "The Kings of Judah" in *Jeremiah 22*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Outstretched Hand." Choose life.
