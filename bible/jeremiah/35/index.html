Jeremiah 35

The Tents of Jonadab and the Faith of the Rechabites.

We Do Not Drink Wine

“We do not drink wine, because our forefather gave us this command.” 1

Never Build Houses

“You must never build houses, sow seed or plant vineyards.” 2
“You must always live in tents.”

Judah’s Disobedience

“They have kept the command... but these people have not obeyed me.” 3

The Stand of Rechab

“Jonadab son of Rechab will never fail to have a descendant to serve me.” 4

Translation Notes

1 We do not drink Wine (lō nish-teh yā-yin): In the scrolls, this is the "Abstinent Root of Tradition." "Appetite" is "Uprooted" to "Root" the "Identity" in a "Vow." It signifies "The Power of Ancestral Influence."

2 Always live in Tents (be-o-hā-līm tē-she-bhū): The scrolls highlight the "Nomadic Root of Vigilance." "Sedentary Complacency" is "Uprooted." By refusing to "Root" in "Stone" (*Houses*), they remain "Rooted" in "Obedience." It signify "The Detachment from the World."

3 Not Obeyed Me (we-lō she-ma-tem ē-lāy): The scrolls highlight the "Acoustic Root of Rebellion." The "Sound" of the "Father’s" command is "Rooted" in the Rechabites, but the "Sound" of "God’s Voice" is "Uprooted" from "Judah." It signifies "The Irony of Human Devotion."

4 Never fail to have a Descendant (lō yik-kā-rēth īsh): Literally "A man shall not be cut off." The scrolls conclude with this "Succession Root." The "Family Line" is "Rooted" in "Perpetuity" because of "Faithfulness." It marks the transition to "The Burning of the Scroll" in *Jeremiah 36*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unwavering Vow." Stand firm.
