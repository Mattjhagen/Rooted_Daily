Jeremiah 32

The Deed of Purchase and the Faith of the Guardhouse.

Buy My Field

“Buy my field at Anathoth, for it is your right and duty to buy it.” 1

Real Estate of Hope

“Houses, fields and vineyards will again be bought in this land.” 2

Nothing Too Hard

“Ah, Sovereign Lord... Nothing is too hard for you.” 3
“I am the Lord... Is anything too hard for me?” 4

The Sealed Deed

“I gave the deed of purchase to Baruch son of Neriah.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Buy my Field (qe-nē le-khā eth-śā-dhī): In the scrolls, this is the "Economic Root of Faith." "Despair" is "Uprooted" as Jeremiah "Roots" his "Finances" in a "Land" currently controlled by the enemy. It signifies "Investment in the Invisible Future."

2 Again be Bought (yiy-yā-qe-nū): The scrolls highlight the "Commercial Root of Normalcy." "Total Loss" is "Uprooted" and "Regular Life" is "Rooted" in the "Future." It signify "The Mundane as a Miracle."

3 Nothing too Hard (lō yip-pā-lē mim-me-khā): Literally "Nothing is too wonderful/miraculous for you." The scrolls highlight the "Supernatural Root of Possibility." "Human Logic" is "Uprooted." It signifies "The Unlimited Scope of Divine Action."

4 Is anything too hard? (hay-yip-pā-lē): The scrolls highlight the "Rhetorical Root of Assurance." God "Uproots" the prophet’s "Doubt" by "Echoing" his "Statement." It signify "The Echo of Divine Confirmation."

5 Deed of Purchase (se-pher ham-miq-nāh): The scrolls conclude with this "Legal Root of Promise." The "Oral Tradition" is "Rooted" in "Documentary Evidence" (*Sepher*). It marks the transition to "The Call and Answer" in *Jeremiah 33*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Prophetic Investment." Buy the field.
