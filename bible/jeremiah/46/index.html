Jeremiah 46

The Day of the Lord at Carchemish.

Polish the Spears

“Helmet on! Polish the spears! Put on your armor!”
“That day belongs to the Lord; a day of vengeance.” 1

The Loud Noise

“Pharaoh king of Egypt is only a loud noise; he has missed his opportunity.” 2

No Healing

“You multiply remedies in vain; there is no healing for you.” 3

Jacob My Servant

“Do not be afraid, Jacob my servant; do not be dismayed, Israel.” 4
“I will punish you with justice; I will not let you go unpunished.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Day of Vengeance (yōm ne-qā-māh): In the scrolls, this is the "Reciprocal Root of Justice." "Peace" is "Uprooted" as God "Roots" the "Outcome" of Egypt’s pride at "Carchemish." It signifies "The End of Egyptian Hegemony."

2 Loud Noise (shā-ōn): Literally "A roar" or "A crash." The scrolls highlight the "Acoustic Root of Impotence." Pharaoh’s "Power" is "Uprooted" and only the "Sound" (*Shaon*) remains. It signify "The Deception of Appearance."

3 No Healing (te-ā-lāh ē-n lākh): The scrolls highlight the "Medical Root of Ruin." "Balm" is "Uprooted" from "Effectiveness." It signifies "The Finality of the National Wound."

4 Do not be Afraid (al-tī-rā abh-dī Ya-a-qōbh): The scrolls highlight the "Protective Root of the Servant." While the "Nations" are "Uprooted," the "Servant" is "Rooted" in "Safety." It signify "The Preservation of the Remnant."

5 Punish you with Justice (we-yis-sar-tī-khā lam-mish-pāt): The scrolls conclude with this "Pedagogical Root." "Destruction" is "Uprooted" and "Correction" is "Rooted." It marks the transition to "The Philistines" in *Jeremiah 47*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Returning Exile." Fear not.
