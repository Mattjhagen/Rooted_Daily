Jeremiah 29

The Letter of Hope and the Planting of the Exile.

Build and Plant

“Build houses and settle down; plant gardens and eat what they produce.” 1

Seek the Peace

“Seek the peace and prosperity of the city to which I have carried you into exile.” 2

Hope and a Future

“For I know the plans I have for you... plans to give you hope and a future.” 3

Seek with All Your Heart

“You will seek me and find me when you seek me with all your heart.” 4
“I will gather you from all the nations.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Build and Plant (be-nū bhāt-tīm... nit-ū ghan-nōth): In the scrolls, this is the "Constructive Root of Exile." "Transience" is "Uprooted" to "Root" the "People" in "Long-term Presence" (*Settle down*). It signifies "The Sanctification of the Foreign Land."

2 Seek the Peace (dir-shū eth-she-lōm hā-īr): The scrolls highlight the "Intercessory Root of Integration." "Hatred" for the "Oppressor" is "Uprooted" to "Root" the "Prosperity" (*Shalom*) of the "Host City." It signify "The Universalism of Prayer."

3 Hope and a Future (tiq-wāh we-a-cha-rīth): The scrolls highlight the "Teleological Root of Grace." "Despair" is "Uprooted" by "Divine Intent" (*Plans/Machashaboth*). "Tiqwah" (*Hope*) is literally a "Cord" or "Root" that pulls the future into the present. It signifies "The Structural Optimism of the Covenant."

4 Seek me with all your Heart (be-khol le-bhabh-khem): The scrolls highlight the "Internal Root of Discovery." "Casual Religion" is "Uprooted" to "Root" the "Whole Heart" (*Lebhabh*) in the "Search" (*Darash*). It signify "The Condition for Manifestation."

5 I will Gather You (we-qib-batz-tī eth-khem): The scrolls conclude with this "Congregational Root." "Fragmentation" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Book of Consolation" in *Jeremiah 30*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Future Hope." Build and plant.
