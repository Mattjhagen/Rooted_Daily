Jeremiah 49

The Eagle’s Nest and the Haunt of Jackals.

The Eagle of Edom

“Though you make your nest as high as the eagle’s, from there I will bring you down.” 1

Leave Your Orphans

“Leave your orphans; I will protect their lives.” 2

Feeble Damascus

“Damascus has become feeble; she has turned to flee and is overcome by panic.” 3

The Bow of Elam

“I will break the bow of Elam, the mainstay of their might.” 4
“Yet I will restore the fortunes of Elam in days to come.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Nest as High as the Eagle’s (kan-ne-sher qin-ne-khā): In the scrolls, this is the "Altitudinal Root of Pride." Edom "Roots" his "Security" in the "High Rocks" (*Sela*). God "Uproots" them from their "Nest" (*Qen*). It signifies "The Vulnerability of the Impenetrable."

2 Protect their Lives (a-nī a-cha-yeh): Literally "I will give life." The scrolls highlight the "Paternal Root of Mercy." Amidst the "Uprooting" of the "Edomite State," God "Roots" the "Survival" of the "Vulnerable." It signify "Grace in the Midst of Judgment."

3 Damascus has become Feeble (rā-phe-thāh Dam-me-śeq): The scrolls highlight the "Enervating Root of Panic." "Civic Courage" is "Uprooted." Damascus was the oldest inhabited city. It signifies "The Fragility of History."

4 Mainstay of their Might (rē-shīth ge-bhū-rā-thām): Literally "The beginning/chief of their strength." The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of Power." The "Bow" (*Qesheth*) is "Uprooted." It signify "The Disarming of the Mighty."

5 Restore the Fortunes (we-shabh-tī she-bhūth): The scrolls conclude with this "Restorative Root." Like Moab, Elam is "Re-Rooted." It marks the transition to the massive oracle against "Babylon" in *Jeremiah 50*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Broken Bow." Restoration remains.
