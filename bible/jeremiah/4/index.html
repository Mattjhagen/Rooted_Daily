Jeremiah 4

The Scorching Wind and the Return to Chaos.

Circumcise Your Hearts

“Circumcise yourselves to the Lord, circumcise your hearts.” 1

The Scorching Wind

“A scorching wind from the barren heights.”
“See! He advances like the clouds, his chariots come like a whirlwind.” 2

The Prophet’s Agony

“Oh, my anguish, my anguish! I writhe in pain. Oh, the agony of my heart!” 3

Formless and Empty

“I looked at the earth, and it was formless and empty; and at the heavens, and their light was gone.” 4
“The mountains were quaking.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Circumcise your Hearts (hi-mō-lū... or-lōth le-bhabh-khem): In the scrolls, this is the "Internal Root of Holiness." "External Ritual" is "Uprooted" as the primary concern, and "Internal Devotion" (*Heart*) is "Rooted" in "Covenant." It signifies "The Spiritualization of the Law."

2 Like a Whirlwind (kas-sū-phāh): The scrolls highlight the "Atmospheric Root of Judgment." The "Babylonian Army" is "Rooted" as a "Storm" (*Suphah*) that "Uproots" the "Land." It signify "The Unstoppable Nature of Divine Consequence."

3 Agony of my Heart (qī-rōth lib-bī): Literally "The walls of my heart." The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Root of Intercession." The "Prophet" is "Rooted" in the "Suffering" of his people. It signify "The Weeping Nature of Jeremiah’s Office."

4 Formless and Empty (tō-hū wā-bhō-hū): This is the same language used in *Genesis 1:2*. The scrolls highlight the "Un-Creation Root." "Order" is "Uprooted" and the "Earth" is "Re-Rooted" in "Chaos." It signifies "Judgment as the Reversal of Creation."

5 Light was Gone (ēn ō-rām): The scrolls conclude with this "Extinguishing Root." The "Presence of God" (*Light*) is "Uprooted" from the "Heavens." It marks the transition to "The Search for Truth" in *Jeremiah 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Shattered Earth." Creation is undone.
