Jeremiah 43

The Stones in the Clay and the Flight to Tahpanhes.

You Are Lying

“‘You are lying! The Lord our God has not sent you to say, "You must not go to Egypt."’” 1

Tahpanhes

“They went to Egypt because they did not obey the Lord; they went as far as Tahpanhes.” 2

Stones in the Clay

“Take some large stones and bury them in the clay.”
“I will send for my servant Nebuchadnezzar... and he will set his throne over these stones.” 3

Wrap Egypt Around Him

“He will wrap Egypt around him like a shepherd wraps his cloak around him.” 4
“He will demolish the sacred pillars of Egypt.” 5

Translation Notes

1 You are Lying (she-qer at-tāh dhib-bēr): In the scrolls, this is the "Projective Root of Denial." "Truth" is "Uprooted" by "Accusation." They "Root" their "Rebellion" in the "Lies" of others. It signifies "The Mental Shield of the Defiant."

2 Tahpanhes (Tach-pan-chēs): The scrolls highlight the "Geographic Root of Reversion." The "Exodus" is "Uprooted" as the "People" "Return" to "Tahpanhes" (*an Egyptian frontier city*). It signify "The Un-Creation of the Nation."

3 Stones in the Clay (a-bhā-nīm ge-dhō-lōth): The scrolls highlight the "Geological Root of Inevitability." Jeremiah "Roots" the "Promise" of "Conquest" in the "Clay" (*Malben*) of the enemy’s palace. It signifies "The Inescapable Reach of God’s Servant."

4 Wrap Egypt around Him (we-ātāh... ka-a-sher ya-teh hā-rō-eh): The scrolls highlight the "Effortless Root of Power." "Empire" is "Uprooted" and "Worn" as a "Garment" (*Cloak/Ma’ateh*). It signify "The Ease of Babylonian Conquest."

5 Sacred Pillars (matz-tzbhōth): The scrolls conclude with this "Idolatrous Root of Desecration." "False Worship" is "Uprooted" by "Smashing" the "Obelisks" (*Pillars*). It marks the transition to "Disaster in Egypt" in *Jeremiah 44*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Buried Stones." There is no escape.
