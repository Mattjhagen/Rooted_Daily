Jeremiah 17

The Deceitful Heart and the Tree by the Stream.

Engraved with Iron

“Judah’s sin is engraved with an iron tool, inscribed on the tablets of their hearts.” 1

Planted by the Water

“Blessed is the one who trusts in the Lord, whose confidence is in him.” 2
“They will be like a tree planted by the water that sends out its roots by the stream.” 3

Deceitful Above All

“The heart is deceitful above all things and beyond cure.” 4
“I the Lord search the heart and examine the mind.”

Keep the Sabbath Holy

“Be careful not to carry a load on the Sabbath day.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Engraved with Iron (khe-thū-bhāh be-ēt bar-zel): In the scrolls, this is the "Indelible Root of Sin." The "Heart" (*Luach/Tablet*) is "Uprooted" from "Purity" by "Deep Etching" (*Engraved*). It signifies "The Permanence of Sin apart from Grace."

2 Trusts in the Lord (yibh-tach b-Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Safe Root of Faith." "Self-Reliance" is "Uprooted" and "Dependency" is "Rooted" in the "Rock." It signify "The Psychological Anchor of the Soul."

3 Sends out its Roots (ye-shal-lach sho-rā-shāw): The scrolls highlight the "Hydrological Root of Endurance." The "Tree" (*Etz*) is "Rooted" (*Shorash*) specifically "By the Stream" (*Yuval*). It signifies "The Source-Dependency of the Righteous."

4 Deceitful Above All (ā-qōbh hal-lēbh mik-kōl): The scrolls highlight the "Heel-Grabbing Root of the Heart." The word (*Aqob*) shares a root with "Jacob." The "Heart" is "Uprooted" from "Directness" and "Rooted" in "Crookedness." It signify "The Untrustworthiness of Emotion."

5 Carry a Load (śe-ū mas-śā): The scrolls conclude with this "Gravitational Root of Rest." "Labor" is "Uprooted" on the "Seventh Day" to "Root" the "Soul" in "Divinity." It marks the transition to "The Potter’s House" in *Jeremiah 18*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Thirsty Tree." Send out your roots.
