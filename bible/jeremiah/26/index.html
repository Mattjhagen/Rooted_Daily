Jeremiah 26

The Trial of the Prophet and the Shield of the Elders.

Like Shiloh

“Then I will make this house like Shiloh and this city a curse to all nations.” 1

You Must Die

“The priests and prophets seized him.”
“They said, ‘You must die!’” 2

He Spoke in the Name

“This man should not be sentenced to death! He has spoken to us in the name of the Lord.” 3

The Fate of Uriah

“King Jehoiakim killed Uriah with a sword.” 4
“But Ahikam son of Shaphan supported Jeremiah.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Like Shiloh (ke-Shī-lōh): In the scrolls, this is the "Historical Root of Desolation." The "Sacred" is "Uprooted" by "Historical Precedent." It signifies "The End of Institutional Privilege."

2 You Must Die (mōth tā-mūth): The scrolls highlight the "Lethal Root of Religious Jealousy." "Prophetic Truth" is "Uprooted" by "Legalistic Violence." It signify "The Cost of Confrontation."

3 Sentenced to Death (mish-pat mā-weth): The scrolls highlight the "Jurisprudential Root of Protection." "Murder" is "Uprooted" by "Legal Precedent" (*Micah*). It signifies "The Remnant of Justice in the Court."

4 Uriah (Ū-rī-yā-hū): The scrolls highlight the "Martyr’s Root of Warning." Not all prophets are "Rooted" in "Safety." Uriah’s "Fleeing" (*Egypt*) did not "Root" him in "Life." It signifies "The Real Danger of Jeremiah’s Office."

5 Ahikam supported Jeremiah (yadh A-chī-qām... hā-ye-thāh eth-Yir-me-yā-hū): Literally "The hand of Ahikam was with Jeremiah." The scrolls conclude with this "Supportive Root." "Truth" is "Rooted" by "Human Intervention" (*Hand/Yadh*). It marks the transition to "The Yoke of Babylon" in *Jeremiah 27*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Protected Voice." Do not fear.
