Jeremiah 18

The Wheel of the Potter and the Hands of the Maker.

The Potter’s House

“I went down to the potter’s house, and he was working at the wheel.”
“But the pot he was shaping was marred in his hands; so the potter formed it into another pot.” 1

Clay in the Hand

“Like clay in the hand of the potter, so are you in my hand, Israel.” 2

The Power to Reshape

“If at any time I announce that a nation is to be uprooted... and if that nation repents... I will relent.” 3

Stubborn Hearts

“‘It’s no use. We will continue with our own plans.’” 4

Translation Notes

1 Marred in His Hands (ne-she-chath... be-yad hay-yō-tzēr): In the scrolls, this is the "Plastic Root of Creation." "Perfection" is "Uprooted" by "Imperfection" in the "Clay" (*Cho-mer*), but "Hope" is "Rooted" in the "Potter’s" (*Yotzer*) "Authority" to "Reshape" (*Shubh*). It signifies "The Grace of a Second Chance."

2 Clay in the Hand (ke-chō-mer be-yad hay-yō-tzēr): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Root of Identity." "Autonomy" is "Uprooted" and "Subservience" is "Rooted" in the "Maker’s Hand." It signify "The Divine Prerogative."

3 Uprooted... and I will Relent (lin-thōsh... we-ni-cham-tī): The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Root of History." "Judgment" is "Uprooted" by "Repentance" (*Shubh*). It signifies "The Conditional Nature of Prophetic Pronouncement."

4 It’s No Use (nō-āsh): Literally "Desperate" or "Hopeless." The scrolls highlight the "Fatalistic Root of Rebellion." "Repentance" is "Uprooted" by "Resignation" to "Sin." It signify "The Hardening of the National Will."

5 Make Plans against Jeremiah (nash-be-thāh... mach-sha-bhōth): The scrolls conclude with this "Conspiratorial Root." "Truth" is "Uprooted" by "Political Intrigue." It marks the transition to "The Smashed Jar" in *Jeremiah 19*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Reshaped Life." Yield to the Potter.
