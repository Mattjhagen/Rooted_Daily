Jeremiah 30

The Trouble of Jacob and the Health of the Exile.

Jacob’s Trouble

“It will be a time of trouble for Jacob, but he will be saved out of it.” 1

Breaking the Yoke

“I will break the yoke off their necks and will tear off their bonds.” 2

Healing the Incurable

“Your wound is incurable, your injury beyond heal.”
“But I will restore you to health and heal your wounds.” 3

One of Their Own

“Their leader will be one of their own; their ruler will arise from among them.” 4
“So you will be my people, and I will be your God.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Time of Trouble (ēth tza-rāh): In the scrolls, this is the "Parturition Root of Suffering." "Jacob" is "Uprooted" from "Comfort" into "Birth Pains" (*Trouble*). It signifies "Suffering with a Purpose."

2 Break the Yoke (esh-bōr ul-lō): The scrolls highlight the "Liberating Root of Power." The "Babylonian Yoke" is "Uprooted" by "Divine Strength." It signify "The Restoration of Sovereignty."

3 Heal your Wounds (u-mim-mak-kō-tha-yi-kh er-pā-ēkh): The scrolls highlight the "Medical Root of Grace." "Hopelessness" (*Incurable*) is "Uprooted" and "Health" (*Arū-khāh*) is "Rooted." It signifies "The Supernatural Recovery of the People."

4 Leader... arising from Among Them (ad-dī-rō mim-men-nū): Literally "Their Noble One." The scrolls highlight the "Indigenous Root of Leadership." "Foreign Rule" is "Uprooted" to "Root" a "Native King." It signify "The End of Exile."

5 You will be My People (he-yī-them lī le-ām): The scrolls conclude with this "Covenantal Root." "Alienation" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The New Covenant" in *Jeremiah 31*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Saved Jacob." Trouble leads to health.
