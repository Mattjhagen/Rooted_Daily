Jeremiah 16

The Forbidden Marriage and the Day of the Hunters.

No Sons or Daughters

“You must not marry and have sons or daughters in this place.” 1

The Silence of Joy

“I will bring an end to the sounds of joy and gladness in this place.” 2

The New Exodus

“The days are coming...”
“As surely as the Lord lives, who brought the Israelites up out of the land of the north.” 3

Fishers and Hunters

“I will send for many fishermen... After that I will send for many hunters.” 4
“They will know that my name is the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 You must not Marry (lō thiq-qach le-khā ish-shāh): In the scrolls, this is the "Domestic Root of Celibacy." "Normalcy" is "Uprooted" as a "Sign" of the "Abnormal Era" of judgment. It signifies "The Total Interruption of Life."

2 Silence of Joy (mish-bīth... qōl śā-śōn): The scrolls highlight the "Acoustic Root of Mourning." The "Sounds" of "Celebration" are "Uprooted." It signify "The Aesthetic Death of the Community."

3 Land of the North (e-retz tzā-phōn): The scrolls highlight the "Geographic Root of Restoration." The "Exodus" from "Egypt" is "Uprooted" as the "Primary Memory" and "Rooted" in the "Return from Babylon." It signifies "A Greater Salvation."

4 Fishers and Hunters (day-yā-ghīm... tza-yā-dhīm): The scrolls highlight the "Retrieval Root of Judgment." No one can be "Rooted" in "Hiding." They will be "Caught" (*Fisher*) and "Hunted" (*Hunter*). It signify "The Inescapable Nature of Divine Accounting."

5 My Name is the Lord (she-mī Yah-weh): The scrolls conclude with this "Ontological Root." "Ignorance" is "Uprooted" by "Experiential Knowledge" (*Yada*) of God’s "Power" (*Yad*). It marks the transition to "The Deceitful Heart" in *Jeremiah 17*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Living Sign." He is known.
