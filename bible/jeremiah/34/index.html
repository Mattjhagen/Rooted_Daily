Jeremiah 34

The Broken Promise and the Freedom of the Sword.

You Shall Not Escape

“You will not escape from his hand but will surely be captured.” 1

The Retaken Slaves

“They changed their minds and took back the slaves they had freed.” 2

Freedom for the Sword

“I now proclaim ‘freedom’ for you... freedom to fall by the sword, plague and famine.” 3

The Cut Calf

“I will make you like the calf they cut in two.” 4
“I will make the towns of Judah a desolation.” 5

Translation Notes

1 You will not Escape (we-at-tāh lō thim-mā-lēt): In the scrolls, this is the "Inevitable Root of Capture." "Mobility" is "Uprooted" as the King is "Rooted" in "Confinement." It signifies "The End of Royal Autonomy."

2 Changed their Minds (way-yā-shū-bhū): Literally "They returned." This is a dark pun on "Repentance" (*Shubh*). Instead of "Returning" to God, they "Returned" to their "Sin" by retaking the slaves. The scrolls highlight the "Regressive Root of Betrayal." It signify "The Superficiality of their Covenant."

3 Freedom for the Sword (de-rōr... la-che-rebh): The scrolls highlight the "Ironic Root of Judgment." "Liberty" (*Deror*) is "Uprooted" from the "People" and "Rooted" in the "Instruments of Death." It signifies "The Divine Sarcasm."

4 Like the Calf (ha-ē-ghal): The scrolls highlight the "Ritual Root of Self-Malediction." The parties "Rooted" the "Covenant" in a "Death-Threat" (*Walking between the pieces*). Since they "Uprooted" the "Promise," they are "Rooted" in the "Outcome" (*Death*). It signify "The Weight of the Vow."

5 Towns of Judah (ā-rē Ye-hū-dhāh): The scrolls conclude with this "Geographic Root of Desolation." "Life" is "Uprooted" from the "Civic Centers." It marks the transition to "The Rechabites" in *Jeremiah 35*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Broken Word." Keep your promises.
