Jeremiah 36

The Scribe’s Pen and the King’s Firepot.

Write on a Scroll

“Take a scroll and write on it all the words I have spoken.”
“Baruch wrote on the scroll as Jeremiah dictated.” 1

The Scribe’s Knife

“The king cut it with a scribe’s knife and threw it into the firepot.” 2

Take Another Scroll

“Take another scroll and write on it all the words that were on the first.” 3
“Many similar words were added to them.” 4

Exposed to the Heat

“His body will be thrown out and exposed to the heat by day and the frost by night.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Write on a Scroll (kethobh-le-khā al-me-ghil-lath sē-pher): In the scrolls, this is the "Documentary Root of Revelation." "Orality" is "Rooted" in "Inscribed Text" (*Sepher*). It signifies "The Transition to Scripture."

2 Cut it with a Scribe’s Knife (ye-qāra-ehā be-ta-ar has-sō-phēr): The scrolls highlight the "Surgical Root of Rebellion." The King "Uproots" the "Word" (*Scroll*) with a "Knife" (*Ta’ar*). It signify "The Attempt to Kill the Message by Killing the Medium."

3 Take another Scroll (qach-le-khā me-ghil-lāh a-che-reth): The scrolls highlight the "Recursive Root of the Word." "Fire" (*Ash*) cannot "Uproot" the "Logos." It is "Re-Rooted" in a "New Scroll." It signifies "The Indestructibility of Truth."

4 Many similar words (de-bhā-rīm rab-bīm kā-hēm-māh): The scrolls highlight the "Growth Root of Prophecy." Judgment "Uprooted" the first scroll, but the second "Rooted" "More" (*Rabbim*) words. It signify "The Compound Interest of Divine Warning."

5 Body exposed to the Heat (ni-bhē-lā-thō... la-chō-rebh): The scrolls conclude with this "Corpse Root of Ignominy." The King who "Burnt" the scroll will himself be "Burnt" by the "Sun" (*Heat*). It marks the transition to "Jeremiah in the Dungeon" in *Jeremiah 37*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unburnable Word." You cannot kill the Scroll.
