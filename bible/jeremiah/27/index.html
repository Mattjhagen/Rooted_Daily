Jeremiah 27

The Yoke of straps and the Patience of the Vessels.

The Sign of the Yoke

“Make a yoke out of straps and crossbars.”
“Put it on your neck.” 1

All Nations Shall Serve

“I give all these lands to my servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.” 2

Yield Your Neck

“The nation that will yield its neck to the yoke... I will let remain in its own land.” 3

The Temple Vessels

“Do not listen to the prophets who say the articles will be brought back soon.” 4
“They will remain in Babylon until the day I come for them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 straps and crossbars (mō-se-rōth ū-mō-thōth): In the scrolls, this is the "Biological and Mechanical Root of Submission." The "Independence" of the "Neck" is "Uprooted" by "External Constraint." It signifies "The Physical Burden of Sovereignty."

2 My Servant Nebuchadnezzar (Nebh-ū-khadh-retz-tzar abh-dī): The scrolls highlight the "Global Root of Authority." Even "Pagan Kings" are "Rooted" in the "Service" (*Abhed*) of Yahweh. It signify "The Universal Scope of God’s Plan."

3 Yield its Neck (tā-bhī eth-tzaw-wā-rāh): Literally "Bring her neck." The scrolls highlight the "Volitional Root of Survival." "Resistance" is "Uprooted" to "Root" the "Nation" in the "Land." It signifies "The Paradox of Freedom through Service."

4 Articles will be brought back (kē-lē bē-th Yah-weh... yū-sha-bhū): The scrolls highlight the "Material Root of Deception." "False Hope" is "Rooted" in "Temple Gold" (*Vessels*). It signify "The Greed of the False Prophets."

5 Until the Day I come for them (adh yōm poq-dhī ō-thām): Literally "Until the day of my visitation." The scrolls conclude with this "Chronometric Root of Restoration." "Loss" is "Rooted" in a "Fixed Period" (*Visitation/Paqad*). It marks the transition to "Jeremiah vs Hananiah" in *Jeremiah 28*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Heavy Yoke." Submit to the time.
