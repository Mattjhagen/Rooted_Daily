Jeremiah 37

The Benjamin Gate and the Vaulted Cell.

The Benjamin Gate

“You are deserting to the Babylonians!”
“‘It’s a lie!’ Jeremiah said. ‘I am not deserting.’” 1

The Vaulted Cell

“They had him beaten and imprisoned in the house of Jonathan the scribe.” 2
“Jeremiah was put in a vaulted cell in a dungeon.”

Is there any Word?

“‘Is there any word from the Lord?’ ‘Yes,’ Jeremiah replied.” 3

Do Not Send Me Back

“Do not send me back to the house of Jonathan, or I will die there.” 4
“He was placed in the courtyard of the guard.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Benjamin Gate (sha-ar Bin-yā-mīn): In the scrolls, this is the "Threshold Root of Accusation." "Mobility" is "Uprooted" at the "Gate." "Benjamin" means "Son of the Right Hand," but Jeremiah is treated as the "Son of Treason." It signifies "The Perversion of Geography."

2 Vaulted Cell (el-bēth hak-kha-nū-yōth): Literally "The room of the cells." The scrolls highlight the "Subterranean Root of Suffering." "Prophetic Vision" is "Uprooted" and "Buried" in the "Basement." It signify "The Silencing of the Witness."

3 Is there any Word? (ha-yēsh dā-bhār mē-ēth Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Desperate Root of the Secret." The King "Uprooted" his "Public Pride" to "Root" in "Private Fear." It signifies "The Magnetic Pull of Truth."

4 I will die there (lō ā-mūth shām): The scrolls highlight the "Fatal Root of the Dungeon." Jeremiah "Uproots" his "Stoicism" to "Root" in "Survival." It signify "The Physiological Reality of the Prophet."

5 Courtyard of the Guard (be-cha-tzar ham-mat-tā-rāh): The scrolls conclude with this "Custodial Root of Protection." "Isolation" is "Uprooted" for a "Controlled Visibility." It marks the transition to "The Muddy Cistern" in *Jeremiah 38*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Secret Meeting." The Word remains.
