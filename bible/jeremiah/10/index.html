Jeremiah 10

The King of Nations and the Scarecrow of Wood.

Worthless Wood

“They cut a tree out of the forest.”
“Like a scarecrow in a cucumber field, their idols cannot speak.” 1

King of Nations

“No one is like you, Lord.”
“Who should not fear you, King of the nations? This is your due.” 2

Maker of the World

“God made the earth by his power; he founded the world by his wisdom.” 3

Steps Not Our Own

“I know, Lord, that a person’s life is not their own; it is not for them to direct their steps.” 4
“Correct me, Lord, but only with justice.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Scarecrow in a Cucumber Field (ke-thō-mer be-miq-shāh): In the scrolls, this is the "Static Root of Idolatry." The "Idol" is "Rooted" in a "Cucumber Patch" (*Miqshah*) like a "Post" (*Thomer*). It signifies "The Total Impotence of Human-Made Gods."

2 King of the Nations (me-lekh hag-gō-yīm): The scrolls highlight the "Universal Root of Sovereignty." "Tribalism" is "Uprooted" and "Global Rule" is "Rooted" in the "Presence of God." It signify "The Exclusive Claim of the Creator."

3 Founded the World (mē-khīn tē-bhēl): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of Creation." The "World" (*Tebel*) is "Rooted" in "Wisdom" (*Chokhmah*). It signifies "The Intellectual Design of the Universe."

4 Life is not Their Own (lō le-ā-dhām dar-kō): The scrolls highlight the "Dependency Root of Humanity." "Autonomy" is "Uprooted" and "Reliance" is "Rooted" in the "Way of God." It signify "The Inability of Man to Self-Govern."

5 Correct Me (yas-se-rē-nī): The scrolls conclude with this "Pedagogical Root." "Anger" is "Uprooted" from the "Correction" (*Yasser*) so that "Justice" (*Mishpat*) might prevail. It marks the transition to "The Broken Covenant" in *Jeremiah 11*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Directed Step." Lead us.
