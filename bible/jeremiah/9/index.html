Jeremiah 9

The Fountain of Tears and the Only True Boast.

A Fountain of Tears

“Oh, that my head were a spring of water and my eyes a fountain of tears!” 1

Tongues of Lies

“They have taught their tongues to lie.”
“Everyone deceives their neighbor; no one speaks the truth.” 2

Call the Mourners

“Call for the mourning women to come; send for the most skillful of them.” 3

The Only Boast

“Let not the wise boast of their wisdom or the strong of their strength.”
“But let the one who boasts boast about this: that they have the understanding to know me.” 4

Uncircumcised Heart

“Israel is uncircumcised in heart.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Fountain of Tears (me-qōr de-mā-āh): In the scrolls, this is the "Hydrological Root of Lament." "Indifference" is "Uprooted" and "Grief" is "Rooted" as an "Eternal Supply" (*Fountain*). It signifies "The Spiritual Empathy of the Prophet."

2 Everyone Deceives (īsh be-rē-ē-hū ye-hā-thēl-lū): The scrolls highlight the "Societal Root of Corruption." "Trust" is "Uprooted" and "Mockery" (*Hathal*) is "Rooted" in relationships. It signify "The Total Breakdown of the Social Fabric."

3 Skillful Mourning Women (ha-cha-khā-mō-th): Literally "The Wise Ones." The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Grief." Professional "Lament" is "Rooted" in "Wisdom" (*Chakham*) to express the "Unbearable." It signifies "The Art of Sorrow."

4 Boast in Understanding (hath-hal-lēl... has-kēl we-yā-dhō-a ō-thī): The scrolls highlight the "Cognitive Root of Identity." "Pride" in "Human Assets" is "Uprooted" and "Glory" (*Halal*) is "Rooted" in the "Relational Knowledge" of God. It signifies "The Only Legitimate Pride."

5 Uncircumcised in Heart (ar-lē-lēbh): The scrolls conclude with this "Inhibitory Root." "Physical Covenant" is "Uprooted" as a "Safety Net" for those whose "Heart" (*Leb*) is "Closed" (*Arla*). It marks the transition to "The God of the Nations" in *Jeremiah 10*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The True Boast." Know Him.
