Jeremiah 15

The Deceptive Brook and the Wall of Bronze.

Even Moses and Samuel

“Even if Moses and Samuel were to stand before me, my heart would not go out to this people.” 1

I Ate Your Words

“When your words came, I ate them; they were my joy and my heart’s delight.” 2

The Deceptive Brook

“Why is my pain unending?”
“You are to me like a deceptive brook, like a spring that fails.” 3

Wall of Bronze

“If you utter worthy, not worthless, words, you will be my spokesman.” 4
“I will make you a fortified wall of bronze to this people.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Even Moses and Samuel (im-ya-a-mōdh Mō-sheh u-She-mū-ēl): In the scrolls, this is the "Ultimate Intercessory Root." These two figures "Rooted" Israel in "Safety" through prayer in the past. God "Uproots" even their "Influence" here. It signifies "The Point of No Return."

2 I Ate Your Words (way-yō-khe-lēm): The scrolls highlight the "Alimentary Root of Revelation." The "Prophet" "Roots" the "Word" (*Debhar*) in his "Internal Organs" (*Digest*). It signify "The Total Integration of Truth."

3 Deceptive Brook (ke-mō a-khābh): Literally "Like a Liar." The scrolls highlight the "Hydrological Root of Doubt." Jeremiah feels God has "Uprooted" his "Reliability" like a "Spring" (*Ma-yim*) that "Dries up." It signifies "The Crisis of the Prophetic Soul."

4 Worthy, not Worthless (me-iz-zō-lēl): The scrolls highlight the "Sifting Root of Speech." "Worthless thoughts" are "Uprooted" to "Root" "Worthy words" in the "Mouth" (*Spokesman/Pi*). It signify "The Requirement of Prophetic Purity."

5 Wall of Bronze (chō-math ne-chō-sheth): The scrolls conclude with this "Metallurgical Root of Resilience." The "Prophet" is "Re-Rooted" as an "Impenetrable Barrier" (*Wall*). It marks the transition to "The Day of Disaster" in *Jeremiah 16*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Fortified Spokesman." Speak the worthy.
