Jeremiah 44

The Queen of Heaven and the Final Rebellion.

Worship in Egypt

“Why bring disaster on yourselves by burning incense to other gods here in Egypt?” 1

The Queen of Heaven

“We will not listen to the message you have spoken to us in the name of the Lord.” 2
“We will certainly continue burning incense to the Queen of Heaven.” 3

Watching for Harm

“I am watching over you for harm, not for good.” 4

Whose Word Will Stand?

“They will know whose word will stand—mine or theirs.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Burning Incense (le-qat-tēr): In the scrolls, this is the "Aromatic Root of Apostasy." "True Worship" is "Uprooted" and "Rooted" in the "Smoke" (*Qatter*) of false altars. It signifies "The Spiritual Pollution of the Soul."

2 We will not Listen (ē-nen-nū shō-me-īm): The scrolls highlight the "Auditory Root of Defiance." "Obedience" is "Uprooted" by "Willful Deafness." It signify "The Final Rejection of the Prophetic Office."

3 Queen of Heaven (me-le-kheth hash-shā-ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Celestial Root of Idolatry." They "Root" their "Safety" and "Food" (*Plenty of food*) in a "Foreign Goddess." It signifies "The Materialistic Rationale for Sin."

4 Watching over you for Harm (shō-qēdh... le-rā-āh): This is the dark inversion of *Jeremiah 31:28*. The scrolls highlight the "Punitive Root of Vigilance." "Building and Planting" is "Uprooted" and "Destruction" is "Rooted" by the "Watchful Gaze" (*Shoqed*). It signifies "The Removal of Protective Grace."

5 Whose Word will Stand (de-bhar-mī im-mē-hem): The scrolls conclude with this "Ontological Root of Authority." "Human Opinion" is "Uprooted" by "Divine Reality." It marks the transition to "Message to Baruch" in *Jeremiah 45*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Standing Word." His word stands.
