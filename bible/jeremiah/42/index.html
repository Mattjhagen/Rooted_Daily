Jeremiah 42

The Ten Days of Waiting and the Choice of the Soil.

The Faithful Witness

“May the Lord be a true and faithful witness against us if we do not act in accordance with everything.” 1

Stay and Be Planted

“If you stay in this land, I will build you up and not tear you down; I will plant you and not uproot you.” 2

The Sword in Egypt

“Then the sword you fear will overtake you there in Egypt.” 3

The Fatal Mistake

“You made a fatal mistake when you sent me to the Lord.” 4
“You will die by the sword, famine and plague.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Faithful Witness (ēdh e-meth we-ne-e-mān): In the scrolls, this is the "Legalistic Root of the Vow." The people "Root" their "Promise" in "Truth" (*Emeth*) while intending "Deception." It signifies "The Hypocrisy of Prayer."

2 Plant you and not Uproot (u-ne-tat-tī eth-khem we-lō eth-tōsh): This is the defining "Rooted Daily" promise. The scrolls highlight the "Restorative Root of the Soil." Despite the war, "Survival" is "Rooted" in the "Local Land." It signifies "The Requirement of Geographic Obedience."

3 Sword you fear (ha-che-rebh a-sher at-tem ye-rē-īm): The scrolls highlight the "Recursive Root of Judgment." What they "Uproot" themselves to "Avoid" (*Fear*) will "Root" itself in them in the "New Land." It signify "The Futility of Human Evasion."

4 Fatal Mistake (hith-ē-them be-naph-shō-thē-khem): Literally "You led your souls astray." The scrolls highlight the "Internal Root of Error." Their "Hearts" were "Uprooted" from "Honesty" before the "Prayer" began. It signifies "The Spiritual Sabotage of Self."

5 Die by the Sword (bam-mā-weth): The scrolls conclude with this "Mortality Root." "Life" is "Uprooted" by "Disobedience." It marks the transition to "The Flight to Egypt" in *Jeremiah 43*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Ten Days." Wait for the Word.
