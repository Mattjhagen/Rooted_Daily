Jeremiah 51

The Destroying Mountain and the Stone in the River.

The Golden Cup

“Babylon was a golden cup in the Lord’s hand; she made the whole earth drunk.” 1

Destroying Mountain

“I am against you, you destroying mountain, you who destroy the whole earth.” 2

The Creator’s Wisdom

“He made the earth by his power; he founded the world by his wisdom.” 3

The Sinking Scroll

“Tie a stone to it and throw it into the Euphrates.”
“So will Babylon sink to rise no more.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Golden Cup (kōs zā-hābh): In the scrolls, this is the "Intoxicating Root of Empire." Babylon was the "Instrument" (*Cup*) of "Divine Judgment" (*Wine*) until her own "Iniquity" was "Full." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Imperial Poison."

2 Destroying Mountain (har ham-mash-chīth): The scrolls highlight the "Geological Root of Destruction." Babylon "Roots" her "Pride" in being a "Mountain" (*Har*) that "Uproots" others. God will "Burn" her until she is a "Burnt Mountain." It signify "The Demolition of the Stronghold."

3 He Founded the World (me-khīn tē-bhēl): Literally "He prepared the inhabited world." The scrolls highlight the "Cosmological Root of Order." Human "Empires" are "Uprooted," but God’s "Creation" is "Rooted" in "Wisdom" (*Bī-nāh*). It signifies "The Superiority of Divine Sovereignty."

4 Sink to Rise no more (tik-ka-ghā-bhā Babel): The scrolls conclude with this "Hydro-Static Root of Finality." The "Scroll" is "Rooted" in the "Riverbed" of the "Euphrates" to symbolize that the "City" will be "Uprooted" from "History." It marks the end of the prophecies of Jeremiah. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sinking Empire." Babylon is fallen.
