Jeremiah 40

The Chains of Ramah and the Fruits of Mizpah.

Released at Ramah

“Today I am freeing you from the chains on your wrists.” 1

Governor of the Ruins

“Settle down in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you.” 2

Summer Fruits

“Harvest the wine, summer fruit and olive oil.” 3
“They harvested an abundance of wine and summer fruit.”

The Plot of Ishmael

“Are you aware that Ishmael has been sent to take your life?” 4
“Gedaliah... did not believe them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Released at Ramah (ha-khoph-shī... hā-ya-dhay-im): In the scrolls, this is the "Manual Root of Liberty." "Bondage" is "Uprooted" from the "Hands" (*Hands/Yadhayim*). Jeremiah is "Re-Rooted" in "Choice." It signifies "The Paradoxical Freedom of the Captive."

2 It will go well with you (we-yī-tabh lā-khem): The scrolls highlight the "Pragmatic Root of Survival." Gedaliah "Roots" the "Remnant" in "Babylonian Reality." It signify "The Acceptance of the Divine Decree."

3 Summer Fruits (qā-yitz): The scrolls highlight the "Botanical Root of Continuity." Despite the "Uprooting" of the "Nation," the "Soil" still "Roots" "Fruit" (*Qayitz*). It signifies "The Persistent Blessing of the Land."

4 Sent to take your life (le-hak-kōth-khā nā-phesh): Literally "To strike your soul." The scrolls highlight the "Assassination Root of Treachery." "Ishmael" (God hears) represents a "False Root" of "Nationalism." It signify "The Internal Threat."

5 Did not believe (we-lō he-e-mīn): The scrolls conclude with this "Fatal Root of Naivety." Gedaliah "Uproots" the "Warning." It marks the transition to "The Assassination" in *Jeremiah 41*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Summer Fruit." Life continues in the ruins.
