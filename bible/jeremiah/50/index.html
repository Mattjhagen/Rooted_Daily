Jeremiah 50

The Broken Hammer and the Return to Zion.

The Hammer of the Earth

“How the hammer of the whole earth lies broken and shattered!” 1

The Way to Zion

“The people of Israel and Judah together will go in tears to seek the Lord.” 2
“Come, let us bind ourselves to the Lord in an everlasting covenant.” 3

The Arrogant One

“I am against you, you arrogant one... for your day has come.” 4

The Redeemer

“Their Redeemer is strong; the Lord Almighty is his name.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Hammer of the Earth (pat-tish kol-hā-ā-retz): In the scrolls, this is the "Instrumental Root of Conquest." "Babylon" (*The Hammer*) was the "Root" of destruction for others, but it is now "Uprooted" and "Shattered" (*Gada*). It signifies "The Demolition of the Demolisher."

2 In Tears (ha-lōkh ū-bhā-khō): The scrolls highlight the "Penitential Root of Return." "Exile" is "Uprooted" through "Sorrow." It signify "The Aesthetic of Repentance."

3 Everlasting Covenant (be-rīth ō-lām): The scrolls highlight the "Perpetual Root of Union." The "Broken Covenant" is "Uprooted" and replaced by a "Root" that "Will not be Forgotten." It signifies "The Restoration of the Divine Bond."

4 Arrogant One (zā-dhōn): Literally "Pride" or "Arrogance" personified. The scrolls highlight the "Inflationary Root of Sin." Babylon "Roots" her "Identity" in "Zadon." It signifies "The Personification of Hubris."

5 Redeemer (gō-a-lām): The scrolls conclude with this "Legal Root of Kinship." The *Goel* is the "Root" who "Vigorously Defends" the "Cause" of the "Family." It marks the transition to the second part of the oracle in *Jeremiah 51*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Strong Redeemer." The hammer falls.
