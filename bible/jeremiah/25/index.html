Jeremiah 25

The Seventy Years and the Cup of the Nations.

My Servant Nebuchadnezzar

“I will summon my servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.” 1

Seventy Years

“These nations will serve the king of Babylon seventy years.” 2

The Cup of Wrath

“Take from my hand this cup filled with the wine of my wrath.”
“Make all the nations to whom I send you drink it.” 3

The Roar from High

“The Lord will roar from on high; he will thunder from his holy dwelling.” 4
“Weep and wail, you shepherds!” 5

Translation Notes

1 My Servant Nebuchadnezzar (Nebh-ū-khadh-retz-tzar abh-dī): In the scrolls, this is the "Instrumental Root of Paganism." A "Heathen King" is "Rooted" as "God’s Servant" (*Abhed*) to execute judgment. It signifies "The Overruling Sovereignty of Yahweh over Empires."

2 Seventy Years (shibh-īm shā-nāh): The scrolls highlight the "Chronometric Root of Exile." "Permanence" is "Uprooted" and "Duration" is "Rooted" in a "Specific Number" (*70*). It signify "The Sabbath for the Land."

3 Cup of Wrath (kōs hay-ya-yin): The scrolls highlight the "Sacramental Root of Judgment." "Peace" is "Uprooted" and "Staggering" (*Drink*) is "Rooted" in the "Nations." It signifies "The Inevitability of Divine Consequence."

4 The Lord will Roar (Yah-weh mim-mā-rōm yish-āgh): The scrolls highlight the "Leonine Root of Divinity." "Silence" is "Uprooted" by the "Lion’s Roar" (*Sha-agh*). It signify "The Terrifying Presence of the Judge."

5 Weep and Wail (hē-lī-lū): The scrolls conclude with this "Auditory Root of Loss." "Security" is "Uprooted" for the "Leaders" (*Shepherds*). It marks the transition to "Jeremiah’s Trial" in *Jeremiah 26*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Cup of the Nations." Drink and tremble.
