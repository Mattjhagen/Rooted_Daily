Lamentations 5

The Final Prayer and the Fallen Crown.

Remember Us

“Remember, Lord, what has happened to us; look, and see our disgrace.” 1

The Fallen Crown

“The crown has fallen from our head. Woe to us, for we have sinned!” 2

The Eternal King

“You, Lord, reign forever; your throne endures from generation to generation.” 3

Restore Us

“Restore us to yourself, Lord, that we may return; renew our days as of old.” 4
“Unless you have utterly rejected us...” 5

Translation Notes

1 Remember (ze-khōr): The scrolls highlight the "Mnemic Root of Petition." "Indifference" is "Uprooted." To "Root" in "God’s Memory" (*Zekhor*) is the only "Safety." It signifies "The Requirement of Divine Attention."

2 Crown has Fallen (na-phe-lāh a-te-reth): The scrolls highlight the "Regal Root of Shame." "Sovereignty" is "Uprooted." The "Crown" (*Atereth*) is no longer "Rooted" on the "Head" (*Rosh*). It signify "The Loss of National Dignity."

3 Your Throne Endures (kis-a-khā le-dhōr wā-dhōr): The scrolls highlight the "Celestial Root of Stability." Human "Seats" are "Uprooted," but the "Divine Throne" (*Kisse*) is "Rooted" in "Perpetuity." It signifies "The Only Constant in Chaos."

4 Restore us (ha-shī-bhē-nū): Literally "Return us." The scrolls highlight the "Repentant Root of Grace." "Exile" is "Uprooted" through a "Return" (*Shubh*) that is "Initiated by God." It signify "The Aesthetic of Restoration."

5 Utterly Rejected (mā-ōs mā-as-tā-nū): The scrolls conclude with this "Haunting Root of Uncertainty." The book ends with a "Question" rather than a "Full Stop." It marks the transition to "The Visions" in *Ezekiel 1*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Renewed Days." Bring us back.
