Lamentations 4

The Tarnished Gold and the Breath of our Nostrils.

How the Gold has Dimmed

“How the gold has lost its luster, the fine gold become dull!” 1

The Pottery Sons

“The precious sons of Zion... how they are now regarded as pottery jars.” 2

The Famine

“The infant’s tongue sticks to the roof of its mouth.”
“Compassionate women have cooked their own children.” 3

The Life Breath

“The Lord’s anointed, our very life breath, was caught in their traps.” 4

The End of Punishment

“Daughter Zion, your punishment will end; he will not prolong your exile.” 5

Translation Notes

1 How the Gold has Dimmed (ē-khāh yū-am zā-hābh): The scrolls highlight the "Metallurgical Root of Decay." "Glory" is "Uprooted" as the "Gold" (*Zahabh*) becomes "Cloudy" (*Yuam*). It signifies "The Degradation of the Nation’s Value."

2 Pottery Jars (ne-bhē-lē che-reś): The scrolls highlight the "Fragile Root of Worth." "Precious Sons" are "Uprooted" from their "Status" and "Rooted" in the "Common Soil" of "Earthenware." It signify "The Dehumanization of War."

3 Cooked their own Children (biś-śe-lū yal-dhē-hen): The scrolls highlight the "Violated Maternal Root." "Compassion" (*Rachamanith*) is "Uprooted" by "Famine." It signifies "The Absolute Nadir of Human Suffering."

4 Our Life Breath (rū-ach ap-pē-nū): Literally "The breath of our nostrils." The scrolls highlight the "Respiratory Root of the King." The "King" (*Anointed/Mashiach*) is "Rooted" as the "Soul" of the people. His "Capture" is the "Uprooting" of their "Life." It signifies "The Death of the Davidic Shadow."

5 Punishment will End (tam a-wō-nēkh): The scrolls conclude with this "Expiatory Root." "Exile" is "Uprooted" as the "Guilt" (*Awōn*) is "Finished" (*Tam*). It marks the transition to the final prayer in *Lamentations 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Ending Exile." The gold will return.
