Lamentations 2

The Lord as Enemy and the Silence of the Prophets.

The Lord is Like an Enemy

“The Lord is like an enemy; he has swallowed up Israel.” 1

The Wasted Garden

“He has laid waste his dwelling like a garden.” 2

Fainting in the Streets

“Children and infants faint in the streets of the city.” 3

Pour Out Your Heart

“Pour out your heart like water in the presence of the Lord.” 4
“Lift up your hands to him for the lives of your children.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Lord is like an Enemy (hā-yāh a-dhō-nāy ke-ō-yēbh): The scrolls highlight the "Hostile Root of the Divine." "Protection" is "Uprooted" and God Himself "Roots" His "Identity" as the "Enemy" (*Oyebh*). It signifies "The Ultimate Spiritual Crisis."

2 Like a Garden (ke-ghan): The scrolls highlight the "Organic Root of Destruction." The "Temple" (*Dwelling*) is "Uprooted" as easily as a "Garden" (*Gan*) is cleared. It signify "The Fragility of the Sacred Infrastructure."

3 Children Faint (ba-a-tōph ō-lēl): The scrolls highlight the "Physiological Root of Famine." "Life" is "Uprooted" by "Starvation." It signifies "The Biological Death of the Future."

4 Heart like Water (lib-bēkh kam-mā-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Liquid Root of Prayer." "Hardness" is "Uprooted" as the "Heart" (*Leb*) is "Poured" (*Shaphakh*) like "Water." It signify "The Total Emotional Surrender."

5 Lift up your Hands (śe-ī ē-lāw kap-pay-ikh): The scrolls conclude with this "Manual Root of Petition." "Despair" is "Uprooted" through "Active Crying." It marks the transition to the individual lament in *Lamentations 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Poured Heart." Cry out in the night.
