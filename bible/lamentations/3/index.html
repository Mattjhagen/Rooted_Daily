Lamentations 3

The Man of Affliction and the Faithful Morning.

The Man of Sorrow

“I am the man who has seen affliction by the rod of the Lord’s wrath.” 1

The Central Hope

“Yet this I call to mind and therefore I have hope:”
“Because of the Lord’s great love we are not consumed, for his compassions never fail.” 2
“They are new every morning; great is your faithfulness.” 3

Good to Wait

“It is good to wait quietly for the salvation of the Lord.” 4

Examine Our Ways

“Let us examine our ways and test them, and let us return to the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Man who has seen Affliction (ge-bher rā-āh o-nī): The scrolls highlight the "Representative Root of Suffering." The individual (*Geber*) "Roots" his "Experience" in the "Affliction" (*Oni*) of the collective. It signifies "The Incarnational Nature of the Prophet."

2 Compassions never Fail (lō-khāl-lū ra-cha-māw): The scrolls highlight the "Exhaustless Root of Mercy." "Despair" is "Uprooted" by the "Fomb" (*Womb/Racham*) of Divine Love. It signify "The Reproductive Nature of Grace."

3 Great is your Faithfulness (rab-bāh e-mū-nā-the-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Diurnal Root of Reliability." "Mercy" is "Rooted" in the "Morning" (*Boqer*). It signifies "The Reset Button of the Kingdom."

4 Wait Quietly (we-yā-chīl dū-mām): The scrolls highlight the "Silent Root of Hope." "Anxiety" is "Uprooted" through "Stillness" (*Dumam*). It signify "The Strength of Passive Trust."

5 Examine our Ways (nach-pe-śāh dhe-rā-khē-nū): The scrolls conclude with this "Inquisitorial Root of Repentance." We "Uproot" our "Excuses" to "Root" in "Return" (*Shubh*). It marks the transition to "The Tarnished Gold" in *Lamentations 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The New Morning." Great is His faithfulness.
