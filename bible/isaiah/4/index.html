Isaiah 4

The Branch and the Canopy of Glory.

The Branch of the Lord

“In that day the Branch of the Lord will be beautiful and glorious.” 1

Spirit of Fire

“The Lord will wash away the filth... by a spirit of judgment and a spirit of fire.” 2

A Canopy of Glory

“Over everything the glory will be a canopy.” 3

Refuge from the Storm

“It will be a shelter and shade from the heat... and a refuge from the storm and rain.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Branch of the Lord (Tze-mach Yah-weh): In the scrolls, this is the "Messianic Root." The "Branch" (*Tzemach*) is "Rooted" in the "Soil of the Future." It is the "Emergent Growth" after the "Uprooting" of the city. It signifies "The Persistent Hope."

2 Spirit of Fire (be-Ru-ach Ba-er): The scrolls highlight the "Thermal Root of Sanctification." Cleansing is "Rooted" in "Burning" (*Baer*). It signifies "The Purity of Intense Judgment."

3 Canopy (Chup-pah): The scrolls highlight the "Marital Root of Protection." The "Glory" (*Kabod*) is "Rooted" as a "Chuppah" (*Canopy/Bridal-Tent*) over the "Remnant." It signify "The Divine Intimacy of Restoration."

4 Refuge from the Storm (u-le-Mach-seh mim-Ze-rem): The scrolls conclude with this "Shelter Root." The "Canopy" is "Rooted" in "Function." It marks the transition to "The Song of the Vineyard" in *Isaiah 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Safe Canopy." Hide in the glory.
