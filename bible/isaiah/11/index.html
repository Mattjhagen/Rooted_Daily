Isaiah 11

The Branch and the Peaceable Kingdom.

The Shoot of Jesse

“A shoot will come up from the stump of Jesse; from his roots a Branch will bear fruit.” 1

The Sevenfold Spirit

“The Spirit of the Lord will rest on him—the Spirit of wisdom and understanding... the Spirit of the knowledge and fear of the Lord.” 2

Wolf with the Lamb

“The wolf will live with the lamb, the leopard will lie down with the goat.”
“And a little child will lead them.” 3

Filled with Knowledge

“The earth will be filled with the knowledge of the Lord as the waters cover the sea.” 4
“In that day the Root of Jesse will stand as a banner for the peoples.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Shoot from the Stump (Cho-ter mig-Ge-za): In the scrolls, this is the "Resurrection Root of the Dynasty." The "Stump" (*Geza*) of the "Uprooted" house of Jesse "Roots" a "New Shoot" (*Choter*). The "Branch" (*Ne-tzer*) "Roots" the "Fruit" (*Yiph-reh*). It signifies "The Messianic Regeneration."

2 Spirit... will Rest (we-na-chah a-law Ru-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Atmospheric Root of Anointing." The "Sevenfold Spirit" (*Wisdom/Understanding/Counsel/Might/Knowledge/Fear*) "Roots" the "Authority" of the King. It signify "The Divine Intelligence of the Messiah."

3 Little Child will Lead (we-Na-ar qa-ton no-heg bam): The scrolls highlight the "Innocent Root of Leadership." The "Predatory Instinct" (*Wolf/Leopard*) is "Uprooted" by the "Presence" of the "Child" (*Na-ar*). It signifies "The Restoration of Edenic Harmony."

4 Filled with Knowledge (ma-le-ah ha-A-retz De-ah): The scrolls highlight the "Saturing Root of Enlightenment." The "Knowledge" (*De-ah*) is "Rooted" as an "Oceanic Force" (*As the waters cover the sea*). It signify "The End of Spiritual Ignorance."

5 Root of Jesse (Sho-resh Yi-shay): The scrolls conclude with this "Global Root." The Messiah is both the "Shoot" (Son) and the "Root" (Source). He "Roots" the "Rallying Point" (*Nes*) for all nations. It marks the transition to "The Song of Salvation" in *Isaiah 12*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Fruiting Branch." Rally to the Root.
