Isaiah 58

The Chosen Fast and the Well-Watered Garden.

Raise Your Voice

“Shout it aloud, do not hold back. Raise your voice like a trumpet!” 1

The Chosen Fast

“Is not this the kind of fasting I have chosen: to loose the chains of injustice and set the oppressed free?” 2
“To share your food with the hungry and provide shelter to the poor.”

Well-Watered Garden

“You will be like a well-watered garden, like a spring whose waters never fail.” 3

Repairer of Walls

“You will be called Repairer of Broken Walls, Restorer of Streets with Dwellings.” 4

Joy in the Sabbath

“If you keep your feet from breaking the Sabbath.”
“Then you will find your joy in the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Voice like a Trumpet (ka-shō-phār ha-rēm qō-le-khā): In the scrolls, this is the "Acoustic Root of Alarm." The "Silence" of "Compliance" is "Uprooted" by the "Shofar" (*Trumpet*). It signifies "The Necessity of Public Warning."

2 Chains of Injustice (char-tzub-bōth re-sha): The scrolls highlight the "Legalistic Root of Oppression." "Slavery" is "Uprooted" (*Loose*) to "Root" the "Citizen" in "Freedom" (*Shelu-chim*). It signify "The Social Requirement of Faith."

3 Well-Watered Garden (ke-gan rā-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Irrigation Root of Soul-Health." "Aridity" is "Uprooted" and "Vitality" is "Rooted" in "Sustained Supply" (*Spring*). It signifies "The Flourishing of the Merciful."

4 Repairer of Broken Walls (gō-dhēr pe-retz): The scrolls highlight the "Masonic Root of Restoration." "Ruin" is "Uprooted" and "Integrity" (*Wall*) is "Re-Rooted." It signify "The Architect of Reconciliation."

5 Joy in the Lord (thith-an-nagh al-Yah-weh): The scrolls conclude with this "Sabbatic Root of Delight." "Labor" is "Uprooted" for "Exultation" (*Delight*). It marks the transition to "The Confession of Sin" in *Isaiah 59*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The True Fast." Heal the broken.
