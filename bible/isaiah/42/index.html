Isaiah 42

The Gentle Servant and the New Song.

My Chosen One

“Here is my servant, whom I uphold, my chosen one in whom I delight.” 1

The Bruised Reed

“A bruised reed he will not break, and a smoldering wick he will not snuff out.” 2

Light for the Gentiles

“I will keep you and make you to be a covenant for the people and a light for the Gentiles.” 3

Sing a New Song

“Sing to the Lord a new song, his praise from the ends of the earth.” 4

Leading the Blind

“I will lead the blind by ways they have not known.” 5

Translation Notes

1 My Servant (Ab-dī): In the scrolls, this is the "Functional Root of the Messiah." The "Servant" is "Rooted" in "Upholding" (*Et-mokh*) by the Father. It signifies "The Divine Commission."

2 Bruised Reed (qā-neh rā-tzūtz): The scrolls highlight the "Nurturing Root of Mercy." The "Vulnerable" (*Bruised/Smoldering*) is not "Uprooted" (*Broken*) but "Rooted" in "Gentleness." It signify "The Character of the King."

3 Light for the Gentiles (le-ōr gō-yīm): The scrolls highlight the "Illuminating Root of Expansion." The "Covenant" (*Berith*) is "Rooted" in the "Global Scope" (*Goyim*). It signifies "Universal Salvation."

4 New Song (shīr chā-dhāsh): The scrolls highlight the "Acoustic Root of Redemption." The "Old Lament" is "Uprooted" by the "Fresh Praise" (*Chadash*). It signify "The New Era of Grace."

5 Ways they have not Known (be-de-rekh lō yā-dhā-ū): The scrolls conclude with this "Pedagogical Root." "Blindness" (*Iwwer*) is "Uprooted" by "Guidance" (*Lead*). It marks the transition to "The Only Savior" in *Isaiah 43*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Gentle Servant." Follow the Light.
