Isaiah 46

The Idols that are Carried and the God who Carries.

Bowing Idols

“Bel bows down, Nebo stoops low; their idols are borne by beasts of burden.”
“They themselves go into captivity.” 1

I Will Carry You

“Listen to me... you whom I have upheld since you were conceived.”
“Even to your old age and gray hairs I am he... I will carry you.” 2

The Bird of Prey

“From the east I summon a bird of prey; from a far-off land, a man to fulfill my purpose.” 3

Salvation in Zion

“I will grant salvation to Zion, my splendor to Israel.” 4
“My righteousness is not far away.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Borne by Beasts (ha-yū la-a-tzab-bē-khem ma-śā): In the scrolls, this is the "Logistical Root of Idolatry." The "Idols" (*At-zab*) are "Uprooted" from their "Pedestals" and become "Burdens" (*Masa*) for "Animals." It signifies "The Weight of Falsehood."

2 I will Carry (a-nī es-bōl): The scrolls highlight the "Paternal Root of Sustenance." God "Roots" His "Relationship" in the "Act of Carrying" (*Sa-bal*) from "Womb" (*Be-ten*) to "Gray Hairs" (*She-bhah*). It signify "The Divine Stamina."

3 Bird of Prey (a-yit): The scrolls highlight the "Apex Root of Purpose." The "Conqueror" (*Cyrus*) is "Rooted" as a "Bird of Prey" (*Ayit*) summoned to "Uproot" the "Captivity." It signifies "The Speed of Providence."

4 Salvation in Zion (we-nā-that-tī bhe-Tzi-yōn ye-shū-āh): The scrolls highlight the "Topographical Root of Redemption." "Deliverance" is "Rooted" in the "Place" (*Zion*). It signify "The Restoration of the Center."

5 Splendor to Israel (le-Yis-rā-ēl tiph-ar-tī): The scrolls conclude with this "Beauty Root." God’s "Glory" (*Tipharah*) is "Rooted" in His "People." It marks the transition to "The Fall of Babylon" in *Isaiah 47*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Carrying God." He will not drop you.
