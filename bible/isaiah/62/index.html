Isaiah 62

The New Name and the Watchmen of No Rest.

I Will Not Keep Silent

“For Zion’s sake I will not keep silent, for Jerusalem’s sake I will not remain quiet.” 1

Hephzibah and Beulah

“You will be called by a new name.”
“You will be called Hephzibah, and your land Beulah.” 2
“As a bridegroom rejoices over his bride, so will your God rejoice over you.”

No Rest for the Lord

“I have posted watchmen on your walls, Jerusalem; they will never be silent day or night.” 3
“Give him no rest until he establishes Jerusalem.” 4

Prepare the Way

“Build up, build up the highway! Remove the stones. Raise a banner for the nations.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Not keep Silent (lō e-che-she): In the scrolls, this is the "Verbal Root of Determination." God "Roots" His "Identity" in the "Continuous Act" of "Vindication" (*Tzedeq*). It signifies "The Divine Refusal to Ignore Suffering."

2 Hephzibah and Beulah (Cheph-tzī-bhāh... Be-ū-lāh): The scrolls highlight the "Nomenclatural Root of Love." "Deserted" (*Azubhah*) is "Uprooted" and "Delight" (*Cheph-tzi*) and "Marriage" (*Beulah*) are "Rooted" as "New Names." It signifies "The Restoration of the Marital Covenant."

3 Watchmen on your Walls (al-chō-mō-tha-yi-kh hiph-qadh-tī sho-me-rīm): The scrolls highlight the "Vigilant Root of Prayer." The "Watchmen" (*Shomerim*) are "Rooted" as "Intercessors" who "Remind" (*Mazkirim*) the Lord. It signify "The Collaborative Nature of Kingdom Work."

4 Give Him No Rest (al-tit-tā-nū de-mī lō): The scrolls highlight the "Importunate Root of Intercession." "Silence" is "Uprooted" until "Zion" is "Established" (*Kūn*). It signifies "The Boldness of Prayer."

5 Build up the Highway (sal-lū has-me-sil-lāh): The scrolls conclude with this "Structural Root of Return." The "Obstacles" (*Stones*) are "Uprooted" to "Root" the "Path" for the "People" (*Am*). It marks the transition to "The Warrior in Crimson" in *Isaiah 63*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The New Name." No more deserted.
