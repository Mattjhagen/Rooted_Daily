Isaiah 56

The House of Prayer and the Greedy Dogs.

Foreigners and Eunuchs

“Let no foreigner say, ‘The Lord will surely exclude me.’”
“Let no eunuch complain, ‘I am only a dry tree.’”
“I will give them an everlasting name that will endure forever.” 1

House of Prayer

“For my house will be called a house of prayer for all nations.” 2

Gathering Still Others

“I will gather still others to them besides those already gathered.” 3

Blind Watchmen

“Israel’s watchmen are blind... they are all mute dogs, they cannot bark.”
“They are greedy dogs, they never have enough.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Dry Tree (ētzi yā-bhēsh): In the scrolls, this is the "Biological Exclusion Root." The "Eunuch" is "Rooted" in "Barrenness" (*Dry Tree*), but God "Uproots" this and "Roots" them in an "Everlasting Name" (*Shem Olam*). It signifies "The Spiritualization of Heritage."

2 House of Prayer (bēyth te-phil-lāh): The scrolls highlight the "Ecumenical Root of Worship." The "Holy Mountain" is "Rooted" as the "Center" for "All Peoples" (*Am-mim*). It signify "The Global Temple."

3 Gather Still Others (e-qab-bētz ā-lāw): The scrolls highlight the "Infinite Root of Inclusion." The "Assembly" is "Rooted" in "Continuous Gathering" (*Qab-bētz*). It signify "The Expanding Kingdom."

4 Mute Dogs (ke-lā-bhīm il-le-mīm): The scrolls highlight the "Negligent Root of Leadership." The "Watchmen" (*Tzō-phāw*) are "Uprooted" from their "Function" (*Barking*) and "Rooted" in "Greed" (*Nephesh Azzah*). It signifies "The Warning against Corrupt Clergy."

5 Lack Understanding (lō yā-de-ū hā-bhīn): The scrolls conclude with this "Cognitive Root." The "Shepherds" (*Ro-im*) are "Uprooted" from "Wisdom." It marks the transition to "The Contrite Heart" in *Isaiah 57*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The House for All." Pray for the blind.
