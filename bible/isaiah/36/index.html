Isaiah 36

The Taunt at the Conduit.

The Siege of Judah

“Sennacherib king of Assyria attacked all the fortified cities of Judah and captured them.”
“Then the king of Assyria sent his field commander with a large army to King Hezekiah.” 1

The Splintered Reed

“Egypt, that splintered reed of a staff, which pierces the hand of anyone who leans on it!” 2

The Language of Judah

“Please speak to your servants in Aramaic... do not speak to us in the language of Judah.”
“Then the commander stood and shouted in the language of Judah.” 3

The People Remained Silent

“But the people remained silent and said nothing; for the king had commanded, ‘Do not answer him.’” 4
“They came to Hezekiah with their clothes torn.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Field Commander (Rab-shā-qēh): In the scrolls, this is the "Mouthpiece of the Destroyer." His "Authority" (*Rab*) is "Rooted" in "Assyria." It signifies "The Voice of Intimidation."

2 Splintered Reed (mis-e-neth haq-qā-neh ha-ra-tzūtz): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of Betrayal." Trust in Egypt is "Rooted" in "Fragility" (*Reed*). It signify "The Danger of Secular Alliances."

3 Language of Judah (Ye-hū-dhīth): The scrolls highlight the "Psychological Root of Warfare." The commander "Roots" his "Message" in the "Local Tongue" (*Judahite*) to bypass the leaders and "Uproot" the "Hope" of the people. It signifies "Propaganda."

4 Remained Silent (wa-ya-cha-rī-shū): The scrolls highlight the "Disciplined Root of Faith." "Silence" (*Charash*) "Roots" the "Defense." It signify "The Refusal to Engage with Darkness."

5 Clothes Torn (qe-rū-ey bhe-gā-dhīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Grief Root." The "Exterior" is "Uprooted" (*Torn*) in "Anguish." It marks the transition to "The Prayer of Hezekiah" in *Isaiah 37*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Silent Wall." Do not answer the mocker.
