Isaiah 44

The Pouring Spirit and the Folly of the Idol.

Water on Thirsty Land

“For I will pour water on the thirsty land, and streams on the dry ground.” 1
“I will pour out my Spirit on your offspring.”

First and Last

“I am the first and I am the last; apart from me there is no God.” 2

Folly of the Carpenter

“Half of the wood he burns in the fire; he makes a fire and bakes bread.”
“From the rest he makes a god, his idol; he bows down to it.” 3

Cyrus My Shepherd

“Who says of Cyrus, ‘He is my shepherd and will accomplish all that I please.’” 4
“He will say of Jerusalem, ‘Let its foundations be laid.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 Water on Thirsty Land (ma-yim al-tzā-mē): In the scrolls, this is the "Hydraulic Root of Blessing." The "Spirit" (*Ruach*) is "Rooted" as "Water" (*Mayim*) for the "Exhausted" (*Tzame*). It signifies "The Divine Relief."

2 First and Last (ri-shōn wa-a-cha-rōn): The scrolls highlight the "Alphabetic Root of Eternity." God "Roots" the "Entirety of History" (*A to Z*) in His own "Being." It signify "The Exclusion of Rivals."

3 From the Rest he makes a God (u-she-ē-rī-thō le-ēl ā-śā): The scrolls highlight the "Satirical Root of Idolatry." The "Leftover" (*She-erith*) of a "Utility" (*Fuel*) is "Re-Rooted" as an "Object of Worship" (*El*). It signifies "The Spiritual Insanity of the Craftsman."

4 Cyrus My Shepherd (Kō-resh rō-ī): The scrolls highlight the "Imperial Root of Agency." A "Foreign King" is "Rooted" as the "Shepherd" (*Ro-i*) of the "Holy Flock." It signify "God’s Mastery over Geopolitics."

5 Foundations be Laid (tu-was-sēdh): The scrolls conclude with this "Structural Root." Jerusalem is "Re-Rooted" in its "Foundation" (*Tuwassed*). It marks the transition to "The Anointing of Cyrus" in *Isaiah 45*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Living Spirit." Build the foundation.
