Isaiah 53

The Man of Sorrows and the Root of Dry Ground.

Root out of Dry Ground

“He grew up before him like a tender shoot, and like a root out of dry ground.” 1

Man of Suffering

“He was despised and rejected by mankind, a man of suffering, and familiar with pain.” 2

Pierced for Us

“He was pierced for our transgressions, he was crushed for our iniquities.” 3
“By his wounds we are healed.”

Led Like a Lamb

“He was led like a lamb to the slaughter... he did not open his mouth.” 4

Guilt Offering

“It was the Lord’s will to crush him.”
“By his knowledge my righteous servant will justify many.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Root out of Dry Ground (we-kha-shō-resh mē-e-retz tzi-yāh): In the scrolls, this is the "Biological Root of the Incarnation." The "Messiah" is "Rooted" in "Barrenness" (*Dry Ground*). It signifies "Life emerging from Death."

2 Man of Suffering (ish makh-ō-bhōth): Literally "Man of Pains." The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Root of the Servant." His "Identity" is "Rooted" in the "Experience of Human Agony." It signify "The Familiarity of God with Grief."

3 Pierced for our Transgressions (me-chō-lāl mip-pe-shā-ē-nū): The scrolls highlight the "Vicarious Root of Atonement." "Sin" is "Uprooted" by the "Piercing" (*Chalaal*) of the "Representative." It signifies "The Penal Substitution."

4 Led like a Lamb (ka-śeh lat-te-bhach): The scrolls highlight the "Silent Root of Sacrifice." The "Servant" is "Rooted" in "Quiet Submission" (*Open not his mouth*). It signify "The Voluntary Nature of the Passion."

5 Justify Many (yatz-dīq... la-rab-bīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Legal Root of Grace." "Guilt" is "Uprooted" and "Righteousness" is "Rooted" in the "Many." It marks the transition to "The Glory of Zion" in *Isaiah 54*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Suffering Savior." He bore it all.
