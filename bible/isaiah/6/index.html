Isaiah 6

The Vision and the Commission.

High and Exalted

“I saw the Lord, high and exalted, seated on a throne; and the train of his robe filled the temple.” 1

Holy, Holy, Holy

“Holy, holy, holy is the Lord Almighty; the whole earth is full of his glory.” 2

The Live Coal

“Woe to me! I am ruined! For I am a man of unclean lips.”
“See, this has touched your lips; your guilt is taken away.” 3

Here Am I. Send Me!

“Whom shall I send? And who will go for us?”
“And I said, ‘Here am I. Send me!’” 4
“The holy seed will be the stump in the land.” 5

Translation Notes

1 High and Exalted (Ram we-Nis-sa): In the scrolls, this is the "Vertical Root of Sovereignty." The "Vision" (*Mar-eh*) "Roots" the Lord in "Transcendence" (*Ram*) above the "National Crisis" (*The Death of Uzziah*). It signifies "The Stability of the Divine Government."

2 Holy, Holy, Holy (Qa-dosh, Qa-dosh, Qa-dosh): The scrolls highlight the "Triune Root of Alterity." "Holiness" (*Qadosh*) is "Rooted" in "Otherness." The "Repetition" signifies "Infinity." It signifies "The Absolute Purity of the Creator."

3 Live Coal (Ritz-pah): The scrolls highlight the "Thermal Root of Atonement." The "Lips" (*Se-pha-tha-yim*) are "Uprooted" from "Uncleanness" (*Tame*) by the "Burning" (*Ritzpah*) of the "Altar" (*Miz-be-ach*). It signify "Grace through Pain."

4 Here am I. Send me! (Hin-ne-ni she-la-che-ni): The scrolls highlight the "Volitional Root of Apostleship." "Hinneni" (*Behold me*) "Roots" the "Human" in "Availability." It signify "The Integration of Vision and Mission."

5 The Holy Seed (Ze-ra Qo-desh): The scrolls conclude with this "Remnant Root." Even after "Cutting down" (*Shal-le-kheth*), the "Stump" (*Matz-tze-beth*) "Roots" the "Future" (*Zera Qodesh*). It marks the transition to "The Sign of Immanuel" in *Isaiah 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Persistent Stump." Life returns.
