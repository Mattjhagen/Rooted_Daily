Isaiah 8

The Flood and the Sanctuary.

Waters of Shiloah

“Because this people has rejected the gently flowing waters of Shiloah...”
“The Lord is about to bring against them the mighty floodwaters of the Euphrates.” 1

Stone of Stumbling

“The Lord Almighty is the one you are to regard as holy.”
“He will be a holy place; but a stone that causes people to stumble.” 2

Consulting the Dead

“Why consult the dead on behalf of the living?” 3

Utter Darkness

“They will look toward the earth and see only distress and darkness... and they will be thrust into utter darkness.” 4
“I and the children the Lord has given me are signs and symbols.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Waters of Shiloah (Ma-yim hash-Shi-lo-ach): In the scrolls, this is the "Hydraulic Root of Choice." The "Gently Flowing" (*at*) waters of God are "Uprooted" by the "Mighty Flood" (*Ma-yim ha-kab-bi-rim*) of the world (*Euphrates/Assyria*). It signifies "The Consequence of Rejecting Peace."

2 Stone of Stumbling (E-ben Ne-geph): The scrolls highlight the "Obstacle Root of Holiness." The same God who is a "Sanctuary" (*Miq-dash*) "Roots" a "Stumbling" (*Negeph*) for the "Rebellious." It signify "The Double-Edged Nature of Presence."

3 Dead on behalf of the Living (be-ad ha-Chay-yim el-ham-Me-thim): The scrolls highlight the "Necromantic Root of Folly." "Inquiring" (*Darash*) of the "Dead" (*Methim*) "Uproots" the "Connection" to the "Living God" (*Elohim*). It signifies "Spiritual Insanity."

4 Utter Darkness (wa-A-phe-lah me-nud-dach): The scrolls highlight the "Optical Root of Despair." Without the "Law" (*Torah*) and "Testimony" (*Te-u-dah*), the soul is "Rooted" in "Gloom" (*Ts-a-rah*). It signify "The Absence of Light."

5 Signs and Symbols (le-o-thoth u-le-mo-phe-thim): The scrolls conclude with this "Representative Root." Isaiah’s "Children" (*Ye-la-dim*) are "Rooted" in "National Prediction." It marks the transition to "The Child is Born" in *Isaiah 9*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Living Sign." Watch the children.
