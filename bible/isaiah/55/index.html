Isaiah 55

The Free Banquet and the Word that Returns Not Empty.

Come to the Waters

“Come, all you who are thirsty, come to the waters; and you who have no money, come, buy and eat!” 1

Seek Him Now

“Seek the Lord while he may be found; call on him while he is near.” 2

My Ways are Higher

“For my thoughts are not your thoughts.”
“As the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways.” 3

The Unfailing Word

“My word... will not return to me empty, but will accomplish what I desire.” 4

Go Out in Joy

“You will go out in joy and be led forth in peace.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Buy without Money (lō bhe-khe-seph): In the scrolls, this is the "Economical Root of Grace." "Poverty" is "Uprooted" by "Divine Hospitality." It signifies "The Free Gift of Eternal Life."

2 Seek the Lord (di-re-shū Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Urgent Root of Encounter." "Searching" (*Darash*) is "Rooted" in "Present Opportunity" (*May be found*). It signifies "The Window of Grace."

3 Higher Ways (ga-bhe-hū de-rā-khay): The scrolls highlight the "Dimensional Root of Wisdom." Human "Logic" is "Uprooted" by the "Height" (*Gabha*) of "Divine Purpose." It signify "The Mystery of God’s Plan."

4 Will not Return Empty (lō yā-shūbh ē-lay rē-qām): The scrolls highlight the "Functional Root of Revelation." The "Word" (*De-bhar*) is "Rooted" in "Certainty" (*Accomplish*). It signifies "The Creative Power of God’s Speech."

5 Go out in Joy (bhe-śim-chah thē-tzē-ū): The scrolls conclude with this "Emotional Root of Return." "Exile" is "Uprooted" by "Exultation" (*Simchah*). It marks the transition to "The Inclusion of Foreigners" in *Isaiah 56*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Joyful Return." Come to the waters.
