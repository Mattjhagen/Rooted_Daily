Isaiah 14

The Fall of the Haughty Morning Star.

How the Oppressor has Ceased

“How the oppressor has come to an end! How his fury has ended!” 1

Morning Star, Son of the Dawn

“How you have fallen from heaven, morning star, son of the dawn!” 2
“You said in your heart, ‘I will make myself like the Most High.’” 3

The Depth of the Pit

“But you are brought down to the realm of the dead, to the depths of the pit.” 4

The Established Refuge

“The Lord has established Zion, and in her his afflicted people will find refuge.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Oppressor has Ceased (sha-bath no-gesh): In the scrolls, this is the "Sabbath Root of Tyranny." The "Oppressor" (*Nogesh*) is "Uprooted" and made to "Rest" (*Shabath*) in death. It signifies "The Divine Termination of Abuse."

2 Morning Star (He-lel ben-Sha-char): Literally "Shining one, son of the morning." The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of Pride." Lucifer (*Latin for Helel*) is "Rooted" in "Reflected Light" but "Uprooted" by "Ambition." It signify "The Fall of the Celestial Usurper."

3 Like the Most High (e-dam-meh le-El-yon): The scrolls highlight the "Imitative Root of Sin." The desire to "Root" one’s "Being" in the "Identity" of God (*Elyon*) is the "Ultimate Uprooting." It signifies "The Hubris of Self-Deification."

4 Depths of the Pit (yar-ke-they Bor): The scrolls highlight the "Subterranean Root of Shame." The one who sought the "Heights" (*Heavens*) is "Rooted" in the "Depths" (*Pit*). It signify "The Symmetrical Justice of God."

5 Refuge in Her (be-cha-su a-ni-ye am-mow): The scrolls conclude with this "Communal Root of Safety." While the "Morning Star" falls, the "Afflicted" (*Aniye*) are "Rooted" in "Zion" (*Refuge/Chasu*). It marks the transition to "The Burden of Moab" in *Isaiah 15*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Eternal City." Find your rest.
