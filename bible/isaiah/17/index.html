Isaiah 17

The Heap of Ruins and the Forgotten Rock.

Damascus No More

“See, Damascus will no longer be a city but will become a heap of ruins.” 1

The Beaten Olive

“Yet some gleanings will remain, as when an olive tree is beaten.” 2
“In that day people will look to their Maker.”

The Forgotten Rock

“You have forgotten God your Savior; you have not remembered the Rock, your fortress.” 3

Imported Vines

“Therefore, though you plant the finest plants and set out imported vines...”
“The harvest will be as nothing in the day of disease and incurable pain.” 4
“The roar of the nations is like the roar of the sea.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Heap of Ruins (me-i map-pē-lāh): In the scrolls, this is the "Catastrophic Root." Damascus is "Uprooted" from its "Identity" as a "City" (*Ir*) and "Rooted" in "Collapse" (*Mappelah*). It signifies "Total Urban Disintegration."

2 Olive Tree is Beaten (ke-nō-qe-ph Za-yith): The scrolls highlight the "Survivorship Root." The "Remnant" (*O-le-lōth*) is "Rooted" in the "Twigs" after the "Judgment" (*Beating*). It signifies "The Persistent Residue."

3 Forgotten the Rock (Tzūr mā-uz-zēkh lō zā-kār-at): The scrolls highlight the "Amnesiac Root of Exile." "Forgetfulness" (*Lo-Zakar*) "Uproots" the soul from its "Stability" (*Rock/Tzur*). It signifies "The Instability of Secularism."

4 Imported Vines (ze-mō-rath zār): Literally "Strange cuttings." The scrolls highlight the "Agricultural Root of Futility." "Foreign Alliances" (*Vines*) are "Rooted" in "Incurable Pain" (*Ke-eb a-nush*). It signify "The Failure of External Reliance."

5 Roar of the Sea (ke-sha-ōn Ma-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Acoustic Root of Chaos." The "Nations" (*Goyim*) are "Rooted" in "Noise" (*Shaon*), but "God’s Rebuke" (*Ge-a-rah*) "Uproots" them. It marks the transition to "The Burden of Cush" in *Isaiah 18*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sovereign Rebuke." Trust the Rock.
