Isaiah 28

The Cornerstone and the Plumb Line.

Pride of the Drunkards

“Woe to that wreath, the pride of Ephraim’s drunkards.”
“Priests and prophets stagger from beer; they are befuddled by wine.” 1

The Precious Cornerstone

“See, I lay a stone in Zion, a tested stone, a precious cornerstone for a sure foundation.” 2
“The one who relies on it will never be stricken with panic.” 3

Line and Plummet

“I will make justice the measuring line and righteousness the plumb line.” 4

The Farmer’s Wisdom

“Does a farmer plow continually to plant seed?”
“All this comes from the Lord Almighty, whose plan is wonderful.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Befuddled by Wine (nib-le-u min-hay-Ya-yin): In the scrolls, this is the "Toxic Root of Leadership." The "Mind" (*Prophet/Priest*) is "Uprooted" (*Swallowed/Befuddled*) by "Substance." It signifies "The Erosion of Discernment."

2 Tested Stone (e-ben bo-chan): The scrolls highlight the "Architectural Root of the Kingdom." The "Cornerstone" (*Pin-nath*) is "Rooted" in "Zion." It signify "The Messianic Foundation."

3 Never be Stricken with Panic (lo ya-chish): Literally "Will not make haste." The scrolls highlight the "Equilibrium Root." Faith "Roots" the soul in "Patience" (*Lo-Yachish*). It signifies "The End of Anxiety."

4 Plumb Line (mish-qe-leth): The scrolls highlight the "Geometric Root of Ethics." Justice is "Rooted" in "Vertical Truth" (*Plumb line*). It signify "The Precision of Divine Judgment."

5 Farmer’s Wisdom (mip-pi Yah-weh): The scrolls conclude with this "Agricultural Root of Sovereignty." The "Timing" of the farmer is "Rooted" in "Divine Education." It marks the transition to "The Burden of Ariel" in *Isaiah 29*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Firm Stone." Stand on the foundation.
