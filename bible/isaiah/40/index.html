Isaiah 40

Comfort, the Voice, and the Eagle’s Wings.

Comfort My People

“Comfort, comfort my people, says your God.” 1

The Voice in the Wilderness

“In the wilderness prepare the way for the Lord; make straight in the desert a highway for our God.” 2

The Word Endures

“The grass withers and the flowers fall, but the word of our God endures forever.” 3

Incomparable

“The nations are like a drop in a bucket.”
“Who has measured the waters in the hollow of his hand?” 4

Wings Like Eagles

“But those who hope in the Lord will renew their strength. They will soar on wings like eagles.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Comfort (Na-cha-mu): In the scrolls, this is the "Empathetic Root of Restoration." The "Sorrow" of exile is "Uprooted" by the "Double Comfort" (*Nachamu Nachamu*). It signifies "The End of Alienation."

2 Voice calling (Qōl qō-rē): The scrolls highlight the "Vocal Root of Preparation." The "Highway" (*Ma-su-lul*) is "Rooted" in "Obedience" (*Prepare*). It signify "The Clearing of Spiritual Obstacles."

3 Word Endures (de-bhar-E-lo-hē-nu yā-qūm le-ō-lām): The scrolls highlight the "Temporal Root of Truth." Human life is "Uprooted" like "Grass" (*Cha-tzir*), but the "Word" (*De-bhar*) is "Rooted" in "Eternity" (*Olam*). It signifies "The Absolute Reliability of God."

4 Hollow of His Hand (be-sha-a-lo): The scrolls highlight the "Anatomical Root of Omnipotence." The "Cosmos" is "Rooted" in the "Palm" of the Creator. It signify "The Mastery of the Infinite."

5 Wings like Eagles (e-ber ka-ne-shā-rīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Aerodynamic Root of Hope." "Fatigue" is "Uprooted" by "Waiting" (*Qiwwi*) which "Roots" the soul in "Ascension." It marks the transition to "The Lord’s Help" in *Isaiah 41*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Renewed Strength." Soar with the King.
