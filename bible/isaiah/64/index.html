Isaiah 64

Rend the Heavens and the Potter’s Hand.

Oh, That You Would Come Down

“Oh, that you would rend the heavens and come down, that the mountains would tremble before you!” 1

Like Filthy Rags

“All our righteous acts are like filthy rags; we all shrivel up like a leaf.” 2
“No one calls on your name.”

The Potter and the Clay

“Yet you, Lord, are our Father. We are the clay, you are the potter.” 3
“We are all the work of your hand.”

Jerusalem a Desolation

“Your sacred cities have become a wasteland.”
“Our holy and beautiful temple... has been burned with fire.” 4
“Will you keep silent and punish us?” 5

Translation Notes

1 Rend the Heavens (lu qā-ra-tā Shā-ma-yim): In the scrolls, this is the "Atmospheric Root of Desperation." The "Barrier" between heaven and earth is "Uprooted" (*Rend*) for "Divine Descent" (*Ya-rad-ta*). It signifies "The Need for Immediate Manifestation."

2 Filthy Rags (ke-bhe-ghedh id-dīm): The scrolls highlight the "Pollution Root of Self-Righteousness." Human "Effort" is "Uprooted" from its "Pretension" and "Rooted" in "Uncleanliness." It signifies "The Insufficiency of Works."

3 Potter and the Clay (chō-mer... yō-tze-rē-nū): The scrolls highlight the "Plastic Root of Creation." Man is "Rooted" as "Clay" (*Chomer*) and God is the "Shaper" (*Yotzer*). It signify "The Total Dependence of the Work on the Artist."

4 Burned with Fire (hā-yā li-śe-rē-phath ēsh): The scrolls highlight the "Destructive Root of Exile." The "Sacred Space" is "Uprooted" by "Consuming Fire." It signifies "The Loss of the Center."

5 Will you Keep Silent (ha-al ē-leh thith-ap-paq): The scrolls conclude with this "Inquisitive Root of Agony." The "Silence" of God is "Uprooted" as an "Unbearable Weight." It marks the transition to "The New Heavens" in *Isaiah 65*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Clay’s Cry." Rend the heavens.
