Isaiah 59

The Separation and the Armor of the Redeemer.

Separated by Sin

“Surely the arm of the Lord is not too short to save.”
“But your iniquities have separated you from your God.” 1

Vipers and Spiders

“They hatch the eggs of vipers and spin a spider’s web.” 2
“Like the blind we grope along the wall; we stumble at midday as if it were twilight.” 3

The Armor of the Lord

“He put on righteousness as his breastplate, and the helmet of salvation on his head.” 4

The Redeemer Comes

“The Redeemer will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who repent of their sins.” 5
“My Spirit will not depart from your lips.”

Translation Notes

1 Separated (mabh-di-līm): In the scrolls, this is the "Partition Root of Iniquity." "Connection" is "Uprooted" by "Sin" (*Awōn*). It signifies "The Spiritual Great Divide."

2 Viper’s Eggs (bē-tzē tzi-ph-ō-nī): The scrolls highlight the "Toxic Root of Culture." "Societal Peace" is "Uprooted" by the "Hatching" of "Venomous Intent." It signifies "The Inherent Danger of Evil Plans."

3 Stumble at Midday (kā-shal-nū bhatz-tzo-ho-ra-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Sensory Root of Judgment." "Direction" is "Uprooted" even in the "Light" (*Midday*). It signify "The Cognitive Blindness of the Unrepentant."

4 Helmet of Salvation (kō-bha ye-shū-āh): The scrolls highlight the "Military Root of Intervention." God "Roots" His "Defense" in "Righteousness" (*Tzedaqah*) and "Protection" (*Salvation*). It signifies "The Divine Soldier."

5 Redeemer (Gō-ēl): The scrolls conclude with this "Kinsman Root." "Exile" is "Uprooted" by the "One who Pays the Price" (*Go-el*). It marks the transition to "The Rising Light" in *Isaiah 60*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Armored Savior." Repent and be redeemed.
