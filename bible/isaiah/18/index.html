Isaiah 18

The Quiet Before the Pruning.

Whirring Wings

“Woe to the land of whirring wings along the rivers of Cush.” 1

The Quiet God

“I will remain quiet and look from my dwelling place, like shimmering heat in the sunshine.” 2
“Like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.” 3

Pruning the Shoots

“He will cut off the shoots with pruning knives, and take away the spreading branches.” 4

Gifts to Mount Zion

“Gifts will be brought to the Lord Almighty from a people tall and smooth-skinned.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Whirring Wings (tzil-tzal ke-na-pha-yim): In the scrolls, this is the "Onomatopoeic Root." *Tziltzal* mimics the sound of insects or the "clanging" of ships. Cush (*Ethiopia*) is "Rooted" in "Activity" and "Distant Power." It signifies "The Busy Energy of the South."

2 Shimmering Heat (ke-chom tzach): The scrolls highlight the "Atmospheric Root of Presence." God is "Rooted" in "Observation" (*Quiet*), yet his "Presence" is like the "Intense Heat" (*Tzach*) that precedes the harvest. It signify "The Divine Silence of Preparation."

3 Cloud of Dew (a-ban tal): The scrolls highlight the "Moisture Root of Grace." Even in the "Heat," God "Roots" a "Cloud" (*Ab*) of "Dew" (*Tal*). It signifies "The Hidden Sustenance of the Spirit."

4 Pruning Knives (bam-maz-me-roth): The scrolls highlight the "Surgical Root of Judgment." The "Growth" (*Shoots*) is "Uprooted" (*Cut off*) before it can "Fruit" (*Harvest*). It signify "The Preemptive Strike of Justice."

5 Tall and Smooth (me-mu-shakh u-mo-rat): The scrolls conclude with this "Ethnographic Root." The "Smooth-skinned" (*Morat*) people are "Rooted" in "Distant Honor." They "Return" to "Zion" (*Place of the Name*). It marks the transition to "The Burden of Egypt" in *Isaiah 19*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Quiet Watcher." God sees all.
