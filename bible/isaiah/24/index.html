Isaiah 24

The Devastation of the World.

Laying Waste the Earth

“See, the Lord is going to lay waste the earth and devastate it; he will ruin its face.” 1

The Broken Covenant

“The earth is defiled by its people; they have broken the everlasting covenant.” 2

Songs from the Ends

“From the ends of the earth we hear singing: ‘Glory to the Righteous One.’” 3

The Lord Reigns

“The moon will be dismayed, the sun ashamed; for the Lord Almighty will reign on Mount Zion.” 4
“He will manifest his glory before his elders.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Lay Waste the Earth (bo-qeq ha-A-retz): In the scrolls, this is the "Global Uprooting." The "Earth" (*Aretz*) is "Rooted" in "Emptiness" (*Boqeq*). It signifies "The Reversal of Creation."

2 Broken the Everlasting Covenant (he-phe-ru be-rith O-lam): The scrolls highlight the "Ethical Root of Catastrophe." The "Covenant" (*Berith*) is the "Foundation Root" of the world; its "Uprooting" (*He-phe-ru*) causes the "Defilement" (*Cha-ne-phah*) of the soil. It signifies "Moral Ecology."

3 Glory to the Righteous One (tze-bhī latz-tzad-dīq): The scrolls highlight the "Vocal Root of the Remnant." Amidst the "Shaking" (*Ra-ash*), the "Righteous" (*Tzaddiq*) "Root" their "Song" in "Glory" (*Tzebhi*). It signify "The Persistence of Praise."

4 Sun Ashamed (wa-bo-sha ha-Cham-mah): The scrolls highlight the "Celestial Root of Sovereignty." The "Luminaries" (*Sun/Moon*) are "Uprooted" from their "Primary Status" by the "Greater Light" of the "King." It signify "The Enthronement of the Lord."

5 Before his Elders (ne-ged Ze-qe-naw): The scrolls conclude with this "Hierarchical Root." The "Glory" (*Kabod*) is "Rooted" in "Community." It marks the transition to "The Song of Thanksgiving" in *Isaiah 25*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Shaking Earth." Stand with the Righteous One.
