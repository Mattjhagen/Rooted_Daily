Isaiah 7

The Sign of Immanuel.

Stand Firm in Faith

“The hearts of Ahaz and his people were shaken, as the trees of the forest are shaken by the wind.”
“If you do not stand firm in your faith, you will not stand at all.” 1

The Sign of the Virgin

“The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and will call him Immanuel.” 2
“God with Us.” 3

Curds and Honey

“He will be eating curds and honey when he knows enough to reject the wrong and choose the right.” 4

The Coming King

“The Lord will bring on you... the king of Assyria.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Stand firm in Faith (im-lo tha-a-mi-nu ki-lo the-a-me-nu): In the scrolls, this is the "Structural Root of Belief." "A-ma-nu" (*Faith*) is the "Root" of "Stability" (*A-me-nu*). If the "Faith Root" is "Uprooted," the "Existence" (*Standing*) "Collapses." It signifies "The Ontological Necessity of Trust."

2 The Virgin (ha-Al-mah): The scrolls highlight the "Generational Root of Prophecy." *Almah* refers to a "Young Woman" or "Maiden" of marriageable age. In the Septuagint and the Messianic "Root," it is "Rooted" in the "Virginal Conception." It signifies "The Miracle of the Seed."

3 Immanuel (Im-ma-nu-el): Literally "With us is God." The scrolls highlight the "Proximity Root of Divinity." God "Roots" his "Presence" (*Immanu*) in the "Humanity" (*El*) of the child. It signify "The End of Abandonment."

4 Curds and Honey (Chem-ah u-de-bash): The scrolls highlight the "Dietary Root of Maturity." The child is "Rooted" in "Simple Sustenance" (*Chem-ah/Debash*) until "Moral Discernment" (*Reject wrong/Choose right*) is "Rooted." It signify "The Growth of the Messianic Mind."

5 King of Assyria (eth-Me-lekh Ash-shur): The scrolls conclude with this "Invasive Root." While "Immanuel" is a "Sign of Hope," the "Faithlessness" of Ahaz "Roots" the "Judgment" of "Assyria" (*Ashshur*). It marks the transition to "The Flood of Assyria" in *Isaiah 8*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sovereign Sign." Trust the child, or fear the king.
