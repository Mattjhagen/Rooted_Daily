Isaiah 51

The Rock of Origins and the Cup of Staggering.

Look to the Rock

“Look to the rock from which you were cut and to the quarry from which you were hewn.” 1
“Look to Abraham your father.”

Deserts like Eden

“He will make her deserts like Eden, her wastelands like the garden of the Lord.” 2

Awake, Awake!

“Awake, awake, arm of the Lord, clothe yourself with strength!” 3

The Cup of Staggering

“Rise up, Jerusalem, you have drunk the cup of his wrath.”
“See, I have taken from your hand the cup that made you stagger.” 4
“I will put it into the hands of your tormentors.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Quarry from which you were Hewn (maq-qe-bhath bōr): In the scrolls, this is the "Ancestral Root of Identity." Israel is "Rooted" in the "Rock" (*Tzur*) of the Patriarchs. It signifies "The Spiritual DNA of the Nation."

2 Deserts like Eden (mid-ba-rāh ke-Ē-dhen): The scrolls highlight the "Restorative Root of Nature." "Desolation" is "Uprooted" and "Paradise" is "Re-Rooted." It signify "The Aesthetic of Redemption."

3 Arm of the Lord (ze-rō-a Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Muscular Root of Intervention." The "Arm" is "Rooted" in "Creation" and "Exodus" (*Cut Rahab*). It signify "The Divine Capability to Act."

4 Cup of Staggering (qub-ba-at kōs hat-tar-ē-lāh): The scrolls highlight the "Toxic Root of Judgment." The "Poison" of "Consequence" is "Uprooted" from the hand of the faithful. It signifies "The End of Punishment."

5 Hands of your Tormentors (be-yadh mō-gha-yi-khā): The scrolls conclude with this "Reversal Root." The "Pain" is "Re-Rooted" in the "Oppressor." It marks the transition to "The Beautiful Feet" in *Isaiah 52*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Awake Soul." The cup is gone.
