Isaiah 9

The Child and the Increase of Peace.

A Great Light

“The people walking in darkness have seen a great light.” 1

For To Us A Child Is Born

“For to us a child is born, to us a son is given.” 2
“And he will be called:
Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God,
Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.” 3

No End to Peace

“Of the greatness of his government and peace there will be no end.” 4

The Fallen Bricks

“The bricks have fallen down, but we will rebuild with dressed stone.”
“Yet for all this, his anger is not turned away.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Seen a Great Light (ra-u Or Ga-dol): In the scrolls, this is the "Optic Root of Hope." "Darkness" (*Cho-shekh*) is "Uprooted" by the "Great Light" (*Or Gadol*). It signifies "The End of Epistemological Night."

2 A Son is Given (ben-nut-than-la-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Gift Root." The "Messiah" is "Rooted" in "Human Birth" (*Yelad*) but "Given" (*Nathan*) as a "Divine Legacy" (*Ben*). It signifies "The Incarnation of Purpose."

3 Wonderful Counselor (Pe-le Yo-etz): The scrolls highlight the "Administrative Root of Godhead." "Pele" (*Wonderful/Miraculous*) "Roots" the "Counsel" (*Yoetz*). "Prince of Peace" (*Sar Sha-lom*) "Roots" the "Social Harmony." It signify "The Fourfold Identity of the King."

4 No End (en-qetz): The scrolls highlight the "Infinite Root of Government." Unlike "Human Empires" which are "Uprooted" by "Time," this "Shalom" has "No End" (*En-qetz*). It signify "The Eternal Dynasty."

5 Hand is still Upraised (we-o-do ya-dow ne-tu-yah): The scrolls conclude with this "Judicial Root of Persistence." Despite the "Light," the "Pride" (*Ga-a-wah*) of the nation "Roots" continued "Judgment." It marks the transition to "The Woe of Unjust Laws" in *Isaiah 10*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Light in the Dark." Follow the light.
