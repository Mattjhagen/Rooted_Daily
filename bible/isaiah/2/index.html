Isaiah 2

The Mountain of the House of the Lord.

The Highest of Mountains

“In the last days the mountain of the Lord’s temple will be established as the highest of the mountains.” 1
“Come, let us go up to the mountain of the Lord.”

Swords into Plowshares

“They will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks.” 2
“Nation will not take up sword against nation, nor will they train for war anymore.”

Walk in the Light

“Come, descendants of Jacob, let us walk in the light of the Lord.” 3

Mere Humans

“Stop trusting in mere humans, who have but a breath in their nostrils. Why hold them in esteem?” 4

Translation Notes

1 Highest of the Mountains (be-Rosh he-ha-rim): Literally "At the head of the mountains." In the scrolls, this is the "Topographical Root of Authority." Zion is "Rooted" as the "Primary Summit" (*Rosh*) to which all "Nations" (*Goyim*) will "Stream" (*Na-ha-ru*). It signifies "Global Spiritual Centrality."

2 Swords into Plowshares (char-bo-tham le-it-tim): The scrolls highlight the "Technological Root of Peace." "Weapons" (*Charboth*) are "Uprooted" from destruction and "Rooted" in "Agriculture" (*Ittim/Plowshares*). It signify "The Redemption of Industry."

3 Walk in the Light (ne-le-khah be-Or Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of Conduct." To "Walk" (*Nelekhah*) is to "Root" one’s "Path" in the "Illumination" (*Or*) of God. It signifies "Ethical Clarity."

4 Breath in their Nostrils (ne-sha-mah be-ap-pow): The scrolls conclude with this "Mortal Root." Human "Permanence" is "Uprooted" by the "Futility" of the "Breath" (*Neshamah*). It marks the transition to "The Judgment on Leaders" in *Isaiah 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sovereign Creator." Trust only the Breath-Giver.
