Isaiah 47

The Dust of the Eternal Lady.

Sit in the Dust

“Go down, sit in the dust, Virgin Daughter Babylon; sit on the ground without a throne.” 1

Lady of Kingdoms

“You said, ‘I will continue forever—the eternal lady!’”
“But you did not consider these things or reflect on what might happen.” 2

A Single Day

“Both of these will overtake you in a moment, in a single day: loss of children and widowhood.” 3

Failure of Magic

“Let your astrologers come forward... those who gaze at the stars.”
“They are like stubble; the fire will burn them up.” 4
“Not one of them can save you.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sit in the Dust (re-dī u-she-bhī al-A-phar): In the scrolls, this is the "Imperial Uprooting." Babylon is "Rooted" as a "Virgin" (*Be-thu-lah*) who has never been conquered, but is now "Uprooted" from her "Throne" (*Kis-se*) and "Re-Rooted" in the "Dust" (*Aphar*). It signifies "The Total Reversal of Status."

2 Lady of Kingdoms (Ge-be-reth Mam-la-khōth): The scrolls highlight the "Arrogant Root of Continuity." Babylon "Roots" her "Identity" in "Perpetuity" (*Forever*). It signify "The Blindness of Power."

3 In a Single Day (be-yōm e-chādh): The scrolls highlight the "Instantaneous Root of Judgment." "Loss" (*She-khōl*) is "Rooted" in "Suddenness." It signifies "The Fragility of the Empire."

4 Like Stubble (ke-qa-sh): The scrolls highlight the "Combustible Root of Occultism." The "Astrologers" (*Ho-bhe-rē Shā-ma-yim*) are "Uprooted" by "Fire." It signify "The Impotence of Human Wisdom."

5 Not one of them can Save (lō-yi-h-yeh le-ha-tzī-lē-khā): The scrolls conclude with this "Insolvability Root." "Salvation" (*Hatzil*) is "Uprooted" from the "Magic" of the city. It marks the transition to "The Stubbornness of Israel" in *Isaiah 48*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Silent Throne." The stars will not help.
