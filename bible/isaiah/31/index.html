Isaiah 31

The Lion and the Hovering Bird.

Flesh and Not Spirit

“Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help, who rely on horses.”
“The Egyptians are mere mortals and not God; their horses are flesh and not spirit.” 1

The Growling Lion

“As a lion growls over its prey... so the Lord Almighty will come down to do battle on Mount Zion.” 2

The Hovering Bird

“Like birds hovering overhead, the Lord Almighty will shield Jerusalem.” 3

Sword of Spirit

“Assyria will fall by a sword that is not of man.” 4
“Return to him you have so greatly revolted against.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Flesh and not Spirit (ba-sar we-lo Ru-ach): In the scrolls, this is the "Ontological Root of Reliance." "Flesh" (*Basar*) is "Uprooted" from "Permanence." Only "Spirit" (*Ruach*) "Roots" the "Absolute Power." It signifies "The Folly of Biological Trust."

2 As a Lion Growls (ka-a-sher yeh-geh ha-Ar-yeh): The scrolls highlight the "Predatory Root of Sovereignty." God’s "Protection" is "Rooted" in "Ferocity" (*Hageh*). It signify "The Unstoppable Claim of the King."

3 Like Birds Hovering (ke-tzap-po-rim a-phey-oth): The scrolls highlight the "Nurturing Root of Shielding." God "Roots" his "Defense" in the "Over-shadowing" (*A-phe-yoth*) of the "Bird." It signify "The Divine Maternal Instinct."

4 Sword not of Man (che-rebh lo-I-sh): The scrolls highlight the "Supernatural Root of Deliverance." Assyria is "Uprooted" by a "Force" that "Roots" in "Non-Human Origin." It signify "The Intervention of the Holy One."

5 Return (shu-bhu): The scrolls conclude with this "Re-rooting Command." "Rebellion" (*Sa-rah*) is "Uprooted" by the "Return" (*Shubhu*) to the "Source." It marks the transition to "The Righteous King" in *Isaiah 32*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Divine Shield." Rely on the Spirit.
