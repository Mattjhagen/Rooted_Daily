Isaiah 13

The Burden of Babylon.

Noise on the Mountains

“Listen, a noise on the mountains, like that of a great multitude!”
“The Lord Almighty is mustering an army for war.” 1

The Day of the Lord

“Wail, for the day of the Lord is near; it will come like destruction from the Almighty.” 2
“The sun will be darkened... and the moon will not give its light.”

Humbled Pride

“I will put an end to the arrogance of the haughty and will humble the pride of the ruthless.” 3

The Jewel of Kingdoms

“Babylon, the jewel of kingdoms... will be overthrown by God like Sodom.”
“It will never be inhabited or lived in through all generations.” 4
“Jackals will fill her houses; there the owls will dwell.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Mustering an army (me-phā-qēdh tze-bhā mil-chā-māh): In the scrolls, this is the "Mobilization Root." The Lord "Roots" the "Outcome" by "Calling" (*Pe-qēdh*) the "Host" (*Tzebha*). It signifies "The Orchestration of History."

2 Day of the Lord (Yom Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Apocalyptic Root of Time." The "Day" (*Yom*) "Uproots" the "Status Quo" and "Roots" the "Finality" of "Judgment" (*Sod*). It signify "The Divine Deadline."

3 Humble the Pride (Ga-a-wath Ze-dim): The scrolls highlight the "Deflationary Root of Justice." The "Haughtiness" (*Ge-on*) of "Babylon" is "Uprooted" by the "Lowliness" (*Shaphal*) of its "Fall." It signifies "The Gravity of Arrogance."

4 Jewel of Kingdoms (tze-bhī mam-lā-khōth): Literally "The beauty of kingdoms." The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Desolation." What was "Rooted" in "Splendor" (*Tzebhi*) becomes "Rooted" in "Nothingness." It signify "The Mortality of Civilization."

5 Jackals will fill (we-mā-le-ū bat-tē-hem o-chīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Wilderness Root." Human "Inhabitation" is "Uprooted" and "Replaced" by "Wild Creatures" (*O-chim/I-yim*). It marks the transition to "The Fall of Lucifer" in *Isaiah 14*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Empty Throne." Only God remains.
