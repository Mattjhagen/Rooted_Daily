Isaiah 50

The Instructed Ear and the Face like Flint.

The Sustaining Word

“The Sovereign Lord has given me a well-instructed tongue, to know the word that sustains the weary.” 1

Morning by Morning

“He wakens me morning by morning.”
“Wakens my ear to listen like one being instructed.” 2

Obedience in Pain

“I offered my back to those who beat me, my cheeks to those who pulled out my beard.” 3
“I did not hide my face from mocking and spitting.”

Face Like Flint

“I have set my face like flint, and I know I will not be put to shame.” 4
“He who vindicates me is near.”

Translation Notes

1 Well-instructed Tongue (le-shōn lim-mū-dhīm): Literally "The tongue of the learned." In the scrolls, this is the "Communicative Root of Compassion." The "Word" (*De-bhar*) is "Rooted" in "Study" (*Limmud*) to provide "Support" (*Sustain*) for the "Faint" (*Weary*). It signifies "The Messianic Counselor."

2 Wakens my Ear (yā-īr lī ō-zen): The scrolls highlight the "Aural Root of Obedience." Every "Dawn" (*Morning*) is a "Re-Rooting" of the "Ears" (*Ozen*) in the "Voice of God." It signify "The Continuous Education of the Spirit."

3 Cheeks to those who Pulled (le-yō-re-tīm): The scrolls highlight the "Passive Root of Submission." The "Body" is "Uprooted" from "Protection" to bear the "Shame" (*Mocking*) of others. It signify "The Submissive Sacrifice."

4 Face like Flint (pe-nay ka-chal-lā-mish): The scrolls highlight the "Geological Root of Determination." The "Resolution" is "Rooted" in "Hardness" (*Flint*) to withstand the "Assault." It signifies "The Inflexible Will of the Servant."

5 Trust in the Name (yi-be-tach be-shēm Yah-weh): The scrolls conclude with this "Darkness Root." For the one in the "Deep Night," "Security" is "Rooted" in the "Name" (*Shem*) alone. It marks the transition to "The Call to Zion" in *Isaiah 51*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Instructed Heart." Morning is coming.
