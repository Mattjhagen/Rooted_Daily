Isaiah 48

The Iron Neck and the Furnace of Affliction.

Iron and Bronze

“I knew how stubborn you were; your neck muscles were iron, your forehead was bronze.” 1

Furnace of Affliction

“I have tested you in the furnace of affliction.” 2
“For my own sake, I do this.”

Leave Babylon!

“Leave Babylon, flee from the Babylonians! Announce this with shouts of joy!” 3

Water from the Rock

“He made water flow for them from the rock; he split the rock and water gushed out.” 4
“‘There is no peace,’ says the Lord, ‘for the wicked.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 Neck Muscles were Iron (gīd bar-zel ar-peh-khā): In the scrolls, this is the "Metaphorical Root of Obstinacy." Israel’s "Posture" is "Rooted" in "Metallic Rigidity" (*Iron/Bronze*). It signifies "The Hardening of the Will."

2 Furnace of Affliction (kūr o-nī): The scrolls highlight the "Metallurgical Root of Purification." Israel is "Rooted" in the "Heat" (*Furnace*) of "Suffering" (*Oni*) to "Uproot" the "Dross" of idolatry. It signify "The Divine Refinery."

3 Leave Babylon (tze-ū mim-Bā-bhel): The scrolls highlight the "Exodus Root." "Captivity" is "Uprooted" by the "Command to Depart." It signifies "The Re-claiming of Freedom."

4 Water from the Rock (Ma-yim mitz-Tzūr): The scrolls highlight the "Hydrological Root of Provision." "Desert" is "Uprooted" by the "Splitting of the Rock" (*Tzur*). It signify "The Sustenance of the Journey."

5 No Peace for the Wicked (ēn Sha-lōm... la-re-shā-īm): The scrolls conclude with this "Incompatibility Root." "Peace" (*Shalom*) cannot be "Rooted" in "Wickedness" (*Rasha*). It marks the transition to "The Light to the Nations" in *Isaiah 49*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Refined Remnant." Leave the confusion.
