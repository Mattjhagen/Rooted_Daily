Isaiah 32

The King of Righteousness and the Outpouring.

Reigning in Righteousness

“See, a king will reign in righteousness and rulers will rule with justice.” 1

Shadow of a Great Rock

“Each man will be like a shelter from the wind... like the shadow of a great rock in a thirsty land.” 2

The Spirit Poured Out

“Till the Spirit is poured on us from on high, and the desert becomes a fertile field.” 3

Fruit of Righteousness

“The fruit of that righteousness will be peace; its effect will be quietness and confidence forever.” 4
“How blessed you will be, sowing your seed by every stream.” 5

Translation Notes

1 King will Reign (Me-lekh yim-lokh): In the scrolls, this is the "Ideal Root of Governance." The "Authority" (*Melekh*) is "Rooted" in "Righteousness" (*Tze-deq*). It signifies "The Messianic Restoration of Order."

2 Shadow of a Great Rock (ke-tzēl Sē-la kā-bhēdh): The scrolls highlight the "Micro-climatic Root of Mercy." The "Protection" (*Tzel*) is "Rooted" in the "Massive Stability" (*Kabhed/Heavy*) of the "Rock" (*Sela*). It signify "The Sustenance of the Exhausted."

3 Spirit is Poured (ye-ā-reh Ru-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Transformational Root of the Spirit." The "Arid Land" (*Midbar*) is "Uprooted" and "Re-Rooted" as "Orchard" (*Carmel*) by the "Atmospheric Infusion" (*Outpouring*). It signify "The Divine Regeneration of the Soil."

4 Fruit of Righteousness (Ma-a-seh hatz-Tze-deq Sha-lom): The scrolls highlight the "Productive Root of Ethics." "Peace" (*Shalom*) is not a "Status" but a "Fruit" (*Ma-aseh*) "Rooted" in "Righteousness." It signify "The Moral Origin of Tranquility."

5 Seed by Every Stream (al-kol-Mā-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Hydraulic Root of Blessing." "Prosperity" is "Rooted" in "Abundant Supply" (*Water*). It marks the transition to "The Woe of the Destroyer" in *Isaiah 33*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Outpoured Spirit." Sowing in peace.
