Isaiah 35

The Desert Blooms and the Way of Holiness.

The Desert Rejoices

“The desert and the parched land will be glad; the wilderness will rejoice and blossom.” 1

Eyes Opened, Ears Unstopped

“Then will the eyes of the blind be opened and the ears of the deaf unstopped.” 2
“Then will the lame leap like a deer.” 3

The Way of Holiness

“And a highway will be there; it will be called the Way of Holiness.” 4

Everlasting Joy

“The ransomed of the Lord will return... everlasting joy will crown their heads.” 5
“Sorrow and sighing will flee away.”

Translation Notes

1 Desert will Rejoice (ye-su-sum Mid-bar): In the scrolls, this is the "Biological Reversal." The "Barrenness" (*Midbar*) is "Uprooted" by "Exultation" (*Sus*). It signifies "The Spiritual Irrigation of the Soul."

2 Eyes Opened (tip-pa-qa-cha-nah e-ney): The scrolls highlight the "Sensory Root of Revelation." "Darkness" (*Gloom*) is "Uprooted" from the "Sight" (*Eyes*). It signify "The Recovery of Spiritual Perception."

3 Leap like a Deer (ye-dal-leg ka-ay-yal): The scrolls highlight the "Kinetical Root of Joy." The "Inhibition" (*Lameness*) is "Uprooted" by the "Agility" (*Deer*) of the "Restored Body." It signify "The Resurrection Life."

4 Way of Holiness (De-rekh haq-Qo-desh): The scrolls highlight the "Linear Root of Conduct." A "Highway" (*Ma-su-lul*) is "Rooted" in "Sanctification" (*Qodesh*). No "Unclean" (*Ta-me*) will walk there. It signify "The Exclusive Path to God."

5 Everlasting Joy (Sim-chath O-lam): The scrolls conclude with this "Eternal Emotional Root." "Exile" is "Uprooted" and "Return" (*Shubhah*) is "Rooted" in "Zion." It marks the transition to "The Trial of Hezekiah" in *Isaiah 36*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Joyful Highway." Sorrow has fled.
