Isaiah 60

The Rising Glory and the Everlasting Light.

Arise, Shine!

“Arise, shine, for your light has come, and the glory of the Lord rises upon you.” 1

Wealth of Nations

“Nations will come to your light, and kings to the brightness of your dawn.” 2
“The wealth on the seas will be brought to you.”

Walls of Salvation

“Your gates will always stand open, they will never be shut.”
“You will call your walls Salvation and your gates Praise.” 3

Everlasting Light

“The sun will no more be your light by day.”
“For the Lord will be your everlasting light.” 4
“Your days of sorrow will end.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Arise, Shine (qū-mī ō-rī): In the scrolls, this is the "Luminous Root of Resurrection." The "Darkness" (*Choshekh*) of the past is "Uprooted" and Zion is commanded to "Rise" (*Qūm*) into the "Light" (*Or*). It signifies "The Spiritual Dawn of the New Age."

2 Brightness of your Dawn (ne-ghah zar-che-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Solar Root of Attraction." The "Nations" (*Goyim*) are "Rooted" in "Search" for the "Radiance" (*Zarach*) of God’s people. It signify "The Global Magnetism of Truth."

3 Walls Salvation (chō-mō-tha-yi-kh ye-shū-āh): The scrolls highlight the "Defensive Root of Grace." "Fortifications" are "Uprooted" as "Physical Barriers" and "Re-Rooted" as "Deliverance" (*Yeshuah*). It signifies "The Absolute Security of the Holy City."

4 Everlasting Light (le-ōr ō-lām): The scrolls highlight the "Metaphysical Root of Illumination." The "Temporary" (*Sun/Moon*) is "Uprooted" by the "Eternal" (*Olam*). It signify "The Unending Presence of God."

5 Days of Sorrow will End (shā-le-mū ye-mē e-bhe-lēkh): The scrolls conclude with this "Consolatory Root." "Grief" (*Ebhel*) is "Uprooted" by "Completion" (*Shalam*). It marks the transition to "The Year of Favor" in *Isaiah 61*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Rising Light." The sun is setting on sorrow.
