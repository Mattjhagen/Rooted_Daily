Isaiah 37

The Prayer and the Remnant Root.

Hezekiah’s Prayer

“Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, you alone are God over all the kingdoms of the earth.” 1

Virgin Daughter Zion

“The Virgin Daughter Zion despises and mocks you.” 2
“I will put my hook in your nose and my bit in your lips.” 3

Take Root Below

“Once more a remnant of Judah will take root below and bear fruit above.” 4

The Angel’s Strike

“The angel of the Lord went out and put to death 185,000 in the Assyrian camp.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Lord Almighty (Yah-weh Tze-bha-ōth): In the scrolls, this is the "Military Root of Sovereignty." God is "Rooted" as the "Commander of Hosts" (*Tzebhaoth*). It signifies "The Absolute Power over Empires."

2 Virgin Daughter (Be-thū-lath bath-Tzi-yōn): The scrolls highlight the "Purity Root of Zion." Despite the siege, she is "Rooted" in "Inviolability" (*Virginity*). It signify "The Divine Protection of the Temple City."

3 Hook in your Nose (chak-qī be-ap-pe-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Animalistic Root of Humiliation." Sennacherib is "Uprooted" from his "Pretended Divinity" and treated as "Livestock" (*Hook/Bit*). It signify "The Devaluation of the Tyrant."

4 Take Root Below (shā-re-shāh lo-mat-tāh): The scrolls highlight the "Botany of Survival." The "Remnant" (*She-ē-rīth*) is "Re-Rooted" in the "Soil of Promise" (*Below*) to ensure "Productivity" (*Fruit Above*). It signify "The Resilience of Grace."

5 Angel of the Lord (Mal-akh Yah-weh): The scrolls conclude with this "Agentic Root." The "Execution" is "Rooted" in "Spiritual Presence." It marks the transition to "The Illness of Hezekiah" in *Isaiah 38*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Remnant Root." God defends His Name.
