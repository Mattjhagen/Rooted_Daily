Isaiah 52

The Beautiful Feet and the Marred King.

Shake off the Dust

“Awake, awake, Zion, clothe yourself with strength!”
“Shake off your dust; rise up, sit enthroned, Jerusalem.” 1

Beautiful Feet

“How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of those who bring good news.” 2
“Who say to Zion, ‘Your God reigns!’”

Depart in Purity

“Depart, depart, go out from there! Touch no unclean thing!” 3

Marred Beyond Likeness

“His appearance was so marred beyond that of any human being.” 4
“So he will sprinkle many nations.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Shake off your Dust (hith-na-a-rī mē-ā-phār): In the scrolls, this is the "Imperial Re-Rooting." Zion is "Uprooted" from the "Dust" (*Aphar*) of "Defeat" and "Re-Rooted" on the "Throne" (*Kisse*). It signifies "The Recovery of Dignity."

2 Beautiful Feet (ma-nā-wū... ra-ge-lē): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Evangelism." The "Messenger" is "Rooted" in "Mountain Travel" to bring "Peace" (*Shalom*). It signify "The Divine Arrival."

3 Touch no Unclean Thing (ta-mē al-tig-gā-ū): The scrolls highlight the "Sanctified Root of Departure." The "Exodus" is "Rooted" in "Purity" (*Tame*). It signifies "The Separation from Confusion."

4 Marred Beyond Human (mish-chath mē-īsh): The scrolls highlight the "Physical Root of Suffering." The "Servant" is "Uprooted" from "Human Recognizability" to pay the price. It signify "The Infinite Cost of Salvation."

5 Sprinkling Nations (yaz-zeh gō-yīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Sacerdotal Root." The "Marred One" becomes the "Priest" who "Sprinkles" (*Yaz-zeh*) the "World." It marks the transition to "The Man of Sorrows" in *Isaiah 53*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Exalted Servant." Watch and see.
