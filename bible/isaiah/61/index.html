Isaiah 61

The Anointed Messenger and the Oaks of Righteousness.

The Spirit is on Me

“The Spirit of the Sovereign Lord is on me, because the Lord has anointed me to proclaim good news to the poor.” 1

Bind the Brokenhearted

“He has sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim freedom for the captives.” 2

Beauty for Ashes

“To bestow on them a crown of beauty instead of ashes, the oil of joy instead of mourning.” 3

Oaks of Righteousness

“They will be called oaks of righteousness, a planting of the Lord for the display of his splendor.” 4

Garments of Salvation

“For he has clothed me with garments of salvation and arrayed me in a robe of his righteousness.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Spirit is on Me (Rū-ach A-dō-nāy... ā-lay): In the scrolls, this is the "Pneumatic Root of Authorization." The "Servant" is "Rooted" in the "Spirit" (*Ruach*) for "Good News" (*Bas-sar*). It signifies "The Divine Mission of Christ."

2 Bind the Brokenhearted (la-cha-bhōsh le-nish-be-rē-lēbh): The scrolls highlight the "Surgical Root of Mercy." "Brokenness" is "Uprooted" by "Binding" (*Chabhash*). It signify "The Healing Power of the Gospel."

3 Beauty for Ashes (pe-ēr ta-chath ē-pher): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Transformation." "Ashes" (*Epher*) of "Grief" are "Uprooted" and "Beauty" (*Pe-ēr*) is "Rooted" in their place. It signifies "The Total Reversal of Life State."

4 Oaks of Righteousness (ē-lē hatz-Tze-dheq): The scrolls highlight the "Biological Root of Stability." The "Redeemed" are "Rooted" as "Mighty Trees" (*Oaks*) for "Permanence." It signify "The Strength of the Faithful."

5 Garments of Salvation (big-dhē ye-shū-āh): The scrolls conclude with this "Vestimentary Root." "Shame" is "Uprooted" and "Victory" is "Rooted" in the "Robe" (*Me-il*). It marks the transition to "The New Name of Zion" in *Isaiah 62*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Year of Favor." Put on the robe.
