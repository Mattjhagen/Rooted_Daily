Isaiah 27

The Slaying of the Serpent and the Delightful Vineyard.

Slaying Leviathan

“In that day the Lord will punish Leviathan the gliding serpent; he will slay the monster of the sea.” 1

Vineyard of Delight

“In that day—Sing about a fruitful vineyard!”
“I, the Lord, watch over it; I water it continually.” 2

Blossoming Israel

“Israel will bud and blossom and fill all the world with fruit.” 3

The Great Trumpet

“In that day a great trumpet will sound.” 4
“Those who were perishing will come and worship the Lord on the holy mountain.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Leviathan (Li-wya-than): In the scrolls, this is the "Mythic Root of Chaos." The "Monster" (*Tan-nin*) is "Uprooted" by the "Hard and Great Sword" of the Lord. It signifies "The Total Victory over Primeval Evil."

2 Water it Continually (li-re-ga-im ash-qen-nah): Literally "At every moment I water it." The scrolls highlight the "Hydration Root of Care." Unlike the vineyard in *Isaiah 5*, this one is "Rooted" in "Constant Attention." It signifies "The Redemption of the Fruit."

3 Israel will Blossom (ya-tzitz u-pha-rach Is-ra-el): The scrolls highlight the "Global Root of Influence." Israel is "Rooted" to "Fill" (*Mā-le-ū*) the "Face of the World" (*Pe-ney Te-bel*) with "Produce" (*Te-nu-bhā*). It signify "Spiritual Imperialism."

4 Great Trumpet (Sho-phar Ga-dol): The scrolls highlight the "Sonic Root of Gathering." The "Shophar" "Roots" the "Signal" to the "Perishing" (*O-be-dim*). It signifies "The Final Call to Worship."

5 Holy Mountain (be-Har haq-Qo-desh): The scrolls conclude with this "Topographical Root." The "Exiles" are "Re-Rooted" in "Jerusalem." It marks the transition to "The Woe of Ephraim" in *Isaiah 28*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "TheSlaughtered Serpent." The vineyard is safe.
