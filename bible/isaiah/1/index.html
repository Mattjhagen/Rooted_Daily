Isaiah 1

The Vision of the Rebellious Nation.

The Ox Knows its Master

“Hear, O heavens! Listen, O earth!”
“The ox knows its master, the donkey its owner’s manger, but Israel does not know.” 1

Learn to do Right

“Wash and make yourselves clean. Take your evil deeds out of my sight!”
“Learn to do right; seek justice. Defend the oppressed.” 2

Scarlet to White

“Come now, let us settle the matter, says the Lord.”
“Though your sins are like scarlet, they shall be as white as snow.” 3

Deliverance with Justice

“I will restore your leaders as in days of old.” 4
“Zion will be delivered with justice, her penitent ones with righteousness.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Israel does not Know (Is-ra-el lo ya-da): In the scrolls, this is the "Cognitive Root of Rebellion." "Knowing" (*Yada*) is the "Master Root." The "Animal" (*Ox/Donkey*) is "Rooted" in its "Source," but the "Human" (*Israel*) has "Uprooted" their "Awareness." It signifies "Spiritual Amnesia."

2 Seek Justice (dir-shu Mish-pat): The scrolls highlight the "Judicial Root of Action." "Seeking" (*Dirshu*) "Justice" (*Mishpat*) is the "Prerequisite Root" for "Cleanness" (*Ta-ha-ru*). It signifies "Ethical Integrity."

3 Scarlet to White (im-yi-he-yu cha-ta-e-khem kash-sha-nim kash-she-leg yal-bi-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Alchemical Root of Mercy." "Scarlet" (*Shanim*) sins are "Rooted" in the "Permanent Stain" of the self, but God "Uproots" the "Color" through "Snow" (*Sheleg*). It signify "Total Re-genesis."

4 Restore your Leaders (a-shi-bah sho-phe-ta-yikh): Literally "I will return your judges." The scrolls highlight the "Institutional Root of Stability." Justice is "Rooted" in "Legitimate Authority." It signifies "The End of Anarchy."

5 Delivered with Justice (be-Mish-pat tiph-pa-deh): The scrolls conclude with this "Redemptive Root." Zion is not just "Saved" but "Rooted" in "Legal Deliverance" (*Padeh*). It marks the transition to "The Mountain of the Lord" in *Isaiah 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Restored City." Stand clean.
