Isaiah 34

The Day of Vengeance and the Burning Pitch.

Come Near, You Nations

“Come near, you nations, and listen; pay attention, you peoples!”
“My sword has drunk its fill in the heavens; see, it descends in judgment on Edom.” 1

Day of Retribution

“For the Lord has a day of vengeance, a year of retribution, to uphold Zion’s cause.” 2

Burning Pitch

“Edom’s streams will be turned into pitch, her dust into burning sulfur.” 3

The Scroll of the Lord

“Look in the scroll of the Lord and read: None of these will be missing.” 4
“For it is his mouth that has given the order.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sword has Drunk (ri-wa-thah ba-sha-ma-yim char-bi): Literally "My sword is satiated in the heavens." In the scrolls, this is the "Celestial Root of Judgment." The "Sword" (*Cherebh*) is "Rooted" in the "High Places" before it "Uproots" the "Pride" of Edom (*Adam/Red*). It signifies "The Divine Authority of War."

2 Year of Retribution (she-nath Shil-lu-mim): The scrolls highlight the "Compensatory Root of Justice." Every "Uprooting" of Zion is met with a "Re-Rooting" of "Retribution" (*Shillumim*). It signify "The Balancing of the Global Scales."

3 Burning Pitch (la-ze-phe-th bo-er-eth): The scrolls highlight the "Alchemical Root of Desolation." The "Water" (*Streams*) is "Uprooted" and "Replaced" by "Pitch" (*Zepheth*). It signifies "The Perpetual Uninhabitability of Rebellion."

4 Scroll of the Lord (Se-pher Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Documentary Root of Destiny." Everything is "Rooted" in the "Written Word" (*Sepher*). It signify "The Inevitability of Prophecy."

5 His Mouth has Ordered (pi-hu hu tzi-wa): The scrolls conclude with this "Verbal Root of Command." The "Outcome" is "Rooted" in "Divine Breath" (*Mouth*). It marks the transition to "The Joy of the Wilderness" in *Isaiah 35*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Satiated Sword." Read the scroll.
