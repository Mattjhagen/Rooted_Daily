Isaiah 45

The Anointed King and the Ends of the Earth.

To His Anointed

“This is what the Lord says to his anointed, to Cyrus, whose right hand I take hold of.” 1

Light and Darkness

“I form the light and create darkness, I bring prosperity and create disaster.” 2
“I, the Lord, do all these things.”

Quarrel with the Maker

“Woe to the one who quarrels with his Maker, one shard among the shards on the ground!” 3

Turn and Be Saved

“Turn to me and be saved, all you ends of the earth.” 4
“Before me every knee will bow; by me every tongue will swear.” 5

Translation Notes

1 To His Anointed (li-Me-shī-chō): In the scrolls, this is the "Imperial Messiah Root." A "Pagan King" is "Rooted" in the "Anointing" (*Mashach*) of the True God. It signifies "The Instrumental Use of Global Powers."

2 Create Disaster (bō-rē rā): Literally "Creator of Evil/Chaos." The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Root of Causality." God "Roots" the "Outcome" of all events. It signify "The Denial of Dualism."

3 Shard among Shards (che-resh eth-char-śē a-dhā-māh): The scrolls highlight the "Fragility Root." Man is "Rooted" in the "Clay" (*Earth*) and has no standing to "Uproot" the "Plan" of the "Potter" (*Yotzer*). It signify "The Folly of Finite Rebellion."

4 Turn and Be Saved (pe-nū ē-lay we-hi-wā-she-ū): The scrolls highlight the "Universal Centripetal Root." Salvation is "Rooted" in the "Turn" (*Penū*) of the "Ends of the Earth" (*Aph-sey Aretz*). It signifies "Global Monotheism."

5 Every Knee will Bow (kol-be-rekh tikh-ra): The scrolls conclude with this "Genuflective Root." "Pride" is "Uprooted" and "Submission" is "Rooted" in "Every Tongue" (*Lashon*). It marks the transition to "The Falling Gods" in *Isaiah 46*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sovereign One." Turn and live.
