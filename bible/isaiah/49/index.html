Isaiah 49

The Polished Arrow and the Engraved Palms.

Called from the Womb

“Before I was born the Lord called me.”
“He made me into a polished arrow and concealed me in his quiver.” 1

Too Small a Thing

“It is too small a thing for you to be my servant to restore the tribes of Jacob.” 2
“I will also make you a light for the Gentiles.”

Engraved on My Palms

“Can a mother forget the baby at her breast?”
“See, I have engraved you on the palms of my hands.” 3

Rescue from the Fierce

“Can plunder be taken from warriors?”
“Even the captives of warriors will be taken, and plunder rescued from the fierce.” 4
“Then all mankind will know that I, the Lord, am your Savior.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Polished Arrow (chē-tz bā-rūr): In the scrolls, this is the "Ballistic Root of Mission." The "Servant" is "Rooted" as a "Weapon" (*Arrow*) "Polished" (*Barur*) for "Precision." It signifies "The Hidden Potential of the Messiah."

2 Too Small a Thing (nā-qēl mih-yō-the-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Expansive Root of Grace." Restoring "Israel" (*Jacob*) is "Uprooted" as the "Only Goal" to make room for "Global Light" (*Or Goyim*). It signify "The Universal Scope of the Cross."

3 Engraved on Palms (al-kap-pa-yim cha-qaq-tīkh): The scrolls highlight the "Indelible Root of Memory." Zion is "Rooted" not just in "Mind," but in "Flesh" (*Palms/Kappayim*). It signifies "The Perpetual Presence of the Beloved."

4 Rescue from the Fierce (mal-qō-ach tzā-dīq): The scrolls highlight the "Liberative Root of Justice." "Captivity" (*Shebi*) is "Uprooted" from the "Strong" (*Gibbor*). It signify "The Irresistible Rescue."

5 Your Savior (mō-shī-ēkh): The scrolls conclude with this "Identity Root." God’s "Self-Revelation" is "Rooted" in the "Deliverance" of His people. It marks the transition to "The Obedience of the Servant" in *Isaiah 50*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Engraved Name." You are not forgotten.
