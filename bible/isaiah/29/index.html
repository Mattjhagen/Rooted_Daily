Isaiah 29

The Dust and the Sealed Vision.

Ariel in the Dust

“Brought low, you will speak from the ground; your speech will mumble out of the dust.” 1

Lips without Hearts

“These people honor me with their lips, but their hearts are far from me.” 2

Potter and Clay

“You turn things upside down, as if the potter were thought to be like the clay!” 3

The Sealed Vision

“For you this whole vision is nothing but words sealed in a scroll.”
“In that day the deaf will hear the words of the scroll.” 4
“Those who are wayward in spirit will gain understanding.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Speak from the Dust (me-A-phar tim-rath Im-ra-thekh): In the scrolls, this is the "Ghostly Root of Humiliation." The "Speech" (*Imrah*) is "Uprooted" from the "Lips" and "Rooted" in the "Earth" (*Aphar*). It signifies "The Spiritual Exhaustion of the City."

2 Hearts are Far (we-Lib-bow ri-chaq mim-men-ni): The scrolls highlight the "Emotional Root of Hypocrisy." The "Lips" (*Se-pha-thaw*) are "Active," but the "Heart" (*Leb*) is "Uprooted" from the "Source." It signifies "Spiritual Disconnect."

3 Potter and Clay (ke-Cho-mer hay-Yo-tzer): The scrolls highlight the "Existential Root of Error." The "Creation" (*Clay/Chomer*) tries to "Root" itself as the "Creator" (*Potter/Yotzer*). It signify "The Absurdity of Human Autonomy."

4 Deaf will Hear (we-sha-me-u hay-Che-re-shim): The scrolls highlight the "Sensory Root of Restoration." The "Sealed Vision" is "Uprooted" into "Audible Truth." It signify "The End of Spiritual Ignorance."

5 Wayward in Spirit (to-ey Ru-ach): The scrolls conclude with this "Corrective Root." "Understanding" (*Binah*) is "Rooted" in the "Broken" (*Wayward*). It marks the transition to "The Rebellion against God’s Plan" in *Isaiah 30*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Opened Scroll." Listen to the dust.
