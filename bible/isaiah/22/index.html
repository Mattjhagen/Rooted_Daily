Isaiah 22

The Burden of the Valley of Vision.

Valley of Vision

“What troubles you now, that you have all gone up on the roofs?”
“For the Lord has a day of tumult and trampling and terror in the Valley of Vision.” 1

Physical Walls, Spiritual Gaps

“You made a reservoir for the water of the Old Pool.”
“But you did not look to the One who made it, or have regard for the One who planned it long ago.” 2

Eat and Drink

“Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die!” 3

The Key of David

“I will place on his shoulder the key to the house of David.” 4
“I will drive him like a peg into a firm place.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Valley of Vision (Gē Chiz-zā-yōn): In the scrolls, this is the "Optical Root of Judgment." Jerusalem is "Rooted" as the place of "Vision" (*Chizzayon*), but it has become a "Valley" (*Ge*) of "Tumult." It signifies "The Paradox of the Blind City."

2 Did not look to the One (we-lō hi-bat-tem el-ō-se-yā): The scrolls highlight the "Source Root." The people "Rooted" their "Defense" in "Physical Structures" (*Reservoirs/Walls*) but "Uprooted" their "Gaze" (*Hibat*) from the "Maker" (*Oseya*). It signifies "The Folly of Materialism."

3 Tomorrow we Die (ki-mā-chār nā-mūth): The scrolls highlight the "Nihilistic Root of Hedonism." "Revelry" (*Sa-son*) is "Rooted" in "Despair." It signify "The Banquet at the Edge of the Abyss."

4 Key of David (maph-tē-ach Bēyth-Dā-wīd): The scrolls highlight the "Authoritative Root of Stewardship." The "Key" (*Maphteach*) "Roots" the "Absolute Access" of the faithful. It signify "The Messianic Delegation of Power."

5 Peg in a Firm Place (yā-thēdh be-mā-qōm ne-e-mān): The scrolls conclude with this "Structural Root." Stability is "Rooted" in "Faithfulness" (*Ne-eman*). However, even the "Peg" (*Yathedh*) can "Give Way" if the "Load" is too "Heavy." It marks the transition to "The Burden of Tyre" in *Isaiah 23*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The True Support." Regard the Planner.
