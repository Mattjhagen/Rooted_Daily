Isaiah 12

A Song of Salvation.

God is My Salvation

“Surely God is my salvation; I will trust and not be afraid.” 1

The Wells of Salvation

“With joy you will draw water from the wells of salvation.” 2

Proclaim His Name

“Give praise to the Lord, proclaim his name; make known among the nations what he has done.” 3

Shout and Sing

“Shout aloud and sing for joy, people of Zion, for great is the Holy One of Israel among you.” 4

Translation Notes

1 God is my Salvation (El Ye-shu-a-thi): In the scrolls, this is the "Ontological Root of Security." "Salvation" (*Yeshua*) is "Rooted" in the "Being" (*El*) of God. It signifies "The Personalization of Deliverance."

2 Draw Water from the Wells (u-she-abh-tem Ma-yim mim-Ma-y-ney): The scrolls highlight the "Hydraulic Root of Joy." "Joy" (*Sa-son*) is the "Bucket" that "Roots" the soul in the "Deep Springs" (*Ma-y-ney*) of "Salvation." It signify "Continuous Resourcefulness."

3 Make known among the Nations (ho-di-u bā-am-mīm): The scrolls highlight the "Informational Root of Testimony." The "Work" (*Ali-lo-thaw*) of God is "Uprooted" from "Secrecy" and "Rooted" in "Global Proclamation." It signifies "Spiritual Evangelism."

4 Great is the Holy One (Ga-dol be-qir-bekh Qe-dosh Is-ra-el): The scrolls conclude with this "Inhabitory Root." The "Holy One" is "Rooted" in the "Midst" (*be-qir-bekh*) of Zion. It marks the transition to the "Oracles against the Nations" in *Isaiah 13*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Resident Majesty." Draw from the well.
