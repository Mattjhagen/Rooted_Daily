Isaiah 19

The Swift Cloud over the Nile.

The Swift Cloud

“See, the Lord rides on a swift cloud and is coming to Egypt.” 1

The Parched River

“The waters of the river will dry up, and the riverbed will be parched and dry.” 2

An Altar in Egypt

“In that day there will be an altar to the Lord in the heart of Egypt.” 3

The Triple Blessing

“In that day Israel will be the third, along with Egypt and Assyria.”
“Blessed be Egypt my people, Assyria my handiwork, and Israel my inheritance.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Swift Cloud (Ab qal): In the scrolls, this is the "Meteorological Root of Judgment." God "Roots" his "Arrival" in "Velocity" (*Qal*) and "Vapor" (*Ab*). It signifies "The Overwhelming Presence."

2 Waters... dry up (we-nish-te-thu Ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Ecological Root of Bankruptcy." The "Nile" (*Yeor*), which Egypt "Rooted" as a "God," is "Uprooted" (*Drained*). It signifies "The Desacralization of Nature."

3 Altar in Egypt (Miz-be-ach Yah-weh be-thokh E-retz Mitz-ra-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Conversion Root." The "Heart" of the "Old Enemy" is "Re-Rooted" in "Sacrifice" (*Mizbeach*) to the True God. It signify "The Redemption of Geography."

4 Egypt my People (Am-mi Mitz-ra-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Inclusionary Root." The "Dividing Walls" are "Uprooted." Egypt, Assyria, and Israel are "Rooted" in a "Triune Blessing." It marks the transition to "The Sign of the Captives" in *Isaiah 20*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unified Family." Peace to the river.
