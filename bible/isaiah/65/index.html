Isaiah 65

The New Heavens, the New Earth, and the Peace of the Lamb.

Found by the Unseeking

“I revealed myself to those who did not ask for me; I was found by those who did not seek me.” 1

Juice in the Cluster

“As when juice is still found in a cluster of grapes... ‘Don’t destroy it, there is still a blessing in it.’” 2

New Heavens and New Earth

“See, I will create new heavens and a new earth. The former things will not be remembered.” 3

Jerusalem a Delight

“I will create Jerusalem to be a delight and its people a joy.” 4
“No longer will the sound of weeping be heard.”

The Peace of the Kingdom

“The wolf and the lamb will feed together, and the lion will eat straw like the ox.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Revealed to those who did not Ask (nid-rash-tī le-lō shā-ā-lū): In the scrolls, this is the "Proactive Root of Grace." "Search" (*Darash*) is "Uprooted" as the "Condition" for "Manifestation." It signifies "God’s Initiative toward the Outsider."

2 Juice in the Cluster (hat-tī-rōsh ban-ash-kōl): The scrolls highlight the "Organic Root of the Remnant." "Total Destruction" is "Uprooted" because of the "Potential" (*Juice/Tirosh*) within the "Community" (*Cluster*). It signify "The Preservation of the Elect."

3 New Heavens and New Earth (sha-ma-yim cha-dhā-shīm we-e-retz cha-dhā-shāh): The scrolls highlight the "Cosmological Root of Restoration." The "Old" (*Former*) is "Uprooted" from "Memory" (*Zik-ka-ron*) and the "New" (*Chadash*) is "Rooted" in "Perpetuity." It signifies "The Ultimate Renewal."

4 Jerusalem a Delight (le-ghi-lāh): The scrolls highlight the "Emotional Root of the City." "Sorrow" is "Uprooted" and "Exultation" (*Gil*) is "Rooted." It signify "The Aesthetic of the Eternal City."

5 Wolf and Lamb (ze-ēbh we-ta-leh): The scrolls conclude with this "Zoological Root of Peace." "Predation" is "Uprooted" and "Harmony" is "Rooted." It marks the transition to "The Final Judgment" in *Isaiah 66*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The New Creation." The former is forgotten.
