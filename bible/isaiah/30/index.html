Isaiah 30

Quietness, Trust, and the Gracious Way.

Rebellious Children

“Woe to the rebellious children... who go down to Egypt without consulting me.”
“Pharaoh’s protection will be to your shame.” 1

Quietness and Trust

“In quietness and trust is your strength.” 2
“In repentance and rest is your salvation.” 3

The Gracious Lord

“The Lord longs to be gracious to you; he will rise up to show you compassion.” 4

This is the Way

“Your ears will hear a voice behind you: ‘This is the way; walk in it.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 Egypt’s Shadow (tze-el Mitz-ra-yim): In the scrolls, this is the "Parasitic Root of Reliance." The people "Root" their "Safety" in a "Shadow" (*Tze-el*) that has no "Substance." It signifies "The Folly of Earthly Alliances."

2 Quietness and Trust (be-Hash-qe-et u-be-Bit-chah): The scrolls highlight the "Passive Root of Power." "Quietness" (*Hashqe-et*) and "Trust" (*Bit-chah*) "Root" the soul in "Strength" (*Ge-bu-rah*). It signifies "The Spiritual Power of Stillness."

3 Repentance and Rest (be-Shu-bhah wa-Na-chath): The scrolls highlight the "Restorative Root of Salvation." "Returning" (*Shubhah*) "Roots" the "Rest" (*Nachath*). It signify "The Recovery of the True Center."

4 Longs to be Gracious (ye-chak-keh Yah-weh la-cha-nan-khem): Literally "The Lord waits/longs." The scrolls highlight the "Divine Patience Root." God "Roots" his "Action" in "Waiting" for the human "Response." It signify "The Proactive Mercy of God."

5 This is the Way (zeh had-De-rekh le-khu-bhah): The scrolls conclude with this "Directional Root." The "Voice" (*Qol*) "Roots" the "Path" (*Derekh*). It marks the transition to "The Folly of Horses" in *Isaiah 31*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Guiding Voice." Walk in it.
