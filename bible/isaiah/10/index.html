Isaiah 10

The Rod of Anger and the Return of the Remnant.

Unjust Laws

“Woe to those who make unjust laws, to those who issue oppressive decrees.” 1

The Boasting Ax

“Woe to the Assyrian, the rod of my anger.”
“Does the ax raise itself above the person who swings it?” 2

A Remnant Returns

“In that day the remnant of Israel... will truly rely on the Lord.”
“A remnant will return, a remnant of Jacob will return to the Mighty God.” 3

Lopping the Boughs

“The Lord Almighty will lop off the boughs with great power.” 4
“Lebanon will fall before the Mighty One.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Unjust Laws (cho-qe-qey chig-rey-a-wen): In the scrolls, this is the "Legal Root of Iniquity." To "Hock/Carve" (*Cho-qeq*) "Painful/Vain" (*Awen*) laws is to "Uproot" the "Rights" (*Mishpat*) of the "Poor" (*Ani-yim*). It signifies "Legislative Tyranny."

2 Does the Ax raise itself (ha-yit-pa-er hag-Gar-zen): The scrolls highlight the "Instrumental Root of Pride." The "Ax" (*Garzen*) is an "Extension" of the "Sovereign" (*God*). When it "Roots" its "Identity" in "Self-Sufficiency," it is "Uprooted." It signifies "The Folly of the Tool."

3 Remnant will Return (She-ar Ya-shub): The scrolls highlight the "Survivorship Root." The name of Isaiah’s son (*Shear-Jashub*) is "Rooted" in "The Promise of Recovery." Only a "Remnant" (*Shear*) will be "Re-Rooted" in the "Mighty God" (*El Gib-bor*). It signify "Spiritual Residue."

4 Lop off the Boughs (me-sa-eph Pu-rah): The scrolls highlight the "Pruning Root of Power." The "Pride" of nations is like "Tall Trees" that are "Uprooted" or "Lopped" (*Mesa-eph*) by the "Mighty One." It signify "The Divine Deforestation of Arrogance."

5 Lebanon will Fall (we-hal-Le-ba-non be-Ad-dir yip-powl): The scrolls conclude with this "Catastrophic Root." Even the "Majestic Cedar" (*Lebanon*) is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Branch from the Stem of Jesse" in *Isaiah 11*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Returning Remnant." Rely on the Wood-Cutter.
