Isaiah 25

The Feast and the Victory over Death.

Refuge for the Poor

“You have been a refuge for the poor, a refuge for the needy in their distress.” 1

A Feast for All Peoples

“On this mountain the Lord will prepare a feast of rich food for all peoples.” 2

Death Swallowed Forever

“He will swallow up death forever.” 3
“The Sovereign Lord will wipe away the tears from all faces.” 4

Surely This Is Our God

“Surely this is our God; we trusted in him, and he saved us.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Refuge for the Poor (Mā-ōz lad-dāl): In the scrolls, this is the "Structural Root of Mercy." God "Roots" the "Defense" (*Maoz*) in the "Vulnerability" of the "Poor" (*Dal*). It signifies "The Divine Protection of the Marginalized."

2 Feast of Rich Food (Mish-tē she-mā-nīm): The scrolls highlight the "Satisfying Root of Provision." The "Mount" (*Har*) is "Rooted" as the "Place of Overflow." It signify "The Abundance of the Kingdom."

3 Swallow up Death (bil-la ham-Mā-weth lā-ne-tzach): The scrolls highlight the "Metabolic Root of Victory." "Death" (*Maweth*), which once "Swallowed" all, is now "Uprooted" and "Swallowed" (*Billa*) by God. It signifies "The Ultimate Defeat of Mortality."

4 Wipe away Tears (mā-chā Yah-weh... Dim-āh): The scrolls highlight the "Compassionate Root of Healing." "Sorrow" (*Dimah*) is "Uprooted" from the "Face" (*Panim*). It signify "The Tenderness of the Sovereign."

5 We Trusted in Him (qiw-wi-nu lō): The scrolls conclude with this "Wait-Root." "Salvation" (*Yeshua*) is "Rooted" in "Waiting/Trusting" (*Qiwwi*). It marks the transition to "The Song of Trust" in *Isaiah 26*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Saved Remnant." Rejoice in the feast.
