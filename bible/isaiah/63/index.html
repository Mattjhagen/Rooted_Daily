Isaiah 63

The Winepress of Wrath and the Plea to the Father.

Garments Stained Crimson

“Who is this coming from Edom, from Bozrah?”
“It is I, proclaiming vindication, mighty to save.” 1

Trodden Alone

“I have trodden the winepress alone; no one from the nations was with me.” 2
“The year for me to redeem had come.”

Grieving the Spirit

“In all their affliction he was afflicted.”
“But they rebelled and grieved his Holy Spirit.” 3

Our Father

“Look down from heaven and see, from your lofty throne.”
“But you are our Father... our Redeemer from of old is your name.” 4
“Why do you harden our hearts?” 5

Translation Notes

1 Mighty to Save (rab le-hō-shī-a): In the scrolls, this is the "Sovereign Root of Rescue." The "Warrior" is "Rooted" in "Authority" (*Rab*) to "Deliver" (*Hoshia*). It signifies "The Active Salvation of God."

2 Winepress Alone (pū-rāh dā-rakh-tī le-bhad-dī): The scrolls highlight the "Solitary Root of Judgment." The "Nations" are "Uprooted" (*Trodden*) by the "Divine Foot" in the "Press" (*Purah*). It signify "The Exclusive Agency of God in History."

3 Grieved His Holy Spirit (atz-tze-bhū eth-Rū-ach qādh-shō): The scrolls highlight the "Emotional Root of God." "Rebellion" (*Mara*) "Roots" "Pain" (*Atz-tze-bhū*) in the "Divine Spirit" (*Ruach*). It signify "The Personhood and Sensitivity of the Holy Spirit."

4 Redeemer from of Old (Gō-a-lē-nū mē-ō-lām): The scrolls highlight the "Ancestral Root of Redemption." God is "Rooted" as the "Kinsman" (*Go-el*) from "Eternity" (*Olam*). It signifies "The Biological and Legal Claim of God on His People."

5 Harden our Hearts (taq-shī-ach lib-bē-nū): The scrolls conclude with this "Hardening Root." The "Obstinacy" is "Rooted" in a "Divine Judicial Hiding." It marks the transition to "The Rend the Heavens Prayer" in *Isaiah 64*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Father in Crimson." Return for your servants’ sake.
