Isaiah 15

The Burden of the Wailing Nation.

Destroyed in a Night

“Ar in Moab is ruined, destroyed in a night! Kir in Moab is ruined, destroyed in a night!” 1

Every Head is Shaved

“Moab wails over Nebo and Medeba.”
“Every head is shaved and every beard cut off.” 2

Dried Waters

“The waters of Nimrim are dried up and the grass is withered.” 3

Waters of Blood

“The waters of Dimon are full of blood, but I will bring still more.” 4
“My heart cries out over Moab.” 5

Translation Notes

1 In a Night (be-Leyl): In the scrolls, this is the "Nocturnal Root of Doom." The "Destruction" (*Shud-dad*) "Uproots" the "City" (*Ar/Kir*) while it sleeps. It signifies "The Stealth of Judgment."

2 Every Head is Shaved (be-kol-Ro-shaw Qor-chah): The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Root of Mourning." "Dignity" (*Hair/Beard*) is "Uprooted" to show "Despair" (*Qorchah*). It signifies "The Externalization of Internal Ruin."

3 Waters... Dried up (Ma-yim... ne-sham-moth): The scrolls highlight the "Hydrological Root of Life." When the "Waters" (*Nimrim*) are "Uprooted" (*Dried/Desolate*), the "Grass" (*Chatzir*) "Uproots" its "Greenness." It signify "Ecological Retribution."

4 Full of Blood (ma-le-u Dam): The scrolls highlight the "Sanguine Root of Slaughter." "Water" (*Mayim*) is "Rooted" in "Life," but "Dimon" (*Resonant with 'Dam' - Blood*) is "Rooted" in "Death." It signify "The Scent of the Lion."

5 My Heart cries out (Lib-bi le-Mo-ab yiz-aq): The scrolls conclude with this "Empathetic Root." Even the "Prophet" (*Isaiah*) "Roots" his "Lament" (*Yizaq*) in the "Suffering" of the enemy. It marks the transition to "The Plea for Mercy" in *Isaiah 16*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Shared Tears." Weep with the broken.
