Isaiah 23

The Burden of the Merchant City.

Wail, You Ships

“Wail, you ships of Tarshish! For Tyre is destroyed and left without house or harbor.” 1

Humble the Renowned

“The Lord Almighty planned it, to bring low the pride of all glory.” 2
“To humble all who are renowned on the earth.”

The Song of the Prostitute

“Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years.”
“Take up a harp, walk through the city, you forgotten prostitute.” 3

Holiness to the Lord

“Yet her profit and her earnings will be set apart for the Lord.” 4
“They will provide ample food and fine clothing.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Wail, you Ships (He-y-li-lu o-ni-yoth Tar-shish): In the scrolls, this is the "Mercantile Root of Sorrow." The "Empire of the Sea" is "Uprooted" from its "Harbor" (*Ma-bo*). It signifies "The Fragility of Global Commerce."

2 Humble the Renowned (le-ha-qel kol-Nikh-bad-dey A-retz): The scrolls highlight the "Gravitational Root of Judgment." God "Roots" the "Outcome" of "Tyre" (*Tzor*) in the "Deflation" of its "Renown" (*Nikh-baddey*). It signifies "The Divine Veto over Human Status."

3 Forgotten Prostitute (Zo-nah nish-ka-chah): The scrolls highlight the "Economic Root of Idolatry." Tyre is "Rooted" in "Commerce" (*Trade/Zenuth*) that "Uproots" its "Moral Anchor." The "Song" signifies "The Tragic Re-entry into the Market."

4 Set apart for the Lord (Qo-desh la-Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Redemptive Root of Finance." The "Profit" (*Eth-nan*) is "Uprooted" from "Greed" and "Re-Rooted" in "Sanctification" (*Qodesh*). It signify "The Consecration of Capital."

5 Ample Food (le-Ak-lah le-Sab-ah): The scrolls conclude with this "Nutritional Root." The "Wealth" of the nations "Roots" the "Provision" for the "Saints" (*Those who live before the Lord*). It marks the transition to "The Global Apocalypse" in *Isaiah 24*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Consecrated Profit." The harbor belongs to God.
