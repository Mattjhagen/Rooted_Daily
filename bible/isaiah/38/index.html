Isaiah 38

The Added Years and the Backward Shadow.

Turn to the Wall

“Put your house in order, because you are going to die.”
“Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the Lord.” 1

The Backward Shadow

“I will add fifteen years to your life.” 2
“I will make the shadow go back the ten steps it has gone down.” 3

Bitterness to Peace

“In your love you kept me from the pit of destruction.” 4
“You have put all my sins behind your back.”

The Poultice of Figs

“Prepare a poultice of figs and apply it to the boil, and he will recover.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Turned to the Wall (wa-yas-sēbh pe-nāw el-ha-qīr): In the scrolls, this is the "Introspective Root of Prayer." Hezekiah "Uproots" his "Gaze" from the "World" and "Roots" it in the "Presence" (*Wall/Qir*). It signifies "The Privacy of Final Intercession."

2 Fifteen Years (cha-mēsh es-rēh shā-nāh): The scrolls highlight the "Extension Root of Grace." "Death" (*Maweth*) is "Uprooted" and "Time" is "Re-Rooted" in "Life" (*Chay*). It signify "The Divine Modification of the Calendar."

3 Shadow go Back (wa-tā-shobh hash-She-mesh): The scrolls highlight the "Chronological Root of Sign." God "Uproots" the "Law of Time" (*The Shadow*) to "Root" the "Certainty" of His Word. It signify "Sovereignty over the Sun."

4 Pit of Destruction (mi-sha-chath be-lī): Literally "The Pit of Nothingness." The scrolls highlight the "Existential Root of Salvation." The King is "Rooted" in "Love" (*Cha-shaq-tā*) which "Uproots" him from "Annihilation." It signify "The Tether of Love."

5 Poultice of Figs (de-bhē-lath te-ē-nīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Medicinal Root." God "Roots" the "Healing" in "Nature" (*Figs*). It marks the transition to "The Babylonian Envoys" in *Isaiah 39*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Added Years." Use the figs, trust the King.
