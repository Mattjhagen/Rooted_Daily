Isaiah 57

The High and Lofty One and the Tossing Sea.

Righteous Taken Away

“The righteous are taken away to be spared from evil.” 1

The High and Lofty One

“I live in a high and holy place.”
“But also with the one who is contrite and lowly in spirit.” 2
“To revive the heart of the contrite.”

Peace Far and Near

“‘Peace, peace, to those far and near,’ says the Lord.” 3

The Tossing Sea

“But the wicked are like the tossing sea, which cannot rest.” 4
“‘There is no peace,’ says my God, ‘for the wicked.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 Righteous perish (hatz-tzad-dīq ā-bhādh): In the scrolls, this is the "Protective Root of Death." The "Righteous" (*Tzaddiq*) are "Uprooted" from the "Earth" (*Abhad*) to "Root" them in "Safety" (*Spared from evil*). It signifies "The Merciful Timing of God."

2 Contrite and Lowly (dak-kā u-she-phal rū-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Shattered Root of Presence." God "Roots" His "Dwelling" (*Shakhanti*) not just in the "High" (*Marom*), but in the "Crushed" (*Dakka*). It signifies "The Paradox of Divine Immanence."

3 Peace Far and Near (Sha-lōm Sha-lōm la-rā-chōq we-la-qā-rōbh): The scrolls highlight the "Spatial Root of Reconciliation." "Peace" (*Shalom*) is "Rooted" as a "Double Blessing" for the "Exile" (*Far*) and the "Remnant" (*Near*). It signify "The Universal Scope of Healing."

4 Tossing Sea (ke-yām nig-rāsh): The scrolls highlight the "Unstable Root of Iniquity." The "Wicked" (*Rasha*) are "Uprooted" from "Rest" (*Shā-qat*) and "Rooted" in "Turbulence" (*Tossing*). It signifies "The Internal Chaos of Sin."

5 No Peace for the Wicked (ēn Sha-lōm... la-re-shā-īm): The scrolls conclude with this "Absence Root." "Peace" is "Uprooted" from the "Wicked" life. It marks the transition to "The True Fast" in *Isaiah 58*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Revived Spirit." Find rest in Him.
