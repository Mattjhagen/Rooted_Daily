Isaiah 39

The Envoys and the Revealed Treasures.

Visitors from Babylon

“Merodach-Baladan... sent Hezekiah letters and a gift.”
“Hezekiah received them gladly and showed them his storehouses.” 1

Carried to Babylon

“Everything in your palace will be carried off to Babylon. Nothing will be left.” 2
“Your descendants will become eunuchs in the palace of Babylon.” 3

The Good Word

“The word of the Lord you have spoken is good.” 4
“For he thought, ‘There will be peace and security in my lifetime.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 Showed them his Storehouses (wa-yar-ēm eth-bēyth ne-kho-thow): In the scrolls, this is the "Exhibitory Root of Pride." Hezekiah "Roots" his "Identity" in "Material Wealth" (*Silver/Gold*) rather than the "Divine Deliverance." It signifies "The Vulnerability of the Revealed Storehouse."

2 Nothing will be Left (lō-yi-wā-thēr dā-bhār): The scrolls highlight the "Empty Root of Exile." The "Inheritance" is "Uprooted" and "Carried" (*Yūsā*) to "Babylon" (*Confusion*). It signify "The Consequence of Political Pride."

3 Eunuchs (sā-rī-sīm): The scrolls highlight the "Sterility Root." The "Future" (*Descendants*) is "Uprooted" from "Generative Power" to serve the "Foreign King." It signify "The Mutilation of the Dynasty."

4 Word is Good (tōbh de-bhar-Yah-weh): The scrolls highlight the "Acceptance Root." Hezekiah "Roots" his "Reaction" in "Agreement" (*Tobh*), but the "Motivation" is "Individualistic." It signify "The Acceptance of Divine Sentence."

5 In my Lifetime (be-yā-māy): The scrolls conclude with this "Temporal Root." Hezekiah "Roots" his "Peace" (*Shalom*) in "Short-term Security." It marks the transition to "The Message of Comfort" in *Isaiah 40*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sovereign Future." Peace is coming, but not as you think.
