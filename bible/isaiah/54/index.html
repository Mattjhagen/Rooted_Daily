Isaiah 54

The Singing Barren and the Unshakable Love.

Sing, Barren Woman

“Sing, barren woman, you who never bore a child; burst into song, shout for joy!” 1

Enlarge Your Tent

“Enlarge the place of your tent, stretch your tent curtains wide, do not hold back.” 2
“Lengthen your cords, strengthen your stakes.”

Unfailing Love

“Though the mountains be shaken and the hills be removed, yet my unfailing love for you will not be shaken.” 3

No Weapon Prevails

“No weapon forged against you will prevail.” 4
“This is the heritage of the servants of the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sing, Barren Woman (ron-nī a-qā-rāh): In the scrolls, this is the "Biological Reversal Root." "Childlessness" (*Aqarah*) is "Uprooted" by "Exultant Song" (*Rannī*). It signifies "The Fruitfulness of the Spirit."

2 Enlarge the Place (har-chī-bhī me-qōm): The scrolls highlight the "Architectural Root of Expansion." The "Restrictions" of the past are "Uprooted" and "Space" (*Me-qom*) is "Rooted" in "Growth." It signify "The Preparation for the Multi-ethnic Family of God."

3 Unfailing Love (chas-dī): The scrolls highlight the "Geological Stability Root." Even "Mountains" (*He-ha-rīm*) are "Uprooted" before "Covenant Love" (*Chesed*) is shaken. It signify "The Permanent Security of Zion."

4 No Weapon (kol-ke-lī): The scrolls highlight the "Defense Root." Every "Instrument" (*Keli*) of "Warfare" is "Uprooted" by "Divine Protection." It signify "The Invincibility of the Church."

5 Heritage of the Servants (na-cha-lath ab-dhē): The scrolls conclude with this "Legative Root." "Victory" is "Rooted" as the "Inheritance" (*Nachalah*) of those who "Serve" (*Abde*). It marks the transition to "The Great Invitation" in *Isaiah 55*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unshakable Love." Enlarge your heart.
