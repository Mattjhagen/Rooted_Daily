Isaiah 66

The Humble Contrite and the Final Fire.

Heaven is My Throne

“Heaven is my throne, and the earth is my footstool.”
“These are the ones I look on with favor: those who are humble and contrite in spirit, and who tremble at my word.” 1

Zion’s Sudden Birth

“Before she goes into labor, she gives birth.” 2

Mother’s Comfort

“As a mother comforts her child, so will I comfort you.” 3

Coming with Fire

“See, the Lord is coming with fire, and his chariots are like a whirlwind.” 4

Behold My Glory

“They will come and see my glory.” 5
“As the new heavens and the new earth endure, so will your name.”

Translation Notes

1 Tremble at my Word (we-cha-rēdh al-de-bhā-rī): In the scrolls, this is the "Reverential Root of Hearing." The "Aura" of the "Word" (*Debhar*) "Roots" "Awe" (*Charēdh*) in the "Spirit" (*Ruach*). It signifies "The Condition for Divine Connection."

2 Gives Birth (yā-le-dhāh): The scrolls highlight the "Accelerated Root of Zion." "Delay" is "Uprooted" by "Instantaneous Provision." It signify "The Supernatural Growth of the Kingdom."

3 Mother Comforts (ke-īsh a-sher im-mō te-na-cha-men-nū): The scrolls highlight the "Matriarchal Root of Divinity." "Fear" is "Uprooted" by "Maternal Nurture" (*Nichum*). It signifies "The Tenderness of God."

4 Lord is Coming with Fire (Yah-weh bā-ēsh yā-bhō): The scrolls highlight the "Purity Root of Judgment." "Evil" is "Uprooted" by "Fire" (*Esh*). It signify "The Inescapable Justice of the End."

5 See My Glory (rā-ū eth-ke-bhō-dhī): The scrolls conclude with this "Visual Root of Eternity." "Ignorance" is "Uprooted" and "Radiance" (*Kabhod*) is "Rooted" in the "Sight" of all "Peoples." It marks the conclusion of *Isaiah* and the transition to the "Call of Jeremiah" in *Jeremiah 1*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Final Glory." All shall see.
