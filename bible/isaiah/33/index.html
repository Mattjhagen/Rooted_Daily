Isaiah 33

The King in Beauty and the Divine Judge.

Woe to the Destroyer

“Woe to you, destroyer, you who have not been destroyed!”
“‘Now I will arise,’ says the Lord. ‘Now I will be exalted; now I will be lifted up.’” 1

Consuming Fire

“Who of us can dwell with the consuming fire? Who of us can dwell with everlasting burning?” 2

The King in Beauty

“Your eyes will see the king in his beauty and view a land that stretches afar.” 3

Judge, Lawgiver, King

“For the Lord is our judge, the Lord is our lawgiver, the Lord is our king; it is he who will save us.” 4
“It will be like a place of broad rivers and streams.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Now I will Arise (at-tah a-qum): In the scrolls, this is the "Initiative Root of God." After the "Activity" of the "Destroyer" (*Shō-dhēdh*), God "Roots" his "Manifestation" in the "Present Moment" (*At-tah*). It signifies "The Divine Intervention."

2 Consuming Fire (Esh o-khe-lah): The scrolls highlight the "Thermal Root of Sanctity." Only the "Righteous" (*Tzaddiq*) can "Root" their "Existence" in the "Presence" (*Fire*) of God. It signify "The Purity Required for Communion."

3 King in His Beauty (Me-lekh be-yo-phyow): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of the Messiah." The "Vision" (*Mar-eh*) is "Rooted" in "Beauty" (*Yophy*). It signifies "The Spiritual Attraction of the King."

4 Judge, Lawgiver, King (Sho-phe-te-nu, me-cho-qe-qe-nu, Mal-ke-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Triune Root of Authority." God "Roots" all "Branches of Government" in His own "Being." It signify "The Divine Monopoly of Power."

5 Broad Rivers (ne-ha-rim ye-o-rim ra-cha-bey ya-da-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Hydrological Root of Defense." Unlike cities with physical moats, Zion is "Rooted" in "Spiritual Abundance" (*Broad Rivers*). It marks the transition to "The Judgment on Nations" in *Isaiah 34*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sovereign Savior." He is your Law.
