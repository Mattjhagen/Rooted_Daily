Isaiah 41

The Chosen Servant and the Fearless Heart.

Chosen and Not Rejected

“But you, Israel, my servant, Jacob, whom I have chosen.”
“I said, ‘You are my servant’; I have chosen you and have not rejected you.” 1

Do Not Fear

“So do not fear, for I am with you; do not be dismayed, for I am your God.” 2
“I will uphold you with my righteous right hand.” 3

The Worm Jacob

“Do not be afraid, you worm Jacob, little Israel, for I myself will help you.” 4

Rivers on Barren Heights

“I will make rivers flow on barren heights, and springs within the valleys.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Chosen and Not Rejected (be-char-tī-khā we-lō me-as-tī-khā): In the scrolls, this is the "Election Root of Security." Israel is "Rooted" in the "Choice" (*Be-char*) of God, which "Uproots" the "Fear of Rejection" (*Me-as*). It signifies "The Permanent Status of the Chosen."

2 Do Not Fear (al-tī-rā): The scrolls highlight the "Courage Root." "Fear" (*Yira*) is "Uprooted" by "Presence" (*I am with you*). It signify "The Psychological Armor of God."

3 Righteous Right Hand (bi-mīn tzid-qī): The scrolls highlight the "Dextrous Root of Support." God "Roots" the "Upholding" (*Te-makh*) in His "Right Hand" (*Yamin*). It signify "The Divine Agency of Strength."

4 Worm Jacob (to-la-at Ya-a-qob): The scrolls highlight the "Humility Root." The "Insignificance" (*Worm*) is "Re-Rooted" in "Divine Help" (*Azar*). It signify "The Strength of the Weak."

5 Rivers on Barren Heights (ne-ha-rōth al-she-phā-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Irrigation Root of Miracle." "Aridity" is "Uprooted" and "Supply" (*Rivers*) is "Rooted" in "High Places" (*Shephayim*). It marks the transition to "The Servant of the Lord" in *Isaiah 42*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Fearless Chosen." Drink from the heights.
