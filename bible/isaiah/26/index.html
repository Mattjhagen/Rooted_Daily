Isaiah 26

The Song of Perfect Peace.

Salvation as Walls

“We have a strong city; God makes salvation its walls and ramparts.” 1

Perfect Peace

“You will keep in perfect peace those whose minds are steadfast, because they trust in you.” 2

The Rock of Ages

“Trust in the Lord forever, for the Lord is the Rock eternal.” 3

Wake Up and Shout

“But your dead will live, Lord; their bodies will rise.” 4
“You who dwell in the dust, wake up and shout for joy!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Salvation its Walls (Ye-shu-ah ya-shith Cho-moth): In the scrolls, this is the "Metaphysical Root of Defense." The "City" is "Rooted" not in stone, but in "Deliverance" (*Yeshua*). It signifies "The Security of the Spirit."

2 Perfect Peace (Sha-lom Sha-lom): Literally "Peace, Peace." The scrolls highlight the "Recursive Root of Stability." A "Steadfast Mind" (*Ye-tzer sa-mukh*) is "Rooted" in the "Double Peace" of God. It signifies "Total Psychological Equilibrium."

3 Rock Eternal (Tzur O-la-mim): Known as the "Rock of Ages." The scrolls highlight the "Chronological Root of Stability." God is the "Rock" (*Tzur*) "Rooted" in "Eternity" (*Olamim*). It signify "The Unchanging Foundation."

4 Dead will Live (yich-yu Me-the-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Biological Root of Hope." "Death" (*Methim*) is "Uprooted" by "Life" (*Chay*). It signifies "The Prophecy of Resurrection."

5 Dwell in the Dust (sho-khe-ney A-phar): The scrolls conclude with this "Terrestrial Root." Those "Rooted" in "Dust" (*Aphar*) will be "Uprooted" into "Joy" (*Ran-ne-nu*). It marks the transition to "The Destruction of Leviathan" in *Isaiah 27*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Rising Dust." Wake and sing.
