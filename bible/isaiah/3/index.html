Isaiah 3

The Removal of the Support.

Stay and Staff

“The Lord is about to take from Jerusalem and Judah both supply and support.”
“I will make mere youths their officials; children will rule over them.” 1

Paraded Sin

“They parade their sin like Sodom; they do not hide it. Woe to them!” 2

Daughters of Zion

“The daughters of Zion are haughty, walking with outstretched necks.”
“Instead of fragrance there will be a stench; instead of a sash, a rope.” 3

Destitute She Sits

“The gates of Zion will lament and mourn; destitute, she will sit on the ground.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Stay and Staff (Mash-en u-ma-she-nah): In the scrolls, this is the "Structural Root of Society." When God "Uproots" the "Support" (*Mashen*), the "Order" of the city "Falls" (*Ka-she-lah*). It signifies "Institutional Collapse."

2 Parade their Sin (ke-So-dom hig-gi-du): The scrolls highlight the "Exhibitionist Root of Corruption." Sin is no longer "Rooted" in "Shame" but is "Uprooted" into "Public Pride." It signifies "Moral Insolence."

3 Stench for Fragrance (ta-chath Bo-sem Ma-q): Literally "Instead of balsam/perfume, rottenness." The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Judgment." The "Finery" of the body is "Uprooted" by "Rot" (*Maq*) and "Baldness" (*Qar-chah*). It signify "The Exposure of Vanity."

4 Destitute she Sits (ne-qah la-A-retz te-sheb): The scrolls conclude with this "Spatial Root of Mourning." The "City" is "Uprooted" from her "Seat of Power" and "Rooted" in the "Dust" (*Aretz*). It marks the transition to "The Branch of the Lord" in *Isaiah 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Grounded Mourner." Humility is the only soil left.
