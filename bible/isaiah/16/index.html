Isaiah 16

The Pride of Moab and the Throne of Love.

Shelter the Fugitives

“Hide the fugitives, do not betray the refugees.”
“Let the Moabite fugitives stay with you; be their shelter from the destroyer.” 1

The Throne of Love

“In love a throne will be established; in faithfulness a man will sit on it—one from the house of David.” 2

The Arrogance of Moab

“We have heard of Moab’s pride—how great is her arrogance!—of her conceit and her insolence.” 3

Lament like a Harp

“My heart laments for Moab like a harp, my inmost being for Kir Hareseth.” 4
“Within three years... Moab’s splendor will be despised.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Hide the Fugitives (has-ti-ri nid-da-chim): In the scrolls, this is the "Asylum Root." The "Fugitives" (*Niddachim*) are to be "Rooted" in "Safety" (*Hastiri*) within the "Zion-Refuge." It signifies "The Moral Obligation of the Sanctuary."

2 Throne established in Love (we-hu-khan ba-Che-sed Kis-se): The scrolls highlight the "Constitutional Root of the Kingdom." The "Throne" (*Kisse*) is "Rooted" in "Covenant-Love" (*Chesed*). It signify "The Davidic Ideal of Justice."

3 Great is her Arrogance (ge-eh me-od): The scrolls highlight the "Inflationary Root of Folly." Moab is "Uprooted" by "Arrogance" (*Ge-eh*). It signifies "The Spiritual Weight of Conceit."

4 Laments like a Harp (me-ay ke-khin-nor ye-he-mu): Literally "My bowels shall sound like a harp." The scrolls highlight the "Resonant Root of Sympathy." The "Prophet" (*Isaiah*) "Roots" his "Inner Being" in the "Frequency of Mourning." It signifies "Prophetic Empathy."

5 Within Three Years (be-sha-losh sha-nim): The scrolls conclude with this "Chronometric Root." The "Splendor" (*Kabod*) is "Uprooted" on a "Specific Timeline." It marks the transition to "The Burden of Damascus" in *Isaiah 17*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Faithfull Throne." Justice is coming.
