Isaiah 43

The Savior through the Waters.

Summoned by Name

“Do not fear, for I have redeemed you; I have summoned you by name; you are mine.” 1

Through the Waters

“When you pass through the waters, I will be with you.” 2
“When you walk through the fire, you will not be burned.”

My Witnesses

“‘You are my witnesses,’ declares the Lord.” 3
“Apart from me there is no savior.”

A New Thing

“See, I am doing a new thing! Now it springs up; do you not perceive it?” 4

Blotting Out Sins

“I, even I, am he who blots out your transgressions, for my own sake.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Summoned by Name (qā-rā-thī bhe-shim-khā): In the scrolls, this is the "Personal Root of Ownership." God "Roots" the "Redemption" (*Ga-al*) in the "Specific Identity" (*Name*) of the individual. It signifies "The Divine Claim."

2 Through the Waters (ki-tha-a-bhōr bam-Ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Atmospheric Root of Presence." The "Crisis" (*Waters/Fire*) is not "Uprooted," but the "Sustenance" (*I am with you*) is "Rooted" within it. It signify "The Unsinkable Faith."

3 My Witnesses (ē-dhāy): The scrolls highlight the "Evidentiary Root of Chosenness." Israel is "Rooted" as the "Proof" (*Witness*) of God’s "Monotheism." It signifies "The Responsibility of History."

4 Now it Springs Up (at-tāh tith-māch): The scrolls highlight the "Biological Root of Innovation." The "New Thing" is "Rooted" in "Present Emergence" (*At-tah*). It signify "The Perpetual Creativity of God."

5 Blots out Transgressions (mō-cheh phe-shā-e-khā): The scrolls conclude with this "Erasure Root." "Sin" is "Uprooted" (*Wiped/Blotted*) for the sake of the "Divine Reputation" (*My own sake*). It marks the transition to "The Folly of Idols" in *Isaiah 44*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Forgiven New Thing." Perceive it.
