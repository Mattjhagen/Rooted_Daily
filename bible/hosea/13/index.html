Hosea 13

The Kiss of the Calf and the Power of the Grave.

When Ephraim Spoke

“When Ephraim spoke, people trembled; but he became guilty of Baal worship and died.” 1

Kiss the Calf

“They say of these people, ‘They kiss calf-idols!’” 2

Lurking Like a Leopard

“Like a leopard I will lurk by the path; I will attack them like a bear.” 3

Where, O Death?

“I will redeem them from death. Where, O death, are your plagues?” 4
“Where, O grave, is your destruction?”
“The east wind from the Lord will come.” 5

Translation Notes

1 When Ephraim Spoke (ke-dab-bēr eph-ra-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Authoritative Root of the Word." "Relevance" is "Uprooted." Ephraim’s "Speech" (*Dabhar*) is "Rooted" in "Baal" and yields "Death" (*Muth*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Lost Power."

2 Kiss Calf-Idols (a-ghā-līm yish-shā-qūn): The scrolls highlight the "Affectionate Root of Idolatry." "Sacred Love" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Devotion" in "Calves" (*Aghalim*). It signify "The Degradation of the Kiss."

3 Lurk like a Leopard (ke-nā-mēr al-de-rekh ā-shūr): The scrolls highlight the "Predatory Root of Judgment." "Patience" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Observation" in the "Leopard’s Strike" (*Namer*). It signifies "The Stealth of Divine Justice."

4 Where, O Death (e-hī dhe-bhā-re-khā mā-weth): The scrolls highlight the "Challenging Root of the Resurrection." "Mortality" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Redemption" (*Padhah*) in the "Defeat" of the "Grave" (*Sheol*). It signify "The Ultimate Triumph."

5 East Wind (rū-ach qā-dhīm): The scrolls conclude with this "Withering Root of the End." "Moisture" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Hosea’s Final Plea" in *Hosea 14*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Victory over Death." The wind blows where it will.
