Hosea 12

The Struggle of Jacob and the Merchant’s Deceit.

He Struggled with God

“In the womb he grasped his brother’s heel; as a man he struggled with God.” 1

Found Him at Bethel

“He struggled with the angel and overcame him; he wept and begged for his favor.” 2

Dishonest Scales

“The merchant uses dishonest scales and loves to defraud.” 3

Live in Tents Again

“I will make you live in tents again, as in the days of your appointed festivals.” 4
“I spoke to the prophets and gave them many visions.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Struggled with God (śā-rāh eth-E-lō-hīm): The scrolls highlight the "Antagonistic Root of Jacob." "Passivity" is "Uprooted." Jacob "Roots" his "Identity" in the "Struggle" (*Sarah*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Sacred Ambition."

2 Wept and Begged (bā-khā way-yith-chan-nen-lō): The scrolls highlight the "Vulnerable Root of Victory." "Stoicism" is "Uprooted." Jacob "Roots" his "Overcoming" in "Tears" (*Bakhah*). It signify "The Strength found in Entreaty."

3 Dishonest Scales (mō-ze-nē mir-māh): The scrolls highlight the "Commercial Root of Sin." "Equity" is "Uprooted." The "Merchant" (*Kenaan*) is "Rooted" in "Deceit" (*Mirmah*). It signifies "The Spiritual Dimension of Economics."

4 Live in Tents (ō-shībh-khā bā-o-hā-līm): The scrolls highlight the "Nomadic Root of Remembrance." "Urban Pride" is "Uprooted." Israel is "Re-Rooted" in "Tents" (*Ohalim*). It signify "The Return to the Simplicity of the Desert."

5 Gave many Visions (me-shāl-tī): Literally "I told parables." The scrolls conclude with this "Illustrative Root of Prophecy." "Literalism" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Lord’s Anger" in *Hosea 13*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Struggle." God still speaks.
