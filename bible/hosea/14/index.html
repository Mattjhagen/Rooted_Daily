Hosea 14

The Healing of Waywardness and the Roots of Lebanon.

Return, O Israel

“Return, Israel, to the Lord your God. Your sins have been your downfall!” 1

Take Words With You

“Forgive all our sins and receive us graciously.” 2

The Rooted Cedar

“I will be like the dew to Israel; he will blossom like a lily.” 3
“Like a cedar of Lebanon he will send out his roots.”

Who is Wise?

“The ways of the Lord are right; the righteous walk in them.” 4
“But the rebellious stumble in them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Return (shū-bhā): The scrolls highlight the "Circular Root of Restoration." "Downfall" (*Kashal*) is "Uprooted." Israel is commanded to "Root" their "Direction" back to the "Lord" (*Adonay*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Home-Coming."

2 Take Words (qe-chū dī-bhā-rīm): The scrolls highlight the "Verbal Root of Repentance." "Silence/Shame" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Appeal" in "Speech" (*Dabhar*) rather than "Sacrifice." It signify "The Sincerity of the Confession."

3 Send out his Roots (way-yak sho-rā-shāw): The scrolls highlight the "Organic Root of Stability." "Transience" is "Uprooted." Israel is "Rooted" like the "Cedar" (*Erez*). It signifies "The Permanent Restoration of the People."

4 Ways of the Lord (dar-khē YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Linear Root of Truth." "Crooked Paths" are "Uprooted." The "Righteous" (*Tzaddiqim*) are "Rooted" in "Walking" (*Halak*) with God. It signify "The Practicality of Wisdom."

5 Stumble in them (yik-kā-she-lū): The scrolls conclude with this "Ironic Root of the Law." "Security" is "Uprooted" for the "Rebellious" (*Posheim*). It marks the end of *Hosea* and the transition to *Joel 1*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Wisdom." Walk in his ways.
