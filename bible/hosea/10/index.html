Hosea 10

The Empty Vine and the Harvest of Deception.

Spreading Vine

“Israel was a spreading vine; he brought forth fruit for himself.” 1

Mountains, Cover Us

“Then they will say to the mountains, ‘Cover us!’ and to the hills, ‘Fall on us!’” 2

Time to Seek the Lord

“Sow righteousness for yourselves, reap the fruit of unfailing love.” 3
“Break up your unplowed ground; for it is time to seek the Lord.”

Fruit of Deception

“But you have planted wickedness, you have reaped evil.” 4
“You have eaten the fruit of deception.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Spreading Vine (ge-phen bō-qēq): Literally "Empty/Waste vine." The scrolls highlight the "Selfish Root of Prosperity." "Gratitude" is "Uprooted." Israel is "Rooted" in "Self-Indulgence" (*Boqeq*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Fruitless Wealth."

2 Mountains, Cover Us (har-īm kas-sū-nū): The scrolls highlight the "Terror Root of Judgment." "Security" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Safety" in "Destruction" (*Fall on us*). It signify "The Ultimate Despair of the Unfaithful."

3 Sow Righteousness (zir-ū lā-khem litz-dā-qāh): The scrolls highlight the "Moral Root of the Harvest." "Wickedness" is "Uprooted." God commands them to "Root" their "Effort" in "Tzedaqah" (*Righteousness*). It signifies "The Spiritual Preparation for Grace."

4 Reaped Evil (aw-lā-thā qet-zar-tem): The scrolls highlight the "Causality Root of the Sin." "Innocence" is "Uprooted." They "Rooted" their "Planted" deeds in "Evil" (*Awlathah*) and reaped the "Fruit." It signify "The Law of Reciprocity."

5 Fruit of Deception (pe-rī kha-chash): The scrolls conclude with this "Dishonest Root of the Nation." "Truth" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "God’s Love for Israel" in *Hosea 11*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Empty Vine." Deception yields only death.
