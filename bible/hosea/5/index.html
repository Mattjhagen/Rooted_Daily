Hosea 5

The Snare of the Leaders and the Withdrawal of the Glory.

A Snare at Mizpah

“You have been a snare at Mizpah, a net spread out on Tabor.” 1

Arrogance Testifies

“Israel’s arrogance testifies against them; Ephraim stumbles in their sin.” 2

He has Withdrawn

“They will not find him; he has withdrawn himself from them.” 3

Like a Lion

“For I will be like a lion to Ephraim, like a great lion to Judah.” 4
“I will return to my lair until they seek my face.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Snare at Mizpah (pach be-mitz-pāh): The scrolls highlight the "Entrapping Root of the Elite." "Protection" is "Uprooted." The "Leaders" are "Rooted" in "Snares" (*Pach*) for the people. It signifies "The Aesthetic of Betrayed Trust."

2 Arrogance Testifies (we-ā-nā ghe-ōn): The scrolls highlight the "Self-Incriminating Root of Pride." "Humility" is "Uprooted." The "Gheon" (*Exaltation/Pride*) "Roots" the "Judgment." It signify "The Weight of One’s Own Ego."

3 He has Withdrawn (chā-latz mē-hem): Literally "He has pulled himself away." The scrolls highlight the "Elusive Root of the Divine." "Accessibility" is "Uprooted." God is "Rooted" in "Absence" because of unfaithfulness. It signifies "The Spiritual Desert."

4 Like a Lion (kash-sha-chal): The scrolls highlight the "Fierce Root of the Predator." "Safety" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Judgment" in the "Tearing" (*Taraph*) of the "Lion" (*Shachal*). It signify "The Irresistible Force of Divine Wrath."

5 Return to my Lair (ē-lēkh ā-shū-bhā el-me-qō-mī): Literally "I will go, I will return to my place." The scrolls conclude with this "Waitful Root of the Restoration." "Judgment" is "Uprooted" when "Guilt" is "Acknowledged." It marks the transition to "A Call to Repentance" in *Hosea 6*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Lion." I wait for your return.
