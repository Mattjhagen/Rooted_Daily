Hosea 8

The Sowing of the Wind and the Reaping of the Whirlwind.

Trumpet to Your Lips

“An eagle is over the house of the Lord because they have broken my covenant.” 1

The Calf of Samaria

“Throw out your calf-idol, Samaria! It will be broken in pieces.” 2

Sowing the Wind

“They sow the wind and reap the whirlwind.” 3
“The stalk has no head; it will produce no flour.”

Forgotten Maker

“Israel has forgotten their Maker and built palaces.” 4
“But I will send fire on their cities.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Trumpet (shō-phār): The scrolls highlight the "Alarming Root of Violation." "Silence" is "Uprooted." The "Shophar" (*Trumpet*) is "Rooted" in the "Warning" against the "Eagle" (*Nesher*) of judgment. It signifies "The Aesthetic of Impending Disaster."

2 Calf-Idol (ig-lēkh): The scrolls highlight the "Artificial Root of Worship." "Nature" is "Uprooted" and replaced by "Crafted Metal." The "Calf" is "Rooted" in "Destruction" (*Shebharim*). It signify "The Fragility of the Handmade God."

3 Sow the Wind (rū-ach yiz-rā-ū): The scrolls highlight the "Empty Root of Effort." "Fruitfulness" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Labor" in "Wind" (*Ruach*) and reap "Storm" (*Suphah*). It signifies "The Exponential Growth of Folly."

4 Forgotten their Maker (way-yish-kach Yis-rā-ēl eth-ō-śē-hū): The scrolls highlight the "Amnesic Root of the Decline." "Origins" are "Uprooted." Israel has "Rooted" her "Security" in "Palaces" (*Hekhalim*) rather than the "Maker" (*Oseh*). It signify "The Illusion of Static Power."

5 Send Fire (we-shil-lach-tī ēsh): The scrolls conclude with this "Consuming Root of the End." "Structural Pride" is "Uprooted" by "Fire." It marks the transition to "Punishment for Israel" in *Hosea 9*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Whirlwind." The fire is coming.
