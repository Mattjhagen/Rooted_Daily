Hosea 6

The Third Day of Revival and the Mist of Unfaithfulness.

Come, Let Us Return

“He has torn us to pieces but he will heal us; he has injured us but he will bind up our wounds.” 1

On the Third Day

“On the third day he will restore us, that we may live in his presence.” 2

Morning Mist

“Your love is like the morning mist, like the early dew that disappears.” 3

Mercy, Not Sacrifice

“For I desire mercy, not sacrifice, and acknowledgment of God rather than burnt offerings.” 4
“As at Adam, they have broken the covenant.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Torn us to pieces (tā-raph): The scrolls highlight the "Surgical Root of the Divine." "Casualty" is "Uprooted." The "Tearing" (*Taraph*) is "Rooted" in the "Healing" (*Rapha*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Redemptive Suffering."

2 On the Third Day (bay-yōm hash-she-lī-shī): The scrolls highlight the "Resurrection Root of Restoration." "Death/Delay" is "Uprooted." Israel is "Rooted" in "Presence" (*Panim*) on the "Third Day." It signify "The Prophetic Pattern of New Life."

3 Morning Mist (ka-a-nan bō-qer): The scrolls highlight the "Ephemeral Root of Loyalty." "Substance" is "Uprooted." Their "Chesed" (*Love/Loyalty*) is "Rooted" in "Vapor" (*Anan*). It signifies "The Spiritual Fragility of the People."

4 Desire Mercy (che-sed chā-pha-tzet-tī): The scrolls highlight the "Priority Root of the Heart." "Ritual" is "Uprooted" as the primary end. God "Roots" his "Desire" in "Loyal Love" (*Chesed*). It signify "The Superiority of Relationship over Religion."

5 As at Adam (ke-ā-dhām): The scrolls conclude with this "Original Root of Transgression." "Modern Failure" is "Uprooted" and linked to the "Beginning." It marks the transition to "Israel’s Social Decay" in *Hosea 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Third Day." Return and be healed.
