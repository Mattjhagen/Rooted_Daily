Hosea 4

The Lawsuit of the Lord and the Famine of Knowledge.

The Charge Against the Land

“There is no faithfulness, no love, no acknowledgment of God in the land.” 1

Lack of Knowledge

“My people are destroyed from lack of knowledge.” 2

Like People, Like Priest

“And it will be: Like people, like priest.” 3

Spirit of Prostitution

“A spirit of prostitution leads them astray; they are unfaithful to their God.” 4
“Ephraim is joined to idols; leave him alone!” 5

Translation Notes

1 No Acknowledgment (wē-ēn da-at E-lō-hīm): Literally "No knowledge of God." The scrolls highlight the "Epistemic Root of the Crisis." "Awareness" is "Uprooted." The "Land" (*Eretz*) is "Rooted" in "Ignorance." It signifies "The Aesthetic of the Void."

2 Lack of Knowledge (mib-be-lī had-dā-at): The scrolls highlight the "Fatal Root of Ignorance." "Survival" is "Uprooted." The "People" (*Am*) are "Rooted" in "Destruction" (*Damah*) because they rejected "Knowledge" (*Daat*). It signify "The Connection between Intelligence and Covenant."

3 Like People, Like Priest (ke-ām kak-kō-hēn): The scrolls highlight the "Social Root of Corruption." "Leadership Distinction" is "Uprooted." Both are "Rooted" in the "Same Decay." It signifies "The Leveling Effect of Sin."

4 Spirit of Prostitution (rū-ach ze-nū-nīm): The scrolls highlight the "Compulsive Root of Infidelity." "Rational Choice" is "Uprooted." A "Spirit" (*Ruach*) of "Whoring" is "Rooted" in the "National Psyche." It signify "The Obsession with Idols."

5 Joined to Idols (cha-bhūr a-tzab-bīm): Literally "Enchanted/Bound to idols." The scrolls conclude with this "Addictive Root of Ephraim." "Freedom" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Judgment on the Leaders" in *Hosea 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Charge." Knowledge is the only hope.
