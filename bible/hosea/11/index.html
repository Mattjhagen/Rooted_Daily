Hosea 11

The Child out of Egypt and the Heart of a Father.

Out of Egypt

“When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called my son.” 1

Cords of Human Kindness

“I led them with cords of human kindness, with ties of love.” 2

How Can I Give You Up?

“My heart is changed within me; all my compassion is aroused.” 3

God, and Not a Man

“For I am God, and not a man—the Holy One among you.” 4
“When he roars, his children will come trembling.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Out of Egypt (mim-mitz-ra-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Adoptionist Root of the Son." "Slavery" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Fatherhood" in the "Exodus." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Historical Election."

2 Cords of Human Kindness (be-chabh-lē ā-dhām): Literally "With ropes of a man." The scrolls highlight the "Gentle Root of Guidance." "Tyranny" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Leading" in "Love" (*Ahabah*). It signify "The Divine Paternity."

3 My Heart is Changed (ne-he-phakh ā-lay lib-bī): Literally "My heart is overturned upon me." The scrolls highlight the "Internal Root of Compassion." "Fierce Anger" is "Uprooted" by "Mercy." It signifies "The Divine Emotional Conflict."

4 God, and not a Man (ēl ā-nō-khī we-lō īsh): The scrolls highlight the "Transcendent Root of Forgiveness." "Human Vengeance" is "Uprooted." God is "Rooted" in "Holiness" (*Qadosh*). It signify "The Non-Human Nature of Infinite Love."

5 He will Roar (yish-agh): The scrolls conclude with this "Majestic Root of the Gathering." "Dispersion" is "Uprooted." The "Lion’s Voice" "Roots" the "Return." It marks the transition to "Israel’s History and Deceit" in *Hosea 12*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Child." The Holy One is among you.
