Hosea 2

The Wilderness of Allurement and the Door of Hope.

Me She Forgot

“She went after her lovers, but me she forgot, declares the Lord.” 1

Allure Her

“Therefore I am now going to allure her; I will lead her into the wilderness and speak tenderly to her.” 2

Valley of Achor

“I will make the Valley of Achor a door of hope.” 3

Betrothed Forever

“I will betroth you to me forever; in righteousness and justice, in love and compassion.” 4
“You are my people; You are my God.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Me she Forgot (we-o-thī shā-khe-chā): The scrolls highlight the "Amnesic Root of Sin." "Gratefulness" is "Uprooted." Israel has "Rooted" her "Trust" in "Baal" (the masters) rather than the "Lord." It signifies "The Spiritual Erasure of the Creator."

2 Allure her (me-phat-te-hā): The scrolls highlight the "Seductive Root of Grace." "Judgment" is "Uprooted" by "Tenderness." God "Roots" his "Communication" in the "Heart" (*Libbah*). It signify "The Aesthetic of Divine Persuasion."

3 Valley of Achor (ē-meq ā-khōr): Literally "Valley of Trouble." The scrolls highlight the "Redemptive Root of Place." "Trouble" is "Uprooted." The "Valley" (*Emeq*) is "Rooted" as a "Door" (*Petach*) of "Hope" (*Tiqvah*). It signifies "The Transformation of the Scar."

4 Betroth (we-ē-raś-tīkh): The scrolls highlight the "Marital Root of the New Covenant." "Divorce" is "Uprooted." The "Relationship" is "Rooted" in "Chesed" (*Love*). It signify "The Finality of the Divine Union."

5 You are my God (E-lō-hay): The scrolls conclude with this "Reciprocal Root of Identity." "Alienation" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Hosea’s Redemption of Gomer" in *Hosea 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Door of Hope." I will answer the heavens.
