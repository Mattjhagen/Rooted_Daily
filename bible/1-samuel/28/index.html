1 Samuel 28

5When Saul saw the Philistine army, he was afraid; terror filled his heart.
6He inquired of the LORD, but the LORD did not answer him by dreams or Urim or prophets. 1
7Saul then said to his attendants, “Find me a woman who is a medium, so I may go and inquire of her.” “There is one in Endor,” they said. 2
8So Saul disguised himself, putting on other clothes, and at night he and two men went to the woman. “Consult a spirit for me,” he said, “and bring up for me the one I name.”
12When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out at the top of her voice and said to Saul, “Why have you deceived me? You are Saul!” 3
15Samuel said to Saul, “Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?” “I am in great distress,” Saul said. “The Philistines are fighting against me, and God has departed from me.”
Samuel said, “Why do you consult me, now that the LORD has departed from you and become your enemy?”
“Because you did not obey the LORD... he has done this to you today.”
“The LORD will deliver both Israel and you into the hands of the Philistines, and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me.” 4
20Immediately Saul fell full length on the ground, terrified because of Samuel’s words. His strength was gone, for he had eaten nothing all that day and all that night. 5
25...Then they rose and left that same night.

Translation Notes

1 Silence of God: This is one of the most chilling verses in the scrolls. Saul was "Covenantally Isolated." Having killed the priests (Chapter 22) and ignored the prophets (Chapter 15), he found the heavens were "brass." God’s silence was not an absence of power, but a final "Judicial Withdrawal" from a king who had repeatedly chosen his own way.

2 Medium at Endor (Ba-alat Ob): Literally "Mistress of a Ghost-Talent." Saul’s request in the scrolls is a total "Spiritual Apostasy." He was seeking from the "Demonic World" what he had been denied by the "Divine World." It shows the desperation of a man who knows he is lost but cannot repent.

3 The Woman’s Terror: In the scrolls, the woman was genuinely shocked when Samuel appeared. Usually, mediums used trickery or dealt with "Familiar Spirits" (*Obot*). The fact that she screamed and realized Saul’s identity suggests that God actually allowed the real spirit of Samuel to appear to deliver this final message. This was a "Sovereign Override" of her occult ritual.

4 With Me: This phrase in the scrolls refers to *Sheol*—the realm of the dead. Samuel was telling Saul that within 24 hours, the first king of Israel and his heirs (including the innocent Jonathan) would be dead. It is the final "Amen" to the judgment pronounced in Chapter 15.

5 Fell Full Length: In the scrolls, this is the "Anti-Anointing." In Chapter 10, Saul was "Head Taller" than anyone else. Now, he is horizontal on the ground of a witch’s house. He had been "emptied" of everything—strength, spirit, and dignity. His final meal, provided by a medium, is a "Perversion of the Passover"—a meal eaten before a journey into death.
