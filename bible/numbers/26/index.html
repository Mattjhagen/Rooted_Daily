Numbers 26

1After the plague the LORD said to Moses and Eleazar son of Aaron, the priest,
2“Take a census of the whole Israelite community by families—all those twenty years old or more who are able to serve in the army of Israel.”
3So on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho, Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke with them and said,
4“Take a census of the men twenty years old or more, as the LORD commanded Moses.” These were the Israelites who came out of Egypt:

The Census Results: The scrolls meticulously record the numbers for each tribe. Here are the totals for the new generation:

  • Reuben: 43,730
  • Simeon: 22,200
  • Gad: 40,500
  • Judah: 76,500
  • Issachar: 64,300
  • Zebulun: 60,500
  • Manasseh: 52,700
  • Ephraim: 32,500
  • Benjamin: 45,600
  • Dan: 64,400
  • Asher: 53,400
  • Naphtali: 45,400
51The total number of Israelite men was 601,730.
52The LORD said to Moses,
53“The land is to be allotted to them as an inheritance based on the number of names.”
54“To a larger group give a larger inheritance, and to a smaller group a smaller one; each is to receive its inheritance according to the number of those listed.” 1
55“Be sure that the land is distributed by lot. What each group inherits will be according to the names for its ancestral tribe.”
56“Each inheritance is to be distributed by lot among the larger and smaller groups.”
57These were the Levites who were counted by their clans: through Gershon, the Gershonite clan; through Kohath, the Kohathite clan; through Merari, the Merarite clan.
58These also were Levite clans... (The names of Libni, Hebron, Mahli, Mushi, and Korah are listed).
59The name of Amram’s wife was Jochebed, a descendant of Levi, who was born to the Levites in Egypt. To Amram she bore Aaron, Moses and their sister Miriam.
60Aaron was the father of Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.
61But Nadab and Abihu died when they made an offering before the LORD with unauthorized fire.
62The total number of Levite males a month old or more was 23,000. They were not counted along with the other Israelites because they received no inheritance among them.
63These are the ones counted by Moses and Eleazar the priest when they counted the Israelites on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho.
64Not one of them was among those counted by Moses and Aaron the priest when they counted the Israelites in the Desert of Sinai. 2
65For the LORD had told those Israelites they would surely die in the wilderness, and not one of them was left except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

Translation Notes

1 Land Allotment: The scrolls record a dual system of land distribution. The *location* was determined by "Lot" (divine guidance), but the *size* of the territory was determined by the census (human need). It is a beautiful synthesis: God decides where we are, but He expects us to manage resources based on the reality of the community.

2 Not One was Left: This verse in the original scrolls is the somber fulfillment of the judgment in Numbers 14. An entire generation had passed away. The census was not just a counting of people; it was a counting of "survivors" and "replacements." It serves as a reminder that God’s Word is certain—both in its discipline and in its promises.
