Numbers 33

1Here are the stages in the journey of the Israelites when they came out of Egypt by divisions under the leadership of Moses and Aaron. 1
2At the LORD’s command Moses recorded the stages in their journey. This is their journey by stages:

The Forty-Two Stages: The scrolls list 42 distinct stopping points. Key locations include:

  • Rameses to Sinai: Succoth, Etham, Pi Hahiroth, Marah (bitter water), Elim (12 springs, 70 palms), Rephidim (no water), and the Desert of Sinai.
  • Sinai to Kadesh: Kibroth Hattaavah (graves of craving), Hazeroth, and Rithmah.
  • The Wandering: Rimmon Perez, Libnah, Rissah, Kehelathah, Mount Shepher, Haradah, Makheloth, Tahath, Terah, Mithkah, Hashmonah, Moseroth, Bene Jaakan, Hor Haggidgad, Jotbathah, Abronah, Ezion Geber.
  • The Final Approach: Kadesh (Desert of Zin), Mount Hor (where Aaron died), Zalmonah, Punon, Oboth, Iye Abarim, Dibon Gad, Almon Diblathaim, the Abarim range, and the Plains of Moab.
38At the LORD’s command Aaron the priest went up Mount Hor, where he died on the first day of the fifth month of the fortieth year after the Israelites came out of Egypt.
39Aaron was a hundred and twenty-three years old when he died on Mount Hor.
40The Canaanite king of Arad, who lived in the Negev of Canaan, heard that the Israelites were coming.
48They left the mountains of Abarim and camped on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho.
49There on the plains of Moab they camped along the Jordan from Beth Jeshimoth to Abel Shittim.
50On the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho the LORD said to Moses,
51“Speak to the Israelites and say to them: ‘When you cross the Jordan into Canaan,”
52“‘drive out all the inhabitants of the land before you. Destroy all their carved images and their cast idols, and demolish all their high places.” 2
53“‘Take possession of the land and settle in it, for I have given you the land to possess.”
54“‘Distribute the land by lot, according to your clans. To a larger group give a larger inheritance, and to a smaller group a smaller one. Whatever falls to them by lot will be theirs. Distribute it according to your ancestral tribes.”
55“‘But if you do not drive out the inhabitants of the land, those you allow to remain will become barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides. They will give you trouble in the land where you will live.” 3
56“‘And then I will do to you what I plan to do to them.’”

Translation Notes

1 Stages (Massa'ei): The word in the original scrolls is *Massa'ei*, which comes from a root meaning "to pull up stakes." It emphasizes the temporary nature of the wilderness. Jewish mystics often point out that there are 42 stages, just as there are 42 letters in one of the hidden names of God. It suggests that the journey was not just geographical, but a spiritual "spelling out" of the nation's identity.

2 Demolish their High Places: In the scrolls, the "High Places" (*Bamot*) were local shrines where the Canaanites worshipped their gods. God was not just commanding a military conquest, but a cultural and spiritual "cleansing." The Israelites were to ensure that there was no infrastructure of idolatry left to tempt them.

3 Thorns in your sides: This is a vivid metaphor in the original scrolls. God warns that a "partial" obedience will lead to "permanent" irritation. If the toxic influences are allowed to remain, they will inevitably infect the community. This prophecy is sadly fulfilled in the book of Judges and later history.
