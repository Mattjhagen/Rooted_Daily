Numbers 29

1“‘On the first day of the seventh month hold a sacred assembly and do no regular work. It is a day for you to sound the trumpets.” 1
2“‘As a pleasing aroma to the LORD, offer a burnt offering of one young bull, one ram and seven male lambs a year old, all without defect.”
3“‘With the bull prepare a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of the finest flour mixed with oil; with the ram, two-tenths;”
4“‘and with each of the seven lambs, one-tenth.”
5“‘Include one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for you.”
6“‘These are in addition to the monthly and daily burnt offerings with their grain offerings and drink offerings, as a pleasing aroma, a food offering presented to the LORD.”
7“‘On the tenth day of this seventh month hold a sacred assembly. You must deny yourselves and do no work.” 2
8“‘Present as a pleasing aroma to the LORD a burnt offering of one young bull, one ram and seven male lambs a year old, all without defect.”
9“‘With the bull prepare a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of the finest flour mixed with oil; with the ram, two-tenths;”
10“‘and with each of the seven lambs, one-tenth.”
11“‘Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the sin offering for atonement and the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and their drink offerings.”
12“‘On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, hold a sacred assembly and do no regular work. Celebrate a festival to the LORD for seven days.”
13“‘Present as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD, a burnt offering of thirteen young bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old, all without defect.” 3
14“‘With each of the thirteen bulls prepare a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of the finest flour mixed with oil; with each of the two rams, two-tenths;”
15“‘and with each of the fourteen lambs, one-tenth.”
16“‘Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.”

The Seven Days of Tabernacles (Sukkot): Verses 17-34 detail the decreasing number of bulls offered each day: 12 on the second day, 11 on the third, and so on, until 7 bulls are offered on the seventh day. Each day also included 2 rams, 14 lambs, and 1 male goat.

35“‘On the eighth day hold a closing assembly and do no regular work.” 4
36“‘Present as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD, a burnt offering of one bull, one ram and seven male lambs a year old, all without defect.”
37“‘With the bull, the ram and the lambs, prepare their grain offerings and drink offerings according to the number specified.”
38“‘Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.”
39“‘In addition to what you vow and your freewill offerings, offer these to the LORD at your appointed festivals: your burnt offerings, grain offerings, drink offerings and fellowship offerings.’”
40Moses told the Israelites all that the LORD commanded him.

Translation Notes

1 Seventh Month (Tishrei): In the scrolls, the seventh month is the most sacred of the year. Just as the seventh day is the Sabbath of the week, the seventh month is the "Sabbath of the Months." It contains the three major fall festivals: Trumpets, Atonement, and Tabernacles.

2 Deny yourselves: This refers to *Yom Kippur* (Day of Atonement). In the original scrolls, this is the only day of mandatory fasting. It is a day of total focus on repentance and the restoration of the relationship between God and His people.

3 Seventy Bulls: During the seven days of *Sukkot* (Tabernacles), a total of 70 bulls were offered (13+12+11+10+9+8+7). According to ancient Jewish tradition in the scrolls, these 70 bulls represented the 70 nations of the world. Israel was acting as a "Priest for the Nations," offering sacrifices for the well-being of the whole world, not just themselves.

4 The Eighth Day (Shemini Atzeret): After the seven days of celebrating for the world, the eighth day was an intimate time for Israel and God alone. Only one bull was offered on this day. It symbolizes the eternal connection that remains after the temporary "booths" (*Sukkot*) of the world are gone.
