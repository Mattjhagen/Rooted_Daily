Song of Solomon 6

The Dawn and the Unique Dove.

Gathering Lilies

“My beloved has gone down to his garden... to gather lilies.”
“I am my beloved’s and my beloved is mine; he browses among the lilies.” 1

Beautiful as Tirzah

“You are as beautiful as Tirzah, my darling, as lovely as Jerusalem.” 2
“As majestic as troops with banners.”

My Perfect One is Unique

“My dove, my perfect one, is unique.” 3

Appearing Like the Dawn

“Who is this that appears like the dawn, as fair as the moon, as bright as the sun, as majestic as the stars?” 4
“Come back, come back, O Shulammite... that we may gaze on you!” 5

Translation Notes

1 Gather Lilies (li-lo-qot Sho-shan-nim): In the scrolls, this is the "Selection Root of Affection." The "Beloved" (*Dodi*) "Roots" his activity in "Beauty" (*Shoshannim*). It signifies "The Purposeful Pursuit of the Best."

2 Beautiful as Tirzah (Ya-phah... ke-Thir-tzah): The scrolls highlight the "Civic Root of Aesthetic." Tirzah (*Pleasantness*) and Jerusalem (*Foundation of Peace*) are "Rooted" in "Urban Splendor." To be "Majestic" (*A-yum-mah*) as "Banners" (*Nid-ga-loth*) signifies "The Power of Visual Impact."

3 Perfect one is Unique (A-chath hi Yo-na-thi Tham-ma-thi): Literally "One is my dove, my undefiled." The scrolls highlight the "Singular Root of Devotion." Among many (*Queens/Queens*), she is "Rooted" as the "Sole Object" of love. It signify "The Integrity of Monogamy."

4 Appears like the Dawn (han-Nish-qa-phah ke-mo-Sha-char): Literally "Who is she looking forth like the morning?" The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of Presence." She "Roots" her "Appearance" in the "Celestial" (*Sun/Moon*). It signifies "The Divinity of Natural Beauty."

5 O Shulammite (hash-Shu-lam-mith): The scrolls conclude with this "Identity Root." This is the first time the title "Shulammite" (*Peaceful/Perfected*) is used. It marks the transition to the "Dance" in *Song of Solomon 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Radiant Dove." Return and shine.
