Song of Solomon 1

Solomon’s Song of Songs.

Better than Wine

“Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth—for your love is more delightful than wine.” 1
“Pleasing is the fragrance of your perfumes; your name is like perfume poured out.” 2

Dark but Lovely

“Dark am I, yet lovely, daughters of Jerusalem... like the curtains of Solomon.” 3

Where You Rest

“Tell me, you whom I love, where you graze your flock and where you rest your sheep at midday.” 4

Henna Blossoms

“My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms from the vineyards of En Gedi.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Kisses of his Mouth (yish-sha-qe-ni min-ne-shi-qoth Pi-hu): In the scrolls, this is the "Intimate Root." "Kisses" (*Neshiqoth*) "Root" the "Delight" (*Tobim*) in the "Direct Connection" (*Pihu*) rather than the "Proxy" of "Wine" (*Yayin*). It signifies "Unmediated Affection."

2 Name is like Perfume (She-men Tu-raq She-me-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Olfactory Root of Reputation." The "Name" (*Shem*) is "Rooted" in "Fragrance" (*Shemen*). When "Poured out" (*Turaq*), it "Roots" desire in others. It signify "The Scent of Character."

3 Dark but Lovely (She-cho-rah a-ni we-Na-wah): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Resilience." Her "Darkness" (*Shechorah*) is "Rooted" in "Toil" (*The Sun/Shemesh*), but her "Beauty" (*Nawah*) is "Inherent." It signifies "The Beauty of the Experienced Soul."

4 Where you Graze (e-khah Thir-eh): The scrolls highlight the "Locational Root of Belonging." The "Beloved" seeks to "Root" her "Presence" where the "Shepherd" (*Ro-eh*) "Rests" (*Tar-bitz*). It signifies "The Search for Spiritual Rest."

5 Henna Blossoms (Esh-kol hak-Ko-pher): Literally "A cluster of the price/ransom." The scrolls conclude with this "Botanical Root of Rarity." The "Beloved" is "Rooted" in the "Oasis" of "En Gedi." It marks the transition to the "Rose of Sharon" in *Song of Solomon 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Rare Delight." Find the oasis.
