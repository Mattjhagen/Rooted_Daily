Song of Solomon 7

The Palm Tree and the Countryside.

Sandaled Feet

“How beautiful your sandaled feet, O prince’s daughter! Your graceful legs are like jewels.” 1

Climbing the Palm

“Your stature is like that of the palm, and your breasts like clusters of fruit.”
“I said, ‘I will climb the palm tree; I will take hold of its fruit.’” 2

To the Fields

“Come, my beloved, let us go to the countryside, let us spend the night in the villages.” 3

I Will Give You My Love

“Let us go early to the vineyards... there I will give you my love.” 4
“The mandrakes send out their fragrance, and at our door is every delicacy.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Sandaled Feet (pa-a-ma-yi-kh ban-ne-a-lim): In the scrolls, this is the "Kinetic Root of Beauty." Her "Feet" (*Pa-a-ma-yikh*) are "Rooted" in "Nobility" (*Prince’s Daughter*). It signifies "The Dignity of the Path."

2 Climb the Palm Tree (e-e-leh be-Tha-mar): The scrolls highlight the "Aspirational Root of Intimacy." The "Palm" (*Thamar*) is "Rooted" in "Stature" (*Qo-ma-thekh*). To "Take hold" (*O-cha-zah*) of the "Fruit" (*San-sin-naw*) is to "Root" the "Desire" in "Physical Fulfillment." It signify "The Sacred Ascent."

3 To the Countryside (lek-hah do-di ne-tze hash-Sha-deh): The scrolls highlight the "Locational Root of Escape." Love seeks to "Root" itself away from the "System" (*City*) and into the "Wild/Field" (*Shadeh*). It signifies "The Privacy of the Natural World."

4 There I will give (sham et-ten eth-Do-day): The scrolls highlight the "Donative Root of Self." The "Vineyard" (*Ke-ra-mim*) is the "Sacred Space" where love is "Offered" (*Nathan*). It signify "The Gift of the Body."

5 Mandrakes (had-Du-da-im): The scrolls conclude with this "Biological Root of Fertility." "Mandrakes" (*Dudaim*) "Root" their "Fragrance" in the "Proximity" of the "Door" (*Pe-tha-chey-nu*). It marks the transition to "The Seal of Love" in *Song of Solomon 8*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Fragrant Delicacy." Enter the garden.
