Song of Solomon 3

The Night Search and the Royal Procession.

Searching in the Night

“All night long on my bed I looked for the one my heart loves.”
“I looked for him but did not find him.” 1

I Would Not Let Go

“I found the one my heart loves. I held him and would not let him go.” 2

Wilderness of Smoke

“Who is this coming up from the wilderness like a column of smoke, perfumed with myrrh and incense?” 3

Solomon’s Carriage

“King Solomon made for himself a carriage; he made it of wood from Lebanon.”
“Its interior was inlaid with love by the daughters of Jerusalem.” 4
“Look at King Solomon wearing the crown... on the day of his wedding.” 5

Translation Notes

1 I looked for him (biq-qash-tiw): In the scrolls, this is the "Nocturnal Search Root." The "Bed" (*Mishkab*) is the "Place of Absence" (*Uprooting*) until the "Search" (*Biqqashti*) "Roots" the soul in the "Presence" of the beloved. It signifies "The Pain of Separation."

2 Held him... not let him go (a-cha-ze-tiw we-lo ar-pen-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Tenacious Root of Possession." To "Hold" (*Achaz*) is to "Root" the "Finding" (*Matza*). To "Not let go" (*Lo-Arpennu*) is the "Uprooting of Fear." It signify "The Security of Union."

3 Column of Smoke (te-me-roth A-shan): The scrolls highlight the "Atmospheric Root of Presence." The "Procession" is "Rooted" in "Wilderness" (*Midbar*), yet "Perfumed" (*Me-qu-te-reth*). It signifies "The Transformation of the Desert."

4 Inlaid with Love (to-ko ra-tzup A-ha-bah): The scrolls highlight the "Interior Root of Value." The "Structure" is "Wood" (*Etzim*), but the "Core" (*Tok*) is "Rooted" in "Love" (*Ahabah*). It signify "The Soul of the Vessel."

5 Day of his Wedding (be-Yom cha-thun-na-tho): The scrolls conclude with this "Covenantal Root." The "Crown" (*Atarah*) "Roots" the "Authority" in "Joy" (*Simchath Libbo*). It marks the transition to the "Praise of Beauty" in *Song of Solomon 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Royal Wedding." Celebrate the union.
