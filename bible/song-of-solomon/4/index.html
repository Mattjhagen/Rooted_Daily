Song of Solomon 4

The Garden of the Sister-Bride.

How Beautiful You Are

“How beautiful you are, my darling! Oh, how beautiful! Your eyes behind your veil are doves.” 1
“Your lips are like a scarlet ribbon; your speech is lovely.”

The Garden Locked

“You are a garden locked up, my sister, my bride; you are a spring enclosed, a sealed fountain.” 2

An Orchard of Choice Fruits

“Your plants are an orchard of pomegranates with choice fruits, with henna and nard.” 3

Awake, North Wind!

“Awake, north wind, and come, south wind! Blow on my garden.”
“Let my beloved come into his garden and taste its choice fruits.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Behind your Veil (mib-ba-ath le-tzam-ma-thekh): In the scrolls, this is the "Modesty Root." The "Beauty" (*Yapheh*) is "Rooted" in a "Mystery" (*Behind the Veil*) that only the "Beloved" can "Uproot" (*Explore*). It signifies "The Privacy of Intimacy."

2 Garden Locked... Spring Enclosed (Gan na-ul... Ma-yan cha-tum): The scrolls highlight the "Exclusivity Root of the Soul." The "Sister-Bride" (*A-cho-thi Kal-lah*) is "Rooted" in "Covenantal Seclusion" (*Na-ul/Locked*). It signifies "The Sacred Boundary of the Heart."

3 Orchard of Pomegranates (Par-des Rim-mo-nim): The scrolls highlight the "Fruitful Root of Desire." The "Garden" (*Gan*) "Roots" "Choice Fruits" (*Me-ga-dim*). It signifies "The Productivity of a Cultivated Life."

4 Blow on my Garden (ha-phi-chi Gan-ni): The scrolls conclude with this "Inspiratory Root of Fragrance." The "Wind" (*Ruach*) "Roots" the "Scent" (*Bo-se-mo*) of the garden into the "Environment." It marks the transition to the "Beloved’s Arrival" in *Song of Solomon 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Inviting Spirit." Let him come.
