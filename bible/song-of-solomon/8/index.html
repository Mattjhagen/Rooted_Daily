Song of Solomon 8

Love Strong as Death.

Leaning on the Beloved

“Who is this coming up from the wilderness leaning on her beloved?” 1

Strong as Death

“Place me like a seal over your heart, like a seal on your arm; for love is as strong as death.” 2
“Its jealousy is unyielding as the grave; it burns like blazing fire.”

Many Waters

“Many waters cannot quench love; rivers cannot sweep it away.” 3
“If one were to give all the wealth of one’s house for love, it would be utterly scorned.” 4

Spice-Laden Mountains

“Come away, my beloved, and be like a gazelle or like a young stag on the spice-laden mountains.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Leaning on her Beloved (mit-rap-pe-qeth al-Do-dah): In the scrolls, this is the "Support Root." To "Lean" (*Mitrappeqeth*) is to "Root" one's "Security" in the "Other" (*Dodi*). It signifies "The Strength of Dependency."

2 Strong as Death (az-zah ham-Ma-weth A-ha-bah): The scrolls highlight the "Unconquerable Root of Love." "Love" (*Ahabah*) is "Rooted" in a "Force" (*Az*) that equals the "Great Uprooter" (*Death/Maweth*). It signify "The Immortality of Devotion."

3 Many Waters (Ma-yim rab-bim): The scrolls highlight the "Indestructible Root." "Love" cannot be "Extinguished" (*Ka-bah*) by "Quantity" (*Rab-bim*). It signifies "The Resilience of the Spirit."

4 Utterly Scorned (Boz ya-bu-zu lo): The scrolls highlight the "Inestimable Value Root." Love is "Uprooted" from the "Market" because it is "Priceless." It signify "The Scorn of Commercialism."

5 Spice-laden Mountains (ha-rey be-sa-mim): The scrolls conclude the entire Song of Songs with this "Aromatic Root of Departure." The "Gazelle" (*Tze-bi*) "Roots" the "Exit" in "Fragrance" (*Besamim*). It marks the end of the "Wisdom Literature" and the transition to the "Major Prophets" in *Isaiah*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unending Pursuit." Run together.
