Song of Solomon 5

The Knock and the Radiant Beloved.

My Heart Was Awake

“I slept but my heart was awake. Listen! My beloved is knocking.”
“I opened for my beloved, but my beloved had left; he was gone. My heart sank.” 1

Outstanding Among Ten Thousand

“My beloved is radiant and ruddy, outstanding among ten thousand.” 2
“His head is purest gold; his hair is wavy and black as a raven.”

Altogether Lovely

“His mouth is sweetness itself; he is altogether lovely.” 3

This is My Friend

“This is my beloved, this is my friend, daughters of Jerusalem.” 4
“Eat, friends, and drink; drink your fill of love.” 5

Translation Notes

1 My Heart sank (Nap-shi ya-tze-ah be-dab-be-ro): Literally "My soul went out when he spoke." In the scrolls, this is the "Existential Root of Longing." The "Heart" (*Leb*) is "Awake" (*Er*) even in "Sleep" (*Yishenah*). The disappearance "Uproots" her peace. It signifies "The Vulnerability of Openness."

2 Radiant and Ruddy (Tzach we-A-dom): The scrolls highlight the "Vitality Root of the Man." "Radiance" (*Tzach/White/Dazzling*) is "Rooted" in "Purity," while "Ruddy" (*Adom/Red*) is "Rooted" in "Health." It signify "A Balanced Strength."

3 Mouth is Sweetness (Chik-ko Mam-taq-qim): Literally "His palate is sweets." The scrolls highlight the "Conversational Root of Love." His "Speech" (*Dabar*) "Roots" "Sweetness" (*Mam-taq-qim*) in the hearer. It signifies "The Beauty of Character."

4 This is my Friend (zeh Re-i): The scrolls highlight the "Relational Root of Belonging." He is "Beloved" (*Dodi*) and also "Friend/Companion" (*Rei*). It signify "The Completeness of Union."

5 Drink your fill of Love (shik-ru Do-dim): The scrolls conclude with this "Inebriative Root." Love is "Rooted" in an "Abundance" (*Drink your fill*) that surpasses the "Vanity" of wine. It marks the transition to the "Garden of Lilies" in *Song of Solomon 6*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Divine Friendship." Drink deep.
