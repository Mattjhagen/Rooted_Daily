Obadiah 1

The Pride of the Clefts and the Kingdom of Zion.

Nest Among the Stars

“Though you soar like the eagle and make your nest among the stars, I will bring you down.” 1

Gloating Over a Brother

“You should not gloat over your brother in the day of his misfortune.” 2
“As you have done, it will be done to you.”

Deliverance on Mount Zion

“But on Mount Zion will be deliverance; it will be holy.” 3

The Kingdom is the Lord’s

“And the kingdom will be the Lord’s.” 4
“An envoy was sent to the nations.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Nest among the Stars (bēn kō-khā-bhīm): The scrolls highlight the "Aeronautical Root of Pride." "Humility" is "Uprooted." Edom’s "Security" is "Rooted" in the "Heights" (*Marom*) of the "Stars." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Celestial Arrogance."

2 Gloat over (al-tē-re): Literally "Do not look/see." The scrolls highlight the "Voyeuristic Root of Cruelty." "Brotherly Love" is "Uprooted." They "Root" their "Gaze" in the "Disaster" (*Eyd*) of the "Brother" (*Ach*). It signify "The Sin of the Bystander."

3 Deliverance (pe-lē-tāh): The scrolls highlight the "Escaping Root of the Remnant." "Total Destruction" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" his "Survival" (*Peleytah*) on "Mount Zion." It signifies "The Sacred Asylum."

4 The Kingdom will be the Lord’s (we-hā-ye-thā l-YHWH ham-me-lū-khā): The scrolls highlight the "Sovereign Root of the Conclusion." "Human Tyranny" is "Uprooted." The "Kingdom" (*Melukhah*) is "Rooted" in "YHWH." It signify "The Finality of Divine Government."

5 An Envoy (tzīr): The scrolls conclude with this "Diplomatic Root of the Warning." "Secrecy" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "Jonah and the Great Fish" in *Jonah 1*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Kingdom." The Lord reigns.
