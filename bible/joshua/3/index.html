Joshua 3

1Early in the morning Joshua and all the Israelites set out from Shittim and went to the Jordan, where they camped before crossing over.
2Three days later the officers went through the camp,
3giving orders to the people: “When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and the Levitical priests carrying it, you are to move out from your positions and follow it.”
4“Then you will know which way to go, since you have never been this way before. But keep a distance of about two thousand cubits between you and the ark; do not go near it.” 1
5Joshua told the people, “Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will do amazing things among you.” 2
6Joshua said to the priests, “Take up the ark of the covenant and pass on ahead of the people.” So they took it up and went ahead of them.
7And the LORD said to Joshua, “Today I will begin to exalt you in the eyes of all Israel, so they may know that I am with you as I was with Moses.”
8“Tell the priests who carry the ark of the covenant: ‘When you reach the edge of the Jordan’s waters, go and stand in the river.’”
9Joshua said to the Israelites, “Come here and listen to the words of the LORD your God.”
10“This is how you will know that the living God is among you and that he will certainly drive out before you the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites and Jebusites.”
11“See, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth will go into the Jordan ahead of you.”
13“And as soon as the priests who carry the ark of the LORD—the Lord of all the earth—set foot in the Jordan, its waters flowing downstream will be cut off and stand up in a heap.”
14So when the people broke camp to cross the Jordan, the priests carrying the ark of the covenant went ahead of them.
15Now the Jordan is at flood stage all during harvest. Yet as soon as the priests who carried the ark reached the Jordan and their feet touched the water’s edge, 3
16the water from upstream stopped flowing. It piled up in a heap a great distance away, at a town called Adam in the vicinity of Zarethan, while the water flowing down to the Sea of the Arabah (that is, the Dead Sea) was completely cut off. So the people crossed over opposite Jericho. 4
17The priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD stopped in the middle of the Jordan and stood on dry ground, while all Israel passed by until the whole nation had completed the crossing on dry ground.

Translation Notes

1 Two Thousand Cubits: This distance (about 3,000 feet) in the scrolls served two purposes. First, it ensured that everyone in the massive camp could see the Ark as it led the way. Second, it maintained the "Holy Separation" between the people and the presence of God. It teaches that while we follow God, we must never lose our "Awe" of His transcendence.

2 Consecrate yourselves (Hitkadeshu): This is a recurring command before a miracle in the scrolls. It involves both physical washing and spiritual preparation. It teaches that "Divine Action" is often preceded by "Human Preparation." If we want to see God do "Amazing Things," we must first set ourselves apart for His use.

3 Flood Stage: The scrolls mention that this happened during the "Harvest" (spring). This is when the Jordan is at its most dangerous and impossible to cross, swollen by the melting snows of Mount Hermon. God chose the *hardest* possible time for the crossing to ensure that the miracle was undeniable. It was a "Test of Obedience" for the priests to step into a raging river.

4 Town of Adam: The scrolls specify that the water "piled up in a heap" at a town called Adam, about 20 miles upstream. This created a massive stretch of dry riverbed for the millions of Israelites to cross quickly. It is a mirror of the Red Sea crossing, but here, the "Presence of God" (the Ark) is the mechanism of the miracle rather than the staff of Moses.
