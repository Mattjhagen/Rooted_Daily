Joshua 16

1The allotment for the Josephites began at the Jordan east of Jericho, and went up through the hill country, through the desert, to Bethel. 1
2It went on from Bethel (that is, Luz), crossed over to the territory of the Arkites in Ataroth,
3descended westward to the territory of the Japhletites as far as the region of Lower Beth Horon and on to Gezer, ending at the Mediterranean Sea.
4So Manasseh and Ephraim, the descendants of Joseph, received their inheritance.
5This was the territory of Ephraim, according to its clans: The boundary of their inheritance went from Ataroth Addar in the east to Upper Beth Horon
8and continued to the Mediterranean Sea. This was the inheritance of the tribe of Ephraim, according to its clans,
9along with all the towns and villages set aside for the Ephraimites within the inheritance of the Manassites.
10They did not dislodge the Canaanites living in Gezer; to this day the Canaanites live among the people of Ephraim but are required to do forced labor. 2

Translation Notes

1 Josephites (Bnei Yosef): In the scrolls, the tribes of Ephraim and Manasseh are often treated as a single block ("The House of Joseph"). This reflects their prominence as the descendants of the man who saved the family in Egypt. Their territory was the "Heartland" of the land, including the spiritual centers of Bethel and Shechem.

2 Gezer and Forced Labor (Mas Oved): The scrolls record a dangerous compromise. Instead of removing the Canaanites as commanded, Ephraim chose to "Tax" them. They prioritized "Economic Gain" over "Spiritual Purity." This allowed Canaanite religion and culture to remain embedded in the land, which would later lead to the idolatry of the Northern Kingdom.
