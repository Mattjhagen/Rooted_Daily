Joshua 18

1The whole assembly of the Israelites gathered at Shiloh and set up the tent of meeting there. The country was brought under their control, 1
2but there were still seven Israelite tribes who had not yet received their inheritance.
3So Joshua said to the Israelites: “How long will you wait before you begin to take possession of the land that the LORD, the God of your ancestors, has given you?” 2
4“Appoint three men from each tribe. I will send them out to make a survey of the land and to write a description of it, according to the inheritance of each tribe. Then they will return to me.”
5“You are to divide the land into seven parts. Judah is to remain in its territory on the south and the house of Joseph in its territory on the north.”
6“After you have written descriptions of the seven parts of the land, bring them here to me and I will cast lots for you in the presence of the LORD our God.”
8As the men started on their way to map out the land, Joshua instructed them, “Go and make a survey of the land and write a description of it. Then return to me, and I will cast lots for you here at Shiloh in the presence of the LORD.” 3
9So the men left and went through the land. They wrote its description on a scroll, town by town, in seven parts, and returned to Joshua in the camp at Shiloh.
10Joshua then cast lots for them in Shiloh in the presence of the LORD, and there he distributed the land to the Israelites according to their tribal divisions.

Benjamin's Inheritance: The first lot came up for the tribe of Benjamin. Their territory lay between the tribes of Judah and Joseph. It included the slope of Jericho, the hill country of Bethel, and the northern slope of the Jebusite city (that is, Jerusalem). (v.11-28)

Translation Notes

1 Shiloh: In the scrolls, Shiloh becomes the first permanent "Religious Capital" of Israel in the land. The Tabernacle (Tent of Meeting) would remain here for over 300 years. Its location in the center of the land made it accessible to all tribes and represented the "Centrality of Worship" in the national life.

2 How long will you wait (Raphim): Literally in the scrolls, "Why are you showing yourselves slack?" Joshua rebukes the remaining seven tribes for their "Procrastination." They had become comfortable in the camp and were hesitant to take on the work of settling their own territories. It teaches that God’s gifts require "Active Possession"—He gives the land, but we must go and live in it.

3 The Survey: This is an early example of "Systematic Mapping" in the scrolls. Joshua wanted an objective, written description of the land before casting lots. This ensured that the division was based on "Reality" and not just imagination. It shows that spiritual faith does not exclude practical, organized planning.
