Ecclesiastes 3

A Season for Everything.

The Rhythm of the Earth

“There is a time for everything, and a season for every activity under the heavens:”
“A time to be born and a time to die,
a time to plant and a time to uproot.” 1

Eternity Within

“He has made everything beautiful in its time. He has also set eternity in the human heart.” 2
“Yet no one can fathom what God has done from beginning to end.” 3

God’s Work Endures

“I know that everything God does will endure forever; nothing can be added to it and nothing taken from it.” 4

All Return to Dust

“Surely the fate of human beings is like that of the animals; the same fate awaits them both.”
“All come from dust, and to dust all return.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Time to Uproot (we-eth la-a-qor na-tu-a): Literally "And a time to uproot the planted." In the scrolls, this is the "Agricultural Root of Sovereignty." There is a "Cycle" (*Eth/Zeman*) where the "Root" (*Natu-a*) is "Pulled" (*A-qor*). It signifies "The Inevitability of Transition."

2 Eternity in the Heart (gam-eth-ha-O-lam na-than be-Lib-bam): The scrolls highlight the "Metaphysical Root of Desire." God has "Planted" (*Nathan*) the "Eternal/World" (*Olam*) in the "Mind/Heart" (*Leb*). It signifies "The Infinite Capacity of the Finite Soul."

3 No one can Fathom (mi-be-li a-sher lo-Yim-tza ha-A-dam): Literally "Without which man cannot find." The scrolls highlight the "Epistemic Root of Limitation." We have the "Root of Desire" for eternity, but not the "Root of Comprehension" for God’s "Work" (*Ma-a-seh*). It signify "Bounded Infinity."

4 Endure Forever (le-O-lam yi-he-yeh): The scrolls highlight the "Permanent Root of Divinity." Human effort is "Hevel" (*Breath*), but God’s "Action" (*Ma-a-seh*) is "Rooted" in "Eternal Stability" (*Olam*). It signifies "Divine Immutability."

5 To Dust all Return (we-he-kol shab el-he-A-phar): The scrolls conclude with this "Elemental Root of Mortality." The "Common Origin" and "Common End" of all "Flesh" (*Ba-sar*) is the "Dust" (*Aphar*). It marks the transition to the "Oppression of the Poor" in *Ecclesiastes 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sovereign Cycle." Rest in the dust.
