Ecclesiastes 7

Wisdom and the House of Mourning.

Better is the End

“A good name is better than fine perfume.”
“And the day of death better than the day of birth.” 1

The House of Mourning

“It is better to go to a house of mourning than to go to a house of feasting... for the heart of the wise is in the house of mourning.” 2

The Shelter of Wisdom

“Wisdom is a shelter as money is a shelter, but the advantage of knowledge is this: Wisdom preserves those who have it.” 3

Many Schemes

“When times are good, be happy; but when times are bad, consider this: God has made the one as well as the other.” 4
“God created mankind upright, but they have gone in search of many schemes.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Day of Death better (yom ha-Ma-weth mi-Yom hi-wal-do): In the scrolls, this is the "Conclusion Root." The "Day of Birth" (*Hiwaldo*) "Roots" the person in "Vanity" (*Hevel*), but the "Day of Death" (*Maweth*) "Roots" the "Outcome" of the "Good Name" (*Shem Tob*). It signifies "The Value of a Finished Race."

2 House of Mourning (Beyth E-bel): The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Impact of Grief." The "Heart" (*Leb*) of the "Wise" (*Chakamim*) is "Rooted" in the "Reality" of mortality, while the "Fool" (*Kesilim*) is "Uprooted" by "Feasting" (*Simchah*). It signifies "The Sobriety of Truth."

3 Wisdom Preserves (ha-Chok-mah te-cha-yeh be-a-ley-ah): Literally "Wisdom gives life to its owners." The scrolls highlight the "Vitality Root." "Money" (*Kesep*) is a "Shadow/Shelter" (*Tzel*), but "Wisdom" (*Chokmah*) "Roots" the "Life" (*Chayyim*) itself. It signify "Internal vs. External Security."

4 God has made... the Other (gam-eth-zeh le-u-math-zeh a-sah ha-E-lo-him): The scrolls highlight the "Dualistic Root of Providence." God "Roots" the "Bad Day" (*Yom Ra-ah*) alongside the "Good Day" (*Yom Tob*) so that man cannot "Find" (*Yim-tza*) the future. It signifies "The Humility of Uncertainty."

5 Search of many Schemes (we-hem-bi-q-shu chish-bo-noth rab-bim): The scrolls conclude with this "Complexity Root of Folly." God "Rooted" mankind in "Uprightness" (*Ya-shar*), but they "Uprooted" themselves through "Calculations/Schemes" (*Chishbonoth*). It marks the transition to the "Face of the King" in *Ecclesiastes 8*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Simple Uprightness." Return to the start.
