Ecclesiastes 1

The Words of the Teacher.

Breath of Breaths

“Meaningless! Meaningless! Utterly meaningless! Everything is meaningless.” 1

Under the Sun

“What do people gain from all their labors at which they toil under the sun?” 2
“There is nothing new under the sun.” 3

Chasing the Wind

“I have seen all the things that are done under the sun; all of them are meaningless, a chasing after the wind.” 4

The Heavy Burden

“For with much wisdom comes much sorrow; the more knowledge, the more grief.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Meaningless (He-bel ha-ba-lim): Literally "Breath of breaths" or "Mist of mists." In the scrolls, "Hevel" is the "Ephemeral Root." It is the opposite of "Rootedness." It is something that exists but cannot be "Grasped" or "Anchored." It signifies "Transience."

2 Under the Sun (ta-chath hash-Sha-mesh): The scrolls highlight the "Horizontal Root of Perspective." Life "Under the Sun" is "Uprooted" from the "Eternal/Vertical" context. It signify "The Closed System of Mortality."

3 Nothing New (en-kol-Cha-dash ta-chath hash-Sha-mesh): The scrolls highlight the "Cyclical Root of History." Humanity is "Rooted" in a "Repetitive Loop" (*The sun rises, the wind blows*). It signifies "The Illusion of Progress."

4 Chasing after the Wind (re-uth Ru-ach): Literally "Striving/Shepherding of the wind." The scrolls highlight the "Futility Root of Effort." One cannot "Root" the "Wind" (*Ruach*). It signify "The Exhaustion of Autonomy."

5 More Knowledge, more Grief (yo-siph Da-at yo-siph Mak-ob): The scrolls conclude this opening with the "Intellectual Root of Sorrow." "Knowledge" (*Daat*) "Uproots" "Ignorant Bliss" and replaces it with "Pain" (*Makob*). It marks the transition to the "Testing of Pleasure" in *Ecclesiastes 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Honest Search." All is breath.
