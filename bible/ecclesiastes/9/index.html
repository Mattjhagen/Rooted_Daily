Ecclesiastes 9

Life, Pleasure, and the Race of Chance.

The Living Hope

“Anyone who is among the living has hope—even a living dog is better off than a dead lion!” 1

Clothed in White

“Go, eat your food with gladness, and drink your wine with a joyful heart.”
“Always be clothed in white, and always anoint your head with oil.” 2

With All Your Might

“Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with all your might.” 3
“The race is not to the swift or the battle to the strong... but time and chance happen to them all.” 4

The Forgotten Wise Man

“A poor wise man saved the city by his wisdom. But nobody remembered that poor man.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Living Dog better (Ke-leb chay hu tob min-ha-Ar-yeh ham-meth): In the scrolls, "Life" (*Chay*) is the "Hope Root" (*Bitachon*). The "Lion" (*Aryeh*) is the "Root of Majesty," but "Death" (*Meth*) "Uproots" all potential. Even the "Lowly Dog" (*Keleb*) is "Rooted" in the "Possible" because it breathes. It signifies "The Primacy of Existence."

2 Clothed in White (be-kol-Eth yi-he-yu be-ga-de-kha le-ba-nim): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Defiance." To wear "White" (*Lebanim*) in a "Meaningless" (*Hevel*) world is to "Root" one's "Joy" in God’s "Approval" (*Ra-tzah*). It signifies "Sanctified Celebration."

3 Do it with all your Might (be-ko-cha-kha a-seh): The scrolls highlight the "Industrial Root of the Moment." The "Grave" (*She-ol*) "Uproots" all "Planning" (*Chesh-bon*). Therefore, the "Now" must be "Rooted" in "Maximum Intensity" (*Koach*). It signify "Existential Urgency."

4 Time and Chance (Eth wa-Pe-ga): The scrolls highlight the "External Root of Outcome." Human "Rootedness" (*Swift/Strong*) is "Uprootable" by "Moment" (*Eth*) and "Event" (*Pega*). It signifies "The Limits of Meritocracy."

5 Nobody Remembered (we-A-dam lo za-kar): The scrolls conclude with this "Social Root of Vanity." "Wisdom" (*Chokmah*) is "Better" (*Tobah*) than "Weapons," yet it is "Uprooted" from "History" by the "Poverty" of the source. It marks the transition to the "Dead Flies" in *Ecclesiastes 10*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unseen Value." Wisdom is enough, even if forgotten.
