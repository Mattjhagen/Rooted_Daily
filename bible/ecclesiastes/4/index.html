Ecclesiastes 4

Companionship and the Vanity of Toil.

The Tears of the Oppressed

“I saw the tears of the oppressed—and they have no comforter.” 1

Tranquility over Toil

“Better one handful with tranquility than two handfuls with toil and chasing after the wind.” 2

Two are Better than One

“Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their labor.” 3
“If either of them falls down, one can help the other up.”

The Three-Fold Cord

“A cord of three strands is not quickly broken.” 4
“Better a poor but wise youth than an old but foolish king.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Tears of the Oppressed (Dim-ath ha-a-shu-qim): In the scrolls, "Oppression" (*Osheq*) is the "Systemic Uprooter." The "Tears" (*Dimath*) flow because there is "No Comforter" (*en-le-hem me-na-chem*). It signifies "Social Despair under the Sun."

2 One Handful with Tranquility (Tob Ma-lo Kaph Na-chat): The scrolls highlight the "Satiety Root." "Tranquility" (*Nachat*) is a "Durable Root" compared to the "Two Handfuls" (*Melo Choph-na-im*) of "Toil" (*Amal*). It signifies "Balanced Sufficiency."

3 Two are Better (To-bim ha-She-na-im min-ha-E-chad): The scrolls highlight the "Relational Root of Strength." "Isolation" (*Echad*) "Uproots" safety, while "Duality" (*Shenaim*) "Roots" the "Reward" (*Sa-kar*). It signify "Synergistic Survival."

4 Cord of Three Strands (we-ha-Chu-t ha-me-shul-la-sh): The scrolls highlight the "Structural Root of Unity." A "Triple-braid" (*Me-shullash*) "Cord" (*Chut*) provides a "Strength" that cannot be "Uprooted" (*lo bi-me-he-rah yin-na-theq*). It signifies "The Power of Community."

5 Poor but Wise Youth (Tob Ye-led mis-ken we-Cha-kam): The scrolls conclude with this "Inversion Root." "Age" (*Old King*) is "Uprooted" from value if it lacks the "Root of Discernment" (*Hiz-za-her/Warning*). It marks the transition to the "Awe of God" in *Ecclesiastes 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Stronger Cord." Stay together.
