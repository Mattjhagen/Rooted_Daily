Ecclesiastes 10

Folly, Skill, and the Ax’s Edge.

The Stink of Folly

“As dead flies cause even a bottle of perfume to stink,”
“So a little folly outweighs wisdom and honor.” 1

Sharpen the Edge

“If the ax is dull and its edge unsharpened, more strength is needed, but skill will bring success.” 2

The Leaking House

“Through laziness, the rafters sag; because of idle hands, the house leaks.” 3

A Bird on the Wing

“Do not revile the king even in your thoughts... for a bird in the sky may carry your words.” 4
“And a bird on the wing may report what you say.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Folly outweighs Wisdom (ya-qar mi-Chok-mah mi-Ka-bod): In the scrolls, "Folly" (*I-we-leth*) is the "Contaminative Root." A "Little" (*Me-at*) folly "Uproots" the "Preciousness" (*Yaqar*) of "Wisdom" (*Chokmah*). It signifies "The Fragility of Reputation."

2 Ax is Dull (im-qe-hah hab-Bar-zel): The scrolls highlight the "Efficiency Root." "Strength" (*Cha-ya-lim*) is "Uprooted" by "Resistance" unless "Skill" (*Kish-ron*) "Roots" the "Outcome" (*Success/Hash-ker*). It signify "The Intelligence of Preparation."

3 House Leaks (yi-dloph hab-Ba-yith): The scrolls highlight the "Erosive Root of Sloth." "Laziness" (*A-tzal-ta-yim*) "Uproots" the "Integrity" of the "Shelter" (*Bayith*). It signifies "The Decay of Inaction."

4 Bird in the Sky (Oph hash-Sha-ma-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Informational Root of Risk." Private "Thoughts" (*Ma-da*) can be "Uprooted" and made public through "Unseen Channels." It signify "The Lack of True Privacy."

5 Bird on the Wing (ba-al hak-Ke-na-pha-yim): Literally "The owner of the wings." The scrolls conclude with this "Propagative Root of Speech." A "Secret" is "Rooted" only as long as it is "Silent." It marks the transition to "Generosity" in *Ecclesiastes 11*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Careful Word." Guard your breath.
