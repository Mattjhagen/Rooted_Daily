Ecclesiastes 12

The Final Conclusion of the Matter.

Remember Your Creator

“Remember your Creator in the days of your youth, before the days of trouble come.” 1

The Silver Cord

“Remember him—before the silver cord is severed, and the golden bowl is broken.” 2
“The dust returns to the ground it came from, and the spirit returns to God who gave it.” 3

Firmly Embedded Nails

“The words of the wise are like goads, their collected sayings like firmly embedded nails.” 4

The Conclusion

“Fear God and keep his commandments, for this is the duty of all mankind.” 5
“For God will bring every deed into judgment, including every hidden thing.”

Translation Notes

1 Remember your Creator (uz-Kor eth-Bo-re-e-kha): In the scrolls, this is the "Origination Root." To "Remember" (*Za-kar*) is to "Root" the "Youth" (*Be-chu-ro-the-kha*) in the "Source" (*Bo-re*) before the "Vanity" (*Hevel*) of age "Uproots" vitality. It signifies "Foundational Identity."

2 Silver Cord... Golden Bowl (Che-bel hak-Ke-seph... Gul-lath haz-Za-hab): The scrolls highlight the "Fragility Root of Connection." The "Cord" (*Chebel*) and "Bowl" (*Gullath*) are the "Structural Roots" of life that are eventually "Severed" (*Ye-ra-theq*) and "Broken" (*Ta-ru-tz*). It signify "The Point of Disconnect."

3 Spirit returns to God (we-ha-Ru-ach Ta-shub el-ha-E-lo-him): The scrolls highlight the "Repatriation Root." The "Body" returns to the "Dust Root" (*Aphar*), but the "Breath/Spirit" (*Ruach*) is "Rooted" back in its "Giver" (*Nathan*). It signifies "The Divine Custody of Consciousness."

4 Firmly Embedded Nails (mas-me-roth ne-tu-im): Literally "Nails planted." The scrolls highlight the "Fixity Root of Wisdom." Wise words are "Planted" (*Netuim*) to provide "Anchorage" in a "Breath-like" world. It signify "The Stability of Truth."

5 Duty of all Mankind (ki-zeh kol-ha-A-dam): Literally "For this is all the man." The scrolls conclude the entire book with this "Integral Root." To "Fear" (*Yere*) and "Keep" (*Shemor*) is the "Total Definition" of "Humanity." It marks the end of the "Breath Collection" and the transition to the "Song of Love" in *Song of Solomon*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Finished Search." Fear God and be whole.
