Ecclesiastes 2

The Test of Pleasure and Toil.

Tested with Pleasure

“I said to myself, ‘Come now, I will test you with pleasure to find out what is good.’”
“But that also proved to be meaningless.” 1

Great Projects

“I undertook great projects: I built houses for myself and planted vineyards.”
“Yet when I surveyed all that my hands had done... everything was meaningless, a chasing after the wind.” 2

The Same Fate

“I saw that wisdom is better than folly, just as light is better than darkness.”
“But I also came to realize that the same fate overtakes them both.” 3
“Both the wise and the fool must die!”

Satisfaction in Toil

“A person can do nothing better than to eat and drink and find satisfaction in their own toil.” 4
“This too, I see, is from the hand of God.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Test you with Pleasure (a-nas-se-khah be-Sim-chah): In the scrolls, "Pleasure" (*Simchah*) is the "Hedonic Root." The Teacher attempts to "Root" his soul in "Joy," but it "Uproots" itself because it lacks "Substance" (*Hevel*). It signifies "The Failure of Hedonism."

2 Surveyed all... (u-pha-ni-thi a-ni be-kol-Ma-a-say): Literally "I turned myself to all my works." The scrolls highlight the "Retrospective Root of Disillusionment." The "Works" (*Ma-a-say*) of the "Hands" (*Ya-day*) are "Uprooted" from "Value" (*Yi-thron*) by the passage of time. It signify "The Vanity of Legacy."

3 Same Fate (Mi-q-reh E-chad): Literally "One chance/happening." The scrolls highlight the "Equalizing Root of Death." Mortality is the "Great Uprooter" that ignores the "Light" (*Or*) of wisdom. It signifies "Existential Leveling."

4 Satisfaction in Toil (we-her-ah eth-Naph-sho Tob ba-a-ma-lo): Literally "Show his soul good in his labor." The scrolls highlight the "Present-Moment Root of Contentment." To "Find Satisfaction" is to "Root" the "Soul" (*Naphesh*) in the "Now." It signifies "Sanctified Stoicism."

5 Hand of God (mi-Yad ha-E-lo-him hi): The scrolls conclude this experiment with the "Sovereign Root of Provision." "Enjoyment" (*Tob*) is not "Achieved" through "Toil" (*Amal*) but "Received" from the "Hand" (*Yad*) of the Creator. It marks the transition to the "Season for Everything" in *Ecclesiastes 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Divine Gift." Receive it.
