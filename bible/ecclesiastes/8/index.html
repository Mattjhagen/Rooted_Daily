Ecclesiastes 8

Wisdom, Authority, and the Fear of God.

The Brightened Face

“Who is like the wise? Who knows the explanation of things? A person’s wisdom brightens their face.” 1

The Supreme Word

“Since a king’s word is supreme, who can say to him, ‘What are you doing?’” 2

Power over the Wind

“As no one has power over the wind to contain it, so no one has power over the day of their death.” 3

It will Go Well

“Yet I know that it will go better with those who fear God, who are reverent before him.” 4
“No one can comprehend what goes on under the sun.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Wisdom brightens their Face (Chok-math A-dam ta-ir Pa-na-iw): In the scrolls, "Wisdom" (*Chokmah*) is the "Illuminative Root." It "Roots" the "Face" (*Panim*) in "Light" (*Ta-ir*), "Uprooting" the "Hardness" (*Oz*) of the expression. It signifies "The Aesthetic of Character."

2 King’s Word is Supreme (ba-a-sher De-bar-Me-lekh Shil-ton): The scrolls highlight the "Authority Root." The "Word" (*Dabar*) of the "King" (*Melekh*) is "Rooted" in "Dominion" (*Shilton*). It signify "The Irresistibility of Decree."

3 Power over the Wind (en-A-dam Shal-it ba-Ru-ach): Literally "No man is ruler over the spirit/wind." The scrolls highlight the "Biological Limit Root." Mortality "Uproots" all "Human Sovereignty." It signifies "The Boundaries of Control."

4 Fear God (ye-re-im mil-li-pha-ney-E-lo-him): The scrolls highlight the "Safe Harbor Root." Despite the "Delayed Sentence" (*Mishpat*), the Teacher "Roots" his "Certainty" (*Ki-yo-de-a*) in the "Awe" (*Yere*) of God. It signify "The Ultimate Security."

5 No one can Comprehend (lo-yu-khal ha-A-dam lim-tzo): Literally "Man is not able to find." The scrolls conclude with this "Cognitive Root of Mystery." Human "Effort" (*Amal*) to "Discover" (*Limtzo*) meaning is "Uprooted" by the "Vastness" of God’s "Work" (*Ma-a-seh*). It marks the transition to "The Common Destiny" in *Ecclesiastes 9*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sovereign Mystery." Trust the silence.
