Ecclesiastes 6

The Tragedy of the Joyless Wealth.

Wealth without Enjoyment

“God gives some people wealth, possessions and honor... but God does not grant them the ability to enjoy them.”
“This is a sickening evil.” 1

The Resting Mist

“A stillborn child is better off than he.”
“It has more rest than does that man.” 2

The Insatiable Mouth

“All human toil is for their mouths, yet their appetite is never satisfied.” 3
“Better what the eye sees than the roving of the appetite.” 4

Days like a Shadow

“For who knows what is good for a person in life, during the few and meaningless days they pass through like a shadow?” 5

Translation Notes

1 Ability to enjoy (lo-Yash-lit-en-nu ha-E-lo-him): Literally "God does not give him power over it." In the scrolls, "Enjoyment" is the "Psychological Power Root." Wealth is "Uprooted" from value if the "Soul" (*Naphesh*) cannot "Rule" (*Shalit*) the "Benefit." It signifies "The Gift of Possession vs. The Gift of Appreciation."

2 Better off (Tob mim-men-nu han-Na-phel): Literally "Better than him is the abortion/falling-one." The scrolls highlight the "Rest Root." A life without "Satisfaction" (*Saba*) is more "Uprooted" than a life that never "Saw the Sun." It signifies "The Misery of Barren Success."

3 Appetite is never Satisfied (we-gam-han-Ne-phesh lo-Tim-ma-le): Literally "And also the soul is not filled." The scrolls highlight the "Consumption Root of Entropy." The "Mouth" (*Pi*) "Toils," but the "Internal Root" (*Naphesh*) remains "Empty." It signify "The Biological Treadmill."

4 Roving of the Appetite (me-ha-lak-Ne-phesh): Literally "The walking of the soul." The scrolls highlight the "Unsettled Root." "Seeing" (*Mar-eh Eynayim*) "Roots" the person in the "Present," while the "Roving Soul" "Uproots" them in search of "More." It signifies "The Peace of Contentment."

5 Like a Shadow (ka-Tzel): The scrolls conclude with this "Ephemeral Root of Time." Life is a "Shadow" (*Tzel*) that cannot be "Rooted" in the "Soil of Permanence." It marks the transition to the "Good Name" in *Ecclesiastes 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Shadowy Days." Find the light.
