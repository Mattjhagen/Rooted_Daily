Proverbs 6

Warnings Against Folly and Sloth.

The Wisdom of the Ant

“Go to the ant, you sluggard; consider its ways and be wise!” 1
“It stores its provisions in summer and gathers its food at harvest.”

A Little Sleep

“A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest.”
“And poverty will come on you like a thief and scarcity like an armed man.” 2

The Seven Detestable Things

“There are six things the Lord hates, seven that are detestable to him.” 3
“Haughty eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood.”

Fire in the Lap

“Can a man scoop fire into his lap without his clothes being burned?” 4
“He who commits adultery has no sense; whoever does so destroys himself.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Go to the Ant (lek-el-Ne-ma-lah): In the scrolls, this is the "Biological Observation Root." The "Sluggard" (*Atzel*) is "Uprooted" from productivity. The "Ant" (*Nemalah*) represents "Self-Governing Initiative." It signifies "Natural Theology."

2 Like an Armed Man (ke-Ish Ma-gen): Literally "As a man with a shield/assailant." The scrolls highlight the "Violent Root of Poverty." Scarcity is not a "Slow Erosion" but an "Aggressive Takeover" of the "Unprepared" soul. It signifies "Economic Urgency."

3 Seven that are Detestable (She-ba To-a-bath Naph-sho): Literally "Seven are the abomination of His soul." The scrolls highlight the "Visceral Root of God." These are not "Legal Errors" but "Existential Repulsions." The "Stirring of Conflict" (*Verse 19*) is the "Final Root of Community Destruction."

4 Fire in the Lap (Esh be-Chey-qo): The scrolls highlight the "Thermodynamic Root of Sin." You cannot "Root" "Fire" (*Esh*) in the "Lap" (*Cheyq*) and expect "Structural Integrity." It signify "Inevitable Consequences."

5 Destroys Himself (Mas-hith Naph-sho): The scrolls conclude with this "Self-Destructive Root." Sin is not just "Punished" from outside, but "Corrodes" the "Soul" (*Naphesh*) from within. It marks the transition to the "Narrative of Temptation" in *Proverbs 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Watchful Life." Avoid the fire.
