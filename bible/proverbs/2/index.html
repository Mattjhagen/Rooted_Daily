Proverbs 2

The Value of Seeking Wisdom.

Searching for Treasure

“If you look for it as for silver and search for it as for hidden treasure.” 1
“Then you will understand the fear of the Lord and find the knowledge of God.” 2

A Shield to the Blameless

“He holds success in store for the upright, he is a shield to those whose walk is blameless.” 3
“For he guards the course of the just and protects the way of his faithful ones.”

Delivered from the Wayward

“Wisdom will save you from the ways of wicked men.”
“Wisdom will save you also from the adulterous woman, from the wayward woman with her seductive words.” 4

Remaining in the Land

“For the upright will live in the land, and the blameless will remain in it.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Hidden Treasure (mat-mo-nim): In the scrolls, this is the "Subterranean Root of Value." Wisdom is not "Surface Level." It must be "Dug out" (*Search/Chapas*). It is the "Uprooting of Intellectual Laziness."

2 Knowledge of God (Da-at E-lo-him): The scrolls highlight the "Experiential Root." To "Find" (*Matza*) this knowledge is the "Ultimate Harvest." It signifies "Spiritual Intimacy."

3 Shield (Ma-gen): The scrolls highlight the "Defensive Root." God "Roots" His "Protection" (*Shield*) in the "Integrity" (*Blameless/Tom*) of the walk. It signifies "Character as Armor."

4 Seductive Words (He-che-li-qah): Literally "She has made smooth." The scrolls highlight the "Frictionless Root of Temptation." The wayward woman "Uproots" through "Smoothness." It signifies "The Danger of Moral Slippage."

5 Remain in It (Yiw-wa-the-ru bah): The scrolls conclude with this "Persistence Root." Only the "Upright" (*Ye-sha-rim*) have "Staying Power" in the "Land" (*Aretz*). It marks the transition to the "Heart-Trust" in *Proverbs 3*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Enduring Walk." Stay in the land.
