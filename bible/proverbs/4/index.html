Proverbs 4

Get Wisdom at Any Cost.

A Father’s Instruction

“Listen, my sons, to a father’s instruction; pay attention and gain understanding.”
“The beginning of wisdom is this: Get wisdom. Though it cost all you have, get understanding.” 1

The Path of Light

“The path of the righteous is like the morning sun, shining ever brighter till the full light of day.” 2
“But the way of the wicked is like deep darkness; they do not know what makes them stumble.”

Guard Your Heart

“Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it.” 3
“Let your eyes look straight ahead; fix your gaze directly before you.” 4
“Give careful thought to the paths for your feet and be steadfast in all your ways.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Cost all you have (be-kol-Qin-ya-ne-kha): Literally "With all your acquisition/possessions." The scrolls highlight the "Economic Root of Value." Wisdom is the only asset worth a "Total Buy-in." It signify "Prioritized Investment."

2 Morning Sun (Or No-gah): Literally "The light of dawn/brightness." The scrolls highlight the "Incremental Root of Revelation." The "Path" (*O-rach*) of the "Righteous" (*Tzad-di-qim*) is not "Instant Noon" but "Increasing Light." It signifies "Spiritual Momentum."

3 Everything you do flows from it (mi-men-nu To-tz-oth Cha-yim): Literally "From it are the source/outgoings of life." The scrolls highlight the "Hydraulic Root of Character." The "Heart" (*Leb*) is the "Reservoir." If it is "Uprooted," life "Leaks." It is the "Root of Personal Governance."

4 Look straight ahead (ne-khoch-Yab-bi-tu): The scrolls highlight the "Attentional Root." The "Eyes" (*Ey-ne-kha*) must be "Rooted" in the "Forward Gaze" to avoid "Lateral Distraction." It signifies "Sovereign Focus."

5 Thought to the Paths (Pal-les Ma-gal): Literally "Level/Ponder the track." The scrolls conclude with this "Engineering Root of Conduct." To "Ponder" (*Palles*) the "Track" (*Magal*) is to "Root" the "Feet" (*Rag-le-kha*) in "Steadfastness." It marks the transition to the "Warnings of Folly" in *Proverbs 5*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Brightening Path." Shine on.
