Proverbs 1

The Proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel.

The Purpose of Wisdom

“For attaining wisdom and discipline; for understanding words of insight.” 1
“For giving prudence to those who are simple, knowledge and discretion to the young.”

The Beginning of Knowledge

“The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and instruction.” 2

Warning Against Sinners

“My son, if sinful men entice you, do not give in to them.” 3
“Do not go along with them, do not set foot on their paths.”

Wisdom Calls Aloud

“Wisdom calls aloud in the street, she raises her voice in the public squares.” 4
“How long will you who are simple love your simple ways? How long will mockers delight in mockery and fools hate knowledge?”
“But whoever listens to me will live in safety and be at ease, without fear of harm.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Wisdom and Discipline (Chok-mah u-Mu-sar): In the scrolls, "Chokmah" is the "Skillful Living Root." It is not just theory; it is the "Root of Navigational Ability." "Musar" is the "Correction Root." It signifies "Spiritual Training."

2 Fear of the Lord (Yir-ath YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Foundational Root." "Fear" (*Yirah*) is not "Reactive Terror" but "Rooted Awe." It is the "Start" (*Re-shith*) of "Knowing" (*Da-at*). It signifies "Alignment with Reality."

3 Do not give in (al-To-be): Literally "Do not consent." The scrolls highlight the "Willful Root." Sinner (*Chat-ta-im*) "Entice" (*Ye-phat-tu-kha*) to "Uproot" the innocent. It signify "Moral Resistance."

4 Wisdom Calls Aloud (Chok-moth bah-Chutz Tar-ron-nah): The scrolls highlight the "Public Root of Truth." Wisdom is not "Hidden" but "Resounding" (*Tar-ron-nah*). It signifies "Universal Accessibility."

5 Live in Safety (Yish-kan Be-tach): The scrolls conclude with this "Secure Root." To "Listen" (*Sho-me-a*) is to be "Rooted" in "Safety" (*Betach*). It marks the transition to the "Value of Seeking" in *Proverbs 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Safe Path." Listen and live.
