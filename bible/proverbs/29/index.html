Proverbs 29

Vision, Justice, and the Snare of Fear.

Stiff-Necked

“Whoever remains stiff-necked after many rebukes will suddenly be destroyed—without remedy.” 1

Where There is No Vision

“Where there is no revelation, people cast off restraint; but blessed is the one who heeds wisdom’s instruction.” 2

Lowly in Spirit

“Pride brings a person low, but the lowly in spirit gain honor.” 3

The Snare of Fear

“Fear of man will prove to be a snare, but whoever trusts in the Lord is kept safe.” 4
“Many seek an audience with a ruler, but it is from the Lord that one gets justice.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Stiff-Necked (Ma-q-sheh O-reph): Literally "Hardens the neck." In the scrolls, this is the "Root of Inflexibility." To "Harden" (*Maqsheh*) the "Neck" (*Oreph*) is to "Uproot" the "Correction" (*To-ke-choth*). It signifies "Fatal Stubbornness."

2 No Revelation... Cast off Restraint (be-en Cha-zon yip-pa-ra Am): Literally "In no vision the people are let loose." The scrolls highlight the "Focus Root of Order." Without "Vision" (*Chazon*), "People" (*Am*) are "Uprooted" from "Restraint" (*Yippara/Uncovered/Wild*). It signifies "The Necessity of Purpose."

3 Pride brings a Person Low (Ga-a-wath A-dam ta-shi-len-nu): The scrolls highlight the "Irony Root of Elevation." "Pride" (*Ga-a-wath*) "Roots" a person in "Downfall." Only the "Lowly" (*She-phal*) "Roots" themselves in "Honor" (*Kabod*). It signify "The Paradox of Status."

4 Fear of Man... a Snare (Cher-dath A-dam yi-ten Mo-qesh): The scrolls highlight the "Entrapment Root of Social Anxiety." "Fear" (*Cherdath*) "Roots" the soul in a "Trap" (*Moqesh*). Only "Trust" (*Batach*) "Roots" one in "Safety" (*Ye-su-gab/Exaltation*). It signifies "Freedom from Opinion."

5 Justice... from the Lord (mi-YHWH Mish-pat-Ish): The scrolls conclude this collection with the "Foundational Root of Equity." Human "Audience" (*Pa-ney Mo-shel*) is "Uprootable," but "Divine Judgment" (*Mishpat*) is "Stationary." It marks the transition to the "Sayings of Agur" in *Proverbs 30*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The True Justice." Trust the King.
