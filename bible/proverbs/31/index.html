Proverbs 31

The Oracle of Lemuel and the Woman of Valor.

Speak for the Destitute

“Speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves, for the rights of all who are destitute.” 1

Woman of Noble Character

“A wife of noble character who can find? She is worth far more than rubies.” 2
“She is clothed with strength and dignity; she can laugh at the days to come.” 3

The Instruction of Wisdom

“She speaks with wisdom, and faithful instruction is on her tongue.” 4

Praise at the Gate

“Charm is deceptive, and beauty is fleeting.”
“But a woman who fears the Lord is to be praised.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Speak up for the Destitute (Pe-thach-Pi-kha le-Il-lem): Literally "Open your mouth for the mute." In the scrolls, this is the "Advocacy Root." To "Open" (*Pethach*) the "Mouth" (*Pi*) for the "Uprooted" (*Bney Chal-loph*) is the "Royal Duty." It signifies "Vocal Sovereignty."

2 Wife of Noble Character (E-sheth Cha-yil): Literally "Woman of Valor/Strength/Force." The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Root of Agency." She is not "Passive," but a "Force" (*Chayil*) who "Roots" the "Household" (*Bayith*) in "Wealth." It signify "The Heroic Domesticity."

3 Laugh at the days to come (wa-Tis-chaq le-Yom A-cha-ron): Literally "She laughs at the last day." The scrolls highlight the "Eschatological Root of Confidence." She is "Rooted" in "Strength" (*Oz*) and "Dignity" (*Ha-dar*) so deeply that the "Future" (*Acharon*) causes "Laughter" (*Tischaq*) rather than "Fear." It signifies "Ontological Security."

4 Faithful Instruction (To-rath Che-sed): Literally "The Torah of Kindness." The scrolls highlight the "Relational Root of Speech." Her "Instruction" (*Torah*) is "Rooted" in "Covenant Love" (*Chesed*). It signifies "Merciful Pedagogy."

5 Fears the Lord (Yir-ath YHWH): The scrolls conclude the entire Book of Proverbs with this "Summative Root." "Charm" (*Chen*) and "Beauty" (*Yo-phi*) are "Uprootable" (*He-bel/Breath*), but the "Fear of YHWH" is the "Eternal Root" of "Praise" (*Halal*). It marks the transition to the "Breath of Breath" in *Ecclesiastes*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Woman of Valor." Fear God and live.
