Proverbs 18

Safety in the Name and the Power of the Tongue.

The Fortified Tower

“The name of the Lord is a fortified tower; the righteous run to it and are safe.” 1

The Wall of Wealth

“The wealth of the rich is their fortified city; they imagine it a wall too high to scale.” 2

Life and Death

“The tongue has the power of life and death, and those who love it will eat its fruit.” 3

Sticks Closer

“He who finds a wife finds what is good and receives favor from the Lord.” 4
“There is a friend who sticks closer than a brother.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Fortified Tower (Mig-dal Oz): In the scrolls, "Oz" is the "Resistant Strength Root." The "Name" (*Shem*) is a "Rooted Stronghold" (*Migdal*). To "Run" (*Rutz*) is the "Active Movement" toward "Safety" (*Nis-gab/Exaltation*). It signifies "Security through Identity."

2 Imagine it a High Wall (u-ke-Cho-mah nis-ga-bah be-Mas-ki-tho): Literally "As a high wall in his imagination." The scrolls highlight the "Root of False Perception." Wealth (*Hon*) is a "Fortification" (*Qir-yat Oz*) only in the "Mind" (*Maskith*). It signifies "The Vulnerability of Materialism."

3 Power of Life and Death (be-Yad-Ma-weth we-Cha-yim): Literally "In the hand of death and life." The scrolls highlight the "Surgical Root of Language." The "Tongue" (*Lashon*) is the "Tool" (*Yad*) that "Uproots" or "Plants" reality. It signifies "Linguistic Stewardship."

4 Finds what is Good (Ma-tza Tob): The scrolls highlight the "Discovery Root of Grace." A "Wife" (*Ish-shah*) is a "Discoverable Blessing" (*Tob*) that "Roots" "Favor" (*Ra-tzon*) from YHWH. It signify "Marital Providence."

5 Sticks Closer (Da-be-q me-Ach): Literally "Cleaving more than a brother." The scrolls conclude with this "Adhesive Root of Loyalty." There is a "Friend" (*O-heb/Lover*) who "Cleaves" (*Dabeq*) deeper than "Kinship." It marks the transition to the "Walk of the Poor" in *Proverbs 19*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Closest Companion." Cleave to Him.
