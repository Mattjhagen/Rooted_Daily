Proverbs 25

Proverbs of Solomon (The Hezekiah Collection).

Searching Out a Matter

“It is the glory of God to conceal a matter; to search out a matter is the glory of kings.” 1

Apples of Gold

“Like apples of gold in settings of silver is a ruling rightly given.” 2
“Through patience a ruler can be persuaded, and a gentle tongue can break a bone.”

Coals of Fire

“If your enemy is hungry, give him food to eat; if he is thirsty, give him water to drink.”
“In doing this, you will heap burning coals on his head, and the Lord will reward you.” 3

City without Walls

“Like cold water to a weary soul is good news from a distant land.” 4
“Like a city whose walls are broken through is a person who lacks self-control.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Glory of Kings (ke-bod Me-la-khim cha-qor Da-bar): In the scrolls, "Cha-qor" is the "Deep Research Root." God "Roots" His majesty in "Concealment" (*Has-ter*), while the "King" (*Melekh*) "Roots" his honor in "Discovery." It signifies "The Sacred Quest for Truth."

2 Apples of Gold (Tap-pu-chey Za-hab): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Truth." A "Word" (*Dabar*) "Spoken" (*Da-bur*) "Aptly" (*al-Oph-na-iw*) is "Rooted" in "Precious Symmetry." It signifies "The Beauty of Accuracy."

3 Coals of Fire (Ga-cha-lim A-tah cho-theh al-Ro-sho): The scrolls highlight the "Psychological Impact Root." Kindness to an "Enemy" (*So-ne-akha*) "Roots" a "Burning Conviction" in their soul. It is the "Uprooting of Hostility." It signify "The Alchemy of Grace."

4 Cold Water to a Weary Soul (Ma-yim Qa-rim al-Ne-phesh a-ye-phah): The scrolls highlight the "Hydration Root of Hope." "Good News" (*She-mu-ah To-bah*) "Roots" vitality back into the "Exhausted" (*Ayephah*) "Soul" (*Naphesh*). It signify "The Somatic Impact of Encouragement."

5 Walls broken through (Ir Pe-ru-tzah en-Cho-mah): The scrolls conclude with this "Defenseless Root." A person without "Self-Control" (*Ma-atzar*) is "Uprooted" from "Safety" and open to any "Invader." It marks the transition to the "Folly of Fools" in *Proverbs 26*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Internal Fortress." Guard your walls.
