Proverbs 24

Wisdom’s House and the Sluggard’s Field.

Rare and Beautiful Treasures

“By wisdom a house is built, and through understanding it is established.”
“Through knowledge its rooms are filled with rare and beautiful treasures.” 1

Rescue the Oppressed

“Rescue those being led away to death; hold back those staggering toward slaughter.” 2
“If you say, ‘But we knew nothing about this,’ does not he who weighs the heart perceive it?”

Seven Times Up

“For though the righteous fall seven times, they rise again, but the wicked stumble when calamity strikes.” 3

The Sluggard’s Lesson

“I went past the field of a sluggard... it was all overgrown with thorns.” 4
“I applied my heart to what I observed and learned a lesson from what I saw.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Rare and Beautiful Treasures (Hon ya-qar we-Na-im): In the scrolls, "Knowledge" (*Da-at*) is the "Interior Decorator Root." A "House" (*Bayith*) is "Rooted" not just in "Structure" (*Built/Established*) but in the "Wealth" (*Hon*) of "Insight." It signifies "Spiritual Affluence."

2 Rescue those... staggering (hatz-tzal-Le-qu-chim... ma-tim la-He-reg): The scrolls highlight the "Interventionist Root of Mercy." To "Hold back" (*Ma-sak*) those "Rooted" in "Slaughter" (*Hereg*) is the "Highest Moral Duty." It signifies "The Obligation of Presence."

3 Fall Seven Times (She-ba yip-pol Tzad-diq): The scrolls highlight the "Resilient Root of Righteousness." The "Righteous" (*Tzad-diq*) is not "Uprooted" by failure, but has a "Rising" (*Qam*) built into their "Nature" (*Seven/Perfection*). It signifies "Grace-Powered Momentum."

4 Overgrown with Thorns (a-lah kul-lo Qim-sho-nim): The scrolls highlight the "Neglect Root of Entropy." A "Field" (*Sa-deh*) "Uprooted" from "Attention" (*Atzel*) reverts to "Thorns" (*Qimshonim*). It signifies "The Natural Consequence of Sloth."

5 Applied my Heart (e-che-zeh A-shi-th Lib-bi): Literally "I gazed, I set my heart." The scrolls conclude with this "Observational Root." Wisdom is "Rooted" in the "Calibration" of the "Mind" (*Leb*) based on "Reality" (*Chazah*). It marks the transition to the "Hezekiah Collection" in *Proverbs 25*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Observed Lesson." Look and learn.
