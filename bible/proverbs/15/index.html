Proverbs 15

The Gentle Answer and the Cheerful Heart.

A Gentle Answer

“A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.” 1

The Eyes of the Lord

“The eyes of the Lord are everywhere, keeping watch on the wicked and the good.” 2

Cheerful and Crushed

“A cheerful heart brings a smile to the face, but heartache crushes the spirit.” 3
“Better a small serving of vegetables with love than a fattened calf with hatred.” 4

He Hears the Righteous

“The Lord is far from the wicked, but he hears the prayer of the righteous.” 5
“Humility comes before honor.”

Translation Notes

1 Gentle Answer (Ma-a-neh Rak): In the scrolls, "Gentle" (*Rak*) is the "Soft/Pliant Root." It "Uproots" "Wrath" (*Che-mah*) by "Absorbing" the impact. It signifies "De-escalation Wisdom."

2 Eyes are Everywhere (be-kol-Ma-qom Ey-ney YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Omnipresent Root of Oversight." God’s "Gaze" (*Eyney*) is not "Uprooted" by distance or darkness. It signifies "Absolute Accountability."

3 Heartache crushes the Spirit (u-be-at-tzbath-Leb Ru-ach ne-ke-ah): Literally "By the pain of the heart the spirit is stricken/broken." The scrolls highlight the "Somatic Impact Root." The "Spirit" (*Ruach*) is "Uprooted" by internal "Sorrow" (*Atzbath*). It signifies "Emotional Psychosomatics."

4 Vegetables with Love (A-ru-chath ya-raq wa-A-ha-bah sham): The scrolls highlight the "Qualitative Root of Dining." "Love" (*Ahabah*) is the "Essential Nutrient." It signifies "Relational Primacy."

5 Hears the Prayer (u-The-phi-lath Tzad-di-qim yish-ma): The scrolls conclude with this "Auditory Root of Grace." The "Prayer" (*Te-phi-lah*) of the "Righteous" (*Tzad-di-qim*) is "Rooted" in God’s "Attention" (*Shama*). It marks the transition to the "Sovereign Plans" in *Proverbs 16*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Heard Voice." He is listening.
