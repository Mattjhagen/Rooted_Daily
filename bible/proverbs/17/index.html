Proverbs 17

Peace, Refinement, and Friendship.

Better a Dry Crust

“Better a dry crust with peace and quiet than a house full of feasting, with strife.” 1

Testing the Heart

“The crucible for silver and the furnace for gold, but the Lord tests the heart.” 2
“Whoever would foster love covers over an offense.”

Born for Adversity

“A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for a time of adversity.” 3

Good Medicine

“A cheerful heart is good medicine, but a crushed spirit dries up the bones.” 4
“Even fools are thought wise if they keep silent.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Dry Crust (Pa-th Cha-re-bah): In the scrolls, this is the "Minimalist Root of Shalom." A "Crust" (*Path*) "Rooted" in "Quietness" (*Shal-wah*) is "Superior" (*Tob*) to "Abundance" (*Zib-chey*) "Rooted" in "Strife" (*Rib*). It signifies "Domestic Priority."

2 Lord tests the Heart (u-Bo-chen Lib-both YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Metallurgical Root of Character." God is the "Bo-chen" (*Assayer*) who "Uproots" the "Dross" to find the "Pure Root" of the "Heart" (*Leb*). It signifies "Providential Refinement."

3 Brother born for Adversity (we-Ach le-Tza-rah yi-wa-led): The scrolls highlight the "Symmetric Root of Loyalty." "Adversity" (*Tzarah*) is the "Womb" in which a "True Brother" (*Ach*) is "Born" (*Yiwaled*). It signify "Tested Friendship."

4 Good Medicine (Ye-yi-tib Ge-hah): Literally "It does good to the body/cure." The scrolls highlight the "Therapeutic Root of Joy." A "Cheerful Heart" (*Leb Same-ach*) "Roots" healing in the "Somatic System." It signifies "Spiritual Wellness."

5 Fools thought Wise (E-wil ma-cha-rish Cha-kam ye-cha-sheb): The scrolls conclude with this "Ironic Root of Silence." "Silence" (*Ma-cha-rish*) "Roots" the "Fool" (*Ewil*) in "Artificial Wisdom." It marks the transition to the "Fortified Tower" in *Proverbs 18*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Quiet Wisdom." Silence is gold.
