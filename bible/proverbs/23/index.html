Proverbs 23

Restraint and the Purchase of Truth.

Dine with Caution

“When you sit to dine with a ruler, note well what is before you.”
“Put a knife to your throat if you are given to gluttony.” 1

Fleeting Riches

“Do not wear yourself out to get rich; do not trust your own cleverness.” 2
“Cast but a glance at riches, and they are gone, for they will surely sprout wings and fly off to the sky.”

Buy the Truth

“Buy the truth and do not sell it—wisdom, instruction and insight as well.” 3

The Bite of the Serpent

“Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has strife? Who has needless bruises?”
“In the end it bites like a snake and poisons like a viper.” 4
“Your eyes will see strange sights, and your mind will imagine confusing things.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Knife to your Throat (we-Sam-ta Sak-kin be-Lo-e-kha): In the scrolls, this is the "Violent Root of Discipline." To prevent "Gluttony" (*Ba-al Ne-phesh*), one must "Root" their "Restraint" in "Mortal Seriousness." It signifies "Self-Governance."

2 Trust your own Cleverness (u-mi-Bi-nath-kha cha-dal): Literally "Cease from your own understanding." The scrolls highlight the "Epistemic Root of Wealth." The pursuit of "Riches" (*Osher*) is often "Rooted" in a "Cleverness" (*Binah*) that leads to exhaustion. It signify "The Vanity of the Grind."

3 Buy the Truth (E-meth qe-neh): The scrolls highlight the "Transaction Root of Wisdom." "Truth" (*Emeth*) is an "Invaluable Asset" that must be "Acquired" (*Qaneh*) and "Never Sold" (*bal-Tim-kor*). It signifies "The Absolute Priority of Reality."

4 Bites like a Snake (ke-Na-chash yi-shak): The scrolls highlight the "Toxic Transformation Root." "Wine" (*Yayin*) "Roots" its "End" (*Acha-rith*) in "Venom." It signify "The Deception of Pleasure."

5 Confusing things (Tah-pu-khoth): Literally "Perversities/Upside-down things." The scrolls conclude with this "Disorienting Root." Inebriation "Uproots" the "Mind" (*Leb*) from "Perception" (*Eynayim*). It marks the transition to the "Envy of the Wicked" in *Proverbs 24*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sobriety of Truth." Stay alert.
