Proverbs 13

Light, Hope, and Companionship.

The Brightening Light

“The light of the righteous shines brightly, but the lamp of the wicked is snuffed out.” 1

Hope and Fulfillment

“Hope deferred makes the heart sick, but a longing fulfilled is a tree of life.” 2
“A longing fulfilled is sweet to the soul.”

Walk with the Wise

“Walk with the wise and become wise, for a companion of fools suffers harm.” 3

Generational Inheritance

“A good man leaves an inheritance for his children’s children.” 4
“The righteous eat to their heart’s content, but the stomach of the wicked goes hungry.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Light of the Righteous (Or Tzad-di-qim yis-mach): Literally "The light of the righteous rejoices." The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of Joy." Their "Light" (*Or*) is "Internalized Celebration," while the "Lamp" (*Ner*) of the wicked is "Uprooted." It signifies "Spiritual Radiance."

2 Hope Deferred (To-che-leth me-mus-sa-khah): Literally "Expectation drawn out." The scrolls highlight the "Emotional Exhaustion Root." "Fulfillment" (*Ta-a-wah ba-ah*) is the "Tree of Life" (*Etz Chayyim*) that "Roots" the soul back in "Eden." It signifies "The Health of Gratification."

3 Walk with the Wise (Ho-lekh eth-Cha-ka-mim): The scrolls highlight the "Infection Root of Character." Wisdom is "Caught" through "Proximity" (*Walk/Holekh*). It is the "Uprooting of Foolish Associations."

4 Inheritance for Children (Yan-chil Bney Ba-nim): The scrolls highlight the "Generational Root of Value." A "Good Man" (*Tob*) "Roots" his influence deep into the "Future." It signifies "Long-term legacy."

5 Stomach of the Wicked (u-Be-ten Re-sha-im): The scrolls conclude with this "Digestive Root of Hunger." The "Wicked" (*Reshaim*) have an "Unsatisfied Root." It marks the transition to the "Wise Woman" in *Proverbs 14*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Brightening Walk." Shine on.
