Proverbs 14

Building and the Path of the Heart.

The Wise Builder

“The wise woman builds her house, but with her own hands the foolish one tears hers down.” 1

The Way that Seems Right

“There is a way that appears to be right, but in the end it leads to death.” 2
“Each heart knows its own bitterness, and no one else can share its joy.” 3

A Heart at Peace

“A heart at peace gives life to the body, but envy rots the bones.” 4

Righteousness Exalts

“Whoever oppresses the poor shows contempt for their Maker, but whoever is kind to the needy honors God.”
“Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin condemns any people.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Wise Woman Builds (Chok-moth Na-shim ba-ne-thah Bey-thah): In the scrolls, this is the "Constructive Feminine Root." Wisdom is a "Building Force" (*Banah*), while Folly "Uproots" (*Ha-ras*) what it did not plant. It signifies "Domestic Sovereignty."

2 Way that appears Right (De-rekh Ya-shar): The scrolls highlight the "Subjective Root of Error." "Right" (*Yashar*) in one's "Own Eyes" can be a "Deceptive Path" to "Maweth" (*Death*). It signify "The Need for Objective Wisdom."

3 Each Heart knows its Bitterness (Leb Yo-de-a Ma-rath Naph-sho): Literally "The heart knows the bitterness of its soul." The scrolls highlight the "Private Root of Experience." Some "Uprootings" are internal and "Incommunicable." It signifies "Emotional Individuality."

4 Envy rots the Bones (u-Re-qab Atz-mo-thoth Qin-ah): The scrolls highlight the "Corrosive Root of Resentment." "Envy" (*Qin-ah*) is an "Internal Rot" (*Reqab*) that "Uproots" the "Skeleton" of life. It signifies "Somatic Jealousy."

5 Righteousness Exalts (Tze-da-qah te-ro-mem Go-y): The scrolls conclude with this "National Root." "Righteousness" (*Tzedaqah*) "Roots" a "Nation" (*Goy*) in "High Honor." It marks the transition to the "Gentle Answer" in *Proverbs 15*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Peace-filled Heart." Honor God.
