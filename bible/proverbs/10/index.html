Proverbs 10

The Proverbs of Solomon.

Righteousness and Wealth

“Ill-gotten treasures have no lasting value, but righteousness delivers from death.” 1
“Lazy hands make for poverty, but diligent hands bring wealth.”

Fountain of Life

“The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life, but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence.” 2
“Hatred stirs up conflict, but love covers over all wrongs.” 3

The Blessing of the Lord

“The blessing of the Lord brings wealth, without painful toil for it.” 4
“The fear of the Lord adds length to life, but the years of the wicked are cut short.”
“The righteous will never be uprooted, but the wicked will not remain in the land.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Delivers from Death (ta-tzil mi-Ma-weth): In the scrolls, "Righteousness" (*Tze-da-qah*) is the "Rescuing Root." It provides a "Lifeline" that "Uproots" the soul from "Sheol." It signifies "Moral Immortality."

2 Fountain of Life (Me-qor Cha-yim): The scrolls highlight the "Artesian Root of Speech." The "Mouth" (*Pi*) of the "Righteous" (*Tzad-diq*) is a "Spring" that never fails. It signify "Generative Influence."

3 Love covers all Wrongs (al-kol-Pe-sha-im te-khas-seh A-ha-bah): The scrolls highlight the "Shielding Root of Affection." "Hatred" (*Sin-ah*) "Uproots" peace, but "Love" (*Ahabah*) "Covers" the "Fractures" (*Peshaim*). It signifies "Social Glue."

4 Blessing... without Painful Toil (Bir-kath YHWH... lo-Yo-siph E-tzeb im-mah): Literally "The blessing of YHWH... it does not add sorrow with it." The scrolls highlight the "Effortless Root of Grace." True "Wealth" (*Osher*) is "Rooted" in "Divine Favor," not just "Human Sweat" (*Etzeb*). It signifies "Gift-Economy Theology."

5 Never be Uprooted (bal-Yim-mot): Literally "Will not be moved/shaken." The scrolls conclude with this "Foundational Root of Stability." The "Righteous" are "Rooted" (*bal-Yimmot*) for "Eternity" (*le-Olam*). It marks the transition to the "Integrity of the Upright" in *Proverbs 11*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Unshakable Life." You will not be moved.
