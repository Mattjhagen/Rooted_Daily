Proverbs 9

The Invitations of Wisdom and Folly.

Wisdom’s Seven Pillars

“Wisdom has built her house; she has set up its seven pillars.” 1
“Come, eat my food and drink the wine I have mixed.” 2

The Beginning of Wisdom

“The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.” 3

Folly’s Deceptive Sweetness

“Folly is an unruly woman; she is simple and knows nothing.”
“Stolen water is sweet; food eaten in secret is delicious!” 4

Guests in the Realm of Death

“But little do they know that the dead are there, that her guests are deep in the realm of the dead.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Seven Pillars (Am-mu-de-ha Shib-ah): In the scrolls, "Seven" represents "Rooted Perfection." The "House" (*Bayith*) of wisdom is "Rooted" in a "Complete Structure." It signifies "Architectural Integrity."

2 Mixed her Wine (ma-se-khah Ye-nah): The scrolls highlight the "Preparation Root." Wisdom "Uproots" the "Raw" and "Unrefined," offering a "Prepared" (*Tebach*) feast. It signify "Deliberate Hospitality."

3 Holy One (Qo-do-shim): Literally "The Holy Ones" (plural of majesty). The scrolls highlight the "Root of Transcendence." Understanding (*Binah*) is "Rooted" in the "Knowledge" (*Da-at*) of the "Holy." It signifies "Theological Intelligence."

4 Stolen Water is Sweet (Ma-yim Ge-nu-bim yim-ta-qu): The scrolls highlight the "Clandestine Root of Folly." Folly "Roots" her appeal in "Secrecy" (*Se-ther*) and "Illegality." It signify "The Deception of Forbidden Pleasure."

5 Deep in the Realm of the Dead (be-Im-qey She-ol): The scrolls conclude this prologue with the "Terminal Root." Folly’s "Guests" (*Qe-ru-e-ha*) are "Uprooted" from the "Land of the Living" and buried in "Sheol." It marks the transition to the "Proverbs of Solomon" in *Proverbs 10*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Final Choice." Choose life.
