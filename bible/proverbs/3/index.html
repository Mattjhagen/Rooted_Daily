Proverbs 3

Trust and the Reward of Wisdom.

Trust with All Your Heart

“Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding.” 1
“In all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight.” 2

Health to Your Body

“Do not be wise in your own eyes; fear the Lord and shun evil.”
“This will bring health to your body and nourishment to your bones.” 3

The Father’s Discipline

“My son, do not despise the Lord’s discipline, and do not resent his rebuke.”
“Because the Lord disciplines those he loves, as a father the son he delights in.” 4

The Tree of Life

“Blessed are those who find wisdom, those who gain understanding.”
“She is a tree of life to those who take hold of her; those who hold her fast will be blessed.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Lean not on your own Understanding (el-Bi-nat-kha al-Ti-sha-en): In the scrolls, this is the "Structural Support Root." Human "Understanding" (*Binah*) is a "Weak Pillar." To "Lean" (*Sha-en*) on it is to invite a collapse. It signifies "Epistemic Humility."

2 Make your Paths Straight (Ye-yas-sher Or-cho-the-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Navigational Root." God "Rectifies" (*Yashar*) the "Tracks" (*Orchoth*) of the one who "Acknowledges" (*Da-eh*) Him. It signifies "Providential Correction."

3 Nourishment to your Bones (Shi-quy le-Atz-mo-the-kha): Literally "Drink/Moisture to your bones." The scrolls highlight the "Deep Biological Root." Wisdom provides "Internal Hydration" for the "Skeletal Support" of life. It is the "Uprooting of Chronic Stress."

4 Disciplines those He Loves (a-sher Ye-e-hab YHWH Yo-ki-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Pedagogical Root of Love." "Discipline" (*Mu-sar*) and "Rebuke" (*To-ke-chath*) are not "Uprooting" of the person, but "Uprooting" of the "Error." It signifies "Parental Sovereignty."

5 Tree of Life (Etz Cha-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Edenic Root." Wisdom is the "Re-planting" of the "Garden" in the soul. To "Take Hold" (*he-Che-zi-qah*) of her is to be "Rooted" in "Eternal Vitality." It marks the transition to the "Father’s Instruction" in *Proverbs 4*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Core Trust." Lean on Him.
