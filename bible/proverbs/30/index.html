Proverbs 30

The Sayings of Agur.

The Ignorant Seeker

“I am the most ignorant of men; I do not have a man’s understanding.”
“Who has gone up to heaven and come down? What is his name, and the name of his son? Tell me if you know!” 1

The Flawless Word

“Every word of God is flawless; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him.” 2

My Daily Bread

“Two things I ask of you, Lord; do not refuse me before I die.”
“Give me neither poverty nor riches, but give me only my daily bread.” 3

Too Amazing for Me

“There are three things that are too amazing for me, four that I do not understand.” 4
“The way of an eagle in the sky... and the way of a man with a young woman.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Name of His Son (u-Mah-Shem-Be-no): In the scrolls, this is the "Interrogative Root of Divinity." Agur challenges the "Uprooting" of divine mystery. "What is His Name?" refers to the Creator, and "His Son" (*Beno*) suggests a "Relational Essence" before creation. It signifies "Theological Awe."

2 Every word... is Flawless (kol-Im-rath E-lo-ah tze-ru-phah): Literally "Every saying of God is refined." The scrolls highlight the "Purity Root of Scripture." God’s word has been "Tested" (*Tzeruphah*) and has no "Uprootable" error. It signifies "Absolute Reliability."

3 Daily Bread (le-chem Chu-qi): Literally "The bread of my portion/statute." The scrolls highlight the "Sufficient Root of Provision." Agur seeks the "Balanced Root" (*Chuqi*) that prevents the "Uprooting" of character through either "Excess" (*Sabea*) or "Theft" (*Ganab*). It signify "Equilibrium."

4 Too Amazing (Niph-le-u mi-men-ni): The scrolls highlight the "Transcendental Root of Wonder." The "Way" (*De-rekh*) of creation is "Rooted" in a "Mystery" (*Niph-le-u*) that bypasses human "Understanding." It signifies "Natural Revelation."

5 Way of an Eagle (De-rekh han-Ne-sher): The scrolls conclude with these "Four Tracks." The "Eagle" in the sky, the "Snake" on the rock, the "Ship" in the sea, and "Intimacy." They are all "Uprootable" tracks that leave "No Trace" but change "Everything." It marks the transition to the "Oracle of Lemuel" in *Proverbs 31*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Wondering Soul." Look up.
