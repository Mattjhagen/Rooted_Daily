Proverbs 5

Warning Against Adultery.

Lips of Honey

“For the lips of the adulterous woman drip honey, and her speech is smoother than oil.” 1
“But in the end she is bitter as gall, sharp as a double-edged sword.” 2

Keep Your Distance

“Keep to a path far from her, do not go near the door of her house.” 3
“At the end of your life you will groan, when your flesh and body are spent.”

Your Own Well

“Drink water from your own cistern, running water from your own well.” 4
“May your fountain be blessed, and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth.” 5
“For your ways are in full view of the Lord, and he examines all your paths.”
“The evil deeds of the wicked ensnare them; the cords of their sin hold them fast.”

Translation Notes

1 Smoother than Oil (chal-laq mish-Sha-men): In the scrolls, this is the "Slippery Root of Seduction." The "Speech" (*Pi-ah*) lacks "Moral Friction." To be "Rooted" in "Smoothness" is to slide toward destruction. It signifies "The Deceptive Ease of Compromise."

2 Bitter as Gall (Ma-rah kal-La-a-nah): The scrolls highlight the "Chemical Root of Consequence." The "End" (*Acha-rith*) is a "Toxic Transformation." It is the "Uprooting of Temporary Pleasure."

3 Far from Her (Har-chaq me-a-ley-ha): Literally "Distance yourself." The scrolls highlight the "Spatial Root of Prevention." Safety is "Rooted" in "Proximity-Management." It signify "Tactical Retreat."

4 From your own Well (mi-Bney Be-e-re-kha): The scrolls highlight the "Exclusive Root of Intimacy." "Cistern" (*Bor*) and "Well" (*Beer*) signify "Domestic Fulfillment." It signifies "Covenantal Contentment."

5 Wife of your Youth (E-sheth Ne-u-re-kha): The scrolls conclude with this "Foundational Root of Joy." The "Fountain" (*Maqor*) is "Blessed" when "Rooted" in "Early Loyalty." It marks the transition to the "Warnings of Folly" in *Proverbs 6*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Faithful Heart." Drink deep.
