Proverbs 20

Wine, Justice, and the Lamp of the Soul.

Wine is a Mocker

“Wine is a mocker and beer a brawler; whoever is led astray by them is not wise.” 1

The Lamp of the Lord

“The human spirit is the lamp of the Lord that sheds light on one’s inmost being.” 2

Wait for the Lord

“Do not say, ‘I’ll pay you back for this wrong!’ Wait for the Lord, and he will avenge you.” 3

Strength and Splendor

“The glory of young men is their strength.”
“Gray hair is the splendor of the old.” 4
“Blows that wound cleanse away evil, and beatings purge the inmost being.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Wine is a Mocker (Letz hay-Ya-yin): In the scrolls, "Wine" (*Yayin*) is the "Seductive Root of Mockery" (*Letz*). It "Uproots" "Discernment" (*Binah*). It signifies "The Volatility of Substance."

2 Lamp of the Lord (Ner YHWH Nish-math A-dam): Literally "The breath of man is the lamp of YHWH." The scrolls highlight the "Illuminative Root of Consciousness." God "Roots" His "Observation" (*Chopes/Search*) in the "Breath/Spirit" (*Nishmath*) of the individual. It signifies "Internalized Sovereignty."

3 Wait for the Lord (Qa-weh le-YHWH): The scrolls highlight the "Patience Root of Justice." To "Avenge" (*Sho-lem*) oneself is to "Uproot" the "Divine Prerogative." To "Wait" (*Qaweh*) is to "Root" the "Expectation" in the King. It signify "Legal Trust."

4 Splendor of the Old (wa-Ha-dar Ze-qe-nim Se-bah): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Root of Maturity." "Strength" (*Ko-ach*) is the "Glory" (*Tip-e-reth*) of "Youth," but "Gray Hair" (*Sebah*) is the "Splendor" (*Hadar*) of "Experience." It signifies "The Value of Time."

5 Purge the Inmost Being (u-Ma-ko-th Chad-rey-Ba-ten): Literally "Strikes into the chambers of the belly." The scrolls conclude with this "Invasive Root of Correction." True "Cleanliness" (*Muruq*) requires "Deep Uprooting" of "Evil" (*Ra*) from the "Core" (*Baten*). It marks the transition to the "King’s Heart" in *Proverbs 21*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Internal Light." Shine within.
