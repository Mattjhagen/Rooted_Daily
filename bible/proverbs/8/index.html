Proverbs 8

Wisdom’s Call to All Humanity.

Does Not Wisdom Call?

“Does not wisdom call out? Does not understanding raise her voice?” 1
“To you, O people, I call out; I raise my voice to all mankind.”

Precious Beyond Rubies

“For wisdom is more precious than rubies, and nothing you desire can compare with her.” 2
“My fruit is better than fine gold; what I yield surpasses choice silver.”

Before the Foundations

“The Lord brought me forth as the first of his works, before his deeds of old.” 3
“Then I was constantly at his side. I was filled with delight day after day, rejoicing always in his presence.” 4

Find Life and Favor

“For those who find me find life and receive favor from the Lord.” 5
“But those who fail to find me harm themselves; all who hate me love death.”

Translation Notes

1 Wisdom Call (Chok-moth ti-q-ra): In the scrolls, "Chokmoth" is a feminine plural of "Wisdom," suggesting a "Plenitude of Skill." She is not "Uprooted" by the noise of the world but "Resounds" (*Tarronnah*) in it. It signify "Sovereign Intelligence."

2 Nothing... can Compare (kol-Cha-pha-tzim lo yish-wu-bah): Literally "All desired things cannot equal her." The scrolls highlight the "Ultimate Root of Desire." Every human "Want" (*Chaphatz*) is "Uprooted" by the "Superiority" of wisdom. It signifies "Value Hierarchy."

3 First of His Works (Re-shith Dar-ko): Literally "The beginning of His way." The scrolls highlight the "Primordial Root." Wisdom is the "Blueprint" (*Amon*) of creation. It signify "The Rationality of the Cosmos."

4 Filled with Delight (E-hi-yeh Sha-a-shu-im): Literally "I was delights." The scrolls highlight the "Playful Root of Creation." Wisdom "Plays" (*Me-sha-che-qeth*) in the world. It is the "Uprooting of Somber Deism."

5 Find Life (Mat-za Chay-yim): The scrolls conclude with this "Vital Root." To "Find" (*Matza*) wisdom is to find "Life" (*Chayyim*) and "Favor" (*Ra-tzon*). It marks the transition to the "Choice of Feasts" in *Proverbs 9*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Eternal Blueprint." Find her and live.
