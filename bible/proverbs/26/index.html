Proverbs 26

The Mirror of Folly and the Fire of Strife.

The Cycle of the Fool

“As a dog returns to its vomit, so fools repeat their folly.” 1
“Do you see a person wise in their own eyes? There is more hope for a fool than for them.”

The Lion in the Road

“A sluggard says, ‘There’s a lion in the road, a fierce lion roaming the streets!’” 2
“As a door turns on its hinges, so a sluggard turns on his bed.”

Without Wood, the Fire Dies

“Without wood a fire goes out; without a gossip a quarrel dies down.” 3

Silver Dross

“Like a coating of silver dross on earthenware are fervent lips with an evil heart.” 4
“Whoever digs a pit will fall into it; if someone rolls a stone, it will roll back on them.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Dog returns to its Vomit (ke-Ke-leb shab al-Qe-o): In the scrolls, this is the "Regurgitative Root of Folly." A "Fool" (*Ewil*) has no "Internal Digestive Filter" for experience, "Uprooting" themselves from progress by repeating "Errors" (*I-we-leth*). It signifies "The Stagnation of the Unteachable."

2 Lion in the Road (Sha-chal ban-Da-rekh): The scrolls highlight the "Imaginative Root of Paralysis." The "Sluggard" (*Atzel*) "Roots" their "Inaction" in a "Constructed Danger." It signify "The Myth of Impossible Barriers."

3 Without a Gossip (be-Eyn Nir-gan): Literally "In the absence of the whisperer." The scrolls highlight the "Combustive Root of Social Strife." "Gossip" (*Nirgan*) is the "Fuel" (*Wood/Etzim*) that prevents the "Fire" (*Rib/Strife*) from "Extinguishing" (*Ta-sha-qot*). It signifies "Informational Arson."

4 Silver Dross on Earthenware (Ke-seph Si-gim al-Cha-res): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Deception Root." "Fervent Lips" (*Se-pha-thayim do-le-qoth*) "Root" a "Metallic Shine" over a "Fragile/Earthly" (*Chares*) "Heart" (*Leb*). It signify "The Fragility of Hypocrisy."

5 Roll back on them (e-la-iw Ta-shub): The scrolls conclude with this "Kinetic Root of Justice." Evil actions carry "Momentum" that "Uproots" the "Initiator." It marks the transition to the "Boasting of Tomorrow" in *Proverbs 27*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Moral Recoil." Be careful what you roll.
