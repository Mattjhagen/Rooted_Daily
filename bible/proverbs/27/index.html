Proverbs 27

Iron Sharpens Iron.

Do Not Boast

“Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring.” 1
“Let someone else praise you, and not your own mouth.”

Iron and Wounds

“Wounds from a friend can be trusted, but an enemy multiplies kisses.” 2
“As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another.” 3

The Mirror of Life

“As water reflects the face, so one’s life reflects the heart.” 4
“The crucible for silver and the furnace for gold, but people are tested by their praise.”

The Care of the Flock

“Be sure you know the condition of your flocks, give careful attention to your herds.” 5
“For riches do not endure forever, and a crown is not secure for all generations.”

Translation Notes

1 Do not Boast (al-Tit-hal-lel): In the scrolls, "Boasting" (*Halal*) is the "Pre-emptive Shine Root." To "Boast" about "Tomorrow" (*Yom Ma-char*) is to "Uproot" one's "Dependency" on the Present Sovereign. It signifies "Temporal Humility."

2 Wounds from a Friend (Ne-a-man-nim Pit-tzey O-heb): Literally "Faithful are the bruises of a lover." The scrolls highlight the "Healing Pain Root." A "Friend" (*Oheb*) "Roots" their "Rebuke" in "Truth" (*Aman*). It signifies "The Integrity of Conflict."

3 Iron sharpens Iron (Bar-zel be-Bar-zel ya-chad): The scrolls highlight the "Frictional Root of Growth." "One Man" (*Ish*) "Roots" the "Edge" of his "Friend" (*Re-e-hu*) through "Interaction" (*Yachad*). It signify "Mutual Calibration."

4 Life reflects the Heart (ken-Leb ha-A-dam la-A-dam): Literally "So the heart of man to man." The scrolls highlight the "Symmetric Root of Character." "Life" (*Face/Panim*) is the "Artesian Mirror" of the "Internal Root" (*Leb*). It signifies "Moral Transparency."

5 Condition of your Flocks (Ye-do-a te-da Pney Tzo-ne-kha): Literally "Knowing you shall know the face of your flock." The scrolls conclude with this "Attentional Root of Stewardship." "Prosperity" (*Riches/Crown*) is "Uprootable" without "Diligent Care" (*Attention/Leb*). It marks the transition to the "Boldness of the Righteous" in *Proverbs 28*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Attentive Life." Watch your fields.
