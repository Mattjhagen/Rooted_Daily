Proverbs 28

Boldness and the Path of Mercy.

Bold as a Lion

“The wicked flee though no one pursues, but the righteous are as bold as a lion.” 1

Confess and Renounce

“Whoever conceals their sins does not prosper, but the one who confesses and renounces them finds mercy.” 2

Chasing Fantasies

“Those who work their land will have abundant food.”
“But those who chase fantasies will have their fill of poverty.” 3

Trust in Wisdom

“Those who trust in themselves are fools.” 4
“But those who walk in wisdom are kept safe.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Bold as a Lion (u-Tzad-di-qim ke-K-phir yib-tach): Literally "But the righteous like a young lion is confident." In the scrolls, "Boldness" (*Batach*) is the "Internalized Security Root." The "Wicked" (*Ra-sha*) "Uproots" himself through "Paranoia" (*Fleeing/Nasu*), while the "Righteous" (*Tzad-di-qim*) is "Rooted" in "Identity." It signifies "Moral Courage."

2 Confesses and Renounces (u-Mo-deh we-O-zeb): The scrolls highlight the "Dual Action Root of Mercy." To "Confess" (*Modeh*) "Uproots" the "Secret," and to "Renounce" (*O-zeb/Forsake*) "Uproots" the "Practice." God "Roots" His "Mercy" (*Ru-cham*) in this "Total Realignment." It signifies "Active Repentance."

3 Chase Fantasies (u-Ma-re-deph Re-qim): Literally "The pursuer of empty things." The scrolls highlight the "Vanity Root of Poverty." To "Chase" (*Rodeph*) the "Void" (*Reqim*) "Uproots" the "Harvest" (*Sabea/Satiety*). It signify "The Cost of Distraction."

4 Trust in themselves (Bo-te-ach be-Lib-bo): Literally "Trusting in his heart." The scrolls highlight the "Auto-Centric Root of Folly." To "Root" one's "Trust" (*Batach*) in the "Self" (*Heart/Leb*) is "Folly" (*Ewil*). It signifies "Narcissistic Epistemology."

5 Walk in Wisdom (u-Ho-lekh be-Chok-mah): The scrolls conclude with this "Mobile Root of Safety." To "Walk" (*Holekh*) in "Wisdom" (*Chokmah*) is to be "Kept Safe" (*Yim-ma-let/Escape*). It marks the transition to the "Sudden Destruction" in *Proverbs 29*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Lionhearted Path." Walk bold.
