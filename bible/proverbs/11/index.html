Proverbs 11

Integrity and the Generous Soul.

Pride and Humility

“When pride comes, then comes disgrace, but with humility comes wisdom.” 1
“The integrity of the upright guides them, but the unfaithful are destroyed by their duplicity.”

The Paradox of Giving

“One person gives freely, yet gains even more; another withholds unduly, but comes to poverty.” 2
“A generous person will prosper; whoever refreshes others will be refreshed.” 3

The Snout of Discretion

“Like a gold ring in a pig’s snout is a beautiful woman who shows no discretion.” 4

Thrive Like a Leaf

“Whoever trusts in their riches will fall.”
“But the righteous will thrive like a green leaf.” 5
“The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life, and the one who is wise saves lives.”

Translation Notes

1 Pride... Disgrace (Ba-Za-don... Qa-lon): In the scrolls, "Pride" (*Zadon*) is the "Insolent Root." It "Uproots" honor and replaces it with "Shame" (*Qalon*). It signifies "The Instability of Self-Exaltation."

2 Gains even more (we-No-saph Od): The scrolls highlight the "Multiplicative Root of Charity." "Giving" (*Pazar*) "Roots" the soul in "Abundance" (*Od*). It signify "The Economy of the Spirit."

3 Refreshes others (u-Mar-weh gam-Hu yu-re): Literally "The one who waters will also be watered." The scrolls highlight the "Hydraulic Root of Kindness." What you "Root" in others will "Root" in you. It signifies "Reciprocal Grace."

4 Pig’s Snout (be-aph Cha-zir): The scrolls highlight the "Aesthetic Incongruity Root." Beauty without "Taste" (*Ta-am/Discretion*) is a "Misplaced Ornament." It signify "The Uprooting of External Value."

5 Green Leaf (ke-A-leh): The scrolls conclude with this "Vegetative Root of Life." The "Righteous" (*Tzad-di-qim*) are "Rooted" like a "Fresh Leaf" (*Aleh*). It marks the transition to the "Instruction of Discipline" in *Proverbs 12*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Thriving Soul." Flourish in the land.
