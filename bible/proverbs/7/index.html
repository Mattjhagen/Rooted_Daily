Proverbs 7

A Warning Narrative.

The Lattice Window

“At the window of my house I looked down through the lattice.”
“I saw among the simple, I noticed among the young men, a youth who had no sense.” 1

Smooth Talk

“Then out came a woman to meet him, dressed like a prostitute and with crafty intent.” 2
“With persuasive words she led him astray; she seduced him with her smooth talk.” 3

To the Slaughter

“All at once he followed her like an ox going to the slaughter.” 4
“Like a deer stepping into a noose till an arrow pierces his liver.”

Chambers of Death

“Her house is a highway to the grave, leading down to the chambers of death.” 5

Translation Notes

1 No Sense (Cha-sar Leb): Literally "Lacking heart." In the scrolls, the "Heart" (*Leb*) is the "Root of Discernment." To be "Heartless" is to be "Uprooted" from reason. It signifies "Intellectual/Moral Void."

2 Crafty Intent (ne-tzu-rath Leb): Literally "Guarded/Wily of heart." The scrolls highlight the "Hidden Root of Motive." The seductress has a "Heart" (*Leb*) that is "Walled Off" or "Fortified" for "Deception." It signifies "Premeditated Allure."

3 Smooth Talk (be-Chel-qat Spha-te-ha): Literally "With the smoothness of her lips." The scrolls highlight the "Frictionless Root of Seduction." "Smoothness" (*Chelqah*) "Uproots" the youth from the "Path of Safety." It signify "The Lure of the Easy Way."

4 Ox to the Slaughter (ke-Shor el-Tab-bach): The scrolls highlight the "Instinctual Root of Doom." The youth has lost "Human Agency" and is "Rooted" in "Animal Instinct." It signifies "The Loss of Sovereignty."

5 Chambers of Death (el-Chad-rey Ma-weth): The scrolls conclude with this "Inescapable Root of Finality." The "Highway" (*Dar-key*) leads to the "Inner Rooms" (*Chadrey*) of "Death" (*Maweth*). It marks the transition to the "Universal Invitation" of *Proverbs 8*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Fatal Attraction." Do not go near.
