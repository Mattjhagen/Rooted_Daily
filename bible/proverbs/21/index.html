Proverbs 21

The King’s Heart and the Way of Victory.

The Channel of the Heart

“In the Lord’s hand the king’s heart is a stream of water that he channels toward all who please him.” 1
“To do what is right and just is more acceptable to the Lord than sacrifice.”

The Corner of the Roof

“Better to live on a corner of the roof than share a house with a quarrelsome wife.” 2

The Pursuit of Life

“Whoever pursues righteousness and love finds life, prosperity and honor.” 3

Victory Rests with the Lord

“There is no wisdom, no insight, no plan that can succeed against the Lord.” 4
“The horse is made ready for the day of battle, but victory rests with the Lord.” 5

Translation Notes

1 King’s Heart... Stream of Water (Pel-gey-Ma-yim Leb-Me-lekh): Literally "Channels of water is the heart of the king." The scrolls highlight the "Hydraulic Root of Influence." Even the "Highest Authority" (*Melekh*) is "Rooted" in "Divine Irrigation." It signifies "The Fluidity of Power."

2 Corner of the Roof (Pin-nath-Gag): The scrolls highlight the "Survivalist Root of Peace." "Shared Space" (*Beyth Cha-ber*) "Rooted" in "Strife" (*Ma-don*) is "Uprooted" by "Isolation" (*Pinnath Gag*). It signify "The Priority of Tranquility."

3 Pursues Righteousness (Ro-deph Tze-da-qah): The scrolls highlight the "Dynamic Root of Finding." To "Chase" (*Rodeph*) "Righteousness" is to "Find" (*Matza*) "Life" (*Chayyim*). It signifies "The Momentum of Character."

4 No Wisdom against the Lord (En-Chok-mah... le-Ne-ged YHWH): Literally "No wisdom... in the presence of YHWH." The scrolls highlight the "Existential Root of Success." Any "Plan" (*Etzah*) "Uprooted" from the "Creator" is "Nullified." It signifies "Absolute Sovereignty."

5 Victory rests with the Lord (u-le-YHWH hat-Te-shu-ah): The scrolls conclude with this "Strategic Root." Human "Preparation" (*The Horse*) is "Uprooted" by the "Deliverance" (*Teshuah*) of YHWH. It marks the transition to the "Good Name" in *Proverbs 22*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Sovereign Victory." He wins the day.
