2 Samuel 22

1David sang to the LORD the words of this song when the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.
“The LORD is my rock, my fortress and my deliverer; 1
my God is my rock, in whom I take refuge, my shield and the horn of my salvation.”
7“In my distress I called to the LORD; I called out to my God. From his temple he heard my voice; my cry came to his ears.”
“The earth trembled and quaked, the foundations of the heavens shook; they trembled because he was angry.”
“He parted the heavens and came down; dark clouds were under his feet.”
“He mounted the cherubim and flew; he soared on the wings of the wind.” 2
17“He reached down from on high and took hold of me; he drew me out of deep waters.”
“As for God, his way is perfect: The LORD’s word is flawless; he shields all who take refuge in him.” 3
“He makes my feet like the feet of a deer; he causes me to stand on the heights.” 4
“The LORD lives! Praise be to my Rock! Exalted be my God, the Rock, my Savior!”
“He gives his king great victories; he shows unfailing kindness to his anointed, to David and his descendants forever.” 5

Translation Notes

1 My Rock (Sali): In the scrolls, this is the "Language of the Wilderness." David spent years hiding in the rocks of En Gedi and the caves of Adullam. For him, a "Rock" was not an abstract concept, but a literal place of "Safety" and "Defense." He is saying that God is his "Ultimate Hiding Place."

2 Wings of the Wind: This section in the scrolls is a "Theophany"—a manifestation of God. David uses "Cosmic Language" to describe God’s intervention. He is saying that when a child of God cries out in distress, the "Creator of the Universe" bends the heavens to come to their aid. It is the "Macro" responding to the "Micro."

3 Flawless Word (Tzerufah): Literally in the scrolls, "Refined by Fire." God’s promise is like "Pure Gold" that has been tested in the furnace. David had seen many human promises fail (Saul, Absalom), but God’s word had remained "Steady" through every storm.

4 Feet of a Deer (Ayyalot): This refers to the ability to navigate "Precarious Heights" with "Perfect Balance." In the scrolls, David is celebrating how God gave him the "Stability" to stay on the throne despite the constant "Slip-ups" of his own family and enemies. It is "Supernatural Agility" for a "Difficult Path."

5 Unfailing Kindness (Hesed): The song ends on the "Covenant Word." David is not boasting in his own strength, but in God’s *Hesed*—His "Loyal, Unbreakable Love." By mentioning his "Descendants forever," David is looking ahead to the "Eternal Kingdom" promised in Chapter 7. This song is the "Grand Finale" of David’s spiritual journey.
