Nehemiah 4

The Mockery of Rubble

Sanballat said: “What are those feeble Jews doing? Will they restore their wall? Will they finish in a day?” 1
“Tobiah added: ‘If even a fox climbed on it, he would break down their wall!’”

The Prayer and the Post

“But we prayed to our God and posted a guard day and night to meet this threat.” 2

The Sword and the Trowel

“Those who carried materials did their work with one hand and held a weapon in the other.” 3
“Each of the builders wore his sword at his side as he worked.”

The Rallying Cry

“Wherever you hear the sound of the trumpet, join us there. Our God will fight for us!” 4
“Neither I nor my brothers... took off our clothes; each had his weapon, even when he went for water.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Feeble Jews (ha-Yehudim ha-Amelalim): Literally in the scrolls, "The withered or drooping Jews." Sanballat was attacking their "Identity" and their "Capacity." He saw only the "Rubble" (*Heaps*), but Nehemiah saw the "Resources of God." It is a "Rooted" lesson that enemies always mock the "Smallness" of the work before they fear its "Strength."

2 Prayed... and Posted (Na-Abod u-Na’amid): This is the "Chronicler’s Balance." They didn't just "Wait" (Passive) and they didn't just "Watch" (Secular). They "Integrated" their "Spiritual Petition" with "Practical Protection." It is the "Rooted" theology of "Work as Worship."

3 Work with one Hand (Be-Yath-o): In the scrolls, this is a "Visual Masterpiece of Dedication." The building was so "Urgent" and the danger so "Great" that they became "Ambidextrous in the Kingdom." They were "Builders" and "Soldiers" simultaneously. It signifies that the "Restoration of Zion" always requires a "Defensive Edge."

4 Our God will Fight for Us: The scrolls record this "Rallying Cry." The "Trumpet" was the "Signal of Unity." Even though the workers were "Separated" along the wall (*Verse 19*), they were "Rooted" in the same "Defender." The trumpet "Collapsed the Distance" between the workers.

5 Even when he went for Water: Literally in the scrolls, "Each his weapon [even at] the water." They stayed in a "State of Constant Readiness." They didn't even "Un-root" their weapons from their hands to sleep or drink. It marks the "Extreme Vigilance" required to finish the wall in 52 days.
