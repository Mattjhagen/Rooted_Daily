Nehemiah 9

The Israelites gathered together, fasting and wearing sackcloth... they stood and confessed their sins and the iniquities of their ancestors. 1

The Grand Review

“You alone are the LORD. You made the heavens... You give life to everything.”
“But you are a forgiving God, gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and abounding in love. Therefore you did not desert them.” 2
“But in your great mercy you did not put an end to them or abandon them, for you are a gracious and merciful God.”

The Current Plight

“See, we are slaves today, slaves in the land you gave our ancestors... because of our sins, its abundant harvest goes to the kings you have placed over us.” 3

The Binding Agreement

“In view of all this, we are making a binding agreement, putting it in writing, and our leaders are affixing their seals to it.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Confessed their Sins (Way-Yith-waddu): In the scrolls, this is the "Root of True Return." After the "Joy" of Chapter 8, they had to deal with the "Grief" of their history. They were "Uprooting" the generational patterns of rebellion that had led to the Exile.

2 Forgiving God (Elah Selichot): Literally in the scrolls, "A God of Pardons." This is the "Center of the Prayer." Despite the "Stiff-necked" nature of Israel (*Verse 16*), God’s "Character" remained "Gracious." The prayer is not "Rooted" in Israel’s performance, but in God’s "Attributes."

3 We are Slaves Today (Abadim Anachnu): This is the "Sobering Reality" in the scrolls. Even though they had a "Wall" and a "Temple," they were still under "Persian Rule." They were "Slaves" in their own "Promised Land." It was a "Rooted" admission that "Physical Restoration" is not the same as "Spiritual Liberty."

4 Binding Agreement (Amanah): In the scrolls, this is the "Legal Response" to the prayer. They weren't just "Feeling Sorry"; they were "Signing a Contract." It signifies the "Institutionalizing of Repentance." They were "Rooting" their future in a "Document of Commitment."
