Nehemiah 12

The Clean City

“When the priests and Levites had purified themselves, they purified the people, the gates and the wall.” 1

The Two Choirs

“I had the leaders of Judah go up on top of the wall. I also assigned two large choirs to give thanks.” 2
“The two choirs that gave thanks then took their places in the house of God.” 3

The Sound of Victory

“And on that day they offered great sacrifices, rejoicing because God had given them great joy.” 4
“The sound of rejoicing in Jerusalem could be heard far away.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Purified the Wall (Way-Yithaharu): In the scrolls, this is a "Sobering Ceremony." The wall was not just a "Military Structure"; it was a "Holy Perimeter." They were "Rooting" the stones in the "Purity of God." They realized that a "Pure Wall" cannot protect a "Defiled People."

2 Two Large Choirs (Shete Thodoth Gedoloth): Literally in the scrolls, "Two Large Thanksgivings." They marched in opposite directions on top of the wall. It was a "Spiritual Circumambulation." They were "Reclaiming the Air" above the ruins with the "Music of David." It is the "Rooted" climax of the physical rebuilding.

3 Took their Places: The two processions started at different points and met at the Temple. It signifies that all "Spiritual Effort" must find its "Center" in the Presence. The "Wall" was built to protect the "Worship."

4 God had Given them Great Joy (Simchah Gedolah): The scrolls highlight the "Source of Emotion." Their joy wasn't "Self-Generated" because of their hard work, but "Divinely Granted" as a reward for their "Obedience." It proves that "Great Joy" is the "Fruit" of "Great Consecration."

5 Heard Far Away (Me-Rachoq): This is the "Public Testimony" of the scrolls. The "Mockery" of the enemies (*Chapter 4*) was drowned out by the "Music" of the Remnant. The "Sound of Zion" was a "Warning" to the neighbors that the "Root" was now "Secure and Flourishing."
