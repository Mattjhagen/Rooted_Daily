Nehemiah 6

The Great Project

“I am carrying on a great project and cannot go down. Why should the work stop while I leave it and go down to you?” 1

The Open Letter

“It is reported... that you and the Jews are plotting a revolt... and according to these reports you are about to become their king.” 2
“I sent him this reply: ‘Nothing like what you are saying is happening; you are just making it up out of your head.’”

The Wall Completed

“So the wall was completed on the twenty-fifth of Elul, in fifty-two days.” 3
“They lost their self-confidence, because they realized that this work had been done with the help of our God.” 4
“In those days the nobles of Judah were sending many letters to Tobiah...” 5

Translation Notes

1 Great Project (Melakah Gedolah): In the scrolls, this is the "Focus of the Finisher." Nehemiah didn't see the "Plain of Ono" as a "Diplomatic Opportunity" but as a "Distraction." He was "Rooted" in the task. His "Cannot go down" was a "Spiritual Refusal" to lower his standards for the sake of a false peace.

2 Making it up (Bedo): Literally in the scrolls, "Devising it from your heart." Nehemiah recognized the "Open Letter" as a "Fabrication." It was an attempt to use "Fear of the Empire" to stop the "Work of the Kingdom." He met the "Lies" with a "Blunt Denial."

3 Fifty-two Days: This is a "Miraculous Timeline" in the scrolls. Rebuilding the massive walls of Jerusalem in less than two months was "Humanly Impossible." It signifies the "Momentum of Unity." The "Root" had finally produced a "Fast-growing Hedge" of protection around the city.

4 Lost their Self-confidence (Way-Yipplu be-Ene-hem): Literally in the scrolls, "They fell in their own eyes." The "Mockers" were "Silenced." They realized that the "Jews" weren't just "Feeble" workers, but "Partners with the Almighty." The "Success of the Wall" was a "Sermon" to the nations.

5 Nobles... sending Letters: The scrolls conclude with this "Dark Undercurrent." Even with the wall finished, the "Internal Compromise" remained. The "Nobles" were "Related by Marriage" to Tobiah the enemy. It proves that a "Physical Wall" is only effective if there is a "Spiritual Boundary" in the hearts of the leaders.
