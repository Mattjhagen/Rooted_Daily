Nehemiah 3

Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests went to work and rebuilt the Sheep Gate. 1

The Community Effort

“Uzziel son of Harhaiah, one of the goldsmiths, repaired a section; and Hananiah, one of the perfume-makers, made repairs next to that.” 2

The Daughters

“Shallum son of Hallohesh... repaired the next section with the help of his daughters.” 3

The Warning of the Unwilling

“The next section was repaired by the men of Tekoa, but their nobles would not put their shoulders to the work.” 4

Next To Him

“Above the Horse Gate, the priests made repairs, each in front of his own house.” 5

Translation Notes

1 High Priest Rebuilt (Way-Yibnu): In the scrolls, it is significant that the "Spiritual Leader" led the physical labor. By building the Sheep Gate (where the sacrificial animals entered), Eliashib was "Rooting" the wall project in the life of the Temple. He wasn't too "Holy" to handle stone and mortar.

2 Goldsmiths and Perfume-Makers: The scrolls highlight the "Eclectic Unity" of the workforce. Men who were used to delicate work (*Gold and Perfume*) were now lifting heavy beams. It signifies that in the "Restoration of Zion," every "Skill" is reassigned to the "Covenant Cause."

3 With the help of his Daughters (u-Benoth-av): This is a "Beautiful Exception" in the scrolls. In a culture of male labor, Shallum’s daughters stepped in to build the wall. It proves that the "Call to Restore" is inclusive. The "Holy Seed" was being planted by both sons and daughters.

4 Nobles would not put their Shoulders: Literally in the scrolls, "They did not bring their necks to the service." They considered the work "Beneath Them." It is the only "Negative Note" in the chapter, serving as a "Rooted" warning that "Status" often becomes a "Barrier" to the work of God.

5 In front of his own House (Neged Beith-o): This is the "Key Strategy" of Nehemiah in the scrolls. By assigning people to build "In front of their own houses," he ensured they had a "Personal Stake" in the quality of the work. If the wall failed there, their own families would be the first to suffer. It is the "Rooted" principle of "Local Responsibility."
