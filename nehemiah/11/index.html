Nehemiah 11

Now the leaders of the people settled in Jerusalem. The rest of the people cast lots to bring one out of every ten of them to live in Jerusalem, the holy city. 1
“The people commended all who volunteered to live in Jerusalem.” 2

The Guardians of the City

“From the descendants of Judah: Athaiah son of Uzziah...”
“From the descendants of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam...”

The Servants of Praise

“Mattaniah... the director who led in thanksgiving and prayer.” 3

The Rest of Israel

“The rest of the Israelites, with the priests and Levites, were in all the towns of Judah, each on their ancestral property.” 4
“Jerusalem, the Holy City.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Cast Lots (Goralot): In the scrolls, this is the "Sacrifice of Residency." Jerusalem was a "Target" for enemies and still in "Ruins" internally. Living there was a "Hardship." The lot was the "Sovereign Draft" to ensure the "Holy City" had a "Living Remnant." It signifies that "God’s Plan" often requires "Personal Relocation."

2 Commended the Volunteers (ha-Mitnadbim): Literally in the scrolls, "They blessed those who offered themselves willingly." While some were drafted by lot, others chose the "Hardship" for the sake of the "Kingdom." They were the "Rooted Volunteers" who prioritized "Zion’s Welfare" over "Ancestral Comfort."

3 Director of Thanksgiving (Rosh ha-Techillah): Literally in the scrolls, "The Head of the Beginning." Mattaniah’s role was to "Launch the Praise." It proves that the "Repopulation" was not just about "Demographics," but about "Atmosphere." A city without "Thanksgiving" is just a "Collection of Buildings."

4 Ancestral Property (Nachalatho): The scrolls highlight the "Rooted Connection" to the land. While Jerusalem was the "Center," the "Towns" were the "Support System." Every family was "Back in their Place," fulfilling the promise that they would return to their "Heritage."

5 The Holy City (Ir ha-Qodesh): This is the first time in the scrolls that Jerusalem is explicitly called the "Holy City." It signifies a "Shift in Status." Now that the "Wall" is finished and the "People" are returned, the city is no longer a "Disgrace" (*Chapter 1*), but a "Sanctuary." It is the "Rooted" goal of the entire book.
