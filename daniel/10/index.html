Daniel 10

The Man of Lightning and the Conflict of Princes.

Three Weeks of Mourning

“I ate no choice food; no meat or wine touched my lips.” 1

Appearance like Lightning

“His body was like topaz, his face like lightning.”
“His eyes were like flaming torches.” 2

Prince of Persia

“The prince of the Persian kingdom resisted me twenty-one days.” 3
“Then Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me.” 4

The Future of Your People

“I have come to explain what will happen to your people in the future.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Choice Food (le-chem cha-mu-dhōth): Literally "Bread of desires." The scrolls highlight the "Disciplined Root of the Soul." "Self-Indulgence" is "Uprooted." Daniel "Roots" his "Focus" in "Mourning." It signifies "The Aesthetic of Spiritual Preparation."

2 Flaming Torches (lap-pī-dhē ēsh): The scrolls highlight the "Illuminating Root of the Celestial Being." "Obscurity" is "Uprooted." The "Eyes" (*Enaw*) are "Rooted" in "Fire" (*Esh*). It signify "The Divine Scrutiny of the Messenger."

3 Prince of the Persian Kingdom (sar mal-khūth Pa-rās): The scrolls highlight the "Territorial Root of Spiritual Conflict." "Peace" is "Uprooted" in the "Heavenlies." The "Sar" (*Prince*) is "Rooted" in "Resistance" (*Amad*). It signifies "The Angelic Dimension of World Empires."

4 Michael (Mī-khā-ēl): Literally "Who is like God?" The scrolls highlight the "Protective Root of Israel." "Vulnerability" is "Uprooted." Michael is "Rooted" in the "Defense" of the "Prophet." It signify "The Hierarchy of Heaven."

5 In the Future (be-a-cha-rīth hay-yā-mīm): Literally "In the end of the days." The scrolls conclude with this "Teleological Root of the Vision." "Immediate Concern" is "Uprooted." It marks the transition to "The Kings of the North and South" in *Daniel 11*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Radiant Man." The conflict is revealed.
