Daniel 6

The Lions’ Den and the Windows toward Jerusalem.

An Excellent Spirit

“Daniel so distinguished himself by his exceptional qualities that the king planned to set him over the whole kingdom.” 1

Toward Jerusalem

“Three times a day he got down on his knees and prayed, giving thanks to his God.” 2

The Pit of Lions

“They brought Daniel and threw him into the lions' den.” 3

Shut Mouths

“My God sent his angel, and he shut the mouths of the lions.” 4
“He is the living God and he endures forever.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Exceptional Qualities (rū-ach yat-tī-rā): Literally "A surpassing spirit." The scrolls highlight the "Pneumatic Root of Excellence." Daniel is "Rooted" in a "Spirit" (*Ruach*) that "Uproots" the "Corruption" of the satraps. It signifies "The Superhuman Integrity of the Prophet."

2 Toward Jerusalem (ne-ghadh Ye-rū-she-lem): The scrolls highlight the "Oriented Root of Devotion." "Babylonian Influence" is "Uprooted." Daniel’s "Prayer" (*Tzelah*) is "Rooted" in the "Direction" of the "Sanctuary." It signify "The Aesthetic of Constant Faith."

3 Lions' Den (gub-bā dī a-ryā-wā-thā): The scrolls highlight the "Predatory Root of the Crisis." "Safety" is "Uprooted." Daniel is "Rooted" in the "Pit" (*Gubba*) of "Lions." It signifies "The Ultimate Test of Divine Protection."

4 Shut the Mouths (śe-ghadh pum a-ryā-wā-thā): The scrolls highlight the "Inhibitory Root of the Miracle." The "Lions' Nature" is "Uprooted" by the "Angel." It signify "The Divine Control over the Wild."

5 Living God (E-lā-hā chay-yā): The scrolls conclude with this "Vital Root of Acknowledgment." "Dead Idols" are "Uprooted." Darius "Roots" the "God of Daniel" as "The One who Endures." It marks the transition to "The Four Beasts" in *Daniel 7*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Lions' Den." He saves and delivers.
