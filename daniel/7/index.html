Daniel 7

The Sea of Beasts and the Throne of the Ancient of Days.

Up from the Sea

“Four great beasts, each different from the others, came up out of the sea.” 1

The Iron Teeth

“Terrifying and frightening and very powerful. It had large iron teeth.” 2

The Ancient of Days

“Thrones were set in place, and the Ancient of Days took his seat.” 3
“A river of fire was flowing, coming out from before him.”

One Like a Son of Man

“Coming with the clouds of heaven, there before me was one like a son of man.” 4
“His dominion is an everlasting dominion.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Out of the Sea (min-yam-mā): The scrolls highlight the "Chaotic Root of the Empires." "Order" is "Uprooted." The "Beasts" (*Cheyvan*) are "Rooted" in the "Great Sea" (*Yamma*), representing the turbulent nations. It signifies "The Aesthetic of Predatory Power."

2 Iron Teeth (shin-na-yin dī-phar-zel): The scrolls highlight the "Mechanical Root of Destruction." "Mercy" is "Uprooted." The "Fourth Beast" is "Rooted" in "Iron" (*Pharzel*). It signify "The Severity of the Final Human Empire."

3 Ancient of Days (at-tīq yō-mīn): Literally "The Advanced in Days." The scrolls highlight the "Eternal Root of the Judge." "Transience" is "Uprooted." God is "Rooted" in "Age" (*Attiq*). It signifies "The Absolute Authority of the Court of Heaven."

4 Son of Man (bar e-nāsh): The scrolls highlight the "Representative Root of the Kingdom." "Monstrosity" is "Uprooted." The "True King" is "Rooted" in "Humanity" (*Enash*) rather than "Beastliness." It signify "The Divine Intervention in the Clouds."

5 Everlasting Dominion (shol-tān ā-lam): The scrolls conclude with this "Permanent Root of Sovereignty." "Terminal Power" is "Uprooted." The "Kingdom" is "Rooted" in "Eternity" (*Alam*). It marks the transition to "The Ram and the Goat" in *Daniel 8*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Son of Man." All nations shall serve him.
