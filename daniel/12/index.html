Daniel 12

The Great Distress and the Shining of the Stars.

Time of Distress

“There will be a time of distress such as has not happened from the beginning of nations.” 1

Awakening from the Dust

“Multitudes who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake.” 2

Shining like Stars

“Those who are wise will shine like the brightness of the heavens.” 3
“Like the stars for ever and ever.”

Seal the Scroll

“Seal the words of the scroll until the time of the end.” 4

Daniel’s Rest

“You will rise to receive your allotted inheritance.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Time of Distress (ēth tzā-rāh): The scrolls highlight the "Compressive Root of the End." "Comfort" is "Uprooted." The "Time" (*Eth*) is "Rooted" in "Distress" (*Tzarah*). It signifies "The Spiritual Labor Pains of the Kingdom."

2 Sleep in the Dust (ye-shē-nē adh-math ā-phār): The scrolls highlight the "Inert Root of Mortality." "Life" is "Uprooted" by "Death," but "Death" is "Uprooted" by "Awakening" (*Qitz*). It signify "The Aesthetic of the First Resurrection."

3 Shine (yiz-hi-rū): The scrolls highlight the "Luminous Root of Wisdom." "Darkness" is "Uprooted." The "Wise" (*Maskilim*) are "Rooted" in "Brightness" (*Zohar*). It signifies "The Eternal Reward of Righteous Influence."

4 Seal the Scroll (cha-thōm has-sē-pher): The scrolls highlight the "Confidential Root of the Prophecy." "Full Disclosure" is "Uprooted" until the "Time of the End" (*Eth Qetz*). It signify "The Preservation of Mystery for the Final Generation."

5 Allotted Inheritance (le-ghō-rā-le-khā): Literally "To your lot." The scrolls conclude with this "Restful Root of the Prophet." "Labor" is "Uprooted." Daniel is "Rooted" in "Rest" (*Nuach*) and then in his "Inheritance." It marks the end of *Daniel* and the transition to *Hosea*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The End of Days." Rise and receive.
