Daniel 9

The Prayer of Sackcloth and the Seventy Sevens.

The Plea for Mercy

“I pleaded with him in prayer and petition, in fasting, and in sackcloth and ashes.” 1

We Have Sinned

“We have sinned and done wrong. We have been wicked and have rebelled.” 2

Seventy Sevens

“Seventy ‘sevens’ are decreed for your people and your holy city.” 3

The Anointed One

“Until the Anointed One, the ruler, comes.” 4
“The Anointed One will be put to death and will have nothing.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Fasting and Sackcloth (be-tzōm ū-be-śaq): The scrolls highlight the "Ascetic Root of Intercession." "Comfort" is "Uprooted." Daniel "Roots" his "Appeal" in "Sorrow" (*Saq*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Desperate Humility."

2 We have Sinned (chā-tā-nū): The scrolls highlight the "Collective Root of Failure." "Personal Innocence" is "Uprooted" as Daniel identifies with the "Nation." They have "Rooted" themselves in "Rebellion" (*Marad*). It signify "The Honesty of True Confession."

3 Seventy Sevens (shā-bhu-īm shibh-īm): The scrolls highlight the "Mathematical Root of Prophecy." "Time" is "Uprooted" from the "Ordinary" and "Rooted" in a "Sacred Calendar." It signifies "The Divine Countdown to Restoration."

4 Anointed One (Mā-shī-ach): The scrolls highlight the "Messianic Root of the Vision." "Atheism" is "Uprooted." The "King" (*Nagid*) is "Rooted" in "Anointing" (*Mashach*). It signify "The Arrival of the Promised Deliverer."

5 Put to Death (yik-kā-rēth): Literally "Cut off." The scrolls conclude with this "Sacrificial Root of the Messiah." "Success" is "Uprooted" by "Death." It marks the transition to "The Man in the Vision" in *Daniel 10*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Seventy Weeks." The covenant is confirmed.
