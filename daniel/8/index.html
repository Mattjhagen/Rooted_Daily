Daniel 8

The Prominent Horn and the Sanctuary of Evenings and Mornings.

The Goat from the West

“A goat with a prominent horn came from the west, crossing the whole earth without touching the ground.” 1

The Broken Horn

“It struck the ram and shattered its two horns.”
“At the height of its power the large horn was broken off.” 2

Magnified against the Host

“It set itself up to be as great as the commander of the army of the Lord.” 3

2,300 Evenings and Mornings

“Then the sanctuary will be reconsecrated.” 4
“Gabriel, tell this man the meaning of the vision.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Without Touching the Ground (ēn nō-ghē-a bā-ā-retz): The scrolls highlight the "Velocity Root of the Empire." "Gravity" is "Uprooted." The "Goat" (*Tzaphir*) is "Rooted" in "Speed" (*Flight*). It signifies "The Rapid Expansion of the Greek Conquest."

2 Large Horn was Broken (nish-be-rā haq-qe-ren): The scrolls highlight the "Fragile Root of the Hero." "Greatness" is "Uprooted" at its "Peak." It signify "The Abrupt End of Alexander’s Life."

3 Commander of the Army (sar-hatz-tzā-bhā): The scrolls highlight the "Antagonistic Root of the Horn." "Humility" is "Uprooted." The "Horn" is "Rooted" in "Insolence" against the "Sar" (*Prince/Commander*). It signifies "The Spiritual Warfare of the Antiochus Figure."

4 2,300 Evenings and Mornings (e-rebh bō-qer al-pa-yim ū-she-lōsh mē-ōth): The scrolls highlight the "Temporal Root of the Desolation." "Sacrifice" is "Uprooted." The "Cleansing" (*Nitzdaq*) is "Rooted" in this specific "Duration." It signify "The Aesthetic of a Counted Sorrow."

5 Gabriel (Gabh-rī-ēl): Literally "Man of God" or "God is my Strength." The scrolls conclude with this "Angelic Root of Instruction." "Confusion" is "Uprooted" by "Gabriel." It marks the transition to "The Seventy Weeks" in *Daniel 9*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Goat’s Horn." The vision is for the end time.
