2 Kings 10

When the letter arrived, these men took the king’s sons and slaughtered all seventy of them. They put their heads in baskets and sent them to Jehu... 1
“He ordered, ‘Pile them in two heaps at the entrance of the city gate until morning.’”
Jehu said to Jehonadab, “Are you in accord with me, as I am with you?” “I am,” Jehonadab answered.
“Come with me and see my zeal for the LORD.” 2

The Trap of Baal

Then Jehu brought all the people together and said to them, “Ahab served Baal a little; Jehu will serve him much.” 3
“As soon as Jehu had finished making the offering, he ordered the guards... ‘Go in and kill them! Let no one escape!’”
“They demolished the temple of Baal and used it for a public latrine to this day.” 4

The Partial Reformation

The LORD said to Jehu, “Because you have done well... your descendants will sit on the throne of Israel to the fourth generation.”
Yet Jehu was not careful to keep the law of the LORD... with all his heart. He did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam. 5

Translation Notes

1 Heaps of Heads: In the scrolls, this is a "Macabre Declaration of Fulfillment." By piling the heads at the gate, Jehu was making the "House of Ahab" a "Public Warning." He was forcing the people to see that the "Word of the LORD" (Chapter 21) was not a metaphor. It was a "Bloody Finality."

2 My Zeal (Qin’ati): Jehu’s zeal in the scrolls was a "Double-Edged Sword." While it was effective for "Destruction," it was not rooted in "Devotion." He was a "Political Surgeon" who cut out the cancer but didn't know how to "Nurture" the life. His alliance with the Rekhabites (a group known for their strict devotion) was an attempt to gain "Religious Legitimacy."

3 Ahab served Baal a little: In the scrolls, this is "Holy Deception." Jehu used the very "Language of Idolatry" to gather the idolaters together. It was a "Reverse Contrapasso"—he gathered them into their "Temple of Safety" to make it their "Tomb of Judgment."

4 Public Latrine (Le-Motza'ot): This is the "Ultimate Defilement" in the scrolls. By turning the Temple of Baal into a sewer, Jehu was saying that Baal was "Useless" and "Vile." It was a "Theological Insult" that could never be erased. It signaled the "Total Rejection" of Phoenician influence in Israel.

5 Sins of Jeroboam: This is the "Tragedy of Jehu" in the scrolls. He destroyed "Baal" (the foreign god) but he kept the "Calves" (the convenient gods). He was a "Reformer" who stopped "Halfway." Because he didn't follow the LORD with "All his Heart," his dynasty was limited to four generations. He cleaned the "Outside of the Cup," but the "Inside" remained stained with Jeroboam’s pragmatism.
