2 Kings 7

1Elisha said, “Hear the word of the LORD. This is what the LORD says: About this time tomorrow, a seah of the finest flour will sell for a shekel... at the gate of Samaria.” 1
The officer said, “Look, even if the LORD should open the floodgates of the heavens, could this happen?” “You will see it with your own eyes,” Elisha answered, “but you will not eat any of it.” 2

The Four at the Gate

Now there were four men with leprosy at the entrance of the city gate. They said to each other, “Why stay here until we die?” 3
“We’re not doing right. This is a day of good news and we are keeping it to ourselves.” 4
For the LORD had made the Arameans hear the sound of chariots and horses and a great army...
So they got up and fled in the twilight and abandoned their tents and their horses and donkeys. They left the camp as it was and ran for their lives.

The Word Fulfilled

Then the people went out and plundered the camp of the Arameans. So a seah of the finest flour sold for a shekel... 5
“The people trampled him in the gateway, and he died.”

Translation Notes

1 About this Time Tomorrow: In the scrolls, this is the "Immediacy of God." The city was at its "Breaking Point" (Chapter 6), and God’s answer was not a "Process" but an "Event." A "Shekel for a Seah" was a 100-fold drop in price, representing a "Supernatural Market Correction."

2 Floodgates of Heaven (Arubbot): This was the officer’s "Cynical Sarcasm." He used the same word used in the Flood of Noah (Genesis 7). He was mocking the "Possibility of Grace." His judgment was a "Poetic Justice"—he would see the "Evidence" but be "Excluded" from the "Benefit" because of his unbelief.

3 Why stay here until we Die: This is the "Logic of the Outcast" in the scrolls. The "Leprous Men" were the first to move because they had the "Least to Lose." God used the "Socially Dead" to bring life back to the city. Their "Hopelessness" became the "Gateway to Hope" for everyone else.

4 Day of Good News (Yom Besorah): This is the "Moral Turning Point" in the scrolls. The lepers realized that "Grace" is not for "Hoarding." If you find "Bread," you have a "Responsibility" to the "Starving." It is the "Rooted" principle of the Gospel—the discovered must become the messenger.

5 Plundered the Camp: In the scrolls, the Arameans fled because of a "Ghost Army"—a "Supernatural Sound." God defeated them without a single Israelite drawing a sword. The "Provision" was already there, waiting in the darkness. The city didn't need a "Crop"; it needed a "Discovery."
