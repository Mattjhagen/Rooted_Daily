2 Kings 19

Hezekiah received the letter from the messengers and read it. Then he went up to the temple of the LORD and spread it out before the LORD. 1
“Now, LORD our God, deliver us from his hand, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that you alone, LORD, are God.” 2

The Answer of Isaiah

“Because you rage against me... I will put my hook in your nose and my bit in your lips, and I will make you return by the way you came.” 3
“Once more a remnant of the kingdom of Judah will take root below and bear fruit above.” 4

The Night of the Angel

That night the angel of the LORD went out and put to death a hundred and eighty-five thousand in the Assyrian camp. 5
“So Sennacherib king of Assyria broke camp and withdrew.”
“One day, while he was worshipping in the temple of his god Nisroch, his sons... killed him with the sword.”

Translation Notes

1 Spread it out (Way-Yipreshehu): In the scrolls, this is a "Visual Prayer." Hezekiah didn't just talk to God; he "showed" God the evidence of the enemy’s pride. He was presenting the "Legal Case" for divine intervention. It is the "Rooted" practice of bringing the "Hard Facts" into the "Holy Presence."

2 So that All the Kingdoms may Know: This was Hezekiah’s "Motive" in the scrolls. He wasn't just asking for "Survival," but for "Vindication." He wanted the world to see that there is a God who is not "Limited" like the idols of the nations. It is a "Global Mission" prayer in the middle of a "National Siege."

3 Hook in your Nose: This is a "Sarcastic Reversal" in the scrolls. The Assyrians were famous for putting hooks in the noses of their captives to lead them away. God is telling Sennacherib that he, the "Great Conqueror," is nothing but a "Captive Animal" in God’s hand. He would be led back to Nineveh in "Humiliation."

4 Take Root Below (Sheresh le-Mata): This is the "Promise of Renewal" in the scrolls. After the "Trauma of the Siege," the nation was like a stump. God promised that they would "Root" again. It is a "Biological Metaphor" for "Spiritual Recovery"—the internal strength (roots) will lead to external productivity (fruit).

5 A Hundred and Eighty-Five Thousand: This "Massive Destruction" in the scrolls was an "Instant Judgment." In a single night, the "Invincible Machine" was broken. It was not a "Military Victory" of Israel, but a "Direct Strike" from Heaven. It stands as the "Greatest Deliverance" in the history of the Kingdom of Judah.
