2 Kings 17

In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and deported the Israelites to Assyria. 1

The Theological Autopsy

“All this took place because the Israelites had sinned against the LORD their God... they worshipped other gods.”
“The LORD warned Israel and Judah through every prophet and seer: ‘Turn from your evil ways!’”
“But they would not listen and were as stiff-necked as their ancestors.” 2
“They followed worthless idols and became worthless themselves.” 3
“The LORD was very angry with Israel and removed them from his presence. Only the tribe of Judah was left.” 4

The Confusion of Samaria

“They worshipped the LORD, but they also served their own gods in accordance with the customs of the nations from which they had been brought.” 5
“To this day their children and grandchildren continue to do as their ancestors did.”

Translation Notes

1 Deported the Israelites: In the scrolls, this is the "Un-Promising" of the land. 700 years after the Exodus, the Northern Kingdom was returned to a state of "Displaced Persons." They were moved to the "Cities of the Medes"—the periphery of the known world—marking the end of the Ten Tribes as a sovereign political entity.

2 Stiff-necked (Qishu Orpam): Literally in the scrolls, "Hardened their necks." It’s an agricultural metaphor for an ox that refuses to be guided by the yoke. Israel had become "Un-teachable," rejecting the "Gentle Nudges" of the prophets until the "Heavy Hammer" of Assyria was the only thing left.

3 Followed Worthless Idols (Havel): In the scrolls, the word for idol is *Havel* (Breath/Vanity/Vapor). The "Pun" is that those who worship "Vapor" become "Vapor" themselves. Their national identity had no "Substance" (*Kabod*) because it wasn't rooted in the "Substantial Reality" of God.

4 Removed from His Presence (Va-Yasirem me-al Panav): Literally, "He turned them away from His face." This is the "Spiritual Definition of Exile." It wasn't just a "Geography Shift," it was a "Relational Severance." The North had "Turned their Back" for so long that God finally "Turned His Face." Only Judah remained as the "Last Custodian" of the Lamp.

5 Served their Own Gods: The scrolls describe the "Origin of the Samaritans." It was a "Syncretistic Disaster"—trying to worship the "God of the Land" (Yahweh) to avoid the lions, while keeping the "Gods of their Fathers." It is the "Rooted" warning against "Insurance-Policy Religion," where God is just one of many options used for "Survival" rather than "Sovereignty."
