2 Kings 2

Elijah said to Elisha, “Stay here; the LORD has sent me to Bethel.” But Elisha said, “As surely as the LORD lives and as you live, I will not leave you.” 1
“Please let me inherit a double portion of your spirit.” 2

The Chariots of Fire

Suddenly a chariot of fire and horses of fire appeared and separated the two of them, and Elijah went up to heaven in a whirlwind. 3
“My father! My father! The chariots and horsemen of Israel!” 4
13Elisha then picked up Elijah’s cloak that had fallen from him and went back and stood on the bank of the Jordan.
“Where now is the LORD, the God of Elijah?” 5
“This is what the LORD says: ‘I have healed this water. Never again will it cause death...’”

Translation Notes

1 I will not Leave You: In the scrolls, this is a "Test of Loyalty." Elisha was being offered "Permission" to quit, but he chose "Persistence." He was proving that he was "Rooted" in the call of God, not just following a man. His refusal to leave was his "Credential" for succession.

2 Double Portion (Pi-Shnayim): This was the "Right of the Firstborn" in the scrolls (Deuteronomy 21). Elisha was not asking to be "Twice as Great" as Elijah, but to be recognized as Elijah’s "Legitimate Heir" and "Lead Successor" among the prophets. He was asking for the "Official Spirit" of the office.

3 Chariot of Fire (Rekhev Esh): This is the "Most Famous Translation" in the scrolls. It signifies that Elijah did not die; he was "Changed" and "Removed." The fire and whirlwind show that the "Presence of God" which had met Elijah in the "Whisper" (1 Kings 19) was now his "Vehicle of Departure."

4 Chariots and Horsemen: In the scrolls, this was Elisha’s "Recognition." He was saying that Elijah himself was the "Real Defense" of Israel—more powerful than any physical army. This same phrase would later be used of Elisha himself (Chapter 13).

5 Where is the God of Elijah: This was the "First Act of the Successor" in the scrolls. Elisha was testing whether the "Power" was tied to the "Person" or the "Office." By striking the water and seeing it divide, he (and the other prophets) knew that the "Covenant of Power" had successfully transferred. The mantle had changed shoulders, but the God remained the same.
