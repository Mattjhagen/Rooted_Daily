2 Kings 11

1When Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she proceeded to destroy the whole royal family. 1
But Jehosheba... took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away... She put him and his nurse in a bedroom to hide him from Athaliah; so he was not killed. 2
“He remained hidden with his nurse at the temple of the LORD for six years while Athaliah ruled the land.”

The Restoration of David

Jehoiada brought out the king’s son and put the crown on him; he presented him with a copy of the covenant and proclaimed him king. They anointed him, clapped their hands and shouted, “Long live the king!” 3
When Athaliah heard the noise... she went to the people at the temple.
“She saw the king standing by the pillar... She tore her robes and shouted, ‘Treason! Treason!’” 4

The Renewal

Jehoiada then made a covenant between the LORD and the king and people that they would be the LORD’s people. 5
“Joash was seven years old when he began to reign.”

Translation Notes

1 Destroy the Royal Family: In the scrolls, Athaliah (the daughter of Ahab and Jezebel) was attempting to do in the South what Jehu was doing in the North: "Extinguishing a Dynasty." Her goal was to permanently break the "Covenant with David" and replace it with the "Cult of Baal." She was the "Anti-Covenant" incarnate.

2 Jehosheba Stole Him: This was the "Pivot of History" in the scrolls. If Joash had died, the "Messianic Line" would have been broken. Jehosheba’s courage (the daughter of a king and wife of a priest) was the "Secret Movement of Grace." The "Temple" became the "Womb" of the future kingdom, protecting the "Last Spark" of David’s lamp.

3 Long Live the King (Yechi ha-Melekh): This coronation in the scrolls was a "Liturgical Act." By putting the "Covenant" (the Law) in the king’s hand, Jehoiada was re-establishing the "Constitutional Monarchy" of Israel. The king was not a "Despot" (like Athaliah), but a "Subject of the Word."

4 Treason! Treason! (Qesher! Qesher!): In the scrolls, this is the "Ironic Cry" of the usurper. Athaliah had murdered her way to the throne, yet she accused the "Rightful Heir" of treason. She didn't realize that the "Treason" was her own existence on the throne of David. Her death at the "Horse Entrance" was the "Cleaning of the Palace."

5 The Covenant: This was a "Tri-partite Agreement" in the scrolls. It wasn't just political; it was "Rooted" in the spiritual identity of the nation. The people were not just "Citizens of a State," but "The People of Yahweh." The destruction of the temple of Baal immediately followed, showing that "True Reform" always includes "Repentance and Removal."
