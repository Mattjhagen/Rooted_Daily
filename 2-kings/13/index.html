2 Kings 13

The LORD’s anger burned against Israel, and for a long time he kept them under the power of Hazael king of Aram and Ben-Hadad his son. 1

The Last Arrow

Now Elisha was suffering from the illness from which he died. Jehoash king of Israel went down to see him and wept over him. “My father! My father! The chariots and horsemen of Israel!” he cried. 2
“The LORD’s arrow of victory, the arrow of victory over Aram!” Elisha declared.
“The man of God was angry with him and said, ‘You should have struck the ground five or six times; then you would have defeated Aram and completely destroyed it.’” 3

The Resurrection in the Cave

Elisha died and was buried... 4
As soon as the body touched Elisha’s bones, the man came to life and stood up on his feet. 5
“Three times Jehoash defeated him, and so he recovered the Israelite towns.”

Translation Notes

1 For a Long Time: In the scrolls, this is a "Generational Consequence." The Northern Kingdom was being "Ground Down" for their persistent idolatry. The army was so reduced that they were "As Dust" (Verse 7). It shows that "National Power" is a "Lease from God" that can be revoked when the "Tenant" defaults on the Covenant.

2 Chariots and Horsemen: In the scrolls, the King of Israel used the exact same words Elisha used when Elijah was taken (Chapter 2). It was a "Recognition" that the "Defense of the Nation" was not in the 10 chariots remaining, but in the "Dying Prophet." It was a moment of "Spiritual Clarity" for a otherwise wicked king.

3 Five or Six Times: This was a "Test of Passion" in the scrolls. The king’s "Three Strikes" showed his "Lack of Zeal." He wanted a "Quick Fix" rather than a "Total Victory." Elisha was angry because the "Opportunity of Grace" was being limited by the "Mediocrity of the King’s Heart." We receive exactly as much as our "Faith" is willing to "Strike."

4 Elisha Died: The scrolls record the end of a "Double Portion" ministry. Elijah was taken in a "Whirlwind"; Elisha died of an "Illness." One was "Spectacular," the other was "Natural." Yet both were "Prophets of Fire." Elisha’s ministry was "Rooted" in the soil of common life, performing miracles in kitchens and fields.

5 Touched Elisha’s Bones: This is one of the most "Mysterious Miracles" in the scrolls. It shows that the "Anointing" didn't leave the body even in death. It was a "Sign" to Israel that while the Prophet was gone, the "Word of the LORD" remained alive and powerful. Even the "Remains" of a man of God are a "Source of Life."
