2 Kings 9

The young prophet poured the oil on Jehu’s head and declared, “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘I anoint you king over the LORD’s people Israel.’” 1
“You are to destroy the house of Ahab your master... I will avenge the blood of my servants the prophets.”
The watchman reported, “The driving is like that of Jehu son of Nimshi; he drives like a maniac.” 2

The Reckoning at Naboth’s Field

“Do you come in peace, Jehu?” Joram asked. “How can there be peace,” Jehu replied, “as long as all the idolatry and witchcraft of your mother Jezebel abound?” 3
Jehu drew his bow and hit Joram between the shoulders. The arrow pierced his heart.

The End of Jezebel

Jezebel painted her eyes, arranged her hair and looked out of a window.
Jehu looked up and shouted, “Who is on my side? Who?” Two or three eunuchs looked down at him.
“Throw her down!” Jehu said. So they threw her down, and some of her blood splattered the wall and the horses as they trampled her underfoot. 4
“Jezebel’s body will be like dung on the ground in the plot at Jezreel, so that no one will be able to say, ‘This is Jezebel.’” 5

Translation Notes

1 I Anoint You (Meschachtikha): In the scrolls, this is a "Revolutionary Commission." Jehu was a commander in the army. God was officially "De-legitimizing" the house of Ahab and "Handing the Sword" to Jehu. The young prophet’s instruction to "run" shows the "Dangerous Volatility" of this political coup.

2 Drives like a Maniac (Beshiiggaon): Literally in the scrolls, "Driving in Madness." Jehu’s driving was a "Physical Manifestation" of his internal "Zeal." He was a man of "Action" who didn't wait for "Diplomacy." His "Furious Speed" was the "Velocity of Judgment" catching up to the royal house.

3 Idolatry and Witchcraft (Zenunim ve-Kesaphim): Literally "Harlotries and Sorceries." Jehu’s response in the scrolls strips away the "Royal Politeness" of Joram. He identifies the "Root" of the nation’s decay: the spiritual corruption introduced by Jezebel. There can be no "Peace" while the "Poison" is still being celebrated.

4 Throw Her Down: This is a "Cold and Calculated Execution" in the scrolls. Jezebel tried to use her "Royal Presence" and "Makeup" (representing her deceptive power) to intimidate Jehu. By asking "Who is on my side?", Jehu proved that even Jezebel’s own attendants had no "Loyalty" to her once the fear was removed. Her "Splattered Blood" was the final "Cleanse" of the city wall.

5 Like Dung on the Ground: This is the "Total Devaluation" of the queen in the scrolls. The woman who had ruled with such "Splendor" and "Terror" was now "Refuse." The dogs left only the "Skulls, Feet, and Hands"—the parts that had directed the "Thought," the "Movement," and the "Action" of her evil. The "Word of the LORD" (Chapter 21) was fulfilled to the letter.
