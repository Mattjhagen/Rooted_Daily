1 Chronicles 1

The Beginning

Adam, Seth, Enosh,
Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,
Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech, Noah. 1

The Sons of Noah

The sons of Noah: Shem, Ham and Japheth. 2

The Path to Abraham

Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah,
Eber, Peleg, Reu,
Serug, Nahor, Terah,
And Abram (that is, Abraham). 3

The Family of Abraham

The sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael.
“Abraham was the father of Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel.” 4

The Kings of Edom

These were the kings who reigned in Edom before any Israelite king reigned. 5

Translation Notes

1 Adam to Noah: In the scrolls, this opening list is a "Foundation of Identity." Chronicles begins with "Adam" (Man) to show that the story of Israel is the story of "All Humanity." It is a "Reset" for the post-exilic community, reminding them that they are "Rooted" in the very beginning of creation.

2 Shem, Ham and Japheth: This is the "Table of Nations" condensed. In the scrolls, it establishes the "Geography of Grace." Israel (through Shem) is placed within the context of a "Global Family." It shows that God’s plan involves the "Whole Earth," even as it focuses on one people.

3 Abram (that is, Abraham): This parenthetical note in the scrolls is crucial. It marks the "Point of Transformation" from a "Single Man" to a "Multitude of Nations." The "Name Change" is the "Pivot" of the genealogy.

4 Esau and Israel: In the scrolls, the names are paired to show the "Twin Origins" of the region’s primary conflict. By calling him "Israel" instead of "Jacob," the Chronicler is emphasizing the "Status" and "Destiny" of the nation rather than the "Struggles" of the individual.

5 Before any Israelite King Reigned: This note in the scrolls is a "Chronological Marker." It acknowledges that Edom achieved "Political Sophistication" (kingship) before Israel did. However, the implication in Chronicles is that Israel’s delay was due to their unique "Covenant Requirement"—they were to be a nation ruled by God before they were ruled by a man.
