1 Chronicles 19

Hanun seized David’s envoys, shaved them, cut off their garments in the middle at the buttocks, and sent them away. 1

The Battle for the Cities of God

“Be strong, and let us fight bravely for our people and the cities of our God. The LORD will do what is good in his sight.” 2

The Aramean Collapse

David killed seven thousand of their charioteers and forty thousand of their foot soldiers... 3
“So the Arameans were not willing to help the Ammonites anymore.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Shaved and Cut: In the scrolls, this was a "Total Assault on Dignity." In that culture, the beard was a symbol of "Manhood and Honor." By shaving them and exposing them, Hanun was treating David’s "Ambassadors of Peace" as "Spies or Slaves." It was a "Rooted" act of war that forced David’s hand.

2 Be Strong (Chazaq): Joab’s speech in the scrolls is a "Classic Military Exhortation." He recognizes that the outcome of the battle is in "God’s Hands," but the "Bravery" is the responsibility of the men. He is fighting for "The Cities of our God," showing that the "Land" was considered "Holy Property."

3 Seven Thousand Charioteers: The scrolls record this "Massive Tactical Defeat." Aram (Syria) was a major power, but David’s army was now "Symphonic" in its execution. They were able to defeat a "Superpower" because they were "Rooted" in a higher purpose.

4 Not Willing to Help: The scrolls conclude with the "Deterrent Effect" of the victory. David’s "Consistency in Victory" eventually "Isolated" his enemies. It proves that a "Just War" fought with "Faith" leads to a "Lasting Peace" through the removal of the enemy’s support network.
