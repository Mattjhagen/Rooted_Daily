1 Chronicles 26

The Guardians of the Threshold

The divisions of the gatekeepers: From the Korahites: Meshelemiah...
“Obed-Edom also had sons... for God had blessed him.” 1
“All these were descendants of Obed-Edom... capable men with the strength to do the work.” 2

The Treasuries of the Dedicated

Shelomith and his relatives were in charge of all the treasuries for the things dedicated by King David... and by Samuel the seer, Saul, Abner and Joab. 3
“Everything that had been dedicated was under the care of Shelomith.”

Officials and Judges

“Chenaniah and his sons were appointed as officials and judges over Israel outside the temple.” 4
“Able men were in charge... for all the work of the LORD and for the king’s service.” 5

Translation Notes

1 God had Blessed Him: The scrolls link the "Large Family" of Obed-Edom back to his "Faithfulness" in hosting the Ark (Chapter 13). His "Blessing" became the "Labor Force" for the Temple. It shows that "Personal Blessing" often translates into "Kingdom Service."

2 Strength to do the Work (Koach): In the scrolls, being a gatekeeper was not a "Sedentary Job." It required "Physical Strength" (*Koach*) and "Alertness." They were the first line of defense for the "Holiness of the House." They were "Gate-Keepers," but they were also "Presence-Protectors."

3 Samuel, Saul, Abner, and Joab: This is a "Fascinating Catalog" in the scrolls. It shows that even the "Disqualified" (Saul) and the "Secular" (Joab) had contributed to the Temple. The "Spoils of History" were being "Sanctified" for the future. The Temple was the "Museum of God’s Victories" through diverse instruments.

4 Outside the Temple (Chutzah): The scrolls highlight that the Levites had "Civil Roles." They were the "Judiciary and Administration" for the nation. They ensured that the "Law of God" was not just a "Temple Document," but the "Daily Reality" of the marketplace and the home.

5 Work of the LORD and King’s Service: In the scrolls, there was no "Sacred-Secular Divide." Serving the King’s "Administration" was seen as part of the "Work of the LORD." This "Integrated Leadership" is the hallmark of a "Rooted" kingdom, where the "Values of the Sanctuary" govern the "Action of the State."
