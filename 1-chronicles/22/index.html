1 Chronicles 22

David said, “My son Solomon is young and inexperienced, and the house to be built for the LORD should be of great magnificence and fame throughout all lands.” 1

The Man of Peace

“You will have a son who will be a man of peace and rest... his name will be Solomon, and I will grant Israel peace and quiet during his reign.” 2
“He is the one who will build a house for my Name. He will be my son, and I will be his father.”

The Charge of David

“May the LORD give you discretion and understanding... Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or discouraged.” 3
“I have taken great pains to provide for the temple of the LORD... a hundred thousand talents of gold, a million talents of silver.” 4
“Now devote your heart and soul to seeking the LORD your God. Begin to build the sanctuary.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Great Magnificence (Le-Hagdil le-Ma’alah): In the scrolls, this is David’s "Legacy Vision." He knew that the Temple must reflect the "Transcendent Glory" of God to the nations. Even though he wasn't the "Builder," he was the "Architect of Preparation." He refused to leave the task of "Magnificence" to an inexperienced son alone.

2 Solomon (Shelomoh): Literally in the scrolls, "Peaceable." God was transitioning the nation from the "Era of the Sword" (David) to the "Era of the Sanctuary" (Solomon). David’s "Hands of Blood" could win the land, but only "Hands of Peace" could build the House. It shows that "Rest" is the prerequisite for "Temple Building."

3 Discretion and Understanding (Sekhel u-Binah): The scrolls highlight that building for God requires more than "Money"; it requires "Mindset." David prayed for Solomon’s "Intellectual and Spiritual" capacity to handle the "Complexity" of the project. It is a "Rooted" leadership principle: the internal character must match the external calling.

4 Taken Great Pains (Be-Onyi): Literally in the scrolls, "In my affliction/poverty." This is David’s "Sacrificial Perspective." Even as the wealthiest king, he viewed his work as an act of "Servant-Hardship." He didn't give from his "Excess," but from his "Devotion." The numbers (100k talents of gold) represent a "Staggering Investment" in the future presence of God.

5 Devote your Heart and Soul (Tenu Levabkhem): The scrolls conclude with the "Spiritual Prerequisite." Building the "Physical House" was useless unless the "Spiritual Heart" was set. David was calling the leaders to a "Holistic Commitment." The "Sanctuary" was to be the "Root" of their national life, not just a building in the city.
