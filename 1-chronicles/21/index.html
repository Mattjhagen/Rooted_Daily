1 Chronicles 21

Satan rose up against Israel and incited David to take a census of Israel. 1

The Choice of David

“Let me fall into the hands of the LORD, for his mercy is very great; but do not let me fall into human hands.” 2
“So the LORD sent a plague on Israel, and seventy thousand men of Israel fell dead.”

The Threshing Floor of Ornan

“No, I insist on paying the full price. I will not take for the LORD what is yours, or sacrifice a burnt offering that costs me nothing.” 3
“David built an altar there... and called on the LORD, and the LORD answered him with fire from heaven on the altar.” 4
“The house of the LORD God is to be here, and also the altar of burnt offering for Israel.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Satan Incited David: In the scrolls, this is a "Spiritual Adversity." While the parallel in 2 Samuel 24 says the LORD’s anger incited him, the Chronicler identifies the "Agency" of the temptation. David’s sin was "Self-Reliance"—trying to count his "Human Resources" instead of trusting in "Divine Provision." It was a "Rooted" failure of faith.

2 Mercy is Very Great (Rabbim Rachamaw): This is David’s "Core Theology" in the scrolls. Even under judgment, he knows that God’s "Compassion" is safer than man’s "Vengeance." He chooses the "Sword of the LORD" (the plague) because he would rather be disciplined by a "Loving Father" than destroyed by a "Hateful Enemy."

3 Costs me Nothing (Chinnam): In the scrolls, this is the "Price of Worship." David refuses to "Co-opt" Ornan’s sacrifice. True worship must be "Sacrificial" for the worshiper. If it costs nothing, it "Proves" nothing about the value of the One being worshiped. It is a "Rooted" principle of "Authentic Devotion."

4 Fire from Heaven: The scrolls record this as the "Divine Acceptance." Just as God answered Moses at the Tabernacle and would later answer Solomon at the Temple, He answered David at the Threshing Floor. The "Fire" signaled that this was the "Chosen Location" for the future house of God.

5 The House of the LORD is to be Here: This "Visionary Identification" in the scrolls transforms a "Place of Judgment" (where 70,000 died) into a "Place of Grace" (the Temple). Mount Moriah was where the "Sword was Sheathed." It teaches that God can take our "Greatest Failures" and turn them into the "Foundations" of His glory.
