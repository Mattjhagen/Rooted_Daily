1 Chronicles 9

The people of Judah were taken captive to Babylon because of their unfaithfulness. 1

The First to Return

Now the first to resettle on their own property in their own towns were some Israelites, priests, Levites and temple servants. 2

The Guardians of the Gates

The gatekeepers: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman and their relatives. Shallum was the chief. 3
“Altogether, those chosen to be gatekeepers at the thresholds numbered 212... David and Samuel the seer had appointed them to their positions of trust.” 4

The Eternal Song

Those who were musicians... stayed in the rooms of the temple and were exempt from other duties because they were responsible for the work day and night. 5

Translation Notes

1 Because of their Unfaithfulness (Ma'al): In the scrolls, this is the "Root Cause" of the exile. *Ma'al* is a technical term for "Breach of Trust" or "Sacrilege." The Chronicler prefaces the list of returnees with this reminder so that the "New Community" understands that their "Stability" depends on their "Faithfulness."

2 First to Resettle: The scrolls record the "Pioneer Spirit" of the returning exiles. They weren't just "Moving Back"; they were "Re-rooting." The priority was given to the "Temple Servants" (*Nethinim*) because the first task of the new community was to restore the "Worship of God."

3 Shallum the Chief: In the scrolls, the "Gatekeepers" were not just "Security Guards." They were "Sacred Custodians." They controlled the "Access to the Presence." Shallum’s position was one of "High Honor," ensuring that the "Holy" was protected from the "Common."

4 David and Samuel the Seer: This is a "Chronicler’s Connection" in the scrolls. It links the post-exilic gatekeepers directly to the "Founding Fathers." It shows that the "Structure of Worship" was not a "New Invention" but a "Restoration" of the original "Vision" given to the Prophets.

5 Work Day and Night (Yomam va-Laylah): This is the "Perpetual Ministry" in the scrolls. The musicians were the only ones "Exempt" from other work because their "Work" was the "Atmosphere of Heaven." They ensured that the "Song of Praise" never ceased in Jerusalem. It signifies that the "Re-rooted" nation was to be defined by its "Unending Worship."
