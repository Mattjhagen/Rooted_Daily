1 Chronicles 5

Reuben was the firstborn of Israel, but because he defiled his father’s bed, his rights as firstborn were given to the sons of Joseph. 1
“Though Judah became powerful... the rights of the firstborn belonged to Joseph.” 2

The Battle of the Transjordan

“They were helped in fighting them, and God delivered the Hagrites... into their hands.”
“They cried out to God during the battle. He answered their prayers, because they trusted in him.” 3

The Loss of the Land

But they were unfaithful to the God of their ancestors and prostituted themselves to the gods of the peoples of the land. 4
“So the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul king of Assyria... who took them into exile.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Rights as Firstborn: In the scrolls, this is a "Disqualification of Character." Reuben’s "Physical Priority" was overruled by his "Moral Failure." It shows that in the Kingdom of God, "Position" is always subordinate to "Character." The birthright (the double portion) went to Joseph (Ephraim and Manasseh).

2 Judah became Powerful: This is the Chronicler’s "Royal Balance." While Joseph got the "Property" (the birthright), Judah got the "Preeminence" (the King). It was a "Sovereign Division" of honors that shaped the future of the nation.

3 Cried out during the Battle (Va-Yiz'aqu): In the scrolls, this is the "Victory of the Voice." These tribes were on the "Frontiers," facing constant raids. Their success was not in their 44,760 men, but in their "Immediate Dependence" on God in the middle of the conflict. God responded to their "Trust," not just their "Need."

4 Prostituted Themselves (Va-Yiznu): This graphic language in the scrolls describes the "Spiritual Betrayal." Living on the "Edge" of the promised land made them vulnerable to "Pagan Influence." They stopped being "Rooted" in the Covenant and began to "Mix" with the environment until they became indistinguishable from the nations.

5 Stirred up the Spirit (Way-Ya'ar): The scrolls present the Assyrian invasion as a "Divine Act." Pul (Tiglath-Pileser) was the "Instrument" of God’s discipline. The tribes that were the "First to Settle" (Numbers 32) became the "First to be Deported." It is a warning that the "Privilege of Priority" carries the "Weight of Responsibility."
