Translation Notes

1 Descendants of Aaron: In the scrolls, the "Priestly Purity" was maintained through the lines of Eleazar and Ithamar. Nadab and Abihu (Leviticus 10) served as a "Permanent Warning" that the sacred office must be approached with "Holy Fear." The Chronicler emphasizes that the current priests are the "Rooted Heirs" of the original command.

2 Impartially by Casting Lots (Goral): The scrolls show that the "Order of Service" was determined by the "Lot." This was not "Chance," but a way to let "God Decide" the schedule. It removed "Human Rivalry" and "Favoritism." Whether you were a "High-Status" family or a "Lower" one, your turn was determined by the "Sovereign Lot." It ensured that the "Service of the Altar" was a "Common Responsibility."

3 Appointed Order (Pequddatam): In the scrolls, this is the "Liturgy of Time." The 24 orders meant that every priest knew exactly when his "Season of Service" would occur (two weeks a year). This "Symmetry" provided "Consistency" for the Temple. It is the "Spiritual Clockwork" of the nation.

4 They Also Cast Lots: The scrolls record that the Levites followed the same "Egalitarian Principle" as the priests. The "Head of the Family" was treated the same as the "Youngest Brother." In the Sanctuary, "Position" was determined by "Divine Choice" rather than "Human Seniority." It is a "Rooted" lesson in "Equality before God."
