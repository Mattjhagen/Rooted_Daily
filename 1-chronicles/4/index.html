1 Chronicles 4

The Prayer of Jabez

Jabez was more honorable than his brothers. His mother had named him Jabez, saying, “I gave birth to him in pain.” 1
“Oh, that you would bless me and enlarge my territory! Let your hand be with me, and keep me from harm so that I will be free from pain.” 2
“And God granted his request.”

The Potters of the King

They were the potters who lived at Netaim and Gederah; they stayed there and worked for the king. 3

The Clan of Simeon

The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah and Shaul.
“They went to the outskirts of Gedor... in search of pasture for their flocks.”
“They killed the remaining Amalekites who had escaped, and they have lived there to this day.” 4

Translation Notes

1 Jabez (Ya'betz): In the scrolls, his name is a pun on the Hebrew word for pain (*Otzeb*). His mother named him after her "Struggle." Jabez was "Rooted in Pain" from birth, but he refused to let his "Label" define his "Destiny." His "Honor" was found in his "Spiritual Ambition."

2 Enlarge my Territory (Gevuli): This is not just a request for "Real Estate" in the scrolls, but for "Influence and Opportunity." Jabez was asking God to break the "Boundaries of his Limitation." He wanted his life to have a "Greater Reach" for the glory of God. It is a prayer of "Faith-Expansion."

3 Worked for the King: In the scrolls, this identifies the "Industrial Heritage" of Judah. These weren't just common potters; they were "Royal Artisans." They lived "with the King" even when the King was in a different location. It represents the "Quiet Service" of those who use their "Craft" for the Kingdom.

4 Killed the remaining Amalekites: The scrolls record this as a "Delayed Fulfillment." The Amalekites were the ancient enemies of Israel. The fact that Simeonites were still "Clearing the Land" during the reign of Hezekiah shows that the "Conquest" was a "Long Process." It signifies the "Ongoing Battle" to remove the "Remnants of Evil" from the heart of the inheritance.
